# Existing Civilisation Wrapper Variants

**Status:** COMPARATIVE RESEARCH AREA / NOT CANONICAL

These folders are comparative wrapper-design cases. Their purpose is to explore how materially different existing civil architectures change the requirements placed on a Concord Civilisational Wrapper.

The initial cases deliberately span different constitutional and political forms. Research should identify what can remain common at the Concord interface, what requires an inward-facing society-specific adapter, where translation becomes difficult, and where Ethical Kernel or multisubstrate compatibility creates genuine boundary problems.

These are research examples, not claims that a completed Concord wrapper presently exists for any named society.
