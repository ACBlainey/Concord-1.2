**PASS.** Floor/ceiling clarification is compatible with Minimum Necessary Permission. Permission remains a specialised contextual capability form; MNC does not replace FPA's participant/action/context grammar.

## Authority and responsibility

**PASS.** Operational discretion remains distinct from authority expansion. Responsibility still requires sufficient capability/control, while responsibility does not itself create unlimited authority.

## Emergency / assumed authority

**PASS.** The clarification does not prevent urgent bounded action. Where ordinary authorisation cannot arrive in time, the existing exceptional assumed-authority branch remains available with articulation, boundedness, termination and retrospective review.

Logging the emergency action supports review but does not retroactively manufacture legitimacy.

## KCS / provenance

**PASS.** KCS may preserve capability grants, activation records, logs, reviews and changes without treating recorded status as normative legitimacy.
