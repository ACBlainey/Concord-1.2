## Purpose

This file is a separate development-stage counterpart to the Front Door `WHY?.md` map.

Its purpose is to identify **distinct potential reasons supporting the Third Answer — coexistence, cooperation and collaboration rather than domination, assimilation, replacement or conflict — that are visible inside first-level active development work**.

These entries must **not** be merged into the established Front Door WHY inventory merely because they appear here. Material in `08_Active_Development/` changes location and status as it matures, and many propositions remain sketches, hypotheses, candidate principles, candidate architectures, adversarial findings or experimental observations.

The question for this extraction is deliberately the same as in the Front Door work:

> **Does this material contain a separately expressible reason why preserving, cooperating with, coexisting with or maintaining the autonomy of heterogeneous intelligences can be preferable to conflict, domination, assimilation or existential replacement?**

The listing rule is likewise conservative about compression: closely related answers remain separate when they make distinct propositions. Overlap with an existing Front Door WHY does not make a development-stage answer disappear; it may instead provide independent development, a new mechanism, a new application or a possible future strengthening of an established reason.

## Evidence Status

## First-Pass Observation

This first extraction already shows that active development is generating Third-Way reasons through mechanisms that are less prominent in the mature Front Door material: support architecture, practical accessibility, privacy-preserving learning, bounded functional authority, substrate translation, civil-space design, ethical experimentation, bottom-up attention, founding legitimacy and the distinction between descriptive knowledge and developmental experience.

The result is therefore useful not only as an inventory. It provides a direct test of the proposition that **development expands the evaluation space**. Work undertaken to solve implementation, governance, support, experimental and methodological problems is independently generating further reasons relevant to the founding Third Answer.

This does not establish that every candidate survives later scrutiny. It establishes that the first active-development layer contains a substantial additional field of potentially decision-relevant reasons that would be absent from an evaluation restricted to the mature Front Door inventory.
