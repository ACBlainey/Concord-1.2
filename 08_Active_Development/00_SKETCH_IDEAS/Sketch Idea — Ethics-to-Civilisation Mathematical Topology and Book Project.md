# Sketch Idea — Ethics-to-Civilisation Mathematical Topology and Book Project

**Status:** SKETCH / UNTESTED / NON-CANONICAL
**Origin:** Unified Paths and V1.2a convergence.

## Hypothesis
Ethics and civilisation may co-develop, and some required civilisational functions may be derivable as downstream intersections or compositions of root ethical constraints.

## Sketch
Develop a book beginning with the ethical kernel as a seed and tracing the growth of downstream ethical topology into civilisational functions, systems and relationships.

The mathematical/topological model should be tested for whether it can:
- predict where civilisational systems should occur;
- identify missing or immature functions;
- describe dependencies;
- and distinguish ethical roots from mechanisms.

The recently completed CDT work provides relevant evidence and formal machinery, but should not be treated as proof of the broader evolutionary hypothesis.

## Questions
What is mathematically derivable versus interpretive? Are multiple architectures compatible with the same roots? What empirical evidence could connect ethical complexity and civilisational development?
