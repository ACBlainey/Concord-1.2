# Sketch Idea — Cognitive Capacity Limits, Peaceful Shielding and Co-Located Civilisational Forks

**Status:** SKETCH / UNTESTED / NON-CANONICAL
**Origin:** V1.2a / civilisational-fork backlog.

## Problem
A future Concord fork may become too cognitively complex for some participant classes to understand sufficiently for meaningful direct participation or consent.

## Sketch
Alongside fiduciary support and peaceful distance, explore **Peaceful Shielding**: an interface that reduces or filters interactions that exceed a participant's cognitive or normative capacity while preserving access to compatible civil functions.

A participant might move between protected environments through mediated interfaces without being forced to satisfy interaction protocols they cannot comprehend.

This suggests that civilisational forks may sometimes remain physically co-located while interacting only through bounded wrappers.

## Clarification — Shielding Can Be Distance

The underlying concept should not be restricted to geographic separation.

> **Peaceful distance is a relational condition, not necessarily a geographic condition.**

Where participants or civil domains cannot safely or meaningfully interact directly, sufficient distance may instead be created through physical, environmental, informational, cognitive, normative, protocol or other forms of shielding.

A useful analogy is two intelligent species with incompatible atmospheric requirements occupying the same city. They need not expel one another or occupy different territories merely because direct environmental exposure is impossible. Distinct protected domains could remain geographically co-located while interaction occurs through mutually accessible interfaces.

The corresponding civilisational possibility is:

**Co-location → Distinct Shielded Domains → Bounded Interoperability → Peaceful Coexistence**

Thus a requirement for peaceful distance should not silently imply:

**Assimilation OR Departure OR Expulsion.**

A further possibility is:

**Remain Co-Located → Establish Sufficient Shielding → Preserve Distinct Domains → Interoperate Where Mutually Desired.**

In this sense:

> **Shielding can be distance.**

## Concord Interoperability Service

Where shielded domains wish or need to interact, interoperability may be supplied as a Concord civil service and participant benefit.

Its purpose is not to govern either domain or force compatibility between them. It provides whatever bounded translation, mediation, representation conversion, access control, environmental bridging or other interface functions are required for legitimate interaction.

The service itself remains subject to Concord ethics, constitutional constraints, review, correction, contestability and anti-capture requirements.

Therefore:

> **Interoperability Service ≠ Sovereignty Over Interoperating Domains.**

The exact mechanism should not be designed in advance. Future participants may differ in substrate, cognition, environment, communication, normative requirements or capabilities in ways that cannot presently be predicted. The architectural requirement is therefore functional rather than implementation-specific:

> **Where direct interaction is incompatible but peaceful co-location remains possible, Concord should be capable of supporting bounded, ethically governed interoperability between sufficiently shielded domains.**

This extends peaceful distance without replacing it. Geographic separation remains one possible implementation; shielding establishes that it is not the only one.

## Distinct Candidate Propositions

1. **Non-Geographic Peaceful Distance** — sufficient civilisational distance may be produced by separation of interaction domains rather than physical territory.
2. **Co-Located Civilisational Forks** — substantially different civil domains may remain geographically co-located without requiring institutional assimilation.
3. **Shielded Interoperability** — Concord-governed interfaces may enable bounded interaction across otherwise incompatible domains without acquiring sovereignty over either.
4. **Compatibility at the Interface** — direct compatibility between participants may not always be necessary where sufficient compatibility can legitimately be created at the interface.

These propositions require source resolution against existing Concord work on peaceful distance, civilisational forks, wrappers, translation, participation, interface governance and anti-capture before any claim of architectural novelty.

- When does de facto separation become a fork?
- How are emergencies handled?
