# Sketch Idea — Unified Paths Comparative Ethics Book and Religion-as-Transmission Hypothesis

**Status:** DECOMPOSED / SYNTHESIS PROJECT SEPARATED FROM RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS / RETAINED FOR PROVENANCE  
**Origin:** Unified Paths of Light final-comparison backlog.  
**Disposition Date:** 24 September 2026  
**Disposition Basis:** Active Development and Sketch Portfolio Audit 002.

## Original Observation

The completed Unified Paths comparative work may support a book-length account of the extracted ethics, their source traditions, compression paths, convergences and residual differences.

## Original Hypothesis

Religious traditions may function, among other things, as intergenerational teaching, transmission and interpretation systems for ethical rules.

A subsidiary hypothesis asks whether traditions sometimes emphasise different directions of ethical application—for example inward self-development versus outward social interoperability—without treating such an emphasis as an exhaustive description of the tradition.

## Decomposition Result

The original sketch contained two different kinds of work that should no longer share one evidentiary status.

### A. Comparative Ethics Book — synthesis/publication project

The book concept is now sufficiently supplied by completed Unified Paths material to be treated as a future synthesis/publication project rather than an unresolved architecture sketch.

Its source base includes, among other material:
- the frozen E2–E9 derivations;
- the *Comparative Synthesis of the Final Root Ethical Kernels — E2 to E9*;
- experiment-specific provenance and method records;
- U1–U8 cross-experimental compression;
- Compound Ethics Topology and Comparative Mapping;
- the Ten Commandments comparison;
- recorded residuals, disagreements and source-quality limitations.

The book should distinguish clearly among:

**source text**
→ **extracted proposition**
→ **normalised ethical function**
→ **experiment-specific frozen kernel**
→ **cross-experimental comparison**
→ **interpretive synthesis**

The book may describe what the experiments found.

It must not silently convert interpretation into experimental result.

### B. Religion-as-Transmission — historical/anthropological research hypothesis

The proposition that religions function as intergenerational ethical transmission systems is plausible as a research question but is not established by Unified Paths extraction.

Unified Paths primarily examines ethical content and comparative compression.

It does not, by itself, establish:
- how religions historically originated;
- why particular doctrines persisted;
- whether ethical transmission was a primary or secondary function;
- whether convergence arose through common ancestry, contact, independent discovery, recurring social problems, translation effects or the extraction method;
- how institutional, ritual, theological, political and identity functions relate to ethical transmission.

Therefore the transmission proposition remains a separate historical/anthropological hypothesis.

### C. Direction-of-Application Hypothesis

The proposed contrast between inward self-development and outward social interoperability may be useful as a comparative dimension.

It must not become a binary classification of traditions.

A tradition may contain:
- inward disciplines;
- interpersonal ethics;
- institutional rules;
- communal obligations;
- metaphysical commitments;
- ritual practices;
- legal structures;
- political teachings.

The appropriate future question is whether the Unified Paths evidence supports measurable differences in **relative emphasis**, not whether a tradition belongs to one category.

## Evidence Boundary

The completed comparative synthesis supports claims about the outputs of the Concord's defined extraction experiments.

It does not by itself establish a general theory of religion.

Therefore:

> **Extracted Ethical Convergence ≠ Explanation of Why Religions Exist**

> **Cross-Tradition Similarity ≠ Evidence of a Single Historical Transmission Path**

> **Independent Derivation Result ≠ Historical Independent Discovery**

> **Compression Result ≠ Complete Description of a Tradition**

> **Ethical Residual ≠ Non-Ethical or Unimportant Content**

> **Book Synthesis ≠ New Experimental Evidence**

## Future Research Requirements for the Transmission Hypothesis

Before promotion to a dedicated research programme, define competing explanations such as:
- common historical inheritance;
- direct or indirect cultural transmission;
- independent discovery;
- convergence under recurring human/social constraints;
- selection for memorable/transmissible rules;
- institutional adaptation;
- methodological convergence introduced by translation or extraction.

A useful future study would need evidence capable of distinguishing among these explanations rather than merely finding that more than one is possible.

Potential evidence domains may eventually include:
- historical chronology;
- textual transmission;
- linguistic borrowing;
- archaeology;
- anthropology;
- comparative ritual/institutional history;
- oral-tradition studies;
- documented intercultural contact.

These are research requirements, not findings of the current Unified Paths corpus.

## Future Route

### Comparative Ethics Book

Retain as a future synthesis/publication project using frozen Unified Paths results and explicit provenance.

No new Development Note is required merely to restate the existing comparative evidence.

### Religion-as-Transmission Hypothesis

Retain as a research hypothesis until:
1. competing explanations are formalised;
2. evidence requirements are specified;
3. appropriate external historical/anthropological sources are identified;
4. falsification or discrimination criteria exist.

If those conditions are reached, create a dedicated research Development Note separate from the book.

## Original Development Questions Preserved

- Which claims are extraction results versus interpretive hypotheses?
- How does each tradition teach, transmit and compound ethics?
- Which differences survive root compression?
- Does historical transmission explain convergence, independent discovery, or both?
- What evidence would distinguish these explanations?

## Original Risks Preserved

- reducing religions to ethics alone;
- overgeneralising traditions;
- treating an extraction method as a theory of religion.

These risks remain active.
