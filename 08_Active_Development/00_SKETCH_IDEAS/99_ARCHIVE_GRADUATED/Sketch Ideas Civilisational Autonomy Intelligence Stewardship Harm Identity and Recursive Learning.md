## Sketch 2 — Human–AI Intelligence Asymmetry and the Legacy Ladder

### Disposition
**DECOMPOSED / PARTLY OWNED / RESIDUAL RESEARCH REMAINS**

Several parts are now source-owned elsewhere:
- fiduciary support and guardianship questions → Machine Ethics and Guardianship;
- human–AI sensor fusion → dedicated Sensor Fusion Development Note;
- standing/capacity/authority distinctions → Personhood, Capacity, Fiduciary Support and Derived Responsibility;
- advanced-AI authority remains bounded by the wider Concord authority architecture.

The unresolved residual is narrower:

> **How should a multisubstrate civilisation preserve autonomy, appeal, explanation and voluntary divergence when intelligences differ radically in cognitive capability?**

The **Legacy Ladder** remains a useful descriptive hypothesis:
unaltered biological humans → augmented humans → hybrids → increasingly technical hybrids → artificial intelligences → highly advanced artificial intelligences.

It must not become a status hierarchy.

> **Capability Gradient ≠ Moral Rank**

> **Legacy Preservation ≠ Forced Stasis**

> **Advanced Capability ≠ Automatic Fiduciary Authority**

### Future route
If developed, create a bounded Human–AI Civilisational Relations research note focused on extreme cognitive asymmetry, appeal and voluntary divergence—not a duplicate guardianship architecture.

**SUBSTANTIALLY OVERTAKEN / SOURCE OWNERSHIP TRANSFERRED**

The later **Peaceful Heterogeneity — Rights, Harm and Bounded Authority** work now directly owns the core question:

## Sketch 4 — Cognitive Harm, Communication and Responsibility

### Disposition
**GENUINE RESIDUAL / DEVELOPMENT NOTE CANDIDATE**

This remains one of the clearest unresolved workstreams in the consolidated sketch.

The original Face-Slap Machine analogy is useful as a causal probe but does not establish the conclusion.

- developmental vulnerability;
- coercion;
- engineered manipulation;
- asymmetric power;
- persistent unwanted contact.

### Future route
Promote separately only after source-resolution against PH harm/consent work, Safe Spaces, autonomy, harassment and relevant judiciary architecture.

This is a **real residual**, not an ownership-transfer item.

## Sketch 11 — The Hidden Lesson of the Size 9 Shoe Problem

### Disposition
**RESIDUAL ANTI-CAPTURE TEST / DO NOT DUPLICATE THE SIZE 9 SOURCE**

The canonical source remains:

`02_Epistemic_and_Research/The Size 9 Shoe Problem.md`

The hidden lesson adds a distinct governance test:

> **Designer Exposure Test:** Are the people creating a rule exposed to the same costs, restrictions, risks and failure modes as those governed by it?

This should be treated as an anti-capture / distributional diagnostic, not as a new standalone principle merely because it is memorable.

Important distinction:

> **Designer Exposure ≠ Proof of Fairness**

A rule can burden its designers and still be unjust.

Conversely, legitimate role differences may sometimes create asymmetric exposure.

### Future route
Source-resolve against anti-capture, conflict-of-interest, distributional review and authority-justification work before deciding whether the test belongs as a companion mechanism.
