# Sketch Idea — Unified Paths Compound Ethics Map and Comparative Ethics Compendium

**Status:** SKETCH / UNTESTED / NON-CANONICAL
**Origin:** Unified Paths of Light final-comparison backlog.

## Problem
The extraction work now contains many principles at different levels of abstraction. A flat list obscures how compound ethics relate to compressed roots.

## Sketch
Construct a comparative ethics map/compendium covering the traditions used in Unified Paths. Arrange extracted principles by abstraction level and trace how downstream or compound principles relate to root ethics.

Candidate representation:
**Source Passage/Tradition → Extracted Principle → Compound Relation → Higher Abstraction → Root Kernel**

The result could eventually support a single comparative ethics volume, but the map and the book are separable projects.

## Development Questions
- What constitutes a defensible parent/child ethical relation?
- Can one compound principle have multiple root parents?
- How should disputed mappings be represented?
- How can source provenance survive compression?
- Can the topology be machine-readable?

## Possible Tests
Reconstruct selected extracted principles from roots and compare with independent classification; measure disagreement among classifiers.

Formalised from `compound ethics map.txt`.
