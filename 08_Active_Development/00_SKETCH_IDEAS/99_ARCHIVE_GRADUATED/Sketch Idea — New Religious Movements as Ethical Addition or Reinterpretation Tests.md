# Sketch Idea — New Religious Movements as Ethical Addition or Reinterpretation Tests

**Status:** SKETCH / UNTESTED / NON-CANONICAL
**Origin:** Unified Paths comparative backlog.

## Problem
A hypothesis arising from Unified Paths is that a later religious tradition may add ethical rules, reinterpret existing ones, change their weighting, or transmit them through a new compound structure.

## Sketch
Use later religious movements as out-of-sample tests rather than assuming the hypothesis is true.

Initial proposed cases from the source note:
- the Book of Mormon / Latter-day Saint textual tradition;
- Scientology.

## Questions
- Does independent extraction produce a new root ethic?
- Are apparent novelties compounds or reinterpretations of existing roots?
- Does weighting change without changing the root kernel?
- What corpus boundaries and source-status distinctions are required for a fair comparison?

## Method
Freeze corpus and extraction method before comparison with U1-U8. Preserve theological and textual distinctions rather than treating movements as interchangeable.

## Provenance
Formalised from `Mormon.txt`.
