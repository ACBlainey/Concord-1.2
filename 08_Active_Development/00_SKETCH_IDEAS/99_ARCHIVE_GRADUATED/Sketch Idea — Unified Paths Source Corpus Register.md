# Sketch Idea — Unified Paths Source Corpus Register

**Status:** SKETCH / NON-CANONICAL ADMINISTRATIVE DEVELOPMENT
**Origin:** Unified Paths final-comparison backlog.

## Problem
The completed comparative work needs a definitive list of the religious texts and source corpora actually used.

## Sketch
Create a provenance register for every Unified Paths comparison recording:
- tradition;
- experiment identifier;
- exact text/corpus;
- translation/edition where known;
- extraction scope;
- exclusions;
- source location;
- number of extracted principles;
- compression outputs;
- and final kernel.

Examples named in the source note include Torah, Talmud, Psalms and Qur'an, but the definitive list must be reconstructed from the actual project records rather than memory.

## Purpose
This is necessary for reproducibility, auditability and any future comparative book.

## Provenance
Formalised from `Religious text list.txt`.

## Source Resolution — 22 September 2026

### Result

**INFRASTRUCTURE TASK / SUBSTANTIALLY SUPPLIED IN DISTRIBUTED FORM / CONSOLIDATION STILL REQUIRED.**

The requested provenance information has not been lost. It is already distributed across the completed Unified Paths experiment records, especially the corpus-boundary freezes, source ledgers, provenance audits, extraction closures, kernel freezes and the post-experimental *Comparative Synthesis of the Final Root Ethical Kernels — E2 to E9*.

The synthesis already performs part of the proposed register function by bringing the experiment sequence, primary source anchors and final ethical architectures into one document. Later experiments also have substantially stronger corpus-boundary and provenance records than the original E2 exploratory work.

However, there is still no single maintained **Source Corpus Register** whose primary purpose is to answer, experiment by experiment:

- what exact corpus was admitted to primary derivation;
- what was withheld for validation;
- what translation/source hierarchy was used;
- what extraction boundary and closure mode applied;
- where the detailed provenance records are located;
- what final frozen output resulted;
- and what limitations or historical methodological differences apply.

Therefore the residual task is **consolidation and navigation**, not reconstruction of the experiments from memory.

### Source-resolved experiment anchors

The existing records establish at least the following high-level anchors:

- **E2** — Psalm-anchored exploratory eight-tradition comparison; 150 Psalms as primary anchor, then comparison with Jewish, Buddhist, Hindu, Islamic, Sikh, Chinese/Confucian and African traditional materials. E2 predates the later strict corpus-freeze/provenance architecture and must retain that methodological distinction.
- **E3** — Qur'an anchor reversal; all 114 surahs traversed, with dedicated Qur'anic source index, source/provenance repair and later comparative evidence architecture.
- **E4** — Tanakh minus Psalms as frozen primary derivation corpus; Psalms withheld as internal canonical holdout; post-biblical Jewish material reserved for later validation.
- **E5** — frozen Early Buddhist Pāli corpus: four core Nikāyas plus predefined Dhammapada, Udāna, Itivuttaka, Sutta Nipāta, Theragāthā and Therīgāthā extension; Vinaya, Abhidhamma and later traditions withheld from primary derivation.
- **E6** — frozen Hindu corpus combining Bhagavad Gītā, predefined principal Upaniṣads, selected Mahābhārata didactic strata and structured Rāmāyaṇa sampling; Dharmaśāstra/Manusmṛti, Yoga Sūtras and later materials withheld for validation.
- **E7** — frozen pre-Qin / early classical Chinese corpus: Analects, Mencius, Xunzi, Daodejing, Zhuangzi Inner Chapters and selected ethical/political/social-order Mozi material; later strata and traditions withheld.
- **E8** — Guru Granth Sahib as the structural primary Sikh corpus, systematically traversed using canonical ang numbering; later Sikh institutional/commentarial corpora withheld from primary derivation.
- **E9** — Akan oral-medium stress test with the primary extraction corpus frozen to J. G. Christaller's 1879 Twi proverb collection, supported by extensive translation, provenance, mediation and source-quality controls; later scholarship used only under bounded audit functions.

E10–E12 are later comparative extensions and are not part of the completed E2–E9 founding comparison. Their source registers should be added only as their own corpus boundaries become frozen and their experiments proceed.

### Required artifact

Create a maintained file in the Unified Paths methodology/provenance area rather than a Development Note. Suggested function:

# Unified Paths Source Corpus Register

For each experiment record:

1. experiment ID and tradition/anchor;
2. experimental status;
3. primary derivation corpus;
4. exact boundary/scope;
5. withheld/validation corpus;
6. translation/edition/source hierarchy where documented;
7. extraction closure mode;
8. detailed source/provenance record locations;
9. extraction/catalogue scale where explicitly documented;
10. frozen compression/kernel output;
11. methodological limitations;
12. later amendments/provenance repairs without rewriting the historical experiment.

### Important controls

- **Register ≠ New Evidence.** It indexes existing evidence and must not silently repair missing provenance.
- Unknown edition/translation details remain **UNKNOWN / NOT YET CONSOLIDATED** until source-resolved.
- E2 must not be retroactively described as having the stricter controls introduced in E3–E9.
- Validation/holdout sources must not be relabelled as primary derivation sources.
- E10–E12 must remain distinct from the completed E2–E9 synthesis.
- The register should point to detailed records rather than duplicate hundreds of provenance files.
- Future experiments should update the register as part of experiment closure.
