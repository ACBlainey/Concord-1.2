# Active Development and Sketch Portfolio Audit 002 — Promotion and Graduation Review

**Project:** The Concord Framework  
**Author:** Alexander C. Blainey  
**Date:** 24 September 2026  
**Document Type:** Portfolio Audit / Development-State Review  
**Status:** COMPLETED / DEVELOPMENT ROUTING REVIEW / NOT CANONICAL

## 1. Purpose

This audit reviews the current live `00_SKETCH_IDEAS` and `08_Active_Development` portfolio after:

- Unified Paths development;
- Civilisational Developmental Topology work;
- PMEDG portable-module development;
- the completed BPM back-propagation programme;
- the Layer-Zero / Layer −0 candidate-principle and candidate-architecture audits;
- and the first Ethical Guardian experimental cycle.

The questions are:

1. Which live sketches have matured enough to leave sketch status?
2. Which active Development Notes or candidate objects have reached a legitimate graduation threshold?
3. Which apparently mature objects must remain where they are because their own evidence explicitly says further work is required?
4. Which live sketches have been partly overtaken by later architecture and should be rerouted rather than duplicated?

The audit follows the existing rule:

> **Interesting Idea ≠ Established Fact**  
> **Adversarial Survival ≠ Validation**  
> **Portable Location ≠ Graduation**  
> **Promotion Means Developmental Ownership Transfer, Not Canonicalisation**

---

# 2. Executive Finding

The portfolio is generally healthy but contains a small amount of **development-state lag**: several live sketches are now more mature than their folder status suggests.

At the same time, the audit finds **no major Active Development architecture that should now be silently graduated to canonical or supported status**.

The correct result is asymmetric:

> **SEVERAL SKETCHES ARE READY FOR PROMOTION TO DEVELOPMENT NOTE**

but:

> **CURRENT CANDIDATE PRINCIPLES, CANDIDATE ARCHITECTURES, GOVERNANCE SYSTEMS AND ETHICAL-GUARDIAN WORK ARE NOT YET READY FOR GRADUATION**

This is evidence that the development pipeline is discriminating rather than merely ratcheting upward.

# 12. Portfolio-Level Finding

The current Active Development area does not suffer primarily from under-development.

It now suffers mildly from **status lag**.

Some ideas have accumulated enough definition that leaving them in the sketch queue obscures the fact that they already have a stable development question and identifiable source family.

At the same time, the candidate layers correctly resist automatic graduation after surviving adversarial examination.

This is the desired behaviour of the pipeline:

**Sketch maturity → promote ownership**

while:

**Adversarial survival → continue testing, not automatic canonicalisation**
