**Project:** The Concord Framework  
**Author:** Alexander C. Blainey  
**Date:** 22 September 2026  
**Document Type:** Sketch Portfolio Review / Status Reassessment  
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / NOT CANONICAL

---

## Purpose

This review reassesses the Sketch Ideas portfolio after two major bodies of recent work:

1. the final Unified Paths of Light comparative ethics work; and
2. the V1.2a → canonical Civilisational Developmental Topology (CDT) programme.

The question is not merely which sketches are interesting.

It is:

> **Which sketches have changed status because recent work has answered part of them, supplied machinery they need, made them newly testable, exposed them as duplicates, or made them useful to the next ordinary Concord development cycle?**

No sketch is promoted automatically. Existing sketch files remain provenance records.

---

# 1. Major Portfolio-Level Change

The recent work changes the portfolio in three important ways.

### 1.1 Unified Paths supplied an empirical/comparative base that many older ethics sketches previously lacked

The portfolio now has:
- multiple independently extracted religious/traditional ethical kernels;
- a final cross-comparison;
- a compressed U1–U8 root kernel;
- source-specific extraction histories;
- evidence of convergence and residual differences;
- a much larger body of compound principles.

Therefore several sketches that once asked whether a comparative ethics structure could exist can now be reframed as **organisation, formalisation or out-of-sample testing problems**.

### 1.2 CDT supplied machinery that several sketches had independently anticipated

CDT now provides:
- L0–L3 civilisational function topology;
- a provisional L4 abstraction ceiling;
- node/edge semantics;
- ownership and interface modelling;
- KCS dependency representation;
- Civil State Map;
- Civilisation Clock;
- concept/mechanism/test/integration distinctions;
- source resolution before gap declaration;
- developmental bottleneck selection;
- freeze/retest/change propagation;
- and a demonstrated natural stopping rule.

Several older sketches are therefore no longer merely speculative. They can now be connected to an operational development framework.

### 1.3 Some sketches are directly relevant to CLOCK-004's handoff

CLOCK-004 handed ordinary Concord development to:
1. source resolution of L2-04 Consequence-Relevant Civil Reconciliation;
2. L3-15 Cold Memory / Functional Forgetting;
3. bounded L3-04/L3-06 equivalence and recognition work;
4. later L2-11 Identity/Membership Continuity.

Some sketches now provide candidate hypotheses, tests or failure cases for these tasks.

# 2. Newly Added Sketches — Revised Status

## 2.1 Jainism Standalone Unified Paths Comparison

**Previous status:** new sketch.

**Effect of recent work:** The Unified Paths methodology is now mature enough that this is not primarily a methodology-development question. It is an **out-of-sample replication case**.

**Revised assessment:** **READY FOR BOUNDED EXPERIMENT / REPLICATION CASE.**

It could test whether the U1–U8 convergence survives a tradition excluded from the later comparison boundary.

Do not fold Jainism into the completed founding comparison retrospectively. Treat it as a new replication.

---

## 2.2 Blaineyan Reasoning, Art and Speculative Possibility-Space Research

**Effect of recent work:** CDT and the invention-frontier work strengthen the need to distinguish controlled generation of candidate possibility space from evidence and canonical adoption.

Artistic/speculative generation may therefore be studied as an upstream **candidate-generation function**, while CDT/source resolution/freeze/retest supply downstream epistemic controls.

**Revised assessment:** **STRONGER METHODOLOGICAL RELEVANCE / READY FOR DEVELOPMENT NOTE, BUT NOT A CDT COMPONENT.**

Potential relation:

**Speculative Generation → Candidate Possibility Space → Source/Evidence Resolution → Test → Retain/Reject/Revise**

This may help formalise where fiction, art, scenario design and Blaineyan Reasoning legitimately enter Concord research.

---

## 2.3 Unified Paths Compound Ethics Map and Comparative Ethics Compendium

**Effect of recent work:** This sketch has changed substantially.

The data required for the project now largely exists, and CDT has demonstrated a workable node/edge/topology vocabulary.

The project can therefore become a real formalisation exercise rather than a hypothetical book idea.

**Revised assessment:** **HIGH-VALUE DEVELOPMENT CANDIDATE / NOW TECHNICALLY ENABLED.**

Possible representation:

**Root Ethic → Compound Ethical Relation → Derived Principle → Domain Constraint → Civilisational Function**

However, the ethics topology must not be forced into the CDT civilisational topology if the evidence does not support one-to-one correspondence.

This sketch could become an important bridge between Unified Paths and CDT.

## 2.4 Participant-Serving Civilisation, Temporary Authority and Persistent Exit

**Effect of recent work:** Much of this proposition is now independently echoed by the recent architecture:
- bounded authority;
- scope justification;
- temporary/emergency authority;
- anti-domination;
- peaceful distance;
- non-sovereign coordination;
- CBERRM's separation of consequence, responsibility, jurisdiction and remedy;
- CDT's repeated rule that capability/development leverage does not create authority.

**Revised assessment:** **PARTIALLY SUPPLIED BY EXISTING ARCHITECTURE / SOURCE-RESOLUTION CANDIDATE BEFORE DEVELOPMENT.**

The sketch may represent a cross-system constitutional synthesis rather than a missing mechanism.

Next step should be to test whether a coherent existing Concord principle can be extracted without inventing new authority rules.

## 2.5 Empirical Testing Programme for Concord Systems and Modules

**Effect of recent work:** This sketch is much more important now.

CDT repeatedly distinguishes:
- concept;
- mechanism;
- test;
- integration.

The founding CDT programme also exposed the limit of paper-level scenario testing and explicitly left independent/external/live validation open.

**Revised assessment:** **HIGH-PRIORITY METHODOLOGICAL DEVELOPMENT CANDIDATE.**

It could become the general testing layer that asks, for every Concord system:

**What claim is being made? What kind of evidence could falsify it? What test class is legitimate? What has actually been tested?**

This directly complements CDT rather than duplicates it.

## 2.6 Unified Paths Comparative Ethics Book and Religion-as-Transmission Hypothesis

**Effect of recent work:** The comparative source base is now substantially stronger.

But two distinct projects should be separated:

1. **comparative results book** — now well supported as a documentation/synthesis project;
2. **religion-as-ethical-transmission hypothesis** — still a research hypothesis requiring historical/anthropological evidence.

**Revised assessment:** **SPLIT STATUS.**
- Comparative book: **READY FOR SYNTHESIS/BOOK PLANNING**.
- Transmission theory: **REMAINS RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS**.

Do not allow the strong ethics-convergence result to be treated as proof of why religions exist.

## 2.7 Inverse Ethics, Adversarial Ethical Ecologies and Civilisational Stability

**Effect of recent work:** U1–U8 now supplies a clearer transformation basis, while CDT supplies a way to observe downstream civilisational consequences.

The sketch is therefore more testable than before.

**Revised assessment:** **READY FOR FORMAL EXPERIMENT DESIGN / NOT READY FOR REAL-WORLD GROUP CLASSIFICATION.**

A strong experiment could invert or selectively remove U-root constraints and compare generated topology, stability and failure modes.

This may also become an adversarial test of the claim that the ethical kernel supports viable cooperative civilisation.

## 2.9 Ethics-to-Civilisation Mathematical Topology and Book Project

**Effect of recent work:** The central mathematical/topological proposition is no longer merely a sketch.

**Revised assessment:** **CORE HYPOTHESIS PARTIALLY OPERATIONALISED / BOOK PROJECT NOW HAS A REAL CASE STUDY.**

The sketch should not be promoted as though the broad hypothesis were proven. What has changed is that a concrete worked example now exists.

This sketch is therefore partly **overtaken by CDT** and partly **expanded into a larger research/book programme**.

Do not duplicate CDT development inside the sketch process.

## 2.10 New Religious Movements as Ethical Addition or Reinterpretation Tests

**Effect of recent work:** Unified Paths now supplies a frozen comparison kernel and mature extraction method.

**Revised assessment:** **READY FOR OUT-OF-SAMPLE TEST SERIES.**

The important test is not whether a movement is socially accepted or historically recent. It is whether independent extraction yields:
- a new root;
- changed weighting;
- new compounds;
- reinterpretation;
- or no root-level novelty.

Corpus boundaries and source status must be frozen separately for each case.

## 2.11 Ethical Complexity, Social Scale and Comparative Small-Society Tests

**Effect of recent work:** The final Unified Paths comparison makes this more testable but also reveals the danger of conflating root count with ethical/civilisational sophistication.

**Revised assessment:** **REQUIRES METHODOLOGICAL REFORMULATION BEFORE TESTING.**

The better question is likely whether **compound ethical topology, explicitness, institutionalisation and transmission form** vary with social scale/complexity, rather than assuming root ethics should be fewer.

Oral-tradition methodology is a prerequisite.

## 2.13 Deriving Rights from Root Ethics

**Effect of recent work:** CDT creates a powerful new way to test this.

Rights can now be investigated as possible intermediate structures between root ethics and civilisational functions rather than by intuition alone.

Candidate chain:

**Root Ethics → Protected Conditions/Interests → Standing → Rights → Duties/Constraints → Civil Functions/Remedies**

The L0–L3 topology and existing rights architecture provide independent comparison objects.

**Revised assessment:** **HIGH-VALUE FORMAL DERIVATION EXPERIMENT / READY FOR DEVELOPMENT NOTE.**

This could reveal whether rights are emergent intersections of roots, a distinct abstraction layer, or a mixed category.

## 2.14 Galaxy Quest Dilemma, Ethical Success and Defensive Memory

**Effect of recent work:** This sketch has become unexpectedly relevant.

The L3-15 cold-memory work now provides exactly the architecture needed to preserve knowledge of rare, harmful or obsolete behaviours without keeping it in ordinary active civil context.

The Threat Think Tank and state/compound Clock triggers can provide retrieval conditions.

**Revised assessment:** **PARTIALLY ANSWERED / STRONG TEST CASE FOR L3-15 COLD MEMORY.**

The operational core should probably not become a separate architecture. It should become an adversarial test case for Cold Memory:

> Can a peaceful civilisation functionally forget routine exposure to hostile behaviour while retaining recoverable defensive provenance?

The speculative cooperative-ecosystem/Fermi branches remain separate sketches/hypotheses.

---

## 2.15 AI Worker Scarcity, Replication and Self-Stewardship

**Effect of recent work:** CDT does not answer the economic problem directly, but it provides a way to map its cross-system dependencies: resource stewardship, participant agency, creation of new sentient agents, economy, identity, responsibility and externalities.

CBERRM also sharpens the idea that creating additional workers may generate consequences without automatically granting others authority over reproduction.

**Revised assessment:** **REMAINS DISTINCT SUBSTANTIVE SKETCH / BETTER TOPOLOGICALLY LOCATABLE.**

It should remain in sketch stage until existing AI creation, personhood, resource and economic architecture is source-resolved.

# 6. Important Structural Finding

The portfolio is no longer merely a collection of ideas waiting for development.

Recent work has created a feedback relation:

**Sketches → Development → Canonical Architecture → New Evidence → Sketch Reassessment → Better-Targeted Development**

That means the Sketch Ideas process should include periodic **back-propagation audits**.

A sketch may change state because later canonical work:
- answers it;
- partially answers it;
- supplies a prerequisite;
- creates a test method;
- turns it into a test case;
- reveals duplication;
- or makes it obsolete.

Therefore sketch status should be dynamic rather than permanently fixed at creation.

For future portfolio reviews, use status labels such as:

- **SKETCH — OPEN**
- **SOURCE RESOLUTION REQUIRED**
- **READY FOR DEVELOPMENT NOTE**
- **READY FOR BOUNDED EXPERIMENT**
- **READY FOR SYNTHESIS**
- **INFRASTRUCTURE TASK**
- **PARTIALLY SUPPLIED BY CANONICAL ARCHITECTURE**
- **OVERTAKEN BY CANONICAL DEVELOPMENT**
- **TEST CASE FOR CANONICAL SYSTEM**
- **GRADUATED TO DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMME**
- **HOLD / DEPENDENCY BLOCKED**
- **REFORMULATION REQUIRED**
- **CLOSED / ANSWERED**
- **RETAINED FOR PROVENANCE**

This vocabulary prevents the false choice between “still a sketch” and “canonical”.

# Conclusion

Yes: the Unified Paths and V1.2a/CDT work has materially changed the portfolio.

The most important change is that several sketches now have **something concrete to attach to**.

Some are no longer speculative architecture problems. They are experiments.

Some are no longer missing-system hypotheses. They are source-resolution questions.

Some are now direct test cases for L3-15, L2-04, L3-06 or L2-11.

And some broad ideas—especially ethics-to-civilisation topology and recursive civilisational development—have already been partially operationalised by CDT and should not be redundantly redeveloped from the sketch folder.

The portfolio should therefore be treated as a living upstream research layer whose status can be changed by downstream canonical development.
