# Sketch Idea — Unified Paths Comparative Ethics Book and Religion-as-Transmission Hypothesis

**Status:** SKETCH / UNTESTED / NON-CANONICAL
**Origin:** Unified Paths of Light final-comparison backlog.

## Observation
The comparative work may now support a book-length account of the extracted ethics, their sources and differences among traditions.

## Hypothesis
Religious traditions may function, among other things, as intergenerational teaching, transmission and interpretation systems for ethical rules. Traditions may differ greatly while parts of their compressed ethical cores converge.

A subsidiary observation to test is whether some traditions emphasise particular directions of ethical application—for example inward self-development versus outward social interoperability—without assuming that this exhausts the tradition.

## Development Questions
- Which claims are extraction results versus interpretive hypotheses?
- How does each tradition teach, transmit and compound ethics?
- Which differences survive root compression?
- Does historical transmission explain convergence, independent discovery, or both?
- What evidence would distinguish these explanations?

## Risks
Reducing religions to ethics alone; overgeneralising traditions; treating an extraction method as a theory of religion.
