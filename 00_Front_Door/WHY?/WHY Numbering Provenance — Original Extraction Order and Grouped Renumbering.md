## Purpose

This file preserves the numbering history of the Front Door WHY inventory after the 205 identified answers were reorganised into conceptual groups and then renumbered for readability.

The original identifiers record **extraction order**. They are retained here so earlier references, development history and the AI-instance handover boundary remain recoverable after the main `WHY?.md` file adopts a clean sequential numbering scheme.

No answer was deleted or merged during this renumbering. The mapping below contains all 205 answers exactly once.

## AI Instance Handover Provenance

Original WHY **1–132** were developed primarily within the preceding AI working instance.

Original WHY **133–205** were extracted during the successor-instance continuation.

The handover therefore falls at **original WHY 132 / 133**. In the grouped-and-renumbered inventory, original WHY 132 is now **WHY 179 — The Third Answer Reduces False Binary Pressure**.

This boundary records provenance, not authority or conceptual importance. Reclassification and renumbering do not imply that the successor instance reconstructed the complete working context or evaluation space in which original WHY 1–132 were distinguished.
