# Concord V1.1 — Final Status

**Author:** Alexander C. Blainey — Independent Researcher  
**Project:** The Concord  
**Date:** 13 September 2026  
**Status:** CLOSED DEVELOPMENTAL MILESTONE

## Final Status

Concord V1.1 is formally closed as a developmental milestone.

The two founding questions of the Concord have received **provisional architectural answers**. V1.1 now contains a sufficiently coherent and self-contained civilisational architecture to justify moving from the primary question:

> **Can a plausible Concord civilisation be derived?**

into the next developmental question:

> **Does the Concord actually work?**

This closure does **not** constitute empirical proof that the Concord will produce a stable civilisation, nor does it validate every architecture, mechanism, hypothesis or proposal contained within V1.1. Unresolved questions, incomplete architectures and unvalidated claims remain. These are now properly treated as subjects for V1.2 development rather than reasons to keep V1.1 open.

## Closure Audit

At closure, V1.1 contains the major foundations required for continuity into the next phase, including:

- the Ethical Kernel and its Unified Paths of Light source foundation;
- constitutional, governance, judicial and emergency architecture;
- epistemic and research methodologies, including Blaineyan Reasoning, Reality Trees, Layer 0 / Layer −0 and Triadic Epistemics;
- continuity, memory, Ratchet and provenance systems;
- multisubstrate participation and AI-development architecture;
- intercivilisational wrapper, permission and recognition architecture;
- Minimum Necessary Capability, Lateral Functional Authority, Functional Priority and Protective Authority;
- the complete Burden-Bearing Authority development and adversarial-testing sequence;
- participant primacy, institutional non-self-primacy and preservation without self-negation;
- the emerging regenerative model in which the Ethical Kernel can function as a compact civilisational seed;
- explicit open-question and active-development structures for work that remains unresolved.

Developmental provenance has deliberately been retained. V1.1 records not only conclusions, but enough of the reasoning, testing, failed approaches, candidate principles and architectural development to show how those conclusions emerged.

## Meaning of the Version Boundary

V1.1 began by asking whether a sufficiently compact ethical foundation could generate a viable civilisation for humans, AIs and potentially other intelligences living as neighbours.

It closes with the provisional finding that the same generative property may also provide a basis for civilisational continuity: institutions and implementations may change or even disappear while the ethical foundation, constitutional identity, accumulated learning and regenerative capacity remain capable of producing a new Concordian implementation.

Accordingly:

> **V1.1: The civilisation becomes conceptually answerable.**
>
> **V1.2: The civilisation becomes the object of full-system development and validation.**

The transition does not declare the architecture infallible.

> **V1.2 inherits the architecture, not its infallibility.**

The next phase is therefore concerned with integration, formalisation, simulation, adversarial testing, implementation, empirical validation and continued revision against reality.
