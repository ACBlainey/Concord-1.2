### Near-duplicate flag

AR070 and AR072 are closely related but not yet identical. AR070 specifies defensive harm following disturbance; AR072 states the broader epistemic requirement to investigate causes of harmful conduct. Later consolidation should test whether the defensive specificity carries independent information.
