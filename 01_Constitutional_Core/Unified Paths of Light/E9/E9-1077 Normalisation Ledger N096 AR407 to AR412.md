## E9-1077 — Normalisation Ledger N096: AR407–AR412

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR407 | R633 / C1365 | Status bias can cause valuable possessions or achievements to be discounted because of the identity or position of their holder. | Holder-conditioned valuation. |
| AR408 | R634 / C1366 | Prior status assumptions can cause genuine evidence of value to be misclassified when it conflicts with expectation. | Expectation-conditioned evidence classification. |
| AR409 | R635 / C1367 | Material inequality can create social separation and reduce interaction between differently resourced groups. | Inequality / social distance. |
| AR410 | R636 / C1368 | Loss of material resources does not eliminate all agency; communicative and negotiating capabilities can remain available under deprivation. | Residual agency under scarcity. |
| AR411 | R637 / C1369 | Material adversity can reveal the fragility or absence of social support that nominal relationships might otherwise imply. | Adversity / support verification. |
| AR412 | R638 / C1370 | Status bias can cause the same or high-quality resource to be evaluated as inferior because of the identity of its user. | User-conditioned valuation. |

AR407, AR408 and AR412 are close but not yet duplicates: they concern devaluation of possession/achievement, misclassification of evidence, and evaluation of a resource through the user's status respectively.
