## E9-1072 — Normalisation Ledger N093: AR390–AR396

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR390 | R614 / C1335 | Acute vulnerability can induce surrender of substantial autonomy in exchange for protection or subsistence. | Need-conditioned autonomy loss. |
| AR391 | R615 / C1337 | Material deprivation can suppress practical participation in public affairs even without formal exclusion. | Resource-conditioned participation. |
| AR392 | R616 / C1339 | Severe scarcity can force repurposing of resources normally reserved for other uses into emergency subsistence. | Emergency resource conversion. |
| AR393 | R617 / C1340 | Deprivation constrains practical choice and can change which resources become acceptable or necessary for survival. | Scarcity-constrained choice. |
| AR394 | R618 / C1341 | Pre-existing deprivation can amplify the practical burden of additional adverse events. | Compounding vulnerability. |
| AR395 | R619 / C1342 | Major resource loss can arrive unpredictably, creating a need for resilience before the loss occurs. | Anticipatory resilience. |
| AR396 | R620 / C1344 | Loss of material security can produce loss of practical autonomy and social status. | Material security / autonomy coupling. |

AR390 and AR396 are related but not duplicates: AR390 concerns an agent accepting autonomy loss under acute need, whereas AR396 concerns material loss itself driving a transition into subordinate status.
