# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-444 — Mechanical Ledger Block 161: Rattray 801–805

801 / C2186 = Y — if the person who goes out dancing all night avoids trouble, the person sleeping safely at home is even less exposed. Risk depends partly on exposure; lower exposure generally reduces opportunity for the corresponding hazard.

802 / C2195 = H — the proverb prefers immediate death to a postponed execution. It clearly concerns the burden of awaiting inevitable severe harm, but because the literal context is lethal and no further gloss establishes the transferable scope, it remains H.

803 / C2226 = Y — what is not wanted is not turned back for. Effort should not be spent recovering an object or option that has genuinely ceased to serve the agent's objective.

804 / C2236 = Y — asking who is wrong, the person who spread a mat across the path or the person who stepped on it, foregrounds responsibility for creating an obstruction as distinct from the later actor encountering it. Responsibility analysis should include the agent who created the hazardous or obstructive condition, not only the final actor.

805 / C2285 = Y — people who claim to abandon an old halting-place are challenged for retaining the ancient three-stone hearth unchanged. Claimed departure from tradition should be tested against continued practice; verbal rejection is incomplete when constitutive practices remain unchanged.

### AR549 — R801/C2186
Hazard exposure depends partly on participation in the conditions that create opportunity for that hazard.

### AR550 — R803/C2226
Recovery effort need not be spent on an object or option that has genuinely ceased to serve the agent's objective.

### AR551 — R804/C2236
Responsibility analysis should include agents who create hazardous or obstructive conditions, not only those who later encounter them.

### AR552 — R805/C2285
Claims of institutional or normative change should be tested against continuing constitutive practices rather than accepted from declaration alone.
