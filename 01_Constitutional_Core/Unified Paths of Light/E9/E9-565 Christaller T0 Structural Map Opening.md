# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-565 — Christaller T0 Structural Map Opening

The direct 1879 T0 witness is now treated as the structural authority for expansion.

### Confirmed bibliographic structure
The digitised volume is the 1879 Basel German Evangelical Missionary Society edition and is catalogued as xii + 152 pages.

The collection is explicitly described as alphabetically arranged and as including variations.

### Structural task
Map:
- preliminary matter;
- beginning of numbered proverb text;
- major alphabetical transitions;
- variant notation;
- final numbered proverb;
- any appendices or non-proverb material.

### Constraint
Page/image count in a digitisation is not equivalent to printed pagination.

The map will therefore preserve both scan locator and printed page where visible.
