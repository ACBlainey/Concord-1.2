# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-457 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Repair Before Destruction and Correctability

R818 gives one of the clearest repair formulations yet encountered:

A dirty cloth is washed; it is not burned.

This aligns structurally with earlier E9 occurrences:
- R732: do not break the water-pot after the first stumble;
- R741: do not burn the yam store for the smaller benefit of roasted food;
- R767: a condition bent over thirty years cannot be straightened in one.

The emerging source-internal distinction is between:
- **damage or degradation that remains correctable**, and
- conditions that would justify abandonment or replacement.

Where useful structure remains and the defect is repairable, destructive response is disproportionate.

This is recorded as an occurrence pattern only. No cross-tradition X6 comparison or root compression is performed while the E9 firewall remains closed.
