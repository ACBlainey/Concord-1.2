## E9-956 — Normalisation Ledger N016: AR049–AR051

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR049 | R131 / C3416 | An adverse condition should be causally attributed to another agent when that agent's activity is what creates the relevant threat or pressure. | Causal responsibility; distinguish cause identification from moral blame. |
| AR050 | R133 / C3418 | Persistent exclusion of a contributor from benefits generated by their productive activity can undermine willingness to continue contributing. | Contribution, benefit-sharing and participation stability. |
| AR051 | R136 / non-Christaller Rattray item | Separately claimed control over access can generate destructive conflict even when the competing agents ultimately share the same underlying resource or outcome. | Shared-resource coordination; preserve non-Christaller provenance flag. |

AR049 is deliberately phrased as causal attribution rather than automatic culpability. AR050 records a cooperation-stability mechanism without assuming a particular distributive formula.
