# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-217 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Causal Attribution, Responsibility and Affected Parties

R333/OCR-383, R334 and R337 create a coherent causal-responsibility cluster.

- C3389: a person who merely eats venison is not blamed for the gun accident that injured the hunter.
- R334: an event in one disconnected location does not injure a person elsewhere.
- R337: injury to one animal does not create pain in another.

Together these occurrences repeatedly resist transferring responsibility or harm by mere association.

The emerging occurrence-level rule is:
**identify the operative causal chain and the actually affected party before assigning responsibility or harm.**

No compression is performed.
