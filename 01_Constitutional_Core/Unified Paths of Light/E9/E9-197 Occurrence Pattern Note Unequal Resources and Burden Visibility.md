# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-197 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Unequal Resources and Burden Visibility

R305 supplies an explicit social interpretation rather than requiring inference.

All animals sweat, but hair can conceal the sweat. Rattray says the saying is used to mean that a rich or powerful person can bear losses or troubles better than a poor person, although both may equally have worries.

The occurrence therefore separates:
- existence of burden;
- visible manifestation of burden;
- capacity to absorb burden.

A low-visible-impact outcome does not establish absence of cost. Resource or power differences can change how strongly an equivalent class of trouble becomes externally visible.

This remains an Akan occurrence-level result; no cross-tradition mapping is undertaken.
