### Emerging attribution structure

The source material is increasingly distinguishing several attribution questions that should not be collapsed prematurely:
- **causal responsibility** for harm;
- **credit** for productive contribution;
- **benefit received** from an outcome;
- **formal position** within a system;
- **enabling contribution** from infrastructure or components.

This may later require a parent with multiple children rather than one undifferentiated attribution principle.
