# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-209 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Failure Reporting, Attribution and Observable Outcome

R321, R324 and R325 create a compact accountability/epistemic cluster.

- R321 explicitly concerns exaggeration after a missed shot.
- R324 keeps the hunter's name attached to elephant meat, linking result and producer.
- R325 allows the visible substitute outcome — mushrooms — to speak to the absence of expected hunting success.

These distinguish:
- self-serving narrative after failure;
- attribution of successful production;
- inference from observable outcomes.

The cluster reinforces the corpus's repeated preference for reality-responsive evaluation over unsupported assertion.
