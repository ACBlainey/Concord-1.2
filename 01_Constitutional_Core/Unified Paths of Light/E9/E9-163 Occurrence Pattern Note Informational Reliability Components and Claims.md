## E9-163 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Informational Reliability, Components and Claims

R233 and R235 reinforce a dense E9 epistemic/reliability pattern.

### Unreliable component
R233 removes a feather that lies to the organism/system it serves. The concern is not merely falsehood in isolation but false information supplied by a component relied upon by a larger whole.

### Claim versus demonstrated outcome
R235 contrasts the hawk's declared ambition to capture a person with its actual capture of a fowl.

These connect with earlier occurrences concerning hidden failures, observable capability tests, superficial performance, failed protective instruments and excessive confidence in wisdom.

The corpus is repeatedly distinguishing **assertion, information source, actual capability and observable outcome**.

No compression is attempted.
