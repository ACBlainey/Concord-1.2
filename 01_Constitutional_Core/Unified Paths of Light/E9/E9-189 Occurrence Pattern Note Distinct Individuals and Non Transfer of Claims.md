# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-189 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Distinct Individuals and Non-Transfer of Claims

R281 states:

> They have bought my mother, but they have not bought me.

At occurrence level this is a clear distinction between:
- a claim or transaction concerning one individual; and
- an attempted extension of that claim to a related but distinct individual.

The functional implication is that **relationship does not by itself collapse distinct agents into a single transferable object of claim or control**.

This is noteworthy alongside earlier E9 identity and agency occurrences, but no deeper Akan root or Concord mapping is asserted during the firewall phase.
