# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-207 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Rumour, Information and Crisis Resilience

R312–313 place information quality directly inside a crisis setting.

R312 states that war brings rumours. R313 goes further: rumours cause the town to fall.

The important occurrence-level distinction is between:
- the presence of uncertain circulating information during crisis; and
- its potential to damage collective functioning or resilience.

This connects with the already dense E9 reliability topology, but no cross-tradition comparison or root compression is performed.
