## E9-792 — NC-ID Text-Alignment Source Recovery: R91, R150

Primary local source recovery gives:

| R | source-language row recovered | printed C-ID |
|---:|---|---|
|91|`Esono kuntan na adowa di panyin.`|none|
|150|`Mmoadoma nhina foro ho, akyekyere nkoforo bi, wapon afwe.`|none|

R91 is especially important because the OCR prints its item marker as `01.` between R90 and R92. The R-ID is therefore a source-reconciled Rattray numbering repair; no C-ID is inferred from that sequence.
