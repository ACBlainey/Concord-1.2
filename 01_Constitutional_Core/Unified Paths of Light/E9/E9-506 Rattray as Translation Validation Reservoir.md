# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-506 — Rattray as Translation Validation Reservoir

The closed Rattray subset provides 830 numbered items with:
- Christaller C-ID;
- Twi as reproduced by Rattray;
- Rattray English translation;
- frequent explanatory notes.

This makes it a large internal validation reservoir.

### Use
Select validation items prospectively from Rattray, hide the English translation from the fallback translation process, translate from the Twi/source layer, then compare against the historical translation.

### Limitation
Rattray is not an infallible gold standard.

Disagreement may reflect:
- OCR damage;
- historical orthography;
- Rattray's translation choice;
- metaphor ambiguity;
- genuine semantic error in the fallback.

Therefore disputed cases should be triangulated with Lange/T3 where possible.

### Benefit
This permits translation-method testing without contaminating the unseen Christaller-only extraction.
