# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1254 — Minimum-Stability Checkpoint 002: Eight-Family Survival

### Leave-one-family-out result

All eight organising substantive families failed removal:

1. **MS-P01 — Responsible information relation** — necessary
2. **MS-P02 — Development of effective capability** — necessary
3. **MS-P03 — Protected agency under power and dependency** — necessary
4. **MS-P04 — Causal responsibility and accountable action** — necessary
5. **MS-P05 — Protection from avoidable harm and vulnerability** — necessary
6. **MS-P06 — Sustaining relationships of care and reciprocity** — necessary
7. **MS-P07 — Responsible resource relation** — necessary
8. **MS-P08 — Preservation through adaptive continuity** — necessary

In each case, reconstruction from the remaining seven plus operators/structural conditions caused identifiable substantive loss.

### Cross-family constraint result

MS-C01 did **not** survive as a ninth substantive family.

It is better represented as:

**O-C10 — Association/non-transfer boundary operator**

This operator prevents unsupported transfer of identity, property, responsibility, entitlement or control through mere association.

### Current architecture

The internally derived architecture therefore currently contains:

- **8 independently necessary substantive organising families**
- **10 operator/condition candidates (O-C01–O-C10)**
- structural-condition layer
- domain implementations and lower branches

### What this does and does not establish

This is strong evidence that the eight-family set is **locally minimal under leave-one-family-out testing**.

It does **not yet prove global minimality**. A different repartitioning of the substantive space might theoretically recover the same distinctions with fewer differently defined roots.

Therefore one further test is required before freeze:

**alternative-partition challenge** — attempt to reconstruct the 23 SP distinctions using fewer than eight newly formulated substantive parents, without simply hiding multiple independent obligations inside compound labels.
