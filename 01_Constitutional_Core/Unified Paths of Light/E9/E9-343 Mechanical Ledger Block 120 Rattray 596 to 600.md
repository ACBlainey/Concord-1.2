## E9-343 — Mechanical Ledger Block 120: Rattray 596–600

596 / C2554 = Y — wisdom is not gold dust to be tied up and stored away. Knowledge has a use/share dimension unlike a resource whose value lies merely in possession or hoarding.

597 / C2555 = Y — the clever person exchanges a tiny amount of value for a very large amount from the fool. Capability asymmetry can be used to engineer radically unequal exchange; cleverness alone does not make the resulting distribution fair.

598 / C2558 = H — the local OCR prints the item number as 698. Two equally wise people playing together are said to generate discord. Competition between peers may be intended, but the causal/general scope is too broad for secure admission.

599 / C2559 = Y — while two wise people divide a damaged yam, each exposes the concealed diseased side rather than accepting concealment. Shared competence can enable mutual detection of attempts to hide defects during allocation.

600 / C223 = Y — no one stores away a “wisdom-bag” and then asks others to explain the matter. Capability deliberately withheld from use cannot simultaneously serve as the basis for competent participation; knowledge must be brought into the problem if it is to help resolve it.

### AR374 — R596/C2554
Knowledge or wisdom has functional value through use and availability rather than mere possession or hoarding.

### AR375 — R597/C2555
Capability asymmetry can be exploited to produce radically unequal exchange; superior cleverness does not by itself establish fairness.

### AR376 — R599/C2559
Mutual competence can expose concealed defects in allocation or exchange that one party might otherwise hide.

### AR377 — R600/C223
Capability withheld from application cannot contribute to resolving the problem for which that capability is needed.
