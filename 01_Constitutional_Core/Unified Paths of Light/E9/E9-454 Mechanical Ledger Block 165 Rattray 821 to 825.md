# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-454 — Mechanical Ledger Block 165: Rattray 821–825

821 / C3239 = H — Rattray translates this as “History repeats itself,” literally “the very same ancient things are to-day.” Recurrence is clear, but no independent ethical prescription is secure without further context.

822 / C3265 = Y — the head is not a papaw fruit that can be broken open to see the thoughts inside. Internal mental states are not directly observable; claims about another agent's thoughts require inference and should not be treated as direct access.

823 / C3524 = Y — if the distant sun already burns strongly, it would scorch still more if brought near. Hazard intensity can depend on proximity; reducing distance to a harmful source can increase exposure.

824 / C3525 = H — “in the world all things are two and two.” The statement is too broad and unexplained to yield a secure transferable ethical occurrence.

825 / C239 = Y — one does not make a fowl taboo and then eat its chickens. A rule or prohibition should be applied consistently to closely dependent derivatives when consuming the derivative would defeat the purpose or substance of the original restriction.

### AR565 — R822/C3265

### AR566 — R823/C3524

### AR567 — R825/C239
