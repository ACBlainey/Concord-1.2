# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1190 — Parent-Child Compression Register 016: AR330–AR353

### Existing parents extended

**PC-P001 — Performance-grounded capability**
- AR330 — knowledge/capability can overcome an environmental barrier.
- AR345 — ignorance of a capability does not establish its absence.

**PC-P052 — Behaviour/signals as evidence**
- AR334 — concern expressed for dependents can indirectly communicate the speaker's own need.
- AR348 — stated intent is an evidence channel distinct from behavioural threat-signalling.

**PC-P062 — Dependency-constrained agency**
- AR337 — urgent need does not suspend all behavioural constraints.
- AR338 — immediate basic need can outrank deferred liability.
- AR343 — assistance can support agency while some remedial action remains irreducibly the recipient's.
- AR353 — avoiding a burdensome process can also forfeit its benefits.

**PC-P051 — Reversibility-preserving hazard management**
- M012/AR347 — hazard exposure/intensity can depend on proximity/accessibility.
- AR349 — anticipated threat can justify protective adaptation.

**PC-P055 / PC-P076 — vulnerability despite apparent strength/status**
- AR335 — basic bodily need persists across status.
- AR341 — illness vulnerability persists across status.
- AR350 — challenge can develop genuine resilience rather than merely reveal it.

### New provisional parents

**PC-P078 — Interpretive mediation of information**
- AR331 — an intermediary can shape how information is interpreted before it reaches a decision-maker.

This is an information-architecture child relevant to the forthcoming split of PC-P058.

**PC-P079 — First-person standing for embodied need**
- AR305 — intimate bodily action cannot be independent of the affected person's awareness.
- AR336 — another agent cannot literally experience one's need.

**PC-P080 — Delay can preserve harm**
- AR339 — delay during crisis can reduce remedy effectiveness.
- AR352 — avoiding a necessary task can prolong the unresolved condition.

**PC-P081 — Intervention-condition fit**
- AR344 — intervention quality depends on fit to the target condition.

**PC-P082 — Boundary integrity under approval pressure**
- AR351 — pressure for approval can induce surrender of legitimate boundaries.
