# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1292 — Clean Christaller Rerun Test Specification

### Status

Deferred replication specification. This is not part of the frozen E9 evidence.

### Primary question

Given reliable full access to Christaller's 3,600-proverb collection, does an independent full traversal reproduce, refine or materially challenge the functional architecture derived from the bounded Rattray witness?

### Required blind sequence

1. acquire and checksum the clean source;
2. serialize every proverb and variant;
3. define translation/provenance fields;
4. traverse the complete corpus;
5. extract ethical occurrences without E9 architecture access during classification;
6. normalise;
7. merge genuine duplicates;
8. perform parent-child compression;
9. separate substantive roots, operators, structural conditions and implementations;
10. minimum-stability test;
11. freeze;
12. only then compare with E9.

### Predefined comparison outcomes

**Replication:** E9 functional architecture is substantially recovered.

**Refinement:** E9 functions survive but the larger corpus adds, splits or reclassifies stable structure.

**Challenge:** one or more E9 substantive functions fail recovery, or genuinely new minimum-stable functions appear.

### Important non-test

The rerun is not required to reproduce eight roots. Reproduction of the number eight alone would be weak evidence.

The stronger question is whether the **functional topology and architectural layering** survive substantially increased coverage.
