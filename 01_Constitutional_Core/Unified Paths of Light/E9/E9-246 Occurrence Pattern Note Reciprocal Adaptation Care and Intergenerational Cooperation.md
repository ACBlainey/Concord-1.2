# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-246 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Reciprocal Adaptation, Care and Intergenerational Cooperation

R390 and R404 are complementary.

R390 describes elder and children adapting their steps to one another, after which the children carry the elder's bag.

R404 describes the old woman caring for hens while the hens materially support her in return.

Neither model reduces cooperation to one-directional dependency. Instead, differently capable participants can produce stable cooperation through reciprocal adjustment, care and contribution.

This is notable within the age/development chapter because capability difference does not automatically map to unilateral dependence.

No compression is performed.
