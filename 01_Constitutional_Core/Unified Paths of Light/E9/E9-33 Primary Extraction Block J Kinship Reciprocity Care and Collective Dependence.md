# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-33 — Primary Extraction Block J: Kinship, Reciprocity, Care and Collective Dependence

## Coverage
Relational material involving household, kin, dependency and wider association was traversed while preserving historically local role structure.

## Provisional functions observed
- reciprocal obligation;
- assistance under dependency;
- responsibility generated by relationship;
- care toward vulnerable or dependent persons;
- contribution to collective life;
- limits of isolated self-sufficiency;
- consequences of abandonment or unreliability;
- cooperation across differentiated roles;
- social support as a condition of individual capability.

## Control
Specific kinship rules are retained as local applications. They are not silently converted into universal family structures or modern welfare principles.

## Emerging pattern
Relational dependency is unusually dense in the bridge material, but density is not yet evidence of root status.
