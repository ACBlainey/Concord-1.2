# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-249 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Resilience, Reserves, Infrastructure and Recoverability

R389, R396 and R398 expose three different components of resilience.

- R389: reserve or alternative resources provide something to fall back on during trouble.
- R396: useful productive infrastructure must be protected from preventable deterioration.
- R398: experienced agents avoid positions from which they cannot extricate themselves.

These correspond respectively to:
1. reserve capacity;
2. maintenance of enabling systems;
3. preservation of recoverability.

The cluster suggests that resilience in the Akan material is not merely endurance after harm; it also includes maintaining options and preventing avoidable loss before crisis occurs.

No compression is performed.
