# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-746 — Christaller Corpus Cardinality Verification Gate

The preferred T0 witness remains J. G. Christaller, *Twi mmebusem, mpensa-ahansia mmoaano* (Basel German Evangelical Missionary Society, 1879), Library of Congress LCCN 43019087. The Library of Congress catalogue confirms xii + 152 pages, image/PDF access, and public-domain status. Google Books and University of Ghana remain secondary witness routes.

The title describes a collection of **three thousand and six hundred** proverbs, but this phrase is not treated as proof that the mechanically usable identifier domain is exactly C1–C3600.

Before final C-only arithmetic, T0 inspection must establish:
- first printed identifier;
- last printed identifier;
- gaps;
- repeated identifiers;
- suffix/sub-number forms;
- separately numbered variants or supplements;
- whether title count and identifier cardinality coincide.

Therefore expressions such as `3600 − 818` remain provisional planning arithmetic, not corpus closure arithmetic.
