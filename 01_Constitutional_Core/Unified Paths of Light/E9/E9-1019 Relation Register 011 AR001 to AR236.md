### Emerging asymmetry constraint

A recurrent structure now appears in relationships where one agent has greater capability, authority or experience. The stronger position can create:
- responsibility to preserve support after assigning a vulnerable task (AR224);
- capability-sensitive expectations rather than undifferentiated accountability (AR225);
- value in experienced mediation/counsel (AR227, AR235);
- a constraint against deliberately exposing a dependent to foreseeable harm (AR236).

This is recorded as an internal relational structure only; no parent principle is assigned.
