# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-06 — Primary Tradition Selection: Akan

## Status
**PRE-EXTRACTION SELECTION DECISION**

E9 selects the **Akan proverb tradition of Ghana** as its first independently bounded African holdout.

## Selection basis
The choice is methodological rather than outcome-driven.

Akan provides:
- a historically early large published proverb collection;
- source-language Twi material;
- recorded variants;
- an independently identifiable ethnolinguistic tradition;
- substantial later scholarship demonstrating that proverb use extends across ordinary conversation, conflict resolution, court discourse and other social contexts.

The primary historical collection is J. G. Christaller's 1879 *Twi mmebusem...*, described in library catalogues as a collection of approximately 3,600 Tshi/Twi proverbs with variations, arranged alphabetically.

## Why Akan first
Akan offers a comparatively auditable bridge between a living oral tradition and a bounded historical documentary witness. This makes it suitable for testing the E9 oral-medium adaptations without beginning from a modern ethics-themed anthology.

## Important limitation
The Christaller collection is a nineteenth-century missionary-era documentary witness. It is not treated as an unmediated transcription of timeless pre-contact Akan ethics.

Its collection context is itself part of E9 provenance.

## Scope
E9-A will derive an Akan architecture independently.

Other African traditions, including Yorùbá, remain possible later independent replications and must not be pooled with Akan before separate freeze.
