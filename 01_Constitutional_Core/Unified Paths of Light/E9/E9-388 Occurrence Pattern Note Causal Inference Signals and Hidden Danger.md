# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-388 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Causal Inference, Signals and Hidden Danger

R667, R677, R678 and R680 form an epistemic-causal cluster.

R667: a developing fire has distinctive smoke — consequences may have precursors.

R677: calm water can still kill — absence of conspicuous disturbance is not proof of safety.

R678: an unusual condition is presumed to have an underlying reason.

R680: water does not dry without a cause; Rattray explicitly glosses the phrase in causal/reason terms.

Together these warn against two opposite errors:
1. ignoring meaningful precursor or anomaly signals;
2. assuming safety merely because expected warning signals are absent.

The source therefore supports investigation that is responsive both to positive indicators and to the possibility of hidden causal structure.
