# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-179 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Support, Deterrence and Power Removal

R261–263 form a compact capability-and-power sequence.

### Support
R261 explicitly states that the cat could be more capable if it had assistance.

### Deterrence
R262 shows the mouse refraining from intrusion in the presence of the cat.

### Removal
R263 shows mice rejoicing once the cat dies.

These occurrences distinguish:
- capability amplified through support;
- power operating without active confrontation through deterrence;
- behavioural freedom changing when constraining power disappears.

R264 is deliberately not folded into this pattern as a normative proposition because its “slave” relation lacks sufficient interpretive context.
