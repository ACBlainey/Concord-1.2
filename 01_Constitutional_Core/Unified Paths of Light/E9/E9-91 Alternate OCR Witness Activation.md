# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-91 — Alternate OCR Witness Activation

The original Cornell OCR endpoint became intermittently unavailable. A second independently digitised Internet Archive witness has now been successfully opened:

**Internet Archive object:** `ashantiproverbst00rattuoft`

This is another scan/OCR of R. Sutherland Rattray, *Ashanti Proverbs*, Clarendon Press, 1916.

The alternate witness exposes the printed Rattray numbering, Twi text, English translation, Christaller cross-reference numbers and notes. It therefore satisfies the E9-90 requirement for visible source-level verification.

The first recovered window visibly contains Rattray 107–120 and establishes the continuation identifiers:
109/768, 110/278, 111/346, 112/3597, 113/1084, 114/1260, 115/611, 116/1257, 117/800, 118/1852, 119/1851, 120/1853.

No identifier is inferred from sequence.

## Witness rule
The original Cornell scan remains part of provenance, but this alternate scan may serve as the active mechanical witness. Where OCR materially affects interpretation, a second witness or page image remains required.
