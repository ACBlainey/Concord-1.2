# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-349 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Truth, Falsehood, Scrutiny and Correction

R601–604 begin an unusually explicit truth/falsehood sequence.

R601 directly opposes truth and deceit.

R602 treats falsehood as reducing the already limited supply or integrity of truth.

R603 connects lying with fear of a venue of serious adjudication, making scrutiny consequential.

R604 depicts truth eventually overtaking the liar despite the liar's apparent head start.

These yield four distinct functions:
1. truth/deception incompatibility;
2. informational contamination by fabrication;
3. accountability under scrutiny;
4. eventual correction of fabricated accounts by truthful evidence.

The cluster should be kept distinct during extraction rather than compressed prematurely into a generic “tell the truth” rule. The E9 firewall remains closed.
