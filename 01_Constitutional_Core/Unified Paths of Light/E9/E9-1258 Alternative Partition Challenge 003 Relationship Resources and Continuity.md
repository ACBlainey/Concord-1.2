# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1258 — Alternative-Partition Challenge 003: Relationship, Resources and Continuity

### Candidate reduction E
**MS-P06 Care/relationship + MS-P07 Resource relation**

Candidate replacement:
**AP-E — Responsible provision and reciprocity**

#### Failure
Resource entitlement and contribution apply outside care relations; durable relational obligation can exist without material provision. “Provision and reciprocity” merely compounds both.

**Decision: REJECT.**

### Candidate reduction F
**MS-P07 Resources + MS-P08 Continuity**

Candidate replacement:
**AP-F — Sustainable stewardship**

#### Failure
This captures the genuine bridge represented by stewardship, but loses:
- entitlement basis;
- productive contribution and differentiated claims;
- non-continuity resource sufficiency questions.

If broadened to recover those, “stewardship” ceases to have a single generative meaning.

**Decision: REJECT.**

### Candidate reduction G
**MS-P06 + MS-P07 + MS-P08 → sustaining relations**

#### Failure
The phrase hides three distinct objects:
- relations among agents;
- claims/use of resources;
- persistence of function through time.

Their children cannot be regenerated without reintroducing those distinctions as substantive premises.

**Decision: REJECT.**
