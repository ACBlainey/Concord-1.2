# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-363 — Mechanical Ledger Block 128: Rattray 636–640

636 / C1368 = Y — even lacking material resources, the poor person retains speech with which to negotiate postponement of debt. Loss of material capability does not eliminate all agency; communicative/negotiating capability can remain available.

637 / C1369 = Y — “the poor man has no friend.” Taken with R628, the source depicts material adversity as a stress test that can expose the absence or fragility of nominal social support.

638 / C1370 = Y — good tobacco used by a poor person is treated as though it were inferior residue. Status-based expectations can cause identical or high-quality goods to be evaluated differently depending on the user.

639 / C1372 = Y — repairing an old wooden bowl can make it serve the poor person as effectively as a more prestigious pewter dish. Repair and continued use can preserve functional value without requiring replacement by higher-status resources.

640 / C1373 = Y — a poor person does not choose the sleeping place. Deprivation can remove practical choice over basic living conditions.

### AR410 — R636/C1368

### AR412 — R638/C1370
Status bias can cause the same or high-quality resource to be evaluated as inferior because of the identity of its user.

### AR413 — R639/C1372

### AR414 — R640/C1373
