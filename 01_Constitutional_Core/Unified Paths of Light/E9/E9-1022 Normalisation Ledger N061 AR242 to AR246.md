| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |

| AR243 | R404 / C101 | Care and material support can be reciprocal between differently capable participants. | Cross-capability reciprocity. |
| AR244 | R405 / C102 | Voluntary assumption of a task can entail its ordinary performance expectations despite otherwise relevant status-based accommodation. | Voluntary role assumption / accountability. |
