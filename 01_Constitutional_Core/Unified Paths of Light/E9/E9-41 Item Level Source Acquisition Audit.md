## E9-41 — Item-Level Source Acquisition Audit

## Purpose
Attempt the source access required by E9-38 before generating any mechanical ledger entries.

## Sources located
Three independent public records expose the 1916 Rattray work:
- Open Library / Internet Archive records identify downloadable PDF and plain-text representations and the Internet Archive identifier `ashantiproverbs00chrigoog`;
- University of Ghana UGSpace exposes a 61.31 MB PDF of the 1916 volume;
- Google Books identifies a full-view 1916 edition and confirms the 190-page structure.

These establish that the source exists digitally and is public-domain historical material.

## Access result in the present research environment
Bibliographic pages and structural metadata are accessible, but the full item-level payload required for systematic reconstruction could not be retrieved through the current web interface. The UGSpace PDF download endpoint timed out, and the Internet Archive plain-text payload was not exposed through the available retrieval interface.

## Consequence
The source has been located but not yet ingested at item level.

No AR item records are created from search snippets.
