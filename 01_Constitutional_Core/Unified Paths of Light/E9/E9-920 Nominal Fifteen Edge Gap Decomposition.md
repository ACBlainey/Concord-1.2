## E9-920 — Nominal Fifteen-Edge Gap Decomposition

The Pass-001 parser gap is **15 edges** (820 expected minus 805 recovered).

The known exceptional register does not automatically equal those fifteen edges because some OCR-normalisable repairs may already have been captured by the widened parser. Therefore adding all exceptional relations to 805 would risk double-counting.

The gap must be resolved by **set difference**, not by arithmetic addition of exception counts.
