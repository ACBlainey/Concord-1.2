### Emerging power-information tension

The governance occurrences now expose a structurally interesting tension:
- high office can receive broad information from many channels (AR252–AR253);
- power asymmetry can simultaneously suppress candid direct speech (AR248);
- governance quality depends partly on adviser quality (AR250);
- status does not independently validate competence or testimony (AR230, AR245).

Thus **information volume, information diversity and information candour are not equivalent**. This distinction is preserved for later architecture testing.
