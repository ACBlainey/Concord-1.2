# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1208 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Register 003: Truth, Verification and Correction

### Higher internally derived parents

**HC-P007 — Representation should remain answerable to reality**

Subsumes the truth-integrity portions of:
- PC-P017;
- PC-P032 commitment-conduct integrity where declared standard and conduct diverge;
- the AR378/379/385 truth-contamination structure;
- PC-P057 where claimed normative change must be checked against continuing constitutive practice.

Core structure:
> Claims, representations and declared states should remain answerable to the conditions they purport to describe; fabrication, contradiction and merely declared change do not make the represented condition true.

**HC-P008 — Claims require accessible and independent verification where stakes demand it**

Subsumes:
- verification portions of PC-P017;
- AR187/230/382/516/545-type independent-evaluation structure;
- PC-P101 where controlled exploration/probing generates evidence;
- PC-P060 where absence of evidence must not be overread.

Core structure:
> Confidence should increase through evidence that can be independently examined or generated; blocked access, sole self-certification and weak absence-signals reduce justified confidence.

**HC-P009 — Judgment should remain correctable over time**

Subsumes:
- PC-P002 where experience corrects mistaken assessment;
- PC-P080 where delay preserves unresolved error/harm;
- AR381/384/560 correction dynamics;
- PC-P075 temporal sampling bias.

Core structure:
> Beliefs and evaluations should remain revisable as evidence develops, because early framing, delayed correction and incomplete temporal sampling can preserve error.

### Boundary

HC-P007, HC-P008 and HC-P009 are **siblings**, not one parent:
- truthfulness concerns correspondence;
- verification concerns epistemic warrant;
- correctability concerns temporal revision.

Collapsing them would erase a recurring three-way distinction in the corpus.
