## E9-831 — Overlap-Cardinality Duplicate Audit Reopened

Following the successful Rattray yield-arithmetic audit, the frozen **818 unique C-ID** overlap cardinality was subjected to the same backward-integrity test.

E9-739 derived 818 from 819 R-items with printed C-IDs, subtracting two known duplicate relations and adding one for R369's one-to-many relation. E9-739 itself explicitly left the count subject to later discovery of additional duplicate relations.

A direct duplicate probe of the primary local Rattray witness found additional repeated printed C-IDs not included in that arithmetic. The 818 figure is therefore reopened for correction rather than silently retained.
