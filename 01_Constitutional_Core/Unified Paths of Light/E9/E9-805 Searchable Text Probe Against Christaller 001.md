## E9-805 — Searchable-Text Probe Against Christaller 001

Targeted web searches were run for likely item-level Christaller text and numbering patterns within the 1879 work.

### Result

### Disposition
No C-ID/Twi row is admitted from this probe. The Google route improves acquisition possibilities but has not yet crossed the item-evidence threshold.
