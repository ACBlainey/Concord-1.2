# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-564 — Christaller Corpus-Size Arithmetic Caution

The title describes the source as a collection of “three thousand and six hundred” proverbs.

That phrase establishes the published corpus description but does not by itself prove that the mechanically usable identifier universe is exactly the integers 1–3600.

### Before C-only arithmetic
Verify:
- first and final printed identifiers;
- whether variants receive independent numbers;
- whether numbering contains gaps;
- whether supplements/appendices use nonstandard identifiers;
- whether the printed total is rounded/title-level language or exact item arithmetic.

### Rule
Do not generate a synthetic 1–3600 inventory until T0 numbering has been mechanically inspected.
