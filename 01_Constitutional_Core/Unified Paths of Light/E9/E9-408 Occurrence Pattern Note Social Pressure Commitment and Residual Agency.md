# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-408 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Social Pressure, Commitment and Residual Agency

R718 gives a clear constraint on reputational pressure: a person should not undertake an unwanted marriage simply to avoid being called shy.

This connects with earlier E9 occurrences in which:
- deprivation constrains practical choice;
- hierarchy constrains participation;
- fear suppresses candid speech;
- status changes how actions are interpreted.

R718 adds another mechanism: **social ridicule can pressure choice without physically eliminating it**.

The occurrence therefore distinguishes formal ability to choose from the quality of the choice environment. An agent can retain nominal agency while facing social costs designed to push the agent toward a major commitment.

No broader marriage doctrine is inferred from this single proverb.
