# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-268 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Prestige, Material Capacity and Practical Constraint

R429, R434 and R437 repeatedly separate inherited status from material capability.

R429 shows a chief's grandson preserving a status convention despite poverty.

R434 says noble birth does not generate the same outward fame as wealth.

R437 states most sharply that an ancient name cannot be cooked and eaten.

The cluster indicates that **symbolic or inherited status and practical material capacity are distinct variables**. Prestige may structure behaviour and recognition without supplying the resources needed for material needs.

Only the securely transferable elements are admitted as Y occurrences; R429 remains H because its normative direction is unclear.
