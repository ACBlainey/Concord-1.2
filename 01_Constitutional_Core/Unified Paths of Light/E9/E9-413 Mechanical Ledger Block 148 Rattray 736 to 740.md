# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-413 — Mechanical Ledger Block 148: Rattray 736–740

736 / C193 = H — one does not see a person by day and then light a lamp at night to inspect the person's face. Redundant verification is plausible, but the source does not securely establish whether the target is wasted effort, mistrust, recognition, or another mechanism.

737 / C204 = Y — one does not enter another village and tell its chief that village's own origin/history. Local or situated knowledge should not be presumptively displaced by an outsider claiming superior authority over the community's own history.

738 / C205 = Y — one does not enter another's village and call oneself “Agyeman,” glossed as “father of a nation.” Entry into another community does not itself confer supreme local status or authority; authority cannot simply be self-assigned.

739 / C207 = Y — the OCR misprints the Rattray number as 730. A person begging a meal is not the one to serve out the soup. A beneficiary/requester does not automatically acquire control over allocation of the resource being provided.

740 / C208 = Y — if no one had gone and returned, people could not know whether the road was safe. Some uncertainties require exploratory exposure and return of information; collective knowledge can depend on experience generated by agents who test unknown conditions.

### AR498 — R738/C205

### AR499 — R739/C207
