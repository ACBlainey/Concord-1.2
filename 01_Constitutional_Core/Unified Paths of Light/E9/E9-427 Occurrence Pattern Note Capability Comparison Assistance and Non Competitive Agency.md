# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-427 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Capability Comparison, Assistance and Non-Competitive Agency

R750–752 form a compact sequence about capability without requiring domination or status defence.

R750: offered carrying can be accepted rather than insisting on walking unaided.

R751: if another claims greater speed, the way can be left open.

R752: the person who exceeds you is also exceeded by someone else.

Together these distinguish agency from compulsory self-sufficiency and capability from universal rank.

An agent can:
- accept assistance;
- allow another's superior domain-specific capability to operate;
- recognise that comparative superiority is relational rather than absolute.

This is a useful counterpoint to status-based capability distortions elsewhere in E9. Capability differences need not automatically become humiliation, domination or exclusion.
