# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1226 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Register 018: Continuity, Resilience and Succession

### Higher internally derived parents

**HC-P041 — Continuity requires preservation of enabling conditions across time**

Subsumes:
- PC-P029 continuity aspects;
- PC-P074 specialist capability as continuity infrastructure;
- PC-P090 continuity-reserve aspects;
- M014 intergenerational transmission;
- AR271/307/571.

Core structure:
> Continued function depends on preserving resources, knowledge, capabilities and transmission pathways needed by future states of the system.

**HC-P042 — Resilience requires alternatives, buffers and recoverable pathways**

Subsumes:
- PC-P053 — resilience through alternatives;
- PC-P051 buffering aspects;
- AR233/395/449/456;
- PC-P077 where bottlenecks expose fragile transition points.

Core structure:
> Systems become more resilient when critical functions have alternatives, protective buffers, accessible support and reduced dependence on a single fragile pathway.

**HC-P043 — Succession should be prepared before critical absence or component loss**

Subsumes:
- PC-P100 — continuity planning before uncertain absence;
- AR429/483/523;
- AR524 delayed failure after sustaining-source loss.

Core structure:
> Where continuity depends on agents, roles or resources that may become unavailable, transfer or succession should be prepared before loss makes replacement ineffective.

**HC-P044 — Stability claims require exposure to relevant stress conditions**

Subsumes:
- PC-P055 robustness portions;
- AR350/459/530;
- adversity-test portions of PC-P068.

Core structure:
> Present stability or apparent strength should not be treated as robust until performance has been observed under conditions capable of revealing relevant failure modes.

### Bridge

HC-P044 bridges systems, capability assessment and epistemic verification.
