### Classification coverage

All **HC-P001–HC-P053** now have a primary architectural classification.

Provisional primary classes:

- **23 substantive-orientation candidates**
- **9 operator/condition candidates** already formalised as O-C01–O-C09
- **10 structural-condition candidates** already formalised as S-C01–S-C10
- multiple explicit domain implementations/mechanisms
- several secondary bridge roles

These classes overlap through secondary roles; they are architectural classifications, not arithmetic partitions intended to sum mechanically to 53.

### Major methodological result

The Akan corpus has generated a **layered ethical architecture** rather than a flat list.

At minimum the internal structure now distinguishes:

1. substantive orientations — what is protected, required or sustained;
2. operators — how judgments/actions should be evaluated;
3. structural conditions — architectures that enable or distort outcomes;
4. implementations — domain-specific expressions of more general structures.

### Why this matters

A flat compression could falsely inflate the root count by counting verification, timing, scarcity, resilience and succession alongside agency, protection and care as if all occupied the same logical level.

Architecture separation prevents that category error.

**Upward compression:**  
Can two or more SP candidates be replaced by a deeper substantive parent without meaningful ethical information loss?

Only compressions that pass both tests should survive.
