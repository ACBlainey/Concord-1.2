### Quality finding
The exercise confirms that OCR corruption is common enough that a clean normalised Twi layer should not be fabricated automatically.

The validation pilot therefore needs both:
- raw-source field;
- optional normalised field whose changes are auditable.

This becomes a useful additional control for the later Christaller T0 workflow.
