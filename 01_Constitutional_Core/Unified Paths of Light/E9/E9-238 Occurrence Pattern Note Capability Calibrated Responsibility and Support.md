# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-238 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Capability-Calibrated Responsibility and Support

R366, R376 and R377 create a coherent responsibility structure around developmental capability.

- R366 explicitly identifies a small child carrying responsibility beyond the child's years as a condition deserving pity.
- R376 says that after sending a child aloft, one does not remove the ladder.
- R377 says that after assigning a difficult errand, one does not become angry when the child performs poorly.

Together they distinguish responsible task assignment from mere delegation. The assigning agent must consider:
1. whether the responsibility exceeds developmental capability;
2. whether necessary support remains available;
3. whether performance expectations are proportionate to difficulty and capability.

This is a strong occurrence-level developmental architecture. No deepest-root compression is attempted.
