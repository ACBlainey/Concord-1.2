### Emerging need-response topology

Need is no longer represented simply as a state that triggers aid. The corpus distinguishes:
- direct versus indirect signalling of need;
- universal bodily need versus first-person experience;
- urgency among competing obligations;
- timeliness of remedy;
- secondary burdens caused by the condition;
- assistance versus actions the affected agent must still perform;
- suitability of the intervention;
- time required for expert selection.

Later compression should preserve these functional differences unless a higher-order formulation can recover them without loss.
