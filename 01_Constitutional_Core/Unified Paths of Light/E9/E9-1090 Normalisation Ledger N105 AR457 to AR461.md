## E9-1090 — Normalisation Ledger N105: AR457–AR461

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR457 | R689 / C3093 | Resource sufficiency is function-relative: inadequacy for one purpose does not establish inadequacy for another. | Function-relative sufficiency. |
| AR458 | R691 / C3097 | Effective power or confidence can depend on favourable environmental conditions rather than intrinsic capability alone. | Environment-enabled capability. |
| AR459 | R693 / C3053 | Persistent external pressure does not necessarily displace a sufficiently stable or resilient system. | Stability under pressure. |
| AR460 | R694 / C3055 | Adverse impact should not be understated through euphemistic description when accurate description is available. | Accurate harm description. |
| AR461 | R695 / C3056 | A responsibility structure is defective when mutually incompatible outcomes both trigger blame, making exoneration impossible regardless of performance. | Impossible accountability / scapegoating. |

AR461 closely neighbours AR289's impossible social-evaluation structure, but here the mechanism is specifically accountability for outcomes rather than general social approval.
