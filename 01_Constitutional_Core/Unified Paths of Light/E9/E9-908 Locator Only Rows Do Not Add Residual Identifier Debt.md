# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-908 — Locator-Only Rows Do Not Add Residual Identifier Debt

The same checkpoints record several damaged R-number markers with clear C-IDs, including R323, R333 and later R491/C2068, R551/C1691, R564/C3125 and R574/C432.

These rows require R-locator reconstruction but **not C-ID reconstruction**. Their identifiers are already part of the machine-readable numeric population and should not be counted again as residual C-ID edges.
