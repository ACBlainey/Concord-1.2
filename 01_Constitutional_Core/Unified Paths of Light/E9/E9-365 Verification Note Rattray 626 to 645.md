### Explicit source glosses
R626: Rattray explains the “power” of poverty through desperation in a person with nothing to lose.

R628: Rattray supplies “(a friend)” after “who is a man,” supporting the relationship-testing interpretation.

R629: the translation explicitly states that the sole worker tires quickly but the household does not eat if she does not work.

R644: Rattray gives a practical note on palm-wine tree felling and why the tree is ordinarily handled differently.

### Method caution
R627's equation of poverty with madness is not adopted as a psychological fact. It remains H.

The slavery/dependency language in R629 is historical source content. The extracted occurrence concerns single-worker dependency and exhaustion, not endorsement of slavery.
