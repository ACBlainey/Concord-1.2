# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1231 — Higher-Parent Bridge Audit 001: Capability, Epistemic and Context

### Purpose

Begin bridge/compound audit across HC-P001–HC-P053 without reopening external comparison.

### Bridge findings

**HC-P001 ↔ HC-P005 ↔ HC-P008 — BRIDGE, not duplicate**

Capability assessment is a domain application of reality-responsive and verifiable judgment, but capability adds function-specific performance content.  
Decision: **retain HC-P001 as domain implementation; HC-P005/008 remain operators.**

**HC-P003 ↔ HC-P048 — PARTIAL SUBSUMPTION**

HC-P003 says sufficiency/value are function-relative. HC-P048 generalises the requirement to specify agent, population, function, domain or comparison class.  
Decision: **HC-P003 becomes a capability/resource-domain implementation of HC-P048**, while its fit/sufficiency content remains recoverable.

**HC-P004 ↔ HC-P009 — BRIDGE**

Developmental correction and epistemic correctability share revision structure but differ in object: capability versus judgment.  
Decision: retain both; flag **correctability** as a cross-domain operator candidate.

**HC-P006 ↔ HC-P050 — BRIDGE**

Observational limits and situated access jointly constrain inference. Situatedness can explain why evidence access differs, but not all observational limits are positional.  
Decision: retain both.

**HC-P010 ↔ HC-P048/049 — BRIDGE**

Recipient-sensitive communication is context/reference sensitive, but adds a distinct communicative objective: intelligibility.  
Decision: retain as domain implementation.

### Architecture signal

This audit identifies a recurrent pattern:

> a general operator can have domain-specific implementations that should remain traceable without being counted as independent roots.
