# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-362 — Mechanical Ledger Block 127: Rattray 631–635

631 / C1361 = Y — a proverb made by a poor person does not spread. Material/social status can determine whether knowledge receives circulation independently of the content's intrinsic quality.

632 / C1362 = Y — a very small expenditure by a poor person is treated as equivalent to a much larger consumption. The practical significance of the same nominal resource expenditure depends on the agent's available resource base.

633 / C1365 = Y — a poor person wearing a silk robe is compared to a tree stump wearing it. Status bias can cause observers to discount or ridicule valuable goods merely because of who possesses them.

634 / C1366 = Y — gold worn by a poor person is called brass. Prior assumptions about status can cause observers to misclassify genuine value or evidence when it conflicts with expectation.

635 / C1367 = Y — poor and rich do not play together. Material inequality can produce social separation and reduce interaction across economic strata.

### AR405 — R631/C1361
Social or material status can affect whether knowledge circulates independently of the knowledge's intrinsic content.

### AR406 — R632/C1362
The practical burden of a fixed expenditure depends on the agent's underlying resource capacity.

### AR407 — R633/C1365
Status bias can cause valuable possessions or achievements to be discounted because of the identity or position of their holder.

### AR408 — R634/C1366
Prior status assumptions can cause genuine evidence of value to be misclassified when it conflicts with expectation.

### AR409 — R635/C1367
Material inequality can create social separation and reduce interaction between differently resourced groups.
