## E9-1179 — Parent-Child Compression Register 007: AR133–AR152

### Existing parents extended

**PC-P001 — Performance-grounded capability assessment**
- AR133 — grossly implausible capability claims need not be accepted solely by assertion.
- AR139 — capability is object/context-sensitive.
- AR147 — absence of some resources or advantages does not erase distinct capabilities.
- AR152 — visible equipment/outward form does not establish the actual source of capability.

**PC-P012 — Property-responsive classification and treatment**
- AR136 — apparent evidence should be checked against underlying reality.
- AR151 — attractive appearance is insufficient for judgment or imitation.

These propositions strengthen a general appearance/reality branch without erasing their specific applications.

**PC-P010 — Capability-sensitive support**
- AR140 — outcomes beyond unaided capacity warrant consideration of assistance/intervention.
- AR142 — support can increase realised capability.

AR140 is evidential; AR142 is causal. They are **SIBLINGS**.

**PC-P024 — Timing-sensitive obligation/evaluation**
- AR149 — future condition should not be represented as already present.
This is a **BRIDGE** to epistemic temporal accuracy rather than a straightforward child of obligation timing.

### New provisional local parents

**PC-P032 — Commitment-conduct integrity**
- AR134 — explicit moral commitment does not establish compliant conduct; behaviour can contradict the declared standard.

**PC-P033 — Incentive-sensitive disposition**
- AR141 — disposition can be intensified by incentive, responsibility or urgency.

**PC-P034 — Non-instructed and practice-based development**
- **M002 (AR145+AR146)** — competencies/dispositions can develop without explicit instruction.
- AR174 later supplies a demanding-practice development mechanism.

**PC-P035 — Dependent-position coupling**
- AR148 — a dependent's position can be determined by caregiver position/movement.

**PC-P036 — Agency preservation under irreversible loss**
- AR150 — effective choice depends on continued agency; irreversible loss of agency removes later choice.
