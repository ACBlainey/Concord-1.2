# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-256 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Authority, Collective Basis and Advisory Constraint

R411 and R416 create an important governance pairing.

R411 states that when a chief compels a person regarding wrongdoing, the authority comes from the people.

R416 states that a king with good councillors has a peaceful reign.

Together these sayings do not portray governing capability as residing solely inside the ruler. Its operation depends upon:
1. a collective basis of authority; and
2. the quality of surrounding advisers.

This is an internally derived E9 occurrence pattern. It should not yet be equated with any modern constitutional model, and no X-kernel comparison is performed.
