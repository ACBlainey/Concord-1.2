# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-07 — Akan Primary Corpus Boundary Freeze

## Primary extraction corpus
The primary E9-A source is frozen as:

**J. G. Christaller (1879), _Twi mmebusem, mpensa-ahansia mmoaano: A collection of three thousand and six hundred Tshi proverbs..._**

The historical edition contains Twi/Tshi proverb material and recorded variations and is publicly digitised by the Library of Congress.

## Extraction boundary
Primary extraction will traverse the proverb collection systematically rather than select entries by ethical topic.

Prefatory/editorial material may be used for source and linguistic context but does not enter the ethical occurrence ledger as Akan ethical evidence.

## Primary-evidence rule
An occurrence enters the primary ledger only where the source proverb itself supports an ethically interpretable behavioural, relational, epistemic, developmental, material, collective or power-related function.

Collector commentary does not become an ethical occurrence merely because it interprets the proverb.

## Variants
Variants recorded with a proverb remain linked to their proverb family unless they materially alter ethical function.

## Secondary sources
Later Akan ethics scholarship may be used only for:
- translation/context audit;
- interpretation challenge;
- provenance clarification;
- checking whether a reading is culturally plausible.

It may not add missing primary occurrences or rescue a predicted root before the Akan architecture is frozen.

## Exclusions from the primary corpus
The following are not part of the primary derivation:
- modern ethics summaries;
- contemporary proverb selections assembled by ethical theme;
- philosophical reconstructions;
- cross-African proverb compilations;
- non-Akan traditions;
- X1–X7 and all prior E-series architectures.

## Boundary status
The primary corpus is now frozen before ethical extraction.
