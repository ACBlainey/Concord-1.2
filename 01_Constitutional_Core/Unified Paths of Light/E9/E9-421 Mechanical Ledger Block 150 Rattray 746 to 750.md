# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-421 — Mechanical Ledger Block 150: Rattray 746–750

746 / C272 = Y — no one knows the story of tomorrow's dawn. Future conditions are uncertain; claims or plans should not presume knowledge of events that have not yet occurred.

747 / C317 = H — the OCR prints the Rattray number as “74=1.” The proverb says no one wishes well for another. Rattray substantially qualifies the literal wording, arguing that it does not necessarily imply wishing evil but rather prioritising one's own welfare/luck. Because the proverb and editorial interpretation pull in different directions, no secure transferable ethical occurrence is admitted.

748 / C360 = H — one does not spit on the ground and then lick it up. Irreversibility, repudiation, contamination, or consistency are plausible abstractions, but the source gives no gloss sufficient to fix the intended mechanism.

749 / C390 = Y — one does not wait until reaching the Volta before washing one's cloth. Necessary action should not be deferred merely because an ideal or especially large future opportunity exists when adequate action is available earlier.

750 / C408 = Y — when someone offers to carry you, one does not insist “I shall walk.” Offered assistance can appropriately substitute for unnecessary self-burden; autonomy does not require rejecting beneficial voluntary support.

### AR505 — R746/C272
Future conditions are uncertain; decisions should not presume knowledge of events that have not yet occurred.

### AR506 — R749/C390
Necessary action should not be deferred solely for an ideal future opportunity when an adequate opportunity exists earlier.

### AR507 — R750/C408
Accepting beneficial voluntary assistance can reduce unnecessary burden without negating agency.
