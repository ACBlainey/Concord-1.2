# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-364 — Mechanical Ledger Block 129: Rattray 641–645

641 / C1374 = Y — a poor person's complaint is investigated only briefly. Economic status can influence the procedural attention given to a claimant, reducing equality of adjudicative consideration.

642 / C1375 = H — the poor person's elephant tusk is the wart-hog's tooth. The saying clearly contrasts resource scale/prestige under poverty, but the intended transferable function is not sufficiently determined without further gloss.

643 / C1378 = Y — when a poor person wears a necklace of soft decorative leaves, observers call it a sheep's halter. Status bias can transform interpretation of the same act or adornment into ridicule/devaluation.

644 / C146 = Y — even poverty does not cause a person to fell a palm tree standing in a swamp. Severe need does not eliminate all practical constraints: some resource-extraction choices remain sufficiently unproductive or unsuitable to reject.

645 / C147 = Y — no one can extort from another by using the target's poverty as a threat. Vulnerability itself does not create legitimate entitlement for another agent to coerce or extract from the vulnerable person.

### AR415 — R641/C1374
Economic status can bias procedural attention, causing materially disadvantaged claimants to receive less thorough consideration.

### AR416 — R643/C1378
Status bias can cause an otherwise meaningful possession or act to be reinterpreted as degrading or ridiculous because of the actor's poverty.

### AR417 — R644/C146
Severe need does not erase every practical constraint; some resource-extraction choices remain too unsuitable or unproductive to justify.

### AR418 — R645/C147
Another agent's material vulnerability does not create legitimate entitlement to coerce or extract from that agent.
