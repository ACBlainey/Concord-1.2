# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1286 — Natural-Law Hypothesis Reassessment After Akan

### Hypothesis

A working Unified Paths hypothesis proposes that some ethical constraints may recur because they solve persistent problems of agency, cooperation, vulnerability, information, resources, development and continuity rather than because one tradition copied another.

### Evidence strengthened by E9

E9 adds:
- a different medium: oral/proverbial rather than primarily systematic scripture/philosophy;
- a different compression outcome: eight minimum-stable substantive families;
- complete X functional recovery: 7 R / 0 A / 0 Ø;
- reverse functional representation: 8/8 Akan families represented within X topology;
- independent emergence of operator, structural-condition and implementation layers.

These observations are consistent with recurrent functional constraint.

### What E9 does not establish

E9 does not prove:
- metaphysical natural law;
- biological genetic encoding of ethics;
- historical isolation of Akan traditions;
- causal evolutionary origin;
- universality across all human cultures;
- necessity for every possible intelligent system.

### Updated evidential statement

The evidence now supports a stronger but still bounded formulation:

> Across multiple culturally and textually different derivations, ethical compression repeatedly recovers a common functional region concerning harm, truth/reality, agency/power, care, resources, accountability/correction and development, while partition boundaries and culturally salient branches vary.

The natural-law hypothesis remains **supported as a live explanatory hypothesis**, not established as fact.
