# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-356 — Provenance and Mediation Note: Rattray 614 and 620 — Poverty to Slavery

R614 and R620 make the relation between deprivation and loss of autonomy unusually explicit.

### R614
The proverb states that “I am in want, so look after me” is how some people became slaves. This closely parallels the earlier R461 material in which severe vulnerability could lead an agent to accept dependency for protection.

### R620
The proverb states directly that poverty causes a free person to become a slave.

### E9 treatment
Neither occurrence is treated as evidence that enslavement is ethically legitimate or that consent under severe deprivation is fully voluntary.

The admitted structure is instead:

**material vulnerability can narrow available options so severely that survival or protection is obtained at the price of major autonomy loss.**

This is especially important methodologically because apparent agreement to a dependency arrangement cannot be interpreted without considering the constraint imposed by unmet basic needs.

The historical institution remains descriptive source context. The ethical occurrence concerns vulnerability, constrained choice and autonomy loss.
