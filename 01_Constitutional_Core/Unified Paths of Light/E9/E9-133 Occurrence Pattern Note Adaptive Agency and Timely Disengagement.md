# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-133 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Adaptive Agency and Timely Disengagement

R182 and R185 form a useful contrast in response to adverse conditions.

### Adaptive continuation
R182 preserves agency by changing the method when the existing route is frustrated.

### Adaptive exit
R185 presents the inverse requirement: when continued association will carry the agent into another's destructive outcome, failure to leave becomes the danger.

The combined occurrence-level structure is not simply persistence. Appropriate agency may require either:
- changing means while continuing toward an objective; or
- disengaging from an association whose continuation creates avoidable harm.

This complements R155, where withdrawal from danger need not be shameful, and R156, where both haste and deliberation can be appropriate according to circumstance.

No root is asserted.
