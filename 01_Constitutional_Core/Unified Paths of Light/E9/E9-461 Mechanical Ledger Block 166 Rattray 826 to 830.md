# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-461 — Mechanical Ledger Block 166: Rattray 826–830

826 / C240 = Y — no one makes a vulture taboo and then eats its eggs. A prohibition should be applied consistently to directly dependent derivatives when consuming the derivative would defeat the substance of the restriction.

827 / C1030 = H — unfamiliar food absent from one's parents' hearth is made taboo. The source clearly links unfamiliarity and prohibition, but whether the transferable mechanism is caution toward novelty, inherited practice, identity boundary, or something else is not fixed securely enough for Y.

828 / C2172 = Y — “what strength makes taboo is hunger.” Material necessity can constrain or override preferences and avoidance rules; effective choice depends partly on whether basic needs leave viable alternatives.

829 / C339 = Y — no one knowingly proceeds to the place where they expect to be killed. Where a severe hazard is credibly anticipated and avoidable, agents normally have reason to avoid deliberate exposure.

830 / C3238 = Y — “ancient things remain in the ears,” glossed “Tradition survives.” Information and norms can persist through transmission across time; continuity can be carried in retained collective memory.

### AR570 — R829/C339
