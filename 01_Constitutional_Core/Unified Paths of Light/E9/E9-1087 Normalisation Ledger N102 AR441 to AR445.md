# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1087 — Normalisation Ledger N102: AR441–AR445

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR441 | R672 / C1251 | Exposure to an uncontrolled hazard is partly positional; agents nearest the hazard can bear disproportionate consequences. | Proximity-conditioned exposure. |
| AR442 | R673 / C1252 | Components whose interaction creates catastrophic risk should not be maintained in dangerous proximity. | Hazardous interaction / separation. |
| AR443 | R674 / C2350 | A resource's eventual exhaustion or disposal does not erase the legitimate value it provided earlier in its lifecycle. | Lifecycle value. |
| AR444 | R676 / C3068 | A safety or acceptability judgment should be applied consistently across uses when its underlying reason remains relevant. | Reason-consistent evaluation. |
| AR445 | R677 / C3069 | Absence of conspicuous warning signals does not establish absence of serious danger. | Latent hazard / weak signalling. |

AR441–AR442 distinguish exposure to an existing hazard from the design problem of placing mutually dangerous components together.
