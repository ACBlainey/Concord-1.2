## E9-202 — Mechanical Ledger Block 063: Rattray 311–315

311 / C50 = Y — a man is made at the forefront of battle, not by remaining at home. Capability or status can require direct participation in demanding experience rather than passive distance.

312 / C1600 = H — when war comes, rumours come. Association is explicit but the source gives insufficient guidance on the intended transferable function.

313 / OCR identifier visible as **(IGOl)** = Y — when war comes, rumours cause the fall of the town. Information disorder can materially damage collective resilience. The Christaller identifier is retained exactly as OCR-visible and is not silently normalised.

314 / C1589 = H — pressing forward in battle is said to produce victory. This is a tactical wartime maxim; no broader ethical function is securely established.

315 / C1590 = H — Rattray explicitly interprets the saying as “war is war and not to be waged in kid gloves.” The source also records a historical torture practice involving black ants. This is preserved as historical evidence but not converted into an ethical prescription.
