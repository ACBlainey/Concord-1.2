# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-893 — Residual Collision Criterion

A residual C-ID is marked DUPLICATE only if its reconciled complete identifier equals an identifier already present in the 800-ID majority or an earlier residual relation.

Substring matches, OCR fragments and explanatory note numbers are not collisions.

This preserves the false-positive controls established in E9-843 and E9-865.
