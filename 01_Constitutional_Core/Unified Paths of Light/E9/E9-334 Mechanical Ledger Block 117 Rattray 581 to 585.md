## E9-334 — Mechanical Ledger Block 117: Rattray 581–585

581 / C3176 = Y — even hatred of one's mother does not justify handing her to the enemy. Strong negative feeling does not automatically erase protective obligations arising from a durable relationship.

582 / C3673 = H — a friend's taking the larger share is answered by the expectation that one should do likewise or appear afraid. Reciprocity and power signalling are visible, but the saying can equally reproduce competitive unfairness; the ethical direction is ambiguous.

583 / C3674 = Y — some friends are greater friends than others. Relationship labels do not imply uniform depth, reliability or obligation; actual relational strength can vary within the same nominal category.

584 / C3675 = Y — a secret introduced through friendship spreads as it is retold. Trust-based disclosure can propagate information beyond the original relationship; confidential information is vulnerable to serial retransmission.

585 / C314 = Y — a friend removes something lodged in one's eye. Trusted close relations can provide assistance with vulnerabilities that are difficult for the affected agent to remedy alone.
