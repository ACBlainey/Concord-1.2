# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-308 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Developmental Readiness, Capability and Future Standing

R509, R522 and R524 form a developmental cluster.

R509 distinguishes an existing social status from developmental readiness for sexual activity.

R522 says the archer was not born already holding the bow: specialised capability develops after birth.

R524 says no one fixes a day to give birth to a future elder/person of importance: later standing cannot be known or scheduled from birth.

Together these support a narrow occurrence-level structure:

**present status, mature capability and future standing are distinct variables.**

The cluster does not justify the historical institution surrounding R509. It records only the developmental distinction explicitly present in the source.
