# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-138 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Reciprocity, Harm and Social Support

R190 and R192 together provide a particularly clear reciprocity structure.

R190 states the condition: the biting fly has no one to aid it in trouble.

R192 supplies the cause: because it intends to bite everyone, it has no helper.

This gives a compact source-supported relation:
**indiscriminate harm → erosion of reciprocal relationships → absence of assistance during later vulnerability.**

This can be compared later with other E9 occurrences concerning hospitality, sharing, collective action, exclusion and support, but no root compression is performed here.
