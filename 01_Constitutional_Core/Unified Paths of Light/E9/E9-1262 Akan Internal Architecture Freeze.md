# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1262 — Akan Internal Architecture Freeze

### Freeze declaration

The E9-A Akan architecture is frozen **before external ethical comparison**.

The following identifiers, names and definitions may not be retroactively altered to improve agreement with X1–X7, E3–E8, Blainey's Laws or any other external architecture. Later work may annotate, challenge or supersede this freeze only through an explicit post-freeze revision record.

### Frozen substantive families

**AK-R1 — Responsible information relation**  
Agents should maintain information relations that remain answerable to reality, intelligible for relevant recipients and bounded where legitimate interests require controlled disclosure.  
Children: SP02, SP03, SP04.

**AK-R2 — Development of effective capability**  
Capability should be capable of development, learning and correction through appropriate experience, support, sequencing and enabling conditions.  
Child: SP01.

**AK-R3 — Protected agency under power and dependency**  
Practical agency and standing should be preserved where dependency, vulnerability, authority, status or control can narrow viable choice or erase first-person participation.  
Children: SP05, SP06, SP07, SP08, SP09.

**AK-R4 — Causal responsibility and accountable action**  
Responsibility should track relevant agency, participation and causal contribution rather than mere association, status or diffuse attribution.  
Child: SP10.

**AK-R5 — Protection from avoidable harm and vulnerability**  
Foreseeable serious harm should be prevented where reasonably possible, protection should respond to vulnerability, and avoidable exposure should not be displaced onto vulnerable or uninvolved parties.  
Children: SP11, SP12, SP13.

**AK-R6 — Sustaining relationships of care and reciprocity**  
Relationships can generate differentiated obligations of support, care and reciprocity that respond to proximity, capability, need, asymmetry and relational durability.  
Children: SP14, SP15, SP16, SP17.

**AK-R7 — Responsible resource relation**  
Resource use and claims should track actual function, sufficiency, justified entitlement and relevant productive contribution rather than desire, possession, association or magnitude alone.  
Children: SP19, SP20, SP21.

**AK-R8 — Preservation through adaptive continuity**  
Enabling conditions for continued function should be preserved across time while forms and mechanisms adapt where changed conditions require it.  
Children: SP22, SP23.

### Frozen status

These eight are **minimum-stable substantive families under the declared E9 internal method**. This is a methodological minimum, not a proof that every possible ontology must contain eight labels.
