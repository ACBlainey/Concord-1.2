# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-374 — Mechanical Ledger Block 133: Rattray 661–665

661 / C2953 = Y — there is no shame in working for money. Productive labour undertaken to secure material resources is not inherently degrading.

662 / C2954 = Y — the translation says that when a sick man is drunk, he is merely said to be unwell; the Twi heading appears to contain osikafo (“rich person”), creating possible OCR/translation tension. The secure functional reading is status-sensitive euphemism: behaviour may be redescribed more leniently for a socially advantaged person. Twi uncertainty is preserved as translation debt.

663 / C2955 = Y — a wealthy person may wear an old cloth. Secure status/resources can reduce the need for outward signalling; visible simplicity does not reliably imply underlying deprivation.

664 / C2957 = Y — a rich person is never “sneezed at unluckily.” Rattray's note describes a custom in which a subject sneezing before a chief may be credited or blamed for subsequent luck. The proverb-level occurrence is status bias in attribution: the wealthy are represented as protected from negative interpretation.

665 / C2931 = H — gold close to you becomes pale/no longer glitters. Familiarity, proximity or possession may reduce perceived value, but the source supplies no gloss sufficient to fix the intended mechanism securely.

### AR432 — R661/C2953
Productive labour undertaken to secure material resources is not inherently degrading.

### AR433 — R662/C2954
Social advantage can produce more lenient descriptions of behaviour that might be judged more negatively in others.

### AR434 — R663/C2955
Visible simplicity or low-status presentation does not reliably indicate lack of underlying resources or status.

### AR435 — R664/C2957
High status can bias attribution by insulating an agent from negative interpretations applied to others.
