# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-346 — Provenance and Material Culture Note: Rattray 591 Gold Weights

R591 is followed by a substantial material-culture note that should be preserved without allowing its length to distort the ethical classification.

Rattray describes:
- light balances used for weighing gold dust;
- metal “Ashanti weights” cast by the lost-wax process;
- figurative and symbolic designs;
- named weight units and approximate English-money equivalents;
- several apparent currency bases;
- possible historical derivation of some weight names from seeds.

The proverb itself is narrower: the person squandering gold dust blames the scales.

E9 therefore extracts AR369 as a responsibility/attribution occurrence while recording the weighing system separately as source context.

This distinction matters because the scales are not merely metaphorical in the source: Rattray documents an actual measurement infrastructure. The proverb's force depends on blaming that external instrument for a loss attributed by the saying to the agent's own conduct.
