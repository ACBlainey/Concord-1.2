# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1233 — Higher-Parent Bridge Audit 003: Resources, Systems and Continuity

### Bridge findings

**HC-P032 ↔ HC-P041 — GENERALISATION RELATION**

Stewardship preserves sustaining/resource conditions; continuity preserves resources, knowledge, capability and transmission pathways more generally.  
Decision: **HC-P032 is a resource-domain implementation of HC-P041**, while stewardship remains a useful domain label.

**HC-P037 ↔ HC-P047 — CONFIRMED SUBSUMPTION**

Resource repair is a domain implementation of general recoverability-based intervention.  
Decision remains **HC-P037 → HC-P047**.

**HC-P038 ↔ HC-P002 — BRIDGE**

System dependency architecture explains some enabling conditions for realised capability, but HC-P038 also concerns propagation and component criticality independent of an individual agent.  
Decision: retain both.

**HC-P039 ↔ HC-P029 — BRIDGE**

Coordinated plurality and support networks both involve multiple agents/components. Network support is relational; coordinated plurality is functional architecture.  
Decision: retain both.

**HC-P040 ↔ HC-P033 — BRIDGE**

Scarcity burden can propagate through dependencies, but emergent system outcomes extend beyond scarcity.  
Decision: retain both.

**HC-P042 ↔ HC-P053 — BRIDGE**

Alternatives and buffers create resilience partly by preserving options against irreversible loss.  
Decision: retain both; irreversibility remains an operator/condition candidate.

**HC-P043 ↔ HC-P041 — CHILD**

Succession planning is a specific continuity mechanism.  
Decision: **HC-P043 becomes a child/domain mechanism of HC-P041**.

**HC-P045 ↔ HC-P041 — CHILD**

Maintenance is another continuity mechanism.  
Decision: **HC-P045 becomes a child of HC-P041**, while its timing-specific content remains linked to HC-P051.

### Result

Continuity is emerging as a broad substantive/structural orientation with stewardship, succession and maintenance as implementations/mechanisms rather than independent roots.
