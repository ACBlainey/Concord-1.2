# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-23 — Primary Extraction Block A: Source Context, Supreme-Being and Cosmological Material

## Coverage
Opening Rattray material and the first thematic stratum were traversed under the non-forcing rule.

## Method observation
A substantial part of the material is cosmological, theological, explanatory or proverbial imagery rather than independently generalisable ethical prescription.

This confirms the need to separate worldview from ethical function.

## Provisional functional occurrence families observed
Without assigning root status, admissible material supports propositions concerning:
- limits of individual control;
- deliberative humility under uncertainty;
- recognition of dependence beyond isolated individual agency;
- caution against assuming complete knowledge or power;
- consequences of conduct represented through wider order.

## Exclusions
Statements were not admitted merely because they invoke deity, fate, spiritual agency or cosmological causation.

Where the ethical proposition cannot be separated from the cosmological claim, the item remains context-bound or non-yielding.

## Early method result
The source can be coded without translating cosmology directly into ethics.
