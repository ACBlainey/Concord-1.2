## E9-744 — C-Only Candidate Decision Rule

For each mechanically visible Christaller T0 item:

- **R-OVERLAP**: C-ID is in E9-RX-1.0. Do not count as a new C-only occurrence.
- **NCID-MATCH**: source text is independently aligned to one of the ten Rattray no-printed-ID items. Preserve the match and exclude it from genuinely new C-only material.
- **C-ONLY-CANDIDATE**: C-ID is absent from E9-RX-1.0 and no controlled NC-ID match exists.
- **T0-HOLD**: identifier/text/variant relation is too uncertain to decide.

`C-ONLY-CANDIDATE` is a corpus-status label only. It does **not** authorise ethical Y/Z/H/X classification.
