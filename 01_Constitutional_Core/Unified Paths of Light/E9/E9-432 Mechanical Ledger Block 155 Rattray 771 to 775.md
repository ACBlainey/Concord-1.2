# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-432 — Mechanical Ledger Block 155: Rattray 771–775

771 / C1020 = H — the OCR prints the number as “771>”. A tree that “hates” a person falls on them. Rattray explicitly discusses retrospective attribution of human-like intention to an inanimate event. The material is epistemically interesting but the ethical mechanism is not sufficiently secure.

772 / C1083 = Y — four things are not compared with forty. Comparisons should respect material differences in scale; treating grossly unequal quantities as equivalent produces misleading evaluation.

773 / C1121 = Y — your house is your own house. The source gives a direct boundary of possession/domain: another agent's domain is not interchangeable with one's own.

774 / C1136 = Y — a private matter is not like an old cloth spread to dry in the marketplace. Private information should not automatically be exposed to public view; some matters carry a legitimate confidentiality boundary.

775 / C1145 = Y — to climb a tree, one begins from the bottom, not the top. Processes with dependency order should begin from foundational stages rather than attempting to enter at an unsupported endpoint.

### AR526 — R772/C1083
Valid comparison must respect material differences in scale rather than treating grossly unequal quantities as equivalent.

### AR527 — R773/C1121
Personal or household domains have boundaries and are not automatically interchangeable with another agent's domain.

### AR528 — R774/C1136
Private matters can carry legitimate confidentiality boundaries and should not automatically be exposed publicly.

### AR529 — R775/C1145
Processes with dependency order should begin from foundational stages rather than unsupported endpoints.
