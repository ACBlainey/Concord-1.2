# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-354 — Mechanical Ledger Block 125: Rattray 621–625

621 / C1345 = Y — poverty compels very early labour for palm-nut cutting. Material need can constrain control over one's time and force work at burdensome times.

622 / C1346 = Y — “poverty is stupidity,” which Rattray glosses as a poor person being reckoned a fool. Material status can bias social judgments of intelligence or competence independently of actual capability.

623 / C1347 = Y — the proverb attributes European commercial presence in Africa to poverty in Europe. Whatever the historical accuracy of the causal claim, the admissible occurrence is narrower: material need can motivate migration, trade and outward economic activity across regions.

624 / C1348 = Y — poverty is not peculiar to one place. Material vulnerability is represented as geographically non-exclusive rather than a property of one people or locality.

625 / C1351 = Y — want causes a small bird to alight on a particular plant. The metaphor continues the chapter's repeated structure that deprivation can drive behaviour or choices that would not otherwise occur.

### AR397 — R621/C1345
Material need can reduce control over time and compel labour under burdensome schedules.

### AR398 — R622/C1346
Material status can bias judgments of intelligence or competence independently of actual capability.

### AR399 — R623/C1347
Material need can motivate migration, trade or outward economic activity beyond an agent's place of origin.

### AR400 — R624/C1348
Material vulnerability is not inherently confined to a particular place or population.

### AR401 — R625/C1351
Deprivation can drive behaviour or resource choices that would not occur under less constrained conditions.
