# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-765 — LoC Search-Indexed Page Visibility

The Library of Congress search layer independently indexes individual image pages of the Christaller object, including image 12, and the collection view reports **172 results**.

### Result
This confirms that individual canvases/pages exist as separately addressable digital resources even though direct page-byte retrieval has been restricted in the current execution path.

This narrows the blocker to retrieval mechanics rather than absent digitisation.
