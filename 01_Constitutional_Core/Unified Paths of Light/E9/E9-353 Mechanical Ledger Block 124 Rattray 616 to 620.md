# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-353 — Mechanical Ledger Block 124: Rattray 616–620

616 / C1339 = Y — in scarcity, goat or sheep skins are cut up, boiled and eaten. Severe scarcity can convert resources normally used for other purposes into emergency subsistence resources.

617 / C1340 = Y — the poverty sequence depicts even poor-quality maize effectively becoming leaf-food; Rattray notes that many leaves are eaten in times of great scarcity. Deprivation progressively constrains available choice and changes what counts as an acceptable resource.

618 / C1341 = Y — under poverty, even cold water is experienced metaphorically as scalding. Pre-existing deprivation can amplify the burden of an additional adverse event.

619 / C1342 = Y — poverty does not announce the day on which it will arrive. Major resource loss can be unpredictable, creating vulnerability even before deprivation is present.

620 / C1344 = Y — poverty causes the free person to become a slave. Loss of material security can translate directly into loss of autonomy and social/legal status.

### AR395 — R619/C1342

### AR396 — R620/C1344
