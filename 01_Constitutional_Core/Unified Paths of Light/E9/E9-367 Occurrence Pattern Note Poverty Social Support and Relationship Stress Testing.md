# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-367 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Poverty, Social Support and Relationship Stress Testing

R628 and R637 appear superficially contradictory:

- R628 says poverty reveals who is truly a friend.
- R637 says the poor person has no friend.

At occurrence level they can be preserved without forced reconciliation.

Together they show that adversity changes the evidential quality of relationships. Nominal friendship during ordinary conditions does not establish that support will survive material disadvantage.

R628 treats crisis as a test that can reveal genuine support.

R637 records the opposite outcome: poverty can expose social abandonment.

The common functional structure is therefore:

**relationship reliability is better evidenced by behaviour under vulnerability than by the relationship label alone.**

This complements earlier E9 material distinguishing nominal friendship from actual relational strength.
