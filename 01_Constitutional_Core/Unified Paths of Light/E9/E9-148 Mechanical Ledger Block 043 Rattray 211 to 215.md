# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-148 — Mechanical Ledger Block 043: Rattray 211–215

211 / C1669 = Y — when drunk, the cock forgets the hawk. Impairment can suppress awareness of persistent external danger without removing the danger itself.

212 / C1673 = Y — the cock reasons that if only enemies remained, its mistimed crowing would have led to death; the attached note states that a cock crowing far before dawn is killed. Survival following a punishable error implies the presence of at least some non-hostile or restraining relation in the surrounding social field.

213 / C1671 = Y — the cock is told to stop being puffed up with pride because its origin was only an eggshell. Present status or development does not erase humble/dependent origin and should not justify inflated self-importance.

214 / C151 = Y — an owner's identifying beads are not taken from one fowl and transferred to a partridge. Identity markers associated with one entity should not be reassigned to a different entity as though they established the same identity.

215 / C1651 (OCR renders 1G51) = Y — although a fowl lacks ears and therefore cannot receive an ear-boxing, it receives its beating by another means. Absence of one mechanism for imposing a consequence does not eliminate the possibility of functionally equivalent consequences through another route.

### AR100 — Rattray 211 / Christaller 1669
**Minimal function:** Impairment of awareness can cause an agent to forget or discount a continuing threat without changing the threat's actual existence.
**Confidence:** high.

### AR101 — Rattray 212 / Christaller 1673
**Minimal function:** Survival after conduct that hostile actors would punish can itself indicate the presence of non-hostile, protective or restraining relations in the surrounding environment.
**Confidence:** moderate-high.

### AR102 — Rattray 213 / Christaller 1671
**Minimal function:** Increased status or development should not erase awareness of dependent or modest origins in ways that produce unjustified pride.
**Confidence:** high.

### AR103 — Rattray 214 / Christaller 151
**Minimal function:** A marker used to identify one entity should not be transferred to another and treated as though the marker alone establishes identity.
**Confidence:** high.

### AR104 — Rattray 215 / Christaller 1651
**Minimal function:** The absence of one specific mechanism for imposing a consequence does not imply immunity from functionally equivalent consequences delivered by another mechanism.
**Confidence:** high.
