# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-242 — Mechanical Ledger Block 079: Rattray 391–395

391 / C2609 = H — speaking face to face with an elder is difficult. A power/status asymmetry affecting communication is visible, but the source does not establish whether it is endorsed, criticised or merely described.

392 / C2610 = Y — words from an old man are said to be better than an amulet. Experienced counsel is represented as practically valuable relative to supernatural protection.

393 / C2613 = H — the axe-head is knocked from its shaft on the elder's head. The surrounding note is largely lexical/archaeological; no secure transferable ethical function is supplied.

394 / C2617 = H — the old man's diminished fatness is said to have gone to the children. Intergenerational transfer/sacrifice may be suggested, but the source does not explicitly secure the intended function.

395 / C2618 = Y — an elder does not heat a stone and put it in a child's hand. Greater age/status carries a clear constraint against deliberately exposing a vulnerable child to foreseeable injury.

### AR235 — R392/C2610
Experienced human counsel can be practically more valuable than reliance on a purported protective charm.

### AR236 — R395/C2618
A more capable or authoritative agent should not deliberately expose a vulnerable dependent to a foreseeable harmful condition.
