# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-04 — Corpus Boundary Selection Criteria and Historical-Layer Policy

## Status
**BOUNDARY-SELECTION RULES FROZEN BEFORE ETHICAL EXTRACTION**

## Admission criteria for an E9 tradition
A candidate tradition must have enough source material to support:
- identifiable cultural/linguistic provenance;
- reproducible source citation;
- broad rather than ethics-targeted sampling;
- reasonable translation control;
- sufficient scale for internal compression;
- an auditable distinction between source occurrence and later interpretation.

## Source hierarchy
Preference order:
1. broad original-language collections with translations and provenance;
2. documented field collections with contextual interpretation;
3. established scholarly editions of oral corpora;
4. secondary philosophical analysis for interpretation/audit only.

Modern thematic selections constructed specifically around ethics, leadership, Ubuntu, cooperation or other expected domains must not define the primary extraction corpus because selection has already filtered the evidence.

## Historical-layer policy
E9 will not claim that a recorded proverb or oral item has a specific ancient date merely because it is described as traditional.

Sources are classified by **attested record and transmission context**, not assumed age.

Christian, Islamic, colonial and post-colonial contact are not grounds for retrospective deletion unless a predeclared source rule independently excludes the material.

Where influence is suspected but unresolved, flag it rather than rescue the experiment by exclusion.

## Medium plurality
Proverbs, narratives, songs, ritual speech and divinatory/literary oral corpora must not be treated as interchangeable evidence merely because all are oral.

Each source form requires a declared extraction unit and contextual rule.

## Completeness
For a large collection, systematic full traversal is preferred where practicable. Where full traversal is impossible, the sampling rule must be fixed before content coding and must not select by expected ethical theme.
