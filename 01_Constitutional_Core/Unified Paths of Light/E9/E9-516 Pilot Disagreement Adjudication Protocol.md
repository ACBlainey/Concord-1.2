# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-516 — Pilot Disagreement Adjudication Protocol

Candidate-versus-Rattray disagreement must be classified before it is called a model error.

### D1 — clear fallback error
Rattray and source structure support the reference reading.

### D2 — OCR/orthography problem
The candidate may be responding to corrupted source text.

### D3 — legitimate translation alternative
Both renderings preserve the material semantic structure.

### D4 — Rattray interpretive expansion
Rattray's English adds an interpretation not strictly required by the Twi.

### D5 — unresolved linguistic dispute
Independent T1/T3 or expert review is required.

### Ethical consequence
Only D1 counts automatically as a material fallback failure.

D2 and D5 create translation debt.

D3 does not fail if ethical structure is preserved.

D4 must not force the fallback to imitate Rattray's interpretive expansion.

This prevents historical translation authority from becoming circular validation.
