# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-788 — Translation Pilot Acceptance Decision Rule

After all 50 scorable items are independently generated and unblinded:

- **≥90% materially clean:** controlled T4 may activate for C-only work, still with item-level translation debt and triangulation;
- **<90% materially clean:** T4 does not become the primary C-only translation route; strengthen lexical/reviewer support or obtain T1/T3 coverage;
- **too many OCR/source failures to score:** repair source rows and rerun affected items independently;
- **contaminated run:** exclude it and repeat in a clean context.

Passing the pilot does not make T4 equivalent to Lange T1.
