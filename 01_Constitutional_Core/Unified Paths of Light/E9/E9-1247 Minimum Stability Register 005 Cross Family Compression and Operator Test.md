# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1247 — Minimum-Stability Register 005: Cross-Family Compression and Operator Test

### Current organising parents

- MS-P01 Responsible information relation
- MS-P02 Development of effective capability
- MS-P03 Protected agency under power/dependency
- MS-P04 Causal responsibility/accountable action
- MS-P05 Protection from avoidable harm/vulnerability
- MS-P06 Sustaining relationships of care/reciprocity
- MS-P07 Responsible resource relation
- MS-P08 Preservation through adaptive continuity

SP18 relational non-transfer remains a cross-family constraint not yet absorbed.

### Cross-family merger tests

**MS-P03 Agency + MS-P05 Protection — REJECT.**  
Protection can sometimes constrain immediate choice; agency can be violated without conventional harm. Both are needed.

**MS-P05 Protection + MS-P06 Care — REJECT.**  
Care can arise without threat/harm, while protection can be owed outside durable relationship.

**MS-P06 Care + MS-P07 Resources — REJECT.**  
Care often distributes resources, but entitlement, contribution and functional allocation extend beyond care relations.

**MS-P07 Resources + MS-P08 Continuity — REJECT.**  
Stewardship bridges them, but claims/entitlement do not reduce to continuity.

**MS-P01 Information + MS-P04 Responsibility — REJECT.**  
Accountability requires evidence but is not itself an information relation.

**MS-P02 Development + MS-P08 Continuity — REJECT.**  
Development concerns increasing/correcting capability; continuity concerns persistence of enabling function across time.

### Operator test

The nine O-C operators remain capable of modifying multiple MS families without becoming their substantive content. This supports keeping the operator layer separate.

### Boundary test: SP18

SP18 is required to prevent relational obligation, association or collective membership from erasing person/claim boundaries. It applies across MS-P03, MS-P04, MS-P06 and MS-P07.

Provisional classification:
**MS-C01 — Non-transfer across association**
A cross-family substantive constraint.
