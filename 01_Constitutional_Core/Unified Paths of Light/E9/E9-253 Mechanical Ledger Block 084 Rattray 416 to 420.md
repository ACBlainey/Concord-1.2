# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-253 — Mechanical Ledger Block 084: Rattray 416–420

416 / C1310 = Y — when a king has good councillors, the reign is peaceful. Quality of governance depends partly on the quality of advisers surrounding the officeholder.

417 / C1311 = Y — so long as the chief leaves a person alone, the person calls the chief a friend. Absence of direct adverse exercise of power can be mistaken for genuine friendship or alignment.

418 / C1312 = Y — the chief is said to hear everything going on. High office can receive unusually broad informational exposure.

419 / C1313 = Y — the chief's ears are compared with a strainer having more than a thousand routes into it. Governance information can arrive through many independent channels rather than a single reporting path.

420 / C1314 = Y — falling into a chief's oath does not leave a person unhurt. Formal power-bound processes can impose serious consequences on those drawn into them.

### AR250 — R416/C1310
Governance quality can depend materially on the competence and quality of advisers surrounding a decision-maker.

### AR251 — R417/C1311
Mere absence of adverse intervention by a powerful actor should not automatically be equated with genuine friendship or alignment.

### AR252 — R418/C1312
High office can create unusually broad informational exposure to events within the governed environment.

### AR253 — R419/C1313
Governance information may arrive through numerous independent channels rather than a single reporting route.

### AR254 — R420/C1314
Entry into a formal power-bound process can expose an agent to serious consequences.
