# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-177 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Boundary, Capability and Entitlement

R249 is unusually important because Rattray explicitly records that the proverb is often used in land disputes.

The long-billed bird is physically capable of reaching across the river, yet feeds on its own side. This distinguishes:
- **capacity to access**, from
- **entitlement to access**.

The occurrence therefore supplies source-grounded evidence that mere technical or physical capability does not itself settle legitimate resource boundaries.

This is particularly relevant to the wider E9 occurrence topology, but the cross-tradition firewall remains closed and no mapping to Concord principles or X-roots is performed.
