### Emerging effective-agency distinction

The current corpus increasingly separates **formal willingness or nominal capability** from **effective agency**. Realised action can depend on:
- opportunity and access (AR327, AR333);
- knowledge of local rules and conditions (AR320, AR322, AR324);
- traversable interfaces and barriers (AR325–AR326);
- developed capability (AR316, AR330);
- interpretive mediation (AR331).

Later architecture testing should therefore avoid treating choice as a purely internal preference variable.
