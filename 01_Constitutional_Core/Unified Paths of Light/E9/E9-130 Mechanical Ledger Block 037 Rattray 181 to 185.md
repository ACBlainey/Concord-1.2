## E9-130 — Mechanical Ledger Block 037: Rattray 181–185

181 / C1112 = H — shame leads Father Spider to wear an antelope-skin hat while asking others to assist with hoeing. Rattray says the meaning is somewhat obscure and offers only a possible interpretation involving concealment of a blemish. Ethical abstraction therefore remains unresolved.

182 / C1237 = Y — after interference with one chosen means, the speaker changes method and asks what the opponent can do then; Rattray explicitly glosses this as finding some way to get the better of the opponent. Adaptive strategy can preserve agency when one route is obstructed.

183 / C359 = Y — no one tells stories to Ntikuma because, as the spider's child, he is presumed already to know them; Rattray explicitly gives the application: “I know all about that, tell me something I do not know.” Communication should account for the recipient's existing knowledge rather than redundantly supplying what is already known.

184 / C2570 = Y — Rattray explicitly interprets the fly's gesture as: much has already been done for another, but further favour may still come. Prior support does not imply that future support is exhausted.

185 / C2571 = Y — a fly that fails to leave a dead body is buried with it. Failure to disengage from a doomed or harmful association can cause the associated agent to share its consequences.

### AR081 — Rattray 182 / Christaller 1237
**Minimal function:** When one route of action is obstructed, an agent may preserve effective agency by changing strategy rather than simply repeating the blocked approach.
**Confidence:** high.

### AR082 — Rattray 183 / Christaller 359
**Minimal function:** Useful communication should be responsive to the recipient's existing knowledge and avoid treating already-known information as new instruction.
**Confidence:** very high because Rattray supplies the explicit application.

### AR083 — Rattray 184 / Christaller 2570
**Minimal function:** Substantial assistance already given does not necessarily exhaust the possibility of future assistance.
**Confidence:** very high because Rattray supplies the explicit interpretation.

### AR084 — Rattray 185 / Christaller 2571
**Minimal function:** Continued attachment to a failing or dangerous association can expose an agent to consequences that could have been avoided by timely disengagement.
**Confidence:** high.
