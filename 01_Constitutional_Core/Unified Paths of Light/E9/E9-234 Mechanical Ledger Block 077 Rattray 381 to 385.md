# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-234 — Mechanical Ledger Block 077: Rattray 381–385

381 / C2611 = H — “grows at the elbow” is explicitly explained as accumulating wealth/savings. The source is descriptive and does not independently establish an ethical function.

382 / C382 = H — one does not pluck a bird and then show it to an elder to ask its name. The saying may concern destroying diagnostic evidence before seeking expertise, but the source does not explicitly secure that reading.

383 / C2597 = Y — if an elder pursues every small offence for punishment, the nation will be ruined. Governance and correction require proportionality and selective restraint rather than maximal punishment of every minor violation.

384 / C2598 = Y — an elder gives no heed to idle rumours. Responsible judgment should not be driven by unverified low-quality information.

385 / C2596 = Y — Rattray explicitly says the proverb means “an old man's tale”: extravagant claims of inaccessible travel are treated as lies rather than accepted merely because an elder states them. Status or age does not make implausible testimony self-validating.

### AR228 — R383/C2597
A governance system that pursues punishment for every minor offence can become destructive; corrective enforcement requires proportionality and restraint.

### AR229 — R384/C2598
Responsible judgment should resist idle or unverified rumours.

### AR230 — R385/C2596
Age or social status does not make an implausible factual claim self-validating; testimony remains answerable to credibility.
