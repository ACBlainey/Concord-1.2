# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-487 — Christaller Expansion Sampling Rule Freeze

Before observing the ethical results of the unprocessed remainder, E9 freezes the initial expansion sampling rule.

### Initial mechanical unit
Use **fixed 20-item Christaller blocks** in Christaller printed order.

### At each 100 newly eligible C-only items
Produce a checkpoint recording:
- translation coverage;
- Y/Z/H/X;
- new AR occurrences;
- genuinely new functional patterns;
- reinforcement-only occurrences;
- unresolved translation debt;
- overlap/variant observations.

### No early saturation stop
The first 500 C-only items must not be halted merely because apparent functional novelty declines.

After 500 C-only items, a formal saturation review may assess whether the sampling design should continue unchanged, expand, or move to a stratified coverage method.

### Rationale
Freezing the rule before seeing expansion outcomes prevents a favourable convergence result from determining the stopping rule retrospectively.
