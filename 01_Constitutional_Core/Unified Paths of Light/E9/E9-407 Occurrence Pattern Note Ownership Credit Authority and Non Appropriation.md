# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-407 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Ownership, Credit, Authority and Non-Appropriation

R709, R713 and R723 form a coherent boundary cluster.

R709: abundance on another person's plantation does not create permission to loot it.

R713: one should not boast of property belonging to another.

R723: a chief should not allow others to use the chief's prestige as leverage to extort and rob.

The protected object differs:
- material resources;
- credit/status;
- authority/reputation.

But the recurring structure is similar: **another agent's possession, achievement or standing is not automatically available for appropriation or exploitation by third parties.**

This expands the E9 ownership material beyond physical property into attributional and institutional domains.
