## E9-183 — Mechanical Ledger Block 056: Rattray 276–280

276 / C1232 = Y — the ewe wants offspring like the leopard after seeing its beautiful skin; Rattray explicitly says the saying means one should not be guided by appearances, because the ewe fails to investigate the leopard's ferocious nature.

277 / C1060 = Y — a ram's bravery comes from its heart, not its horns. Outward equipment or appearance should not be mistaken for the internal source of a capability.

278 / C94 = H — a goat attempting to become a sheep retains a black patch. Persistent identity/characteristic despite attempted transformation is suggested, but no explicit ethical interpretation is supplied.

279 / C95 = Y — the goat says no one willingly walks to death; Rattray notes goats often have to be carried to slaughter. Agents can resist participation in processes they recognise as leading to their own destruction.

280 / C97 = H — where there is much blood, there is food. The relation is explicit but ethically underdetermined without additional context.
