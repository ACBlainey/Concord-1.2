# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1212 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Register 006: Agency, Dependency and Effective Choice

### Higher internally derived parents

**HC-P013 — Agency requires practically viable choice**

Subsumes:
- PC-P006 — effective agency under option constraints;
- PC-P036 — agency preservation under irreversible loss;
- PC-P062 — dependency-constrained agency;
- PC-P089 — material vulnerability reducing practical autonomy;
- AR327/414/569 effective-choice structures.

Core structure:
> Agency is not exhausted by nominal permission: meaningful choice depends on viable alternatives, access, material conditions and preservation of the capacity to choose.

**HC-P014 — Dependency changes bargaining and control relations**

Subsumes:
- M005 need-conditioned autonomy surrender;
- PC-P062 dependency/control mechanisms;
- PC-P063 treatment affecting cooperation under dependency;
- PC-P066 protective affiliation under predation;
- dependency portions of PC-P093.

Core structure:
> Dependency can alter bargaining power, contestability and willingness to accept control, while treatment and protective affiliation affect how dependency relations function.

**HC-P015 — Agency can persist under constraint and should not be erased by assistance**

Subsumes:
- AR343/410/507-type residual-agency propositions;
- assistance-without-erasure portions of PC-P010;
- PC-P079 where embodied need has irreducible first-person standing.

Core structure:
> Constraint, vulnerability or receipt of assistance does not automatically eliminate the affected agent's residual agency or first-person role in actions concerning them.

### Retained bridges

- PC-P070 consensus pressure — **BRIDGE** to collective power.
- PC-P099 reputational pressure — **BRIDGE** to social power.
- PC-P082 approval pressure and boundary surrender — **BRIDGE** to coercion.
- HC-P011 information boundaries — **BRIDGE** because information control can protect or restrict agency.
