# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-338 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Relationship Persistence, Differentiation and Assistance

R581, R583 and R585 produce three complementary relationship functions.

R581 shows relational obligation surviving strong negative feeling: hatred of one's mother does not justify surrendering her to an enemy.

R583 distinguishes nominal friendship from degree of friendship: some friends are closer or stronger friends than others.

R585 depicts a friend assisting with a vulnerability — something in the eye — that is difficult for the affected person to address alone.

The resulting occurrence-level structure is:
- relationships can carry obligations that persist through emotional conflict;
- relationship strength varies rather than being binary;
- stronger/trusted relations can supply capability where the individual is temporarily constrained.

This pattern remains internal to E9 pending later compression.
