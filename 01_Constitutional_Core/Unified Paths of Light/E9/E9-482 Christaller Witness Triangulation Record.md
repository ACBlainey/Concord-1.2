# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-482 — Christaller Witness Triangulation Record

The 1879 Christaller work has been independently located in several digital catalogues.

### Library of Congress
Direct image/PDF witness, xii + 152 pages, LCCN 43019087.

### Google Books
A full-view 1879 edition is indexed under Christaller's title. Search results expose both a Columbia-derived 152-page record and a Harvard-derived digitisation whose digital pagination differs.

### University of Ghana UGSpace
UGSpace indexes *Twi Mmebusem*, author J. G. Christaller, date 1879, and exposes a PDF file.

### Consequence
Multiple independent repositories corroborate the identity and availability of the 1879 work.

However, pagination differences between digitised presentations mean that E9 should use:
- Christaller printed item identifiers as the primary alignment key;
- printed-page location as secondary provenance;
- repository image/page index only as witness-specific navigation.

Digital page number must not be mistaken for Christaller item number.
