# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-224 — Mechanical Ledger Block 073: Rattray 361–365

361 / C558 = H — when a child pretends to die, one pretends to bury the child. Reciprocal simulation/mirroring is explicit, but the transferable ethical function remains underdetermined.

362 / C554 = Y — the child who goes for water is the one who breaks the pot. Exposure to a task creates exposure to its characteristic risks; responsibility analysis should account for who actually undertook the activity.

363 / C555 = Y — of nine mischievous tricks intended for others, the child suffers five. Harmful schemes can rebound upon their initiator rather than remaining externalised onto intended targets.

364 / C559 = Y — a child fidgeting beside the fire sets the child's own cloth alight. Risky behaviour near a known hazard can generate foreseeable self-harm.

365 / C563 = Y — a child looking into the soup pot is not doing so without purpose; the child expects some. Observable attention/behaviour can provide evidence of an unspoken need or expectation.

### AR214 — R362/C554
Participation in a task creates exposure to the task's characteristic risks and should be considered when tracing responsibility for resulting loss.

### AR215 — R363/C555
Conduct intended to impose harm or mischief on others can rebound upon the initiating agent.

### AR216 — R364/C559
Risky behaviour in proximity to a known hazard can create foreseeable self-harm.

### AR217 — R365/C563
Observable behaviour can provide evidence of an unspoken need, expectation or intention.
