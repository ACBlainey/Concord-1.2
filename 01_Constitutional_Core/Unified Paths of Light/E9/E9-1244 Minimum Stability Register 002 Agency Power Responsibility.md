# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1244 — Minimum-Stability Register 002: Agency, Power and Responsibility

### Candidate set tested
SP05–SP10.

### Candidate parent

**MS-P03 — Protected agency under power and dependency**

Candidate children:
- SP05 viable choice;
- SP06 residual agency under constraint/assistance;
- SP07 non-creation/non-exploitation of vulnerability;
- SP08 legitimate authority;
- SP09 status should not displace standing/evidence/responsibility.

### Result: PARTIAL ACCEPTANCE

SP05–SP09 share a coherent orientation: preserving agency and standing where dependency, authority, vulnerability or status can distort them.

However they are not mutually replaceable. The deeper parent can organise them but cannot erase:
- viability of options;
- residual first-person agency;
- exploitation;
- legitimacy;
- status distortion.

### SP10 responsibility

**RETAIN SEPARATE.**

SP10 asks where accountability belongs according to agency and causal role. It can constrain power but also applies where no domination or dependency exists.

Candidate parent:
**MS-P04 — Causal responsibility and accountable action**
- child: SP10
- linked operators: evidence, reference point, proportional response.
