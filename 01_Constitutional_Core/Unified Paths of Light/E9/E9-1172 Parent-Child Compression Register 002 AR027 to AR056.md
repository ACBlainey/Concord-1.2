# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1172 — Parent-Child Compression Register 002: AR027–AR056

### Provisional local parents

**PC-P005 — Evidence-responsive causal attribution**  
Causal judgment should track evidence and the operative activity rather than convenient attribution.

Children:
- AR036 — evidence-responsive causal attribution.
- AR050 — adverse condition attributed to causative activity.

Relation: **CHILD / CHILD**. AR050 is a concrete application of the more general causal discipline represented by AR036, but both remain recoverable.

**PC-P006 — Effective agency under option constraints**  
Agency is materially affected by whether viable options remain available.

Children:
- AR041 — severe need constrains agency.
- AR054 — deficient options constrain accommodation/choice.

Relation: **CHILD / CHILD**. Neither is deleted; need and option-deficiency remain distinct mechanisms.

**PC-P007 — Situated knowledge and environment**  
Local embedding can alter both what agents know and what competencies or harms circulate through an environment.

Children:
- **M001 (AR052+AR053)** — embedded agents can possess context-specific knowledge unavailable to outsiders.
- AR043 — environment can transmit harmful competence.

Relation: **SIBLING** beneath a broader situated-environment parent. Knowledge possession and competence transmission must not be conflated.

**PC-P008 — Sufficiency is reference-dependent**  
Whether limited capability or resources suffice depends on the relevant need/function and access conditions.

Children:
- AR046 — limited capability can suffice for a relevant function.
- AR055 — proximity of a resource does not itself satisfy need.

Relation: **SIBLING** pending later function-relative adequacy material.

**PC-P009 — Threat-responsive protection**  
Threat can justify assessment, restraint, protection or defensive response, but these are not identical actions.

Children:
- AR047 — assess vulnerable points under threat.
- AR048 — common threat can suspend internal conflict.

AR013 is a **BRIDGE candidate** into this neighbourhood because failed remedy/protection tests claimed capability, but it is not assigned as a child yet.
