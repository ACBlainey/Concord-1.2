# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-109 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Situated Knowledge, Need and Hospitality

Rattray 140–150 produces a small cluster worth preserving for later compression without pre-empting root formation.

### Situated knowledge
R140 and R144 independently associate knowledge with sustained location/proximity:
- R140: the crab living near the river knows its language.
- R144: the crab knows where the gold dust is found.

### Need under apparent advantage
R145 and R146 distinguish environmental appearance from actual need:
- inadequate shelter options constrain where the crab sleeps;
- proximity to water does not eliminate thirst.

### Social provision
R148 adds an explicit human social interpretation: a visitor relies on the hospitality of a host.

### Status
This note records recurrence only. It does not merge these occurrences into roots and does not compare them with X1–X7 while the E9 firewall remains closed.
