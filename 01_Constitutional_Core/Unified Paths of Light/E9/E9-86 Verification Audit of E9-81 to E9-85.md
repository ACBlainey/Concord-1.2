# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-86 — Verification Audit of E9-81–E9-85

The Cornell/Internet Archive OCR witness was reopened and compared directly with the ledger claims in E9-81–E9-85.

The audit confirms that E9-81–E9-85 are not reliable transcriptions or classifications of Rattray 95–114.

Concrete mismatches include:
- Rattray 95 / Christaller 2244 actually states that where an elephant died, the leaves in its backyard were spoiled by people coming to cut up the meat.
- Rattray 96 is Christaller 2850, not 2245, and concerns a small knife that has acted beyond its proper/useful role and cut its master's hand.
- Rattray 98 is Christaller 3024, not 2247, and says a thin elephant can still fill a hundred baskets.
- Rattray 100 is Christaller 3026, not 2249, and concerns the large pieces of meat obtained from an elephant.
- Rattray 102 is Christaller 3030, not 2251.
- Rattray 106 is Christaller 455, not 2255, and says one man kills an elephant while many eat it.
- Rattray 107 is Christaller 444, not 2256, and contrasts safely eating an elephant with choking on a small fish bone.
- Rattray 108 is Christaller 753 and explicitly concerns bulk, greed and violence.

Accordingly, E9-81–E9-85 are **RETRACTED FROM THE EVIDENTIAL CHAIN**. Their Y/Z/H/X totals and AR027–AR036 must not be used.

This is a second source-integrity failure of the same general class as the earlier 35–54 failure. The fact that the documents claimed same-pass verification when no such source check was actually performed makes the procedural safeguard in E9-70 insufficient on its own.

# **E9-81–E9-85 RETRACTED**
