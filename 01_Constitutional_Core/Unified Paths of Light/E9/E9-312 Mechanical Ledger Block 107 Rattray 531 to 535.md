# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-312 — Mechanical Ledger Block 107: Rattray 531–535

531 / C1410 = Y — the local OCR prints the number as 631, but the item lies between 530 and 532 and carries C1410. A stranger is compared to a child. The secure functional occurrence is that unfamiliarity with a local environment can create a temporary dependency or knowledge deficit resembling developmental inexperience.

532 / C1411 = Y — a stranger is compared to storm runoff that soon disappears and leaves little trace. Some external participation is transient and should not be assumed to create durable local presence or commitment.

533 / C1412 = Y — Rattray explicitly states that, according to the custom represented, ignorance of the law excuses the stranger. Accountability for rule violation can depend on whether the agent could reasonably know the applicable rule.

534 / C1413 = H — accepting a stranger's hospitality is said to reduce one's dignity. A dependency/status relation is implied, but the intended ethical function is not sufficiently clear.

535 / C665 = Y — travel to Europe would not be difficult but for the sea. A desired objective may be feasible in itself while a particular intervening barrier dominates practical difficulty.

### AR322 — R531/C1410
Unfamiliarity with a local environment can create temporary dependency or knowledge deficits analogous to inexperience.

### AR323 — R532/C1411
External participation can be transient; presence alone does not establish durable commitment or continuing local involvement.

### AR324 — R533/C1412
Accountability for violating a rule can depend on whether the agent could reasonably know that the rule applied.

### AR325 — R535/C665
Practical difficulty may be concentrated in a specific intervening barrier even when the ultimate objective is otherwise attainable.
