# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-412 — Mechanical Ledger Block 147: Rattray 731–735

731 / C185 = Y — one does not hide and then light a fire. Actions can reveal the actor or condition they are inconsistent with; concealment is defeated by behaviour that generates observable evidence.

732 / C186 = Y — one does not break the water-pot the first time one stumbles. An initial failure or error should not trigger destruction/abandonment of a still-useful system or resource.

733 / C189 = Y — Rattray explicitly glosses “Had I known” as remorse/regret. Prior regret should function as learning: repeated exposure to the same avoidable error without correction defeats the informational value of experience.

734 / C191 = Y — laughing while departing a funeral is presented as lacking “sense of the fitness of things.” Conduct should be responsive to social context; behaviour appropriate in one setting may be inappropriate in another.

735 / C192 = Y — one should not tell a person seen only for the first time that they have become thin. Claims about change require a comparison baseline; a single observation cannot establish deterioration from a prior state.

### AR492 — R731/C185
Concealment can be defeated by actions that generate observable evidence inconsistent with remaining hidden.

### AR493 — R732/C186
An initial failure should not automatically trigger destruction or abandonment of a still-useful resource or system.

### AR494 — R733/C189
Experience of avoidable regret should inform later action; repeated error without correction wastes information gained from prior failure.

### AR495 — R734/C191
Appropriate conduct is context-sensitive; behaviour suitable in one social setting may be unsuitable in another.

### AR496 — R735/C192
A claim of change requires an adequate comparison baseline; a single observation cannot establish deterioration from a prior state.
