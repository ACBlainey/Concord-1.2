# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-294 — Mechanical Ledger Block 101: Rattray 501–505

501 / C28 = H — Rattray glosses the proverb as saying an inherent property cannot be removed by artificial means, but his explanation is entangled with racialised and gendered 1916 commentary. The abstract causal proposition is visible, yet the mediation burden is too high for immediate Y admission.

502 / C29 = H — “where the gold dust is, there women like to be” is a gendered material-interest generalisation. No secure transferable ethical occurrence is extracted.

503 / C30 = H — praise of a man's appearance by women is said to predict debt. The causal/normative function is insufficiently specified and gender-stereotyped.

504 / C19 = H — a woman's beauty is attributed to her husband, perhaps through purchased ornaments or clothing. The source gives only a tentative explanation and embeds the claim in a gendered dependency frame.

505 / C20 = Y — “it is the woman who knows her husband.” Knowledge generated by close relational experience may exceed that available to outsiders.

### AR306 — R505/C20
Close relational experience can generate agent-specific knowledge unavailable or less available to outside observers.
