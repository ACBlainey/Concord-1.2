“Of all the wide earth, the Supreme Being is the elder.”

The item opens Chapter I, whose heading explicitly concerns belief in a Supreme Being and related religious/material.

### Classification

Rattray's extensive following note enters a historical argument about Onyame/Nyankopon and competing European interpretations. That commentary is mediation/context, not a basis for manufacturing an ethical occurrence.
