# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-323 — Mechanical Ledger Block 112: Rattray 556–560

556 / C3546 = Y — sickness destroys even the person considered most worthy. Merit or social worth does not confer immunity from physical vulnerability.

557 / C3548 = Y — an illness demanding delicacies also demands repeated purchasing. A condition can generate secondary resource burdens beyond its primary harm.

558 / C1045 = Y — the physician does not drink the medicine for the patient. Assistance can provide knowledge, treatment or resources, but some necessary actions must still be performed by the affected agent.

559 / C2579 = H — pretending to be ill is said to lead to real illness. A warning against false claims may be intended, but the asserted causal mechanism is not secure enough for a general occurrence.

560 / C394 = Y — a person with stomach pain does not attempt to cure it with parched corn. Response should be appropriate to the condition; having a remedy-like resource does not make it suitable for every problem.

### AR341 — R556/C3546
Merit, worth or status does not eliminate vulnerability to illness.

### AR342 — R557/C3548
A harmful condition can create secondary resource burdens in addition to its direct effects.

### AR343 — R558/C1045
External assistance cannot substitute for every action required of the affected agent; some parts of remedy must be enacted by the person receiving help.

### AR344 — R560/C394
A response should be matched to the condition; availability of a remedy or resource does not establish suitability for a particular problem.
