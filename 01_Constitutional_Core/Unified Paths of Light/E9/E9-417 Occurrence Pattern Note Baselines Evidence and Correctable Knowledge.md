# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-417 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Baselines, Evidence and Correctable Knowledge

R731, R735, R740 and R745 form a compact epistemic sequence.

R731: conduct can reveal what an agent attempts to conceal.

R735: a claim that someone has changed cannot be supported by a single first observation because there is no baseline.

R740: road safety becomes knowable because someone travels and returns with experience.

R745: one person's ignorance can be corrected by another person's explanation.

Together they distinguish several requirements for reliable knowledge:
- observable evidence can defeat concealment;
- comparative claims require baselines;
- some unknowns require empirical exploration;
- knowledge can be distributed across agents and transferred through explanation.

The common structure is not mere “truth.” It is the production, correction and transmission of evidence-responsive knowledge.
