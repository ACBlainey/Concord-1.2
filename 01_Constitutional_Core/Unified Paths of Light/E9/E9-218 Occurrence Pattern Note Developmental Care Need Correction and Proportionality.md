# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-218 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Developmental Care, Need, Correction and Proportionality

R340 and R342–345 form one of the densest developmental clusters yet encountered.

- R340: development is not achieved through comfort alone.
- R342: dependent care should not wait for distress-signalling.
- R343: correction should clean or repair rather than destructively overreact.
- R344: affection does not remove the duty to give truthful corrective feedback.
- R345: standards against cheating apply in both directions, including when one's own child is the beneficiary.

Together these occurrences portray care as active and developmental rather than merely protective or indulgent: anticipate need, correct proportionately, communicate truthfully, and apply standards consistently.

No deepest-root compression is attempted.
