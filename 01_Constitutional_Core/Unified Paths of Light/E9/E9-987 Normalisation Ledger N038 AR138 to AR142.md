# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-987 — Normalisation Ledger N038: AR138–AR142

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR138 | R256 / C1768 | Immediate need does not necessarily justify consuming or damaging resources integral to one's own continuing function. | Need restraint / sustaining-resource preservation. |
| AR139 | R257 / C1766 | Capability is object- and context-sensitive; effectiveness in one mode does not establish suitability for handling every target safely. | Contextual capability / fragility. |
| AR140 | R259 / C1772 | When an observed outcome exceeds an agent's unaided capacity, causal analysis should consider assistance or intervention by another agent. | Causal inference from capability limits. |
| AR141 | R260 / C1771 | Existing behavioural disposition can be intensified by additional incentive, responsibility or urgency. | Motivation amplification. |
| AR142 | R261 / C1285 | Realised capability can be constrained by lack of support and increased through effective assistance or cooperation. | Support-sensitive capability. |

AR139 and AR142 further weaken any simple treatment of capability as an isolated, fixed scalar: realised capability depends on target, context and support.
