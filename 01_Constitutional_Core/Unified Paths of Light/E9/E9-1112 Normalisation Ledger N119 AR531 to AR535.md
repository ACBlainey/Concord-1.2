## E9-1112 — Normalisation Ledger N119: AR531–AR535

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR531 | R778 / C1214 | Reciprocal interaction should account for material capability or power asymmetry; nominally identical play may not be equivalent across unequal participants. | Asymmetry-sensitive reciprocity. |
| AR532 | R779 / C1383 | Observable surface effects can provide evidence of an underlying state or cause. | Observable indicator / causal inference. |
| AR533 | R780 / C1381 | Failure of a containing system need not destroy every component within it; subsystem survivability can exceed container survivability. | Nested-system resilience. |
| AR534 | R781 / C1390 | High individual capability remains bounded; competence does not imply unlimited workload or indefinite substitution for missing capacity. | Capability ceiling / workload. |
| AR535 | R782 / C1391 | Agents may externalise failure by blaming tools or conditions when the limiting factor lies in their own capability. | Self-serving causal attribution. |

AR531 complements AR511: reciprocity can fail through unequal permissions or through unequal capability that makes formally identical interaction materially non-equivalent.
