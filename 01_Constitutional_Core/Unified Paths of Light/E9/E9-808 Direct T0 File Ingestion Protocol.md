# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-808 — Direct T0 File-Ingestion Protocol

When a Christaller PDF/EPUB/image package becomes available, ingest it without immediately performing ethical extraction.

### Required sequence
1. hash/name/source provenance;
2. verify title, author, publisher/date;
3. establish page/canvas count;
4. locate printed page 1 and final printed page;
5. map digital offset(s);
6. inspect identifier typography and variant notation;
7. extract first 20 mechanically visible items under E9-743/E9-768;
8. test against E9-RX-1.0 and NC-ID register;
9. freeze the batch;
10. leave translation and ethical fields closed until their gates permit them.

OCR, if later used, remains a transcription aid and must be checked against page images.
