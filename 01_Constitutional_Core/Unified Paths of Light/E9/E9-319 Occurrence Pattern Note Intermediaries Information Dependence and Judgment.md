# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-319 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Intermediaries, Information Dependence and Judgment

R542 supplies a particularly clear information-power relation.

Rattray explains that the English-speaking local interpreter can direct the European official whom to praise and whom to blame because the official depends on the interpreter in dealings with local people.

The occurrence is not merely about translation accuracy. It shows that an intermediary positioned between:
- the evidence/source population; and
- the decision-maker

can influence the decision-maker's model of reality.

This creates a distinct E9 occurrence pattern:

**dependence on an informational intermediary creates dependence on that intermediary's selection, framing and transmission of information.**

No claim is made here that the interpreter necessarily deceives. The structural dependence exists even without deception.
