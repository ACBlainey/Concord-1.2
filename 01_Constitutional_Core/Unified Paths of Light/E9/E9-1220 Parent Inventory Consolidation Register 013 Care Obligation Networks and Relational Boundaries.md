# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1220 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Register 013: Care, Obligation Networks and Relational Boundaries

### Higher internally derived parents

**HC-P028 — Care obligations are vulnerability- and capability-sensitive**

Subsumes:
- PC-P004 care portions;
- PC-P010 support portions not already assigned to capability;
- AR057/224/225 caregiver-capability and stronger-agent support;
- AR298 functional self-maintenance for demanding obligation.

Core structure:
> Care obligations should respond to the recipient's dependency and vulnerability while remaining calibrated to the caregiver's actual capacity and ability to sustain the obligation.

**HC-P029 — Support is networked and can depend on relational nodes**

Subsumes:
- PC-P072 — network-node dependency;
- AR284 support through kin/network relations;
- AR299 loss of a central relational node;
- PC-P066 protective affiliation where support networks reduce predation.

Core structure:
> Support is often produced by networks rather than isolated dyads; loss or absence of key relational nodes can materially reduce protection, information and assistance.

**HC-P030 — Relationship does not erase boundaries between persons, claims or property**

Subsumes:
- PC-P031 — non-transfer through relationship;
- AR154/157 person/property non-transfer;
- AR294 ownership boundaries within close relations;
- PC-P048 where responsibility is not transferred by association;
- AR555 possession by a related agent does not create entitlement.

Core structure:
> Relation or association does not automatically transfer identity, property, responsibility, entitlement or control from one member to another.

### Bridge

HC-P030 links relationship ethics to accountability, agency and resource/property structure. Its recurrence across all four domains argues for preserving it as a cross-domain structural principle.
