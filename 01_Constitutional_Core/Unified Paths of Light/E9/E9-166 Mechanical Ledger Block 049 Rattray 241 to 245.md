## E9-166 — Mechanical Ledger Block 049: Rattray 241–245

241 / C2481 = Y — when a bird dies in the sky, its feathers nevertheless fall to earth. Consequences of an event can propagate beyond the location in which the initiating event occurs.

242 / C2482 = Y — the bad bird fouls its own nest. Harmful conduct can degrade the agent's own sustaining environment.

243 / C2483 = Y — the bird makes its nest by repeated going and coming. Durable structures can be built incrementally through repeated small contributions rather than one complete act.

244 / C2484 = Y — a full-grown bird that does not trouble to fly in search of food sleeps hungry. Possessing mature capability does not secure need satisfaction if the capability is not exercised.

245 / C2487 = Y — when one bird alone eats the grain, it tramples the excess because there is more than it can consume. Exclusive access beyond one's capacity to use a resource can produce waste.
