# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-418 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Local Knowledge, Authority and Allocation Boundaries

R737–739 form an unusually compact three-step boundary sequence.

R737: an outsider does not enter another village and instruct its chief in that village's own origin.

R738: an outsider does not enter another village and declare himself “father of a nation.”

R739: a person requesting food is not thereby entitled to control serving/allocation.

The three occurrences separate:
1. access/presence from epistemic authority;
2. presence from political/status authority;
3. beneficiary status from allocation authority.

This strengthens an emerging E9 distinction between **participation and control**. Being present in a system, receiving from it, or interacting with it does not automatically confer authority over its knowledge, governance or resource allocation.
