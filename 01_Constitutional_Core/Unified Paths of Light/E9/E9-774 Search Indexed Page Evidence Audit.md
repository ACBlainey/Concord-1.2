# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-774 — Search-Indexed Page Evidence Audit

Search indexing of LoC image 12 and image 21 exposes bibliographic metadata, format, rights and the fact that those individual images exist. It does **not** expose the scanned Twi page content itself.

Therefore indexed image records remain **digital-structure evidence**, not item evidence under E9-769.

No C-ID or proverb wording is transcribed from these records.
