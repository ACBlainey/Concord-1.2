# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-488 — Christaller Expansion Translation Debt Register Template

Translation debt will be tracked independently from ethical ambiguity.

### Debt levels
**TD0 — none:** reliable aligned translation visible.

**TD1 — low:** minor orthographic/OCR uncertainty not material to ethical meaning.

**TD2 — material:** one or more words/relations could alter the abstraction.

**TD3 — high:** no reliable aligned translation or source text too uncertain for classification.

### Rule
Translation debt is not the same as H.

- **H** means ethical yield is plausibly present but unresolved after adequate source access.
- **TD3** means the translation/source gate itself is insufficient; no Y/Z/H decision should be forced.

### Register fields
C-ID | T0 locator | translation witness | debt level | issue | resolution action | status.

This prevents linguistic uncertainty from being disguised as ethical uncertainty.
