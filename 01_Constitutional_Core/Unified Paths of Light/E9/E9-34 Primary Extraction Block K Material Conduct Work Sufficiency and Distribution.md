# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-34 — Primary Extraction Block K: Material Conduct, Work, Sufficiency and Distribution

## Coverage
Material bearing on labour, possession, scarcity, contribution and use of resources was processed under the same source-led rule.

## Provisional functions observed
- diligence and productive contribution;
- consequences of waste or improvidence;
- prudence under scarcity;
- reciprocal material assistance;
- restraint of excessive acquisition where context supports it;
- dependence between individual livelihood and collective relations;
- capability-sensitive expectations;
- responsibility for maintaining means of subsistence.

## Control
Economic success is not treated as moral worth.

Instrumental wealth advice remains prudential unless the source supports a separable relational, responsibility or restraint function.
