# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-208 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Supporting Infrastructure and Functional Dependency

R317 and R320 expose two complementary forms of dependency.

R317 describes protective gun-lock covers that preserve powder in adverse conditions. The primary capability depends on apparently secondary infrastructure.

R320 reverses the direction: once the hunter's hand is disabled by the gun accident, the attendant's provisioning activity loses much of the purpose for which it was organised.

Together they show:
1. primary capabilities can depend on supporting systems;
2. supporting systems derive part of their function from the primary activity they enable.

No compression is performed.
