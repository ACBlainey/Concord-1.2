### Emerging source-claim distinction

AR354–AR359 sharpen an epistemic distinction that should survive later compression:
- a hostile source may over-attribute blame;
- hostility may distort evaluative speech;
- early framing may bias later reception;
- representation may caricature;
- a uniformly hostile adjudicator set compromises procedure;
- yet a hostile source can still make a true claim.

Therefore **source reliability is not binary**. Motive, framing, representation, evidence and claim truth may need separate evaluation.
