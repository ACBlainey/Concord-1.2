# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-411 — Mechanical Ledger Block 146: Rattray 726–730

726 / C160 = Y — no one puts a finger in another person's mouth and then beats that person over the head. An agent should not create a condition of dependency/exposure and then exploit that condition to inflict harm.

727 / C161 = H — no one cracks a palm nut with their own teeth and gives it to a companion. The action may concern inappropriate transfer, contamination, or the limits of service, but the source provides no gloss sufficient to fix the mechanism.

728 / C168 = Y — one does not take a calabash with no opening to ask for palm oil. A request for assistance/resources requires a receiving mechanism capable of accepting what is requested.

729 / C172 = Y — one does not descend from a many-forked shrub and only then ask for a forked stick. Available enabling resources should be recognised and secured while they are accessible rather than sought only after leaving the opportunity behind.

730 / C182 = Y — no one keeps searching for a sleeping place until dawn. Search for an optimum solution can become self-defeating when delay consumes the period in which any solution has value.
