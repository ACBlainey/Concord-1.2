### First possible near-duplicate cluster

AR005, AR010 and AR013 now constitute a clear local cluster concerning **claimed capability versus demonstrated performance**. They remain separate occurrence-normalisations because their source functions differ:

- AR005 — test the claim against consequences;
- AR010 — assertion does not erase actual limits;
- AR013 — demonstrated failure undermines reliance.

Their later consolidation must preserve any function lost by merging them.
