# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-241 — Mechanical Ledger Block 078: Rattray 386–390

386 / C2600 = Y — if an elder eats greedily, the elder must remove his own dish. Status does not exempt an agent from consequences or burdens generated by the agent's own excess.

387 / C2601 = H — Rattray records two readings: evasion of responsibility through “I have not heard,” and prudent refusal to attend to tittle-tattle. Because these point in importantly different normative directions, the item remains unresolved rather than selecting one.

388 / C2603 = Y — an elder may take the stool from the speaker's hand but cannot take the ground on which the speaker sits. Removal of one resource or status-bearing object does not imply control over every underlying condition or remaining basis of agency.

389 / C2607 = Y — Rattray explicitly explains that the elder has other resources to fall back on when trouble overtakes him. Resilience can depend on reserves accumulated or retained for adversity.

390 / C2608 = Y — when elder and children adapt their steps to one another, the children carry his bag. Cooperative support becomes possible through mutual adjustment across capability/age difference.

### AR231 — R386/C2600
Status does not exempt an agent from consequences or burdens generated by the agent's own excess.

### AR232 — R388/C2603
Control or removal of one resource does not necessarily establish control over the deeper conditions on which another agent continues to depend or act.

### AR233 — R389/C2607
Resilience under trouble can depend on having alternative or reserve resources available.

### AR234 — R390/C2608
Cooperation across capability differences can depend on reciprocal adjustment rather than unilateral adaptation by only one party.
