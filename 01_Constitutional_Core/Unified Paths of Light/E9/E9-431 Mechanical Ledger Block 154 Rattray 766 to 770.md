## E9-431 — Mechanical Ledger Block 154: Rattray 766–770

766 / C1006 = Y — one tree does not make a forest. A functioning collective or complex system cannot be constituted by a single component alone; plurality can be structurally necessary.

767 / C1011 = Y — the OCR prints the Rattray number as “IQl.” A tree bent for thirty years cannot be expected to be straightened in one. Long-developed conditions may require proportionate time and sustained corrective effort rather than instantaneous repair.

768 / C1012 = Y — when a great tree falls, the papaw takes its place. Removal of a dominant/large component creates space that can be occupied by a smaller successor; system roles can persist through replacement even when the replacement differs in scale.

769 / C1013 = Y — when a great tree falls, its young shoots emerge but will die once the sustaining sap dries. Dependents or successor structures can remain temporarily active after loss of their sustaining source while nevertheless facing delayed failure.

770 / C1016 = Y — when a tree stands in a small village, its roots are in the houses. Large or embedded systems can extend effects/dependencies deeply into surrounding local structures; apparent boundaries may understate actual integration.
