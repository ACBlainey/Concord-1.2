### Emerging scarcity cascade

The latest occurrences form a multi-stage structure rather than a single proposition that poverty is harmful:

**resource loss or shortage → constrained resource quality and choice → increased effort/risk → reduced participation → vulnerability to dependency → possible autonomy/status loss → amplified impact of further shocks.**

AR395 adds a temporal implication: because major loss may be unpredictable, resilience has value before deprivation becomes visible.

### Truth as system-cost variable

AR378–AR385 also show that falsehood can:
- contaminate otherwise truthful information;
- increase dispute-resolution cost;
- hide evidence beyond practical verification;
- persist temporarily;
- collapse rapidly under accurate evidence;
- damage trust in a wider claim set.

This remains an internal functional neighbourhood, not a compressed parent.
