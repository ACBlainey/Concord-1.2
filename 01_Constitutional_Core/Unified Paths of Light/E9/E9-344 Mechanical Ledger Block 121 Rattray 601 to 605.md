# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-344 — Mechanical Ledger Block 121: Rattray 601–605

601 / C2475 = Y — “in truth there is no deceit.” Truthfulness and deception are represented as incompatible modes of representation.

602 / C2477 = Y — truth is said to be too scarce to be cut down further by falsehood. The proverb treats truthful information as something whose integrity should not be degraded through added fabrication.

603 / C3403 = Y — telling a lie produces fear of Kumase, where Rattray says important cases were tried. False representation creates vulnerability to accountable scrutiny and adjudication.

604 / C3338 = Y — the liar travels for a thousand years while the truth-speaker catches up in a day. Falsehood may temporarily outrun correction, but truthful evidence can eventually overtake a fabricated account.

605 / C3339 = H — the “smooth-tongued” person offered a seat claims already to have a stone. The likely function concerns unnecessary or reflexive falsehood, but the source provides no explanatory gloss sufficient to fix the intended ethical mechanism.

### AR378 — R601/C2475
Truthful representation excludes deliberate deception within the same claim or account.

### AR379 — R602/C2477
The integrity of truthful information is degraded when fabrication is inserted into it.

### AR380 — R603/C3403
False representation creates vulnerability when claims are exposed to accountable scrutiny and adjudication.

### AR381 — R604/C3338
Falsehood may gain temporary distance, but later truthful evidence can overtake and correct a fabricated account.
