# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-198 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Differentiated Capability and Collective Effectiveness

R292 and R296 provide complementary collective-capability evidence.

R292 shows that simply increasing the number of participants does not necessarily deepen the result.

R296 shows a different route to collective benefit: one agent lacks a specific skill, another possesses it, and the product is shared.

Together they distinguish:
- participant quantity from effective coordination;
- identical capability from complementary capability.

Collective effectiveness can therefore depend on how capabilities are organised, not merely on how many agents are present.
