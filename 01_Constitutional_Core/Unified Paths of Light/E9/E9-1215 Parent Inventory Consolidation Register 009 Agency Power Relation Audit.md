### Information-loss tests

**HC-P013 vs HC-P014 — RETAIN SEPARATE.**  
One concerns conditions for effective choice; the other concerns relational consequences of dependency.

**HC-P014 vs HC-P016 — RETAIN SEPARATE.**  
Dependency is one generator of power asymmetry, but asymmetry can also arise through status, collective pressure, coercive infrastructure or control of consequences.

**HC-P016 vs HC-P017 — RETAIN SEPARATE.**  
Power structure can exist without deliberate exploitation; exploitation adds a responsibility relation.

**HC-P018 vs HC-P019 — RETAIN SEPARATE.**  
Legitimacy of authority and distortion caused by status are different questions.

**HC-P019 vs HC-P020 — RETAIN SEPARATE.**  
Status neutrality constrains judgment; causal accountability determines where responsibility belongs.

### Higher-order signal

The eight parents form two broad descriptive concentrations:

**A. Agency under conditions**
- HC-P013, HC-P014, HC-P015

**B. Power, standing and accountability**
- HC-P016, HC-P017, HC-P018, HC-P019, HC-P020

These concentrations are **not roots**. The boundary between them is porous because dependency is a major generator of power.
