# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-332 — Mechanical Ledger Block 115: Rattray 571–575

571 / C3179 = H — hatred of a friend's child is said to cause one's own child's sudden death. A reciprocity warning may be intended, but the asserted causal mechanism is not secure enough for admission.

572 / C3180 = Y — once a person is disliked, that person is blamed for all kinds of things. Prior hostility can bias attribution of responsibility beyond the evidence for particular acts.

573 / C431 = Y — hatred produces malignant remarks about the target. Hostile disposition can distort or weaponise evaluative speech.

574 / C432 = H — the local OCR prints the item number as 674. Hatred is linked to scratching/scarring, followed by Rattray's extended note on tattoo stigma, slave-origin marking and protective scarification of children whose siblings had died. The historical material is important, but a transferable ethical occurrence is not sufficiently secure from the proverb itself.

575 / C429 = Y — hostility causes an adversary to remind the target of promises left unfulfilled. Even a hostile source may identify a real outstanding obligation; source hostility does not by itself make the underlying claim false.

### AR354 — R572/C3180
Prior dislike can bias attribution, causing an agent to be blamed broadly without act-specific evidence.

### AR355 — R573/C431
Hostile disposition can distort or weaponise evaluative speech about its target.

### AR356 — R575/C429
A source's hostility does not by itself invalidate a factual claim about an outstanding obligation; claim content and source motive should be distinguished.
