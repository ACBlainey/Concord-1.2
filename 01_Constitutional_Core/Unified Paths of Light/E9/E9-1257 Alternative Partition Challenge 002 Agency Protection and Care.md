# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1257 — Alternative-Partition Challenge 002: Agency, Protection and Care

### Candidate reduction C
**MS-P03 Agency + MS-P05 Protection**

Candidate replacement:
**AP-C — Preservation of persons against damaging constraint**

#### Failure
This either:
- defines loss of agency as “damage,” making harm vacuously broad; or
- excludes non-harmful domination and agency restriction.

It also cannot represent cases where protective intervention legitimately constrains immediate choice.

**Decision: REJECT.**

### Candidate reduction D
**MS-P05 Protection + MS-P06 Care/relationship**

Candidate replacement:
**AP-D — Vulnerability-responsive support**

#### Failure
SP14–SP16 include relationship-generated obligation, reciprocity and durable bonds without requiring vulnerability or hazard. Conversely SP11–SP13 can apply to strangers or uninvolved parties without a sustaining relationship.

**Decision: REJECT.**

### Three-way candidate
**Agency + protection + care → “human/agent welfare”**

#### Failure
“Welfare” becomes a vacuous super-category unless it separately restates agency, harm and relational-care constraints. Once restated, no compression has occurred.

**Decision: REJECT.**
