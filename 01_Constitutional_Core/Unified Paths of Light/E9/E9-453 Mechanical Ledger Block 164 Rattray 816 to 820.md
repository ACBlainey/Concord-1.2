# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-453 — Mechanical Ledger Block 164: Rattray 816–820

816 / C3043 = Y — the moon moves slowly but crosses the town. Slow progress can still complete a substantial objective; rate alone does not establish failure.

817 / C3148 = Y — the elder twin does not eat salt so that it trickles into the younger twin's mouth. One agent's consumption or experience does not automatically satisfy another agent's need merely because the agents are closely related or similarly situated.

818 / C3163 = Y — a dirty cloth is washed rather than burned. Correctable degradation should invite proportionate repair rather than destruction of the underlying useful object.

819 / C3202 = Y — if another person runs away with one's cloth while one is bathing, first obtain another covering before pursuing, lest observers mistake both parties alike. Response to another agent's disorderly conduct should preserve one's own standards and legibility rather than reproducing conduct that makes the responder indistinguishable from the original problem.

820 / C3236 = H — old palm nuts are not used to make soup. Obsolescence, deterioration or fitness-for-purpose is plausible, but the source provides no explanatory note fixing which mechanism is intended.
