# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-168 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Stewardship, Surplus and Sustaining Environment

R242 and R245 form a notable resource/environment pair.

- R242 depicts an agent degrading its own nest: harmful conduct can damage the environment on which the agent itself relies.
- R245 depicts exclusive access to more grain than one bird can consume, with the surplus then trampled and wasted.

At occurrence level these distinguish two forms of self-defeating resource relation:
1. degrading the sustaining environment;
2. controlling surplus beyond usable need and thereby wasting it.

This is relevant to the emerging stewardship pattern, but no root compression or comparison to X5 is permitted while the firewall remains closed.
