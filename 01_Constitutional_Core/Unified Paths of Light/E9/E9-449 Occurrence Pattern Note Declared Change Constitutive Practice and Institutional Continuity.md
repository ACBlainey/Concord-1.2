# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-449 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Declared Change, Constitutive Practice and Institutional Continuity

R805 is unusually useful for change analysis.

The younger generation says it will no longer halt at the old stopping-place. The proverb challenges this declaration by asking why the inherited three-stone hearth remains intact.

The mechanism is not simply “respect tradition.” It tests whether **declared change has propagated into constitutive practice**.

This produces a useful distinction:
- rhetorical rejection of an inherited arrangement;
- operational removal or replacement of the practices that reproduce it.

A system can therefore describe itself as changed while continuing to instantiate the old structure through retained practices.

This may later be relevant to institutional drift, reform and continuity analysis, but no Concord mapping or root compression is performed while the E9 firewall remains closed.
