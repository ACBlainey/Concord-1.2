# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-13 — Translation-Layer Independence Protocol

## Principle
E9 must preserve the distinction:

source occurrence → translation → functional coding.

Translation and ethical interpretation must not collapse into one operation.

## Preferred translation hierarchy
1. published scholarly translation aligned to the frozen source;
2. independent lexical/grammatical verification of uncertain items;
3. AI-assisted translation only as a flagged provisional aid and never as sole support for a high-confidence ethical occurrence.

## Translation disagreement
Where translations differ materially:
- preserve alternatives;
- identify the disputed term/construction;
- lower confidence or place the occurrence on HOLD;
- do not choose the version that better matches a predicted ethical function.

## Copyright/access control
A later translation may be used for analysis where legitimately accessible, but E9 documentation should record functional propositions and limited necessary source information rather than reproduce a copyrighted translation at scale.

## Audit requirement
Before normalisation, a translation-dependence audit must identify:
- occurrences resting on one translation only;
- high-impact ambiguous terms;
- interpretations requiring contextual scholarship;
- items whose ethical classification changes under plausible alternative translations.
