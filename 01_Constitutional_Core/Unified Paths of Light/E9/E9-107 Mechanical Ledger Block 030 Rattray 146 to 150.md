## E9-107 — Mechanical Ledger Block 030: Rattray 146–150

146 / C1747 = Y — even a crab associated with water becomes thirsty, still more a monkey living above. Apparent environmental advantage does not eliminate basic need.

147 / C505 = H — the otter is described as unprepared for a soul-washing and therefore searching for crabs to offer. Rattray provides extensive ritual context, but a general ethical function is not sufficiently explicit.

148 / C1750 = Y — Rattray explicitly explains that an Ashanti visiting another Ashanti does not take food because he relies on the hospitality of his host. This is direct evidence of a reciprocal social expectation of hospitality/support.

149 / C522 = H — the tortoise lacks cloth, hair or wool yet does not feel cold. Adaptation despite absence of expected protection is clear, but no ethical norm is supplied.

150 / **no visible Christaller identifier in the retrieved text** = H — all animals can climb stones, but the tortoise falls when it tries; Rattray says this is used of an unlucky person. Capability mismatch is visible, but ethical abstraction remains uncertain.
