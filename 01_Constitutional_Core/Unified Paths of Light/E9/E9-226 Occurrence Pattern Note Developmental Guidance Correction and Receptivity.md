# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-226 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Developmental Guidance, Correction and Receptivity

R346, R352 and the immediately preceding R344 form a strong developmental-feedback sequence.

- R344: caregivers should truthfully identify poor performance rather than indulgently affirm it.
- R346: guidance cannot correct behaviour when advice is not taken.
- R352: failure to hear parental guidance is associated with misfortune.

The sequence distinguishes three necessary parts of a developmental feedback loop:
1. corrective information must be given;
2. the recipient must be capable of receiving it;
3. guidance must be acted upon for the corrective loop to function.

This is retained as an occurrence pattern rather than compressed into a deepest root.
