# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-299 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Learning Through Proximity, Guidance and Relationship

R493, R494 and R505 show three distinct knowledge pathways.

- R493: behaviour is acquired by following and observing a parent.
- R494: guidance directed to other children can also educate an observing child.
- R505: close relationship generates knowledge of a particular person.

These distinguish:
1. observational imitation;
2. developmental spillover from instruction;
3. relationship-specific experiential knowledge.

The pattern reinforces the E9 observation that knowledge and capability are not produced solely through explicit formal teaching.
