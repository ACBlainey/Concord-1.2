# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-211 — Mechanical Ledger Block 066: Rattray 326–330

326 / C604 = Y — one expression of sympathy for the hunter includes rain, insects, cold and thorns. A compact act of care can recognise a bundle of hardships rather than requiring each burden to be separately named.

327 / C605 = Y — when the hunter waits but the game does not come, he returns home. Continued investment may reasonably cease when the enabling condition for the activity fails to materialise.

328 / C606 = H — the hunter does not spare the sick animal. The source gives no interpretation sufficient to determine whether this is descriptive hunting practice, severity, impartiality, or another function.

329 / C3062 = Y — successful hunting in rain is attributed to the protective gun-lock cover. Outcomes should credit enabling protective infrastructure, not only the visible primary actor.

330 / C1262 = Y — merely being handed a tortoise to deliver is rhetorically distinguished from hunting. Receiving an outcome without performing the productive activity should not be represented as having performed that activity.

### AR183 — R326/C604
An expression of care can recognise multiple associated hardships together rather than requiring every burden to be separately enumerated.

### AR184 — R327/C605
Persistent effort may reasonably cease when the condition required for productive success fails to materialise.

### AR185 — R329/C3062
Credit for successful performance should include enabling protective infrastructure where that infrastructure materially made the outcome possible.

### AR186 — R330/C1262
Receiving or transporting an achieved outcome is not equivalent to having performed the productive activity that generated it.
