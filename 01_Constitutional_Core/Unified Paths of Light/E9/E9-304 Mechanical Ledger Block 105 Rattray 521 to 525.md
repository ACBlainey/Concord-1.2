# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-304 — Mechanical Ledger Block 105: Rattray 521–525

521 / C3464 = H — a person's enemy is said already to have been born when the person is born. The saying asserts persistent or inevitable opposition, but no sufficiently specific ethical function is established.

522 / C3465 = Y — the archer did not hold a bow at birth. Specialised capability is developed rather than fully present at birth; current competence should not be projected backward as innate completed ability.

523 / C3467 = Y — “I am weary of having borne you” is something no one wants to say. The proverb marks rejection of one's dependent child as a strongly disfavoured endpoint despite burdens of care.

524 / C194 = Y — no one fixes a day on which to give birth to a future elder/person of importance. Future standing and developed capability cannot be reliably predetermined at birth.

525 / C3466 = H — Rattray gives two interpretations linking greed and extortion, explicitly saying both are found. Because the intended relation is unresolved between similarity and consequential reversal, the item remains H.

### AR316 — R522/C3465
Specialised capability is developed over time rather than fully present at birth.

### AR317 — R523/C3467
Care burdens do not make repudiation of a dependent child an ordinary or desired endpoint; relational responsibility persists under strain.

### AR318 — R524/C194
Future importance or developed capability cannot be reliably predetermined from an individual's condition at birth.
