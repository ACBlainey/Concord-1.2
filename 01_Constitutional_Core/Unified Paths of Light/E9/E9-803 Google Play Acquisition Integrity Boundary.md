# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-803 — Google Play Acquisition Integrity Boundary

The phrase **Get for free** establishes user-facing acquisition/read availability but does not itself expose proverb text to the present execution environment.

Therefore:
- do not transcribe from metadata;
- do not equate ebook page number with printed page number;
- do not assume downloadable EPUB/PDF bytes until actually obtained;
- do not treat text-to-speech/readability as machine-readable source access.

The route remains valid but item evidence still requires directly readable pages/text.
