# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-193 — Mechanical Ledger Block 060: Rattray 296–300

296 / C1557 = Y — Mr Rat cannot crack the kernel but Mrs Rat can, so he eats some of hers. Differentiated capability within a relationship can permit one agent's competence to meet another's need through sharing.

297 / C1572 = Y — when visiting the rat's town, one eats the food eaten there; the source explicitly cross-references R158, which carries the same local-practice structure. Entry into another community can involve adaptation to ordinary local practices.

298 / C171 = Y — no one begins making the rope in front of the animal they hope to catch. Effective action may require withholding operational information from a target whose awareness would defeat the action.

299 / C495 = Y — the approaching animal does not know the hunter is concealed behind a screen. An agent can act under materially incomplete information while another agent intentionally exploits that informational asymmetry.

300 / C496 = Y — sweat on a hairy animal is not easily noticed. A real condition may be present while surface properties make it difficult for observers to detect.

### AR162 — Rattray 296 / Christaller 1557
**Minimal function:** Differentiated capabilities among associated agents can allow one agent's competence to satisfy a need another cannot meet alone.
**Confidence:** high.

### AR163 — Rattray 297 / Christaller 1572
**Minimal function:** Participation in another community can involve adapting to its ordinary local practices.
**Confidence:** high, reinforced by explicit cross-reference to R158.

### AR164 — Rattray 298 / Christaller 171
**Minimal function:** Operational information may be withheld where revealing it to an opposed target would directly defeat the intended action.
**Confidence:** high; this records the source function without generalising deception as normatively permissible.

### AR165 — Rattray 299 / Christaller 495
**Minimal function:** An agent's behaviour can be shaped by materially incomplete information that another agent intentionally exploits.
**Confidence:** high.

### AR166 — Rattray 300 / Christaller 496
**Minimal function:** Absence of visible evidence does not establish absence of an underlying condition when the condition can be masked by surface properties.
**Confidence:** high.
