# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-359 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Truth, Trust Fragility and Verification Access

R606–609 continue the explicit truth sequence begun at R601.

R606 shows a verification-access problem: alleged evidence is placed in Europe, beyond immediate practical checking.

R607 shows a maintenance-cost problem: falsehood makes the case laborious while truth is associated with rapid settlement.

R608 shows a robustness problem: a long-running falsehood can be displaced rapidly by truth.

R609 shows a trust-fragility problem: one demonstrated falsehood can damage confidence in a much larger body of truthful claims.

Together with R601–604, the source is no longer merely repeating a generic preference for truth. It is identifying different functional consequences of truth and falsehood:
- verifiability;
- dispute cost;
- correction;
- trust preservation;
- adjudicative exposure.

These distinctions should be preserved until compression rather than collapsed prematurely.
