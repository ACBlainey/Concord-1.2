# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-419 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Preservation, Proportionality and Avoidable Destruction

R732 and R741 provide two different anti-destruction structures.

R732: the water-pot is not broken after the first stumble. Initial failure does not justify destroying a still-useful resource.

R741: the yam store is not allowed to burn merely to gain the smaller benefit of eating some roasted yams.

These distinguish:
- **failure-triggered overreaction**, where a setback causes disproportionate abandonment;
- **benefit-triggered destruction**, where a small derivative gain is used to rationalise a much larger loss.

Both require evaluating the retained value of the underlying system before choosing destructive action.

This pattern may later connect to repair, resilience and stewardship, but no compression is performed while the firewall remains closed.
