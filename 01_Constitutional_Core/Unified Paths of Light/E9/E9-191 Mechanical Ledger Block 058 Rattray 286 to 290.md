## E9-191 — Mechanical Ledger Block 058: Rattray 286–290

286 / C2707 = Y — a horse does not turn aside without a cause; Rattray explicitly explains that it is responding to the rein. Observable behavioural change should prompt investigation of an operative cause rather than be treated as causeless.

287 / C2708 = Y — even if the horse is foolish, it does not follow that the rider is foolish. A property of one member of a linked system should not automatically be attributed to another distinct member.

288 / C2709 = H — if the horse does not go to war, its tail does; the note explains the tail's use as a victory charm carried by generals and captains. This is culturally informative but does not yield a sufficiently secure transferable ethical function.

289 / C2710 = H — horse and elephant both have tails but the horse's is slightly larger. Comparative difference is explicit but ethically underdetermined.

290 / C1837 = H — even if the mouse were cow-sized, it would remain the cat's slave. A persistent domination relation is stated, but the source does not establish whether it is descriptive, normative, critical, or otherwise transferable.
