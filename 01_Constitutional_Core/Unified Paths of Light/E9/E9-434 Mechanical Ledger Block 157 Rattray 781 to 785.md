# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-434 — Mechanical Ledger Block 157: Rattray 781–785

781 / C1390 = Y — even if strong, one does not do two men's work. Individual capability has limits; high competence does not justify assuming unlimited workload or substituting indefinitely for missing capacity.

782 / C1391 = Y — when one is not strong, one says the head-rest is no good. Agents may externalise failure by blaming tools or conditions when the limiting factor is their own capability.

783 / C1397 = Y — personal beauty does not pay a debt. Status or valued traits in one domain do not discharge obligations arising in another; capability/value is domain-specific.

784 / C1469 = Y — if killed by a trap, one does not die from an arrow wound. Causal attribution should identify the operative cause rather than assigning an alternative cause merely because it is another known hazard.

785 / C1522 = Y — when counting cattle, one counts their tails. Complete accounting requires inclusion of the defining members/components rather than relying on an incomplete or impressionistic estimate.

### AR534 — R781/C1390
High individual capability remains bounded; competence does not imply unlimited workload or indefinite substitution for missing capacity.

### AR535 — R782/C1391
Agents may externalise failure by blaming tools or conditions when the limiting factor lies in their own capability.

### AR536 — R783/C1397
A valued trait in one domain does not discharge an obligation arising in another; value and capability are domain-specific.

### AR537 — R784/C1469
Causal attribution should identify the operative cause rather than substitute a different known hazard.

### AR538 — R785/C1522
Reliable accounting requires inclusion of the relevant members or components rather than impressionistic estimation.
