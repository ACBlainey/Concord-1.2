# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-274 — Mechanical Ledger Block 093: Rattray 461–465

461 / C1335 = Y — Rattray links this to the class of people who voluntarily entered dependency for protection because of need. Severe vulnerability can lead an agent to accept substantial loss of autonomy in exchange for care or security.

462 / C3501 = Y — between master and slave there is no equal struggle for mastery. The saying clearly records structural asymmetry of power within the relationship.

463 / C3503 = Y — a hostile master uses the label “free-born slave” against a dependent who had lost original status through debt or misfortune. Status labels can be strategically invoked by a more powerful party as instruments of degradation or control.

464 / C352 = Y — no one buys a slave to act as a restraint on the buyer. A controller does not ordinarily acquire a subordinate in order to be constrained by that subordinate; the relation is structurally asymmetric in who may impose restraint.

465 / C921 = Y — without a master, a person may be captured and sold. The source records a social environment in which absence of protective affiliation can expose an agent to extreme predation.

### AR277 — R461/C1335
Severe need or vulnerability can lead an agent to accept major autonomy constraints in exchange for protection or care.

### AR278 — R462/C3501
Some dependency relations are structurally asymmetric in the participants' ability to contest control.

### AR279 — R463/C3503
A powerful party can weaponise status labels to degrade or control a dependent participant.

### AR280 — R464/C352
A structurally controlling relationship can be asymmetric in who possesses the practical ability to impose restraint.

### AR281 — R465/C921
Absence of protective affiliation in a predatory environment can expose an agent to severe loss of autonomy and exploitation.
