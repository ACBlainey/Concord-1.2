## E9-1063 — Normalisation Ledger N086: AR354–AR358

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR354 | R572 / C3180 | Prior dislike can bias attribution, causing an agent to be blamed broadly without act-specific evidence. | Hostility-conditioned attribution. |
| AR355 | R573 / C431 | Hostile disposition can distort or weaponise evaluative speech about its target. | Hostility-conditioned evaluation. |
| AR356 | R575 / C429 | A source's hostility does not by itself invalidate a factual claim about an outstanding obligation; claim content and source motive should be distinguished. | Claim/source separation. |
| AR357 | R576 / C3169 | Adjudication is structurally compromised when all decision-makers share a hostile disposition toward the party being judged. | Decision-maker conflict / adjudicative bias. |
