# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-01 — African Oral-Medium Method Stress Test

## Status
**PRE-EXTRACTION — ETHICAL CONTENT FIREWALL REMAINS CLOSED**

## Purpose
E9 begins by testing whether the existing Unified Paths method can be transferred from bounded textual corpora to orally transmitted African ethical traditions without silently changing the object of study.

## Immediate finding
The existing method remains usable at the level of experimental logic, but its previous assumption of a comparatively fixed canonical text cannot simply be carried across.

For E9 the source object may be:
- orally transmitted rather than authored as a fixed book;
- preserved in multiple performances and collections;
- context-dependent in meaning;
- translated after collection;
- temporally layered;
- affected by collector selection;
- distributed across proverb, narrative, song, ritual speech, instruction and other forms.

Therefore a printed anthology cannot automatically be treated as equivalent to a canon.

## Method retained unchanged
The following controls remain valid:
1. predeclare the source boundary;
2. register predictions before extraction;
3. extract source-led ethical occurrences;
4. retain provenance;
5. separate theological/cosmological statements from separable ethical function;
6. complete provenance audit before compression;
7. permit unrestricted root count;
8. freeze the internal architecture before comparison;
9. reopen X only after freeze;
10. score without post-hoc rescue.

## Method requiring adaptation
E9 requires additional provenance dimensions:
- ethnolinguistic community;
- source form;
- collector/recorder;
- collection date where known;
- publication date;
- original language;
- translator;
- performance/use context where known;
- variant status;
- degree of source mediation;
- historical-contact risk.

## Core methodological warning
The unit of analysis must be an ethically interpretable **attested occurrence**, not an imagined original African statement reconstructed behind the surviving record.

Multiple variants may be evidence of persistence, contextual adaptation or transmission, but must not be counted automatically as independent principles.

## Decision
No fatal problem with the Unified Paths method has been identified. E9 requires a **medium-aware provenance layer** before the ordinary extraction/compression sequence can begin.
