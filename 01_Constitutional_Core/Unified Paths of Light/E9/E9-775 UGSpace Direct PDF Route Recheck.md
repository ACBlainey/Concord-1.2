# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-775 — UGSpace Direct-PDF Route Recheck

University of Ghana continues to expose the Christaller record as **Twi Mmebusem.pdf (28.3 MB)**, dated 1879 and attributed to J. G. Christaller.

A search for direct UGSpace bitstream endpoints surfaced other repository PDFs that cite Christaller but did not reliably identify the 28.3 MB Christaller bitstream itself.

### Integrity disposition
No unrelated UGSpace bitstream is accepted merely because it contains a Christaller citation. The direct PDF route remains active but unresolved at byte level.
