# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-424 — Mechanical Ledger Block 153: Rattray 761–765

761 / C822 = Y — when one's possession is beautiful, another person should say so rather than oneself. Positive evaluation of one's own quality has greater credibility when independently recognised rather than self-certified.

762 / C824 = Y — when one's own possession is poor, one does not take another person's possession. Dissatisfaction or deprivation does not create entitlement to appropriate another agent's property.

763 / C994 = Y — one breaks the point of the stick about to pierce one's eye rather than sharpening it. When a foreseeable hazard threatens serious harm, action should reduce the hazard rather than intensify it.

764 / C999 = Y — the stick used to roast meat is itself burned at the end. Tools or intermediaries that enable a process can bear costs generated by that process; burdens may fall on enabling components rather than only on the final beneficiary.

765 / C1004 = Y — no tree bears fruit without first having flowers. Mature outcomes depend on prior developmental stages; expecting the endpoint while bypassing necessary precursors is unrealistic.

### AR516 — R761/C822
Positive evaluation gains credibility when independently recognised rather than solely self-certified.

### AR517 — R762/C824
Dissatisfaction with one's own resources does not create entitlement to appropriate another agent's property.

### AR518 — R763/C994
When a foreseeable hazard threatens serious harm, action should reduce rather than intensify the hazard.

### AR519 — R764/C999
Tools or intermediaries enabling a process can bear costs generated by that process and should not be treated as costless.

### AR520 — R765/C1004
Mature outcomes depend on necessary prior developmental stages; endpoints cannot always be reached by bypassing precursors.
