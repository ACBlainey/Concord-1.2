# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-498 — Controlled T4 Activation Conditions

Because complete T1 access remains unavailable, E9 records the conditions under which T4 lexical/AI assistance may later be activated.

### T4 may not silently become T1
Any T4 translation must be labelled as model/lexical reconstruction.

### Minimum inputs
Before attempting T4 on a C-only item:
- exact T0 Twi must be available;
- material OCR uncertainty must be resolved or flagged;
- dictionary/lexical support should be available where needed;
- variant structure must be preserved.

### Output status
A T4 rendering begins with translation debt and cannot automatically support Y.

Where wording materially affects ethical meaning, retain TD2/TD3 until an independent witness resolves it.

### Validation
A sample of T4 outputs should be tested against known Rattray/Lange-aligned items before T4 is permitted at scale.

No T4 corpus extraction is started in this document.
