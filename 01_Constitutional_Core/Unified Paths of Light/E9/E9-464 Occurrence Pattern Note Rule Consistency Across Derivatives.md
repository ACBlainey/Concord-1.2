# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-464 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Rule Consistency Across Derivatives

R825 and R826 form an explicit paired construction:

- R825: one does not make a fowl taboo and then eat its chickens.
- R826: one does not make a vulture taboo and then eat its eggs.

The repeated structure is stronger than a single occurrence because the source deliberately varies the animal and derivative while preserving the relation.

The secure abstraction is:

**A restriction should not be formally applied to a source while functionally evaded through directly dependent derivatives when doing so defeats the substance of the restriction.**

This is a source-internal consistency pattern. It may later be relevant to rule circumvention, loopholes and anti-evasion architecture, but no Concord mapping or cross-tradition comparison is performed while the E9 firewall remains closed.
