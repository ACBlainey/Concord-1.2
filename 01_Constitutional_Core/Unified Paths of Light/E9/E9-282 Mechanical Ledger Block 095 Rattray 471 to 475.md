# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-282 — Mechanical Ledger Block 095: Rattray 471–475

471 / C684 = H — family names blossom in clusters. The proverb describes clustered kinship structure, but no independent ethical function is explicit.

472 / C685 = Y — the family is compared with an army, while the child of one's own mother is identified as the closest kin. Collective kinship provides organised support, but degrees of relational proximity remain differentiated.

473 / C686 = Y — “the family tree is not cut.” Kinship continuity is represented as persistent rather than casually severable.

474 / C2199 = Y — what one people discuss with laughter another discusses with tears. The same subject or event can carry materially different significance for different communities; one's own reaction is not sufficient evidence of another group's experience.

475 / C1996 = Y — when a nation approaches ruin, the cause begins in its homes. Large-scale collective failure can emerge from distributed local or household-level conditions rather than only from a central event.

### AR284 — R472/C685
Collective kinship can function as organised mutual support while still containing differentiated degrees of relational proximity.

### AR285 — R473/C686
Some social relationships are represented as carrying durable continuity rather than being casually severable.

### AR286 — R474/C2199
Different communities can experience and evaluate the same subject very differently; one group's response should not be assumed to represent another's.

### AR287 — R475/C1996
Large-scale collective failure can emerge from distributed local conditions within the constituent units of the system.
