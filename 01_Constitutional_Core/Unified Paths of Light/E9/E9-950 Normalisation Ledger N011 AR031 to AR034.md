## E9-950 — Normalisation Ledger N011: AR031–AR034

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR031 | R104 / C256 | The scale or value of an objective can change the practical significance of lesser obstacles encountered in pursuing it. | Goal-sensitive prioritisation; primarily action-structuring. |
| AR032 | R105 / C893 | Alignment with a sufficiently significant path or undertaking can reduce exposure to lesser impediments. | Path/coordination relation; similar surface to AR031 but different mechanism. |
| AR033 | R106 / C455 | Benefits produced by an individual's successful action can extend beyond the producer to a wider community. | Distributed benefit / collective participation. |
| AR034 | R107 / C444 | Success against large challenges does not confer immunity from smaller hazards; risk assessment must remain sensitive to small but effective threats. | Vulnerability despite demonstrated capability; relation with AR026. |

AR031 and AR032 remain separate because one changes the relative weight of obstacles while the other concerns a path that reduces exposure to them.
