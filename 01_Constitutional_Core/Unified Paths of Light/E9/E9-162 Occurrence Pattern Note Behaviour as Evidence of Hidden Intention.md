# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-162 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Behaviour as Evidence of Hidden Intention

R228 and R231 add to the existing E9 cluster concerning stated reason, operative motive and behavioural evidence.

- R228 explicitly gives an instrumental reason for the vulture's location: remaining on the dung-hill avoids payment.
- R231 infers desire to enter the house from repeated voluntary presence immediately outside it.

Together with R176–177, these occurrences strengthen a recurring epistemic structure:
**declared or undeclared motive can be tested against strategic behaviour and positioning.**

This remains occurrence-level evidence. No deepest root or cross-tradition mapping is performed.
