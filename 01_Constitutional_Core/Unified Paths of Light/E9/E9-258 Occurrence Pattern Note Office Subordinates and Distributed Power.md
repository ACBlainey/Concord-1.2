# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-258 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Office, Subordinates and Distributed Power

R416 and R425 show two ways in which an officeholder's effective power depends upon surrounding agents.

R416: good councillors contribute to a peaceful reign.

R425: the chief's servant causes the chief's person to excite fear.

The surrounding network can therefore:
- improve decision quality and governance outcomes; or
- amplify the coercive presence of the office.

This suggests that political power in the sampled material is functionally distributed across an office and its surrounding human system rather than residing entirely in the nominal leader.

No normative endorsement of the historical hierarchy is implied.
