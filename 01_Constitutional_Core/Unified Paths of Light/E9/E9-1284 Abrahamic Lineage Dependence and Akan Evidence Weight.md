# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1284 — Abrahamic Lineage Dependence and Akan Evidence Weight

### Problem

E3 Islam and E4 Judaism cannot be counted as fully independent cultural replications. They share historical, textual and regional ancestry and therefore provide less independent evidence than their raw experiment count suggests.

### E9 contribution

The Akan experiment changes the evidential structure because its source medium and cultural lineage are substantially different from the E3/E4 scriptural pair.

However E9 is not treated as perfectly independent:
- historical Christian and Islamic contact is possible;
- the Rattray witness is colonial-era and mediated;
- translation/collector framing can import categories;
- direct Christaller reconstruction remains incomplete.

### Weighting rule

Evidence should therefore be described by **lineage breadth**, not number of experiments.

A cluster of closely related traditions does not equal the evidential weight of the same number of widely separated traditions.

### Current interpretation

E3/E4 establish strong within-related-family recurrence.

E6/E7/E8 broaden the cultural and philosophical test space.

E9 adds an African oral/proverbial-medium test with a distinct source architecture and a frozen result that still recovers all X functions.

This strengthens breadth while preserving contamination/contact uncertainty.
