# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-815 — T0 Acquisition Stop Condition v2

After the Google Play and machine-metadata attempts, automated acquisition is considered **tool-blocked**, not research-blocked.

Do not generate further T0-acquisition documents unless one of these changes:
1. a complete file becomes available;
2. direct page images/text become readable;
3. a manifest/bitstream endpoint becomes directly retrievable;
4. a new tool capability materially changes acquisition.

This supersedes repeated attempts against unchanged endpoints and protects the experiment from administrative-document inflation.
