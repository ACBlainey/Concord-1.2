## E9-1053 — Normalisation Ledger N080: AR326–AR329

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR326 | R536 / C666 | The principal risk in pursuing an otherwise acceptable objective can lie in the transition or access interface rather than in the destination itself. | Transition risk / interface safety. |
| AR327 | R537 / C2431 | Desire or willingness does not establish effective choice when access or opportunity is absent. | Opportunity-constrained agency. |
| AR328 | R538 / C668 | High status, office or material protection does not eliminate fundamental vulnerability to mortality. | Status-independent vulnerability. |
| AR329 | R539 / C669 | Possessing or supplying a useful capability does not guarantee applying that capability to one's own condition. | Capability possession versus self-application. |

AR325–AR326 should remain distinct: one identifies a concentrated obstacle to feasibility; the other identifies risk specifically in the transition or interface through which an objective is reached.
