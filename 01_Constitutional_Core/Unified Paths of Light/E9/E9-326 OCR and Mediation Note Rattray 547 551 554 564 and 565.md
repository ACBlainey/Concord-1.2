### R554 — disability stigma
The source describes historical exclusion of people with physical impairments from chieftaincy and reports killing of an infant born with six fingers. Rattray then says the proverb **perhaps** concerns willingness to undergo treatment because of feared social slights after permanent disability.

E9 does not convert those discriminatory practices into norms. Because Rattray himself marks the interpretation as uncertain, R554 remains H.

### R565 — execution context
Rattray's note describes a historical method of execution and an additional practice intended to prevent the condemned person from swearing a major oath.

The mechanical occurrence extracted from the proverb is limited to behavioural adaptation under credible lethal threat. The historical practices themselves are not endorsed or generalised.
