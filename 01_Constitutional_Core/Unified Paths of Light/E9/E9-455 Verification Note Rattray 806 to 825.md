### Classification restraint
R812 remains H because clarity/ease of stating a case is not sufficient evidence that the case is good.

R813 remains H because the absence of a flag during “big business” may encode secrecy, seriousness or another convention; the source does not fix the mechanism.

R820 remains H because “old palm nuts” may concern deterioration, obsolescence or fitness for purpose, but the source gives no explanatory gloss.

R821 remains H because recurrence of history is descriptive without a secure ethical prescription.

R824 remains H because the universal claim that all things are “two and two” is insufficiently explained.

### R817 mediation
Rattray supplies extensive ethnographic notes about twins. Those notes are preserved as recorder/contextual material but are not required for the admitted occurrence, which derives from the proverb's explicit separation of one twin's consumption from the other's receipt.
