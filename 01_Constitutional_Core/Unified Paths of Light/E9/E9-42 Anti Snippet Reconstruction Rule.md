# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-42 — Anti-Snippet Reconstruction Rule

## Problem
Search engines and catalogue pages expose isolated words, sample phrases, metadata and occasional text snippets from Rattray.

Those fragments are insufficient for an 830-item mechanical ledger.

## Rule
E9 must not reconstruct the Rattray corpus from:
- search-result snippets;
- common-term lists;
- quotations in secondary papers;
- modern proverb websites;
- remembered or famous Akan sayings;
- inferred gaps between accessible items.

## Reason
Such reconstruction would create an undocumented selection process exactly where E9-38 requires source-order completeness.

## Permitted use
Snippets may verify bibliographic facts or help locate the complete source. They cannot instantiate Y/Z/H/X item records.
