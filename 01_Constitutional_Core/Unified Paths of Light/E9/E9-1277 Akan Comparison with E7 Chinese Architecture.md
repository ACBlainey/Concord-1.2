# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1277 — Akan Comparison with E7 Chinese Architecture

### Prior E7 result

E7 compressed to seven roots and recovered the seven X functions in both directions.

### Akan comparison

The Akan architecture again covers the same broad functional regions:
- protection;
- reality-responsive information;
- agency/power;
- care/relationship;
- resources/stewardship;
- accountability/correction/repair;
- development.

Akan additionally elevates adaptive continuity into AK-R8 and explicitly separates an extensive operator/structural layer.

### Structural contrast

The Akan derivation makes dependency, bargaining, contestability, networks, coordinated plurality, propagation and resilience unusually explicit at the architecture level.

This does not establish that these are absent from E7. It establishes that E9's compression method/corpus caused them to remain explicit rather than disappear into branches or contextual applications.

### Result

**Functional convergence remains strong; partition and architectural-layer expression differ.**
