## E9-955 — Normalisation Ledger N015: AR046–AR048

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR046 | R127 / C3412 | Capability can be limited in absolute terms yet sufficient for an agent's relevant requirements. | Sufficiency is requirement-relative; resists absolute capability ranking. |
| AR047 | R128 / C3413 | Assessment of an agent or system should attend to its most vulnerable relevant point because failure can concentrate there. | Weak-point / resilience relation. |
| AR048 | R130 / C3415 | A sufficiently serious common external threat can supersede internal conflict and make temporary cooperation rational. | Threat-induced coordination; cooperation is conditional rather than assumed permanent. |

AR046 adds an important distinction between **maximum capability** and **sufficient capability**. AR047 concerns structural vulnerability rather than overall weakness.
