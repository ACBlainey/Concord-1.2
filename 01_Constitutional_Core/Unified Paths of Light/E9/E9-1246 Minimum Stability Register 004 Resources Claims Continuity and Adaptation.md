# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1246 — Minimum-Stability Register 004: Resources, Claims, Continuity and Adaptation

### Candidate set tested
SP19–SP23.

### Resource/claim candidate

**MS-P07 — Responsible resource relation**

Candidate children:
- SP19 functional use/sufficiency;
- SP20 entitlement requires basis;
- SP21 productive contribution can ground differentiated claims.

### Result: ACCEPT AS ORGANISING PARENT, CHILDREN REQUIRED

Use, entitlement and contribution all concern material/resource relations, but they answer different questions:
- what is adequate/useful;
- who has a justified claim;
- how productive causal role affects claims.

### Continuity/adaptation candidate

**MS-P08 — Preservation through adaptive continuity**

Candidate children:
- SP22 preservation of enabling conditions across time;
- SP23 function-preserving adaptation.

### Result: STRONG ACCEPTANCE

These two are complementary temporal directions:
- continuity preserves what must persist;
- adaptation changes what must change to preserve function.

They are not identical, but their joint parent is unusually coherent and is supported by maintenance, succession, resilience, stewardship and context-sensitive implementation below.

### Cross-test MS-P07 → MS-P08

Stewardship aspects of resource relation can serve continuity, but entitlement and contribution do not reduce to continuity.

Decision: **BRIDGE only**, no merger.
