### Emerging dependency topology

The current sequence distinguishes several variables that should remain separate during later compression:
- whether entry into dependency is meaningfully chosen;
- whether exit or contest is possible;
- whether the controller shapes subordinate conduct;
- whether treatment quality affects cooperation;
- whether contribution changes resource control or standing;
- whether fear suppresses information;
- whether external predation makes protective affiliation instrumentally necessary;
- whether the relationship can override life or agency.

This is richer than a single scalar of "more" or "less" power. No parent is assigned.
