# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-26 — Bridge-Stratum Structural Coverage Map

## Purpose
Before continuing substantive coding, the Rattray bridge stratum is fixed by its published structural order.

Independent bibliographic description confirms that Rattray arranged the selected Christaller material into fifteen subject groups spanning:
- Supreme Being;
- fauna;
- war;
- social types;
- human estates and virtues;
- natural phenomena;
- general precepts and maxims.

Rattray's selection was made partly for anthropological/customary interest and partly for grammatical or syntactic interest. Therefore subject-group density must not be interpreted as ethical importance.

## Coverage rule
All published groups must be traversed, including grammatically selected and ethically zero-yield material.

## Consequence
E9-23 through E9-25 are treated as opening traversal blocks rather than a complete structural representation.

The remaining bridge extraction must explicitly cover the later human-estate, virtue, natural-phenomena, and general-precept strata before bridge closure.
