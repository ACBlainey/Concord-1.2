## E9-1175 — Parent-Child Compression Relation Register 001

### Purpose

This relation register records the first higher-order connections among provisional parents PC-P001–PC-P021 without yet collapsing them.

No higher parent is frozen because assessment, sufficiency, support and collective composition remain distinct.

- PC-P021 — appearance/knowledge/entry conditions.

Current relation: **SIBLING/BRIDGE CLUSTER**.  
The repeated internal signal is orientation toward conditions as they actually are rather than toward labels, unsupported attribution or untested assumptions.

Current relation: **BRIDGE CLUSTER**.

### Compression discipline

No cluster above is a frozen root or final parent. The register is an index for subsequent passes across the remaining catalogue.
