# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-511 — Translation Validation Pilot Sample Freeze

A 50-item blinded validation sample is now frozen before any fallback translation is run.

### Sampling method
The sample is drawn from the closed Rattray reservoir and deliberately spans the corpus rather than concentrating on one chapter.

### Frozen sample
R1, R14, R24, R27, R31,
R35, R41, R46, R54, R66,

### Design properties
The sample intentionally includes:
- religious/cosmological language;
- truth and falsehood;
- authority;
- vulnerability;
- capability;
- causation;
- reciprocity;
- repair;
- resource/property relations;
- social context;
- figurative and literal constructions;
- items with substantial recorder commentary.

### Freeze rule
No item may be removed merely because it proves difficult to translate. Any replacement requires a documented source-access reason, not poor performance.
