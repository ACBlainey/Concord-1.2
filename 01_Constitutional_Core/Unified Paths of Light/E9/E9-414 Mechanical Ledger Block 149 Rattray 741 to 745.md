# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-414 — Mechanical Ledger Block 149: Rattray 741–745

741 / C213 = Y — one does not allow a yam store to burn merely to obtain roasted food from the loss. A minor derivative benefit does not justify permitting destruction of the substantially more valuable underlying resource.

742 / C214 = Y — one does not mix good kola nuts with false/spurious ones and sell them to one's countrymen. Product quality and representation should not be deliberately corrupted through admixture of inferior or counterfeit goods.

743 / C221 = Y — one does not dispense with the thumb when tying a knot. A component whose contribution is necessary to successful completion should not be excluded merely because it is one part among several.

744 / C226 = H — “No one shows another, saying, ‘Buy salt and eat.’” The source gives no explanatory gloss sufficient to determine the intended transferable mechanism.

745 / C265 = Y — if one person does not know, another explains. Knowledge deficits can be corrected through distributed social knowledge; ignorance in one agent need not become collective ignorance when another agent can teach.

### AR501 — R741/C213
A minor derivative benefit does not justify allowing destruction of the substantially more valuable underlying resource.

### AR502 — R742/C214
Goods or information should not be deliberately corrupted by mixing authentic/high-quality contents with false or inferior substitutes while representing the whole for exchange.

### AR503 — R743/C221
A component necessary to successful completion should not be excluded merely because it is only one part of a larger system.

### AR504 — R745/C265
Individual ignorance can be corrected through distributed social knowledge when another agent can explain or teach.
