# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-244 — Mechanical Ledger Block 081: Rattray 401–405

401 / C2625 = Y — grown-ups frighten a person into running and then laugh at the resulting flight. An authority or stronger party should not manufacture another's reaction and then ridicule that reaction as though it arose independently.

402 / C1877 = H — an old man existed before a chief was born. Temporal seniority and formal authority are distinguished, but the intended ethical function remains underdetermined.

403 / C100 = Y — the toothless old woman keeps hard “tiger” nuts because she may have a reason unknown to the observer. Apparent mismatch between capability and possession should not be judged without considering information or purposes unavailable to the observer.

404 / C101 = Y — the old woman cares for the hens and the hens support her through eggs and offspring. Care relationships can be reciprocal across differently capable participants.

405 / C102 = Y — Rattray explicitly says that when older persons undertake tasks younger people do, they should not expect special consideration on account of age. Voluntary assumption of a task can carry its ordinary performance expectations despite status-based claims to accommodation.

### AR241 — R401/C2625
An agent should not induce another's reaction through fear or manipulation and then treat that induced reaction as grounds for ridicule.

### AR242 — R403/C100
Apparent mismatch between an agent's capability and possessions or choices does not justify judgment where relevant reasons may be unknown to the observer.

### AR243 — R404/C101
Care and material support can be reciprocal between differently capable participants.

### AR244 — R405/C102
Voluntarily assuming a task can entail its ordinary performance expectations even when the agent could otherwise claim status-based accommodation.
