262 / C1283 = Y — when the cat visibly carries its bag through the house, the mouse does not reach inside. Credible presence of a capable protector/deterrent can prevent opportunistic intrusion.

263 / C1286 = Y — when the cat dies, the mice rejoice. Removal of a constraining or threatening power changes the freedom and welfare of those previously subject to it.

265 / C228 = Y — no one teaches a cat how to steal. Some behavioural competencies or dispositions emerge without explicit instruction.
