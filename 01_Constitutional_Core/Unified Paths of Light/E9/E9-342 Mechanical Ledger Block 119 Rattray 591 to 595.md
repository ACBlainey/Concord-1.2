# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-342 — Mechanical Ledger Block 119: Rattray 591–595

591 / C1904 = Y — a person squandering gold dust blames the scales. An agent may externalise responsibility for self-caused loss by blaming the measurement system or instrument.

592 / C1910 = Y — by the time the fool learns the game, the players have dispersed. Capability acquired too late can lose practical value because the opportunity for its use has passed.

593 / C1911 = Y — a person who knows little else nevertheless knows the person's own food. Limited general capability does not imply absence of domain-specific knowledge.

594 / C2238 = Y — the local OCR prints the item number as 694. Someone exploiting a “fool” is observed by a wise person. Exploitation of a vulnerable or less capable agent may be visible to third parties and can affect the exploiter's standing beyond the immediate transaction.

595 / C1909 = Y — the fool's own produce is taken and sold back to him. Information or capability asymmetry can permit an agent to be exploited through transactions involving resources that already belong to that agent.

### AR369 — R591/C1904
Agents may externalise responsibility for self-caused loss by blaming an instrument, process or measurement system.

### AR370 — R592/C1910
Capability acquired after the relevant opportunity has passed may have sharply reduced practical value; timing is part of effective development.

### AR371 — R593/C1911
Limited general competence does not imply absence of genuine domain-specific knowledge.

### AR372 — R594/C2238
Exploitation of a vulnerable or less capable agent can be observed by third parties; the transaction's ethical and reputational effects need not remain confined to the immediate parties.

### AR373 — R595/C1909
Information or capability asymmetry can enable exploitation in which an agent is induced to pay for access to resources already belonging to that agent.
