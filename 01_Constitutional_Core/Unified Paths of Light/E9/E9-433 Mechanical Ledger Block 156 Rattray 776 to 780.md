## E9-433 — Mechanical Ledger Block 156: Rattray 776–780

776 / C1152 = Y — before the wind comes, long grass is motionless. Current stability does not establish future stability when a relevant external force has not yet arrived; evaluation should account for untested conditions.

777 / C1174 = H — if a plantation has nothing else, it has at least silence. Silence as residual value, absence of disturbance, or consolation is plausible, but the ethical mechanism is underdetermined.

778 / C1214 = Y — play should be with one of one's own size. Reciprocal interaction should account for capability/power asymmetry; an activity treated as harmless play among peers may not be equivalent across strongly unequal participants.

779 / C1383 = Y — the OCR prints the Rattray number as “770.” A pot's glistening surface indicates water on it. Observable surface effects can provide evidence of an underlying state or cause.

780 / C1381 = Y — when the water-pot breaks, the calabash inside remains unharmed beside it. Failure of a containing system need not destroy every component within it; subsystem survivability can exceed container survivability.
