# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-568 — Overlap Index Identifier-Debt Taxonomy

Identifier debt is separate from translation debt.

### ID0
Identifier directly clear in working witness.

### ID1
Minor OCR typography issue but number unambiguous.

### ID2
Identifier damaged; independent same-edition witness resolves it.

### ID3
Identifier unresolved or conflicting.

### ID4
Nonstandard identifier requiring source-structure investigation.

### Expansion rule
Only ID0–ID2 may enter the reconciled overlap set automatically.

ID3–ID4 remain quarantined from C-only subtraction until resolved.

### Example
R585 moved from apparent local “B14” to reconciled C814 by independent witness: ID2.
