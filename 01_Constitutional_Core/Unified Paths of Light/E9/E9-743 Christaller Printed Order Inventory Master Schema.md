# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-743 — Christaller Printed-Order Inventory Master Schema

The master T0 inventory row is frozen as:

`C-ID-RAW | C-ID-RECONCILED | T0 page/image | source text RAW | source text NORMALISED | variant marker | R-overlap? | R-item(s) | NC-ID text-match? | translation witness | TD | yield state | AR relation | notes`

### Entry rules
1. A row exists only after direct T0 inspection.
2. C-ID and source text are transcribed, never generated from expected sequence.
3. Gaps, repeated IDs, suffixes and variants remain visible.
4. R-overlap is tested against E9-RX-1.0.
5. C-only status means absent from E9-RX-1.0 and not subsequently matched to an NC-ID Rattray item.
6. Translation/yield fields remain blank until their gates are satisfied.
