# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-283 — Mechanical Ledger Block 096: Rattray 476–480

476 / C1998 = Y — if an entire nation can be destroyed, a single household is even less immune. Scale or collective size does not eliminate vulnerability, while smaller constituent units may be still more exposed.

477 / C1999 = Y — the same community criticises a person when wealthy and disparages the person when poor. Social evaluation can be structured so that incompatible conditions both attract condemnation, making approval impossible through the criticised variable alone.

478 / C2000 = H — the united people want a person killed and the chief performs the killing. Collective backing of coercive authority is visible, but because the action is lethal and the source gives no normative qualification, it is retained as historical power structure rather than admitted as an ethical prescription.

479 / C2001 = Y — a people are compared to a load whose many contents cannot all be perceived in one day. Complex communities cannot be reliably understood through brief observation; adequate understanding requires time and exposure to internal diversity.

480 / C2002 = Y — unanimous communal preference compels an individual to adopt a specified hairstyle. Collective consensus can exert strong coercive pressure over individual conduct even in personal or symbolic domains.

### AR290 — R479/C2001
