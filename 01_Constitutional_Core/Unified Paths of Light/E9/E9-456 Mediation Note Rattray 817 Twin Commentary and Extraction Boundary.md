# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-456 — Mediation Note: Rattray 817 — Twin Commentary and Extraction Boundary

R817/C3148 states that the elder twin does not eat salt so that it may trickle into the younger twin's mouth.

Rattray then supplies a substantial ethnographic note on:
- terminology for first and second twins;
- precedence;
- treatment of twins at birth;
- ceremonial procession and song;
- dressing twins alike;
- comparison with an Nkoranza practice concerning a ninth child.

### Extraction boundary
The admitted occurrence is narrow:

**One agent's consumption or experience does not automatically satisfy another agent's need, even where the agents are closely related or similarly situated.**

The surrounding ethnographic commentary is not converted into additional ethical occurrences in this ledger block.

In particular, the comparative statement concerning Nkoranza practice is recorder-supplied context and is not treated as evidence about Akan ethics without a separately controlled source basis.

This preserves E9's distinction between:
1. proverb text;
2. Rattray commentary;
3. mechanically admissible abstraction.
