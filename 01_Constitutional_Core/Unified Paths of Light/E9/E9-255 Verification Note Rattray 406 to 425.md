### Chapter transition
R408 begins Chapter IX: Chiefs, Free Men and the Nobility, Slaves, the Family, Nationality, Parents and Relations, Women and Wives, Marriage, Birth.

### Interpretive caution
This chapter contains explicit hierarchical and coercive political material. Statements about kings, chiefs, execution and authority are not automatically treated as ethical endorsement. Items remain H where descriptive, ideological and normative readings cannot be securely separated.

### Explicit source anchors
R410 is glossed “Times and manners change.”
R411 explicitly says the chief's compulsion operates by authority of the people.
R416 explicitly links good councillors with a peaceful reign.
R423's note states that executed persons' heads were brought to the chief's house; this is retained as historical context, not normative endorsement.
