### Why 8 ≠ 7 is not a contradiction

The architectures partition overlapping functional space differently.

Examples:
- X6 packages correctability, accountability and repair. Akan separates these across AK-O4, AK-R4 and repair/recoverability mechanisms.
- X5 stewardship is distributed between AK-R7 resource relation and AK-R8 adaptive continuity.
- AK-R8 has no one-label X equivalent; its function is distributed across X5, X6 and X7.
- AK-R1 includes communication fit and legitimate information boundaries beyond the compact X2 title.
- AK-R6 explicitly includes reciprocity and relational durability beyond the compact X4 title.

### Topological interpretation

The comparison therefore supports:
**functional convergence without partition identity.**

Akan has:
- one more substantive organising family than X;
- a richer explicit operator layer;
- strong structural representation of dependency, power, networks, system propagation and resilience.

Yet all seven X functions are recoverable and all eight Akan families find functional representation within the X topology.

### Caution

This demonstrates convergence within the tested abstractions. It does not establish historical independence, universality, natural-law status or exhaustive Akan coverage.
