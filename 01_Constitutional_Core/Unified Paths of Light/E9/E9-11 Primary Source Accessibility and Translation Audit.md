# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-11 — Primary Source Accessibility and Translation Audit

## Trigger
This audit was conducted at the point E9-A was ready to begin primary extraction.

## Source verification
The frozen 1879 Christaller source is publicly available as page images/PDF and is catalogued as a 152-page collection of approximately 3,600 Twi/Tshi proverbs with variations.

However, bibliographic verification also identifies a later dedicated English translation: Kofi Ron Lange's 1990 *Three Thousand Six Hundred Ghanaian Proverbs*, explicitly catalogued as a translation of Christaller's 1879 work.

This creates a methodological issue that must be resolved before ethical extraction.

## Problem
The 1879 source can securely define:
- corpus membership;
- proverb order;
- source-language form;
- recorded variants.

It cannot be assumed, without item-level verification, to provide sufficient English translation for ethical coding by an English-language AI instance.

The existence of a separate 1990 English translation is evidence that translation is a distinct layer rather than something E9 should silently manufacture.

## Decision
Primary ethical extraction is temporarily held at the translation gate.

The corpus boundary remains frozen. No prediction is altered.
