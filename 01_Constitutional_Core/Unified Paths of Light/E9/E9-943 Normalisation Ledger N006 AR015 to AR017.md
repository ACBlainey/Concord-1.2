## E9-943 — Normalisation Ledger N006: AR015–AR017

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR015 | R66 / C3486 | An agent should not redirect an unavoidable serious threat onto another vulnerable person merely to substitute that person for themselves. | Non-substitution under threat; vulnerability qualifier retained. |
| AR016 | R74 / C59 | Adverse social classification does not erase an agent's capacity for attachment, grief and vulnerability to loss. | Recognition across condemned/feared category boundaries; culturally specific source category retained in provenance. |
| AR017 | R76 / C61 | Even limited sharing can support reciprocal protection, while complete refusal to share can weaken social reciprocity. | Reciprocity/sharing; source mechanism highly context-bound and therefore not strengthened beyond the occurrence. |

### Normalisation note

AR015 is kept distinct from generic harm avoidance because the source structure concerns transferring one's own danger to a substitute. AR016 is not converted into a universal equality claim; it establishes a narrower correction against category-based erasure of ordinary vulnerability. AR017 remains a conditional reciprocity proposition rather than an unconditional duty to share.
