# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1245 — Minimum-Stability Register 003: Harm, Protection, Care and Relationship

### Candidate set tested
SP11–SP18.

### Harm/protection candidate

**MS-P05 — Protection from avoidable harm and vulnerability**

Candidate children:
- SP11 preventive restraint;
- SP12 vulnerability-sensitive protection;
- SP13 non-displacement of harm.

### Result: ACCEPT AS ORGANISING PARENT, CHILDREN REQUIRED

All three concern protection, but each contributes a distinct relation:
- prevention before harm;
- heightened obligation under vulnerability;
- distribution/non-externalisation of exposure.

### Relationship/care candidate

**MS-P06 — Sustaining relationships of care and reciprocity**

Candidate children:
- SP14 relation-generated support;
- SP15 asymmetry-responsive reciprocity;
- SP16 durable/non-instrumental relations;
- SP17 vulnerability/capability-sensitive care.

### Result: ACCEPT AS ORGANISING PARENT, CHILDREN REQUIRED

The four form a coherent relational orientation but cannot be losslessly collapsed.

### Boundary candidate SP18

SP18 — relational boundaries/non-transfer — tested beneath MS-P06.

### Result: RETAIN AS BRIDGE / DISTINCT CONSTRAINT

A relationship can generate care and obligation while still requiring boundaries between persons, property, responsibility and entitlement. SP18 therefore constrains MS-P06 rather than being reducible to it.
