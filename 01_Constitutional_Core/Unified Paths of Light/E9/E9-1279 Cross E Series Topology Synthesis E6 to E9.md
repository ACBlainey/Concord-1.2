# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1279 — Cross-E-Series Topology Synthesis: E6–E9

### Observed pattern

Across E6 Hindu, E7 Chinese, E8 Sikh and E9 Akan, recurring ethical functions occupy broadly overlapping regions despite different compression outcomes.

The most persistent regions concern:
- harm/protection;
- truth/reality/information;
- agency/power;
- care/support;
- resources/material relation;
- accountability/correction/repair;
- capability/development.

### Partition variation

The experiments do not require identical root counts or labels:
- E6: seven-root compression;
- E7: seven-root compression;
- E8: seven frozen functional parents with two ambiguous X recoveries;
- E9: eight minimum-stable substantive families with 7/7 X recovery.

### E9 contribution

E9 clarifies why counts can vary:
- some traditions/corpora place functions in substantive parents;
- others distribute them across parents;
- some place them in operators;
- some preserve them as structural conditions or implementations.

Thus raw root count is a weak cross-traditional statistic unless architectural role is also compared.

### Stronger comparison object

The better comparison object is the **functional topology**:
which ethical functions are present, how they connect, and at what architectural layer they are represented.
