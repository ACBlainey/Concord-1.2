# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-17 — Triangulated Translation Architecture

## Translation layers

### T0 — Primary identity layer
Christaller 1879 determines proverb identity, ordering, source-language form and variants.

### T1 — Direct aligned translation
Lange 1990 is the preferred complete English alignment where accessible.

### T2 — Historical independent translation subset
Rattray 1916 provides an independently translated subset of approximately 830 Christaller proverbs.

### T3 — Akan bilingual/context resources
Akrofi and later Akan collections may clarify language, variants and cultural use but do not alter T0 corpus membership.

### T4 — Lexical/AI aid
Used only for unresolved wording and always marked as provisional where not independently verified.

## Confidence rule
A proverb may enter the admissible ethical ledger when its functional reading is stable under the available translation evidence.

Translation disagreement affecting ethical function produces HOLD rather than selection of the interpretation most favourable to a prediction.
