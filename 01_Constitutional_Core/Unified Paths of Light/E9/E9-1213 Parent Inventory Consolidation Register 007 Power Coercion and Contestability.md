# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1213 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Register 007: Power, Coercion and Contestability

### Higher internally derived parents

**HC-P016 — Power is ethically shaped by asymmetry and contestability**

Subsumes:
- PC-P093 — coercive amplification and control asymmetry;
- PC-P082 — boundary surrender under approval pressure;
- PC-P070 — consensus pressure;
- PC-P099 — reputational pressure;
- PC-P065 — status labels used as instruments of domination.

Core structure:
> Power becomes more constraining where one party can impose consequences, define status, mobilise collective pressure or restrict exit while the affected party has reduced capacity to contest, refuse or impose reciprocal restraint.

**HC-P017 — Creating or exploiting vulnerability increases responsibility**

Subsumes:
- PC-P056 — manufactured vulnerability;
- condition-creating portions of AR551;
- AR418 coercive extraction from vulnerability;
- exploitative portions of PC-P086.

Core structure:
> Responsibility is heightened where an agent creates, amplifies or knowingly exploits another's dependency, exposure, informational deficit or inability to resist.

### Distinction

HC-P016 concerns the **structure of power**.  
HC-P017 concerns **responsibility for creating or exploiting asymmetric vulnerability**.

They are strongly related but not safely mergeable.
