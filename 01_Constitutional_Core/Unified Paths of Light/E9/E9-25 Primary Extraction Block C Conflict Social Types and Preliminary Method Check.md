## Method stress check
Three distinctions are becoming important:

### 1. Norm versus prudential strategy
Some proverbs advise effective action without clearly asserting an ethical obligation. These must not automatically enter the ethical ledger as moral rules.

### 2. Social convention versus deeper function
A proverb may encode a historically local role expectation. The local rule must be retained before any later abstraction.

### 3. Functional implication versus explicit content
Compression may later reveal shared functions, but extraction must not promote an inferred general principle beyond what the proverb supports.
