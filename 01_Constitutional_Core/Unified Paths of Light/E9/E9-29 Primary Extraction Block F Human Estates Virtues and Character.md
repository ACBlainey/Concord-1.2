# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-29 — Primary Extraction Block F: Human Estates, Virtues and Character

## Extraction focus
The human-estate and virtue material provides a more explicitly normative stratum than several earlier subject groups, but virtue vocabulary is not automatically treated as a deepest ethical root.

## Provisional functional signals
Occurrences support:
- humility and restraint of arrogance;
- patience and endurance;
- reliability;
- foresight;
- learning from experience;
- self-restraint;
- courage constrained by circumstance;
- diligence and contribution;
- moderation;
- responsiveness to correction;
- character demonstrated through conduct rather than assertion.

## Virtue-to-function rule
Character labels are retained as source-level descriptions.

Later normalisation must ask what relational or agency function the virtue performs rather than assuming each named virtue is an independent root.

## Early comparison prohibition
The presence of familiar virtue families is not evidence of correspondence with any previous E-series architecture until the Akan architecture is independently frozen.
