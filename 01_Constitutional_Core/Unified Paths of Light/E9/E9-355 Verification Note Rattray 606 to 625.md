### Provenance exception
R609 has a visible Rattray number and proverb but no Christaller identifier visible in the local working witness. None is inferred.

### Explicit glosses
R607: Rattray records an often-added antithesis stating that when truth is used in a case, the matter “dies,” meaning it is quickly settled.

R611: Rattray explains that need compels a person to travel far afield hunting or fishing to find food.

R613: Rattray explicitly glosses “do not die” as “do not give up hope.”

R616–617: Rattray's notes document emergency food practices during scarcity.

R622: Rattray explicitly glosses “poverty is stupidity” as meaning that a poor man is reckoned a fool.

### Historical-causal caution
R623 makes a historical claim linking poverty in Europe to European cloth trading in Africa. E9 does not adopt this as a verified explanation of European/African economic history. Only the source-supported functional proposition that material need can motivate outward economic activity is admitted.
