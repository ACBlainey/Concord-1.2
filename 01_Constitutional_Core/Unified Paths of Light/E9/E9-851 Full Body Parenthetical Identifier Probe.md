# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-851 — Full-Body Parenthetical-Identifier Probe

The primary local Rattray OCR was scanned across the proverb body for **every purely numeric parenthetical token**, rather than only rows captured by the earlier clean-header parser.

The scan found 771 numeric parenthetical occurrences / 758 distinct numeric values. Ten occurrences were obvious explanatory-note markers `(1)` or `(2)`, not Christaller identifiers. Removing those leaves **761 candidate numeric C-ID-style occurrences / 756 distinct values** for duplicate discovery.

This is a duplicate probe, not a substitute for the reconciled R→C table, because some numeric parentheses may occur in commentary and damaged C-IDs may fail the numeric filter.
