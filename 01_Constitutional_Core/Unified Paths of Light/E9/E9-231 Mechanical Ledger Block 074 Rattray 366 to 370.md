# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-231 — Mechanical Ledger Block 074: Rattray 366–370

366 / no Christaller identifier visible = Y — Rattray explicitly explains that the small child alone in a great house has “responsibility beyond his years.” Burden and responsibility can exceed developmental capability, creating a condition deserving care or pity.

367 / C568 = H — the child squats beside the elder. The source supplies extensive contextual commentary on posture but no sufficiently secure ethical function.

368 / C571 = Y — even a loud/quarrelsome child uses the mouth for a horn, not an enormous mortar. Capability remains constrained by actual scale despite forceful signalling or temperament.

369 / C574 and C403 = Y — a child insisting on climbing the stump may be allowed limited experience, after which the child turns back. Some mistaken capability claims can be corrected through bounded direct experience.

370 / C575 = Y — a child insisting on grasping fire learns through the immediate consequence and releases it. Direct experience can rapidly correct dangerous misunderstanding where the consequence is self-evident.
