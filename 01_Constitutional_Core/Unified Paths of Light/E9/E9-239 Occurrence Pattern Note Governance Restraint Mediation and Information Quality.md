# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-239 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Governance Restraint, Mediation and Information Quality

R380, R383, R384 and R385 produce a dense elder/governance cluster.

- R380 explicitly associates wise eldership with settling disputes.
- R383 warns that pursuing punishment for every small offence would ruin the nation.
- R384 says an elder does not follow idle rumours.
- R385 rejects implausible testimony despite the claimant's age/status.

The resulting occurrence topology combines:
- conflict mediation;
- proportional enforcement;
- resistance to low-quality information;
- refusal to substitute status for evidential credibility.

This is unusually close to a compact governance epistemology, but it remains an internally derived E9 occurrence cluster while the firewall is closed.
