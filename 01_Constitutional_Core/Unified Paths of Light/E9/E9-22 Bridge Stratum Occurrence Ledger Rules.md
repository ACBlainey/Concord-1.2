# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-22 — Bridge-Stratum Occurrence Ledger Rules

## Identifier
Bridge-stratum ethical occurrences use provisional identifiers:

**AR001, AR002, ...**

The AR number identifies an ethical occurrence, not necessarily the printed proverb number.

Each record must retain the Rattray locator and, where recoverable, the corresponding Christaller identity.

## Admission
An occurrence is admitted only where the proverb plus available translation/context supports a separable functional proposition.

## Normative classes
The E8 classes remain available:
- P prescriptive
- V virtue/character
- C consequential/causal
- D deliberative
- N narrative/exemplary
- X context-bound/non-general

## E9 additions
Each record also receives:
- mediation grade;
- translation state;
- source-form category;
- contextual dependence;
- Rattray-selection flag.

## Zero yield
Thematic headings do not create ethical evidence. Fauna, natural phenomena, religious reference, social type or other subject matter may yield zero.

## Compression firewall
Occurrence coding must remain descriptive. No E9 root family, X label or prior E-series family may be assigned during extraction.
