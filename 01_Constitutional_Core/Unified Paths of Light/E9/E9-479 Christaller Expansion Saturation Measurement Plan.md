# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-479 — Christaller Expansion Saturation Measurement Plan

The larger Christaller phase provides an opportunity to test whether the Rattray subset was already near functional saturation.

### Prospective measurements
At regular fixed-size expansion checkpoints record:
- new items processed;
- Y/Z/H/X distribution;
- new ethical occurrences;
- genuinely new functional patterns;
- repetitions/extensions of existing patterns;
- unresolved translation debt;
- Rattray overlap rate.

### Saturation signal
Evidence for increasing saturation would be:
- continuing new Y occurrences;
- but declining rate of genuinely new functional structures;
- increasing proportion of occurrences that reinforce already observed structures.

### Non-saturation signal
Evidence against saturation would be:
- persistent emergence of qualitatively new functions;
- major shifts in the provisional internal architecture;
- new high-density domains absent from Rattray.

### Discipline
No stopping rule should be retrofitted after seeing favourable convergence.

A later explicit sampling/coverage rule must be frozen before using saturation as a reason to halt expansion.
