# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-176 — Provenance Exception: Rattray 255 Missing Christaller Identifier

Rattray 255 is visibly present in the local working witness:

> If you take a dog (i.e. a quarrelsome, noisy person) as a relation, tears will never dry in your eyes.

Unlike surrounding entries, no parenthetical Christaller identifier is visible before the explanatory note.

### Rule applied
- R255 counts in the Rattray mechanical sequence.
- Its ethical yield may count in Rattray-derived occurrence analysis.
- It must **not** be treated as a mapped Christaller item unless an independent witness later supplies the identifier.
- No identifier may be reconstructed from neighbouring Christaller numbers.

This follows the same provenance discipline used for earlier exceptional items.
