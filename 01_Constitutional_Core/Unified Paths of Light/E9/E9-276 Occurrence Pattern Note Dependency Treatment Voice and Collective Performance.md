# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-276 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Dependency, Treatment, Voice and Collective Performance

R447, R457 and R459 create a coherent relationship between treatment and system performance.

- R447 traces poor conduct by a dependent back to the master.
- R457 explicitly says kind treatment influences the dependent.
- R459 says fear of speaking directly prevents dependents from gaining victories for the controller.

The cluster suggests that a controller cannot treat subordinate behaviour as independent of the environment the controller creates.

Treatment affects:
1. behaviour;
2. willingness to cooperate;
3. willingness to communicate candidly;
4. collective performance.

This is retained as an internal E9 occurrence pattern. No external governance framework is imported while the firewall remains closed.
