# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1216 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Register 010: Harm, Hazard and Protection

### Higher internally derived parents

**HC-P021 — Foreseeable serious harm supports preventive restraint**

Subsumes:
- PC-P013 — harm prevention/defence;
- PC-P051 — hazard management, proximity and buffering;
- M012 proximity-sensitive hazard;
- AR518 hazard reduction;
- AR570 avoidance of known severe hazard.

Core structure:
> Where serious harm is credibly foreseeable, conduct should respond to the probability, proximity, exposure and avoidability of the hazard rather than waiting for damage to occur.

**HC-P022 — Protection should be vulnerability-sensitive**

Subsumes:
- PC-P004 — dependency-sensitive vulnerability/care;
- PC-P009 — threat-responsive protection;
- PC-P055/PC-P076 — vulnerability despite apparent strength or status;
- PC-P066 where protective affiliation reduces predation risk.

Core structure:
> Protective response should track actual vulnerability and threat, including vulnerability hidden by status, apparent strength or temporary resilience.

**HC-P023 — Harm should not be displaced onto vulnerable or uninvolved parties**

Subsumes:
- PC-P019 — destructive exposure undermining reciprocal support;
- AR440 transfer of one's own hazard;
- AR441/450 unequal exposure;
- relevant non-transfer portions of PC-P048.

Core structure:
> Agents should not externalise avoidable harm or hazard onto parties who are vulnerable, uninvolved or disproportionately exposed.

**HC-P024 — Protective response should remain proportionate and function-preserving**

Subsumes:
- PC-P049 — proportionate correction and repair;
- PC-P094 — repair/reuse of adequate resources;
- AR106 proportionate force;
- M011 correctable degradation;
- AR493 restraint against destruction after initial failure.

Core structure:
> Response to harm, failure or degradation should be proportionate to the condition and preserve recoverable function where doing so remains viable.

### Retained bridge

PC-P077 bottlenecks/transition hazards remains a **BRIDGE** to systems architecture because it concerns where hazard concentrates structurally.
