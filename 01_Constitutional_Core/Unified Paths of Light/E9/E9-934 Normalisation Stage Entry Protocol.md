# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-934 — Normalisation-Stage Entry Protocol

E9 now enters normalisation.

The purpose of normalisation is to express each admitted occurrence in a consistent ethical form while preserving the distinction between **source occurrence** and **derived principle**.

### For each AR occurrence preserve
- AR identifier;
- source locator/provenance;
- source meaning or translation used at extraction;
- existing extraction judgement;
- uncertainty or qualification.

### Produce
- a concise normalised ethical proposition;
- actor/subject where materially relevant;
- protected interest, obligation, restraint or relational function;
- contextual qualifier where removing it would change meaning;
- candidate equivalence links to other AR occurrences.

### Do not yet
- force synonymous occurrences into one root;
- map to X1–X7;
- map to Blainey's Laws;
- import categories from E3–E8;
- force a target number of principles;
- erase culturally specific distinctions merely to improve compression.

Normalisation therefore reduces linguistic and contextual surface variation while keeping semantic distinctions available for the later duplicate and parent/child compression stages.
