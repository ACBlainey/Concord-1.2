## E9-1059 — Normalisation Ledger N084: AR345–AR349

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR345 | R561 / C1021 | Lack of knowledge about a capability is not evidence that the capability is absent or useless. | Epistemic humility / hidden capability. |
| AR346 | R562 / C1972 | Expert search and selection can require adequate time; imposed urgency can reduce intervention quality or suitability. | Time-sensitive expertise. |
| AR347 | R563 / C3124 | Threat assessment should account for practical proximity or accessibility, not merely abstract harmful capacity. | Proximity-sensitive risk. |
| AR348 | R564 / C3125 | Stated adverse and beneficial intentions provide different evidence for threat assessment and should not be treated as equivalent. | Intent-sensitive risk. |
| AR349 | R565 / C3126 | Credible threat of severe harm can alter behaviour toward protective adaptation. | Threat-responsive self-protection. |

AR347–AR349 distinguish threat capacity, accessibility, stated intent and behavioural response rather than treating danger as a single undifferentiated variable.
