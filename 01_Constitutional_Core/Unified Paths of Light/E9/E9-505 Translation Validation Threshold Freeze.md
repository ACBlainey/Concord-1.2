# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-505 — Translation Validation Threshold Freeze

The validation threshold is frozen before results are observed.

### Item-level
A translation may support provisional ethical classification only if:
- no material agent/patient reversal;
- no material negation/modality error;
- no material ownership/authority reversal;
- no material causal reversal;
- no unsupported interpretive expansion.

### Workflow-level
Before T4 is used at scale, test at least **50 independently translated items** distributed across the frozen validation strata.

Target:
- at least 90% free of material semantic error;
- 100% of detected material errors explicitly surfaced rather than hidden by fluent wording.

If the 90% threshold is not met, T4 cannot be the primary translation route for C-only extraction.

### Conservative override
Even after workflow validation, any item with TD2/TD3 remains individually gated.
