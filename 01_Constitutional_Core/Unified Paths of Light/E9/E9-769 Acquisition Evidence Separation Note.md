# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-769 — Acquisition-Evidence Separation Note

Three evidence levels are now explicitly separated:

1. **bibliographic evidence** — proves edition identity, extent, repository;
2. **digital-structure evidence** — proves canvases/images/full-view availability and order infrastructure;
3. **item evidence** — directly visible C-ID, Twi wording and variants.

Only level 3 may populate Christaller inventory rows. This prevents repeated discovery work from being mistaken for corpus coverage.
