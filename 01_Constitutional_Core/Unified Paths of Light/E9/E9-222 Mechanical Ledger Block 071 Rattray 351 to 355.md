# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-222 — Mechanical Ledger Block 071: Rattray 351–355

351 / C3564 = Y — a child is told not to laugh at a short man. Developmental immaturity does not justify ridicule of another's bodily characteristic.

352 / C581 = Y — failure to heed father and mother is said to bring misfortune. The source links developmental outcomes to receptivity toward experienced guidance.

353 / C564 = Y — when a child learns the relevant cleanliness/practice, the child can eat with elders. Demonstrated competence in an expected practice can expand participation across an age/status boundary.

354 / C583 = Y — a child cuts a portion of yam sized to fit the child's own mouth. Resource-taking should be proportionate to actual capacity to use or consume.

355 / C566 = H — a child's ring does not fit an elder, while the child's food can enter the elder's mouth. The contrast between non-transferable fit and transferable consumption is clear, but its intended ethical function is insufficiently explicit.

### AR206 — R351/C3564
Developmental immaturity does not justify ridicule of another person's physical characteristic.

### AR207 — R352/C581
Development can depend on receptivity to guidance from more experienced caregivers.

### AR208 — R353/C564
Demonstrated competence in a relevant practice can justify expanded participation despite developmental/status difference.

### AR209 — R354/C583
Resource-taking should be proportionate to the agent's actual capacity to use or consume the resource.
