# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1263 — Akan Operator and Structural Layer Freeze

### Frozen operator/condition layer

**AK-O1 — Reality/evidence responsiveness**  
Judgment should track relevant evidence and operative reality.

**AK-O2 — Bounded inference**  
Inference should respect observational and evidential limits.

**AK-O3 — Verification**  
Claims should be independently or accessibly testable where stakes require it.

**AK-O4 — Correctability/revisability**  
Judgments and practices should remain open to correction when evidence or performance warrants revision.

**AK-O5 — Reference-point specification**  
Evaluation should identify the relevant agent, population, function, domain or comparison class.

**AK-O6 — Context-sensitive implementation**  
Implementation may vary with local/material/social context without erasing the underlying constraint.

**AK-O7 — Timing sensitivity**  
Timing can alter obligation, effectiveness, hazard and opportunity.

**AK-O8 — Dependency/prerequisite sequencing**  
Action should respect dependency order where later function requires earlier enabling conditions.

**AK-O9 — Irreversibility sensitivity**  
Loss of future options or inability to recall action raises the threshold for action, verification or preservation.

**AK-O10 — Association/non-transfer boundary**  
Association alone does not justify transfer of identity, property, responsibility, entitlement or control.
