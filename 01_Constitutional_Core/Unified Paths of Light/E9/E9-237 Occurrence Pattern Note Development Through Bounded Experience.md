# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-237 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Development Through Bounded Experience

R369 and R370 form a distinctive developmental-learning pair.

In R369, the child who overestimates climbing capability is allowed to begin and then turns back.

In R370, the child who insists on grasping fire encounters the direct consequence and releases it.

These occurrences describe learning not through verbal correction alone but through **contact with reality**. The mechanism is experiential error-correction: an inaccurate belief about capability or consequence is tested against the environment.

The source does not establish that all dangerous learning should be allowed through direct exposure. The occurrence is therefore retained narrowly as evidence that bounded or immediate experience can correct some mistaken beliefs.

No compression is performed.
