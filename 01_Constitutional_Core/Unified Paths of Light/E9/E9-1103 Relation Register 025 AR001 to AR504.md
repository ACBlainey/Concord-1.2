### Emerging exploration-learning topology

The corpus now distinguishes at least four stages in collective knowledge formation:
1. **safe exploration** preserves margin under uncertainty (AR487);
2. **exploratory exposure** can generate information unavailable without testing (AR500);
3. **returned information** converts individual exposure into collective knowledge (AR500);
4. **teaching/explanation** redistributes knowledge where individual deficits remain (AR504).

This creates a possible later neighbourhood linking uncertainty, bounded risk, experience, transmission and collective capability without assuming they form one parent.

### Role separation

AR497–AR499 reinforce a recurring structural pattern: possession of local knowledge, legitimate authority, beneficiary status, ownership, contribution and allocation control are distinct roles. Later compression should avoid granting one role merely because another is present.
