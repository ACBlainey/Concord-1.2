# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-882 — Cardinality Conditional on Residual Tail

The reconstructed majority contains 800 unique IDs from 805 edges.

If all fifteen residual edges are unique relative to the 800-ID set and to one another, final cardinality is:

**800 + 15 = 815.**

Every additional duplicate discovered in the fifteen-edge tail would reduce the final cardinality by one (or more for higher multiplicity).

Thus 815 is now an explicit conditional result rather than a loose estimate.
