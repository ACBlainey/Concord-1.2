## E9-1102 — Normalisation Ledger N113: AR500–AR504

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR500 | R740 / C208 | Some uncertainties require exploratory exposure and returned information; collective knowledge can depend on agents testing unknown conditions. | Exploration / distributed learning. |
| AR501 | R741 / C213 | A minor derivative benefit does not justify allowing destruction of the substantially more valuable underlying resource. | Resource hierarchy / destructive trade-off. |
| AR502 | R742 / C214 | Goods or information should not be deliberately corrupted by mixing authentic or high-quality contents with false or inferior substitutes while representing the whole for exchange. | Integrity of represented goods/information. |
| AR503 | R743 / C221 | A component necessary to successful completion should not be excluded merely because it is one part of a larger system. | Indispensable component / system function. |
| AR504 | R745 / C265 | Individual ignorance can be corrected through distributed social knowledge when another agent can explain or teach. | Distributed knowledge / teaching. |

AR500 and AR504 show complementary collective epistemic mechanisms: new knowledge can be generated through exploration and existing knowledge can be redistributed through teaching.
