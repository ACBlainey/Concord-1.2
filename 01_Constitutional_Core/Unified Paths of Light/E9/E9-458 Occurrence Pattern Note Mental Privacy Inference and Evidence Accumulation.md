# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-458 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Mental Privacy, Inference and Evidence Accumulation

R815 and R822 form an important epistemic pair.

R815 compares ears to scales that become weighted as more is put into them. The secure abstraction is that judgment can change as information accumulates.

R822 says the head cannot be split open like a papaw fruit to inspect the thoughts within. Internal mental states are not directly observable.

Together they impose two constraints on judgment about other agents:
1. do not treat inferred internal states as directly observed facts;
2. remain willing to update judgment as additional evidence arrives.

This reinforces earlier E9 structures involving baselines, local knowledge, observation versus explanation, causal attribution and independent evaluation.

The combined pattern is strongly reality-responsive without claiming that uncertainty about another mind can always be eliminated.
