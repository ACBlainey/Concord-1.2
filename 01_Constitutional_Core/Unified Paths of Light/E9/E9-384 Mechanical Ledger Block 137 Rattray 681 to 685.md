# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-384 — Mechanical Ledger Block 137: Rattray 681–685

681 / C3073 = Y — when drowning, the person finds even the bank creepers unavailable for rescue. In severe crisis, nominally nearby resources may prove unusable; resilience depends on actually accessible support rather than mere environmental presence.

682 / C3079 = Y — a river does not flood out toucans roosting in high trees. Exposure to a common hazard differs according to position or protective elevation; shared environment does not imply equal vulnerability.

683 / C3080 = Y — clear water is not required to extinguish a fire. In urgent functional tasks, a resource can be adequate without possessing qualities irrelevant to the immediate purpose.

684 / C3086 = Y — while water is drowning a person, the person nevertheless drinks some. A resource can be simultaneously necessary/beneficial in one function and dangerous in another; evaluation must be use- and context-sensitive.

685 / C3087 = Y — if plain water fully satisfied the fish, it would not take the baited hook. Unmet need or desire can create susceptibility to risky inducements that would otherwise be rejected.

### AR449 — R681/C3073
Resilience depends on support being practically accessible during crisis, not merely present nearby.

### AR450 — R682/C3079
Agents sharing the same environment can have very different hazard exposure because of position or protective conditions.

### AR451 — R683/C3080
A resource can be functionally adequate without possessing qualities irrelevant to the immediate task.

### AR452 — R684/C3086
The same resource can be beneficial or necessary in one function while dangerous in another; evaluation must remain context-sensitive.

### AR453 — R685/C3087
Unmet need or desire can increase susceptibility to risky inducements.
