# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1048 — Normalisation Structural Note 002: Privacy, Procedure and Relational Knowledge

The current sequence adds a cluster of propositions concerning who can know, disclose, adjudicate or act within close relationships.

### 1. Situated knowledge

AR306 and AR309 indicate that intimate or close relationships can contain information unavailable to outsiders. This creates an epistemic limit on external judgment.

### 2. Privacy does not imply unrestricted internal disclosure

AR312 adds the converse constraint: even within a close relationship, not every sensitive item should automatically be disclosed if onward propagation creates risk.

Thus:
- outsiders may lack legitimate informational access;
- insiders may possess privileged knowledge;
- privileged access does not erase confidentiality obligations.

### 3. Affected-person awareness

AR305 identifies the directly affected person's awareness as relevant in an intimate bodily action. It is not reduced to the observer's or relationship partner's perspective.

### 4. Procedure as conflict transformation

AR304 introduces a different mechanism: structured adjudication can move conflict out of escalating private confrontation into investigation, public accountability and review.

### 5. Methodological significance

These occurrences suggest that later architecture testing should distinguish at least:
- **knowledge access**;
- **privacy boundary**;
- **disclosure responsibility**;
- **affected-person standing**;
- **adjudicative procedure**.

Collapsing these prematurely into a single truth or relationship category would lose source-supported functional differences.
