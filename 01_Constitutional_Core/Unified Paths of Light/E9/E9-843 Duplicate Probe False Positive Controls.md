# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-843 — Duplicate-Probe False-Positive Controls

A broader naïve scan produced apparent repetitions that disappeared when source context was inspected. Examples include OCR-spaced identifiers such as R629 `(1 359)` = C1359 rather than C359, and R252 `(l 770)` = C1770 rather than C770.

### Rule
Whitespace/OCR-normalisation must be performed before duplicate comparison, and numbers appearing in explanatory notes must not be treated as the proverb's C-ID.

This explains why unrestricted numeric searching is unsuitable for final overlap cardinality.
