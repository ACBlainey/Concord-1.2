# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1290 — Source-Access Failure and Replication Requirements

### Recorded limitation

E9 intended systematic traversal of Christaller's 1879 Akan proverb collection. Direct clean machine-readable access proved insufficient for a reproducible full reconstruction.

Rattray therefore became the operational bridge witness.

The experiment formally stopped the attempted Christaller expansion rather than converting uncertain reconstruction into apparent evidence.

### What remains valid

The frozen E9 derivation is based on the explicitly bounded working corpus:
- Rattray witness exhausted through R830;
- Y572 / Z41 / H215 / X2 = 830 classified witness items;
- AR001–AR571 = 571 admitted ethical occurrences;
- 571 normalised occurrences → 557 distinct propositions;
- subsequent compression and architecture were performed on that frozen input.

The known Y572 versus AR571 distinction remains preserved and must not be silently normalised away.

### Future replication requirements

A clean Christaller rerun should require:
1. stable full-text or page-image access;
2. deterministic proverb boundary detection;
3. serialized proverb identifiers and source text;
4. reproducible Rattray↔Christaller crosswalk where applicable;
5. explicit variant-family handling;
6. independent translation checks for ambiguous items;
7. preservation of zero-yield and HOLD records;
8. complete traversal before coverage closure;
9. a new derivation branch rather than retroactive modification of E9;
10. comparison with E9 only after the rerun architecture freezes.

### Interpretation

The source failure is itself a methodological result: **bounded incompleteness is preferable to false completeness**.
