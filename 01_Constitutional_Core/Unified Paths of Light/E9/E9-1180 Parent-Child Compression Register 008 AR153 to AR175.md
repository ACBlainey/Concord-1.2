# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1180 — Parent-Child Compression Register 008: AR153–AR175

### Existing parents extended

**PC-P031 — Boundary between capability/control and entitlement**
- AR154 — a claim concerning one individual does not extend control to a related individual.
- AR157 — a property or defect of one linked member should not be imputed to another.

These form a strong **non-transfer through relationship** sub-branch.

**PC-P009 / PC-P013 — Threat, vulnerability and harm response**
- AR153 — an agent may resist a process recognised as self-destructive.
- AR155 — outward resilience does not establish absence of vulnerability.
- AR169 — resources/power can buffer visible effects of an equal burden.

AR155 and AR169 are sibling children concerning hidden/buffered vulnerability.

**PC-P028 — Consequence propagation through relations**
- AR137 from the prior range and AR169 here reinforce propagation/buffering as distinct system effects; AR169 remains primarily under vulnerability and is marked **BRIDGE**.

**PC-P034 — Non-instructed and practice-based development**
- AR174 — capabilities/statuses can develop through direct demanding practice.
This is a distinct child beside M002.

### New provisional local parents

**PC-P037 — Causal explanation of behavioural change**
- AR156 — behavioural change can indicate an operative external cause and should be investigated.

Candidate bridge to PC-P005.

**PC-P038 — Residual moral significance after irreversible harm**
- AR158 — treatment after death can retain moral/social significance distinct from the act of killing.

**PC-P039 — Coordination and complementary plurality**
- AR159 — more participants do not guarantee greater output without conditions for concentrated progress.
- AR162 — differentiated capabilities can allow one agent to satisfy a need another cannot.

These are **SIBLINGS**: plurality alone versus complementary differentiation.

**PC-P040 — Productive means versus realised benefit**
- AR160 — access to finished benefit does not imply access/control of productive means.
Candidate bridge to PC-P008 resource-value structure.

**PC-P041 — Preference sensitivity to access/abundance**
- AR161 — abundance or repeated access can reduce desire for an ordinarily valued resource.

**PC-P042 — Strategic information boundaries**
- AR164 — operational information may be withheld where disclosure defeats the action.
- AR168 — concealment fails when revealing traces remain exposed.

**PC-P043 — Information asymmetry and inference limits**
- AR165 — an agent can act under incomplete information intentionally exploited by another.
- AR166 — lack of visible evidence does not establish absence where surface properties mask condition.
- AR167 — threat-signalling can evidence harmful intent.
- AR173 — opposed agents can have incomplete knowledge beyond observation.

This parent is intentionally broad and may split into exploitation, hidden-state inference and signal inference as more children accumulate.

**PC-P044 — Collective information resilience**
- AR175 — rumour/unreliable circulating information can damage collective resilience in crisis.
