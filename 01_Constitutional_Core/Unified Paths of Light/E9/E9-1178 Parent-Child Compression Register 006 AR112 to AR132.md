# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1178 — Parent-Child Compression Register 006: AR112–AR132

### Existing parents extended

**PC-P001 — Performance-grounded capability assessment**
- AR118 — declared ambition is not achieved capability when outcomes fall short.
- AR126 — capability satisfies needs only when exercised.
- AR132 — capability expectations should be calibrated to demonstrated performance under comparable conditions.

**PC-P010 — Capability-sensitive support**
- AR119 — protective/support function may be delegated where the primary agent has incompatible duties.
- AR142 is anticipated as a later related child, but is not imported into this range prematurely.

**PC-P017 — Communication calibrated to epistemic conditions**
- AR120 — an isolated member/trace can indicate a wider unseen collective/context.
- AR121 — vulnerable or valuable dependents can be protected by restricting visibility/accessibility.
- AR135 remains a later candidate for the information-boundary branch.

**PC-P008 — Reference-dependent sufficiency/value**
- AR122 — a smaller secured resource can be more valuable than a larger unrealised/inaccessible prospect.
- AR127 — exclusive control beyond usable capacity converts surplus into waste.
- AR129 — division cannot satisfy all claims where total resource is insufficient.

These are **SIBLINGS**, not one rule: security/access, usable capacity and total scarcity are distinct reference conditions.

### New provisional local parents

**PC-P026 — Affinity and mutual intelligibility**
- AR112 — similar dispositions, practices or interests can support mutual intelligibility.

**PC-P027 — Intent inference from voluntary conduct**
- AR115 — persistent voluntary proximity to an opportunity can evidence intention.

**PC-P028 — Consequence propagation through relations**
- AR123 — consequences can propagate beyond their point of origin.
- AR137 is anticipated as a later concrete associative-harm child.

**PC-P029 — Stewardship of sustaining conditions**
- AR124 — harming one's own sustaining environment undermines conditions of dependence.
- AR138 later tests immediate need against sustaining-resource protection.

**PC-P030 — Incremental capability/system construction**
- AR125 — durable structures or capabilities can be built incrementally over time.

**PC-P031 — Boundary between capability and entitlement**
- AR128 — prolonged exposure in one position can increase vulnerability.
- AR131 — ability to reach or exploit beyond a boundary does not create entitlement.

AR128 is retained as a **BRIDGE/HOLD** rather than a settled child because its strongest parent may ultimately be hazard/exposure rather than entitlement.
