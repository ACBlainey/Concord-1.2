# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-475 — Rattray Selection Bias and Coverage Boundary Final Note

Mechanical completion of Rattray must not be mistaken for representative completion of Christaller.

Rattray states that Christaller's 1879 work contained roughly 3,600 proverbs and that the 1916 volume presents “eight hundred odd” selected items.

Rattray also states that his selection was made chiefly to show:
1. customs, beliefs or ethical determinants of anthropological interest;
2. grammatical or syntactical constructions of linguistic interest.

The Rattray corpus is therefore a deliberately selected subset, not a random sample.

### Consequence for E9
The high ethical-yield density observed in Rattray cannot be projected mechanically onto the unprocessed Christaller remainder.

Nor can root saturation be claimed from Rattray alone.

The next phase must test whether:
- new functions continue to appear;
- existing functions merely gain density;
- H/Z proportions change;
- Rattray's selection over-represented ethically salient material.

This selection-bias boundary remains active throughout Christaller expansion.
