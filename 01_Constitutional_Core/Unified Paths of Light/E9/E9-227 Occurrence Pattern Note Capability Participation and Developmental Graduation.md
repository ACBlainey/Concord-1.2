# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-227 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Capability, Participation and Developmental Graduation

R353 and R360 provide complementary evidence about capability-sensitive participation.

R353 allows the child who has mastered the relevant practice to eat with elders. Demonstrated competence expands participation.

R360 moves in the opposite direction: a child who undertakes adult conduct encounters adult consequences.

Together they suggest an occurrence-level structure in which **participation, responsibility and consequences track demonstrated or assumed capability more closely than chronological category alone**.

This is a particularly relevant result for later E9 compression, but no comparison with the frozen X-kernel or Concord architecture is permitted while the firewall remains closed.
