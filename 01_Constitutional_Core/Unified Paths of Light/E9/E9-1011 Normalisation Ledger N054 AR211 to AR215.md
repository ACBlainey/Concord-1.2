## E9-1011 — Normalisation Ledger N054: AR211–AR215

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR211 | R358 / C578 | Dependent distress should not automatically be answered by destructive or punitive restraint. | Vulnerability-sensitive response. |
| AR212 | R359 / C587 | Departure from ordinary practice can expose an agent to consequences or information not encountered through ordinary conduct. | Novel action / novel consequence or knowledge. |
| AR213 | R360 / C586 | Assuming the actions of a higher-responsibility role can expose an agent to consequences associated with that role. | Role-linked responsibility. |
| AR214 | R362 / C554 | Participation in a task creates exposure to its characteristic risks and is relevant when tracing responsibility for resulting loss. | Activity/risk/responsibility linkage. |
| AR215 | R363 / C555 | Conduct intended to impose harm or mischief on others can rebound upon the initiating agent. | Harm rebound / self-exposure. |

AR213 and AR214 both connect participation with consequence, but at different levels: assumed role versus undertaken task.
