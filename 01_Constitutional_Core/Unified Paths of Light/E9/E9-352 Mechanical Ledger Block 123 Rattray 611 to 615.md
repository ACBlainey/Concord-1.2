# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-352 — Mechanical Ledger Block 123: Rattray 611–615

611 / C1331 = Y — want compels a person to travel far afield and sleep in the forest to hunt or fish for food. Severe need can force costly, risky or disruptive effort merely to secure basic subsistence.

612 / C1333 = Y — in want, a person eats palm nuts from the refuse heap. Scarcity can lower the quality or social acceptability of resources an agent must use to survive.

613 / C1334 = Y — “when you are in want, do not die,” explicitly glossed by Rattray as “do not give up hope.” Severe deprivation is not represented as grounds for abandoning continued effort or survival.

614 / C1335 = Y — “I am in want, so look after me” is given as a route by which some became slaves. Acute vulnerability can induce an agent to surrender major autonomy in exchange for protection or subsistence.

615 / C1337 = Y — poverty keeps a person at home and out of public affairs. Material deprivation can suppress civic/social participation even without a formal prohibition on participation.

### AR387 — R611/C1331
Severe need can compel costly, risky or disruptive effort simply to secure basic subsistence.

### AR388 — R612/C1333
Scarcity can force an agent to rely on lower-quality or normally rejected resources for survival.

### AR389 — R613/C1334
Severe deprivation need not justify abandonment of continued effort or hope of recovery.

### AR390 — R614/C1335
Acute vulnerability can induce surrender of substantial autonomy in exchange for protection or subsistence.

### AR391 — R615/C1337
Material deprivation can suppress practical participation in public affairs even without formal exclusion.
