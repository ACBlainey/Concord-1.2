The corpus is now beginning to provide repeated evidence for several distinctions without requiring imported categories:

- absolute capability versus requirement-relative sufficiency;
- overall capability versus weakest-point vulnerability;

- unconstrained preference versus behaviour under limited options;

The recurrence of these structures is useful evidence for later compression, but frequency alone will not determine parent status.
