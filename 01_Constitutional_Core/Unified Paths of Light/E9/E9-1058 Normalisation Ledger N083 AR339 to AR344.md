| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |

| AR341 | R556 / C3546 | Merit, worth or status does not eliminate vulnerability to illness. | Status-independent physical vulnerability. |
| AR342 | R557 / C3548 | A harmful condition can create secondary resource burdens in addition to its direct effects. | Cascading burden. |
