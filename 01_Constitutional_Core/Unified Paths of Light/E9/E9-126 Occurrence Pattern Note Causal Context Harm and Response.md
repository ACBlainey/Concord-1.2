# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-126 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Causal Context, Harm and Response

Rattray 167–169 forms an unusually compact causal sequence:
- disturbance can precede defensive harm;
- once injury occurs, remedy may be time-sensitive;
- harmful action may have a cause that should not be ignored.

Combined with earlier E9 occurrences concerning prevention, collateral damage and responsibility, this strengthens a recurring corpus-level concern with the causal structure surrounding harm rather than harm being treated only as an isolated event.

This observation does not excuse harmful conduct and does not convert causal explanation into normative justification. It records the source-supported distinction between cause, response and consequence.

No root compression is attempted.
