## E9-333 — Mechanical Ledger Block 116: Rattray 576–580

576 / C3169 = Y — when all deliberators deciding a verdict are enemies of the accused, there is no one positioned to find the accused innocent. Adjudication is structurally compromised when decision-makers share a hostile interest against one party.

577 / C3170 = Y — an enemy who states the case first can cause later truthful speech to be received negatively. Early hostile framing can bias subsequent interpretation even when later information is accurate.

578 / C3173 = Y — an enemy imitating another's dancing deliberately distorts the performance. Representation by a hostile observer may caricature rather than faithfully reproduce the target.

579 / C3174 = H — “there is no medicine to cure hatred.” The proverb presents hatred as highly persistent, but the absolute claim is too broad for a secure transferable occurrence without qualification.

580 / C3175 = H — hatred is expressed through seducing the adversary's wife. The saying describes retaliatory interpersonal harm in a gendered relational frame; no independent general ethical occurrence is needed.
