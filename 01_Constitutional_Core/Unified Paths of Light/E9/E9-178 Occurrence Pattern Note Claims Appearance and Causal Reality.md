## E9-178 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Claims, Appearance and Causal Reality

R251, R254 and R259 strengthen the dense E9 reality-testing cluster.

- R251: an implausible capability promise is explicitly deception.
- R254: superficial appearance generates a false four-eye claim that inspection corrects.
- R259: an observed state outside the dog's unaided capability implies another causal agent.

Together these distinguish three epistemic operations:
1. testing claims against plausible capability;
2. testing appearance against underlying structure;
3. testing outcomes against causal capability.

The recurrence is increasingly robust, but no compression is undertaken during extraction.
