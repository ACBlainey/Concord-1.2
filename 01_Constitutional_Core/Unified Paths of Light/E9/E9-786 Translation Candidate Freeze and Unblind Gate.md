## E9-786 — Translation Candidate Freeze and Unblind Gate

The 50 candidate translations must be frozen, timestamped/committed, and assigned a run identifier before historical English is reopened.

Only after freeze may the scoring context inspect Rattray/Lange answer-key translations.

No candidate wording may be silently edited after unblinding. If a correction is proposed, preserve both PRE-UNBLIND and POST-UNBLIND forms and score the pre-unblind form for validation.
