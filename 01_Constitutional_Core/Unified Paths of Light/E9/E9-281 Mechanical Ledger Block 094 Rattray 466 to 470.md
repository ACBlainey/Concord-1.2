# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-281 — Mechanical Ledger Block 094: Rattray 466–470

466 / C1115 = H — fear of reprimanding a servant is linked to receiving poor food. A supervision/accountability mechanism is suggested, but the proverb remains embedded in a coercive dependency and Rattray's Mayfair analogy is editorial rather than a secure ethical gloss.

467 / C1614 = Y — Rattray records dependents, wives and attendants being killed or entombed to accompany an important dead person. The occurrence securely exposes extreme lethal subordination in which one person's status or ritual purpose overrides another person's continued life and agency.

468 / C187 = H — no one rejoices at another person's strong slave. Envy, rivalry or comparative capability may be involved, but the transferable ethical function is not sufficiently specified.

469 / no Christaller identifier visible = H — acquisition below stated value is followed by the new owner bearing an additional debt. The saying may concern hidden liabilities in apparently advantageous transactions, but no Christaller mapping is visible and the ethical reading is not sufficiently anchored.

470 / C683 = Y — all family names are acknowledged as family names, while distinctions among members are still sought. Category-level recognition does not eliminate evaluation of differences within the category.

### AR282 — R467/C1614
A hierarchical or ritual system can subordinate a dependent person's life and agency to the status or purposes of another; source description does not itself legitimise that power.

### AR283 — R470/C683
Equal recognition of membership in a broad category need not imply that all members are identical in qualities or practical evaluation.
