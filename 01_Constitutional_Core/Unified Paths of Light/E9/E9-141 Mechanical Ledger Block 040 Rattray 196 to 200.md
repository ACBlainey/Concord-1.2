## E9-141 — Mechanical Ledger Block 040: Rattray 196–200

196 / C215 = Y — no one tracks the offensive-smelling black ant to its own hole and then complains that it stinks. Deliberately entering a known condition and then objecting to its defining characteristic weakens the complaint.

197 / C313 = Y — no one stands among black ants and picks them off. Effective intervention must account for the danger created by entering the hostile environment itself.

198 / C1539 = Y — when an egg falls among black ants, leave it there because they cannot harm it. Apparent exposure to many hostile agents does not imply actual vulnerability when those agents lack the relevant capability.

199 / C363 = Y — no one sells a laying hen without a good reason. Voluntary disposal of a productive asset is represented as presumptively reason-responsive rather than arbitrary.

200 / C1653 = H — the fowl appears to show water to the Supreme Being before drinking. Rattray identifies this as an observation-derived “pretty idea” but supplies no transferable ethical interpretation.
