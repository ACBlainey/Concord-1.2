# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-213 — Mechanical Ledger Block 068: Rattray 336–340

336 / C3392 = Y — a gun with a failed lock is functionally like a stick. Nominal identity or possession of most components does not preserve capability when a critical subsystem fails.

337 / C3394 = Y — a shot that wounds one animal does not thereby cause pain in another. Harm should be attributed to the actually affected target rather than transferred by association.

338 / C3395 = Y — a soldier absent from one flank but found contributing at the forefront has not necessarily gone astray. Apparent deviation from assigned position should be evaluated against actual functional contribution.

339 / C3422 = Y — the gun's performance is credited to the flint-stone, explicitly cross-referenced to R332. Small enabling components can be indispensable to the capability of a larger system.

340 / C6 = Y — a child expected to develop well is not reared entirely on even a beautiful mat. Development requires more than comfort or passive protection.

### AR192 — R336/C3392
Failure of a critical subsystem can remove the functional capability of an otherwise intact tool or system.

### AR193 — R337/C3394
Harm attribution should track the actually affected target rather than being transferred to an unaffected associated party.

### AR194 — R338/C3395
Departure from an expected position should be evaluated against actual functional contribution before being classified as failure or misdirection.

### AR195 — R339/C3422
A small enabling component may be indispensable to the realised capability of a larger system.

### AR196 — R340/C6
Healthy development requires more than comfort or passive protection alone.
