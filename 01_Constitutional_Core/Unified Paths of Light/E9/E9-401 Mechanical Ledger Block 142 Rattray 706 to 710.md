# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-401 — Mechanical Ledger Block 142: Rattray 706–710

706 / C121 = Y — an old thing belonging to one person becomes new to another. Utility and experienced novelty can depend on the receiving agent's prior access rather than the object's absolute age.

707 / C126 = Y — if another person killed something by mistake, one should not then “flay it by mistake.” Another agent's error does not excuse a subsequent deliberate or avoidable act by oneself; responsibility remains agent-specific.

708 / C127 = Y — being told one resembles one's father should not immediately be treated as praise because the referenced trait may be undesirable. Ambiguous positive-seeming feedback should be interpreted only after the comparison's relevant content is known.

709 / C128 = Y — ownership of a very large plantation does not license others to arrive with bowls and loot it. Resource abundance does not erase ownership boundaries or create unrestricted entitlement in others.

710 / C130 = Y — standing on another person's shoulders permits one to see over the market. Cooperative support can expand an agent's effective capability beyond what the agent could achieve unaided.
