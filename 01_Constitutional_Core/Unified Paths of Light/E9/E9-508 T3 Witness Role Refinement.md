# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-508 — T3 Witness Role Refinement

T3 witnesses are now assigned three distinct roles.

### T3-A — lexical/context triangulation
Use later bilingual collections to clarify words, idioms and cultural usage.

### T3-B — independent translation validation
Where the same proverb can be securely identified, compare its English rendering against Rattray/Lange/fallback translation.

### T3-C — variant evidence
Where wording differs materially, record the later form as a variant rather than forcing identity.

### Non-role
T3 is not automatically a replacement numbered translation of Christaller.

A proverb that “looks similar” is not enough. Alignment must be evidenced.

This prevents a large later Akan collection from silently changing the frozen 1879 corpus boundary.
