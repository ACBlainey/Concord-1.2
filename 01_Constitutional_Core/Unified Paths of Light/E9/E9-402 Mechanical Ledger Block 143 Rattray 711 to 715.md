# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-402 — Mechanical Ledger Block 143: Rattray 711–715

711 / C134 = Y — one person's road does not go far without meeting another's. Independent courses of action eventually intersect; agents cannot assume indefinite isolation from the effects, claims or presence of others.

712 / C135 = Y — a recipient thanks the giver of a present. Rattray explicitly interprets the idiom as recognition of obligation to the giver. Receipt of voluntary benefit can generate acknowledgement or reciprocal social obligation.

713 / C137 = Y — no one boasts of what belongs to another. Credit or status claims should not be grounded in resources or achievements belonging to someone else.

714 / C138 = Y — a whole unprepared animal is not dried over a fire; Rattray explains that it must first be flayed and cut for drying/roasting. A process can require appropriate preparation before the final treatment can work effectively.

715 / C140 = H — a propitiatory offering left to remove harm is not subsequently retrieved. The source clearly concerns completion/finality of a ritual act, but transfer beyond its ritual frame is not sufficiently secure.

### AR477 — R711/C134
Independent courses of action can intersect; agents should not assume indefinite isolation from others.

### AR478 — R712/C135
Receipt of a voluntary benefit can appropriately generate acknowledgement of the giver and a recognised social obligation.

### AR479 — R713/C137
Credit, status or self-praise should not be grounded in resources or achievements belonging to another agent.

### AR480 — R714/C138
Some processes require appropriate preparation before the intended final treatment can function effectively.
