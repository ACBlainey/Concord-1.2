## E9-51 — Source-Access Gate Resolution: Cornell Internet Archive Witness

## Resolution
A second Internet Archive identifier supplied by the researcher successfully exposes the complete OCR text:

`cu31924027161961`

Machine-readable witness:

`https://archive.org/stream/cu31924027161961/cu31924027161961_djvu.txt`

The retrieved object contains 10,519 indexed text lines and identifies the Cornell University Library copy of R. Sutherland Rattray's *Ashanti Proverbs*, Clarendon Press, 1916.

## Integrity checks passed
- correct title and author;
- 1916 Clarendon Press edition;
- preface and author's note present;
- numbered proverb text present;
- Twi forms and English translations present;
- Christaller numbers printed after individual proverbs;
- explanatory notes present;
- terminal matter present.

Rattray explicitly states that Christaller's 1879 book contained about 3,600 proverbs, was entirely vernacular, and that Rattray selected “eight hundred odd” sayings. He also states that the numbers printed after each proverb are their identifiers in Christaller's original collection.
