## E9-1081 — Normalisation Ledger N098: AR419–AR423

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR419 | R646 / C116 | Prolonged material deprivation can constitute or be experienced as harm of exceptional severity. | Severity of deprivation. |
| AR420 | R647 / C919 | Stated dislike of an inaccessible good can reflect constrained access rather than an independent preference. | Preference/access confounding. |
| AR421 | R648 / C2960 | Material wealth can confer disproportionate social or deliberative influence independently of the intrinsic quality of an agent's contribution. | Wealth-conditioned influence. |
| AR422 | R649 / C917 | Practical resource value depends partly on usable function rather than physical possession alone. | Functional resource value. |
| AR423 | R650 / C2935 | Resource strategies available to surplus holders may be unavailable to agents whose resources are only sufficient for basic needs. | Surplus-sensitive resource strategy. |

AR420 extends the effective-agency distinction into preference interpretation: constrained opportunity can alter what an agent reports wanting, so expressed preference and unconstrained preference should not automatically be equated.
