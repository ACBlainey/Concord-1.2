# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-331 — Mechanical Ledger Block 114: Rattray 566–570

566 / C3127 = Y — agents who face physical battle are represented as still less intimidated by verbal confrontation. Capability or resilience developed under severe challenge can alter response to lesser forms of opposition.

567 / C3128 = Y — fear of unpopularity leads to lending a knife for a use that destroys its utility. Rattray explicitly glosses this as weak popularity-seeking inviting exploitation. The secure occurrence is that surrendering necessary boundaries merely to preserve approval can expose an agent and resources to misuse.

568 / C3130 = Y — fear or reluctance to perform an unpleasant necessary task causes repeated exposure to the unresolved problem. Avoidance can prolong contact with the very condition one is reluctant to address.

569 / C3131 = Y — fear of participating in public disputes leads to a smaller share of the associated fees. Avoiding a burdensome collective process can also mean forgoing benefits attached to participation.

570 / C3132 = H — fear of marrying a chief is linked to bearing a “nameless” child. Status, marriage and lineage recognition are entangled, but the transferable ethical function is insufficiently secure.
