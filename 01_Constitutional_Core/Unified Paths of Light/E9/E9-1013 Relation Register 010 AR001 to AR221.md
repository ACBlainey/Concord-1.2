### Developmental structure becoming clearer

The corpus now independently distinguishes several conditions of development:
- guidance must be available;
- the recipient must be receptive;
- competence can expand participation;
- burden can exceed developmental capability;
- direct experience can correct mistaken self-assessment;
- development requires more than passive protection;
- corrective feedback may be part of care.

These remain children/neighbourhood candidates, not a compressed parent.
