### R747 mediation warning
Rattray explicitly resists a straightforward literal interpretation of “No one wishes well for another,” supplying a broad evolutionary/social explanation and characterising the life and mentality of “primitive man.” Those claims are recorder interpretation, not mechanically adopted source evidence. Because the proverb's transferable meaning is not secure without relying on that interpretation, R747 remains H.

### R751 mediation warning
Rattray adds a racialised generalisation about “the African mind” and climate. This is quarantined as 1916 recorder commentary. The admitted occurrence derives only from the proverb's capability-comparison structure.
