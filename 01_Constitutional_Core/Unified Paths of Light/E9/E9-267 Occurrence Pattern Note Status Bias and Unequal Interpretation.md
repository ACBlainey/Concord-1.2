# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-267 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Status Bias and Unequal Interpretation

R431 is unusually explicit evidence that social status can change how the same apparent condition is described.

A noble person displaying what would otherwise be called madness is instead said to be merely affected by wine.

The occurrence therefore separates:
1. the observed condition or behaviour; and
2. the social interpretation assigned to it.

Status can alter the second without establishing that the first has changed.

This is useful for later analysis of epistemic reliability and unequal standards, but no cross-tradition comparison is made while the E9 firewall remains closed.
