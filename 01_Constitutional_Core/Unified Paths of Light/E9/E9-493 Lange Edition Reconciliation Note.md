# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-493 — Lange Edition Reconciliation Note

Search results expose several publication dates for the Lange translation.

### 1990 edition
Edwin Mellen Press bibliographic records describe a 1990 edition, approximately x + 302 pages, ISBN 0-88946-234-8.

### 1999/2000 edition
Mellen and library records describe a later edition/reissue:
- ISBN 0-7734-7855-8;
- approximately xi + 306 or 323 pages depending on catalogue convention;
- new foreword by Bishop Peter Kwasi Sarpong;
- described as originally published by Mellen in 1990.

### E9 treatment
These are not treated as separate proverb corpora.

The 1990 and 1999/2000 records are treated as editions/reissues of the same Lange translation unless item-level comparison later demonstrates substantive differences.

Edition-specific page numbers must therefore be recorded with edition identity whenever used.
