### Compound checks

P013 and P015 remain separate: viable choice and preservation of residual agency are not equivalent.

P017 cannot be collapsed into P023: exploitation requires asymmetric use of vulnerability; harm displacement does not.

P019 cannot be reduced to an epistemic operator because the corpus applies status-neutrality to dignity, standing and responsibility as well as evidence.
