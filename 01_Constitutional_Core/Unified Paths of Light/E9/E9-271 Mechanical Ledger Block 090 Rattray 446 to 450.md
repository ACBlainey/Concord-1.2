# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-271 — Mechanical Ledger Block 090: Rattray 446–450

446 / C1612 = Y — unusual costly behaviour by a dependent is treated as evidence of serious hidden trouble or undisclosed circumstances. Observable departures from ordinary resource use can warrant investigation rather than surface judgment.

447 / C1626 = Y — when the dependent is not well behaved, the cause is traced to the master. Conduct within a strongly controlled dependency can partly reflect the quality of the controlling or supervisory relationship.

448 / C1619 = H — Rattray states that the master was held absolutely responsible for every act of the slave, including murder. This is clear historical responsibility doctrine, but automatic transfer of all responsibility from actor to controller is too institution-specific to admit as a general ethical occurrence without qualification.

449 / C1620 = Y — capable service can lead to permission to retain one's own earnings. Demonstrated contribution can expand control over the fruits of one's labour within an otherwise constrained relationship.

450 / C1621 = Y — capable service can lead to succession to the master's property. Demonstrated competence can alter practical standing and access to responsibility/resources despite low initial status.

### AR265 — R446/C1612

### AR266 — R447/C1626

### AR267 — R449/C1620
Demonstrated contribution can support increased control over the fruits of one's own labour.

### AR268 — R450/C1621
Demonstrated competence can expand practical standing, responsibility and resource access despite initially subordinate status.
