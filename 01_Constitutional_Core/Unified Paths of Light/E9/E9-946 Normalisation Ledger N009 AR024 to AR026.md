## E9-946 — Normalisation Ledger N009: AR024–AR026

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR024 | R90 / C3028 | Capability is multidimensional; an agent weaker in one visible dimension may exceed a stronger agent through another capacity relevant to the task. | Capability comparison without single-axis ranking. |
| AR025 | R91 / no printed C-ID | Experience and judgement can outweigh physical magnitude when assessing practical competence or seniority. | Experience-sensitive competence; close but not identical to AR012 and AR024. |
| AR026 | R94 / C1515 | High overall power does not eliminate vulnerability; a relatively small or local harm can seriously impair a powerful agent. | Vulnerability under power; avoid equating strength with invulnerability. |

### Local architecture signal

AR024–AR026 strongly resist one-dimensional evaluation of agents. They separately encode:
- multidimensional capability;
- experience as a competence dimension;
- residual vulnerability despite power.

They are retained separately for later consolidation.
