# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1219 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Register 012: Relationship, Reciprocity and Care

### Higher internally derived parents

**HC-P025 — Relationships can generate differentiated obligations of support**

Subsumes:
- PC-P003 — reciprocity-support relation;
- PC-P054 — reciprocity adjusted to capability;
- AR284/293 graded relational proximity;
- AR296 dignity obligation within close relation;
- AR363 trusted relationship as assistance under vulnerability.

Core structure:
> Social relationships can generate obligations of care, support, acknowledgement or reciprocity whose form may vary with proximity, capability, need and the history of the relation.

**HC-P026 — Reciprocity should remain responsive to asymmetry**

Subsumes:
- PC-P054;
- AR234/243 capability-sensitive reciprocity;
- AR478 benefit-generated reciprocal obligation;
- AR511 asymmetrically imposed reciprocal norm;
- AR531 formally equal exchange under unequal capability.

Core structure:
> Reciprocity is not secured by identical treatment alone; relevant asymmetries in capability, burden, benefit and permitted conduct affect whether an exchange or reciprocal norm is substantively reciprocal.

**HC-P027 — Durable relationships should not be treated as purely replaceable instruments**

Subsumes:
- PC-P068 — relational durability under stress;
- AR285/297/300/360/403/411;
- PC-P063 where treatment affects cooperation within dependency.

Core structure:
> Established relationships can retain significance through disadvantage, defect, hostility or adversity and should not be evaluated solely by immediate instrumental advantage.

### Boundary

HC-P025 concerns obligations generated by relation.  
HC-P026 concerns fairness/structure within reciprocal exchange.  
HC-P027 concerns persistence and non-instrumental durability.

They remain siblings.
