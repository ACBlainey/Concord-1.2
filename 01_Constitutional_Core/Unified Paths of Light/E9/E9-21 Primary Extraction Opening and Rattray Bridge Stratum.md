# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-21 — Primary Extraction Opening and Rattray Bridge Stratum

## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION OPEN**

Systematic E9-A extraction begins with a controlled **bridge stratum**: the approximately 830 Christaller proverbs independently selected, translated and annotated by R. S. Rattray in 1916.

This does not replace the frozen Christaller 1879 corpus. It supplies the first translation-secure traversal window into it.

## Why begin here
Rattray explicitly states that his material was selected from Christaller's collection and that his own task was translation/commentary. The volume presents Twi with English translations and is publicly accessible.

This permits E9 to test the oral-medium coding protocol on source-linked material before expanding into Christaller entries for which translation evidence is weaker.

## Anti-selection warning
Rattray's 830 are **not** treated as a representative sample of all 3,600 Christaller proverbs. Rattray's own selection is a historical selection layer and must be retained as such.

No whole-Akan root conclusion may be drawn from this stratum alone.

## Extraction order
The bridge stratum will be traversed in Rattray's published order, including material whose subject appears ethically irrelevant.

Zero-yield passages remain part of coverage.
