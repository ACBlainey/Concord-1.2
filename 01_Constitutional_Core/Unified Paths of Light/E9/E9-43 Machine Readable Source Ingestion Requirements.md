# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-43 — Machine-Readable Source Ingestion Requirements

## Minimum requirement
Mechanical reconstruction may begin once the complete Rattray source is available to the extraction process as one of:
- searchable plain text;
- searchable PDF;
- complete page images with reliable page-by-page reading;
- another complete representation preserving printed order.

## Validation before coding
The ingested representation must be checked for:
1. title/edition identity;
2. beginning and terminal pages;
3. continuity of proverb numbering/sequence where printed;
4. missing-page detection;
5. OCR damage to Twi and English;
6. separation of proverb text from notes and headings.

## OCR rule
OCR may aid navigation but cannot silently repair source text. Ambiguous readings affecting function become HOLD and should be checked against page image.

## Provenance
The exact digital witness and access route used for reconstruction must be recorded before AR001 is instantiated.
