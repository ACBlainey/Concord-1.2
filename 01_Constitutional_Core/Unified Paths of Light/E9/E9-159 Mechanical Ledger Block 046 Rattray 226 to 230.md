## E9-159 — Mechanical Ledger Block 046: Rattray 226–230

226 / C513 = Y — the vulture advises the hyena, which heeds it. Rattray's attached story explains that the vulture has observed the hyena secretly eating its mother's corpse; fear of disclosure causes the hyena to end the delay and permit burial. Rattray then explicitly states that persons of similar natures and tastes soon mutually understand one another.

227 / C514 = Y — the vulture has no gun but sells elephant tusks; the note explains that vultures locate dead elephants and other game from above. Access to a valuable outcome can arise from specialised information/location capability rather than from possessing the conventional production tool.

228 / C516 = Y — the vulture says it remains on the dung-hill to avoid payment for what it eats. Behaviour can be strategically organised to obtain resources while avoiding associated liabilities or costs.

229 / C517 = H — the vulture consumes everyone's excrement while no one consumes its own. Asymmetry is explicit, but the source gives no interpretation sufficient to determine whether reciprocity, privilege, ecological role, or another function is intended.

230 / C1679 = H — the vulture has neither a good name nor a good smell. Reputation and physical condition are juxtaposed, but the source supplies no transferable ethical application.
