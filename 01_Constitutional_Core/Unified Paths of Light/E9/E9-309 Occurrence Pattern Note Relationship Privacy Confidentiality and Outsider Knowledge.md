# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-309 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Relationship Privacy, Confidentiality and Outsider Knowledge

R512 and R516 reveal two different informational boundaries.

R512 says outsiders do not know the conversation between husband and wife. Intimate relationships can contain genuinely private informational domains.

R516 adds a different constraint: information too sensitive for public speech should not automatically be disclosed merely because the listener is one's spouse.

Thus relationship privacy has two directions:
- outsiders may lack legitimate or practical access to internal information;
- insiders still face responsibility over what sensitive information they disclose to one another.

Privacy and confidentiality are therefore related but not identical functions within this occurrence cluster.
