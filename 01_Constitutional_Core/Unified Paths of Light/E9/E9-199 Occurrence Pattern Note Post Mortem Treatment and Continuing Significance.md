## E9-199 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Post-Mortem Treatment and Continuing Significance

R291 is unusual in the E9 corpus so far.

The mouse distinguishes being killed from being thrown to the ground after death, describing the latter as the greater hurt.

Whatever metaphysical assumptions may underlie the saying, the source itself clearly treats **post-mortem treatment as socially or morally significant rather than irrelevant once biological life has ended**.

This occurrence should be preserved carefully for later compression because it may concern dignity, treatment of remains, social identity, or another deeper function. The current source does not justify deciding among those possibilities.

Accordingly, the AR remains deliberately minimal.
