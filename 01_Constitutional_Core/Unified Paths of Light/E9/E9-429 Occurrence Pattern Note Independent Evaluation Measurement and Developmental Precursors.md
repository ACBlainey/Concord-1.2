# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-429 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Independent Evaluation, Measurement and Developmental Precursors

R758, R761 and R765 concern different ways of evaluating states that cannot be established merely by assertion.

R758: a small measure can be used to evaluate something larger.

R761: beauty/quality is more credibly spoken by another than self-proclaimed.

R765: fruit presupposes the prior flowering stage.

These suggest three distinct evidential structures:
- **measurement:** infer larger properties through an appropriate reference;
- **independent recognition:** reduce self-evaluation bias through external assessment;
- **developmental evidence:** mature outcomes imply or require precursor stages.

The source is therefore continuing to generate not just substantive norms but methods for deciding whether claims about quality, scale and development are warranted.
