# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-151 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Status, Identity and Functional Substitution

R213–215 add three distinct warnings against confusing form with underlying function.

- R213: present status does not erase humble/dependent origin.
- R214: an identifying marker cannot simply be moved to another entity and thereby make the second entity the first.
- R215: loss of one specific route for imposing a consequence does not remove the consequence when another route can perform the same function.

These extend the strong E9 recurrence around actual properties, causal function and identity rather than surface form, label or mechanism.
