# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-368 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Resource Scarcity, Burden, Repair and Residual Agency

R632, R636, R639 and R640 distinguish several consequences of low resource capacity.

R632: a small nominal expenditure can represent a large practical burden.

R636: even when material resources are absent, communicative capability remains available for negotiating debt.

R639: repairing an old object can preserve useful function without obtaining a prestigious replacement.

R640: deprivation can remove choice over a basic condition such as where one sleeps.

This prevents “poverty” from being represented as a single undifferentiated state.

The source distinguishes:
- increased marginal cost of expenditure;
- retained non-material agency;
- adaptive conservation/repair;
- loss of practical choice.

Thus constrained material capability can coexist with meaningful residual agency and adaptive behaviour.
