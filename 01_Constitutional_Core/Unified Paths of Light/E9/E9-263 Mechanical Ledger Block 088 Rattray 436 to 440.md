# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-263 — Mechanical Ledger Block 088: Rattray 436–440

436 / C840 = H — among royalty no one is a child. The statement concerns status and developmental classification, but whether descriptive, responsibility-imposing or ideological is not sufficiently established.

437 / C841 = Y — an ancient name cannot be cooked and eaten; money is what matters. Inherited prestige cannot substitute for resources needed to satisfy material needs.

438 / C842 = H — a person of royal blood does not need their name proclaimed. Status recognition is asserted, but the transferable ethical function remains unclear.

439 / C843 = Y — Rattray explains that the saying is used to answer a reprimand for delay: there is still plenty of time and no hurry. Perceived long time horizons can reduce urgency in fulfilling an outstanding obligation.

440 / C844 = H — when the free man is present, the slave does not take command; Rattray immediately notes that this is not strictly accurate and that slaves sometimes succeeded instead. Because the source itself qualifies the rule and the saying encodes historical hierarchy, it remains H.
