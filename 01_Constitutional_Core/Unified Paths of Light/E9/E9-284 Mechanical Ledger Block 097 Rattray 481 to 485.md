# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-284 — Mechanical Ledger Block 097: Rattray 481–485

481 / C1997 = Y — Rattray's attached story explains that the salamander generated near-war between two peoples by giving each false reports that the other intended attack. False information strategically supplied across groups can manufacture conflict. The story also records eventual discovery and accountability for the false reports.

482 / C1238 = H — a child may not tell a father to stop so the child can instruct him. The saying records age/kinship authority over speech, but its normative transferability is unclear.

483 / C1239 = Y — benefit to paternal half-siblings is welcomed, but benefit to children of one's own mother is welcomed more strongly. Care or preference can vary with perceived relational proximity.

484 / C1243 = Y — a child repeatedly treating parents' possessions as freely available develops into stealing. Failure to respect ownership boundaries in close relationships can generalise into wrongful taking.

485 / C1244 = Y — when a father's slave cuts down a tree, the observer dismisses the achievement by saying the wood was easy to cut. Social distance or status can bias evaluation downward by attributing another's accomplishment to task ease rather than capability or effort.

### AR292 — R481/C1997
Strategically false reports about another group's hostile intentions can manufacture intergroup conflict; information integrity is therefore materially relevant to peace.

### AR293 — R483/C1239
Care and preference can vary with perceived relational proximity even where goodwill extends across the broader kin group.

### AR294 — R484/C1243
Failure to maintain ownership boundaries within close relationships can cultivate patterns of wrongful taking beyond those relationships.

### AR295 — R485/C1244
Status or relational bias can discount another agent's achievement by attributing success to task ease rather than capability or effort.
