# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-221 — Mechanical Ledger Block 070: Rattray 346–350

346 / C13 = Y — a bad child does not take advice. Development can fail when corrective guidance is not received or acted upon.

347 / C2124 = Y — Rattray explicitly says this concerns a foolish person who must have everything seen and desired. Unregulated desire can produce indiscriminate acquisition rather than selective, reasoned choice.

348 / C557 = Y — a child can break a snail but not a tortoise. Effective action depends on the resistance and properties of the target, not merely the actor's intention.

349 / C2846 = Y — an unhandled knife blade frees itself from a child's hand by cutting the child. Hazardous objects can impose consequences when handled without the protective interface required for safe use.

350 / C573 = Y — it is not difficult to fill a child's hand. Needs or satisfiable demands can be capability/scale-sensitive; what is sufficient depends partly on the recipient's scale.

### AR201 — R346/C13
Developmental guidance cannot perform its corrective function when the recipient refuses or fails to receive it.

### AR202 — R347/C2124
Unregulated desire can produce indiscriminate acquisition rather than reasoned selection among wants.

### AR203 — R348/C557
An agent's effective capability depends partly on the resistance and properties of the target.

### AR204 — R349/C2846
Safe use of a hazardous capability can depend on protective interfaces; absent those safeguards, the capability may injure the user.

### AR205 — R350/C573
What counts as sufficient provision can depend on the scale or capacity of the recipient.
