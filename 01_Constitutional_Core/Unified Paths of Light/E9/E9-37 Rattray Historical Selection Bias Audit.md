# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-37 — Rattray Historical Selection-Bias Audit

## Finding
The bridge stratum is not a neutral sample of Christaller.

Rattray states that the selected sayings were chosen to show either customs/beliefs/ethical determinants of anthropological interest or grammatical/syntactic constructions of linguistic interest.

Secondary historical discussion also reports that Rattray excluded material he considered affected by European influence.

## Consequence
The bridge stratum may contain:
- enrichment for culturally salient or ethically interesting material;
- enrichment for linguistically unusual material;
- depletion of material Rattray judged externally influenced.

It therefore cannot be used to estimate whole-Christaller ethical frequencies without correction.

## Experimental use
The bridge remains valuable for:
- validating the coding method;
- establishing translation-secure occurrences;
- identifying interpretation problems;
- testing provenance fields.

It is not sufficient by itself for the final Akan architecture.
