# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1177 — Parent-Child Compression Register 005: AR095–AR111

### Existing parents receiving children

**PC-P012 — Property-responsive classification and treatment**
- AR097 — environmental alteration of outward condition need not alter identity.
- AR103 — transfer/reassignment of an identifying marker does not establish identity.

Both are **CHILDREN** of the appearance/property/identity distinction already represented by AR065 and AR073.

**PC-P001 — Performance-grounded capability assessment**
- AR099 — possession of knowledge does not itself establish authorised role.
- AR113 — specialised information/detection can itself constitute relevant capability.

These extend PC-P001 toward separation of knowledge, capability and role. AR099 is also a **BRIDGE** to authority structure.

**PC-P009 / PC-P013 — Threat-responsive protection / harm response**
- AR100 — impaired awareness does not remove a continuing threat.
- AR105 — protective characteristics can raise the cost or difficulty of exploitation.
- AR106 — force should be proportionate to vulnerability and intended outcome.

AR106 is a **BRIDGE** between protection and proportional harm constraint.

### New provisional local parents

**PC-P022 — Status humility and anti-superiority constraint**
- AR102 — increased status/development should not erase memory of dependent/modest origins.
- AR107 — shared fundamental origin constrains superiority claims.

Relation: **SIBLING**. The grounds differ: developmental memory versus common origin.

**PC-P023 — Mechanism-independent consequence**
- AR104 — absence of one consequence mechanism does not establish immunity where an equivalent mechanism remains.

**PC-P024 — Timing-sensitive obligation**
- M004/AR108 — predictable approaching return/access can make immediate pursuit unnecessary.
- AR109 — timely fulfilment prevents avoidable cost being shifted onto an accountable party.

Relation: **SIBLING**. Both are timing-sensitive, but one supports restraint and the other prompt fulfilment.

**PC-P025 — Reciprocity generated by provision**
- AR111 — hospitality/material provision can create an expectation of future reciprocity.

PC-P025 is a candidate child/extension of PC-P003 but remains separate until wider reciprocity material is incorporated.
