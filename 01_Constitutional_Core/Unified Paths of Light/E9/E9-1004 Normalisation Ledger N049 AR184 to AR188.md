## E9-1004 — Normalisation Ledger N049: AR184–AR188

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR184 | R327 / C605 | Continued effort may reasonably cease when a condition necessary for productive success fails to materialise. | Rational stopping / enabling-condition failure. |
| AR185 | R329 / C3062 | Credit for successful performance should include enabling protective infrastructure when that infrastructure materially made the outcome possible. | Distributed causal credit. |
| AR186 | R330 / C1262 | Receiving or transporting an achieved outcome is not equivalent to performing the productive activity that generated it. | Benefit/activity distinction. |
| AR187 | R331 / C3589 | Claims about events without independent observers have reduced external verifiability. | Witness independence / epistemic confidence. |
| AR188 | R332 / C3388 | A tool's realised capability can depend on an agent who activates or directs it. | Agent-tool dependency. |

AR181, AR185 and AR186 together begin to distinguish attribution by actual productive contribution from mere possession, receipt or visibility.
