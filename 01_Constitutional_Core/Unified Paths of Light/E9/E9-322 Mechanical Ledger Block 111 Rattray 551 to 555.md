# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-322 — Mechanical Ledger Block 111: Rattray 551–555

551 / C1691 = Y — the local OCR prints the item number as 651. Even under hunger, a person does not use both hands for eating. Urgent need does not necessarily suspend all behavioural constraint or ordinary manner of action.

552 / C1692 = Y — between hunger and debt, debt is said to be preferable. Immediate basic need can be treated as more urgent than a deferred financial liability.

553 / C3540 = Y — when severe illness finally arrives, the patient forgets the doctor who could have cured it. Crisis can impair or displace timely use of available remedial capability; intervention may matter before the condition becomes overwhelming.

554 / C3543 = H — Rattray tentatively interprets the saying through fear of disability-related insult and describes exclusion of disabled people from chieftaincy and historical killing of an infant born with six fingers. The material is important evidence of stigma and lethal discrimination, but the proverb's intended lesson is explicitly uncertain (“perhaps”), so no general ethical occurrence is admitted here.

555 / C3545 = Y — the physician's fee is not paid until the sickness is over. Compensation is tied to completion or demonstrated outcome rather than merely to commencement of treatment.

### AR337 — R551/C1691

### AR338 — R552/C1692
Immediate basic need can take practical priority over a deferred financial liability.

### AR339 — R553/C3540
Waiting until a crisis becomes overwhelming can impair effective use of remedial capability that was available earlier.

### AR340 — R555/C3545
Compensation can be made contingent on completion or demonstrated outcome rather than merely on attempted service.
