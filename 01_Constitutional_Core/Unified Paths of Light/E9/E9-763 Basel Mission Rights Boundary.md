# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-763 — Basel Mission Rights Boundary

The Basel Mission Archives catalogue states that images in its collection are controlled by the archive and may carry publication-use conditions.

### Consequence
BMA is useful for provenance, witness triangulation and potentially reading difficult passages, but the public-domain Library of Congress 1879 scan remains preferable for corpus reproduction and mechanical transcription whenever accessible.

No BMA image is treated as freely reusable merely because the underlying 1879 text is public domain.
