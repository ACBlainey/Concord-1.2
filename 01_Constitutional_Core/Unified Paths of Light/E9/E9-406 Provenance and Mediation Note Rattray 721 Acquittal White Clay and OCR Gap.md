# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-406 — Provenance and Mediation Note: Rattray 721 — Acquittal, White Clay and OCR Gap

The local OCR omits the Rattray item number for the proverb carrying Christaller identifier C150.

The item appears:
- immediately after R720/C149;
- immediately before R722/C155;
- as a complete proverb with translation and explanatory note.

It is therefore reconstructed as R721.

### Source meaning
The proverb says one does not take white clay and follow someone who has run into the forest.

Rattray explains:
- white clay is used ceremonially;
- one use is marking a person accused of a crime who has been acquitted;
- the person who fled to the forest in this proverb had instead been found guilty and escaped punishment.

The secure occurrence is consequently conditional recognition: a marker associated with acquittal is inappropriate when acquittal did not occur.

### Mediation boundary
Rattray additionally records a belief that the Milky Way is white with clay-decked bodies of the dead. This is ethnographic commentary and is not needed for the occurrence classification.

The OCR number defect and the cultural explanation remain preserved separately from the extracted abstraction.
