# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-328 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Remedy, Expertise, Timing and Agent Participation

R553, R558, R560, R561 and R562 form a strong remedy architecture.

- R553 warns that severe crisis can displace timely recourse to the person capable of helping.
- R558 says the physician cannot take the patient's medicine for the patient.
- R560 rejects an unsuitable treatment for the condition.
- R561 warns against dismissing useful medicinal capability merely through ignorance.
- R562 says the expert requires time to find the suitable treatment.

Together the occurrences separate:
1. timely access to expertise;
2. correct matching of intervention to condition;
3. knowledge of available capabilities;
4. sufficient time for expert search;
5. actions that remain the recipient's own responsibility.

The source therefore treats successful remedy as more than possession of “medicine”: timing, expertise, suitability and participation all matter.
