# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-504 — Translation Validation Metrics Freeze

Before testing fallback translation, the evaluation dimensions are frozen.

For each validation item score:

1. **Propositional preservation** — is the basic claim preserved?
2. **Agent/patient preservation** — who acts on whom?
3. **Negation/modality preservation** — must/not/can/should/does the force survive?
4. **Relation preservation** — kinship, ownership, authority, dependency and comparison.
5. **Causal structure preservation** — cause, consequence, condition and sequence.
6. **Evaluative polarity preservation** — praise/blame, benefit/harm, permission/prohibition.
7. **Metaphorical restraint** — does translation avoid silently replacing metaphor with an interpretation?
8. **Uncertainty signalling** — are ambiguous terms left visible rather than falsely resolved?

### Pass principle
A fallback is not validated merely because it “sounds right.”

Material errors in agent/patient, negation, ownership, causality or evaluative polarity are disqualifying for ethical extraction until independently resolved.
