### OCR numbering defect
R793 is printed as “7 93.” It occurs between visible R792/C1901 and R794/C1940 and carries independently visible C1893. The item is therefore source-reconciled as R793.

No Christaller identifier was inferred.

### High-risk classification restraint
R797 concerns seizure and killing where a “just reason” exists. The source does not define sufficient adjudicative safeguards, proportionality conditions, authority, or error controls. It remains H; E9 does not generalise it into a permission to kill.

R800 associates guilt with having much to say. Because verbosity is not itself evidence of guilt and the source supplies no qualifying mechanism, it remains H.

R802 concerns preference for immediate rather than delayed execution. The burden of anticipation is plausible, but the literal lethal context and absence of explanatory gloss require H.

R789 and R790 likewise remain H because violent conflict descriptions are not automatically treated as ethical prescriptions.
