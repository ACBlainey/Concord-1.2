# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-452 — Mechanical Ledger Block 163: Rattray 811–815

811 / C2856 = Y — a word one would wish to take back after speaking should remain unspoken. Anticipated irreversibility and regret should inform restraint before communication is released.

812 / C2873 = H — “a good case is not difficult to state.” Clarity may correlate with a sound case, but ease of presentation is not sufficient evidence of truth or justice. The source supplies no further qualification, so the item remains unresolved.

813 / C2901 = H — when very big business is on hand, no flag is flown. Secrecy, seriousness, or suspension of display are plausible readings, but the transferable mechanism is underdetermined.

814 / C2976 = Y — carrying a box also carries what is inside it. Actions directed at a containing system can propagate to dependent contents; indirect effects should be included in consequence analysis.

815 / C2986 = Y — ears are like scales and become weighted as more is put into them. Accumulated information can alter judgment; conclusions should remain responsive to additional evidence rather than being fixed before hearing enough.

### AR558 — R811/C2856

### AR559 — R814/C2976
Actions affecting a containing system can propagate to dependent contents; indirect effects belong in consequence analysis.

### AR560 — R815/C2986
Judgment should remain responsive to accumulating information rather than becoming fixed before relevant evidence is heard.
