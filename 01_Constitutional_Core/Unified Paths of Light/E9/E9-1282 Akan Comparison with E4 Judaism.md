# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1282 — Akan Comparison with E4 Judaism

### E4 reference architecture

E4 independently froze eight Jewish attractors:
JA1 Protection, Proportionality and Repair; JA2 Epistemic Integrity and Correctability; JA3 Fairness, Accountability and Legitimate Judgement; JA4 Voluntary Agency, Conscience and Personal Boundaries; JA5 Care Under Need and Power Asymmetry; JA6 Responsible Possession and Resource Use; JA7 Bounded Collective Power and Institutional Continuity; JA8 Development of Judgement and Capability-Proportionate Responsibility.

### Akan correspondence

- AK-R5 strongly overlaps JA1 protection, while proportionality/repair sit partly in Akan operators and implementations.
- AK-R1 strongly overlaps JA2 epistemic integrity; Akan separates several epistemic procedures into operators.
- AK-R4 overlaps JA3 accountability, while fair judgement is more distributed in Akan.
- AK-R3 strongly overlaps JA4 and the power-asymmetry component of JA5/JA7.
- AK-R6 overlaps JA5 care under need and vulnerability.
- AK-R7 strongly overlaps JA6.
- AK-R8 overlaps JA7 institutional continuity and resilience-related structure.
- AK-R2 strongly overlaps JA8 development/capability.

### Result

Both E4 and E9 freeze at eight higher-order substantive groupings, but the matching is **not 8 one-to-one labels**. Their partition boundaries differ.

The significant result is functional coverage across the architectures, not numerical equality.
