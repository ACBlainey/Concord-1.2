# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1209 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Register 004: Communication, Information Boundaries and Collective Epistemics

### Higher internally derived parents

**HC-P010 — Communication should be fitted to recipient and purpose**

Subsumes:
- recipient-calibration portions of PC-P017;
- AR082/367 communication-comprehension structure;
- PC-P026 where affinity can support mutual intelligibility.

Core structure:
> Communication effectiveness depends on recipient knowledge, interpretive conditions and purpose; transmitting information is not equivalent to making it intelligible.

**HC-P011 — Information boundaries can be ethically functional**

Subsumes:
- PC-P042 — strategic information boundaries;
- confidentiality/disclosure portions of AR309/312/362/492/513/544/558.

Core structure:
> Information may require bounded disclosure where openness would defeat legitimate action, violate relational boundaries or create irreversible propagation; concealment nevertheless fails where revealing traces remain exposed.

**HC-P012 — Information systems should resist distortion and bottleneck**

Subsumes:
- PC-P044 — collective information resilience;
- PC-P078 — interpretive mediation;
- PC-P092 — authority information architecture;
- AR292 hostile false reports;
- AR405 status-conditioned circulation.

Core structure:
> Collective judgment depends on information channels that resist suppression, hostile distortion, bottlenecks and intermediary bias while allowing relevant knowledge to circulate.

### Important bridge

HC-P011 is not reducible to HC-P007 truthfulness. A communication can be truthful yet legitimately bounded; conversely, secrecy can be strategically justified without licensing fabrication.

HC-P012 bridges epistemics and governance because authority structures alter who can speak, what reaches decision-makers and how information is interpreted.
