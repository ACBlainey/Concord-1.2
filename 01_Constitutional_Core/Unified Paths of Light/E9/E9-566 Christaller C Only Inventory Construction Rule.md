# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-566 — Christaller C-Only Inventory Construction Rule

A Christaller item becomes a **C-only candidate** only when:

1. its C-ID is directly visible in T0;
2. it is absent from the reconciled unique Rattray C-ID overlap set;
3. it is not merely a variant already represented under the frozen deduplication rules;
4. its source text is mechanically recoverable.

### Candidate does not mean ethically usable
C-only candidate status is purely corpus arithmetic.

Translation and ethical classification remain gated.

### Required fields
C-ID | T0 locator | source text | variant marker | R-overlap status | translation status | debt.

This keeps corpus reconstruction independent of later semantic decisions.
