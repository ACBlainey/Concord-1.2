# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-229 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Intergenerational Transmission and Cultural Continuity

R357 contains one of the clearest explicit statements of cultural transmission in the corpus so far.

Children in a village sing songs that elders once sang and left to them. Rattray directly glosses this as **tradition being handed down**.

This is important methodologically for E9 because the experiment is studying an oral proverb tradition. The occurrence demonstrates, from within the sampled tradition itself, an explicit model of cultural material being transmitted from older to younger generations.

It does not establish historical age for any specific proverb and must not be used to bypass E9's historical-depth controls. It does, however, provide direct evidence that intergenerational transmission is itself recognised within the source tradition.
