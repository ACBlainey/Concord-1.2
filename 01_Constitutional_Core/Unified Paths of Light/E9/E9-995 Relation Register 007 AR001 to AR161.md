### Emerging separability structure

Several independent occurrences now resist invalid transfer across a relation:
- responsibility for one agent's claim does not automatically attach to another (AR003);
- a transaction concerning one individual does not automatically extend to a related individual (AR154);
- a property of one linked agent should not automatically be imputed to the other (AR157).

This may later support a broader parent concerning correct attribution or individual separability, but no compression is performed now.
