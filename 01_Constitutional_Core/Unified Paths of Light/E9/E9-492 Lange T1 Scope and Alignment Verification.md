# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-492 — Lange T1 Scope and Alignment Verification

Independent scholarly usage confirms that Lange is not merely a thematic selection.

Published scholarship explicitly cites individual entries as **Christaller Number (CN)** followed by the collection number and states that Christaller's collection was later arranged with English translations by Lange.

Examples in the accessible scholarly record include:
- CN2554 cited directly to the Christaller/Lange collection;
- Christaller proverb no. 685 quoted with a Lange English translation and a Lange page locator.

### Consequence
T1 is suitable in principle for the frozen Christaller-ID alignment architecture.

The important alignment unit remains the **Christaller number**, not Lange page number.

### Limitation
Only scattered quoted items are presently exposed through accessible secondary scholarship. These fragments do not constitute mechanical access to all ~3,600 translations.
