# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-277 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Functional Importance Versus Formal Status

R458 is especially important within the hierarchical material.

The dependent is compared to the flint of a gun: remove that apparently small component and the larger system becomes useless. Rattray explicitly glosses the person as a **necessary member of the community**.

This directly separates:
- formal status in a hierarchy; and
- functional importance to the system.

R450 provides a related mobility signal: demonstrated competence can result in succession to property despite initially subordinate status.

The emerging occurrence-level principle is that **formal rank is not a reliable measure of functional contribution or capability**.

No compression is performed.
