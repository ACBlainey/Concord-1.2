# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1251 — Leave-One-Family-Out Test 003: MS-P05 and MS-P06

### MS-P05 — Protection from avoidable harm and vulnerability

Removal attempt:
- MS-P03 protects agency;
- MS-P06 supplies care;
- MS-P08 preserves continuity.

**Failure of reconstruction.**

Agency does not cover all harm; care need not be triggered by hazard; continuity can preserve systems while individuals remain exposed. Preventive restraint, vulnerability-sensitive protection and non-externalisation of harm disappear as a coherent substantive orientation.

**Decision: INDEPENDENTLY NECESSARY ORGANISING FAMILY.**

### MS-P06 — Sustaining relationships of care and reciprocity

Removal attempt:
- MS-P05 protects vulnerability;
- MS-P07 can allocate resources;
- MS-P03 preserves agency.

**Failure of reconstruction.**

Protection does not generate relationship-specific support, reciprocity or durable non-instrumental obligation. Resource allocation can be correct without sustaining relationships. Agency preservation does not entail care.

**Decision: INDEPENDENTLY NECESSARY ORGANISING FAMILY.**

### Result

The corpus requires both protective and relational-care orientations.
