# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1256 — Alternative-Partition Challenge 001: Epistemic–Accountability and Development–Continuity

### Candidate reduction A
**MS-P01 Responsible information relation + MS-P04 Causal responsibility**

Candidate replacement:
**AP-A — Reality-answerable responsible action**

#### Failure
The formulation is compound. “Reality-answerable” supplies epistemic obligations; “responsible action” supplies causal accountability.

Removing the accountability component loses SP10. Removing the information component loses SP02–SP04. Neither is generated by the other.

**Decision: REJECT.**

### Candidate reduction B
**MS-P02 Development + MS-P08 Adaptive continuity**

Candidate replacement:
**AP-B — Sustained adaptive capability**

#### Failure
This conflates two temporal directions:
- development can appropriately transform/increase capability even where no prior function is being preserved;
- continuity can preserve inherited function without increasing capability.

SP01 cannot generate SP22–SP23, and SP22–SP23 cannot generate SP01 without adding a distinct developmental premise.

**Decision: REJECT.**

### Result
Two plausible seven-family routes fail without reference to external architectures.

# **ALTERNATIVE PARTITION 001 — BOTH REDUCTIONS REJECTED**
