# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-382 — Mechanical Ledger Block 135: Rattray 671–675

671 / C1250 = H — a person burned by fire keeps licking the injury despite not finding the burn pleasant. The behaviour is clear but the transferable ethical mechanism is insufficiently fixed.

672 / C1251 = Y — a rolling firebrand burns the person sleeping nearest it. Hazard exposure is partly positional: those nearest an uncontrolled danger may bear the first or greatest consequences.

673 / C1252 = Y — fire and gunpowder do not sleep together. Components whose interaction creates catastrophic risk should not be placed or maintained in dangerous proximity.

674 / C2350 = Y — firewood is first taken into the house even though its ash is eventually discarded. A resource's eventual exhausted state does not erase the value or legitimate use it had earlier in its lifecycle.

675 / C3067 = H — a river whose fish are taboo is rejected even for a gold nugget. Rattray explicitly uses this to show the strength of taboo. The source clearly demonstrates rule-consistency extending beyond the original prohibited object, but whether this should be generalised as ethical integrity or treated as ritual taboo remains unresolved.

### AR441 — R672/C1251
Exposure to an uncontrolled hazard is partly positional; agents nearest the hazard may bear disproportionate consequences.

### AR442 — R673/C1252
Components whose interaction creates catastrophic risk should not be maintained in dangerous proximity.

### AR443 — R674/C2350
A resource's eventual exhaustion or disposal does not erase the legitimate value it provided earlier in its lifecycle.
