| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR536 | R783 / C1397 | A valued trait in one domain does not discharge an obligation arising in another; value and capability are domain-specific. | Domain-specific value / obligation. |

| AR538 | R785 / C1522 | Reliable accounting requires inclusion of the relevant members or components rather than impressionistic estimation. | Complete accounting. |
