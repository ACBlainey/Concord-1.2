# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-379 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Poverty and Wealth as Symmetric Status Distorters

The consecutive poverty and wealth sequences permit an internal E9 observation without reopening the cross-tradition firewall.

Earlier items show poverty causing:
- valuable goods to be treated as inferior;
- knowledge to circulate less;
- complaints to receive less attention;
- intelligence to be discounted;
- social participation to contract.

R648 onward shows wealth causing:
- greater weight in council;
- potentially more favourable description of conduct;
- insulation from negative attribution.

This creates a symmetric structural pattern:

**evaluation can be distorted downward by low status and upward by high status.**

The common problem is not the possession or absence of wealth itself. It is the substitution of status cues for direct evaluation of evidence, capability, conduct or claims.

This pattern should remain available for later internal compression because it may represent a higher-order operator spanning multiple occurrence families.
