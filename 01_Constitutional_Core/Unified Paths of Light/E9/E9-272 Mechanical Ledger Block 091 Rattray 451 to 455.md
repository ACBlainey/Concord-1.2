# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-272 — Mechanical Ledger Block 091: Rattray 451–455

451 / C1623 = Y — a person who becomes rich and free adopts identification with a noble family. Changes in material/legal status can alter claimed social identity.

452 / C1611 = H — “a slave is, as a matter of course, guilty.” The saying records status-based presumption of guilt. It is retained as evidence of unequal judgment but not admitted as a valid ethical prescription.

453 / C1617 = Y — Rattray explains that the dependent who sees suitable building material is expected to bring it, not merely report it. Effective responsibility can require completing the useful action when the needed next step is within one's capability.

454 / C1618 = Y — eating seed yams reserved exclusively for next season signals an intention to flee before planting. Consumption of resources reserved for future continuity can reveal abandonment of the future obligation those resources support.

455 / C1624 = H — “a slave's wisdom is in his master's head.” The saying encodes hierarchical denial or relocation of agency/intelligence; without further gloss its descriptive/normative function is insufficiently secure.

### AR269 — R451/C1623
Changes in material and legal standing can alter claimed social identity or affiliation.

### AR270 — R453/C1617
Where the useful next step is evident and within capability, responsible performance may require completing it rather than merely reporting the opportunity.

### AR271 — R454/C1618
Using resources reserved for future continuity can signal abandonment of the future obligation those resources were intended to sustain.
