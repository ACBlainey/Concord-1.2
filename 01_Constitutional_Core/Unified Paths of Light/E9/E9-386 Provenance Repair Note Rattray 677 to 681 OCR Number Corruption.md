### Basis for repair

This repair is supported by multiple independent internal features:
1. the items occur in one uninterrupted run between visible R676 and R682;
2. there are exactly five intervening proverbs;
3. their Christaller identifiers are independently visible;
4. R681's note explicitly refers back to “No. 677”;
5. the chapter pagination and textual continuity show no missing intervening section.

The repair therefore satisfies the E9 source-integrity rule requiring more than simple sequential inference.
