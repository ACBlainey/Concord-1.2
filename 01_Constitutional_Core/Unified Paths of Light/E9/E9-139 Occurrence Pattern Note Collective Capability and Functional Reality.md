# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-139 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Collective Capability and Functional Reality

Two structures in this block reinforce patterns already visible elsewhere in E9.

### Collective capability
R193 states that ants must unite in numbers before they can produce their collective sound. Capability therefore can emerge from coordination rather than residing fully in isolated individuals.

### Functional reality over appearance
R186 shows effective capability despite absence of an expected mechanism, while R194 again distinguishes superficial resemblance from actual identity.

Together these continue a strong occurrence-level tendency to assess agents and systems by actual capability, organisation and properties rather than by expected form or surface appearance.

No deepest root is asserted.
