### Emerging reference-point discipline

The newest occurrences sharpen a methodological feature already present in the corpus: many evaluations are only meaningful once the correct **reference point** is specified.

Examples include:
- whose consequences are being assessed (AR470);
- which relationship is under evaluation (AR471);
- what prior access the recipient had (AR472);
- whose act generated responsibility (AR473);
- whose resource or achievement grounds credit (AR479);
- which purpose determines resource adequacy (AR451, AR457).

This is not unrestricted subjectivity. It is a requirement to identify the relevant agent, relationship, function or causal pathway before making the evaluation.

### Agency under non-physical pressure

AR482 adds reputational ridicule to the existing set of constraints on effective agency. The corpus therefore includes material, informational, hierarchical and social-pressure mechanisms that can alter practical choice without all taking the form of direct force.
