# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1181 — Parent-Child Compression Register 009: AR176–AR194

### Existing parents extended

**PC-P005 / PC-P037 — causal attribution and explanation**
- AR179 — self-serving failure attribution can exaggerate target difficulty.
This is a candidate child of biased causal attribution, with later M008 expected to provide a related but distinct mechanism.

**PC-P001 — Performance-grounded capability assessment**
- AR191 — assertive signalling is credible only insofar as materially supported capability exists.
This joins AR172 as a signalling/capability distinction.

**PC-P017 / PC-P043 — evidence and verification**
- AR182 — observed outcome can evidence prior intervention.
- AR187 — absence of independent witnesses reduces verifiability.

They remain siblings under evidence conditions rather than being compressed into one claim.

### New provisional local parents

**PC-P045 — Dependency-chain functionality**
- AR176 — failure of a prerequisite can block downstream benefit.
- AR178 — a support task can lose purpose when its enabling/primary function fails.
- AR184 — effort can rationally stop when a productive enabling condition fails.

These are differentiated children describing consequences of dependency failure.

**PC-P046 — Critical enabling infrastructure**
- AR177 — protective infrastructure can determine realised system capability.
- AR192 — failure of a critical subsystem can remove system function.
- AR195 later supplies the positive small-component formulation.

PC-P046 is closely related to PC-P045 but distinct: one concerns **what is critical**, the other **what follows when dependencies fail**.

**PC-P047 — Productive causal-role attribution**
- AR181 — productive credit should track the producer/contributor.
- AR185 — enabling infrastructure can deserve causal credit.
- AR186 — transport or transfer should not be mistaken for production.

This parent separates producer, enabler and carrier roles.

**PC-P048 — Non-transferable responsibility through association**
- AR189 — later benefit does not automatically transfer responsibility for prior action.
- AR190 — remote category association does not establish responsibility.
- AR193 — an unaffected associated party should not bear another's consequence absent relevant connection.

Candidate higher relation to PC-P031's non-transfer branch.

### Compression observation

PC-P045 and PC-P046 constitute the clearest system-dependency structure yet seen. They should remain separate intermediate parents until later system and continuity propositions test whether a higher parent is justified.
