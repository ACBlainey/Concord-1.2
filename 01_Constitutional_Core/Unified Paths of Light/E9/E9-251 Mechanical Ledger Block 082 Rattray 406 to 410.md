# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-251 — Mechanical Ledger Block 082: Rattray 406–410

406 / C103 = Y — if an old woman claims to know everything, she is told to put up her own fence. A broad claim of competence can legitimately be tested against the claimant's ability to perform the relevant task; age/status does not make the claim self-validating.

407 / C1992 = H — repeated greetings are said eventually to kill an old woman who spends the day returning them. The source may concern cumulative burden, but no sufficiently secure transferable ethical function is supplied.

408 / C1300 = H — the saying denies the category of a “bad king” while allowing a “bad vassal chief.” This is a hierarchical political claim whose descriptive, ideological and normative status cannot be securely separated from the source alone.

409 / C1301 = H — a future chief may eventually kill a person who has served many chiefs. The saying exposes uncertainty and vulnerability under changing rulers, but the intended ethical function is underdetermined.

410 / C1303 = Y — Rattray explicitly glosses the proverb: “Times and manners change.” Practices vary across administrations or periods; current convention should not be mistaken for an invariant form.

### AR245 — R406/C103
A broad claim of competence can be tested against demonstrated ability; status alone does not validate the claim.

### AR246 — R410/C1303
Practices and conventions can change across time and administrations; present custom is not necessarily invariant.
