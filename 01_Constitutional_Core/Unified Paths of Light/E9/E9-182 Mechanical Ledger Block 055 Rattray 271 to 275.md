## E9-182 — Mechanical Ledger Block 055: Rattray 271–275

271 / **no Christaller identifier visible** = H — no one compares a goat with a sheep. A categorical distinction is asserted, but its transferable ethical function is unclear and the Christaller mapping is unresolved.

272 / C1227 = Y — a sheep expected to die but not yet dead is not called a dead sheep. Anticipated future state should not be treated as though it were already the present state.

273 / C1230 = H — once a sheep tastes salt, it does not stop eating it. Reinforcing appetite/habit is visible, but no sufficiently explicit ethical norm is supplied.

274 / C1228 = Y — a dead sheep does not choose the knife with which it will be cut. Once agency is irreversibly lost, choice over subsequent treatment is no longer exercisable; meaningful choice therefore depends on the continued presence of agency.

275 / C1231 = H — the day a sheep dies is also the day a person dies. The source provides no explanatory note sufficient to determine the intended transferable relation.
