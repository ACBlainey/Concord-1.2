# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1201 — Parent-Child Compression Register 025: AR526–AR545

### Existing parents extended

**PC-P098 — prerequisite/dependency sequencing**
- AR529 — foundations/prerequisites must precede dependent operations.
This is the general process sibling of AR520's developmental sequencing.

**PC-P055 — robustness under tested conditions**
- AR530 — present stability does not establish robustness under untested conditions.

**PC-P054 — reciprocity under asymmetry**
- AR531 — nominally identical exchange can be non-equivalent where capabilities differ materially.

**PC-P005 / PC-P052 — causal inference from effects**
- AR532 — observable surface effects can evidence an underlying event or state.
- AR537 — causal inquiry should identify the operative cause rather than substitute a familiar hazard.

**PC-P046 — bounded component capacity**
- AR533 — components may survive failure of their containing system.
- AR534 — even highly capable individual components have workload limits.

**PC-P001 — capability and domain specificity**
- M008/AR535 — agents can externalise their own capability deficit onto tools/conditions.
- AR536 — value/capability in one domain does not discharge an obligation in another.
- AR541 — wider exposure can correct unjustified claims of uniqueness/superiority.
- AR543 — observation does not guarantee explanatory competence.

**PC-P017 — independent evaluation**
- AR545 — quality claims require evaluation beyond self-assertion.

**PC-P051 / PC-P060 — hazard inference**
- AR542 — familiarity or low perceived risk does not establish safety.

**PC-P042 — information boundaries**
- AR544 — disclosure in an open setting can reach unintended listeners.

### New provisional parents

**PC-P102 — Complete accounting across membership**
- AR538 — valid accounting may require inclusion of all relevant members rather than a convenient subset.

**PC-P103 — Host-condition-sensitive demand**
- AR539 — demands placed on a host/system should respect its actual resource conditions.

**PC-P104 — Self-assigned superiority and status**
- AR540 — self-asserted superiority, especially through degradation of incumbents, does not establish legitimate status.
