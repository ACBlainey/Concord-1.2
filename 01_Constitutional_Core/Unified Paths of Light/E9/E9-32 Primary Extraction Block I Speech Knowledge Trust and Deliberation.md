# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-32 — Primary Extraction Block I: Speech, Knowledge, Trust and Deliberation

## Coverage
Bridge material whose conventional application concerns speech, information, judgement and trust was examined without importing prior truth-family labels.

## Provisional functions observed
- caution before assertion;
- reliability of testimony or communicated information;
- consequences of careless speech;
- deliberation before commitment;
- recognition of incomplete knowledge;
- learning through attention and experience;
- trust conditioned by conduct;
- reputational consequences of unreliability;
- correction when experience defeats prior judgement.

## Medium observation
Several proverb forms communicate epistemic guidance indirectly through consequence rather than explicit commands. Consequential and deliberative classes therefore remain important.

## Control
Cleverness, secrecy or strategic information advantage is not automatically coded as epistemic ethics.
