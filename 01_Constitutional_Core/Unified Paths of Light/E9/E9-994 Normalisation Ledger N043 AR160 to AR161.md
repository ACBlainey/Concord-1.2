# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-994 — Normalisation Ledger N043: AR160–AR161

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR160 | R293 / C511 | Access to a finished benefit does not imply access to or control over the productive means that created it. | Product/means distinction. |
| AR161 | R295 / C1555 | Excessive abundance or repeated access can reduce an agent's desire for a resource it ordinarily values. | Abundance-dependent valuation. |

AR160 preserves an important structural separation between benefit consumption and control of productive infrastructure. AR161 records context-sensitive valuation rather than assuming stable preference under abundance.
