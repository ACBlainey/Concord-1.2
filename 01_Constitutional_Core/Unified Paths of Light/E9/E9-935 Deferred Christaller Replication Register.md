## E9-935 — Deferred Christaller Replication Register

The unfinished Christaller expansion is transferred from the active critical path to a deferred replication register.

### Reopen when
A sufficiently clear Christaller witness becomes available, preferably:
- high-quality page images or transcription;
- stable proverb numbering;
- legible identifiers;
- inspectable source order;
- sufficient fidelity for independent translation/provenance checks.

### Future task
A later E9 replication should rerun the Christaller expansion from the frozen corpus boundary and compare the result with:
1. the present Rattray-derived AR corpus;
2. the preserved partial overlap reconstruction;
3. the eventual E9 normalised/compressed architecture.

Later evidence may expand or challenge E9, but must not be rewritten as though it had been available during the present experiment.
