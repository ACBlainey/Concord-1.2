# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-289 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Collective Authority, Consensus and Individual Constraint

R478 and R480 show collective power operating through different mechanisms.

R478 depicts the chief carrying out lethal action when the united people desire it. Because the source supplies no normative qualification, this remains H rather than an ethical prescription.

R480 is clearer as a functional occurrence: unanimous communal preference compels an individual to adopt a particular hairstyle.

The important analytical distinction is that **collective legitimacy or consensus and protection of individual agency are separate variables**. A preference can be broadly shared and still operate coercively upon an individual.

This observation remains internal to E9 and does not invoke the frozen cross-tradition kernel.
