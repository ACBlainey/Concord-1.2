# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-494 — Lange Full-Text Accessibility Audit

A targeted web search was performed for:
- title + PDF;
- translator + title + PDF;
- 1990 ISBN;
- 1999/2000 ISBN.

### Result
No reliable, complete, openly accessible full-text copy of the Lange translation was located in the search results.

Available evidence consists of:
- library catalogue records;
- publisher records;
- scholarly citations and quotations of individual proverbs;
- a JSTOR review record;
- secondary works quoting selected Lange translations.

### Method consequence
The T1 witness is **bibliographically verified but not mechanically acquired**.

E9 must not pretend that catalogue metadata or scattered quotations provide complete translation coverage.

The C-only ethical ledger therefore remains gated.
