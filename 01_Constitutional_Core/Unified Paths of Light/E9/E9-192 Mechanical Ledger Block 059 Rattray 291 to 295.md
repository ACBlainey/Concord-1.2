# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-192 — Mechanical Ledger Block 059: Rattray 291–295

291 / C1836 = Y — the mouse says the person who throws its corpse on the ground hurts it more than the person who killed it. The occurrence assigns significance to treatment after death and distinguishes physical killing from post-mortem indignity.

292 / C1838 = Y — when many mice dig one hole, it does not become deep. Increasing participant count does not guarantee effective collective output where coordination or concentrated effort is lacking.

293 / C511 = Y — the rat can consume the fufu but cannot take the pestle into its hole. An agent may appropriate a product while lacking access to or control over the productive means that generated it.

294 / C1553 = H — the rat discards palm nuts when lightning flashes. A threat-triggered abandonment response is visible, but the ethical function remains insufficiently explicit.

295 / C1555 = Y — Rattray explicitly says the rat tiring of palm nuts means a person tires of what they have too much of. Excess abundance can reduce the experienced value or desirability of a resource.

### AR158 — Rattray 291 / Christaller 1836
**Minimal function:** Treatment of a being after death can retain social or moral significance distinct from the physical act that caused death.
**Confidence:** high, while avoiding claims beyond the source.

### AR159 — Rattray 292 / Christaller 1838
**Minimal function:** More participants do not necessarily produce greater effective output when collective activity lacks the conditions required for concentrated progress.
**Confidence:** high.

### AR160 — Rattray 293 / Christaller 511
**Minimal function:** Access to a finished benefit does not imply access to or control over the productive means that created it.
**Confidence:** moderate-high.

### AR161 — Rattray 295 / Christaller 1555
**Minimal function:** Excessive abundance or repeated access can reduce an agent's desire for a resource it ordinarily values.
**Confidence:** very high because Rattray supplies the explicit interpretation.
