### R507 extended note
Rattray's long drumming note was retrieved in full before classification. It describes:
- rapid communication by drum;
- drum-talking rather than a simple abstract signal code;
- restricted/prearranged message vocabularies;
- specialist drummers trained from childhood;
- required knowledge of royal genealogies, traditions and proverbs;
- preservation of tribal memory;
- alarm messages, including fire;
- secrecy constraints on military instructions;
- ceremonial and historical messages.

The ledger extracts the specialist-training/memory/communication function, not R507's gender hierarchy.

### Sensitive historical material
R509 describes child marriage while also saying sexual intercourse did not usually occur until the girl had reached puberty. E9 records only the developmental distinction supported by the source; it does not legitimise child marriage.

R517 can be read as rationalising intimate partner violence. It remains H and is not converted into an intervention norm.
