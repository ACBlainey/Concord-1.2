# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-371 — Mechanical Ledger Block 130: Rattray 646–650

646 / C116 = Y — the proverb represents a curse of poverty as more harmful than a curse of death. Without adopting the supernatural causal frame, the secure occurrence is that prolonged deprivation can be experienced or represented as a harm of exceptional severity.

647 / C919 = Y — lacking the money to buy wine, a person claims that wine is not sweet. Resource inability can be rationalised as a negative evaluation of the inaccessible good; stated preference may therefore be confounded by constrained access.

648 / C2960 = Y — the rich person is treated as the elder/important person whose words carry weight in council. Material wealth can translate into disproportionate social and deliberative influence independently of the content of speech.

649 / C917 = Y — gold dust would be mere sand if it could not be used. Resource value is partly functional: usefulness or exchange capability helps constitute practical value rather than mere physical possession.

650 / C2935 = Y — when money is only sufficient for one's own needs, it is not lent at interest. Surplus capacity is a precondition for some forms of resource lending/investment; agents near subsistence cannot safely deploy the same resources as surplus holders.

### AR419 — R646/C116
Prolonged material deprivation can constitute or be experienced as a harm of exceptional severity.

### AR420 — R647/C919
Stated dislike of an inaccessible good can reflect constrained access rather than an independent preference.

### AR421 — R648/C2960
Material wealth can confer disproportionate social or deliberative influence independently of the intrinsic quality of an agent's contribution.

### AR422 — R649/C917
Practical resource value depends partly on usable function rather than physical possession alone.

### AR423 — R650/C2935
Resource strategies available to surplus holders may be unavailable to agents whose resources are only sufficient for basic needs.
