# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-422 — Mechanical Ledger Block 151: Rattray 751–755

751 / C413 = Y — when another claims superior running ability, leave the road open for that person. Where another agent claims or demonstrates greater capability, unnecessary status competition need not obstruct their performance.

752 / C422 = Y — if someone excels you, let them; that person in turn is exceeded by another. Capability is comparative and distributed rather than absolute; being surpassed in one comparison does not establish universal inferiority.

753 / C464 = Y — nothing hurts like shame. Social/reputational injury can constitute severe experienced harm and should not be dismissed merely because it is non-physical.

754 / C472 = Z — whatever is above must come down to earth. Rattray treats this as recognition of a natural law and cross-references R241. No independent ethical occurrence is secure at this level.

755 / C481 = Y — “Hit me, but I must not hit you” is not play. A supposedly reciprocal interaction is not genuinely reciprocal when one party claims permission to impose harm while denying equivalent standing to the other.

### AR508 — R751/C413
Unnecessary status competition should not obstruct another agent's exercise of genuinely greater capability.

### AR509 — R752/C422
Capability is comparative and distributed; being surpassed in one relation does not establish universal inferiority.

### AR510 — R753/C464
Social or reputational injury can constitute severe experienced harm even when it is non-physical.

### AR511 — R755/C481
An interaction presented as reciprocal is defective when one party claims permission to impose harm while denying equivalent standing to the other.
