# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-233 — Mechanical Ledger Block 076: Rattray 376–380

376 / C341 = Y — no one sends a child aloft and then removes the ladder. An agent who places a dependent in a vulnerable position retains responsibility not to withdraw the support required for safe return.

377 / C342 = Y — no one sends a child on a difficult errand and then becomes angry if performance is poor. Accountability should be calibrated to the difficulty of the task and the capability of the agent assigned to it.

378 / C2602 = H — strict parenting produces fear/respect. The source records the relation but does not establish a sufficiently secure general ethical endorsement of fear as a developmental mechanism.

379 / C2613 = Y — threatened punishment that is never carried out ceases to produce fear. Repeated signalling without corresponding action degrades the credibility of the signal.

380 / C2606 = Y — Rattray explicitly interprets the elder portioning the dish as a wise elder knowing how to settle disputes. Experienced mediation can cool conflict.

### AR224 — R376/C341
Assigning a dependent to a vulnerable task creates responsibility to preserve the support necessary for safe completion or return.

### AR225 — R377/C342
Performance expectations and accountability should be calibrated to task difficulty and the agent's developmental capability.

### AR226 — R379/C2613
Repeated threats or commitments unsupported by action lose credibility and behavioural force.

### AR227 — R380/C2606
Experienced mediation can reduce conflict and enable settlement.
