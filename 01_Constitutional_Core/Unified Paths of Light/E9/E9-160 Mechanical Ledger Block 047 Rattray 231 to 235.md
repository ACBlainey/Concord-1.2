## E9-160 — Mechanical Ledger Block 047: Rattray 231–235

231 / C1680 = Y — if the vulture did not wish to enter the house, it would not remain around the dung-hill. Repeated positioning near an opportunity can provide evidence about underlying intention despite absence of direct declaration.

232 / C1681 = Y — the foul-smelling vulture says the civet cat stinks. Criticism of another for a defect also possessed by the critic exposes inconsistency in judgment.

233 / C2691 = Y — when a vulture's feather lies to its master, the vulture removes and discards it. A component that persistently supplies false information can justifiably be removed from a system that depends on it.

234 / C2687 = H — even the foolish-looking vulture does not eat before bathing. The attached interpretation supplied to Rattray invokes a disparaging ethnic stereotype concerning Hausa people while noting ceremonial ablutions. The practice is recorded, but the framing is not converted into an ethical norm.

235 / C2775 = Y — the hawk announces an intention to catch and eat a person but instead carries off a fowl. Stated ambition should be distinguished from achieved capability/outcome.
