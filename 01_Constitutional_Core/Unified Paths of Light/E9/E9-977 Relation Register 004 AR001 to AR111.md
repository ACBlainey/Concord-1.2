### Important distinction emerging

The corpus is repeatedly separating **function from mechanism**:
- care can be achieved through different means (AR057);
- capability can exist without the expected visible mechanism (AR085);
- consequences can arrive through alternative mechanisms (AR104);
- knowledge possession can differ from the social signalling role (AR099).

This may become architecturally significant later, but no parent principle is assigned now.
