# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1221 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Register 014: Relationship and Reciprocity Relation Audit

### Higher parents

- HC-P025 — differentiated relational obligations
- HC-P026 — asymmetry-responsive reciprocity
- HC-P027 — durable/non-instrumental relationships
- HC-P028 — vulnerability/capability-sensitive care
- HC-P029 — networked support
- HC-P030 — relational boundaries/non-transfer

### Relation tests

**HC-P025 vs HC-P028 — RETAIN SEPARATE.**  
Relationship can generate obligation without vulnerability; vulnerability-sensitive care adds a distinct condition.

**HC-P025 vs HC-P026 — RETAIN SEPARATE.**  
Obligation arising from relation is not the same as evaluating reciprocity within an exchange.

**HC-P027 vs HC-P025 — RETAIN SEPARATE.**  
Durability describes persistence/value of a bond, not the specific duties it generates.

**HC-P029 vs HC-P002 — BRIDGE.**  
Support networks are enabling conditions for realised capability, but their relational structure is independently represented.

**HC-P030 vs HC-P020 — BRIDGE.**  
Both resist responsibility transfer, but HC-P030 generalises the non-transfer relation to property, identity, control and entitlement.

### Structural observation

The relationship region is not reducible to generic beneficence. It contains at least:
- relation-generated obligation;
- substantive reciprocity;
- durability;
- care under vulnerability;
- network structure;
- person/property/responsibility boundaries.
