# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-144 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Harm Beyond the Immediate Victim

R201 gives a particularly direct extension of the harm structure.

The fowl is eating a friend's grain, not the speaker's. Nevertheless, the instruction is to intervene now because tolerated conduct may later consume the speaker's grain as well.

At occurrence level this links:
- concern for another's present loss;
- refusal to treat current non-victimhood as a reason for inaction;
- recognition that unchecked harmful behaviour can propagate across victims.

This complements R190/R192, where indiscriminate harm destroys future reciprocal support, and earlier occurrences concerning collateral harm and preventive action.

No deepest root is asserted.
