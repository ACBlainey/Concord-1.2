# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-08 — Akan Extraction Unit, Coding and Coverage Protocol

## Extraction unit
The basic extraction unit is the **proverb family as presented in the frozen collection**, including explicitly associated variants.

A proverb may yield:
- zero ethical occurrences;
- one ethical occurrence;
- more than one occurrence only where separable functions are genuinely supported.

## Non-forcing rule
A proverb is not ethical merely because it contains a person, social action, consequence or evaluative image.

Descriptive, comic, observational, lexical, opaque or context-insufficient material may yield zero.

## Coding
For each usable occurrence record:
- proverb/family locator;
- source-language form where recoverable;
- translation/gloss where available;
- variant relation;
- normative class;
- scope;
- minimal functional proposition;
- interpretation dependence;
- mediation grade;
- translation debt;
- contact/history flag where relevant;
- confidence;
- HOLD/quarantine state.

## Coverage
The collection will be traversed in source order.

Coverage closure requires reaching the terminal proverb/variant boundary. Saturation, recovery of expected functions, or diminishing novelty cannot terminate extraction early.

## Thematic-index firewall
Any later thematic classification, ethical commentary or external discussion must not determine which primary proverbs are read or retained.

## Repetition
Repeated functions count as density evidence, not additional root types.

## Ambiguity
Where ethical meaning depends heavily on missing performance context or uncertain translation:
- retain as HOLD if potentially material;
- quarantine if unusable for primary derivation;
- do not force a functional reading.
