## E9-958 — Normalisation Ledger N018: AR055–AR056

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR055 | R146 / C1747 | Apparent proximity to a needed resource does not establish that an agent's underlying need for that resource has been satisfied. | Need versus apparent access; avoid inferring welfare from proximity. |
| AR056 | R148 / C1750 | Recognised social relationships can carry reciprocal expectations that a host will provide for a visitor's immediate needs. | Hospitality / relational support; culturally grounded social obligation. |

AR055 distinguishes **resource proximity** from **need satisfaction**, complementing earlier distinctions between visible condition and actual capability. AR056 records a positive social-support expectation with unusually explicit contextual interpretation from Rattray.
