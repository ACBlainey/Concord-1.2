### Required split of PC-P058

**PC-P091 — Collective constitution and legitimacy of authority**
- AR247 — operative authority depends on collective support/relations.
- AR498 later expected to test outsider self-appointment.

**PC-P092 — Authority information architecture**
- AR248 — power can suppress candour.
- AR250 — adviser competence affects decision quality.
- AR252 — office position changes informational exposure.
- AR253 — multi-channel reporting reduces bottlenecks.
- AR275 — fear of authority suppresses upward candour.
- AR331 — interpretive intermediaries shape received information.
- AR405 — status affects knowledge circulation.

**PC-P093 — Coercive amplification and control asymmetry**
- AR255 — subordinates can amplify officeholder coercive power.
- AR266 — controller conduct shapes dependent behaviour.
- AR278 — dependency reduces contestability.
- AR280 — controlling relations are asymmetric in restraint capacity.
- AR282 — hierarchy can subordinate dependent life/agency.

PC-P058 remains in the provenance record but is no longer an active unsplit parent.
