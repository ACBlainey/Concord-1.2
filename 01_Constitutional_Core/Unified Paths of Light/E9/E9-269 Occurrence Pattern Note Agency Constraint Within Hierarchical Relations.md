# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-269 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Agency Constraint Within Hierarchical Relations

R443 and R444 provide direct statements about constrained agency within the historical master/slave relation.

- R443: the slave does not choose the master.
- R444: the slave does not independently decide to go to war.

These are analytically important because they concern **choice structure**, not merely status labels.

The source's surrounding historical and evaluative commentary does not need to be accepted in order to observe that the proverbs themselves encode restrictions on:
- selection of the controlling relationship;
- participation in a dangerous collective action.

E9 records these constraints descriptively. It does not treat the historical hierarchy as ethically legitimate.
