# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1214 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Register 008: Authority, Legitimacy, Status and Accountability

### Higher internally derived parents

**HC-P018 — Authority requires a basis beyond self-assertion**

Subsumes:
- PC-P091 — collective constitution and legitimacy of authority;
- PC-P104 — self-assigned superiority/status;
- AR498 outsider self-appointment;
- relevant authority portions of PC-P247/PC-P058 provenance.

Core structure:
> Authority or superior standing is not created merely by assertion, outsider claim or degradation of incumbents; its operative basis lies in recognised relations, functions and collective conditions.

**HC-P019 — Status should not displace evidence, responsibility or functional importance**

Subsumes:
- PC-P022 — status humility/anti-superiority;
- status-bias portions of PC-P012;
- AR258/259/260/261/268/274/398/405-type structures;
- PC-P067 where category membership does not establish identical properties.

Core structure:
> Status can alter social position or informational reach, but should not substitute for evidence, erase responsibility, determine intrinsic capability, or make low-status components functionally unimportant.

**HC-P020 — Responsibility should track relevant agency and causal role**

Subsumes:
- PC-P015 — responsibility and causal inquiry;
- PC-P016 — consequence-sensitive responsibility;
- PC-P048 — non-transferable responsibility through association;
- PC-P050 — impartial accountability;
- PC-P064 — diffuse responsibility and neglect;
- responsibility portions of PC-P047.

Core structure:
> Accountability should follow relevant agency, participation and causal contribution rather than mere association, favour, status or diffuse attribution.

### Bridge

PC-P092 authority information architecture remains under **HC-P012** as an epistemic-governance bridge rather than being folded into legitimacy.
