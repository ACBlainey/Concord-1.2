# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-483 — Christaller T0 Witness Freeze

For the expansion phase, the Library of Congress digitisation is frozen as the preferred direct T0 witness unless a materially superior source is later documented.

### T0 functions
T0 controls:
- corpus identity;
- Christaller item identifier;
- printed ordering;
- Twi/source-language wording as visible in the scan;
- printed variants;
- printed structural divisions.

### T0 does not control
T0 alone does not license:
- invented English translation;
- semantic repair of unclear Twi;
- ethical classification from model intuition;
- collapsing variants;
- assumptions that adjacent numbers are related.

### Secondary T0 witnesses
Google Books and UGSpace may be used for:
- page recovery;
- scan comparison;
- resolving unreadable images;
- confirming pagination or variants.

Any divergence must be documented rather than silently harmonised.
