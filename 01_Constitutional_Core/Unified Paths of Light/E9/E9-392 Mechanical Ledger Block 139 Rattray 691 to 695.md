# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-392 — Mechanical Ledger Block 139: Rattray 691–695

691 / C3097 = Y — when the water floods, the fish becomes proud. Favourable environmental conditions can increase an agent's effective power or confidence; behaviour should not be interpreted independently of enabling context.

692 / C3051 = H — rain falling on one hill also falls on another part of the same range. Shared exposure or connected conditions are plausible, but the intended transferable ethical function is not sufficiently fixed.

693 / C3053 = Y — rain beats on the stone but it remains firm. Persistent external pressure does not necessarily displace a sufficiently stable or resilient system.

694 / C3055 = Y — the printed Rattray number is absent in the OCR, but the item is independently located between R693 and R695 and carries C3055. The proverb says that when rain beats a person, the person calls it a beating rather than euphemistically reducing it to drizzle. Harm or adverse impact should not be understated merely to soften its description.

695 / C3056 = Y — whether rain falls or does not fall, the fetish priest is killed. An accountable role can become a scapegoat when mutually incompatible outcomes both produce blame, making exoneration impossible regardless of performance.

### AR459 — R693/C3053

### AR460 — R694/C3055
Adverse impact should not be understated through euphemistic description when accurate description is available.

### AR461 — R695/C3056
