# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-12 — Correction to Pre-Extraction Assumption

## Correction
E9-06 through E9-10 correctly freeze Christaller 1879 as the primary corpus, but any wording implying that the primary edition itself supplies a complete usable English translation must not be relied upon.

The correct distinction is:

**Christaller 1879**
- primary corpus authority;
- Twi/Tshi source forms;
- ordering and variants;
- historical documentary witness.

**Later translation/interpretive sources**
- translation layer;
- semantic checking;
- contextual interpretation;
- not authority for adding or removing primary corpus items.

## Why this correction matters
If the AI generated its own English meanings from unfamiliar nineteenth-century Twi forms and then coded those generated meanings, the experiment would partially measure model translation behaviour rather than the source tradition.

That would be a more serious contamination risk than the translation debt encountered in the scriptural holdouts.

## Experimental effect
No extraction result has yet been generated, so the correction occurs before evidence coding and does not invalidate an existing E9 result.
