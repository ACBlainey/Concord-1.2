# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-757 — T0 Acquisition Route Priority Revision

The T0 acquisition order is revised without changing the frozen corpus identity:

1. Library of Congress direct image/PDF pages;
2. University of Ghana 1879 PDF mirror;
3. Google Books full-view 1879 scans;
4. HathiTrust page images;
5. other independently verified scans only if provenance matches the 1879 edition.

The first route that exposes readable item-level pages may supply the mechanical inventory. Cross-witness comparison is required where typography or identifiers are materially ambiguous.
