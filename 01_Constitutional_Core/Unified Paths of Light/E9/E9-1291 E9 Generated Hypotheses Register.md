# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1291 — E9-Generated Hypotheses Register

These hypotheses arise from E9 and are registered for later testing. They are not findings merely because E9 suggested them.

### H-E9-01 — Partition-Geometry Hypothesis
Root count varies more strongly than functional topology because compression boundaries can cut the same ethical constraint network differently.

### H-E9-02 — Medium-Layer Visibility Hypothesis
Oral/proverbial corpora may expose operators, conditions and relational structures more explicitly than systematic doctrinal corpora.

### H-E9-03 — Continuity Elevation Hypothesis
Continuity/adaptation may become an independent substantive family in traditions or corpora where intergenerational persistence and changing conditions are especially salient.

### H-E9-04 — Accountability Decomposition Hypothesis
Correctability, accountability and repair may not constitute one irreducible root; different corpora may distribute them among substantive orientations, operators and mechanisms.

### H-E9-05 — Structural-Ethics Hypothesis
Dependency, contestability, network structure and resilience may systematically mediate whether nominal ethical rules succeed in practice.

### H-E9-06 — Lineage-Breadth Hypothesis
Cross-traditional evidential strength will track genealogical and medium breadth better than raw number of traditions sampled.

### H-E9-07 — Resolution-Stability Hypothesis
Deep functional regions will remain more stable across changes in ethical resolution than domain rules, labels and institutional implementations.

### H-E9-08 — Recoverability-over-Identity Hypothesis
Bidirectional functional recoverability will be a stronger measure of ethical convergence than identical root names, counts or one-to-one mappings.
