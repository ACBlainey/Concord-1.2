# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-555 — Multi-Translator Replication Option

The primary gate requires one valid isolated 50-item run.

A stronger optional replication may later use two or more independent translators.

### Replication questions
- Do materially failed items cluster?
- Are failures caused by the same Twi constructions?
- Does one translator systematically over-expand metaphor?
- Are OCR-sensitive failures shared?
- Does confidence predict semantic reliability?

### Rule
Replication is supplementary.

The first run's scoring rules and threshold are not changed after seeing additional runs.
