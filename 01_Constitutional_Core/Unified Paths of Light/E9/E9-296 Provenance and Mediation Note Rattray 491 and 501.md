## E9-296 — Provenance and Mediation Note: Rattray 491 and 501

### R491 — missing OCR item number
The OCR line for C2068 lacks the visible “491.” prefix. Unlike an ordinary sequence guess, independent internal evidence identifies the item:
- Rattray's kinship note names No. 491 in the same cluster;
- the item lies between 490 and 492;
- the content belongs to the cited maternal-kinship sequence.

The ledger therefore accepts R491 while explicitly retaining OCR debt.

### R501 — editorial mediation
Rattray's explanation of C28 says the proverb means that an inherent property cannot be removed by artificial means. He then supports the explanation with racialised language about bodily smell.

The abstract gloss is attributable to Rattray, but the supporting commentary is a product of his 1916 framing and is not adopted as an empirical or ethical claim by E9.

Because the abstract proposition is inseparable here from a high mediation burden and the proverb itself is gender-targeted, R501 remains H rather than being promoted to Y.
