# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-932 — Bounded-Incompleteness Rule

E9 introduces a general methodological rule arising from the Christaller source problem:

> When source degradation prevents reliable completion, preserve bounded incompleteness rather than infer completion.

A corpus experiment may continue downstream when:
1. the usable subset has a defined boundary;
2. provenance for admitted material is preserved;
3. unresolved material is explicitly quarantined;
4. completeness is not claimed;
5. later replication or expansion remains possible.

This rule prevents poor source quality from becoming either an indefinite procedural blocker or a justification for manufactured certainty.
