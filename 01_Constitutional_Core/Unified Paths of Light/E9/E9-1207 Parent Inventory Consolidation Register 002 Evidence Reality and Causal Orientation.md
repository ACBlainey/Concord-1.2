# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1207 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Register 002: Evidence, Reality and Causal Orientation

### Higher internally derived parents

**HC-P005 — Judgment should track evidence and operative reality**

Subsumes:
- PC-P005 — evidence-responsive causal attribution;
- PC-P012 — property-responsive classification where appearance/label is tested against underlying condition;
- PC-P037 — causal explanation of behavioural change;
- PC-P060 — non-event evidence limits;
- PC-P075 — sampling/temporal evaluation bias;
- PC-P102 — complete accounting across relevant membership.

Core structure:
> Judgment should remain responsive to relevant evidence, underlying properties, causal mechanisms and adequate sampling rather than labels, absence-signals, convenient attribution or incomplete subsets.

**HC-P006 — Inference must respect observational limits**

Subsumes:
- PC-P043 — information asymmetry and inference limits;
- PC-P052 — behaviour/signals as evidence of latent state;
- PC-P079 — first-person standing for embodied need;
- PC-P095 — precursors and anticipatory signals;
- relevant inferential portions of PC-P007 situated knowledge.

Core structure:
> Unobserved states may sometimes be inferred from behaviour, effects or precursors, but inference must remain bounded by who can observe what and by the limits of indirect evidence.

### Retained distinctions

PC-P007 remains a **BRIDGE** because situated/local knowledge is not merely an inference rule; it concerns differential epistemic access created by embedding.

PC-P101 exploration as information generation remains a **BRIDGE** because it concerns active evidence production rather than evaluation of already available evidence.
