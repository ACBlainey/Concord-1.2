## E9-232 — Mechanical Ledger Block 075: Rattray 371–375

371 / C576 = H — a child may act as though already a chief, but whether the child will actually become one is unknown. Aspiration/status performance is distinguished from future attainment, but the intended ethical function remains underdetermined.

372 / C577 = H — a child's gold dust is compared with a firebrand that soon burns out when broken up. Resource dissipation may be implied, but no explicit interpretive anchor establishes the transferable function.

373 / C579 = Y — a learner leather-worker does not practise on rare leopard skin; Rattray explicitly explains that rare material is not used for experimental work. Learning should use resources whose loss is proportionate to the learner's uncertainty.

Unnumbered OCR item / C591 = Y — children mistake a rain-draggled eagle for a vulture. Surface condition can distort category recognition. The local OCR contains no visible Rattray number, so none is inferred.

375 / C592 = H — children pluck tiny mushrooms unskilfully while adults trample them; Rattray says the proverb refers to something almost impossible to do. The difficulty is clear, but no sufficiently specific ethical function is selected.
