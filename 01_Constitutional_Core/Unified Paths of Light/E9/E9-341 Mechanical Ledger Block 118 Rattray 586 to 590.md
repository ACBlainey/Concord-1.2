# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-341 — Mechanical Ledger Block 118: Rattray 586–590

586 / C1583 = Y — when a host indirectly sends away the host's own child during a visitor's play visit, the message is really directed at the visitor. Communication can be indirect: an action addressed to one agent may function as a signal to another who must infer the intended meaning from context.

587 / C2291 = Y — if one desires something belonging to a friend, one works to obtain an equivalent rather than stealing it. Desire does not itself create entitlement to another person's property; acquisition should proceed through productive or legitimate means rather than appropriation.

588 / C1907 = Y — the “fool” assumes a warning concerns the friend but not himself. Agents can wrongly exempt themselves from information or criticism that is relevant to similarly situated others.

589 / C1908 = Y — the meaning of a proverb must be explained to the person who does not understand it. Communication effectiveness depends on recipient comprehension; compressed or indirect knowledge may require interpretation appropriate to the receiver.

590 / C1906 = Y — the fool's sheep breaks loose twice. Failure to learn or correct after an initial preventable failure permits recurrence of the same problem.

### AR364 — R586/C1583
An action apparently directed at one agent can function as an indirect message to another; interpretation may require contextual inference.

### AR365 — R587/C2291
Desire for another's resource does not create entitlement to it; acquisition should proceed through legitimate productive means rather than appropriation.

### AR366 — R588/C1907
Agents can incorrectly exempt themselves from warnings or evidence that they recognise as applicable to similarly situated others.

### AR367 — R589/C1908
Effective communication depends on recipient comprehension; compressed or indirect knowledge may require explanation suited to the receiver.

### AR368 — R590/C1906
Failure to learn or correct after an initial preventable failure can allow the same failure to recur.
