# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1250 — Leave-One-Family-Out Test 002: MS-P03 and MS-P04

### MS-P03 — Protected agency under power and dependency

Removal attempt:
- MS-P05 protects against harm;
- MS-P06 supplies care;
- MS-P04 assigns responsibility;
- structural conditions describe dependency/power.

**Failure of reconstruction.**

Protection from harm does not guarantee viable choice or residual first-person agency. Care can itself become controlling. Describing power asymmetry does not normatively protect agency within it.

**Decision: INDEPENDENTLY NECESSARY ORGANISING FAMILY.**

### MS-P04 — Causal responsibility and accountable action

Removal attempt:
- MS-P01 supplies truth/evidence relations;
- MS-P03 constrains power;
- MS-P05 constrains harm;
- MS-P07 addresses contribution/claims.

**Failure of reconstruction.**

These do not independently require responsibility to follow relevant agency and causal role. Harm may occur without correctly assigning accountability; truthful evidence alone does not determine who bears responsibility.

**Decision: INDEPENDENTLY NECESSARY ORGANISING FAMILY.**

### Result

Agency and accountability remain adjacent but irreducible.
