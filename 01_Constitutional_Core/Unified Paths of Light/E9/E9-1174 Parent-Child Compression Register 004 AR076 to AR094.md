# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1174 — Parent-Child Compression Register 004: AR076–AR094

### Provisional local parents

**PC-P016 — Consequence-sensitive responsibility**  
Agents should account for consequences generated through their conduct, obstruction or associations.

Children:
- AR076 — deliberate obstruction externalises responsibility/consequence.
- AR084 — failing association can expose members to shared consequences.

Relation: **SIBLING**; direct obstruction and associative exposure are different routes.

**PC-P017 — Communication calibrated to epistemic conditions**  
Useful communication depends on both willingness to contribute knowledge and the recipient's knowledge state.

Children:
- AR078 — knowledge contribution need not be purely transactional.
- AR082 — communication should be calibrated to recipient knowledge/comprehension.

**PC-P018 — Role, visibility and capability are separable**  
A visible or named role is not identical to functional capability, and capability can exist without obvious role visibility.

Children:
- AR080 — a role need not be indispensable.
- AR085 — capability can be present without visibility.

**PC-P019 — Destructive exposure can undermine reciprocal support**  
Harm can destroy the conditions under which agents remain able or willing to assist one another.

Children:
- AR086 — avoid destructive individual exposure.
- AR087 — persistent harm can eliminate reciprocal aid.
- AR088 — indiscriminate harm can destroy reciprocity.

AR087 and AR088 remain distinct children because persistence and indiscriminateness are different failure modes.

**PC-P020 — Collective effectiveness is not reducible to number**  
Numerical scale alone does not establish collective effectiveness, safety or danger.

Children:
- AR089 — collective action depends on more than numerical magnitude.
- AR093 — number and danger/effect are not interchangeable.

**PC-P021 — Appearance, knowledge and entry conditions**  
Observed appearance and known conditions must be distinguished from underlying identity and from knowingly entering a condition.

Children:
- AR090 — appearance does not establish identity.
- AR092 — knowingly entering a known condition changes the agency/evaluation relation.

Relation: **SIBLING** only; this is a provisional epistemic/agency bridge rather than a stable parent.
