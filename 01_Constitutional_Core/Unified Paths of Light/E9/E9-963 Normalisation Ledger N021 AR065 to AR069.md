## E9-963 — Normalisation Ledger N021: AR065–AR069

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR065 | R161 / C1545 | Similar labels or names are insufficient evidence that differently constituted entities are substantively equivalent. | Label/reality distinction. |
| AR066 | R162 / C1546 | When foreseeable harm can be reduced in advance, preventive action is preferable to waiting for avoidable harm to occur. | Prevention before repair. |
| AR067 | R163 / C1547 | A claimed remedy or protective capability should be tested against whether the condition it claims to address remains observably unresolved. | Strong near-neighbour of AR005/AR013. |
| AR068 | R164 / C1548 | Consequences generated by an agent's own action should not be displaced onto an unrelated other; responsibility follows the actor. | Strong relation with AR003; consequence-bearing emphasis differs. |
