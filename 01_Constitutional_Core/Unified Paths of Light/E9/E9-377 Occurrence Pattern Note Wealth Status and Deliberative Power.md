# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-377 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Wealth, Status and Deliberative Power

R648, R662 and R664 form a complementary counterpart to the poverty/status-bias sequence.

R648 says the rich person is treated as the elder whose words carry weight in council.

R662 presents behaviour being redescribed more leniently for an advantaged subject, although a Twi/translation discrepancy remains flagged.

R664 represents the rich person as insulated from an unlucky interpretation that can attach to others.

Together with R631–643, the corpus now depicts status bias in both directions:
- poverty can cause knowledge, goods, claims and presentation to be discounted;
- wealth can increase deliberative weight and soften negative interpretation.

The functional concern is therefore not merely deprivation. **Material status itself can alter epistemic, procedural and social evaluation independently of the underlying act, claim or evidence.**
