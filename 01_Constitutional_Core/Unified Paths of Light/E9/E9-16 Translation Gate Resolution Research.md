# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-16 — Translation Gate Resolution Research

## Result
The translation problem is resolvable without abandoning the frozen Christaller corpus.

Bibliographic research confirms:
- Christaller 1879 remains the 3,600-proverb primary source witness.
- Kofi Ron Lange produced a dedicated English translation published by Edwin Mellen Press in 1990, catalogued as a translation of the 1879 work and extending to roughly 302 pages.
- R. S. Rattray's *Ashanti Proverbs* (1916) translates approximately 830 of Christaller's 3,600 Akan proverbs.
- C. A. Akrofi's *Twi Mmebusem: With English Translations and Comments* supplies a further Akan/Twi bilingual interpretive resource.
- Appiah, Appiah and Agyeman-Duah's *Bu Me Bɛ* supplies a later bilingual Akan collection of more than 7,000 proverbs and can serve as a contextual/variant check.

## Consequence
No corpus replacement is required.

The primary derivation remains anchored to Christaller 1879. Translation can be treated as a separate triangulated evidence layer.
