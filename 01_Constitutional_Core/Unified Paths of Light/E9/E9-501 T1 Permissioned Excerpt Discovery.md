# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-501 — T1 Permissioned Excerpt Discovery

A new translation-access route has been located.

The African Proverbs, Sayings and Stories project hosts historical “Weekly African Proverbs” pages containing blocks explicitly headed **Three Thousand Six Hundred Ghanaian Proverbs (From the Asante and Fante Language), J. G. Christaller**.

The page states that the material is copyright © 1990 Edwin Mellen Press and was **used by permission**, with all rights reserved.

### Importance
This is not a complete T1 copy, but it is stronger than an unattributed secondary paraphrase:
- the blocks identify the Christaller/Lange work;
- English proverb translations are reproduced with publisher permission;
- multiple translated items are visible in sequence.

### Limitation
The exposed page does not, in the search representation presently available, provide Christaller numbers beside each translated sentence.

Therefore these translations cannot yet be mechanically joined to T0 C-IDs without an independent alignment step.

### Status
