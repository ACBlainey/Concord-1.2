# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-357 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Poverty, Choice Compression and Autonomy Loss

R611–621 form one of the densest functional sequences yet encountered in E9.

Need produces:
- long-distance subsistence effort (R611);
- use of refuse resources (R612);
- pressure against giving up hope (R613);
- entry into dependency/slavery for care (R614);
- withdrawal from public affairs (R615);
- emergency repurposing of animal skins as food (R616);
- progressive substitution toward scarcity foods (R617);
- amplification of other burdens (R618);
- unpredictable onset (R619);
- conversion of free status into slavery (R620);
- burdensome labour timing (R621).

The recurring mechanism is **choice compression**.

As material resources fall, the practical option set contracts. Behaviour that may appear voluntary at the surface can therefore be strongly shaped by the disappearance of acceptable alternatives.

This is retained as an E9 occurrence pattern only; no cross-kernel comparison is made while the firewall remains closed.
