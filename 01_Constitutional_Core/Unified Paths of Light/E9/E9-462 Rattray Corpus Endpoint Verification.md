# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-462 — Rattray Corpus Endpoint Verification

The local working witness terminates the numbered proverb sequence at:

**830 / C3238 — “Ancient things remain in the ears. (Tradition survives).”**

Immediately after R830 the witness prints **FINIS** and proceeds into publisher material advertising other works.

Targeted searches for R831 through R845 returned no numbered proverb matches.

### Conclusion
For the Rattray 1916 selected corpus represented by the primary mechanical working witness:
- final numbered item = R830;
- no R831+ proverb sequence is present;
- R826–830 therefore constitute the final mechanical block of the Rattray selection.

This resolves the earlier approximate phrase “eight hundred odd” into an observed working-witness endpoint of 830 numbered entries.

This does **not** imply that the full Christaller corpus ends at 830. Rattray explicitly selected a subset from Christaller's much larger collection.
