# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1222 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Register 015: Resources, Scarcity and Stewardship

### Higher internally derived parents

**HC-P031 — Resource judgment should track functional use and actual sufficiency**

Subsumes:
- resource portions of PC-P008;
- PC-P059 — concentration/surplus and distributive function;
- PC-P096 — resource purpose/enabling expenditure;
- AR122/127/129/422/423/424/430/431/451/452/457/472.

Core structure:
> Resource value, sufficiency and appropriate allocation depend on actual function, need, accessibility, usable capacity and purpose rather than possession, magnitude or desire alone.

**HC-P032 — Stewardship should preserve sustaining and regenerative conditions**

Subsumes:
- PC-P029 — stewardship of sustaining conditions;
- AR124/271/425/427/428/454;
- continuity-reserve aspects of PC-P090.

Core structure:
> Resource use should preserve or regenerate the conditions that sustain future function rather than consume, neglect or degrade the basis on which continued use depends.

**HC-P033 — Scarcity changes feasible action without erasing all constraint**

Subsumes:
- PC-P087 — scarcity propagation through dependency;
- PC-P088 — scarcity changes feasible behaviour/resource choice;
- AR387/388/391/393/394/397/401/417;
- substitution portions of AR547.

Core structure:
> Scarcity can narrow feasible options, alter acceptable substitutes, propagate burden and compel costly action, while not automatically suspending all behavioural, extraction or responsibility constraints.

**HC-P034 — Material conditions shape practical autonomy**

Subsumes:
- resource/autonomy portions of PC-P089;
- AR390/396/414/453/569;
- overlaps with HC-P013 and HC-P014.

Core structure:
> Material need, loss and deprivation can change bargaining position, inducibility and the range of viable choices, thereby altering practical autonomy.

### Bridge

HC-P034 is explicitly a **BRIDGE** between resource structure and agency rather than evidence for merging those domains.
