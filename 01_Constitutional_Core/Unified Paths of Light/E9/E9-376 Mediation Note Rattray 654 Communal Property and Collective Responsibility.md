# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-376 — Mediation Note: Rattray 654 — Communal Property and Collective Responsibility

R654 states that if a substantial quantity of gold dust is in a town, “it is for the whole people.”

Rattray then makes a much broader interpretation:
- that the saying points to an earlier “system of communism”;
- that survivals remain in land tenure;
- that one person's private debts may be recoverable from the person's entire family;
- that this represents collective clan responsibility for the acts of an individual member.

### E9 treatment

These layers must remain separate.

**Proverb-level evidence:** local wealth can be conceptualised as having community-wide significance or claims.

**Rattray-level interpretation:** a specific historical theory of communal property and collective responsibility.

The latter is useful evidence about Rattray's 1916 ethnographic interpretation, but it cannot be mechanically treated as if the proverb itself established the full institutional theory.

This distinction is especially important for later comparison with other resource-stewardship occurrences. E9 must not manufacture a stronger collectivist doctrine by importing the recorder's explanatory expansion into the source-derived architecture.
