# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-104 — Provenance Exception: Rattray 136

Rattray 136 requires an explicit provenance exception.

The item appears in Rattray's numbered sequence but the source itself states:

> “This proverb is not among those in the 'Tshi Proverb' book.”

It therefore has:
- a valid Rattray locator: **136**;
- no valid Christaller locator;
- admissibility within the Rattray bridge stratum;
- no admissibility as evidence of coverage of the frozen Christaller corpus unless independently located there later.

### Arithmetic rule
Rattray 136 counts toward reconstruction of Rattray's published sequence and toward Rattray-derived occurrence analysis. It must **not** be used as one of the reconstructed Christaller-selected items when calculating Christaller coverage.

This distinction is necessary because Rattray's volume is a bridge/translation stratum, not an exact subset with perfect one-to-one Christaller membership.
