# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-428 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Irreversibility, Diagnostics and Preventive Action

R756, R757 and R763 describe three different positions in a harm-prevention cycle.

**Diagnosis — R756:** controlled tapping reveals the crack.

**Irreversibility — R757:** once speech is released it cannot simply be reclaimed.

**Prevention — R763:** a stick threatening the eye should have its point broken, not sharpened.

Together they support a practical sequence:
1. probe to identify hidden vulnerability;
2. recognise which actions become difficult or impossible to reverse;
3. reduce known hazards before they produce serious harm.

The items remain separate occurrences, but their adjacency strengthens an internal E9 pattern in which safety depends not only on reacting to harm but on diagnosis and anticipatory reduction of risk.
