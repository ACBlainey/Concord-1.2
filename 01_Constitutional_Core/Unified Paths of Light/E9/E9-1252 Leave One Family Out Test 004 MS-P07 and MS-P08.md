# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1252 — Leave-One-Family-Out Test 004: MS-P07 and MS-P08

### MS-P07 — Responsible resource relation

Removal attempt:
- MS-P06 covers care;
- MS-P08 covers stewardship/continuity;
- MS-P04 covers causal contribution.

**Failure of reconstruction.**

Continuity recovers stewardship-like use but not entitlement, sufficiency or differentiated claims. Responsibility can recognise contribution without defining resource claims. Care cannot govern all resource relations.

**Decision: INDEPENDENTLY NECESSARY ORGANISING FAMILY.**

### MS-P08 — Preservation through adaptive continuity

Removal attempt:
- MS-P02 develops capability;
- MS-P07 stewards resources;
- structural resilience conditions preserve alternatives;
- context operator permits adaptation.

**Failure of reconstruction.**

None supplies the substantive orientation toward preserving enabling function across time while adapting form to changed conditions. Resources and capability are components of continuity, not substitutes for it.

**Decision: INDEPENDENTLY NECESSARY ORGANISING FAMILY.**

### Result

Both resource relation and adaptive continuity survive independently.
