# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-36 — Bridge Coverage Audit Constraint

## Purpose
Prevent the interpretive block summaries from being mistaken for an item-level mechanical audit.

## Verified source facts
Rattray's 1916 volume contains approximately 830 proverbial sayings selected from Christaller's 1879 collection. The proverbs are presented in Twi with English translations. Rattray organised the material thematically and described his role as translator/commentator.

## Audit finding
E9-23 through E9-35 demonstrate that the major functional and thematic regions can be processed under the E9 coding rules, but those documents do not contain an 830-item occurrence ledger.

Therefore they cannot establish:
- exact processed-item count;
- exact ethical-yield count;
- exact zero-yield count;
- exact HOLD count;
- exact variant count.

## Rule
Bridge closure requires item-level source reconstruction from the Rattray text.
