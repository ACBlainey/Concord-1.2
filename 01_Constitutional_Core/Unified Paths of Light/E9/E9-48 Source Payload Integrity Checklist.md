## E9-48 — Source-Payload Integrity Checklist

When the identified text/PDF payload becomes available, the following checks must be performed before item coding:

- title page identifies Rattray 1916;
- Clarendon Press edition confirmed;
- author's note present;
- opening proverb section present;
- terminal proverb section present;
- page/sequence continuity checked;
- chapter boundaries recoverable;
- Twi and English distinguishable;
- notes distinguishable from proverb entries;
- obvious OCR loss marked;
- total sequence reconciled against the approximately 830 selected sayings.

A text representation may be used for mechanical indexing while page images remain authority for damaged OCR.
