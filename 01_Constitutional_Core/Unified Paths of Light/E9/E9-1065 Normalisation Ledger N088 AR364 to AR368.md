## E9-1065 — Normalisation Ledger N088: AR364–AR368

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR364 | R586 / C1583 | An action apparently directed at one agent can function as an indirect message to another; interpretation may require contextual inference. | Indirect signalling / contextual meaning. |
| AR365 | R587 / C2291 | Desire for another's resource does not create entitlement to it; acquisition should proceed through legitimate productive means rather than appropriation. | Desire versus entitlement / property boundary. |
| AR366 | R588 / C1907 | Agents can incorrectly exempt themselves from warnings or evidence they recognise as applicable to similarly situated others. | Self-exemption / inconsistent application. |
| AR367 | R589 / C1908 | Effective communication depends on recipient comprehension; compressed or indirect knowledge may require explanation suited to the receiver. | Recipient-sensitive communication. |
| AR368 | R590 / C1906 | Failure to learn or correct after an initial preventable failure can allow the same failure to recur. | Corrective learning / recurrence. |

AR364 and AR367 form a useful pair: indirect communication can be legitimate, but successful transmission still depends on whether the recipient can recover the intended meaning.
