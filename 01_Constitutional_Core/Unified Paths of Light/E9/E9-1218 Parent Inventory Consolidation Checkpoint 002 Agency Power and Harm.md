### Emerging architecture

Three observations are becoming increasingly important.

**1. Agency and harm are coupled but not reducible to one another.**  
Some constraints damage agency without direct physical harm; some protective interventions constrain immediate choice while preserving longer-term agency or safety.

**2. Power is structurally represented.**  
The corpus distinguishes dependency, contestability, authority legitimacy, status effects, causal responsibility and exploitation. This is richer than a simple prohibition on domination.

**3. Harm is process-sensitive.**  
Anticipation, vulnerability, exposure distribution and proportional response form different stages of a harm-management topology.

### Operator hypothesis strengthens

They may ultimately belong to an operator layer rather than a root-value layer. This remains an internal hypothesis to be tested later.
