# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-311 — Mechanical Ledger Block 106: Rattray 526–530

526 / C1403 = H — after a stranger leaves, something good or bad is said about the person. Post-visit social evaluation is visible, but no sufficiently specific ethical function is established.

527 / C1404 = Y — the local OCR prints the item number as 627, but the item lies between 526 and 528 and carries C1404. The proverb says a stranger can generate both money and bad debts. Interaction with outsiders can create both benefit and liability rather than being uniformly advantageous or harmful.

528 / C1406 = Y — a stranger may have large eyes but does not know the town's affairs as the local person does. Direct local embeddedness can produce context-specific knowledge that conspicuous observational capacity alone does not supply.

529 / C1408 = H — a stranger does not carry the head of the corpse. The saying indicates role restriction in funerary practice, but the transferable ethical function is underdetermined without the cross-referenced custom note.

530 / C1409 = Y — a guest may leave debts even when leaving no other material trace. Hosting or temporary association can create residual liabilities for the host.

### AR319 — R527/C1404
Interaction with an outsider can generate both benefits and liabilities; category membership alone does not determine the net consequence of the relationship.

### AR320 — R528/C1406
Local embeddedness can generate context-specific knowledge unavailable to an outsider despite strong general observational capability.

### AR321 — R530/C1409
Temporary association or hospitality can create continuing liabilities that remain after the visitor has departed.
