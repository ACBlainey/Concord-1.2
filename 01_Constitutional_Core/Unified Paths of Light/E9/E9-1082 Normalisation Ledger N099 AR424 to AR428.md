## E9-1082 — Normalisation Ledger N099: AR424–AR428

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR424 | R651 / C2936 | Scarce resources are not acquired by desire alone; practical acquisition requires an effective causal pathway. | Desire versus acquisition capability. |
| AR425 | R652 / C2938 | Resources can be productively deployed so that use generates additional resources rather than merely consuming or storing them. | Productive resource deployment. |
| AR426 | R654 / C2942 | Some locally held resources can carry community-wide claims or significance rather than functioning as purely isolated private holdings. | Collective resource claim. |
| AR427 | R655 / C2944 | Approaching scarcity can induce prudent resource behaviour, but prudence activated only near exhaustion leaves reduced adaptive margin. | Late stewardship / adaptive margin. |
| AR428 | R657 / C2946 | Resources require active stewardship; possession without maintenance or management does not guarantee continued retention. | Active resource stewardship. |

AR425 and AR428 jointly distinguish preservation from productive stewardship: maintaining a resource and deploying it to generate further capacity are different functions.
