## E9-171 — Mechanical Ledger Block 050: Rattray 246–250

246 / C2488 = Y — a bird remaining too long on a tree receives a stone. Prolonged exposure in one position can increase vulnerability to intervention or attack.

247 / C2489 = Y — there is insufficient meat in one bird to divide among many persons. A resource too small for the claimant population cannot satisfy all demands through division.

248 / C2490 = Y — a bird roosts with one of its own family; Rattray clarifies family through maternal descent. Social affiliation and proximity are structured by recognised kinship.

249 / C2492 = Y — a long-billed bird feeds on its own side of the river rather than reaching across; Rattray explicitly states the saying is often quoted in land disputes. Capability to reach beyond a boundary does not itself establish entitlement to resources beyond that boundary.

250 / C1765 = Y — if the dog, the stronger/expected hunter, has failed, the cat has still less reason to expect success. Expected capability should be evaluated relative to demonstrated performance under comparable conditions.

### AR128 — Rattray 246 / Christaller 2488
**Minimal function:** Remaining exposed in one position for too long can increase vulnerability to adverse intervention.
**Confidence:** high.

### AR129 — Rattray 247 / Christaller 2489
**Minimal function:** A resource whose total quantity is insufficient for the number of claimants cannot satisfy all demands merely by division.
**Confidence:** high.

### AR130 — Rattray 248 / Christaller 2490
**Minimal function:** Recognised kinship can structure affiliation, proximity and ordinary social association.
**Confidence:** high.

### AR131 — Rattray 249 / Christaller 2492
**Minimal function:** Capability to reach or exploit a resource beyond a recognised boundary does not by itself establish entitlement to that resource.
**Confidence:** very high because Rattray explicitly records use in land disputes.

### AR132 — Rattray 250 / Christaller 1765
**Minimal function:** Capability expectations should be calibrated against demonstrated performance under comparable conditions rather than assumed independently of evidence.
**Confidence:** moderate-high.
