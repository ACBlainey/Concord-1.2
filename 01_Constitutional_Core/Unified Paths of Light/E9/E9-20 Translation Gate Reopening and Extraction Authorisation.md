# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-20 — Translation Gate Reopening and Extraction Authorisation

## Review
MP-E9-05 was opened because systematic ethical coding directly from nineteenth-century Twi by the evaluating AI would collapse translation and interpretation.

The resolution is a layered translation architecture:
- Christaller 1879: corpus authority;
- Lange 1990: preferred complete aligned English translation;
- Rattray 1916: independent historical subset validation;
- Akrofi/later bilingual Akan sources: contextual and linguistic validation;
- AI translation: only flagged fallback support.

## Gate decision
The method no longer requires unrestricted AI self-translation as the evidential basis.

The original E9-A corpus remains unchanged and all prospective predictions remain frozen.

## Practical extraction rule
Where aligned published translation is directly available, code normally with translation provenance.

Where it is not directly available for a specific item, the occurrence may be processed only if independent translation/context evidence is sufficient. Otherwise it remains translation-HOLD until resolved.

No difficult item may be silently omitted.
