# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-302 — Mechanical Ledger Block 103: Rattray 511–515

511 / C3651 = H — a thousand wives are said to produce a thousand disputes. Relationship-number/coordination burden is plausible, but the saying is too gendered and hyperbolic for a secure general occurrence.

512 / C24 = Y — outsiders do not know the conversation between husband and wife. Close relationships contain private informational domains not accessible to external observers.

513 / C3652 = Y — the wife-as-blanket metaphor depicts a relationship that imposes costs when maintained yet creates loss when abandoned. Some valuable dependencies can involve unavoidable trade-offs rather than a cost-free option.

514 / C3653 = Y — a wife is explicitly said not to be meat that can be divided and sent to others. Despite the possessive historical frame, the metaphor rejects treating a person as a divisible transferable commodity.

515 / C3654 = H — an unfaithful wife does not mean the husband will sleep alone. The implied response/relationship logic is too ambiguous and gender-specific for secure extraction.

### AR309 — R512/C24
Close relationships can contain private informational domains that outsiders cannot reliably observe.

### AR310 — R513/C3652
Some valued relationships or dependencies involve real costs both in maintaining them and in abandoning them; evaluation may require recognising the trade-off rather than assuming a cost-free option.

### AR311 — R514/C3653
A person should not be treated as a divisible transferable commodity.
