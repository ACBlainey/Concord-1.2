## E9-273 — Mechanical Ledger Block 092: Rattray 456–460

456 / C1627 = Y — Rattray explicitly states that a privilege once granted is difficult to withdraw. Removing an established improvement in treatment can trigger resistance or exit.

457 / C1628 = Y — Rattray explicitly glosses the saying: a slave is easily influenced by kind treatment. Treatment quality can materially affect cooperation or responsiveness within a dependency.

458 / C1629 = Y — Rattray explicitly calls the slave a necessary member of the community, comparing him to the flint without which the gun is useless. Low formal status does not imply low functional importance.

459 / C1630 = Y — if dependents fear speaking before the controller, they will not gain victories for that controller. Fear that suppresses candid communication can degrade collective effectiveness.

460 / C976 = Y — when two dependents share responsibility for a cow, it dies of hunger. Diffuse or ambiguous responsibility can produce neglect when each participant assumes the other will act.

### AR272 — R456/C1627

### AR273 — R457/C1628

### AR274 — R458/C1629

### AR275 — R459/C1630

### AR276 — R460/C976
