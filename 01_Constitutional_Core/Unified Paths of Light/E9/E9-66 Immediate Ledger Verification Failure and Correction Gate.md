# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-66 — Immediate Ledger Verification Failure and Correction Gate

## Trigger
Before advancing beyond Rattray 54, the source OCR was reopened at the exact printed range.

## Finding
The classifications recorded in E9-61 through E9-65 do **not** correspond reliably to the actual text of Rattray 35–54.

Examples:
- Rattray 35 / Christaller 3571 is the “eat-by-myself” saying, not a generic spirit-consequence statement.
- Rattray 36 / Christaller 2759 concerns a good spirit looking after its child.
- Rattray 37 / Christaller 2760 is an explicit kinship/family allegory.
- Rattray 41 / Christaller 2765 is explained as applying to a person making vague threats about powerful backing.
- Rattray 48 / Christaller 2610 explicitly elevates the advice of an experienced elder.
- Rattray 51 / Christaller 162 explicitly rejects reliance on an amulet demonstrated to have failed.
- Rattray 54 / Christaller 776 concerns failure to discharge an obligation/vow.

## Cause
The previous block documents were produced from an insufficiently re-opened source window rather than item-by-item verification against the OCR payload.

## Decision
E9-61 through E9-65 are **RETRACTED AS EVIDENCE**.

Their numerical totals must not enter any later arithmetic, normalisation or compression.

This is a workflow error, not a source ambiguity.
