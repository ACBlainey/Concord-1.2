# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1249 — Leave-One-Family-Out Test 001: MS-P01 and MS-P02

### Test method

Remove one organising family while retaining:
- the other MS families;
- O-C operators;
- structural conditions;
- lower SP/HC distinctions only as recovery targets, not substitute parents.

A family fails removal if its distinctive substantive content can be recovered only by smuggling the removed orientation into another label.

### MS-P01 — Responsible information relation

Removal attempt:
- O-C01 reality/evidence responsiveness can test claims;
- O-C03 verification can warrant claims;
- MS-P04 can assign responsibility for falsehood;
- MS-P03 can protect agency against manipulative information.

**Failure of reconstruction.**

None requires agents to represent reality truthfully, communicate intelligibly, or respect legitimate disclosure boundaries. Operators tell us how to assess information, not what obligations govern the information relation itself.

**Decision: INDEPENDENTLY NECESSARY ORGANISING FAMILY.**

### MS-P02 — Development of effective capability

Removal attempt:
- MS-P03 protects existing agency;
- MS-P06 provides care/support;
- MS-P08 preserves continuity;
- O-C04 correctability permits revision.

**Failure of reconstruction.**

These can support development but do not generate the substantive orientation that capability itself may appropriately be developed, learned and corrected. Preserving current agency or continuity is not equivalent to developmental increase.

**Decision: INDEPENDENTLY NECESSARY ORGANISING FAMILY.**

### Result

MS-P01 and MS-P02 both survive leave-one-family-out reconstruction.
