### Explicit glosses
R533: Rattray states that the saying shows ignorance of the law excuses according to the represented native custom.

R542: Rattray explicitly identifies the English-speaking native interpreter and says the proverb summarises the position of a European official who must rely on an interpreter.

### Method caution
The European/African material is recorded as historical perception and interaction. Group-level generalisations, including R543's belief that all Europeans are prosperous, are not adopted as factual descriptions by E9.
