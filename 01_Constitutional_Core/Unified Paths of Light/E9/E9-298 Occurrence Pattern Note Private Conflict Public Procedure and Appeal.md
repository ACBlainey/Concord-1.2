# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-298 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Private Conflict, Public Procedure and Appeal

The extended note attached to R496 contains unusually detailed institutional material.

Rattray describes an oath that:
1. interrupts an escalating private quarrel;
2. transfers the matter into the chief's public jurisdiction;
3. triggers investigation;
4. can lead to an open hearing;
5. provides a route of appeal through successively higher courts.

The source also describes coercive and violent features of the historical system. Those are not converted into normative endorsements.

The important occurrence-level structure is narrower: **a formal procedure can move conflict away from private retaliation and into an accountable adjudicative process with review above the first decision-maker.**

This is internally derived from E9; no comparison to Concord governance is made while the firewall remains closed.
