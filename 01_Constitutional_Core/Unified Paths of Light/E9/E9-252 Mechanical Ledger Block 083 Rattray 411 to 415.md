# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-252 — Mechanical Ledger Block 083: Rattray 411–415

411 / C1304 = Y — when a chief compels payment/action for wrongdoing, the authority is said to come from the people. Exercise of coercive governing power is represented as deriving authority from the governed collective rather than from the officeholder alone.

412 / C1305 = H — when a chief is going to kill you, consulting divination is useless. Power asymmetry/futility is clear, but the source does not provide a sufficiently secure general ethical proposition.

413 / C1307 = H — excessive intimacy with a chief is said eventually to result in death. The warning about proximity to power is visible, but its intended ethical/normative function remains underdetermined.

414 / C1308 = Y — people do not speak their minds in a chief's presence but do so behind his back. Power asymmetry can suppress candid communication and create a divergence between public speech and privately held judgment.

415 / C1309 = Y — when a chief has plenty of milk, everyone drinks from him. Abundant resources held by a central/high-status agent can support broad distribution beyond the holder.

### AR247 — R411/C1304
Coercive governing authority can be represented as deriving legitimacy or operative authority from the governed collective rather than solely from the officeholder.

### AR248 — R414/C1308
Power asymmetry can suppress candid communication, producing divergence between public expression and privately held judgment.

### AR249 — R415/C1309
Resource abundance held by a central or high-status agent can support benefits distributed across a wider community.
