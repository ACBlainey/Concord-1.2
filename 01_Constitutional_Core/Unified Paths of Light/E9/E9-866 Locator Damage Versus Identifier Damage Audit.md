# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-866 — Locator-Damage versus Identifier-Damage Audit

Many overlap rows have damaged or absent **R-number markers** while retaining a clear C-ID. Examples include R491/C2068, R547/C1686, R551/C1691, R564/C3125, R574/C432 and several later items.

These are locator debts, not C-ID debts. They do not create additional identifier values and therefore cannot change unique-C cardinality unless their clear C-ID collides with another mapping.

The broad source duplicate probe already tests the numeric C-ID values themselves.
