# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-416 — Provenance Repair Note: Rattray 739 OCR Number Substitution

The local OCR prints the item between R738 and R740 as:

“730. Obi nko ahvd nd onka iikwaii. (207)”

The English translation is:

“No one (who) goes begging a meal is the one to serve out the soup.”

### Reconstruction
The item is recorded as R739/C207.

This is supported by:
- visible R738/C205 immediately before it;
- visible R740/C208 immediately after it;
- one complete intervening proverb;
- independently visible Christaller identifier C207;
- continuous pagination and text.

The printed OCR form “730” is retained in the provenance record rather than silently normalised.

No inference is made about the Christaller number.
