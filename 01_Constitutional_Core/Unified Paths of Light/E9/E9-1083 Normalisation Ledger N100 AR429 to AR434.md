## E9-1083 — Normalisation Ledger N100: AR429–AR434

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR429 | R658 / C2950 | Resource accumulation and provision for responsible succession or transfer are distinct stewardship problems. | Succession / continuity stewardship. |
| AR430 | R659 / C2951 | Resource preservation becomes self-defeating when refusal to spend prevents satisfaction of the purpose for which the resource exists. | Hoarding / purpose failure. |
| AR431 | R660 / C2952 | Undertaking a resource-dependent objective while refusing necessary expenditure can prevent successful execution. | Resource commitment / objective viability. |
| AR432 | R661 / C2953 | Productive labour undertaken to secure material resources is not inherently degrading. | Labour / material provision. |
| AR433 | R662 / C2954 | Social advantage can produce more lenient descriptions of behaviour that might be judged more negatively in others. | Status-conditioned description. |
| AR434 | R663 / C2955 | Visible simplicity or low-status presentation does not reliably indicate lack of underlying resources or status. | Appearance versus underlying resources. |

AR430 and AR431 preserve two different failures: refusing expenditure can defeat the resource's own basic purpose, or it can defeat an independently chosen resource-dependent objective.
