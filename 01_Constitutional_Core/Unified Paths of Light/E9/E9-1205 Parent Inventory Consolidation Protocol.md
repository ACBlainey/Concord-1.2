# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1205 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Protocol

### Purpose

The first traversal created an intentionally overcomplete intermediate vocabulary. Consolidation now asks which working parents are:

- genuinely distinct intermediate mechanisms;
- children of higher internally derived parents;
- bridges between parents;
- overly broad and in need of splitting;
- redundant descriptions of the same structural relation.

### Consolidation test

Two working parents may be placed beneath a higher parent only if:

1. the higher wording is induced from their shared structure;
2. each child's distinctive mechanism remains recoverable;
3. the abstraction does not erase reference point, agency, temporal, causal or relational distinctions;
4. the relation is supported by multiple source-linked propositions where possible;
5. no external ethical architecture is needed to justify it.

### Decision states

- **SUBSUME** — working parent becomes child of a justified higher parent.
- **SIBLING** — parents share a neighbourhood but cannot safely be subsumed further.
- **SPLIT** — parent is too heterogeneous.
- **BRIDGE** — parent legitimately connects two or more higher structures.
- **RETAIN** — parent remains an independent intermediate mechanism.
- **HOLD** — insufficient basis for decision.
- **RETIRE** — working identifier is superseded while provenance is preserved.

### Consolidation order

To minimise premature root formation:

1. consolidate capability/function parents;
2. consolidate evidence/information parents;
3. consolidate agency/dependency/power parents;
4. consolidate harm/hazard/protection parents;
5. consolidate relationship/reciprocity parents;
6. consolidate resource/scarcity/stewardship parents;
7. consolidate systems/continuity parents;
8. consolidate context/reference-point parents;
9. audit bridges and compound parents;
10. only then test whether higher-order families qualify as candidate roots.

### No target count

The result may contain more, fewer or differently partitioned higher parents than previous experiments. Consolidation stops when additional abstraction would remove meaningful ethical information.
