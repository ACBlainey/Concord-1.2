### OCR numbering defect
R739 is printed in the local OCR as “730.” It occurs after visible R738/C205 and immediately before visible R740/C208, and independently carries C207. The item is therefore source-reconciled as R739 while the OCR defect remains recorded.

All other Rattray numbers in this block are directly visible. No Christaller identifier was inferred.

### Classification restraint
R727 remains H because its palm-nut sharing image admits several possible abstractions without a source gloss.

R736 remains H because the day/night observation image may concern redundancy, mistrust, recognition or another mechanism.

R744 remains H because the source gives no explanatory note fixing the intended meaning of “Buy salt and eat.”

### R733 mediation
Rattray personifies “Had I known” and identifies it with remorse/regret. The admitted occurrence is limited to learning from experienced regret; the literary/editorial framing is not treated as independent Akan doctrine.
