# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-288 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Collective Scale, Local Failure and Internal Complexity

R475, R476 and R479 create a compact systems-level cluster.

R475 locates the beginnings of national ruin within the homes of the people.

R476 observes that if a nation can be destroyed, a single household is certainly not invulnerable.

R479 says that the contents of a people cannot all be perceived in one day.

Together these distinguish three properties of collective systems:
1. macro-level failure can emerge from distributed local conditions;
2. larger scale does not create immunity from failure;
3. internal complexity prevents reliable understanding through brief observation.

The cluster is retained at occurrence level without importing a modern systems theory vocabulary into the source itself.
