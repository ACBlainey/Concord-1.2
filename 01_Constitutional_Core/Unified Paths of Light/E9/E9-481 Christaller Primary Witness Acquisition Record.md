# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-481 — Christaller Primary Witness Acquisition Record

A direct digitised witness of J. G. Christaller's 1879 *Twi mmebusem, mpensã-ahansĩa mmoaano* has been located in the Library of Congress.

The catalogue identifies:
- author: J. G. Christaller;
- publisher: Basel German Evangelical Missionary Society;
- year: 1879;
- physical extent: xii + 152 pages;
- online formats: image and PDF;
- Library of Congress control number: 43019087.

The catalogue title explicitly describes the work as a collection of three thousand and six hundred Tshi proverbs, with variations, alphabetically arranged.

### Witness status
The Library of Congress scan is accepted as a high-authority **T0 page-image witness candidate** for Christaller corpus identity, pagination, source-language text, numbering and variants.

It is not automatically treated as an English translation witness.

### Rights
The Library of Congress record states that the digitised book is in the public domain and free to use and reuse.

### Gate result
The Christaller Expansion Entry Gate requirement to establish an accessible direct Christaller witness is satisfied at the bibliographic/page-image level.

Mechanical ethical extraction does not begin until item text and translation status are controlled.
