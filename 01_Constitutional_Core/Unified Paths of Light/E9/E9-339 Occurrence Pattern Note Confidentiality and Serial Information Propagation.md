# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-339 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Confidentiality and Serial Information Propagation

R584 is a compact but important information-propagation occurrence.

The proverb presents repeated declarations of friendship as the mechanism by which a secret becomes widely known.

The structure is not simply “friends gossip.” It identifies a propagation mechanism:

1. information is disclosed under a trust relationship;
2. the recipient forms another trust relationship;
3. the information is disclosed again;
4. serial trusted disclosures produce public spread.

This complements the earlier R516 confidentiality occurrence. A disclosure may be locally trusted at each individual step while the overall network nevertheless destroys confidentiality.

Thus confidentiality risk depends not only on the trustworthiness of one recipient but also on the recipient's subsequent disclosure behaviour and the topology of the information network.
