# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-403 — Mechanical Ledger Block 144: Rattray 716–720

716 / C141 = Y — one cannot both feast and become rich. Resource-intensive consumption competes with accumulation; incompatible resource objectives require trade-offs.

717 / C142 = H — one does not take half a loaf from the wayside and then ask who cut the other half. Rattray records a sexual/relationship case in which the proverb was quoted, but the transferable principle is too context-dependent to admit securely.

718 / C145 = Y — one should not enter an unwanted marriage merely to avoid being called shy. Social ridicule or reputational pressure does not by itself justify accepting a major personal commitment.

719 / C148 = H — one does not show the inside of an empty pot to an elder. The source cross-references R382, but the immediate excerpt does not provide enough explanation to establish a secure transferable ethical function.

720 / C149 = Y — a tanner summoned to the chief's house should not leave a hide soaking because the duration of detention is unknown. Before entering an obligation of uncertain duration, an agent should secure time-sensitive responsibilities that may otherwise be damaged through absence.
