# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-247 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Experience, Foresight and Epistemic Humility

R398 and R403 provide two distinct benefits associated with mature judgment.

R398 explicitly links experience with avoiding a position from which one cannot extricate oneself: foresight preserves recoverability.

R403 cautions against assuming that an apparently puzzling possession is irrational, because the old woman may have a reason unknown to the observer: judgment remains sensitive to missing information.

Together these produce an occurrence-level pairing:
- experience can improve anticipatory risk management;
- good judgment also requires humility about reasons and information one does not possess.

No deepest-root compression is attempted.
