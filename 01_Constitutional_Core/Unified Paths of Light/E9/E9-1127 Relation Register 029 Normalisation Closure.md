### Normalisation-stage structural picture

Without compressing into roots, the 571 propositions repeatedly populate several broad neighbourhoods:
- harm, hazard, vulnerability and protection;
- agency, dependency, coercion and effective choice;
- evidence, truth, causal attribution and correction;
- capability, development, learning and assistance;
- resource use, scarcity, stewardship and allocation;
- relationship, reciprocity, care and obligation;
- authority, status, hierarchy and accountability;
- privacy, communication and information boundaries;
- collective coordination, systems, continuity and resilience;
- context-, function-, domain- and reference-point-sensitive evaluation.

These are **descriptive neighbourhoods only**. They are not frozen parents and must not be treated as a predetermined architecture.

### Closure observation

The late corpus does not force a new external comparison or target architecture. Instead, it continues to elaborate mechanisms already visible internally while also adding distinctions such as derivative circumvention, internal-state epistemic limits, nested-system survivability and collective-memory continuity.
