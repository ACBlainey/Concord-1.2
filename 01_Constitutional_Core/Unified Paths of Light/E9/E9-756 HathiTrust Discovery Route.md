# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-756 — HathiTrust Discovery Route

The University of Pennsylvania Online Books Page identifies Christaller 1879 and points to **page images at HathiTrust**. Open Library independently identifies the same edition and LCCN 43019087 and notes that a Library of Congress digital form exists.

### Role
HathiTrust is added as another independent T0 page-image route. It is discovery/access redundancy, not a substitute for direct transcription.
