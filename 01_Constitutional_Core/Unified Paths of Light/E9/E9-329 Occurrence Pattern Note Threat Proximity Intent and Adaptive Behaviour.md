# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-329 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Threat Proximity, Intent and Adaptive Behaviour

R563–R565 produce a compact threat-assessment sequence.

R563 emphasises proximity: the person near enough to act deserves attention.

R564 distinguishes declared adverse intent (“I will take”) from declared beneficial intent (“I will give”).

R565 shows credible lethal threat changing the threatened person's behaviour toward attempted self-protection.

At occurrence level, threat is therefore represented through at least three separable variables:
- **access/proximity**;
- **stated intent**;
- **behavioural response of the threatened agent**.

This is retained as an internal E9 pattern. The violent execution material attached to R565 remains historical context only.
