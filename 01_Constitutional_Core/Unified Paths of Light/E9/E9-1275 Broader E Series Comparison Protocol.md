## E9-1275 — Broader E-Series Comparison Protocol

### Purpose

With the Akan architecture frozen and the primary X1–X7 prospective test complete, E9 may now compare the result with prior E-series architectures without altering AK-R1–AK-R8.

### Comparison dimensions

For each prior experiment distinguish:
1. substantive functional correspondence;
2. partition identity/non-identity;
3. operator placement;
4. structural emphasis;
5. residual elaboration;
6. source-medium effects.

### Anti-retrofitting rule

A similarity may be recorded only as a post-freeze mapping. No Akan family is renamed, merged or split to improve agreement.

### Independence caution

The comparison concerns independently executed derivations, not proof of historical independence among traditions. Shared history, transmission and methodology remain separate questions.

### Required sequence

1. compare Akan with E6 Hindu;
2. compare Akan with E7 Chinese;
3. compare Akan with E8 Sikh;
4. synthesize cross-E-series topology;
5. distinguish recurring functions from recurring partitions.
