# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-409 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Uncertainty, Safety Margins and Time-Sensitive Obligations

R720 and R724 give two different strategies for acting under uncertainty.

R720: before answering a summons of unknown duration, the tanner should not leave a hide in a time-sensitive state that may be ruined during absence.

R724: one should not test unknown river depth with both feet.

The shared structure is **preserving recoverability before uncertainty resolves**.

This can mean:
- securing processes that cannot safely be left unattended;
- avoiding full commitment of one's available support;
- maintaining a fallback position while gathering information.

This is more precise than generic caution. It concerns designing action so that uncertainty does not convert an information deficit into an avoidable irreversible loss.
