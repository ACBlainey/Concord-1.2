# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-439 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Capability Limits, Externalisation and Domain-Specific Value

R781–783 form a compact capability sequence.

R781: strength does not make one person equivalent to two workers — capability remains bounded.

R782: lack of strength can be displaced into criticism of the head-rest — agents can misattribute internal limitations to external tools.

R783: beauty does not pay debt — a valued attribute in one domain does not satisfy an obligation in another.

The combined structure resists three common capability errors:
1. treating high capability as unlimited capability;
2. externalising one's own limitation onto the environment;
3. transferring value from one domain into another where it does not perform the required function.

This extends E9's earlier distinction between possession of capability, relevant capability, and deployed capability.
