# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-19 — Later Akan Contextual Validation Firewall

## Purpose
Permit culturally informed interpretation without allowing later scholarship to manufacture the E9 result.

## Permitted use
Later Akan-language and Akan-authored resources may:
- clarify proverb meaning;
- document customary use;
- identify variants;
- challenge an overly literal English interpretation;
- expose collector or translator distortion.

## Prohibited use
They may not:
- add a proverb absent from the frozen primary corpus to rescue a missing function;
- select which Christaller entries are extracted;
- determine the Akan root architecture before primary normalisation;
- convert a scholar's philosophical synthesis into a primary occurrence.

## Special value
Later bilingual Akan collections demonstrate that proverb meaning is contextual and that Christaller's collection remains an important historical base for subsequent collections.

They therefore strengthen the need for contextual audit while preserving the primary-corpus firewall.
