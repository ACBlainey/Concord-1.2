## E9-194 — Mechanical Ledger Block 061: Rattray 301–305

301 / C500 = Y — an animal that is not going to bite does not show its teeth. Observable threat-signalling can provide evidence concerning likely harmful intent.

302 / C526 = H — the grass-eating animal is found in the grass. Environment-resource association is clear, but no sufficiently explicit ethical function is supplied.

303 / C528 = Y — an animal does not hide while leaving its tail visible. Concealment fails when a revealing component remains exposed.

304 / C529 = H — the animal bites wherever its mouth can reach. Reach constrains possible action, but the source does not establish a sufficiently clear ethical proposition.

305 / C541 = Y — all animals sweat, but hair makes the sweat difficult to notice; Rattray explicitly says the proverb means rich or powerful people can bear losses or troubles better than poor people, although both experience worries.
