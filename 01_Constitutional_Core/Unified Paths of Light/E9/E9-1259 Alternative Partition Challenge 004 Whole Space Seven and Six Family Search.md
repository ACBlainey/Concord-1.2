# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1259 — Alternative-Partition Challenge 004: Whole-Space Seven- and Six-Family Search

### Seven-family search

To reduce eight families to seven, at least one substantive family must:
1. merge with another;
2. become an operator/condition;
3. become derivable from the others.

Prior tests reject the strongest pairwise mergers.

Reclassification tests:
- information relation cannot become a pure operator because truthfulness/disclosure obligations are substantive;
- development cannot become a condition because development of capability is itself normatively supported;
- agency cannot become merely structural because viable choice is substantively protected;
- accountability cannot become an evidence operator because causal responsibility is independently normative;
- protection cannot become a vulnerability condition because non-externalisation/prevention are substantive;
- care cannot become a support structure because relation-generated obligation is substantive;
- resources cannot become a material condition because entitlement/use/claim rules are substantive;
- continuity cannot become temporal context because preservation/adaptation is substantively required.

**No valid seven-family partition found.**

### Six-family search

Any six-family partition requires at least two of the above reductions or a multi-family replacement.

Tested generic replacements:
- “responsible flourishing”
- “right relation”
- “sustainable cooperation”
- “responsible agency”

Each fails the anti-cheating rule: it either becomes vacuous or requires multiple independent obligations in its definition.

Because no valid first reduction from eight to seven survives, a six-family partition cannot be reached through lossless monotonic compression. Non-monotonic repartitioning was also considered, but candidate broad formulations merely relabel the same independent distinctions.

**No valid six-family partition found.**

### Lower counts

Counts below six would require still greater compound loading and are not supported by any internally generated single-orientation parent.
