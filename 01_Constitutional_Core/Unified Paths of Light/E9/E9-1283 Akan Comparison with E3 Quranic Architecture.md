# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1283 — Akan Comparison with E3 Qur'anic Architecture

### Comparison basis

E3 produced a Qur'an-derived ethical architecture later represented through ten higher-order attractors. E4's independently frozen comparison established that all ten E3 attractor functions were represented in E4 despite E4 freezing eight attractors.

### Akan relation

The frozen Akan architecture occupies the same broad recurrent functional regions identified in the E3/E4 topology:
- protection from unjustified harm;
- reliable relation to truth/reality;
- consequential responsibility/judgement;
- protected agency;
- care under vulnerability;
- responsible resource relation;
- constraint of power;
- correction/adaptation/resilience;
- capability/development;
- relational conditions limiting domination and destructive conflict.

E9 repartitions these functions into AK-R1–AK-R8 plus operator and structural layers.

### Important limitation

E3 and E9 differ greatly in medium and derivation conditions:
- scripture versus proverb/oral-medium witness;
- different historical and cultural lineages;
- different translation and mediation problems;
- different extraction granularity.

That makes functional recurrence informative, but it does not establish historical independence or universal natural law.

### Result

No major E3/E4 invariant functional region is absent from the frozen Akan architecture. Several are represented through combinations of Akan roots, operators and structural conditions rather than one-to-one roots.
