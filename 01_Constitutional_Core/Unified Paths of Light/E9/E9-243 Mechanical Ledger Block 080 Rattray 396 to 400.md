# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-243 — Mechanical Ledger Block 080: Rattray 396–400

396 / C2619 = Y — the elder does not remain inside while allowing the loom to become wet. Stewardship requires acting to protect useful productive infrastructure from preventable deterioration.

397 / C2620 = Y — even a strong and healthy old person will not live forever. Present capability does not erase finite duration or future vulnerability.

398 / C2622 = Y — Rattray supplies a detailed Ashanti explanation: experienced people position themselves so that if attacked during sleep they retain the ability to respond; the proverb means a person of experience will not put himself in a position from which he cannot extricate himself. Experience supports anticipatory risk management and preservation of an exit path.

399 / C2623 = Y — elders say they once did what younger people are doing now. Experience includes recognition of one's own prior developmental state, supporting perspective across generations.

400 / C2624 = H — if old people intend to roast and eat you, do not jump over a fire. A strategic warning may be present, but the source gives no explanatory gloss sufficient for a secure ethical abstraction.

### AR237 — R396/C2619
Responsible stewardship includes protecting useful productive infrastructure from foreseeable and preventable deterioration.

### AR238 — R397/C2620
Current strength or health does not remove finite duration or future vulnerability.

### AR239 — R398/C2622
Experience can support anticipatory risk management by avoiding positions from which recovery or escape would be impossible.

### AR240 — R399/C2623
Experienced agents can recognise that they previously occupied the developmental position now occupied by less experienced agents.
