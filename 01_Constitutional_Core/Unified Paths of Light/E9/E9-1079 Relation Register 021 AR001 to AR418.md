### Scarcity cascade extension

The earlier scarcity cascade now extends into additional domains:

**resource deprivation → reduced temporal control → altered behaviour/mobility → desperation-driven risk → social-status bias → reduced knowledge circulation/procedural attention → social separation → reduced basic-condition choice → vulnerability to coercion/extraction.**

Two countervailing structures are also visible:
- deprivation does not erase all agency (AR410);
- severe need does not erase all practical constraints or legitimate boundaries (AR417–AR418).

Thus scarcity constrains agency without necessarily eliminating agency.

### Status as an information distortion mechanism

Status effects now reach beyond prestige:
- competence can be misjudged (AR398);
- knowledge can fail to circulate (AR405);
- genuine value can be misclassified (AR408);
- identical resources can be evaluated differently by user identity (AR412);
- procedural attention can be reduced (AR415).

This should remain distinct from material scarcity itself during later compression.
