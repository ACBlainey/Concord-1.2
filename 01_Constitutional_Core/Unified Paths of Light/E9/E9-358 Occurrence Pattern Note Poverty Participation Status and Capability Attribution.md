# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-358 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Poverty, Participation, Status and Capability Attribution

R615 and R622 expose two different social consequences of poverty.

R615: the poor person remains at home rather than mixing in public affairs. This is participation loss produced through material condition rather than an explicit institutional ban.

R622: the poor person is “reckoned a fool.” This is attributional distortion: material condition becomes evidence, in observers' judgments, for low intelligence or competence.

R620 adds a third dimension: poverty can alter formal/practical status itself by turning a free person into a dependent slave.

Thus poverty operates in the source through at least three channels:
1. reduced practical participation;
2. biased capability attribution;
3. reduced autonomy/status.

These should remain analytically separate during later compression.
