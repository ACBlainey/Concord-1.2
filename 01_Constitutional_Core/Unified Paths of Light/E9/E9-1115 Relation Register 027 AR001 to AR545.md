### Emerging domain-separation principle

The corpus increasingly resists invalid transfer across domains:
- capability in one task does not imply capability in another;
- value or prestige in one domain does not discharge obligations in another;
- resource sufficiency depends on intended function;
- observation does not equal explanation;
- self-asserted status or quality does not establish the relevant property.

This may later compress into one or more higher-order structures, but at normalisation stage the distinctions remain preserved.

### Privacy as both norm and system property

Privacy now appears in two forms:
1. **normative boundary** — some matters should not automatically be made public (AR528);
2. **transmission reality** — apparently informal/open settings may still leak information to unintended recipients (AR544).

Thus confidentiality depends both on legitimate information boundaries and on realistic understanding of transmission pathways.
