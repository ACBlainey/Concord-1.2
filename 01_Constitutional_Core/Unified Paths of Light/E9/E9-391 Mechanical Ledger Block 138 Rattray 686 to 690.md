# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-391 — Mechanical Ledger Block 138: Rattray 686–690

686 / C3089 = Y — water kept too long in a calabash becomes foul. A useful resource can deteriorate under prolonged storage; preservation requires attention to time and condition rather than assuming retained possession means retained quality.

687 / C3090 = Y — one passes through muddy water in order to draw pure water. Obtaining a higher-quality resource can require tolerating temporary difficulty or contamination in the access path without accepting the lower-quality state as the endpoint.

688 / C3091 = Y — when the river dries, the fish eagle can catch the fish. Environmental/resource contraction can remove protective conditions and increase the vulnerability of agents that previously depended on them.

689 / C3093 = Y — the local OCR prints the item number as 680. Water insufficient for bathing can still be sufficient for drinking. A resource inadequate for one purpose may remain adequate for another; sufficiency is function-relative.

690 / C3094 = H — among many waters, the sea is the elder. The saying establishes relative seniority/status by scale or category, but the transferable ethical function is underdetermined.

### AR454 — R686/C3089
A useful resource can deteriorate through prolonged storage; retained possession does not guarantee retained quality.

### AR455 — R687/C3090
Access to a higher-quality resource may require tolerating temporary difficulty in the path without treating the inferior condition as the desired endpoint.

### AR456 — R688/C3091
Environmental or resource contraction can remove protective conditions and increase vulnerability.

### AR457 — R689/C3093
Resource sufficiency is function-relative: inadequacy for one purpose does not establish inadequacy for another.
