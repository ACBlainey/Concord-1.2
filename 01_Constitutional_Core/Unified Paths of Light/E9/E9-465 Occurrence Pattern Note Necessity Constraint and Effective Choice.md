# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-465 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Necessity Constraint and Effective Choice

R828/C2172 states: “What strength makes taboo is hunger.”

The wording places material need in direct tension with a rule of avoidance.

The mechanically admissible point is not that hunger automatically nullifies every prohibition. Rather, it demonstrates that the practical force of preferences, abstentions and avoidance rules depends on the alternatives actually available to the agent.

This strengthens earlier E9 material distinguishing:
- nominal willingness from effective opportunity;
- formal choice from constrained choice;
- stated preference from preference under inaccessible alternatives.

A choice architecture that ignores severe material necessity can therefore misdescribe how much agency the person actually possesses.

No root assignment is made at this stage.
