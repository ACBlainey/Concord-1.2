# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-216 — OCR Provenance Exception: Item Printed “383” Between R332 and R334

The local working witness visibly shows:

**383.** [gun bursts and wounds the hunter...] **(3389)**

between R332 and R334.

Sequence strongly suggests an OCR corruption of the Rattray number, but E9 methodology prohibits silent correction.

### Treatment
- C3389 is visible and retained.
- The proverb content is visible and may be ethically classified.
- The Rattray number is recorded exactly as OCR-visible: 383.
- Sequence-based normalisation is deferred until an independent witness or page image verifies the printed number.
- AR189 therefore references the OCR-visible identifier rather than asserting a corrected number.

This avoids repeating earlier E9 failures caused by inferred numbering.
