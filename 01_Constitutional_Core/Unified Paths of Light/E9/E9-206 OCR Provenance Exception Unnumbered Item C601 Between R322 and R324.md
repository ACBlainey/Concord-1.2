# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-206 — OCR Provenance Exception: Unnumbered Item C601 Between R322 and R324

The local working witness contains, immediately after R322 and before R324:

> Ohqmofo ahoa a oho na oso. (601)

followed by the translation that to the hunter the animal that escapes is always the big one, with an explicit comparison to No. 321.

The OCR does **not** display a Rattray item number on this entry.

### Frozen-rule application
Although sequence strongly suggests a missing printed number, E9 methodology prohibits inferred Rattray identifiers.

Therefore:
- the source item itself is visible;
- Christaller 601 is visible;
- its Rattray identifier remains unresolved;
- it is recorded as X/quarantined for current arithmetic;
- no AR occurrence is issued from it in this pass.

Independent page-image or alternate-witness verification may later repair the identifier and permit reclassification.
