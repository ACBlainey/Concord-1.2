## E9-961 — Normalisation Ledger N019: AR057–AR060

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR057 | R151 / C1924 | Effective care may be delivered through means adapted to the caregiver's actual capabilities rather than through one presumed standard mechanism. | Care-function versus implementation form. |
| AR058 | R152 / C1925 | Functional effectiveness should be judged by achieved function rather than by whether the method appears awkward, unconventional or unimpressive. | Outcome-sensitive evaluation; appearance/function distinction. |
| AR059 | R153 / C1926 | An agent's own behaviour can create or expose a vulnerability that would otherwise remain protected. | Endogenous vulnerability / self-exposure. |
| AR060 | R155 / C1929 | Protective withdrawal from danger need not be rejected merely because withdrawal may attract shame or ridicule. | Safety versus status pressure. |

AR057 preserves plurality of effective care mechanisms rather than assuming a single correct form. AR060 concerns social pressure distorting protective choice, not a general preference for withdrawal.
