# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-797 — NC-ID Alignment Negative-Evidence Rule

Failure to find an NC-ID proverb in web-indexed Christaller text does **not** prove that the proverb is absent from Christaller. The frozen 1879 corpus is not presently exposed as a complete searchable item-level text stream.

Therefore:
- no-hit = `NOT LOCATED`, not `ABSENT`;
- Rattray-only hit = no alignment advance;
- later Akan attestation = contextual corroboration only;
- only direct T0 source-language correspondence can establish an NCID-MATCH.
