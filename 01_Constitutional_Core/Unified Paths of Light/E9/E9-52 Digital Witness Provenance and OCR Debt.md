# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-52 — Digital-Witness Provenance and OCR Debt

## Witness
Cornell University Library scan distributed through Internet Archive, object `cu31924027161961`.

## Representation
Internet Archive DJVU OCR text is used for mechanical navigation and extraction.

## OCR condition
The text is highly usable for:
- printed proverb number;
- English translation;
- Christaller cross-reference;
- explanatory notes;
- source sequence.

Historical Twi typography is substantially more vulnerable to OCR corruption.

## Rule
The English translation and printed locators may support initial mechanical classification. A damaged Twi OCR form is not silently repaired.

Where the exact Twi wording materially affects interpretation, the occurrence receives translation/OCR debt and must be checked against the scan or another representation.

## Important provenance strength
Rattray states that he did not rely on his own language knowledge alone and that the sayings were verified and re-verified through inquiry among Ashantis.

This strengthens the bridge translation layer while not removing Rattray's historical/editorial selection bias.
