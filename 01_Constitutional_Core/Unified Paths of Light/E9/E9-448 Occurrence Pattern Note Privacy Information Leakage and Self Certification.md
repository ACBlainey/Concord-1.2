# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-448 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Privacy, Information Leakage and Self-Certification

R793 and R795 extend two existing E9 epistemic boundaries.

R793: “a path has ears.” Information can travel beyond the intended audience even in an apparently open or impersonal setting.

R795: salt does not announce its own pleasant taste. Quality should not be established solely through self-certification.

These complement earlier occurrences:
- R774's private matter should not be displayed in the marketplace;
- R761's positive quality is more credibly recognised by another than self-proclaimed.

The emerging structure separates:
1. **control of information disclosure** — assume that context may leak information;
2. **control of evaluative authority** — do not make self-assertion the sole basis for judging one's own quality.

Both reduce dependence on an agent's preferred presentation of reality.
