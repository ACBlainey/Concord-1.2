## E9-129 — Mechanical Ledger Block 036: Rattray 176–180

176 / C2098 = Y — a spider that does not truly wish to spin chooses a busy road where passers-by will break the web. Conduct can be arranged so that predictable external interference supplies an excuse for non-performance.

177 / C2099 = Y — the explicit saying tells the addressee to attend either to guarding the beans or to leading the dance; Rattray's story then states that apparently friendly advice may conceal an ulterior motive. The occurrence supports both task-focus and scrutiny of advice where the adviser may benefit from diverted attention.

178 / C2100 = Y — the spider “has not sold words”; Rattray explains that the words/stories were given freely. Knowledge or expressive material can be shared without transactional payment.

179 / C1240 = Y — Father Spider did wrong, was driven away, and lacked another place to go. Wrongdoing produces exclusionary consequences, while the story also exposes the vulnerability created by having no alternative refuge.

180 / C1241 = Y — Rattray explicitly glosses the saying as “you are not indispensable” and notes that another can take the person's place. A role should not be assumed to depend uniquely on its current holder when substitutes exist.

**Confidence:** high.

### AR079 — Rattray 179 / Christaller 1240

**Confidence:** moderate-high.

### AR080 — Rattray 180 / Christaller 1241
