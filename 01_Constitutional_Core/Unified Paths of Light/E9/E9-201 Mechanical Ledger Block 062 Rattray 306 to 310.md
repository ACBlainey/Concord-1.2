# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-201 — Mechanical Ledger Block 062: Rattray 306–310

306 / C956 = Y — when an army is defeated, a horn is not blown in its honour. Recognition or honour is contingent on outcome rather than automatically attached to participation.

307 / C957 = Y — the slain are not counted before the hostile army has been routed. Assessment of losses is deferred until the immediate contest is resolved; evaluation can depend on appropriate procedural timing.

308 / C958 = Y — an army is driven back by courage, not by insults however numerous. Verbal aggression should not be confused with effective capability.

309 / C959 = H — the victorious army kills the challenger who shouts insults but spares the brave warrior. The source contrasts empty provocation and recognised bravery, but the violent wartime framing makes a transferable ethical function insufficiently secure at this stage.

310 / C960 = Y — an army does not know what is behind another army. An opposed agent may lack reliable information about conditions outside its observation.

### AR170 — R306/C956
Recognition or honour may depend on actual outcome rather than participation alone.

### AR171 — R307/C957
Evaluation can require appropriate timing; some consequences cannot be reliably assessed before the relevant process is resolved.

### AR172 — R308/C958
Verbal aggression or repeated insult is not equivalent to effective capability.

### AR173 — R310/C960
Agents in opposition can operate with materially incomplete knowledge of conditions outside their observation.
