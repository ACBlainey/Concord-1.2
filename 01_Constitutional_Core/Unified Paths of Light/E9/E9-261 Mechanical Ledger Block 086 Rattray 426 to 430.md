# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-261 — Mechanical Ledger Block 086: Rattray 426–430

426 / C2756 = Y — Rattray explains that the war captain does not take the oath again before fighting because it has already been taken. A valid prior commitment need not be redundantly repeated each time the already-covered duty is exercised.

427 / C783 = H — whatever a chief wishes is done for him. The saying records extreme responsiveness to authority, but the source does not establish whether this is endorsement, criticism or description.

428 / C2432 = Y — people desire chiefly status but, when unable to obtain it, say ruling has worries. Evaluation of a role can be distorted after frustrated acquisition; stated reasons may rationalise an unattained desire.

429 / C798 = H — the poor chief's grandson carries a gun but not his own mat because carrying the mat is considered degrading. Status-preserving behaviour under poverty is visible, but no secure transferable ethical function is selected.

430 / C797 = H — a free/noble man is said only to lack something when the lack is very great. The status-based claim is insufficiently clear as a transferable ethical proposition.

### AR256 — R426/C2756
A valid prior commitment need not be redundantly renewed whenever the already-covered duty is exercised.

### AR257 — R428/C2432
Stated evaluation of a desired role or good may be distorted after failure to obtain it; post-failure reasons should not automatically be treated as independent evidence of prior preference.
