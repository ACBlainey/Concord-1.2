### Emerging temporal architecture

Time now appears in several distinct ethical functions:
- uncertainty about future conditions;
- acting before an opportunity closes;
- avoiding delay in search for an optimum;
- developmental stages that cannot simply be skipped;
- correction proportional to how long a condition developed;
- delayed failure after a sustaining source is removed.

Later compression should not flatten these into a generic instruction to “act in time.” They concern prediction limits, opportunity windows, maturation, repair and latent dependency.

### Assistance without agency erasure

The corpus now gives both sides of an important distinction:
- assistance can be necessary and can expand effective capability;
- some actions still have to be performed by the affected agent;
- accepting voluntary support does not itself negate agency.

This may become structurally important during parent-child compression.
