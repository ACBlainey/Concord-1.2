### Extended source notes
R554: Rattray discusses disability-based exclusion from chieftaincy, historical killing of an infant with six fingers, and offers only a tentative interpretation of the proverb. Because the gloss is explicitly uncertain, the item remains H.

R562: Rattray explicitly says the herbalist must be given time to search for the suitable plant or root.

R565: Rattray describes historical execution practices and deliberate physical prevention of oath-swearing by the condemned. These details are retained as mediation/context and are not treated as ethical prescriptions.
