# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-306 — Provenance and Mediation Note: Rattray 508, 509 and 517

### R508
The local witness visibly numbers the item as R508 but provides no Christaller identifier. No mapping is inferred. Its gendered moral judgement remains H.

### R509
The source reports very young girls being married and living in husbands' households while saying sexual intercourse did not usually occur until puberty.

This is historical evidence, not ethical endorsement. The only admitted occurrence is the source's own functional distinction between formal relationship status and developmental readiness.

### R517
The proverb advises an outsider not to hurry to intervene when a man is fighting with his wife because there is probably a reason.

The source does not establish that the violence is justified, proportionate or consensual, nor does it provide the wife's perspective. Therefore the item is retained as evidence of a historical norm concerning intervention but is not used to derive a general ethical rule against intervention.

These cases illustrate the E9 rule that source fidelity and ethical extraction are separate operations.
