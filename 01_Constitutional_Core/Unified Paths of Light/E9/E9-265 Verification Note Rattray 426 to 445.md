### Source cautions
R428: Rattray notes that the original gives a verb in the positive but says this is probably an error. The translated sense supplied by Rattray is retained; no independent correction is asserted.

R440: Rattray explicitly says the saying is “not strictly accurate,” noting cases where legitimate heirs were passed over and a stool given to a slave. The item therefore remains H.

R443 begins an extended Rattray note on Ashanti slavery. The note contains Rattray's historical interpretation and evaluative framing. E9 preserves it as mediation/context rather than treating it as ethical endorsement or as a sufficient account of lived conditions.

### Method rule
Historical hierarchy, slavery, warfare and inherited status are not normalised into ethical prescriptions merely because the source records them.
