# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-259 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Changing Convention and Institutional Variability

R410 explicitly states, through Rattray's gloss, that “Times and manners change.”

This is significant within the transition from age/experience to political authority because it places institutional or customary practice inside historical variability.

The occurrence supports a narrow proposition:
**a currently observed social practice need not be treated as invariant merely because it is established practice.**

This should not be expanded into a general theory of progress or historical development. The source establishes change, not direction or improvement.
