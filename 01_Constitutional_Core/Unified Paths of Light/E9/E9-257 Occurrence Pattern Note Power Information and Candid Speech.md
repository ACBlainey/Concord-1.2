# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-257 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Power, Information and Candid Speech

R414, R418 and R419 reveal an important asymmetry in political information.

R418–419 portray the chief as receiving information through extremely broad and numerous channels.

R414 simultaneously says that people do not speak their minds in the chief's presence, while they do so behind his back.

Thus **information quantity and information candour are not the same property**. A powerful office may receive information from many directions while still suppressing truthful direct speech through the social effects of power.

This distinction is potentially important for later governance compression and anti-capture analysis, but the E9 firewall remains closed.
