## E9-976 — Normalisation Ledger N031: AR108–AR111

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR108 | R220 / C1641 | When return or access is reliably predictable, immediate pursuit may be unnecessary and restraint can be more efficient. | Predictability-sensitive restraint. |
| AR109 | R221 / C1642 | Timely fulfilment of an expected return or responsibility can prevent avoidable costs from falling on the accountable party. | Timeliness / responsibility / externalised cost. |
| AR110 | R224 / C2331 | Excessive confidence in one's own wisdom can generate serious error; epistemic capability requires humility and openness to correction. | Epistemic self-correction. |
