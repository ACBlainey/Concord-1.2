# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-18 — Historical Translation-Subset Validation Protocol

## Purpose
Use Rattray's historical translation subset as an independent check on the E9 translation layer rather than as a replacement corpus.

## Method
Where a Christaller proverb is represented in Rattray:
1. retain Christaller as corpus authority;
2. compare the English rendering with the primary working translation;
3. record materially different semantic readings;
4. test whether the ethical functional code survives the difference;
5. downgrade or HOLD unstable occurrences.

## Why this is valuable
Rattray predates the modern E-series hypotheses by more than a century and cannot have been shaped by the present target architecture.

Agreement therefore supplies translation robustness without changing the frozen source boundary.

## Limitation
The Rattray subset is not a random sample and cannot by itself establish whole-corpus translation reliability.
