# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-497 — T3 Bilingual Fallback Candidate Register

A substantial later bilingual Akan proverb source has been identified:

**Peggy Appiah, Kwame Anthony Appiah and Ivor Agyeman-Duah, *Bu Me Bɛ: The Proverbs of the Akan*.**

Bibliographic descriptions characterize it as:
- bilingual;
- containing more than 7,000 Akan proverbs;
- the most extensive bilingual Twi proverb dictionary published since Christaller.

### Role in E9
This is a potentially valuable T3 contextual/translation witness.

It is **not assumed to be Christaller-number aligned**.

Therefore it cannot replace Lange mechanically unless an explicit correspondence between an entry and a Christaller item is established.

### Additional T3
C. A. Akrofi's *Twi Mmbusɛm: Twi Proverbs with English Translations and Comments* remains another relevant later bilingual/context witness.

Both belong below direct aligned T1 in the frozen hierarchy.
