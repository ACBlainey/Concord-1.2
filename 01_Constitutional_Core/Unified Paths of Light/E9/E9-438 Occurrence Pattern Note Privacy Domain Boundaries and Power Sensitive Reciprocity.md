# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-438 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Privacy, Domain Boundaries and Power-Sensitive Reciprocity

R773, R774 and R778 distinguish three kinds of boundary.

R773: one's house is one's own house — personal/household domain.

R774: a private matter is not something displayed in the marketplace — informational confidentiality.

R778: play should be with someone of one's own size — reciprocal interaction changes character when capability/power is strongly asymmetric.

These are not the same rule. Together, however, they show that legitimate interaction depends on respecting boundaries of:
- place/domain;
- information;
- power/capability.

R778 is particularly useful because it prevents “same action” from automatically meaning “same interaction.” Equality of form can conceal inequality of effective impact.
