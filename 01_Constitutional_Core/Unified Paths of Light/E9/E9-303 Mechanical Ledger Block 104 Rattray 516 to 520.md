# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-303 — Mechanical Ledger Block 104: Rattray 516–520

516 / C2858 = Y — information that cannot safely be made public should not automatically be disclosed even inside an intimate relationship. Relational closeness does not eliminate the need to assess confidentiality and downstream disclosure risk.

517 / C1580 = H — the proverb advises hesitation before intervening in a husband fighting with his wife because he probably has a reason. Because the source can operate as justification for intimate violence and supplies no protective qualification, it is preserved as historical evidence rather than converted into an ethical norm.

518 / C2162 = Y — a person seeking a wife does not speak contemptuously of women. Pursuing a relationship or benefit from a class while expressing contempt for that class is practically and normatively incoherent.

519 / C3434 = Y — the honeymoon makes the yams taste sweet. Early novelty or positive relational conditions can bias evaluation of ordinary experience; initial impressions may not represent longer-term conditions.

520 / C3463 = Y — repeated corrective speech to a persistently badly behaving child produces exhaustion. Developmental responsibility can impose cumulative burdens on the caregiver/instructor when correction repeatedly fails.

### AR312 — R516/C2858
Relational closeness does not remove confidentiality constraints; disclosure should account for the sensitivity of information and its potential propagation.

### AR313 — R518/C2162
Seeking relationship or benefit from a group while expressing contempt for that group is internally inconsistent and undermines the sought relationship.

### AR314 — R519/C3434
Novelty or unusually positive early conditions can bias evaluation; initial experience need not represent stable long-term conditions.

### AR315 — R520/C3463
Repeated unsuccessful correction can impose cumulative developmental burden on the responsible caregiver or instructor.
