# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-762 — Basel Mission Manuscript Relation Discovery

The Basel Mission Archives record for D.II.g.03 explicitly records a related **handwritten version: D-20.39.13**.

### Research significance
This creates a potential upstream witness for difficult printed readings, variant history, or damaged typography. It must not silently replace the frozen printed 1879 T0 corpus, but may later serve as a provenance/variant witness if direct inspection becomes available.

### Rule
Any manuscript-derived reading must be labelled manuscript evidence and kept distinct from the mechanically transcribed 1879 printed text.
