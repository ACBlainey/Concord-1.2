## E9-292 — Mechanical Ledger Block 099: Rattray 491–495

491 / C2068 = Y — the printed item number is absent from the OCR line, but sequence and cross-references explicitly identify this proverb as No. 491; it states that loss of one's mother means loss of kindred. Within the source's matrilineal system, loss of a central relational node can radically reduce a person's effective kinship support.

492 / C2069 = Y — even when one's mother is not good, she remains one's mother. Defect or disappointment in a close relation does not automatically erase the underlying relationship.

493 / C898 = Y — following behind one's father teaches one to walk like him. Repeated proximity and observation can transmit behaviour without explicit instruction.

494 / C912 = Y — a mother admonishing her own children can also benefit a visiting motherless child. Developmental guidance directed at some children can produce useful spillover to other learners present.

495 / C1211 = Y — one need not rush elsewhere to see an event that is already coming to one's own family doorway. When access to a desired opportunity is reliably approaching, immediate pursuit elsewhere may be unnecessary.
