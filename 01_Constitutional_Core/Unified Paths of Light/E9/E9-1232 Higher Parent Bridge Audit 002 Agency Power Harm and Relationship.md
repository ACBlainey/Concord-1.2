# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1232 — Higher-Parent Bridge Audit 002: Agency, Power, Harm and Relationship

### Bridge findings

**HC-P013 ↔ HC-P034 — SUBSUMPTION OF DOMAIN IMPLEMENTATION**

Material conditions shaping practical autonomy are a resource-domain implementation of the more general rule that agency requires viable choice.  
Decision: **HC-P034 → HC-P013** as a domain implementation.

**HC-P014 ↔ HC-P016 — BRIDGE**

Dependency frequently generates asymmetric power, but power asymmetry also arises without dependency.  
Decision: retain both.

**HC-P017 ↔ HC-P023 — BRIDGE**

Creating/exploiting vulnerability often externalises harm, but harm can be displaced without deliberate exploitation.  
Decision: retain both.

**HC-P022 ↔ HC-P028 — PARTIAL OVERLAP**

Vulnerability-sensitive protection and vulnerability-sensitive care share beneficiary sensitivity. Protection responds specifically to threat/hazard; care includes support under need without a threat source.  
Decision: retain as siblings under a possible later vulnerability-sensitive obligation operator.

**HC-P015 ↔ HC-P028 — BRIDGE**

Care can support an agent without erasing residual agency.  
Decision: preserve the distinction between **support obligation** and **agency preservation during support**.

**HC-P020 ↔ HC-P030 — GENERALISATION RELATION**

HC-P020 resists transfer of responsibility by association. HC-P030 generalises non-transfer to identity, property, control, entitlement and responsibility.  
Decision: responsibility non-transfer becomes a domain child of HC-P030, while HC-P020 retains positive causal-attribution content.

### Compound warning

Any candidate higher parent combining “care/protection of vulnerability” with “preservation of agency” would be compound. The corpus represents both and their interaction, but neither entails the other.
