# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-212 — Mechanical Ledger Block 067: Rattray 331–335

331 / C3589 = Y — because no one accompanied the hunter, no witness can contradict his account. Claims about privately observed events have weaker independent verification when no other observer was present.

332 / C3388 = Y — the gun performs warlike deeds only when a person operates it. Tool capability can depend on an agent who activates and directs it.

333 / OCR-visible printed number **383** / C3389 = Y — when a gun bursts and wounds the hunter, a person who merely happens to eat venison is not blamed. Responsibility should not be assigned to an unrelated beneficiary who did not cause the harmful event. The apparent Rattray-number OCR error is preserved rather than silently corrected.

334 / C3390 = Y — a gun bursting in Europe does not wound a person in Africa. Harm ordinarily requires an operative causal connection rather than mere category association across disconnected contexts.

335 / C3391 = Y — when one's guns are few, one's words are few. Assertive signalling is constrained by underlying material capability; rhetoric unsupported by capacity is limited.

### AR187 — R331/C3589
Claims concerning events with no independent observer have reduced external verifiability.

### AR188 — R332/C3388
A tool's realised capability can depend upon an agent who activates or directs it.

### AR189 — OCR-visible R383/C3389
Responsibility for harm should not be assigned merely because someone later benefits from an outcome when that person did not cause the harmful event.

### AR190 — R334/C3390
Attribution of harm requires an operative causal connection; remote category association alone is insufficient.

### AR191 — R335/C3391
Credible assertive signalling is constrained by the material capability that supports it.
