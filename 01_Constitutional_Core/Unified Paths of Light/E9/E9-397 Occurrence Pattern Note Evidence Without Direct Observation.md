# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-397 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Evidence Without Direct Observation

R667/R696 and R701 now provide complementary evidence structures.

R667 and R696 show precursor evidence: smoke or wind can indicate an event before the full event arrives.

R701 shows consequence evidence: rain may fall unseen at night, yet the changed ground later reveals that it occurred.

This gives three temporal positions for evidence:
1. precursor evidence before an event;
2. direct observation during an event;
3. consequence evidence after an event.

The absence of position 2 does not invalidate positions 1 or 3.

This matters for E9 because several earlier occurrences also warned against equating “not directly seen” with “not real.” The present sequence provides a compact source-internal model of inference across time.
