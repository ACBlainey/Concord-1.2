# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1191 — Parent-Child Compression Register 017: AR354–AR373

### Existing parents extended

**PC-P005 — Evidence-responsive causal attribution**
- AR354 — prior hostility should not substitute for act-specific evidence when assigning blame.
- AR356 — source motive and claim truth must be evaluated separately.
- AR369 — an agent should not displace responsibility for self-caused loss onto an instrument or process.

**PC-P017 / PC-P043 — communication and epistemic conditions**
- AR358 — hostile first framing can bias reception of later truthful information.
- AR367 — compressed/indirect knowledge may require explanation calibrated to the recipient.
- AR371 — low general competence does not erase domain-specific knowledge.

**PC-P042 — Information boundaries**
- AR362 — disclosure can propagate by serial retransmission beyond the initial recipient.

**PC-P068 — Relational durability**
- AR360 — durable relationship can persist even under active hostility.
- AR363 — trusted relationships can provide assistance for vulnerabilities an agent cannot remedy alone.

**PC-P002 — Learning from failure**
- AR368 — failure to correct after adverse experience permits recurrence.

### New provisional parents

**PC-P083 — Hostility-conditioned judgment**
- AR354 — broad blame can be generated by prior dislike.
- AR355 — evaluative speech can be weaponised against a target.
- AR357 — hostile decision-makers compromise adjudicative fairness.

AR354 is a **BRIDGE** to PC-P005; the others show interpersonal/procedural consequences of hostility.

**PC-P084 — Desire does not establish entitlement**
- AR365 — wanting a resource does not create a claim to it.
This joins the wider entitlement family but is retained as a clean local parent.

**PC-P085 — Anti-self-exemption**
- AR366 — an agent should not exempt itself from warnings or constraints it applies to others.

**PC-P086 — Asymmetry-enabled exploitation**
- AR373 — information/capability asymmetry can make an agent pay for access to its own resource.
- AR375 later supplies radically unequal exchange under capability asymmetry.
