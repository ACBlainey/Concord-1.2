### Emerging resource-stewardship topology

Resource ethics now spans several non-equivalent functions:
- acquisition requires a real causal pathway;
- surplus determines which strategies are safely available;
- resources can be maintained or productively deployed;
- some holdings carry collective claims;
- delayed prudence reduces adaptive margin;
- succession is distinct from accumulation;
- preservation can become self-defeating;
- objectives fail if necessary resource commitment is withheld;
- immediate utility affects depletion rate.

This is broader than conservation alone.

### Preference under constraint

AR420 adds an important epistemic caution: inability to access a good can be rationalised as dislike. Alongside earlier opportunity-constrained agency, this means observed preference may partly encode the constraint environment rather than only the agent's unconstrained valuation.
