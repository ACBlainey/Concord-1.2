# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-317 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Outsider Local Knowledge and Rule Accessibility

R528, R531 and R533 form a coherent newcomer/outsider cluster.

R528 distinguishes conspicuous observational capacity from local embedded knowledge: the outsider sees but does not know the town's internal affairs as the resident does.

R531 compares the stranger to a child, suggesting practical inexperience or dependency within the unfamiliar environment.

R533 then explicitly links stranger status to legal accountability: Rattray says ignorance of the law excuses under the custom represented by the proverb.

Together these occurrences distinguish:
1. general capability;
2. local contextual knowledge;
3. reasonable access to rules;
4. accountability for violating those rules.

The source therefore contains a functional recognition that newcomers can possess ordinary agency while still lacking locally necessary knowledge.
