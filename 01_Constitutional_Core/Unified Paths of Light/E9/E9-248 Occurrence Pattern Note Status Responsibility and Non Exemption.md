# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-248 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Status, Responsibility and Non-Exemption

R386, R395 and R405 constrain status in different directions.

- R386: an elder's status does not remove consequences of the elder's own greed.
- R395: an elder must not deliberately expose a child to foreseeable injury.
- R405: an older person voluntarily undertaking a younger person's task cannot automatically demand special consideration because of age.

The common occurrence-level structure is that **status does not operate as a blanket exemption from responsibility**.

At the same time, these items do not erase capability-sensitive accommodation elsewhere in the corpus. R405 is specifically tied by Rattray to voluntarily taking on the task. The distinction should be preserved for later compression.
