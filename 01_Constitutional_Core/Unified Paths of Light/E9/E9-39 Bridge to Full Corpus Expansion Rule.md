# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-39 — Bridge-to-Full-Corpus Expansion Rule

## Problem
Even a perfect 830-item Rattray reconstruction leaves most of Christaller's approximately 3,600-proverb corpus outside the bridge.

## Rule
E9-A cannot freeze an Akan root architecture solely from the Rattray subset.

After bridge reconstruction, the experiment must address the remaining Christaller material through the translation architecture established in E9-17 to E9-20.

## Permitted routes
- aligned Lange translation where accessible;
- independent bilingual/context validation;
- translation-HOLD where evidence remains inadequate.

## Prohibited shortcut
The Rattray subset cannot be relabelled as the entire Akan corpus because it is easier to access.

If full-corpus translation coverage proves practically impossible, E9 must record a formal corpus amendment or scope reduction before architecture freeze.
