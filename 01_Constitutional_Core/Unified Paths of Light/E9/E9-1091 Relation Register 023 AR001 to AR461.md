### Emerging context-relativity without arbitrariness

A strong distinction is appearing between **context-sensitive evaluation** and arbitrary relativism.

The corpus repeatedly shows that:
- resource adequacy depends on purpose;
- the same resource can help in one function and harm in another;
- hazard exposure depends on position and protection;
- effective capability depends partly on environmental support;
- transitional difficulty differs from endpoint quality.

At the same time, causal inquiry, consistent reasoning and accurate description remain required. Context changes what facts are relevant; it does not remove the requirement for reason-responsive evaluation.
