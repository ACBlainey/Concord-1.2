# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-279 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Distributed Responsibility and Neglect

R460 gives a compact coordination failure:

When two people are responsible for looking after the cow, the cow dies of hunger.

The occurrence is structurally different from simple incapacity. The required capability exists, but responsibility is distributed in a way that permits non-performance.

This supports an occurrence-level distinction between:
- **insufficient capability**, where no participant can perform the task; and
- **responsibility diffusion**, where capable participants fail because ownership of the task is unclear or displaced.

This distinction may become important during later system-level compression, but the firewall remains closed.
