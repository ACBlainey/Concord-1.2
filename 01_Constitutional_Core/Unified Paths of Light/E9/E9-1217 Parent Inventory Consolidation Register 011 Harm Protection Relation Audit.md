# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1217 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Register 011: Harm–Protection Relation Audit

### Higher parents

- HC-P021 — foreseeable harm and preventive restraint
- HC-P022 — vulnerability-sensitive protection
- HC-P023 — non-displacement of harm
- HC-P024 — proportionate/function-preserving response

### Relation tests

**HC-P021 vs HC-P022 — SIBLING.**  
Prevention responds to foreseeable hazard; vulnerability-sensitive protection determines whose condition alters protective obligation.

**HC-P021 vs HC-P023 — SIBLING.**  
Avoiding one's own exposure and avoiding externalisation onto others are distinct.

**HC-P023 vs HC-P017 — BRIDGE.**  
Exploitation and harm displacement can overlap, but HC-P017 requires creation/exploitation of asymmetry whereas HC-P023 also covers non-exploitative externalisation.

**HC-P024 vs HC-P021 — SIBLING.**  
Prevention precedes harm; proportional repair/response operates once intervention or correction is required.

### Structural observation

The Akan material does not present “harm” as a single undifferentiated prohibition. It repeatedly distinguishes:
- anticipation;
- vulnerability;
- exposure distribution;
- protective buffering;
- proportional response;
- recoverability.

This internal differentiation should survive into architecture separation.
