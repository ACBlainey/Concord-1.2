# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-05 — Pre-Extraction Method Review and Prediction-Registration Requirements

## Method review result
The change from canonical written corpora to oral/traditionally transmitted evidence does not require abandonment of the Unified Paths experiment.

It does require E9 to distinguish three uncertainties that previous experiments could often combine:
1. **ethical uncertainty** — what function the occurrence expresses;
2. **textual/transmission uncertainty** — what the attested traditional form is;
3. **historical uncertainty** — how old the occurrence or formulation is.

These must not be collapsed into one confidence score.

## Predictions already requiring prospective registration
Before primary extraction E9 must formally register at least:

### P-E9-1 — Root-compression
A suitably frozen African traditional corpus may yield a minimum-stable architecture at least as compressed as the seven-root X kernel.

Fewer, equal or more roots remain valid outcomes.

### P-E9-2 — Functional topology over root count
Root count and partitioning may vary while substantially similar functional space recurs.

### P-E9-3 — Cultural distinctiveness above roots
Operators, recurrent conditions, domain rules and expression forms may vary more than deepest functions.

### P-E9-4 — Reflexive application
Self-directed forms of ethical constraints may arise naturally from the corpus.

### P-E9-5 — Power-domain derivation
Authority/power ethics may form a dense derived domain without surviving as an independent deepest root.

### P-E9-6 — Generative architecture
Independent normalisation may separate deepest constraints from applications/conditions or produce a functionally analogous layered architecture.

### P-E9-7 — Historical depth exploratory prediction
The pre-existing religious-evolution offshoot predicts that older ethical material may sometimes be more compressed. E9 may test compatibility with this hypothesis, but oral-source dating limitations mean no root-count result alone can establish historical priority or evolutionary direction.

## New medium-specific null discipline
A missing function cannot be interpreted confidently as culturally absent until source-form coverage and mediation have been examined.

Conversely, later or highly mediated material cannot be used automatically to rescue an apparent absence.

## Next gate
Before extraction:
1. select the specific E9 tradition or independently bounded traditions;
2. freeze the actual source collection(s);
3. register the predictions formally;
4. define source-form coverage and sampling/completeness rules;
5. close the firewall.
