# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-28 — Primary Extraction Block E: Social Roles, Status and Dependency

## Extraction focus
Material organised around social types and roles was traversed without treating inherited social convention as automatically generalisable ethics.

## Provisional functions observed
Admissible occurrences include:
- reciprocal dependence across unequal roles;
- conduct affecting social trust;
- reliability and reputation;
- obligations generated by relationship;
- limits on status as a substitute for conduct;
- vulnerability created by dependence;
- consequences of arrogance or social overreach;
- collective belonging and exclusion;
- practical responsibility toward those whose position limits independent action.

## Local-role control
Where a proverb appears to prescribe a historically specific hierarchy or role expectation, the local proposition is retained at source level.

No universal equality, hierarchy, authority, family, gender or governance principle is inferred during extraction.

## Emerging medium observation
Short proverbial forms frequently encode ethical content relationally: the force of the saying depends on who stands in relation to whom and under what condition.

This reinforces the need to preserve scope before later abstraction.
