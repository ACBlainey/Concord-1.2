**MS-C01 — Non-transfer across association**  
A cross-family substantive constraint preserving boundaries of person, property, responsibility, entitlement and control.

### Important status

This is **not yet the frozen Akan root architecture**.

The eight MS-P families are organising parents. Most still require their SP children for full recoverability. Therefore they cannot yet be declared minimal roots merely because eight labels now exist.

### What has been learned

The corpus supports substantial compression from:
- 571 admitted occurrences;
- 557 distinct propositions;
- 104 historical PC identifiers;
- 53 HC identifiers;
- 23 substantive candidates;
- to **8 organising substantive families + 1 cross-family constraint**.

But compression has reached a point where further merger attempts begin causing clear information loss.

### Next stability test

The next stage should test whether each MS-P family is:
1. independently necessary for downward recoverability;
2. removable only by distributing its children across other families;
3. better interpreted as a root, branch, operator-dependent family or compound;
4. stable under leave-one-family-out reconstruction.

MS-C01 must also be tested for whether it is:
- a ninth substantive root;
- a boundary operator;
- or a consequence jointly generated by agency, responsibility and entitlement.
