322 / C600 = Y — killing an inedible vulture wastes powder. Scarce resources expended on an outcome with no usable benefit can constitute waste.

324 / C602 = Y — the hunter's name remains connected with elephant meat. Credit or attribution can remain attached to the agent responsible for producing a valued outcome.
