## E9-1105 — Normalisation Ledger N114: AR505–AR510

| AR | Source | Normalised ethical proposition | First-pass relation note |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR505 | R746 / C272 | Future conditions are uncertain; decisions should not presume knowledge of events that have not yet occurred. | Future uncertainty. |
| AR506 | R749 / C390 | Necessary action should not be deferred solely for an ideal future opportunity when an adequate opportunity exists earlier. | Timely adequacy versus ideal delay. |
| AR507 | R750 / C408 | Accepting beneficial voluntary assistance can reduce unnecessary burden without negating agency. | Assistance compatible with agency. |
| AR508 | R751 / C413 | Unnecessary status competition should not obstruct another agent's exercise of genuinely greater capability. | Capability recognition / non-obstruction. |
| AR509 | R752 / C422 | Capability is comparative and distributed; being surpassed in one relation does not establish universal inferiority. | Multidimensional capability. |
| AR510 | R753 / C464 | Social or reputational injury can constitute severe experienced harm even when it is non-physical. | Non-physical harm. |

AR507 complements AR343: some actions remain irreducibly the recipient's, but accepting assistance is not itself a loss of agency. Assistance and agency therefore need not be opposites.
