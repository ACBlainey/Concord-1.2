## E9-383 — Mechanical Ledger Block 136: Rattray 676–680

676 / C3068 = Y — water one would refuse to bathe in should not be drunk. A safety/acceptability judgment should be applied consistently across uses where the underlying reason remains relevant.

677 / C3069 = Y — the calm, silent water is what drowns a person. Low visible disturbance does not establish low danger; serious hazards can exist without conspicuous warning behaviour.

678 / C3070 = Y — an unusually salty body of water is said to have a reason for that condition. Significant observable anomalies can justify searching for an underlying cause rather than treating them as causeless.

679 / C3071 = Y — any river entering the sea loses its name. Integration into a much larger system can erase or subordinate the distinct identity of a smaller component.

680 / C3072 = Y — water does not dry up without a cause; Rattray glosses the wording as giving or having a reason for an action. Observable change is represented as causally grounded rather than arbitrary.
