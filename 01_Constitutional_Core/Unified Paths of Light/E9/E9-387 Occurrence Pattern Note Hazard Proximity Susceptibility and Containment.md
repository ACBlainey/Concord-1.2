# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-387 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Hazard Proximity, Susceptibility and Containment

R669, R670, R672, R673, R677 and R682 create a strong hazard architecture.

R669: prior exposure changes later susceptibility.

R670: self-protection can displace harm onto a dependent.

R672: proximity determines who is struck first by an uncontrolled hazard.

R673: dangerous interacting components should not be colocated.

R677: calm appearance does not guarantee safety.

R682: protective position can radically change exposure to the same environmental hazard.

The combined structure distinguishes:
- intrinsic hazard;
- susceptibility;
- proximity;
- interaction risk;
- transfer of exposure;
- misleading surface appearance;
- protective positioning.

This is considerably more specific than a generic non-harm theme and should remain uncompressed until the internal E9 derivation phase.
