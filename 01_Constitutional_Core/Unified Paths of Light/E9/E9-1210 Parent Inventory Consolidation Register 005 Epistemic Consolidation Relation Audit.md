# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1210 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Register 005: Epistemic Consolidation Relation Audit

### Higher parents now visible

The evidence/information region has consolidated into eight higher structures:

1. **HC-P005 — Judgment should track evidence and operative reality**
2. **HC-P006 — Inference must respect observational limits**
3. **HC-P007 — Representation should remain answerable to reality**
4. **HC-P008 — Claims require accessible and independent verification where stakes demand it**
5. **HC-P009 — Judgment should remain correctable over time**
6. **HC-P010 — Communication should be fitted to recipient and purpose**
7. **HC-P011 — Information boundaries can be ethically functional**
8. **HC-P012 — Information systems should resist distortion and bottleneck**

### Information-loss audit

These eight cannot yet be safely reduced to a single “truth” or “epistemic integrity” parent.

Key distinctions:
- reality tracking ≠ truthful representation;
- truthful representation ≠ verification;
- verification ≠ revisability;
- inference ≠ direct observation;
- intelligible communication ≠ unrestricted disclosure;
- confidentiality ≠ deception;
- individual epistemic discipline ≠ collective information-system integrity.

### Bridges

- HC-P005 ↔ HC-P001: capability claims are one domain requiring reality-grounded judgment.
- HC-P006 ↔ context/situatedness: embedding changes epistemic access.
- HC-P009 ↔ HC-P004: developmental correction applies to both knowledge and capability.
- HC-P011 ↔ agency/privacy: information control can protect or constrain agency.
- HC-P012 ↔ authority: power changes information flow.

### Decision

**RETAIN all eight as higher intermediate parents for now.**

Any later compression should occur only after the other domains have been consolidated, because several of these structures are likely to operate as cross-domain procedures rather than roots.
