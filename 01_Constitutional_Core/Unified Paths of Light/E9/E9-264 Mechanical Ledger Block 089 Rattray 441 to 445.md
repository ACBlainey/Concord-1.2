# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-264 — Mechanical Ledger Block 089: Rattray 441–445

441 / C845 = H — when the free man dies, a slave succeeds. This is historical succession evidence linked to R440, but no independent transferable ethical proposition is extracted.

442 / C846 = H — royal rank is protected by etiquette against direct association of the chief's name with death. The source documents status-specific speech norms; it does not establish a general ethical principle.

443 / C1625 = Y — a slave does not choose the master. Whatever the historical commentary surrounding Ashanti slavery, the proverb itself clearly records absence of choice in a fundamental dependency relation.

444 / C1615 = Y — a slave does not independently decide to go to war. The source records constrained agency over participation in a high-risk activity.

445 / C1622 = H — a slave knows the master. Without further gloss, the ethical significance of this relational knowledge is underdetermined.

### AR263 — R443/C1625
A dependency relation can be structured so that the dependent lacks choice over the identity of the controlling party.

### AR264 — R444/C1615
A subordinated agent can lack independent agency over participation in a high-risk collective activity.
