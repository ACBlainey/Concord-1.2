# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-203 — Mechanical Ledger Block 064: Rattray 316–320

316 / C1591 = Y — if one fights and does not win, one does not lead away captives. Claimed benefits contingent on success cannot be treated as acquired when the enabling outcome has not occurred.

317 / C2730 = Y — many gun-lock covers go to war; Rattray explains these covers protect powder from moisture. Effective capability in adverse conditions depends partly on supporting protective infrastructure.

318 / C2731 = H — war may place paternal half-siblings on opposing sides because descent is matrilineal. This is important social-structural evidence, but a transferable ethical proposition is not sufficiently explicit.

319 / C377 = H — no one carries a sick person on their back when going to war. Capability/fitness constraints are present, but the wartime context and lack of explanation leave the intended function underdetermined.

320 / C549 = Y — an attendant's hunter has had his hand injured by an exploding gun, making continuation of the attendant's provisioning role questionable. A dependent/supporting task can lose its operative purpose when the primary capability it supports is disabled.

### AR176 — R316/C1591
A benefit contingent on successful completion of an enabling process cannot be treated as obtained when that process fails.

### AR177 — R317/C2730
Operational capability can depend on protective supporting infrastructure that preserves critical resources under adverse conditions.

### AR178 — R320/C549
A supporting task may lose its practical purpose when the primary capability or activity it exists to support becomes unavailable.
