## E9-477 — Christaller Expansion Entry Gate

With Rattray mechanically closed, E9 may enter the Christaller-expansion phase under the already frozen corpus architecture.

### Gate conditions
Before new Christaller-only items enter the ethical ledger:
1. establish an accessible Christaller witness or aligned translation witness;
2. preserve Christaller item identifiers/order exactly;
3. distinguish items already represented in Rattray from Christaller-only items;
4. record translation witness and translation debt;
5. retain T0 source-language identity even where translation relies on another layer;
6. do not silently generate English translations;
7. preserve variants rather than collapsing them without evidence.

### Evidence classes
Each new Christaller item must record:
- Christaller identifier;
- source text availability;
- translation witness;
- Rattray overlap status;
- ethical yield state;
- mediation/context;
- translation debt.

The next work is therefore witness acquisition/alignment, not root comparison.
