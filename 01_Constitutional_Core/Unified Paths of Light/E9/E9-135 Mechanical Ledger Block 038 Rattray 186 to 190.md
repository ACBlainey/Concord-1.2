# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-135 — Mechanical Ledger Block 038: Rattray 186–190

186 / C2572 = Y — the bluebottle fly is said to have no mouth yet can strip green palm nuts. Absence of an expected mechanism does not prove absence of effective capability.

187 / C2573 = H — despite a fly sitting on the dish, one still licks inside it. A threshold of tolerated contamination or imperfection may be implied, but the source supplies no interpretation sufficient for a secure ethical abstraction.

188 / C2575 = Y — wherever else a fly may alight, it does not alight on fire. Behaviour is constrained by recognition of conditions that would cause immediate severe harm.

189 / C1463 = H — Rattray explicitly records a hierarchical interpretation: if mistreatment must occur, the speaker prefers it from a superior rather than an equal/inferior, with a historical slavery example. This is ethically relevant evidence about status-conditioned perceptions of mistreatment, but it cannot safely be abstracted as a normative endorsement of hierarchy.

190 / C1464 = Y — the biting fly has no one to aid it in trouble; R192 later supplies the causal explanation that indiscriminate biting produces this isolation.

### AR085 — Rattray 186 / Christaller 2572
**Minimal function:** Lack of an expected or visible mechanism should not by itself be treated as proof that effective capability is absent.
**Confidence:** high.

### AR086 — Rattray 188 / Christaller 2575
**Minimal function:** Agents may avoid actions or environments whose consequences are recognisably and immediately destructive.
**Confidence:** high.

### AR087 — Rattray 190 / Christaller 1464
**Minimal function:** Persistent harmful conduct toward others can leave an agent without reciprocal aid when that agent later becomes vulnerable.
**Confidence:** high when read with the explicit causal statement in R192.
