## E9-1189 — Parent-Child Compression Register 015: AR304–AR329

### Existing parents extended

**PC-P001 — Performance-grounded capability**
- AR316 — capability can develop over time.
- AR318 — future capability cannot be predetermined from initial condition.
- AR329 — capability has practical value when applied to relevant need.

**PC-P007 / PC-P043 — situated knowledge and epistemic limits**
- AR306 — close relational knowledge can reveal information unavailable to distant observers.
- AR320 — local embeddedness can outperform strong general observation for context-specific knowledge.
- AR322 — newcomer unfamiliarity can create temporary epistemic dependency.

**PC-P042 — Strategic information boundaries**
- AR309 — outsiders can lack epistemic access to private relational domains.
- AR312 — participants have obligations governing disclosure from those domains.

**PC-P068 — Relational durability**
- AR310 — maintaining or abandoning a dependency relation can each impose costs.
- AR313 — seeking relationship while expressing contempt toward the sought group is internally inconsistent.
- AR323 — presence or proximity does not itself establish durable commitment.

**PC-P002 — Correction and receptivity**
- AR315 — repeated failed correction can impose burden on the correcting caregiver/agent.

**PC-P036 / PC-P062 — effective agency under constraints**
- AR327 — practical choice depends on opportunity/access, not abstract permission alone.

**PC-P074 — Specialist capability as continuity infrastructure**
- AR307 — specialist trained capability can preserve cultural/social continuity.

**PC-P075 — Sampling and temporal evaluation bias**
- AR314 — unusually favourable or adverse early conditions can distort evaluation of a longer process.

- AR328 — mortality/vulnerability persists despite status.

- AR325 — feasibility can be limited by a narrow bottleneck.
- AR326 — hazard can concentrate at a transition/interface.
