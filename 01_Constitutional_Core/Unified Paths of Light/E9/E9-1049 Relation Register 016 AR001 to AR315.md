### Emerging information-boundary topology

The corpus now supports several non-equivalent information relations:
1. information may be unavailable to outsiders;
2. insiders may have experience-generated knowledge;
3. sensitive information may still require bounded disclosure;
4. power can suppress candid information;
5. false information can manufacture conflict;
6. formal procedure can investigate contested claims.

These should remain separate until compression establishes whether a higher-order parent can retain all six functions without information loss.
