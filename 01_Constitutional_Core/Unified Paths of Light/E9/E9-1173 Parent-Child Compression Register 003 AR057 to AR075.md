# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1173 — Parent-Child Compression Register 003: AR057–AR075

### Provisional local parents

**PC-P010 — Capability-sensitive support and deliberation**  
Appropriate assistance or decision procedure depends on relevant capability and task conditions.

Children:
- AR057 — care/support should reflect caregiver capability.
- AR060 — speed should not displace needed deliberation.

Relation: **SIBLING**. Shared higher-order sensitivity exists, but care capacity and deliberative timing remain distinct.

**PC-P011 — Context-sensitive participation**  
Participation in a local social environment can require adaptation to its ordinary practices.

Child:
- **M003 (AR062+AR163)** — participation in a community can require adaptation to ordinary local practices.

At present M003 is an internally induced singleton parent candidate; it is retained for later testing against other context-sensitive conduct propositions rather than elevated further.

**PC-P012 — Property-responsive classification and treatment**  
Labels should not substitute for relevant underlying properties, and treatment should respond to those properties.

Children:
- AR065 — label equivalence does not establish actual equivalence.
- AR073 — treatment should respond to actual properties.

Relation: AR073 is a practical **CHILD** of the epistemic distinction represented by AR065, while the two propositions remain separately recoverable.

**PC-P013 — Harm response across prevention and defence**  
Harm governance includes preventing foreseeable harm and responding defensively when harmful disturbance occurs.

Children:
- AR066 — foreseeable-harm prevention.
- AR070 — defensive response to disturbance.

Relation: **SIBLING**, not duplicate and not yet one ethical rule.

**PC-P014 — Remedy evaluation and timeliness**  
A remedy must be assessed both for whether it works and whether it arrives while useful.

Children:
- AR068 — remedy should be evaluated by outcome/effect.
- AR071 — remedy can be defeated by delay/urgency.

**PC-P015 — Responsibility and causal inquiry**  
Responsibility should remain connected to agency while harmful conduct remains open to causal investigation.

Children:
- AR069 — responsibility follows the relevant actor.
- AR072 — harmful conduct should be causally investigated.

Relation: **BRIDGE** toward the causal-attribution family PC-P005; no higher merge is yet authorised.
