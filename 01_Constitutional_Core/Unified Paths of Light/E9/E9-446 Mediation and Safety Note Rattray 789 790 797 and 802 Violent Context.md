# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-446 — Mediation and Safety Note: Rattray 789, 790, 797 and 802 — Violent Context

Four items in this range contain direct lethal or coercive material.

### R789/C1816
“Some are killed before others are put to flight.”

### R790/C1819
“If the men are not slain, the women are not carried off.”

### R797/C1951
Rattray translates the saying as treating killing as non-hurtful when a “just reason” exists.

### R802/C2195
The saying prefers immediate execution to postponement until the following day.

### E9 treatment
All four are preserved faithfully as source material, but none is converted into a broad permission or normative endorsement of violence.

R789, R790 and R802 remain H because their transferable ethical mechanisms are underdetermined.

R797 also remains H because “just reason” is not enough to reconstruct a safe general principle without importing unstated standards of authority, evidence, due process, proportionality or error correction.

This follows the source-first rule: preserve what the witness says, but do not silently supply missing ethical safeguards from outside the witness.
