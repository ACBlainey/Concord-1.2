### Important internal results

**Relationship is richer than beneficence.**  
The corpus independently preserves relation-generated obligation, reciprocity under asymmetry, durable bonds, vulnerability-sensitive care, networked support and boundaries between related persons/claims.

**Scarcity is a condition, not a moral blank cheque.**  
It changes feasible behaviour and acceptable substitutes, but does not automatically erase extraction limits, responsibility or the agency of affected parties.

**Resource ethics separates use from entitlement.**  
Functional value, sustaining stewardship, entitlement, productive contribution and recoverable repair are distinct mechanisms.
