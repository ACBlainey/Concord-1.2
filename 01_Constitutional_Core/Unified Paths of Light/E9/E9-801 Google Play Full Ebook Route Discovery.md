# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-801 — Google Play Full-Ebook Route Discovery

A materially new T0 acquisition route has been identified rather than another catalogue-only mirror.

Google Play Books indexes an 1879 Christaller ebook record (`M-nAI19-wyoC`) and explicitly marks it **Get for free**. The record identifies Johann Gottlieb Christaller, the Basel German Evangelical Missionary Society, January 1879, and 302 digital pages.

### Significance
Unlike a simple bibliographic catalogue record, this route is presented as a readable/acquirable ebook. It therefore qualifies under E9-779 as a new acquisition route worth pursuing.

The 302-page digital count is not treated as Christaller printed pagination.
