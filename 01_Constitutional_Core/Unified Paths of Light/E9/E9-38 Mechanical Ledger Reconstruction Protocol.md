# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-38 — Mechanical Ledger Reconstruction Protocol

## Required source pass
A fresh source-order pass through Rattray is required.

For every proverb item, record:
1. Rattray printed locator;
2. Twi form present/absent;
3. English translation present/absent;
4. Christaller locator if supplied/recoverable;
5. thematic chapter;
6. variant relation;
7. ethical yield state;
8. HOLD reason;
9. mediation/context note;
10. translation debt.

## Yield states
- Y — at least one admissible ethical occurrence;
- Z — zero ethical yield;
- H — possible ethical yield but unresolved;
- X — unusable/quarantined.

## Occurrence rule
Only Y items instantiate AR occurrence records. Multiple occurrences from one proverb require separable supported functions.

## Closure arithmetic
At bridge closure:

Y + Z + H + X = total reconstructed proverb items.

Occurrence count is reported separately and must not be confused with proverb-item count.
