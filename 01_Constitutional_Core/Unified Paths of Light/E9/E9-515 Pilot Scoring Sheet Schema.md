# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-515 — Pilot Scoring Sheet Schema

Each of the 50 validation items will receive one row with:

R-ID | C-ID | candidate translation | Rattray reference translation | additional witness | P | A | N | R | C | E | M | U | material error? | TD | notes.

Where:
- P = propositional preservation;
- A = agent/patient preservation;
- N = negation/modality;
- R = relation preservation;
- C = causal structure;
- E = evaluative polarity;
- M = metaphor restraint;
- U = uncertainty signalling.

### Score values
Use:
- PASS;
- MINOR;
- MATERIAL;
- N/A where the dimension genuinely does not apply.

### Material-error rule
Any MATERIAL result in agent/patient, negation/modality, ownership/authority relation, causal direction or evaluative polarity makes the item materially failed for ethical extraction.

The sheet records disagreement; it does not automatically assume Rattray is correct where independent evidence indicates otherwise.
