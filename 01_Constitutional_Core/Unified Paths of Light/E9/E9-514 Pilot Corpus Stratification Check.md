# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-514 — Pilot Corpus Stratification Check

The frozen 50-item sample is checked against the intended validation strata.

### Present
- metaphoric animal/material imagery;
- authority/status language;
- truth/falsehood;
- property/resource language;
- causation and consequence;
- vulnerability/harm;
- capability and comparative competence;
- procedural/judicial material;
- religious/cosmological vocabulary;
- family/social relations;
- direct negative constructions;
- conditional constructions;
- recorder commentary;
- OCR-sensitive historical orthography.

### Reason for broad spread
A fallback translator that performs well only on short literal proverbs would be inadequate for E9.

The pilot must test the constructions most likely to alter ethical abstraction:
- who acts;
- who receives the action;
- whether an action is negated;
- whether a relation is causal or merely associated;
- whether a statement is descriptive or normative;
- whether a metaphor has been over-expanded.

The frozen sample is adequate to begin that test.
