# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-196 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Information Asymmetry, Concealment and Detection

R298–300 and R303 form a dense information-state cluster.

- R298: revealing preparation to the intended target would defeat the action.
- R299: the target approaches without knowing the hunter is concealed.
- R300: an actual bodily condition is difficult to observe because hair masks it.
- R303: attempted concealment fails if a revealing part remains visible.

These occurrences distinguish several different mechanisms:
1. strategic withholding of operational information;
2. exploitation of another agent's incomplete information;
3. passive masking of a real condition;
4. failure of concealment through residual evidence.

This strengthens the E9 epistemic topology while also showing that “hidden information” is not one homogeneous phenomenon.
