# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-436 — Provenance and Epistemic Note: Rattray 771 — Retrospective Agency Attribution

R771/C1020 says, in Rattray's translation, that when a tree hates you it falls on you.

Rattray then comments that an inanimate object is endowed with a human attribute, possibly only after an event occurred that “demanded a reason,” namely the tree falling.

### E9 treatment
The proverb remains H for ethical extraction.

However, the note is preserved because it documents a potentially important epistemic phenomenon:
1. an adverse event occurs;
2. observers seek an explanation;
3. agency/intention is attributed retrospectively to the object involved.

This is not admitted as an Akan ethical root or principle. It is retained as evidence about explanatory framing and mediation.

### Recorder boundary
Rattray's speculation about spirits and why the attribution arose is his interpretation. E9 does not treat that speculation as established source fact.
