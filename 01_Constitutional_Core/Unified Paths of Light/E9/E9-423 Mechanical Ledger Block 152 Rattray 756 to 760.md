# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-423 — Mechanical Ledger Block 152: Rattray 756–760

756 / C485 = Y — the OCR prints the Rattray number as “15Q.” Tapping a pot reveals where the crack lies. Diagnostic probing can localise a hidden defect by observing how the system responds to a controlled test.

757 / C770 = Y — a word once spoken cannot be unsaid. Communication can create irreversible consequences; speech should therefore be treated as an action whose release may not be fully retractable.

758 / C807 = Y — a small thing is used to measure a large thing. Limited-scale indicators or reference units can support evaluation of larger systems when the relation between measure and target is appropriate.

759 / C809 = H — “the good thing sells itself.” Intrinsic quality, reputation and market signalling are possible mechanisms, but the source gives no gloss sufficient to distinguish them.

760 / C819 = Y — when something is lost, it is in another person's possession. Loss to one agent can correspond to possession/control by another; disappearance from the owner's perspective does not imply disappearance from the system.

### AR512 — R756/C485
Controlled probing can reveal and localise a hidden defect through the system's response.

### AR513 — R757/C770
Communication can create irreversible consequences; speech may not be fully retractable once released.

### AR514 — R758/C807
Appropriate small-scale indicators or reference units can support evaluation of larger systems.

### AR515 — R760/C819
Loss from one agent's control can correspond to possession by another; absence from the owner does not imply absence from the wider system.
