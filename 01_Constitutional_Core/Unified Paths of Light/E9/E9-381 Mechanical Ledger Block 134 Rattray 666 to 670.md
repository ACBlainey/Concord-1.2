# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-381 — Mechanical Ledger Block 134: Rattray 666–670

666 / C2516 = Y — the rich person is hated while the poor person is called bad. Social evaluation can become negative at opposite ends of a status dimension, so approval cannot be inferred simply from movement toward either wealth or poverty.

667 / C1245 = Y — a fire that is going to blaze strongly has different smoke. Emerging consequential states may produce precursor signals before the full event becomes visible.

668 / C1246 = Y — good fuel does not remain long in the plantation because it is quickly taken for use. Resources with immediately recognised utility are likely to be selected and consumed earlier than less useful alternatives.

669 / C1247 = Y — wood previously touched and dried by fire is easier to ignite. Prior exposure can change later susceptibility, making a system more responsive to recurrence of the same hazard.

670 / C1249 = Y — when burned, an agent first shakes the spark onto the agent's child before removing it. Immediate self-protection can displace harm onto a nearby dependent; reducing one's own exposure does not justify transferring the hazard to a more vulnerable party.
