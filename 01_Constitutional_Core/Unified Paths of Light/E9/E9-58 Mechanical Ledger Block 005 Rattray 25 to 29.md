## AR003
**Source:** Rattray 27 / Christaller 1697  
**Normative class:** P/C  
**Scope:** responsibility under differentiated roles  
**Minimal functional proposition:** Responsibility for a false claim should attach to the agent responsible for the claim rather than an associated but distinct subordinate or carrier.  
**Context dependence:** moderate.  
**Translation state:** published Rattray translation.  
**Mediation:** M2.  
**Confidence:** high.

## AR004
**Source:** Rattray 28 / Christaller 1699  
**Normative class:** D/C  
**Scope:** multiple authorities making incompatible or unreliable claims  
**Minimal functional proposition:** The presence of many asserted authorities does not guarantee truth; conflicting expert claims warrant scrutiny because at least some may be false.  
**Context dependence:** moderate.  
**Translation state:** published Rattray translation.  
**Mediation:** M2.  
**Confidence:** high.
