# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-27 — Primary Extraction Block D: War, Conflict and Capability

## Extraction focus
The war/conflict stratum is treated as a high-risk domain for confusing effective strategy with ethical norm.

## Admissible functional signals
Source-led material supports provisional occurrences concerning:
- prudence before confrontation;
- recognition of unequal capability;
- collective dependence in conflict;
- consequences of provocation;
- limits of individual strength;
- endurance under adversity;
- timing and restraint;
- responsibility for consequences generated by one's own action.

## Non-admitted material
Tactical effectiveness alone is not coded as ethics.

Statements whose only function is “how to win” remain prudential unless a separable relational, responsibility, restraint, protection, or collective function is supported.

## Method result
The normative/prudential distinction introduced in E9-25 is necessary and workable.
