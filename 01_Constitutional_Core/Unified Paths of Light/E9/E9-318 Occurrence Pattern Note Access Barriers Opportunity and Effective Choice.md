# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-318 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Access Barriers, Opportunity and Effective Choice

R535, R536, R537 and R541 form a strong access/capability cluster.

- R535 locates the difficulty of reaching Europe in the sea rather than the destination.
- R536 narrows the difficulty further to the dangerous surf at the access point.
- R537 says desire exists but opportunity is lacking.
- R541 attributes successful sea crossing to wisdom/capability.

The pattern separates:
- wanting an outcome;
- the outcome itself;
- access to the outcome;
- barriers at the transition/interface;
- capability for overcoming those barriers.

At occurrence level, **effective choice requires more than preference: opportunity, access and enabling capability materially determine whether preference can be realised.**
