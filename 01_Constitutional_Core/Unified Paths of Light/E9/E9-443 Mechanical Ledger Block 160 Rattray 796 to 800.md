# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-443 — Mechanical Ledger Block 160: Rattray 796–800

796 / C1937 = Y — when the face of a drum is available, one does not leave it and beat the side. Use the component or interface designed for the required function rather than an inferior substitute when the proper mechanism is available.

797 / C1951 = H — Rattray translates the proverb as saying that when there is a “just reason” for seizing and killing a person, doing so does not hurt them. Because this concerns lethal punishment and the source does not establish the adjudicative safeguards or meaning of “just reason,” no general ethical permission is extracted.

798 / C2077 = Y — mushrooms are used for soup because meat is unavailable. Under resource scarcity, functional substitutes can preserve an objective even when the preferred resource is absent.

799 / C2150 = Y — the person who wins a case does not carry the sheep imposed as the court judgment/fine. Burdens resulting from adjudication should attach to the party against whom judgment runs, not to the successful claimant.

800 / C2151 = H — the guilty person is said to have much to say. Excessive speech may be treated as a cue, but verbosity is not reliable evidence of guilt; the source gives no basis for admitting the association as a general ethical principle.

### AR546 — R796/C1937
When the mechanism designed for a function is available, an inferior or indirect interface should not replace it without reason.

### AR547 — R798/C2077
Under resource scarcity, functional substitutes can preserve an objective when the preferred resource is unavailable.

### AR548 — R799/C2150
Burdens imposed by adjudication should attach according to the judgment rather than being transferred to the successful claimant.
