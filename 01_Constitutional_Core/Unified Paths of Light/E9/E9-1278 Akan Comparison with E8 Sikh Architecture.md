## E9-1278 — Akan Comparison with E8 Sikh Architecture

### Prior E8 frozen parents

1. Reliable orientation
2. Developmental correctability
3. Self-regulated agency
4. Outward beneficence
5. Responsible material relation
6. Non-domination and accountable power
7. Constructive collective participation

E8's prospective X comparison was 5 Recovered / 2 Ambiguous / 0 Absent, with X1 and X6 ambiguous because their functions were distributed across multiple parents.

### Akan comparison

Several structural parallels are visible without requiring one-to-one identity:
- AK-R1 ↔ reliable orientation;
- AK-R2 ↔ developmental correctability;
- AK-R3/AK-R4 ↔ self-regulated agency + non-domination/accountable power;
- AK-R5/AK-R6 ↔ outward beneficence/protective-care functions;
- AK-R7 ↔ responsible material relation;
- AK-R8 ↔ continuity/collective-function elements distributed across Sikh parents.

### Key contrast

E8 showed that an X function could be present yet remain **ambiguous** because it was distributed without a sufficiently direct parent-level recovery.

E9 makes protection and accountability more explicit:
- AK-R5 gives X1 a direct recovery;
- AK-R4 plus AK-O4 and repair mechanisms make X6 distributed but sufficiently explicit for recovery.

### Result

E8 and E9 reinforce the methodological distinction between:
**functional presence, recoverability threshold, and partition identity.**
