# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-373 — Mechanical Ledger Block 132: Rattray 656–660

656 / C2945 = H — wealth is said to surpass everything. This is a maximal valuation claim, but its rhetorical scope and intended ethical function are too broad for secure admission.

657 / C2946 = Y — money is compared to a dependent resource that “runs away” if not looked after. Resources require active stewardship; possession without maintenance or management does not guarantee retention.

658 / C2950 = Y — wealth is valuable but finding an heir is difficult. Resource accumulation and continuity are distinct problems; stewardship includes identifying a viable path of succession or transfer.

659 / C2951 = Y — reluctance to spend money even on food results in hunger. Preservation of resources becomes self-defeating when it prevents expenditure necessary for the purpose those resources serve.

660 / C2952 = Y — reluctance to spend necessary resources in war results in failure. In a non-war-generalised form, undertaking a costly objective while refusing the resources necessary for execution can make success unattainable.

### AR428 — R657/C2946
Resources require active stewardship; possession without maintenance or management does not guarantee continued retention.

### AR429 — R658/C2950
Accumulating resources and providing for their responsible succession or transfer are distinct stewardship problems.

### AR430 — R659/C2951
Resource preservation becomes self-defeating when refusal to spend prevents satisfaction of the basic purpose for which the resource exists.

### AR431 — R660/C2952
Undertaking a resource-dependent objective while refusing the expenditure necessary for execution can prevent success.
