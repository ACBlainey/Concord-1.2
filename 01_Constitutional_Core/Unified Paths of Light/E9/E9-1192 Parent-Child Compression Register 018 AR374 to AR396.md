## E9-1192 — Parent-Child Compression Register 018: AR374–AR396

### Existing parents extended

**PC-P001 — Capability becomes functional through use**
- AR374 — available knowledge has functional value when activated/used.
- AR377 — withheld capability cannot solve a task-specific problem.

**PC-P017 / PC-P043 — verification and information integrity**
- AR378 — truthful representation excludes deliberate deception.
- AR379 — fabrication contaminates otherwise truthful information.
- AR380 — accountable scrutiny can expose false representation.
- AR381 — delayed correction allows falsehood a temporal advantage.
- AR382 — placing evidence beyond access obstructs scrutiny.
- AR384 — accurate evidence can displace deceptive advantage.
- AR385 — falsehood can contaminate trust in a wider body of claims.

These now justify a narrower internal split between **truthful representation**, **verification access**, and **correction dynamics**.

**PC-P049 — proportionate correction**
- AR383 — falsehood can increase the burden of dispute resolution.
This is a bridge to adjudication rather than a direct child of repair.

**PC-P053 — resilience through alternatives**
- AR395 — unpredictable major loss gives reason to build resilience in advance.

### New provisional parents

**PC-P087 — Scarcity propagates through dependency**
- AR386 — scarcity can transmit burden through economic/resource dependency.
- AR394 — pre-existing deprivation can amplify later adverse events.

**PC-P088 — Scarcity changes feasible behaviour and resource choice**
- AR387 — subsistence need can compel costly effort.
- AR388 — scarcity can force reliance on lower-quality resources.
- AR393 — deprivation changes what resources are practically acceptable.
- AR391 — deprivation can suppress public participation.

**PC-P089 — Material vulnerability can reduce practical autonomy**
- **M005 (AR277+AR390)** — severe need can induce acceptance of autonomy constraints for protection/care/subsistence.
- AR396 — material loss itself can reduce practical autonomy/status.

These are sibling mechanisms: induced surrender versus direct loss of practical autonomy.

**PC-P090 — Emergency use versus continuity reserves**
- AR392 — emergency scarcity can justify temporary repurposing.
This remains in tension with AR271's continuity-reserve protection and is therefore retained rather than subordinated.
