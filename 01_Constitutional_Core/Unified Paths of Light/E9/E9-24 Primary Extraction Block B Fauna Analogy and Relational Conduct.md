# Unified Paths of Light — E9
## E9-24 — Primary Extraction Block B: Fauna Analogy and Relational Conduct

## Coverage
The next bridge-stratum material, heavily expressed through animal analogy, was traversed as proverbial functional evidence rather than literal zoological description.

## Provisional functions observed
Admissible occurrences include separable propositions concerning:
- consequences of aggression or reckless confrontation;
- prudence under unequal capability;
- mutual dependence;
- cooperation and association;
- limits of isolated action;
- caution in trust and proximity;
- response to vulnerability;
- status or strength not guaranteeing unrestricted success;
- learning from consequence.

## Analogy rule
Animal behaviour in a proverb is not itself coded as an ethical claim.

The occurrence is admitted only when the translation/commentary supports a conventional human-directed application.

## Medium-specific finding
Proverbial analogy often compresses condition + consequence + behavioural guidance into a very short form. This differs structurally from explicit scriptural prescription and may produce higher interpretation dependence.

The existing normative classes remain usable, but contextual-dependence tracking is proving necessary.
