# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-447 — Occurrence Pattern Note: Observation, Explanation and Comparative Exposure

R788 and R792 create a useful epistemic pair.

R788: without visiting another person's farm, an agent may believe they alone are a farmer.

R792: a traveller may report what was seen while still being unable to explain everything.

Together they constrain two opposite errors:
- **too little exposure:** isolation produces overconfidence about one's own uniqueness or superiority;
- **overclaim from exposure:** direct observation is treated as if it automatically supplied complete explanation.

Reliable comparative knowledge therefore requires exposure beyond one's own case, while reliable explanation requires more than exposure alone.

This is consistent with earlier E9 material distinguishing observation, inference, local knowledge, measurement and causal attribution.
