# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1253 — Cross-Family Constraint Test: MS-C01 Non-Transfer Across Association

### Candidate

**MS-C01 — Non-transfer across association**

Content:
Relationship, association or collective membership does not automatically transfer:
- identity;
- property;
- responsibility;
- entitlement;
- control.

### Hypothesis A — ninth substantive root

Test:
Does MS-C01 protect an independent substantive good not otherwise represented?

**Weak support.**

Its content is primarily a boundary rule governing how other substantive relations are interpreted. It does not identify a separate protected end comparable to agency, protection, care, information, resources or continuity.

### Hypothesis B — consequence jointly generated by other roots

Test:
Can MS-C01 be fully reconstructed from:
- MS-P03 agency;
- MS-P04 causal responsibility;
- MS-P06 relationship;
- MS-P07 resource relation?

**Partial but incomplete.**

These recover:
- non-transfer of agency/control;
- non-transfer of responsibility without causal basis;
- non-transfer of entitlement/property through mere relation.

However the recurring general form — **association alone is insufficient for transfer** — is broader than any one family and would have to be restated repeatedly.

### Hypothesis C — boundary operator

Test:
Does MS-C01 specify how association should modify claims across multiple substantive domains?

**Strong support.**

It functions analogously to a cross-domain operator:
> do not infer transfer of person-level properties, claims or responsibilities solely from association.

### Decision

**MS-C01 is provisionally reclassified as O-C10 — Association/non-transfer boundary operator.**

It is **not counted as a ninth substantive family**.

Its substantive applications remain recoverable beneath MS-P03, MS-P04, MS-P06 and MS-P07.
