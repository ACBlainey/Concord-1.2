### Extended-source notes
R467 carries an extended Rattray description of historical funerary killing and entombment of slaves, wives and attendants. The occurrence records the lethal subordination described by the source without treating the custom as ethically legitimate.

R481 carries a substantial explanatory story. The salamander gives false attack reports separately to the chiefs of Accra and Akuapem, bringing the groups close to war; the deception is discovered and the salamander is required to explain it. The story is part of the source evidence for AR292.

### Method caution
Historical violence, coercive hierarchy and collective lethal power are recorded descriptively. Y classification requires a transferable ethical/function occurrence; it does not convert the historical institution or action into an endorsed norm.
