# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-287 — Occurrence Pattern Note: False Information and Manufactured Intergroup Conflict

R481 is unusually explicit.

The attached source story says the salamander went separately to the chiefs of two peoples and told each that the other intended to attack. The false reports brought them to the point of war.

The story therefore provides a concrete causal sequence:

**false report → mistaken threat belief → reciprocal mobilisation → near-conflict.**

The deception is subsequently discovered and the originator is required to explain the false reports.

This is stronger than a generic injunction against lying. It identifies information integrity as a functional condition of intergroup peace and shows how manipulated beliefs about hostile intent can create conflict that neither side's original conduct required.

No cross-tradition comparison is made while the E9 firewall remains closed.
