# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-441 — Mechanical Ledger Block 158: Rattray 786–790

786 / C1566 = Y — when visiting another person's house, if the owner is squatting on the ground one does not demand a stool. A guest should calibrate expectations to the host's own available conditions rather than demand a privilege the host is not enjoying.

787 / C1578 = Y — entering another town and declaring that no one of importance has been encountered invites the reply that the town has likewise not noticed anyone important arriving. Status claims that demean others can be reciprocally challenged; self-importance does not establish superiority.

788 / C1587 = Y — without ever visiting another person's farm one might think oneself the only farmer. Comparative knowledge requires exposure beyond one's own practice; isolation can produce unjustified beliefs in unique or superior capability.

789 / C1816 = H — some are killed before others are put to flight. The saying describes differential outcomes in violent conflict, but the transferable ethical mechanism is not sufficiently specified.

790 / C1819 = H — if the men are not slain, the women are not carried off. The item describes a violent dependency in conflict and gendered capture; without further source gloss, it is retained as historical/contextual evidence rather than abstracted into a general ethical rule.

### AR539 — R786/C1566
A guest should calibrate demands to the host's available conditions rather than claim a privilege the host is not enjoying.

### AR540 — R787/C1578
Self-asserted status does not establish superiority, especially when the claim depends on demeaning those already present.

### AR541 — R788/C1587
Comparative knowledge requires exposure beyond one's own practice; isolation can produce unjustified beliefs in unique or superior capability.
