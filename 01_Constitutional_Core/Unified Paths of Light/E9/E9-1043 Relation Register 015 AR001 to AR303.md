### Emerging network structure

The corpus is now showing relationships not only as pairwise obligations but as **networks**:
- support can depend on central relational nodes (AR299);
- degrees of proximity can coexist inside one support network (AR284, AR293);
- guidance can spill beyond its intended recipient (AR302);
- local household conditions can aggregate into collective failure (AR287);
- false information can propagate between groups and generate conflict (AR292).

This suggests that later compression should test both **agent-to-agent** and **network/system-level** formulations rather than assuming every principle is dyadic.
