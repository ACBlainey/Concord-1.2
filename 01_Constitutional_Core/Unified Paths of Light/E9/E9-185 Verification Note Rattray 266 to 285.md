### Strong source anchor
R276 contains an explicit interpretation: the saying means that one should not be guided by appearances. Rattray explains that the ewe sees the leopard's beautiful skin but fails to investigate its ferocious nature.

### Historical mediation
R285's note states that Salaga was historically a caravan centre and once a major centre of the slave trade, to which the proverb alludes. This contextual fact is preserved without manufacturing a normative meaning not supplied by the source.
