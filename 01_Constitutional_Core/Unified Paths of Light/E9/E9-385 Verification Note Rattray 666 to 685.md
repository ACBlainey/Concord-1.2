### OCR numbering repairs
The local OCR visibly corrupts five consecutive Rattray item numbers:
- R677 appears as “QTJ,”
- R678 appears as “178.”
- R679 appears as “J79.”
- R680 appears as “180.”
- R681 appears as “881.”

These are not bare sequential guesses. The contiguous source passage sits between visible R676 and R682 and preserves the corresponding Christaller identifiers C3069, C3070, C3071, C3072 and C3073 in order. The reconstruction is therefore source-reconciled while the OCR defects remain explicitly recorded.

### R672 textual note
Rattray states that Christaller's *Tshi Proverbs* writes the relevant form as “perew,” whereas Rattray reports hearing the saying as “pirew.” This variant is retained as source history and does not alter the ethical classification.

### R675 taboo note
Rattray explicitly says R675 demonstrates the strength of taboo. E9 therefore leaves the item H rather than silently converting ritual consistency into a universal ethical rule.

### R677 euphemism note
Rattray says the verb for the river “taking” a person is euphemistic, apparently to avoid offending the river spirit. The supernatural explanation is mediation/context, not an admitted causal claim.
