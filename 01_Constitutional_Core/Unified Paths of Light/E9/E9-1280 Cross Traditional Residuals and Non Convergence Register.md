## E9-1280 — Cross-Traditional Residuals and Non-Convergence Register

### Purpose

Convergence must not erase residuals.

### Akan residual emphases

Relative to the compact X kernel and prior compressed architectures, E9 preserves especially explicit treatment of:
- recipient-fit communication;
- legitimate information boundaries;
- causal attribution of responsibility;
- association/non-transfer;
- entitlement and productive contribution;
- adaptive continuity;
- dependency and bargaining structure;
- contestability of power;
- network support;
- local-to-system propagation;
- resilience and robustness.

### Interpretation limits

These residuals may arise from:
- Akan ethical emphasis;
- proverb/oral-medium properties;
- Rattray's selection and thematic organisation;
- translation/collector mediation;
- E9's improved architecture-separation method;
- differences in source density or genre.

E9 alone cannot assign causation among these explanations.

### Non-convergence preserved

No claim is made that:
- all traditions contain identical roots;
- all residuals reduce to X1–X7;
- root counts should converge;
- the Akan corpus is exhaustive;
- historical independence is proven.

Residual structure remains data for later experiments.
