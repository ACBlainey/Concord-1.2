### Provisional local parents induced

**PC-P001 — Performance-grounded capability assessment**  
A capability judgment should be grounded in demonstrated function rather than assertion, appearance, status or irrelevant scale.

Candidate children:
- AR005 — claimed capability tested against consequences/performance.
- AR010 — actual capability rather than assertion.
- AR024 — capability is multidimensional.
- AR026 — experience/judgment can outweigh physical scale.

**PC-P002 — Experience-mediated correction and guidance**

Candidate children:
- AR012 — experience provides epistemic guidance.
- AR019 — experience corrects awareness/danger response.
- AR020 — experience corrects conceit/self-assessment.

AR019 and AR020 remain separate children; they were explicitly retained during duplicate audit.

**PC-P003 — Reciprocity-support relation**  
Benefits, sharing and protection can generate reciprocal expectations without making every reciprocal mechanism identical.

Candidate children:
- AR007 — exclusive self-consumption conflicts with a sharing expectation.
- AR017 — sharing is linked to reciprocal protection.

**PC-P004 — Dependency-sensitive vulnerability and care**  
Dependency or loss of support can create vulnerability and activate obligations or support relations.

Candidate children:
- AR008 — loss of caregiver/support creates dependent vulnerability.
- AR022 — family/dependent concern activates support.
