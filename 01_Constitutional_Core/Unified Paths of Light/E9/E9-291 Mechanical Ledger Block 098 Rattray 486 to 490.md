# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-291 — Mechanical Ledger Block 098: Rattray 486–490

486 / C2060 = Y — even where a close relative bears a low-status label and associated duty, kinship restrains humiliating or taunting treatment. Formal status does not erase relational obligations concerning dignity.

487 / C2061 = H — one's mother's child is one's kinsman. This is a direct statement of matrilineal kin classification, but no independent transferable ethical function beyond the already established kinship structure is required here.

488 / C2063 = Y — poverty does not cancel the mother-child relationship or justify replacing the poor mother with a more advantageous substitute. Material disadvantage does not by itself dissolve a durable care/kinship relation.

489 / C2064 = H — when one's mother does not go to market, a stepmother is sent. Substitution within the polygamous household is visible, but its ethical function is insufficiently specified.

490 / C2067 = Y — before undertaking a demanding funeral obligation, one should first meet a basic bodily need so as not to collapse during the duty. Responsible participation can require maintaining one's own minimum functional capacity.
