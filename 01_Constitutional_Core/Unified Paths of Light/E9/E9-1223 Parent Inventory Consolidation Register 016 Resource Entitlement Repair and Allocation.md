# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1223 — Parent Inventory Consolidation Register 016: Resource Entitlement, Repair and Allocation

### Higher internally derived parents

**HC-P035 — Desire, association or abundance do not by themselves create entitlement**

Subsumes:
- PC-P084 — desire does not establish entitlement;
- relevant portions of PC-P031;
- AR365/475/499/517/555;
- AR131 capability to reach/exploit does not create entitlement.

Core structure:
> A claim to a resource requires a basis beyond desire, another's abundance, relational association, requester status or mere ability to take or control it.

**HC-P036 — Productive contribution and causal role can ground differentiated claims**

Subsumes:
- PC-P047 — productive causal-role attribution;
- AR267 contribution and fruits;
- AR181/185/186/479 producer/enabler/carrier/credit distinctions.

Core structure:
> Claims to credit, benefit or product should track relevant productive contribution and causal role rather than mere transport, possession, proximity or appropriation.

**HC-P037 — Repair and reuse should track recoverable function**

Subsumes:
- PC-P049;
- PC-P094;
- M011;
- AR413/493/550.

Core structure:
> Damaged or failed resources should be repaired, reused, abandoned or replaced according to whether useful function remains recoverable and relevant to the objective.

### Relation

HC-P037 is a resource-domain expression of the proportional/function-preserving response already represented in HC-P024. It remains a **BRIDGE**, not a duplicate, until architecture separation decides whether the general operator and domain implementation should occupy different layers.
