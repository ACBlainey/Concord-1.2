# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-404 — Mechanical Ledger Block 145: Rattray 721–725

721 / C150 = Y — the Rattray number is absent in the OCR. White clay marking acquittal is not taken after a person who has fled into the forest after being found guilty. Recognition or restorative status associated with exoneration should not be conferred where the relevant exonerating condition is absent.

722 / C155 = Y — a debtor asking for time does not reveal how bad affairs really are. Negotiation under vulnerability can involve strategic under-disclosure of the agent's true degree of distress; self-report may therefore understate need.

723 / C156 = Y — Rattray explicitly glosses the proverb as a chief refusing to let others use his prestige to extort and rob. An agent should not allow delegated or associated actors to weaponise the agent's authority or reputation for exploitation.

724 / C158 = Y — one does not test a river's depth with both feet. Under uncertainty, investigation should preserve a margin of safety rather than committing all available support/capacity before the hazard is known.

725 / C159 = H — pointing with the left hand is described as insulting within Ashanti custom, including when indicating one's father's old village. The source securely establishes a culturally specific respect convention, but the hand-specific rule is not admitted as a transferable ethical occurrence.
