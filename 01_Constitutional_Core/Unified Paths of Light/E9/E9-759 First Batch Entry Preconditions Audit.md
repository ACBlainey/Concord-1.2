## E9-759 — First-Batch Entry Preconditions Audit

The first 20-item Christaller inventory batch may begin only when all of the following are simultaneously visible:

1. a direct 1879 T0 page;
2. the printed C-ID or other exact item locator;
3. complete source-language proverb wording;
4. any attached variant marker/variant wording;
5. sufficient surrounding structure to establish printed order.
