# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-491 — Lange T1 Bibliographic Verification

The frozen T1 candidate has been positively identified.

Bibliographic records identify:

**J. G. Christaller, *Three Thousand Six Hundred Ghanian Proverbs (from the Asante and Fante Language)*, translated by Kofi Ron Lange, Edwin Mellen Press, 1990.**

The 1990 record:
- identifies Christaller as compiler;
- identifies Kofi Ron Lange as translator;
- describes the book as a translation of Christaller's 1879 *Twi mmebusem*;
- assigns ISBN 0-88946-234-8;
- records approximately 302 pages;
- classifies the text languages as English and Akan/Twi.

A later Mellen edition/reissue appears in 1999/2000 with ISBN 0-7734-7855-8 and a new foreword.

### Gate result
The existence, identity and intended scope of the T1 witness are verified.

This does not yet mean that its complete item-level text is mechanically accessible to E9.
