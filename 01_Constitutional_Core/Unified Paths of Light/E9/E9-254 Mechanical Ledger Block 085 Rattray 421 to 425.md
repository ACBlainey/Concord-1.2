# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-254 — Mechanical Ledger Block 085: Rattray 421–425

421 / C1315 = H — the chief's enemy is said to be the person who grew up with him from childhood. The association between intimate historical knowledge and later hostility is explicit, but its transferable ethical function is not sufficiently determined.

422 / C1317 = H — a chief is like an odum tree with neither front nor back. Without further explanatory gloss, the political metaphor remains too ambiguous for a secure occurrence.

423 / C1321 = H — a chief's child does not run to view an executed person's severed head because such heads are brought to the chief's house. This is historical/social-context evidence involving execution; no independent ethical prescription is extracted.

424 / C1322 = H — a chief's servant eats fish and “gets ideas,” with Rattray noting a wordplay. The intended normative content is insufficiently clear.

425 / C1324 = Y — the chief's servant is said to cause the chief's person to excite fear. A leader's perceived coercive power can be produced or amplified by subordinate agents acting around the officeholder.

### AR255 — R425/C1324
The perceived coercive power of a leader or office can be produced or amplified by subordinate agents who act in its name or presence.
