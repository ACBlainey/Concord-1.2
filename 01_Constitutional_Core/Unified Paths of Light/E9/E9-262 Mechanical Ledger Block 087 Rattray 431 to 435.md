# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-262 — Mechanical Ledger Block 087: Rattray 431–435

431 / C834 = Y — the same condition described as madness in an ordinary case is redescribed as drunkenness when the person is noble. Social status can bias diagnosis or interpretation of equivalent behaviour.

432 / C835 = H — a free man's name is never lost. Persistence of lineage/status memory is visible, but the ethical function is underdetermined.

433 / C836 = Y — nobility should be borne humbly rather than proudly and boastfully. High inherited status does not justify ostentatious superiority.

434 / C838 = Y — fame from noble birth does not spread as riches do. Inherited status and materially observable resources generate different forms of social recognition; title alone may have limited external reach.

435 / C839 = H — when the free man does not fight, the slave runs away. The hierarchy/dependency relation is clear, but the source does not establish a secure ethical abstraction.

### AR259 — R433/C836
