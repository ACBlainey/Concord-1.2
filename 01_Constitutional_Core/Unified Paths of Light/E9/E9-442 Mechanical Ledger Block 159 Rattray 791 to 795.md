# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-442 — Mechanical Ledger Block 159: Rattray 791–795

791 / C1888 = Y — it is on the path one does not fear that a wild beast catches one. Familiarity or low perceived risk can reduce vigilance; absence of fear is not evidence of absence of hazard.

792 / C1901 = Y — a traveller may report what was seen but cannot explain everything. Observation and explanation are distinct epistemic capabilities; witnessing an event does not automatically confer full causal or interpretive understanding.

793 / C1893 = Y — the OCR prints the number as “7 93.” A path has ears. Information disclosed in apparently open or unowned settings may still reach unintended listeners; context does not guarantee confidentiality.

794 / C1940 = H — salt is obtained from water and then put back into water. Cyclical return, transformation and source relation are plausible mechanisms, but no transferable ethical meaning is fixed by the source.

795 / C1942 = Y — salt does not praise its own taste. Evaluation of quality should not rely solely on self-assertion by the thing or agent being evaluated.

### AR542 — R791/C1888
Low perceived risk or familiarity should not be treated as evidence that a hazard is absent.

### AR543 — R792/C1901
Observation and explanation are distinct epistemic capabilities; direct experience does not guarantee complete understanding.

### AR544 — R793/C1893
Information disclosed in apparently open settings may reach unintended listeners; environmental context does not guarantee confidentiality.

### AR545 — R795/C1942
Claims of quality gain reliability from evaluation beyond the subject's own self-assertion.
