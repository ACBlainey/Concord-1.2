# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-313 — Mechanical Ledger Block 108: Rattray 536–540

536 / C666 = Y — the destination itself does not cause distress; the dangerous surf at the point of access does. A system or objective can be acceptable while the transition/interface required to reach it is the principal source of risk.

537 / C2431 = Y — all people are said to want the opportunity to travel, but opportunity is what they lack. Desire or willingness does not imply practical access; unequal opportunity can constrain realised choice.

538 / C668 = Y — the European governor living in the castle still lies in the ground when dead. High office, wealth or protected residence does not remove basic human mortality.

539 / C669 = Y — the person who supplies knives nevertheless leaves his own hair uncut. Possession or supply of a useful capability/resource does not guarantee its application to one's own needs.

540 / C644 = H — the saying credits Europeans with the community's use of cloth. It records attributed external technological/material influence, but no independent ethical function is sufficiently explicit.

### AR326 — R536/C666
The principal risk in pursuing an otherwise acceptable objective can lie in the transition or access interface rather than in the destination itself.

### AR327 — R537/C2431
Desire or willingness does not establish effective choice where access or opportunity is absent.

### AR328 — R538/C668
High status, office or material protection does not eliminate fundamental human vulnerability to mortality.

### AR329 — R539/C669
Possessing or supplying a useful capability does not guarantee applying that capability to one's own condition.
