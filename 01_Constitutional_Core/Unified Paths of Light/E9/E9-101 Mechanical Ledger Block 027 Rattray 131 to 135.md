## E9-101 — Mechanical Ledger Block 027: Rattray 131–135

131 / C3416 = Y — the antelope's unhappiness is attributed to the hunter. This directly assigns an adverse condition to an external agent whose activity creates the threat.

132 / C3417 = H — a duiker with an abnormal thick leg is avoided/tabooed. The avoidance rule is explicit, but its transferable ethical function is unclear.

133 / C3418 = Y — Rattray supplies a story in which a master repeatedly refuses his hunter any share of the game the hunter obtains; the hunter consequently withholds the antelope rather than deliver it. The occurrence links exclusion from benefit to withdrawal of productive cooperation.

134 / C859 = H — the crocodile lives in water but also breathes air. Multi-domain dependence is clear, but no ethical prescription is explicit.

135 / C860 = Z — crocodile skin is described as especially desirable food; accompanying notes concern animal skins and idiomatic expressions. No admissible ethical function is established.

### AR049 — Rattray 131 / Christaller 3416
**Minimal function:** An agent's adverse condition may be causally attributable to another agent whose activity creates the threat or pressure.
**Confidence:** high.

### AR050 — Rattray 133 / Christaller 3418
**Minimal function:** Persistent exclusion of a contributor from the benefits of their own productive activity can undermine willingness to continue supplying those benefits.
**Confidence:** high; Rattray provides the explanatory story.
