# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-753 — Alternative T0 Full-View Witness Verification

Google Books independently indexes full-view copies of the 1879 Christaller work.

### Verified records
- Columbia-derived digitisation: 152 pages; digitised 19 February 2009.
- Harvard-derived digitisation: Google reports 302 digital pages for another full-view 1879 record.

### Disposition
These are valid secondary T0 witness routes, but the different digital-page counts reinforce the rule that viewer-page sequence cannot substitute for Christaller printed pagination or identifier order.
