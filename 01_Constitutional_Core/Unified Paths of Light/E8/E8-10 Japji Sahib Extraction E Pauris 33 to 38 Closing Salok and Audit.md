## High-recurrence E8-internal themes
Without importing any prior kernel, Japji repeatedly yields:
- truthful/substantive orientation rather than performance;
- limits on knowledge and self-certainty;
- listening, learning and transformation;
- reduction of ego/status inflation;
- critique of coercive force, killing, theft and falsehood;
- inward correction rather than ritual cleansing;
- giving/receiving/consumption responsibility;
- compassion and contentment;
- formation of capability through disciplined practice;
- action and consequence.

## Important absences/limits
Japji alone does not justify freezing:
- a comprehensive theory of property;
- a complete doctrine of political authority;
- a general positive welfare obligation;
- a full conflict/self-defence doctrine;
- a privacy principle;
- a complete institutional accountability architecture.

Those must emerge, if at all, from later corpus strata rather than being projected backward.

## Translation/provenance note
Canonical ang and Gurmukhi were retained as locators; English translation was used as an interpretive aid. Several terms—especially *hukam*, *haumai*, *nām*, *dharam* and devotional/cosmological language—remain translation-sensitive and require later audit before publication-grade claims.
