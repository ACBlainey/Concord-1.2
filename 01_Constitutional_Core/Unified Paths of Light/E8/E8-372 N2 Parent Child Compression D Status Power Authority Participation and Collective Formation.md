# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-372 — N2 Parent-Child Compression D: Status, Power, Authority, Participation and Collective Formation

## Parent candidate: non-domination and accountable power
The following N1 families form one higher-order structure:
- non-superiority by status;
- anti-domination / anti-exploitation;
- authority accountability;
- resistance to prestige capture;
- exemplarity through realised conduct.

The shared function is limiting the conversion of status, prestige or authority into self-validating or exploitative power. The parent is provisionally labelled **non-domination and accountable power**.

## Parent candidate: constructive collective participation
The following form a second structure:
- inclusive participation;
- constructive association;
- collective developmental environment.

The shared function is collective life that permits participation while supporting beneficial formation. The parent is provisionally labelled **constructive collective participation**.

## Boundary
No governmental form is inferred. Participation is not compulsory conformity. Exemplarity remains a child mechanism of accountable influence, not an entitlement to authority.
