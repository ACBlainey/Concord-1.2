# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-149 — Kalyan Extraction A: Ang 1319–1322

## Coverage
Direct-source first-pass extraction across the opening Guru Ram Das material and transition into Guru Arjan.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| KY001 | Ang 1319, Guru Ram Das | D/E | Even virtuous and learned persons are represented as unable to exhaustively appraise reality; developed knowledge therefore does not imply complete knowledge. |
| KY002 | Ang 1319 | C/E | Guidance is represented as a continuing support for agents who explicitly recognise their own limited understanding. |
| KY003 | Ang 1319 | C/G | Living beings are represented as dependent recipients of care/protection; the immediate grounding is theological and is not automatically secularised. |
| KY004 | Ang 1320, Guru Ram Das | D/S | Inner intention remains relevant to evaluating a person's orientation; public speech alone is not the whole evidential field. |
| KY005 | Ang 1320 | D/G | Slander/backbiting is treated as defective conduct rather than a reliable basis for judging another person. |
| KY006 | Ang 1321–1322, Guru Arjan transition | C/E | Guidance and repeated practice are represented as formative where internalised. |
| KY007 | Ang 1322, Guru Arjan | D/S | Trust, perception and repeated orientation are represented as embodied practices rather than merely verbal commitments. |

## Direct-source control
The opening source explicitly states that learned/virtuous persons cannot fully appraise the object of inquiry. This is coded as epistemic limitation, not as anti-intellectualism.

The ang-1320 slander material is recorded conservatively: it supports criticism of malicious information transfer, but does not by itself establish a complete evidentiary procedure.
