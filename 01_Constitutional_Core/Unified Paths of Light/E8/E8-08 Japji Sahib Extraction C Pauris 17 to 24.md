# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-08 — Japji Sahib Extraction C: Pauris 17–24

## Coverage
Guru Granth Sahib ang 4–5; paired catalogues of constructive/destructive conduct, language, impurity/correction, and limits of knowledge.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| SJ023 | Pauri 17 | V/G | Giving, contemplation, disciplined devotion and courageous endurance are presented positively without reducing goodness to one social role. |
| SJ024 | Pauri 18 | P/G | Theft and wrongful taking are negatively classified. |
| SJ025 | Pauri 18 | P/R | Imposing one's will through force is negatively classified. |
| SJ026 | Pauri 18 | P/K | Ruthless killing is negatively classified. |
| SJ027 | Pauri 18 | P/G | Persistent falsehood is negatively classified. |
| SJ028 | Pauris 17–18 | D/G | Human conduct contains many forms of virtue and wrongdoing; enumeration is explicitly incomplete, cautioning against claims of exhaustive classification. |

| SJ033 | Pauris 21–22 | D/S | Claims to comprehensive knowledge are constrained by the scale and limits of the knower. |
| SJ034 | Pauri 22 | D/S | Multiple inherited cosmological accounts are juxtaposed within a larger assertion of epistemic limitation; disagreement does not license false certainty. |
| SJ035 | Pauri 23 | V/S | Praise or participation does not imply comprehension of the whole; proximity to a domain is not exhaustive knowledge of it. |
| SJ036 | Pauri 24 | D/S | Magnitude, generosity and reality are described as exceeding human measurement; quantified description has limits. |

## Context controls
The wrongdoing catalogue is explicit enough to code theft, coercive force, killing and falsehood as behavioural negatives. Broader modern doctrines are withheld.

## Emerging tensions
Knowledge versus overclaim; ritual versus substantive correction; coercive force versus legitimate protection remains unresolved here; generosity appears but positive aid duties are not yet inferred.
