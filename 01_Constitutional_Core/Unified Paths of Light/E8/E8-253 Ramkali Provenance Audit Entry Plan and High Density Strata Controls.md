## Source-derived reason for a dedicated plan

The provenance audit must therefore avoid treating Ramkali as one homogeneous range.

## Audit priorities
1. Reconstruct the complete RK sequence from the actual Ramkali extraction files.
2. Preserve internal composition-family transitions.
3. Preserve contributor identity, especially in dialogue, Var and succession/memory material.
4. Apply exact-ESU scrutiny to any proposition concerning authority, succession, collective order, teaching, institutional memory or social power.
5. Do not infer later Sikh institutional doctrine from later sources.
6. Do not promote the apparent density of a sub-stratum into a root-level conclusion during provenance work.
7. Reconcile the historical Ramkali count only after every source file has been inspected.
