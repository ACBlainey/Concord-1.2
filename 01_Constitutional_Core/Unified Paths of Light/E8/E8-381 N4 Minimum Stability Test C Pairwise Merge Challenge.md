# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-381 — N4 Minimum-Stability Test C: Pairwise Merge Challenge

## Purpose
Attempt the strongest plausible pairwise compressions among the seven parents.

## Reliable orientation + developmental correctability
Rejected. Directional reliability and temporal correction answer different functional questions.

## Reliable orientation + self-regulated agency
Rejected. Epistemic reliability and resistance to internal capture are coupled but non-identical.

## Developmental correctability + self-regulated agency
Rejected. Recovery/formation through time cannot be reduced to present-state self-control.

## Outward beneficence + responsible material relation
Rejected. Helping others does not exhaust ethical acquisition, use and non-exploitation of resources.

## Outward beneficence + constructive collective participation
Rejected. Direct assistance differs from the formative properties of collective association.

## Responsible material relation + non-domination/accountable power
Rejected. Resource ethics overlaps with exploitation but power extends beyond material relations.

## Constructive collective participation + non-domination/accountable power
Rejected. A participatory environment can still contain unaccountable or prestige-captured authority.

## Outward beneficence + non-domination/accountable power
Rejected. Benevolent intention/action does not itself solve the structural problem of power capture.

## Result
No tested pair merges without deleting a distinguishable function or silently broadening one parent beyond its corpus-derived meaning.
