# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-357 — N0 Catalogue Construction D: Equality, Status, Power and Anti-Domination Families

## Stage control
E8-only N0 construction.

## Overcomplete functional families
Provisional families include:
1. rejection of pride based on rank or status;
2. moral non-superiority of socially elevated persons;
3. dignity not reducible to wealth or position;
4. criticism of domination by ego/status;
5. responsibility attached to authority and power;
6. restraint on exploitative or predatory conduct;
7. resistance to deference based solely on social standing;
8. inclusion in collective participation;
9. valuation of persons through conduct/orientation rather than inherited prestige;
10. anti-capture pressure against self-serving authority;
11. independence from praise and reputation as behavioural control;
12. solidarity across socially differentiating boundaries.

## Boundary controls
The catalogue does not infer a modern constitutional model, electoral system, caste statute, rights code or institutional design unless directly supported by an admitted functional occurrence. Spiritual equality is not automatically converted into a complete political equality doctrine.

## N0 result
Status/power material is sufficiently recurrent to remain a distinct N0 domain. Whether equality, humility and anti-domination later share a parent is deliberately unresolved.
