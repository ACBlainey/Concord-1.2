# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-116 — Maru Extraction H: Ang 1093–1106, Terminal and Bhagat Strata

## Coverage
Sequential completion of the canonical Maru range through ang 1106. The terminal material includes contributor diversity and is not treated as a single voice. Tukhari begins at ang 1107.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| MR049 | Ang 1093–1096 | D/R | Social or religious standing remains subject to substantive evaluation rather than validating itself through title. |
| MR050 | Ang 1095–1098 | C/C | Collective norms can transmit both constructive and defective orientations. |
| MR051 | Ang 1097–1100 | D/G | Convention and repetition do not by themselves establish substantive validity. |
| MR052 | Ang 1099–1102 | C/E | Correction requires changed orientation and practice rather than recognition alone. |
| MR053 | Ang 1101–1104, late contributor strata | D/G | Inherited status or public identity is not sufficient evidence of realised worth. |
| MR054 | Ang 1102–1105 | C/S | Egoic self-magnification can persist inside disciplined or religious practice. |
| MR055 | Ang 1104–1106 | V/S | Humility and reduced self-magnification are positively valued. |
| MR056 | Ang 1104–1106 | C/C | Teaching and developed orientation can cross social boundaries through transmission and association. |

## Boundary control
No ang 1107 material is assigned to Maru. The Tukhari transition controls closure.
