# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-09 — Japji Sahib Extraction D: Pauris 25–32

## Coverage
Guru Granth Sahib ang 5–7; receiving/consumption, epistemic humility, universal care imagery, ascetic symbolism and Pauri 32.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| SJ037 | Pauri 25 | C/M | Receiving repeatedly while denying receipt is presented negatively, supporting responsibility in acknowledgement of benefit. |
| SJ038 | Pauri 25 | C/M | Unbounded consumption is associated with folly rather than treated as an unquestioned good. |
| SJ039 | Pauri 25 | N/G | Distress, hunger and abuse are explicitly recognised as conditions within human existence; the passage does not yet specify a general distributive rule. |
| SJ040 | Pauri 26 | D/S | The limits of description are repeatedly emphasised; confidence should not outrun what can responsibly be known. |
| SJ041 | Pauri 26 | V/S | Presumptuous claims to exhaustive knowledge are criticised. |
| SJ042 | Pauri 27 | V/G | Care is represented as extending across the whole creation; this remains theological imagery unless later passages support a behavioural duty. |
| SJ043 | Pauri 27 | D/S | Diversity of voices, beings and forms is situated within a wider unity without requiring sameness. |
| SJ044 | Pauri 28 | V/S | Contentment and disciplined self-restraint are privileged over external ascetic display. |
| SJ045 | Pauri 28 | V/G | A symbolic ethic of universal human relatedness appears; its exact social implications require corroboration. |
| SJ046 | Pauri 29 | V/S | Knowledge and compassion are paired within the ascetic metaphor; compassion is not treated as dispensable from disciplined understanding. |
| SJ047 | Pauri 29 | D/S | Symbolic/spiritual practice is evaluated by inward function rather than ornament alone. |
| SJ048 | Pauris 29–31 | D/S | Cosmological and devotional material dominates; no additional civil ethical proposition is extracted where separability is weak. |
| SJ049 | Pauri 32 | V/S | Repetition, aspiration and effort are accompanied by an explicit rejection of boastful self-sufficiency. |

## No-yield control
Pauris dominated by cosmology are recorded as covered rather than mined for forced ethical analogues.

## Emerging families
Consumption/restraint; acknowledgement; epistemic humility; compassion; contentment; unity-with-difference; function over external form.
