# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-377 — N3 Generative Architecture and Inter-Layer Dependency Test

## Purpose
Test whether the seven E8-internal parents are merely a list or form a generative architecture.

## Dependency pattern
The corpus-derived structure supports a recurrent sequence:

**reliable orientation**
→ improves the direction of agency;

**self-regulated agency**
→ reduces internal capture that prevents orientation becoming action;

**developmental correctability**
→ permits revision and maturation when orientation or agency fails;

these agent-level functions condition:

**outward beneficence**
and
**responsible material relation**;

which operate within:

**constructive collective participation**
and under conditions requiring:

**non-domination and accountable power**.

Across every layer, **inward-outward integrity** tests whether stated orientation is realised.

## Non-linearity
This is not a one-way ladder. Collective environments affect development; power affects truth and agency; material conditions affect participation; beneficent action reinforces formation.

The architecture is therefore **recursive and generative**, not simply sequential.

## Compression test
No one of the seven parents can absorb another without losing a distinct functional question:
- What reliably orients?
- What stabilises agency?
- What permits correction through time?
- How is concern for others enacted?
- How are material relations constrained?
- How does collective participation form agents?
- How is power prevented from becoming self-validating domination?

## N3 result
The seven-parent structure survives architecture-level separation.
