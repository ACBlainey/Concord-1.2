# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-12 — Opening Bani Extraction B: Sohila

## Coverage
Kirtan Sohila material in the opening canonical sequence, preserving contributor and theological context.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| SO011 | Sohila, Guru Nanak | D/S | Human life is framed as finite and consequential; awareness of mortality can re-order priorities. |
| SO012 | Sohila | V/S | Attachment to transient possession/status is treated as an unreliable basis for enduring orientation. |
| SO013 | Sohila | D/S | Multiple systems of time, ritual and religious description are placed within a reality larger than any one convention. |
| SO014 | Sohila | D/G | Diversity of ritual or descriptive form does not by itself establish different ultimate human worth. |
| SO015 | Sohila, Guru Ram Das | C/S | Learned guidance and disciplined remembrance are treated as developmental rather than merely ceremonial. |
| SO016 | Sohila | V/S | Longing/attention can direct sustained practice; desire is not treated as ethically neutral merely because it is inward. |
| SO017 | Sohila, Guru Arjan | V/S | Fear associated with mortality is answered through reorientation rather than accumulation or social prestige. |
| SO018 | Sohila | D/S | The value of practice is assessed through the orientation it produces, not simply the performance of a form. |
| SO019 | Sohila | C/G | Human beings share vulnerability to loss, change and death irrespective of rank. |
| SO020 | Sohila | D/S | Claims extracted from symbolic wedding/death imagery require contextual restraint; no literal family or marriage rule is inferred from metaphor alone. |
