# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-379 — N4 Minimum-Stability Test A: Parent Removal — Reliable Orientation, Development and Agency

## Test rule
Remove each parent in turn and ask whether its distinctive function can be supplied by the remaining E8-internal architecture without semantic substitution.

## Remove reliable orientation
Without reliable orientation, the architecture retains regulation, development and outward conduct but loses the function that distinguishes truthful/reliable direction from merely stable or effective action.

**Result: FAIL REMOVAL. Parent required.**

## Remove developmental correctability
Without developmental correctability, the architecture can describe present orientation and regulation but loses correction across time, formation after error and renewed agency after deficiency/adversity.

**Result: FAIL REMOVAL. Parent required.**

## Remove self-regulated agency
Without self-regulated agency, the architecture retains knowledge of direction and capacity for development but loses the specific control problem created by ego, fear, attachment, anger and destabilising impulse.

**Result: FAIL REMOVAL. Parent required.**

## Cross-substitution test
Reliable orientation cannot substitute for agency stability: knowing does not guarantee acting.  
Agency stability cannot substitute for development: stable present action does not provide correction across time.  
Development cannot substitute for orientation: change alone does not determine reliable direction.

# **N4 REMOVAL TEST A: ALL THREE PARENTS REQUIRED**
