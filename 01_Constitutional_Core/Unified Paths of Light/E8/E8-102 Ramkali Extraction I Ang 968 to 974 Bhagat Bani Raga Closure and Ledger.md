## Information-yield observation
Ramkali is one of the richest E8 strata so far. It adds unusually explicit material concerning:
- limits of formal knowledge;
- inquiry and dialogical challenge;
- realised understanding versus nominal expertise;
- inward self-regulation;
- authority/function distinction;
- collective transmission;
- succession and continuity;
- mortality and social continuity.

Even here, much of the material appears as recombination and domain-specific expression rather than an explosion of new first-order functions.
