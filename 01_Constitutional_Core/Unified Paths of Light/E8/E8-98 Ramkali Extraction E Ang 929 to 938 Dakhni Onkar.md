# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-98 — Ramkali Extraction E: Ang 929–938, Dakhni Onkar

## Coverage
Dakhni Onkar is a distinct 54-stanza composition of Guru Nanak in Ramkali, spanning ang 929–938. Its semi-acrostic and dialogical/teaching structure is preserved rather than flattened into ordinary hymn material.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| RK036 | Ang 929–931 | D/E | Literacy, textual learning and inherited teaching are not sufficient where understanding is not realised in conduct. |
| RK037 | Ang 930–932 | C/E | Knowledge is represented as meaningful when it produces discernment and developed qualities. |
| RK038 | Ang 931 | V/E | Reflecting upon and embodying merit is associated with genuine knowing rather than nominal expertise. |
| RK039 | Ang 931–933 | C/S | Desire, anger and egoic drives can degrade embodied agency and judgement. |
| RK040 | Ang 932–934 | D/G | Learned or religious status does not independently validate the holder's judgement or conduct. |
| RK041 | Ang 933–935 | C/E | Teaching should alter orientation and practice rather than remain symbolic inscription or verbal form. |
| RK042 | Ang 934–936 | D/S | Human understanding remains limited and should not be converted into claims of exhaustive mastery. |
| RK043 | Ang 936–938 | C/S | Action is represented as consequential; present conduct cannot simply be detached from later result. |
| RK044 | Ang 936–938 | V/S | Disciplined attention and restraint are represented as cultivable capacities. |

## Source-sensitive observation
Dakhni Onkar increases the density of epistemic and educational material. It distinguishes textual/formal knowledge from realised judgement without rejecting learning itself.
