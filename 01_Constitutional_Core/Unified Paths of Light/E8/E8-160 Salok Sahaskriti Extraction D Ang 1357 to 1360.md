# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-160 — Salok Sahaskriti Extraction D: Ang 1357–1360

## Coverage
Sequential continuation of Guru Arjan's Salok Sahaskriti through ang 1360.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| SS018 | Ang 1357 | C/S | Pleasure and pain can destabilise orientation when either is treated as permanent. |
| SS019 | Ang 1357 | V/S | Steadiness across gain/loss or pleasure/pain is represented as a developed capacity. |
| SS020 | Ang 1357–1358 | C/E | Corrective orientation remains possible despite prior instability. |
| SS021 | Ang 1358–1359 | D/S | Dispute and verbal activity can coexist with failure to remain aligned with truth. |
| SS022 | Ang 1359 | D/E | Speech or recitation does not substitute automatically for substantive understanding. |
| SS023 | Ang 1360 | D/S | Mortality is presented as certain while visible conditions are impermanent. |
| SS024 | Ang 1360 | C/S | Awareness of mortality functions as a consequence horizon for present conduct. |

## Direct-source anchors
The source explicitly describes the world as afflicted by pleasure and pain, later contrasts truth with dispute, and at ang 1360 states the certainty of death against the instability of visible conditions.

## Interpretive restraint
The dispute material is not converted into a general prohibition on disagreement. The narrower functional signal is that argumentative activity is not itself evidence of truth.
