# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-176 — Swayyas Extraction B: Ang 1392–1396, Guru Amar Das Stratum

## Coverage
First-pass extraction from the Guru Amar Das praise stratum. The admission threshold remains intentionally high.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| SW008 | Ang 1392–1393 | C/E | Instruction is represented as valuable through realised transformation rather than praise alone. |
| SW009 | Ang 1393–1394 | V/S | Humility is represented as compatible with recognised authority and therefore not displaced by status. |
| SW010 | Ang 1393–1395 | C/C | Developed orientation can be transmitted through teaching and formative association. |
| SW011 | Ang 1394–1395 | D/R | Authority language is retained as contextual evidence unless accompanied by a separable responsibility or conduct claim. |
| SW012 | Ang 1395–1396 | D/G | Recognition by others remains distinguishable from substantive ethical quality. |
| SW013 | Ang 1395–1396 | C/E | Continuity of teaching is represented as developmental transmission rather than mere institutional succession. |

## Negative evidence
No complete doctrine of political authority, appointment, accountability or collective decision procedure is extracted from this praise stratum.

This absence is itself important: the Swayyas should not be made to answer institutional questions they do not directly address.
