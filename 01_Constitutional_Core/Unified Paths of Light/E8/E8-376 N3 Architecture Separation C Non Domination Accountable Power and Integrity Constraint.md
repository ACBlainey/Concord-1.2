# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-376 — N3 Architecture Separation C: Non-Domination, Accountable Power and Integrity Constraint

## Non-domination and accountable power
This parent functions as a **power-relation layer**. Its distinctive concern is the risk that status, prestige, authority or influence becomes self-validating, exploitative or insulated from ethical constraint.

This function is not exhausted by beneficence, material honesty or collective participation. A relationship may be generous or participatory while still containing unaccountable power.

## Inward-outward integrity
The N2 cross-cutting integrity constraint is tested for architectural placement.

It is not a separate behavioural domain comparable to material relation or power. Instead it operates across the architecture by requiring that claimed orientation be realised in conduct.

Its role is therefore best retained as a **cross-layer coherence constraint**.

## Function of the constraint
Integrity tests:
- truth claims against conduct;
- inward restraint against outward behaviour;
- compassionate orientation against practical response;
- authority claims against realised accountability;
- developmental claims against actual correction.

## Architectural result
Non-domination/accountable power remains a co-level parent. Inward-outward integrity remains a cross-cutting constraint and is not counted as an eighth co-level parent.
