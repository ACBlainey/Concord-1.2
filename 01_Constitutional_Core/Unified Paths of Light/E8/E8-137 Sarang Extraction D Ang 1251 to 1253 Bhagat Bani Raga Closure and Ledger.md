## Important source-derived observation
Parmanand at ang 1253 explicitly couples failed scriptural listening with failure to give to the hungry. This is stronger source-level evidence for a positive response to severe material need than the more inferential care signals in several earlier strata. It is recorded without yet deciding whether it becomes a root, derived rule or contextual expression.

## Information-yield observation
Sarang is still predominantly recurrence-rich, but the Parmanand record demonstrates why structural completion remains necessary even after marginal novelty has fallen: a late terminal source can sharpen a function that earlier passages only weakly supported.
