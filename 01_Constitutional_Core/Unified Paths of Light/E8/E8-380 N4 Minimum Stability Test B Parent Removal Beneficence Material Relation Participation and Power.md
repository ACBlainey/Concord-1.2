# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-380 — N4 Minimum-Stability Test B: Parent Removal — Beneficence, Material Relation, Participation and Power

## Remove outward beneficence
The remaining architecture can regulate resources, participation and power but loses the direct other-regarding function of compassion, assistance, service and relief.

**Result: FAIL REMOVAL. Parent required.**

## Remove responsible material relation
Beneficence does not fully govern acquisition, use, exploitation, sufficiency or reciprocal material contribution. The resource-specific function is lost.

**Result: FAIL REMOVAL. Parent required.**

## Remove constructive collective participation
The remaining architecture can describe individual relations and accountable power but loses the formative function of association, inclusion and community environment.

**Result: FAIL REMOVAL. Parent required.**

## Remove non-domination and accountable power
Participation and beneficence do not independently prevent authority, prestige or status from becoming self-validating or exploitative.

**Result: FAIL REMOVAL. Parent required.**

## Result
Each of the four outward/social parents supplies a function not preserved by the others.

# **N4 REMOVAL TEST B: ALL FOUR PARENTS REQUIRED**
