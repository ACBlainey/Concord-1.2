# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-189 — Provenance Audit Framework and Gate Criteria

## Purpose
E8-188 closed structural first-pass extraction at a provisional ledger of 1205 usable records plus 38 quarantined records. The next phase is not normalisation. It is an independent audit of whether the occurrence ledger satisfies the frozen E8-03 and E8-05 requirements.

## Gate criteria
Each audit tranche tests:
1. **ID integrity** — IDs exist once and sequence as declared.
2. **locator integrity** — ang/composition/source-family resolution matches the declared extraction resolution.
3. **contributor integrity** — attribution is preserved where the source is multi-author.
4. **normative validity** — P/V/C/D/N/X classification remains defensible.
5. **scope validity** — G/S/F/C/R/M/E/K scope is not silently expanded.
6. **theology-to-ethics control** — no theological claim is converted into civil ethics without a separable functional proposition.
7. **repetition control** — recurrence is evidence of emphasis, not automatic multiplicity.
8. **quarantine integrity** — excluded records remain excluded.
9. **translation-sensitive flagging** — terms such as hukam, nām, haumai, sevā, sangat, sach/sat, santokh, dayā and dharam remain protected where material.

## Audit states
- **PASS** — adequate for E8 internal normalisation.
- **PASS WITH DEBT** — usable only after a specifically identified correction/verification.
- **HOLD** — insufficient provenance to enter normalisation.
- **QUARANTINE** — excluded from usable ledger.

## Critical rule
A summary ledger is never treated as proof of its own count. Counts must be reconstructed from the occurrence files.
