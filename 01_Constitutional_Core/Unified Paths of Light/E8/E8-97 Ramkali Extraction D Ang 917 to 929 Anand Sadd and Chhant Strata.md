# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-97 — Ramkali Extraction D: Ang 917–929, Anand, Sadd and Chhant Strata

## Coverage
This block preserves three composition families rather than blending them:
- **Anand**, Guru Amar Das — ang 917–922
- **Sadd**, Baba Sundar — ang 923–924
- **Chhant**, Guru Arjan — ang 924–929

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| RK027 | Ang 917–919, Anand | C/E | Hearing becomes developmentally meaningful when internalised and integrated into practice. |
| RK028 | Ang 917–922, Anand | D/S | External or merely verbal participation is distinguished from realised transformation. |
| RK029 | Ang 919–922, Anand | V/S | Disciplined receptivity and steadiness are represented as cultivable. |
| RK030 | Ang 923–924, Sadd | D/S | Mortality and bereavement are treated as conditions requiring orientation beyond possession and bodily permanence. |
| RK031 | Ang 923–924, Sadd | C/C | Death and loss occur within relational communities and affect social continuity as well as the individual. |
| RK032 | Ang 923–924, Sadd | D/G | Status does not exempt an agent from mortality or make embodied standing permanent. |
| RK033 | Ang 924–927, Chhant | D/F | Bride/relationship imagery is retained as metaphor unless a separable behavioural rule is explicit. |
| RK034 | Ang 924–929, Chhant | C/S | Attachment and separation imagery is used to diagnose unstable orientation. |
| RK035 | Ang 924–929, Chhant | V/S | Fidelity and steadiness of orientation are positively valued. |

## Methodological observation
Sadd is a useful domain shift: mortality is not merely inward contemplation but is represented within succession, relationship and communal continuity. This is recorded without prematurely deriving a general continuity institution.
