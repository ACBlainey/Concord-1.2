## E8-26 — Sri Raag Boundary Correction and Provenance Control

## Purpose
A structural boundary error was discovered before normalisation.

The canonical rāga index places:
- **Sri Raag: ang 14–93**
- **Maajh: ang 94–150**
- **Gauree: ang 151–346**

Therefore E8-23–25 incorrectly continued to label post-ang-93 synthesis records as Sri Raag.

## Correction
The following distinction is now mandatory:

### Retained Sri Raag first-pass records
SR001–SR093 remain within the declared Sri Raag boundary, subject to later record-by-record provenance audit.

### Quarantined records
SR094–SR124 are **quarantined as mis-bounded first-pass syntheses**. They are not deleted, renumbered into Maajh, or used as evidence.

Reason: relabelling them after the fact would falsely imply that they had been derived through a properly declared Maajh extraction. Maajh must instead be extracted afresh from ang 94.
