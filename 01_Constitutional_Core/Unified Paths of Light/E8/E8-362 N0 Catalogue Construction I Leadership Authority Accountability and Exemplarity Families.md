# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-362 — N0 Catalogue Construction I: Leadership, Authority, Accountability and Exemplarity Families

## Stage control
E8-only N0 construction.

## Overcomplete functional families
Provisional families include:
1. authority constrained by ethical conduct;
2. rejection of status as sufficient evidence of moral worth;
3. accountability of socially elevated actors;
4. exemplary conduct as a source of formative influence;
5. resistance to self-serving authority;
6. humility in positions of influence;
7. truthful rather than deceptive exercise of influence;
8. responsibility for effects on collective formation;
9. distinction between legitimate guidance and status display;
10. resistance to prestige capture;
11. leadership through realised conduct rather than title alone;
12. evaluation of authority by function and conduct rather than inherited standing.

## Boundary controls
These functions do not establish a specific governmental form. Spiritual or pedagogical guidance is not silently equated with state authority. Exemplarity does not create an unrestricted entitlement to rule.

## N0 result
The source supports an authority-accountability family, but institutional form remains underdetermined. This distinction is preserved for later architecture separation.
