# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-56 — Wadhans Extraction A: Ang 557–575

## Coverage
Fresh sequential extraction from the canonical Wadhans opening at ang 557 through the early chhant strata to ang 575.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| WD001 | Ang 557–558, Guru Nanak | D/S | Human possession and embodied life are treated as temporary rather than secure grounds for self-magnification. |
| WD002 | Ang 558–560, Guru Amar Das | C/E | Reliable guidance and disciplined practice are represented as capable of transforming orientation. |
| WD003 | Ang 560–562, Guru Ram Das | V/S | Humility and receptive attention are positively valued. |
| WD004 | Ang 562–564, Guru Arjan | C/S | Egoic attachment can destabilise judgement and practice. |
| WD005 | Ang 564–565, ashtapadi | D/E | Learning and practice are distinguished from mere formal repetition. |
| WD006 | Ang 565–567, Guru Nanak chhant | D/F | Bridal/family imagery is context-sensitive; metaphor is not automatically converted into literal intimate-relationship rules. |
| WD007 | Ang 565–569 | V/S | Steadiness/fidelity of orientation is positively contrasted with distraction. |
| WD008 | Ang 569–572, Guru Amar Das chhant | C/E | Development is represented as continuing rather than completed by affiliation or ceremony. |
| WD009 | Ang 572–575, Guru Ram Das chhant | C/C | Association and shared practice shape realised disposition. |
| WD010 | Ang 572–575 | D/S | Outward ornament or ceremonial form remains subordinate to substantive orientation. |

## Structural note
Wadhans contains marriage/wedding forms. The coding manual's metaphor control is therefore especially important in this rāga.
