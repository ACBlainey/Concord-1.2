# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-04 — Prospective Prediction Registration

## Timing
These predictions are registered **after X1–X7 was frozen and before E8 ethical extraction begins**.

They may not be edited to fit the Sikh result.

## P-E8-1 — Functional recovery
After independent Sikh extraction, normalisation and internal freeze, the resulting architecture is predicted to substantively represent X1–X7.

Not predicted:
- exactly seven Sikh roots;
- identical labels;
- identical partitioning;
- equal emphasis;
- identical theological justification;
- absence of additional roots.

## P-E8-2 — Root-count flexibility
Functional topology is predicted to be more stable than the number or wording of independently derived root labels.

A result with fewer, equal or more Sikh roots is admissible.

## P-E8-3 — Distinctive decomposition above root level
Sikh-specific emphases are predicted to remain especially visible in operators, conditions, virtues, relational practices and domain rules rather than necessarily requiring new deepest roots.

## P-E8-4 — Inward/outward coupling
Because the primary corpus strongly integrates self-cultivation and relation to others, E8 is predicted to generate reflexive ethical applications in which regulation of self affects outward conduct.

This is not a prediction that inward spirituality is reducible to ethics.

## P-E8-5 — Power/status anti-capture density
E8 is predicted to contain significant ethical pressure against status, domination, empty ritual or socially inherited superiority. Whether this becomes a root is deliberately left open.

## P-E8-6 — Generative architecture
E8 is predicted to permit at least partial decomposition of:
deep constraints + application processes + recurrent conditions → domain rules.

Failure to reproduce the exact R+O+C→D architecture remains admissible.

## Scoring discipline
P-E8-1 will be scored R/A/Ø root-by-root only after the Sikh kernel freeze.

P-E8-2–6 will be evaluated against the frozen E8 derivation record without post-hoc redefinition.
