# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-360 — N0 Catalogue Construction G: Work, Contribution, Reciprocity and Social-Participation Families

## Stage control
E8-only N0 construction.

## Overcomplete functional families
Provisional families include:
1. productive contribution rather than passive extraction;
2. honest conduct in material activity;
3. contribution to shared life;
4. sharing from available provision;
5. reciprocity between personal sustenance and collective participation;
6. rejection of exploitative acquisition;
7. participation without status-based exclusion;
8. contribution as a practical expression of orientation;
9. social embeddedness of individual development;
10. collective nourishment and support;
11. restraint of accumulation driven by greed;
12. material activity constrained by ethical conduct.

## Boundary controls
The corpus is not converted into a modern labour code, tax system, market design or welfare model. Productive contribution is not used to deny dignity to persons unable to work. Reciprocity is not reduced to transactional exchange.

## N0 result
Work/material contribution and collective participation overlap strongly but remain distinguishable from generosity, service and general beneficence at N0.
