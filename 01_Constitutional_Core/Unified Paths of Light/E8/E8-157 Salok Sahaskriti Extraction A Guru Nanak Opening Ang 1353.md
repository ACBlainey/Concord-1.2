# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-157 — Salok Sahaskriti Extraction A: Guru Nanak Opening, Ang 1353

## Coverage
Direct-source extraction from the Guru Nanak Salok Sahaskriti material beginning on ang 1353.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| SS001 | Ang 1353, Guru Nanak | D/E | Reading books, learned debate and formal intellectual activity are insufficient where understanding does not transform orientation. |
| SS002 | Ang 1353 | D/S | Ascetic posture, ritual display and external religious markers are insufficient evidence of substantive development. |
| SS003 | Ang 1353 | D/G | Socially recognisable religious performance should not substitute for examination of underlying conduct and understanding. |
| SS004 | Ang 1353 | C/E | Knowledge claims are evaluated by realised understanding rather than textual possession alone. |
| SS005 | Ang 1353 | D/S | False speech can coexist with impressive outward presentation; appearance therefore remains an unreliable proxy for integrity. |
| SS006 | Ang 1353 | C/E | Distinct social roles and traditions may employ different vocabularies while still being subject to deeper evaluative questions. |

## Direct-source control
This block is unusually explicit about the insufficiency of:
- book learning by itself;
- debate by itself;
- ritual display;
- ascetic performance;
- outward markers where false speech or unrealised understanding persists.

## Interpretive restraint
The source is not coded as rejecting literacy, study or ritual categorically. The functional criticism is directed at **substitution**: form or learning standing in for realised understanding and conduct.
