# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-365 — N1 Duplicate Merge B: Self-Regulation, Agency and Resilience

## Merge decisions
N0 self-regulation, agency and conflict overlaps reduce to:
1. **Ego/status self-restraint** — merges egoic self-centering, pride and inward status-seeking.
2. **Contentment and acquisitive restraint** — merges stabilising contentment with closely duplicated craving/excess formulations while retaining material applications for later scope analysis.
3. **Fear/attachment regulation** — merges repeated formulations where fear or attachment destabilises judgement/action.
4. **Equanimity and steadiness** — merges praise/blame, gain/loss and adversity steadiness.
5. **Impulse/anger restraint** — retains reactive-control function.
6. **Resilience and renewed agency** — merges recovery/reorientation after apparent exhaustion or diminished capacity.
7. **Development through disciplined practice** — merges repeated formation/practice formulations.
8. **Inward-outward integrity** — merges occurrences requiring internal orientation to be realised in conduct.

## Non-merges
Fear regulation is not identical to courage. Contentment is not merged with passivity. Resilience is not merged with general correctability where the latter concerns error rather than adversity.
