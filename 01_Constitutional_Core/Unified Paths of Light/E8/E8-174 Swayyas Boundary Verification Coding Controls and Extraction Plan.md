## E8-174 — Swayyas Boundary Verification, Coding Controls and Extraction Plan

## Structural boundary
The Swayya corpus occupies the closing section beginning at ang 1385 and extending through ang 1409. The next major closing stratum begins after this section.

## Internal traversal
The working structural index divides the material into:
1. Guru Arjan's Swayyas — 1385–1389;
2. Bhatt Swayyas concerning Guru Nanak — 1389–1390;
3. Guru Angad — 1391–1392;
4. Guru Amar Das — 1392–1396;
5. Guru Ram Das — 1396–1406;
6. Guru Arjan — 1406–1409.

## Special coding control: praise poetry
Praise or superlative status language is **not** automatically an ethical proposition.

Admit only where a separable behavioural function is present, for example:
- humility rather than rank;
- realised conduct rather than title;
- teaching/development;
- service or beneficence;
- correction or restraint;
- explicit non-exploitation or status criticism.

Exclude mere claims of spiritual greatness, cosmic authority, lineage superiority or devotional supremacy.
