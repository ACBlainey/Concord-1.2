# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-02 — Sikh Primary Corpus Boundary

## Purpose
Freeze the E8 search space before ethical extraction.

## Primary derivation corpus

### 1. Guru Granth Sahib — structural primary corpus
The Guru Granth Sahib is the central Sikh scripture and is treated as the primary E8 derivation source.

The SGPC describes the ordinary edition as 1,430 pages and gives the broad structure:
- Japji — pp. 1–7;
- musical hymns — pp. 8–1351;
- Salok Sanskriti — pp. 1352–1359;
- Gatha — pp. 1359–1361;
- Funhe — pp. 1361–1362;
- Chaubole — pp. 1363–1364;
- Saloks of Kabir and Farid — pp. 1364–1384;
- Swayyas — pp. 1384–1408;
- Saloks of the Gurus — pp. 1409–1428;
- Rag Mala — pp. 1429–1430.

The musical body uses 31 rāgas and contains compositions by the Sikh Gurus and non-Guru contributors admitted into the canon.

### 2. Primary extraction strata
To prevent selective retrieval, E8 will cover:

**A. Opening / high-density bani**
- Japji Sahib in full;
- structurally distinct opening compositions encountered before the main rāga body, recorded by stable ang/page and composition identity.

**B. Rāga body**
- predefined systematic coverage across the 31-rāga body rather than topic search;
- extraction at shabad/pauri/salok or stable passage-family resolution;
- contributor identity retained where relevant.

**C. Closing non-rāga / salok strata**
- Salok Sanskriti;
- Gatha;
- Funhe;
- Chaubole;
- Saloks of Kabir and Farid;
- Swayyas;
- Saloks of the Gurus.

**D. Rag Mala**
Retained as canonical boundary material but presumed non-ethical unless a separable behavioural proposition is actually supported. No ethical yield is required.

## Withheld validation / extension corpora
These are excluded from primary derivation and may not be opened to rescue a weak X correspondence:
- Sikh Rehat Maryada;
- Dasam Granth;
- Bhai Gurdas Varan;
- Janamsakhi traditions;
- historical Rahitnamas;
- later Sikh theological/commentarial literature;
- modern Sikh ethics summaries.

They may later be used for validation, institutional decompression or historical comparison only after the E8 internal kernel is frozen.

## Boundary rationale
The primary corpus is deliberately scripture-first. This avoids allowing later institutional codes to predetermine the deepest ethical architecture.

## Translation/source control
Primary locators will use canonical **ang** numbering. Gurmukhi is authoritative source text; English renderings are aids to interpretation, not substitutes for source identity. Translation-sensitive terms must remain visible.
