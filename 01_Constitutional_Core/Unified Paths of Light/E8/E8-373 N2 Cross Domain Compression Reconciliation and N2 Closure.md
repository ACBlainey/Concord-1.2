# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-373 — N2 Cross-Domain Compression Reconciliation and N2 Closure

## Purpose
Reconcile the N2 parent candidates without forcing a target number of roots.

## Higher-order E8-internal parents
The 31 N1 working families now organise primarily beneath:
1. **Reliable orientation** — truth, discernment, reflection, epistemic humility and resistance to distortion.
2. **Developmental correctability** — correction, formation, finite-opportunity action and recovery of agency.
3. **Self-regulated agency** — restraint of ego, fear, attachment, reactive impulse and destabilising excess.
4. **Outward beneficence** — compassion expressed through assistance, service, generosity and sharing.
5. **Responsible material relation** — honest acquisition/use, anti-exploitation, restraint and reciprocal contribution.
6. **Non-domination and accountable power** — status non-superiority, anti-domination, accountability, anti-capture and realised exemplarity.
7. **Constructive collective participation** — inclusion, beneficial association and community as a formative environment.

## Cross-cutting bridge
**Inward-outward integrity** remains a cross-cutting constraint rather than being forced beneath one parent. It tests whether claimed orientation is realised in conduct across several parents.

## Compression result
N2 therefore yields **seven higher-order parent families plus one cross-cutting integrity constraint**.

This structure is an E8-internal result only. The number seven is not treated as significant, predictive or confirmatory, and no comparison with any withheld architecture is permitted at this stage.

## Stability question opened
The next stage, N3, must determine whether these parents represent:
- genuine co-level architectural components;
- layers of a generative hierarchy;
- partially overlapping dimensions;
- or parents that can still be compressed without functional loss.

N3 must also decide the architectural role of inward-outward integrity.
