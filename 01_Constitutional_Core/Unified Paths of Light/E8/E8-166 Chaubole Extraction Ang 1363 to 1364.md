# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-166 — Chaubole Extraction: Ang 1363–1364

## Coverage
Chaubole, attributed to Guru Arjan, contains 11 short verses across ang 1363–1364. Its compact form permits tighter source anchoring than the large rāga-range syntheses.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| CB001 | Chaubole 1, ang 1363 | D/M | A highest-valued relational commitment is represented as not purchasable by wealth. |
| CB002 | Chaubole 1 | D/M | Material abundance is therefore not treated as sufficient to acquire every form of value. |
| CB003 | Chaubole 2, ang 1363 | C/S | Deep orientation is represented as internalised rather than merely externally displayed. |
| CB004 | Chaubole, ang 1364 | D/S | Worldly distance, effort or impressive action does not automatically establish substantive orientation. |
| CB005 | Chaubole 5, ang 1364 | D/G | Prestige, ascetic accomplishment and worldly comfort are not sufficient measures of deepest worth. |
| CB006 | Chaubole 5 | V/S | Humility is positively contrasted with pride and self-magnification. |
| CB007 | Chaubole late verses, ang 1364 | C/S | Fickleness and pride can persist despite fame, wealth or outward achievement. |
| CB008 | Chaubole late verses | D/S | External adornment and attractive presentation remain distinguishable from developed internal qualities. |
| CB009 | Chaubole terminal verse | V/S | The speaker's self-positioning is explicitly lowly rather than status-maximising. |

## Interpretive restraint
The composition's central object is devotional love. It is not converted wholesale into a secular ethical doctrine. Only separable functional propositions are retained.

## Boundary
Kabir saloks begin on ang 1364 after Chaubole.
