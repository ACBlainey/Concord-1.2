# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-99 — Ramkali Extraction F: Ang 938–946, Siddha Gosh

## Coverage
Siddha Gosh is preserved as a dialogue with Siddhas rather than coded as a collection of isolated aphorisms. The question-answer form is methodologically significant because positions are tested through challenge and response.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| RK045 | Ang 938–940 | D/E | Claims about the right path are subjected to questioning rather than treated as self-validating assertions. |
| RK046 | Ang 938–941 | V/E | Inquiry and responsive dialogue are represented as legitimate means of examining competing orientations. |
| RK047 | Ang 940–942 | D/S | Withdrawal, appearance or ascetic identity alone is insufficient evidence of developed judgement. |
| RK048 | Ang 941–943 | C/S | Ego and attachment can persist within disciplined or renunciant practice. |
| RK049 | Ang 942–944 | C/E | Understanding is represented as requiring integration into lived orientation rather than merely winning an argument. |
| RK050 | Ang 943–945 | D/G | Role, school or group identity does not make a claim correct merely by belonging. |
| RK051 | Ang 944–946 | C/S | Self-regulation is represented as an inward developmental achievement rather than domination of others. |
| RK052 | Ang 944–946 | D/E | The capacity to answer challenge without abandoning substantive orientation functions as a test of developed understanding. |

## Methodological observation
This composition supplies unusually clear dialogical epistemic structure: question, answer, challenge and clarification. It may later bear on operators of inquiry/correction, but no operator is frozen during extraction.
