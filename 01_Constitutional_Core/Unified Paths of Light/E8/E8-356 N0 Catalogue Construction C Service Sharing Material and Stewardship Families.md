# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-356 — N0 Catalogue Construction C: Service, Sharing, Material and Stewardship Families

## Stage control
E8-only N0 construction.

## Overcomplete functional families
Provisional families include:
1. service directed beyond narrow self-interest;
2. practical assistance to others;
3. generosity and giving;
4. sharing of provision;
5. common participation in nourishment/resources;
6. honest or non-exploitative material conduct;
7. restraint of greed and acquisitive excess;
8. material sufficiency/contentment;
9. responsible use rather than possessive fixation;
10. outward beneficence extending beyond the isolated individual;
11. relief of suffering or distress;
12. reciprocity and contribution within collective life.

## Boundary controls
Shared-food imagery is not inflated into a complete economic system. Giving is not assumed to erase property distinctions. Material restraint is not treated as a rejection of productive activity. Service retains agency and does not imply coerced self-sacrifice.

## N0 result
The material/service domain contains separable giving, sharing, restraint, contribution and beneficence functions. Their later relationship remains open.
