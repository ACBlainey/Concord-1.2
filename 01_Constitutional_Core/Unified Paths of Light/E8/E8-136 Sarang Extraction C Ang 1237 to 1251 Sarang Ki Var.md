# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-136 — Sarang Extraction C: Ang 1237–1251, Sarang Ki Var

## Coverage
Sarang Ki Var begins at ang 1237 and extends through ang 1251. The indexed contributor set includes Guru Ram Das with saloks/contributions from Guru Angad, Guru Nanak, Guru Amar Das and Guru Arjan. The Var contains 36 pauris and is preserved as a distinct collective/social stratum.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| SG017 | Ang 1237–1240 | D/R | Social or religious authority remains subject to substantive evaluation rather than validating itself through title. |
| SG018 | Ang 1239–1242 | D/G | Convention and collective repetition do not transform defective conduct into valid conduct. |
| SG019 | Ang 1241–1244 | C/C | Social environments can preserve and transmit both constructive and defective orientations. |
| SG020 | Ang 1243–1246 | D/S | Conduct remains consequential despite affiliation or ceremonial standing. |
| SG021 | Ang 1245–1248 | C/E | Correction remains possible where guidance is received and practice changes. |
| SG022 | Ang 1247–1249 | V/G | Humility is positively contrasted with self-magnifying rank or prestige. |
| SG023 | Ang 1248–1251 | D/R | Entrusted or recognised standing is evaluated by realised function and conduct rather than office alone. |
| SG024 | Ang 1249–1251 | C/C | Cross-contributor recurrence within the Var is evidence of emphasis rather than principle multiplicity. |

## Interpretive restraint
The Var again increases authority-facing density, but it does not by itself establish a complete political theory or independent power root.
