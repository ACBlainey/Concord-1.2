## Purpose
Open the first normalisation stage authorised by E8-352 without contaminating the prospective holdout.

## Frozen N0 input
N0 receives:
- **1070 admissible/pass-with-debt occurrence records**.

N0 does not receive:
- **135 HOLD records**;
- **38 quarantined records**;
- X1–X7;
- E3/E4/E5/E6/E7 architectures;
- Blainey’s Laws;
- Decalogue comparison results;
- prior invariant-topology conclusions.

HOLD records remain available only as provenance debt for later resolution. They may not be used to rescue, enlarge or complete an emerging Sikh architecture.

## N0 objective
Construct an intentionally overcomplete **E8-internal functional catalogue** from the admissible occurrence set.

N0 is not a root-kernel extraction. It is designed to preserve distinctions before compression.

## Permitted operations
N0 may:
- group semantically close E8 occurrences;
- assign neutral E8-internal working labels;
- retain subfamilies where compression would erase functional distinctions;
- record recurrence/density;
- preserve contributor and source-distribution information;
- mark translation-sensitive clusters;
- identify uncertain boundaries between neighbouring internal families.

## Prohibited operations
N0 may not:
- target a predetermined number of principles;
- use X1–X7 vocabulary as a coding template;
- import prior tradition labels;
- merge families merely because they would correspond to a known external root;
- infer absence because an expected external category is not obvious;
- score the Sikh corpus against any withheld architecture.

## Catalogue-unit rule
An N0 catalogue unit must be supported by one or more admissible E8 occurrences and express a distinguishable functional proposition.

Where two candidate units differ only by wording, they may be grouped. Where they differ in behavioural function, agency relation, epistemic role, power relation, developmental function or resource relation, the distinction is initially preserved.

## Debt propagation
Every N0 family inherits relevant provenance debt from its supporting records. Normalisation cannot convert a pass-with-debt source into publication-grade provenance.
