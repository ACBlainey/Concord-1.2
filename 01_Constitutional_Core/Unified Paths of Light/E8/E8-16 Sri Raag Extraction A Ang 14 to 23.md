# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-16 — Sri Raag Extraction A: Ang 14–23

## Coverage
Canonical Sri Raag opening, sequentially from ang 14 through ang 23. Principal contributor in this block: Guru Nanak. Theological imagery is retained where behavioural generalisation is unsafe.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| SR001 | Ang 14 | D/S,R | Luxury, military power, throne, command and taxation are not reliable measures of enduring worth. |
| SR002 | Ang 14 | C/S | Wealth, sensual attraction, extraordinary capability and political power can distort orientation when converted into self-magnification. |
| SR003 | Ang 15 | P/G | Greed, falsehood and cheating are negatively classified. |
| SR004 | Ang 15 | P/G | Slander is negatively classified as conduct that degrades the speaker as well as the target. |
| SR005 | Ang 15 | V/S | Unregulated anger is treated as internally destructive rather than a neutral source of authority. |
| SR006 | Ang 15 | D/G | Speech should be evaluated by whether it preserves integrity/honour rather than merely by rhetorical force. |
| SR007 | Ang 16–18 | D/S | External appearance and ritual display do not substitute for substantive inward orientation. |
| SR008 | Ang 18 | C/S | Attachment to an alternative object of loyalty is represented as destabilising; civil relationship rules are not inferred from bridal metaphor. |
| SR009 | Ang 19–20 | C/E | Reliable guidance and association are represented as altering understanding and conduct over time. |
| SR010 | Ang 20–21 | V/S | Self-centred orientation is repeatedly contrasted with disciplined receptivity to correction. |
| SR011 | Ang 22 | D/S | Apply a standard of truth to one's own mind rather than exempting the evaluator from assessment. |
| SR012 | Ang 22 | C/S | Travel or outward change does not by itself remove inward confusion or corruption. |
| SR013 | Ang 22 | D/M | Commercial metaphor distinguishes goods by whether their value survives the relevant horizon; accumulation alone is not treated as value. |
| SR014 | Ang 23 | D/S | Unity imagery spans opposed roles and perspectives; metaphysical unity is not automatically converted into social policy. |

## Internal themes
Status/power scepticism; anti-deception; speech ethics; anger/self-regulation; self-audit; guidance; value beyond accumulation.
