# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-358 — N0 Catalogue Construction E: Compassion, Non-Harm, Relation and Collective-Formation Families

## Stage control
E8-only N0 construction.

## Overcomplete functional families
Provisional families include:
1. compassion toward suffering;
2. active relief of distress;
3. restraint from harmful or cruel conduct;
4. benevolence and goodwill;
5. reduced hostility and enmity;
6. forgiveness or non-retaliatory restraint where functionally supported;
7. relational reliability and good conduct toward others;
8. formative association with constructive company;
9. collective participation as support for development;
10. mutual reinforcement of beneficial orientation;
11. outward concern extending beyond isolated self-interest;
12. community as a developmental environment rather than mere aggregation.

## Boundary controls
Compassion is not treated as unlimited acquiescence to harmful behaviour. Non-hostility is not automatically pacifism. Formative association is not coded as compulsory conformity. Collective participation does not erase individual agency.

## N0 result
The corpus presents relational ethics and formative collective life as overlapping but distinguishable functions. N0 preserves both.
