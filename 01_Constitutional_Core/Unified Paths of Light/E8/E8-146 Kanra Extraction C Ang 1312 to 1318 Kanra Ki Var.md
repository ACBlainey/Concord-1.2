# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-146 — Kanra Extraction C: Ang 1312–1318, Kanra Ki Var

## Coverage
Kanra Ki Var is preserved as a distinct social/collective stratum rather than blended into the preceding hymns.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| KN015 | Ang 1312–1314 | D/R | Social or religious standing remains subject to substantive evaluation rather than validating itself through title. |
| KN016 | Ang 1313–1315 | D/G | Convention and collective repetition do not transform defective conduct into valid conduct. |
| KN017 | Ang 1314–1316 | C/C | Social environments can preserve and transmit both constructive and defective orientations. |
| KN018 | Ang 1315–1316 | D/S | Conduct remains consequential despite affiliation or ceremonial standing. |
| KN019 | Ang 1316–1317 | C/E | Correction remains possible where guidance is received and practice changes. |
| KN020 | Ang 1317–1318 | V/G | Humility is positively contrasted with self-magnifying rank or prestige. |
| KN021 | Ang 1317–1318 | D/R | Entrusted or recognised standing is evaluated by realised function and conduct rather than office alone. |

## Interpretive restraint
As with earlier Vars, authority-facing density is recorded without promoting power or office to a deepest ethical root during extraction.
