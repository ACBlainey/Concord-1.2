# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-367 — N1 Duplicate Merge D: Status, Power, Authority and Collective Formation

## Merge decisions
N0 status, authority and collective families reduce to:
1. **Non-superiority by status** — merges rank, wealth, prestige and inherited-standing critiques where moral worth is not established by elevation.
2. **Anti-domination / anti-exploitation** — merges outward power-abuse and predatory-control functions.
3. **Authority accountability** — merges responsibility and ethical constraint attached to influence/authority.
4. **Exemplarity through realised conduct** — merges guidance-by-conduct and title-insufficiency formulations.
5. **Resistance to prestige capture** — merges praise/reputation/status-control functions where the central issue is capture of authority rather than personal judgement.
6. **Inclusive participation** — merges participation/non-exclusion functions.
7. **Constructive association** — merges beneficial-company and mutual formative-influence occurrences.
8. **Collective developmental environment** — retains community-level formation where not reducible to individual association.

## Non-merges
Spiritual equality is not expanded into a full political system. Authority accountability is not identical to anti-domination. Constructive association is not identical to compulsory conformity.
