# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-371 — N2 Parent-Child Compression C: Compassion, Service, Material Reciprocity and Beneficence

## Parent candidate: outward beneficence
The following N1 families form a higher-order outward-directed structure:
- compassionate concern;
- active relief/assistance;
- service beyond narrow self-interest;
- generosity and sharing.

These compress under **outward beneficence**, with compassion as disposition and relief/service/sharing as differing action forms.

## Parent candidate: responsible material relation
The following form a related but distinct structure:
- honest/non-exploitative material conduct;
- restraint of greed/acquisitive excess;
- responsible material use/sufficiency;
- reciprocal contribution.

These compress under **responsible material relation**, governing acquisition, use, restraint and contribution.

## Boundary
Outward beneficence is not equivalent to compulsory sacrifice. Responsible material relation does not specify a property regime or economic system.

The two parents remain separate because helping others and regulating one's relation to material resources are functionally distinguishable even where they overlap.
