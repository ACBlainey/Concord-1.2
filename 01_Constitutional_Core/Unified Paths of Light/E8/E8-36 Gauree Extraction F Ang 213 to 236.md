# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-36 — Gauree Extraction F: Ang 213–236

## Coverage
Sequential Gauree coverage from ang 213 through ang 236. This block crosses several Gauree sub-forms while remaining inside the same canonical rāga family.

## Source controls
Direct source results at ang 213–220 confirm continued Gauree attribution and support the themes of transience of property, craving, equanimity, developmental opportunity and compassion-oriented devotional framing.

No civil property rule is inferred merely from impermanence.
