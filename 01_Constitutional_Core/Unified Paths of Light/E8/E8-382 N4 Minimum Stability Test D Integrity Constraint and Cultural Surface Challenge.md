# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-382 — N4 Minimum-Stability Test D: Integrity Constraint and Cultural-Surface Challenge

## Integrity removal
Remove inward-outward integrity while retaining all seven parents.

The parent architecture can still state orientations and functions, but loses the cross-layer test that distinguishes professed orientation from realised conduct.

Because the E8 corpus repeatedly privileges realised conduct over title, declaration or nominal understanding, this loss is substantive.

**Result: integrity constraint required.**

## Root-versus-surface challenge
Each parent is tested for whether it is merely a Sikh-specific surface expression.

- Reliable orientation is broader than any single devotional vocabulary.
- Developmental correctability is broader than any particular practice.
- Self-regulated agency is broader than specific terms for ego, attachment or remembrance.
- Outward beneficence is broader than any one service practice.
- Responsible material relation is broader than a specific sharing custom.
- Non-domination/accountable power is broader than particular status critiques.
- Constructive collective participation is broader than a named congregation or community form.

The source-specific forms remain important provenance, but the parent functions survive abstraction from those forms.

## Result
No parent is eliminated as merely a culturally specific surface label. The integrity constraint also survives removal testing.
