# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-145 — Kanra Extraction B: Ang 1301–1312, Guru Arjan, Ashtapadi and Chhant Strata

## Coverage
Sequential first-pass traversal through the remainder of the large Guru Arjan body, Guru Ram Das ashtapadis and the Guru Arjan Chhant.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| KN008 | Ang 1301–1304 | D/S | Public reputation and formal practice remain insufficient evidence of substantive condition. |
| KN009 | Ang 1303–1306 | C/S | Pride can appropriate constructive practice into self-display. |
| KN010 | Ang 1305–1308 | V/S | Contentment and steadiness are represented as cultivated stabilising qualities. |
| KN011 | Ang 1308–1310, Ashtapadis | D/E | Learning remains corrigible rather than completed by affiliation or recitation. |
| KN012 | Ang 1308–1312, Ashtapadis | C/E | Development requires sustained practice and realised understanding rather than nominal learning. |
| KN013 | Ang 1311–1312 | C/C | Shared practice can preserve and transmit developed orientation. |
| KN014 | Ang 1312, Chhant | D/F | Relational imagery is retained as metaphor unless a separable behavioural rule is explicit. |

## Transition control
Kanra Ki Var begins at ang 1312 and is extracted separately because its collective register differs from the preceding hymn strata.
