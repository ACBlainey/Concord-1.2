# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-94 — Ramkali Extraction A: Ang 876–882, Early Guru Stratum

## Coverage
Sequential extraction from the directly verified Ramkali opening through the Guru Nanak, Guru Amar Das and Guru Ram Das opening strata.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| RK001 | Ang 876, Guru Nanak | D/E | Reading multiple scriptures, ritual repetition and meditative technique are not treated as sufficient for complete understanding. |
| RK002 | Ang 876 | D/S | The speaker explicitly acknowledges limited knowledge rather than converting religious practice into certainty. |
| RK003 | Ang 876 | C/S | Desire for continued life and attachment to worldly condition are represented as capable of trapping judgement. |
| RK004 | Ang 876–879, Guru Nanak | D/S | External practice remains subordinate to substantive transformation and orientation. |
| RK005 | Ang 876–879 | C/E | Understanding is represented as requiring more than accumulated forms of learning. |
| RK006 | Ang 880, Guru Amar Das | C/E | Guidance and realised practice are represented as formative rather than merely declarative. |
| RK007 | Ang 880–882, Guru Ram Das | V/S | Humility and receptivity support continuing correction. |
| RK008 | Ang 880–882 | C/C | Association and shared practice can reinforce developed orientation. |
| RK009 | Ang 880–882 | D/G | Formal standing remains distinguishable from substantive conduct. |

## Direct-source note
RK001–RK003 are closely anchored to the opening of ang 876. The text explicitly juxtaposes scripture-reading, rosary/meditation and admitted lack of complete knowledge.

## Interpretive restraint
This is not coded as rejection of learning. The source criticises sufficiency claims attached to forms of learning/practice.
