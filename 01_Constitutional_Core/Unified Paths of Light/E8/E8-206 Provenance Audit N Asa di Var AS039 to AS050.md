## Structural verification
Asa di Var is independently placed at ang 462–475 and consists of 24 pauris with saloks interspersed. Source descriptions identify Guru Nanak as the principal pauri author, with Guru Angad saloks also incorporated.

This confirms E8-45's reason for treating the section as a distinct contributor-sensitive stratum.

## Provenance problem
The first-pass records are broad syntheses such as ang 462–465, 464–467 and 473–475. They do **not** yet specify:
- exact pauri;
- exact salok;
- contributor for the supporting ESU;
- exact wording underlying each social/power proposition.

Because Asa di Var contains multiple contributors and unusually direct claims about rulers, elites, falsehood, exploitation and social norms, contributor/ESU precision is especially important.

## Gate judgement
The records are not shown to be false or structurally misplaced, but the whole block requires exact pauri/salok reconstruction before it should participate in root normalisation.
