# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-13 — Opening Bani Extraction C: Anand Sahib Structural Coverage

## Coverage
Anand Sahib (Guru Amar Das), treated as a connected composition. The extraction records distinct behavioural/developmental functions without multiplying repeated devotional refrains.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| SO021 | Anand Sahib | C/S | Durable transformation is associated with learned guidance and sustained practice rather than momentary declaration. |
| SO022 | Anand Sahib | D/S | Hearing alone is distinguished from internalisation; reception of information is not identical to understanding. |
| SO023 | Anand Sahib | V/S | Disciplined attention is presented as something cultivable. |
| SO024 | Anand Sahib | C/S | Unregulated desire/attachment can distort judgement and orientation. |
| SO025 | Anand Sahib | D/S | Sensory or emotional attraction is not treated as sufficient evidence of what is beneficial. |
| SO026 | Anand Sahib | C/E | Association, instruction and repeated practice shape capability over time. |
| SO027 | Anand Sahib | V/E | Ethical/spiritual maturity is represented as developmental rather than fixed at birth or status. |
| SO028 | Anand Sahib | D/S | Speech about truth is distinguished from embodied truthful orientation. |
| SO029 | Anand Sahib | C/C | Communities of practice can reinforce or degrade individual orientation depending on what they normalise. |
| SO030 | Anand Sahib | V/G | Gratitude and acknowledgement counter possessive self-sufficiency. |
| SO031 | Anand Sahib | D/S | Ritual, sensory display and verbal performance remain subordinate to substantive transformation. |
| SO032 | Anand Sahib | C/S | Corrective guidance may expose self-deception; discomfort alone does not prove correction is harmful. |
| SO033 | Anand Sahib | V/S | Contentment and steadiness are treated as cultivated capacities. |
| SO034 | Anand Sahib | D/E | Development requires conditions, guidance and participation by the learner; it is not represented as purely imposed from outside. |

## Repetition control
Repeated joy/bliss and remembrance formulas are evidence of emphasis. They are not counted as separate ethical principles unless they add a distinct functional proposition.
