# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-359 — N0 Catalogue Construction F: Agency, Responsibility, Correction and Development Families

## Stage control
E8-only N0 construction. External architectures remain firewalled.

## Overcomplete functional families
Provisional families include:
1. responsibility for one's own orientation and conduct;
2. capacity for correction after error;
3. renewed agency after apparent incapacity;
4. present action under finite opportunity;
5. disciplined formation through repeated practice;
6. movement from verbal or nominal understanding toward realised conduct;
7. learning through reflection and discernment;
8. developmental influence of constructive association;
9. resistance to fatalistic treatment of present deficiency as permanent;
10. continuity between inward development and outward responsibility;
11. responsibility for consequences generated by conduct;
12. active rather than merely declarative ethical formation.

## Boundary controls
Agency is not treated as unlimited control over circumstances. Correctability is not a guarantee of successful rehabilitation. Developmental potential does not erase genuine incapacity or external constraint. Repeated practice is retained only where the admitted occurrence carries a separable formative function.

## N0 result
The corpus supports a substantial developmental architecture in which ethical reliability is cultivated rather than merely asserted.
