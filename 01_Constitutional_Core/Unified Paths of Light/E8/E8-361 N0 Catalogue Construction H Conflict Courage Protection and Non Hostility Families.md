# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-361 — N0 Catalogue Construction H: Conflict, Courage, Protection and Non-Hostility Families

## Stage control
E8-only N0 construction.

## Overcomplete functional families
Provisional families include:
1. reduced fear in the face of adversity;
2. courage without egoic aggression;
3. resistance to hostility and enmity;
4. restraint of anger-driven retaliation;
5. steadiness under threat, loss or social pressure;
6. protection of ethical orientation under adverse conditions;
7. non-domination in conflict;
8. refusal to let praise/blame determine action;
9. resilience after defeat or exhaustion;
10. concern for suffering generated by conflict;
11. disciplined rather than impulsive response;
12. preservation of agency under coercive or adverse pressure.

## Boundary controls
Non-hostility is not automatically coded as pacifism. Courage is not glorification of violence. Protective functions are not expanded into a complete doctrine of warfare, policing or punishment without direct occurrence support.

## N0 result
Conflict-related material is primarily organised around fear, hostility, steadiness, restraint and preservation of responsible agency.
