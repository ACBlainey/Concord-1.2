# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-370 — N2 Parent-Child Compression B: Self-Regulation and Stable Agency

## Parent candidate: self-regulated agency
The following N1 families compress as child mechanisms:
- ego/status self-restraint;
- contentment and acquisitive restraint;
- fear/attachment regulation;
- equanimity and steadiness;
- impulse/anger restraint.

Their common higher-order function is preventing internal drives, fears, attachments, status impulses or reactive states from capturing judgement and action.

The parent is provisionally labelled **self-regulated agency**.

## Boundary
This parent does not imply emotional suppression, withdrawal, passivity or elimination of desire. Its functional criterion is whether agency remains capable of responsible orientation rather than being dominated by destabilising internal pressures.

## Related but not absorbed
Resilience and renewed agency remains under developmental correctability because its defining problem is recovery after adversity rather than routine regulation.

Epistemic humility remains under reliable orientation because its defining function is reliability of understanding, despite overlap with ego restraint.
