## E8-108 — Maru Boundary Verification, Internal Strata and Extraction Plan

## Verified structural boundary
Canonical structural indices place:
- **Maru: ang 989–1106**
- **Tukhari begins at ang 1107**

Maru is one of the larger Guru Granth Sahib rāga strata and requires finer internal control.

## Predeclared internal traversal
The first-pass traversal will preserve major composition/contributor transitions rather than treating all 118 angs as homogeneous:
1. opening Guru bani — from ang 989;
2. Guru Nanak and later Guru strata in canonical order;
3. Guru Arjan's large Maru body;
4. Solhe/other marked composition families where indexed;
5. Maru Ki Var;
6. terminal Bhagat Bani before the Tukhari transition.

Exact internal ang boundaries will be verified before each marked transition rather than inferred from memory.

## Extraction resolution
Use ang-range occurrence families for ordinary dense hymn strata, but tighten resolution for:
- explicit social/power material;
- marked dialogue/narrative;
- Var material;
- Bhagat contributor transitions;
- translation-sensitive ethical vocabulary.
