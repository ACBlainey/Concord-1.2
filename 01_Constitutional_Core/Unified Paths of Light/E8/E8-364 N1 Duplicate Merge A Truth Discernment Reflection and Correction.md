# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-364 — N1 Duplicate Merge A: Truth, Discernment, Reflection and Correction

## Stage control
N1 merges only E8-internal duplicates and near-duplicates identified during N0. No external architecture is consulted.

## Merge decisions
The N0 epistemic catalogue is reduced into the following working families:
1. **Truthful orientation and conduct** — merges repeated truth/falsehood and conduct-alignment formulations.
2. **Discernment and realised understanding** — merges appearance/reality, reflective understanding and understanding demonstrated through conduct where no independent function is lost.
3. **Reflective maintenance of orientation** — retains reflection/remembrance as a formative mechanism rather than merging it entirely with truth.
4. **Correctability and reorientation** — merges correction, renewed orientation and non-finality of present deficiency.
5. **Finite-opportunity deliberation** — retains mortality/time limitation as a distinct decision constraint.
6. **Epistemic independence from social distortion** — merges praise/blame/reputation resistance where the operative function is judgement not governed by social approval.
7. **Epistemic humility** — retains limitation of self-certainty/status as a distinct reliability condition.

## Non-merges
Truth is not merged with humility. Correction is not merged with finite-time awareness. Reflection is not treated as identical to realised understanding.
