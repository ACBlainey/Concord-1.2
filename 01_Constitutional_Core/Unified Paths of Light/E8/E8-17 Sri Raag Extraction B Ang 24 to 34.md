# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-17 — Sri Raag Extraction B: Ang 24–34

## Coverage
Sequential Sri Raag coverage ang 24–34.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| SR015 | Ang 24 | C/E | Good conduct is represented as something cultivated repeatedly rather than acquired by declaration. |
| SR016 | Ang 24 | V/G | Truthfulness/integrity is represented as requiring continuing practice. |
| SR017 | Ang 24 | D/M | Wealth/property accumulation is questioned where it cannot serve enduring legitimate purpose. |
| SR018 | Ang 24 | D/R | A religious/judicial title is insufficient without corresponding restraint of selfishness and conceit. |
| SR019 | Ang 24 | D/R | Ritual observance and textual recitation do not by themselves establish ethical legitimacy of office. |
| SR020 | Ang 24 | D/G | Shared awareness across living beings is asserted; the theological/metaphysical claim is not expanded into a complete rights theory. |
| SR021 | Ang 25–27 | C/S | Desire, greed and ego can alter judgement and action. |
| SR022 | Ang 25–27 | V/S | Contentment and disciplined restraint are treated as counter-conditions to acquisitive distortion. |
| SR023 | Ang 27–29 | D/S | Knowledge without lived transformation is repeatedly treated as incomplete. |
| SR024 | Ang 28–30 | C/G | Conduct generates consequences beyond immediate appearance or social recognition. |
| SR025 | Ang 30–31 | D/G | Social/religious identity labels do not substitute for the conduct associated with them. |
| SR026 | Ang 31–32 | V/G | Humility and truthful conduct are preferred to boastful status display. |
| SR027 | Ang 32–34 | C/E | Repeated instruction, association and practice shape realised disposition. |
| SR028 | Ang 32–34 | D/S | Correction requires receptivity; prestige or self-certainty can obstruct learning. |
