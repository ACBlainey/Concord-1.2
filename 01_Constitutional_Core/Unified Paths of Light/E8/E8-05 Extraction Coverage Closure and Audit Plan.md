# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-05 — Extraction Coverage, Closure and Audit Plan

## Problem
The Guru Granth Sahib is large: the SGPC describes 1,430 canonical pages and 5,894 hymns, organised primarily through musical structure rather than an ethics taxonomy.

E8 therefore requires a declared coverage method that is broad enough to resist cherry-picking while honest about extraction resolution.

## Extraction sequence

### Phase 1 — Opening bani
1. Japji Sahib — full structural extraction.
2. Remaining opening compositions before the main rāga body — composition-level coverage.

### Phase 2 — Rāga body
Proceed in canonical order across the 31 rāgas.

For each rāga block:
- inspect the block structurally rather than by expected ethical keyword;
- extract distinct ethically relevant ESUs;
- preserve contributor and ang;
- record repeated families without multiplying identical propositions;
- record no-yield sections where relevant to coverage.

### Phase 3 — Closing strata
Cover the post-rāga material in canonical order:
- Salok Sanskriti;
- Gatha;
- Funhe;
- Chaubole;
- Kabir/Farid saloks;
- Swayyas;
- Guru saloks;
- Rag Mala boundary check.

## Resolution
The target is **shabad/pauri/salok or stable passage-family resolution**, not line-by-line atomisation of all 1,430 ang.

A record may cite a short ang range where a proposition depends on a connected unit.

## Running audit per block
Each extraction block must record:
- canonical range covered;
- contributor/source resolution;
- translation-sensitive terms;
- repeated proposition families;
- context-bound exclusions;
- ambiguous theology/ethics cases;
- marginal new functional information.

## Closure
Primary extraction closes only when every predeclared structural stratum has been covered at its declared resolution.

Permitted closure:
- structural corpus coverage complete;
- declared passage-family coverage complete.

Not permitted:
- “all predicted roots found”;
- “principle saturation” used to skip later canonical strata;
- corpus expansion because a predicted function appears weak.

## Provenance gate before normalisation
Before compression:
1. mechanical occurrence-ledger audit;
2. ang/source-resolution audit;
3. contributor and synthesis/repetition control;
4. translation-sensitive review;
5. whole-corpus provenance gate.

Normalisation is blocked until the gate passes.

## Normalisation sequence
After authorisation:
N0 overcomplete E8-only catalogue → N1 duplicate merge → N2 parent-child compression → N3 architecture separation → N4 minimum-stability → **E8 internal freeze** → only then X scoring.
