# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-135 — Sarang Extraction B: Ang 1213–1237, Guru Arjan, Guru Tegh Bahadur, Ashtapadi and Chhant Strata

## Coverage
Sequential traversal through the large Guru Arjan body, second Partal, Guru Tegh Bahadur, the ashtapadis and Chhant immediately before Sarang Ki Var.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| SG008 | Ang 1213–1217 | D/S | Public reputation and formal practice remain insufficient evidence of substantive condition. |
| SG009 | Ang 1216–1220 | C/S | Pride can appropriate constructive practice into self-display. |
| SG010 | Ang 1219–1223 | V/S | Contentment and steadiness are represented as cultivated stabilising qualities. |
| SG011 | Ang 1222–1226 | D/E | Learning remains corrigible rather than completed by affiliation or recitation. |
| SG012 | Ang 1225–1229 | D/M | Material possession and worldly capability are not sufficient measures of durable value. |
| SG013 | Ang 1229–1231, Partal | C/S | Desire and attachment can reproduce instability despite acquisition or status. |
| SG014 | Ang 1231, Guru Tegh Bahadur | D/S | Mortality and impermanence expose instability in assumed security. |
| SG015 | Ang 1232–1236, Ashtapadis | C/E | Development requires sustained practice and realised understanding rather than nominal learning. |
| SG016 | Ang 1236–1237, Chhant | D/F | Relational imagery is retained as metaphor unless a separable behavioural rule is explicit. |

## Transition control
Sarang Ki Var begins at ang 1237 and is extracted separately because its social and collective register may differ from the preceding hymn strata.
