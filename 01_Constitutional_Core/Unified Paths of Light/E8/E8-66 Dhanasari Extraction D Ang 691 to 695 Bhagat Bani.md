# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-66 — Dhanasari Extraction D: Ang 691–695, Bhagat Bani

## Coverage
Sequential first-pass extraction of the Dhanasari Bhagat stratum. Indexed contributors are Kabir, Namdev, Ravidas, Trilochan, Sain, Pipa and Dhanna.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| DH025 | Ang 691–692, Kabir | D/G | Religious identity or formal practice does not by itself establish substantive standing. |
| DH026 | Ang 691–692 | C/S | Egoic attachment can persist inside apparently disciplined practice. |
| DH027 | Ang 692–694, Namdev | D/G | Inherited or social standing is not sufficient evidence of realised worth. |
| DH028 | Ang 692–694 | C/E | Transformative understanding is distinguished from public affiliation or reputation. |
| DH029 | Ang 694, Ravidas | V/S | Humility and dependence are positively valued against self-magnification. |
| DH030 | Ang 695, Trilochan | C/G | Action and consequence remain linked; blame or complaint does not erase the actor's relation to conduct. |
| DH031 | Ang 695, Sain | D/S | Formal service/ritual is not treated as an automatic substitute for substantive orientation. |
| DH032 | Ang 695, Pipa | D/S | What is sought outwardly may require inward transformation rather than external performance alone. |
| DH033 | Ang 695, Dhanna | C/E | Exemplars and transmitted teaching can function developmentally across social and occupational boundaries. |

## Contributor-diversity control
Multiple Bhagats at the same terminal ang are not collapsed into one authorial voice. Recurrence across contributors is logged as recurrence, not as independent principles.
