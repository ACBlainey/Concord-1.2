# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-369 — N2 Parent-Child Compression A: Truth, Understanding and Development

## Stage control
N2 tests E8-internal parent-child structure only. External architectures remain firewalled.

## Parent candidate: reliable orientation
The following N1 families form a coherent higher-order structure:
- truthful orientation and conduct;
- discernment and realised understanding;
- reflective maintenance of orientation;
- epistemic humility;
- epistemic independence from social distortion.

These are not identical. They function as truth-direction, discrimination, maintenance, self-limitation and resistance-to-distortion components of a broader **reliable orientation** family.

## Parent candidate: developmental correctability
The following form a second structure:
- correctability and reorientation;
- finite-opportunity deliberation;
- development through disciplined practice;
- resilience and renewed agency.

The shared function is continued ethical development under error, limitation and finite opportunity. Resilience remains a branch concerned specifically with adversity.

## Retained bridge
Inward-outward integrity links reliable orientation to development but is not absorbed entirely into either parent because it tests whether orientation is realised behaviourally.
