# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-374 — N3 Architecture Separation A: Orientation, Agency and Development Layers

## Stage control
N3 tests the internal architecture of the E8-derived parents. External ethical architectures remain firewalled.

## Reliable orientation
Reliable orientation functions primarily as an **epistemic-directional layer**. It determines how an agent distinguishes, maintains and corrects orientation toward what is treated as truthful and reliable.

Its child functions include discernment, reflection, epistemic humility and resistance to socially generated distortion.

## Self-regulated agency
Self-regulated agency functions primarily as an **agency-stability layer**. It constrains capture of judgement/action by ego, fear, attachment, reactive impulse and destabilising excess.

It is not reducible to reliable orientation: an agent may recognise a sound orientation yet fail to act because agency is captured by fear, anger or attachment.

## Developmental correctability
Developmental correctability functions as a **temporal-developmental layer**. It governs the possibility of correction, formation and renewed agency across time.

It is not reducible to either knowledge or self-control because its central function is transition from a deficient state toward a more reliable one.

## Architectural result
The three parents are functionally coupled but non-duplicate:
- orientation supplies direction;
- self-regulation protects usable agency;
- development enables correction and maturation through time.
