# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-175 — Swayyas Extraction A: Ang 1385–1392, Guru Arjan, Guru Nanak and Guru Angad Strata

## Coverage
First-pass traversal across the opening Swayya strata under the praise-poetry exclusion rule.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| SW001 | Ang 1385–1387 | D/G | Formal title or exalted reputation is not coded as ethical standing unless linked to realised conduct. |
| SW002 | Ang 1385–1388 | C/E | Teaching is represented as formative where it changes the learner's orientation. |
| SW003 | Ang 1387–1389 | V/S | Humility and reduced self-magnification remain separable positive qualities within praise material. |
| SW004 | Ang 1389–1390, Guru Nanak praise | D/G | Lineage or recognised authority is not independently converted into a moral rule. |
| SW005 | Ang 1389–1390 | C/C | A teacher's influence is represented as transmissible across persons and generations. |
| SW006 | Ang 1391–1392, Guru Angad praise | C/E | Development can proceed through received guidance and disciplined continuation. |
| SW007 | Ang 1391–1392 | D/R | Succession language is evidence of continuity/transmission, not by itself evidence for hereditary political authority. |

## Exclusion note
A substantial proportion of the source is deliberately not converted into occurrence records because it consists of praise, metaphysical attribution or status claims without separable behavioural content.
