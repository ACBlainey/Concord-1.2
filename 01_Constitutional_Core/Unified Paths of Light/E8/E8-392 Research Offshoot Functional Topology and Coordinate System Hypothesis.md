# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-392 — Research Offshoot: Functional Topology and Coordinate-System Hypothesis

## Status
Candidate research hypothesis arising from E8. Not established as a Concord principle or universal law.

## Observation
E6 and E7 recovered the frozen X kernel directly, while E8 produced a differently partitioned seven-parent architecture that nevertheless mapped completely into X in the reverse direction.

This suggests a possible distinction between:
- **underlying functional topology**, and
- **tradition-specific decomposition or coordinates**.

## Coordinate-system hypothesis
Different ethical traditions may partition a recurrent cooperative constraint-space differently.

One tradition may isolate harm as a deepest root. Another may distribute comparable harm-restraining function across compassion, self-regulation and non-domination. Both may constrain overlapping regions of ethical behaviour while using different conceptual coordinates.

## Testable consequences
If the hypothesis is useful, future independent holdouts should sometimes show:
- different root counts;
- different root boundaries;
- distributed representation;
- distinctive operators and conditions;
- continued coverage of much of the same functional space.

A strong falsifier would be a well-bounded independent tradition producing a minimum-stable deepest function that cannot be represented within X, or repeatedly lacking one or more X functions under fair scoring.

## Relation to natural-law hypothesis
This result does not establish ethics as natural law. It does, however, refine what convergence would look like if recurrent constraints exist: universality should be sought primarily in function and topology, not identical vocabulary or taxonomy.
