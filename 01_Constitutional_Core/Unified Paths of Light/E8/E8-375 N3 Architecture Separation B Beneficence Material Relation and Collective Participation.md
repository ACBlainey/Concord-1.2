# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-375 — N3 Architecture Separation B: Beneficence, Material Relation and Collective Participation

## Outward beneficence
Outward beneficence functions as an **other-directed relational layer**: concern for others is translated into assistance, service, generosity and relief.

## Responsible material relation
Responsible material relation functions as a **resource-interaction layer**: acquisition, possession, use, restraint and contribution are constrained by honesty, non-exploitation and sufficiency.

It cannot be absorbed into beneficence because ethical material conduct applies even where no immediate beneficiary is identifiable.

## Constructive collective participation
Constructive collective participation functions as a **social-developmental layer**: association and participation shape agents and the quality of collective life.

It cannot be absorbed into beneficence because collective formation concerns the environment through which conduct and orientation develop, not only acts of assistance.

## Architectural result
These parents represent three different relational scales:
- direct outward relation;
- relation through material/resource systems;
- relation through collective/formative environments.
