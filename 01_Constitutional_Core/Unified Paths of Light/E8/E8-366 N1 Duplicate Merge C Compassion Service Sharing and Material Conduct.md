# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-366 — N1 Duplicate Merge C: Compassion, Service, Sharing and Material Conduct

## Merge decisions
The relevant N0 families reduce to:
1. **Compassionate concern** — merges recurring concern for suffering and benevolent orientation.
2. **Active relief/assistance** — retains practical response to distress as distinct from compassionate disposition.
3. **Service beyond narrow self-interest** — merges service/contribution formulations whose central function is outward-directed action.
4. **Generosity and sharing** — merges giving, sharing and common provision where no distinct institutional function is specified.
5. **Honest/non-exploitative material conduct** — merges material honesty and anti-exploitation occurrences.
6. **Restraint of greed/acquisitive excess** — merges closely repeated material-desire restraints.
7. **Responsible material use/sufficiency** — retains stewardship-like use and sufficiency where distinct from giving.
8. **Reciprocal contribution** — retains contribution to shared life where the function is mutual participation rather than unilateral service.

## Non-merges
Compassion is not merged with relief action. Sharing is not merged with a property doctrine. Service is not merged with coerced sacrifice.
