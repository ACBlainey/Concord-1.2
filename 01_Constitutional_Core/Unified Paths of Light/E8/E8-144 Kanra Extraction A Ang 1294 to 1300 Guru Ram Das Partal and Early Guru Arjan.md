# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-144 — Kanra Extraction A: Ang 1294–1300, Guru Ram Das, Partal and Early Guru Arjan

## Coverage
Sequential first-pass extraction from the directly verified Kanra opening.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| KN001 | Ang 1294, Guru Ram Das | C/C | Association with developed persons is represented as capable of changing the participant's own orientation. |
| KN002 | Ang 1294 | C/S | A wandering or unstable mind is represented as capable of becoming more governed through formative association. |
| KN003 | Ang 1294–1296 | V/S | Humility and receptivity are represented as conditions supporting transformation. |
| KN004 | Ang 1296–1297, Partal | D/S | Formal or performative variation does not itself establish substantive transformation. |
| KN005 | Ang 1297–1299, Guru Arjan | C/E | Guidance and sustained practice are represented as capable of changing orientation. |
| KN006 | Ang 1298–1300 | D/E | Instruction becomes developmentally meaningful through internalisation and realised practice rather than exposure alone. |
| KN007 | Ang 1299–1300 | C/S | Ego and attachment can destabilise judgement and practice. |

## Direct-source control
KN001–KN002 are anchored closely to ang 1294, which explicitly links joining the Sangat with a changed mind and bringing the wandering mind under control.

## Interpretive restraint
Sangat is not automatically translated into a generic political collective; the behavioural function is formative association.
