## E8-06 — Japji Sahib Extraction A: Opening through Pauri 7

## Coverage
Guru Granth Sahib ang 1–2; Guru Nanak; opening formula, opening salok and Pauris 1–7. Source checked against canonical-ang presentation with Gurmukhi, transliteration and English aid.

## Translation/context controls
- *Hukam* is not normalised into political obedience.
- “One giver of all souls” is retained primarily as theology.
- Status critique is coded only where the passage itself contrasts reputation/praise with deeper worth.
- No X1–X7 labels were consulted.

## Marginal themes
Truthful orientation; epistemic humility; ego reduction; function over ritual; status/reputation scepticism; learning as transformation.
