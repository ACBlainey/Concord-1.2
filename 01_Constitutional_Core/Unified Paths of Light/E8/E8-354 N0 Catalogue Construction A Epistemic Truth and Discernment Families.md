# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-354 — N0 Catalogue Construction A: Epistemic, Truth and Discernment Families

## Stage control
This is an E8-internal N0 catalogue document. It uses only patterns already extracted from the admissible Sikh occurrence ledger. No X1–X7 or prior-tradition architecture is used.

## Overcomplete functional families
The admissible occurrences support distinct provisional families around:
1. truthful orientation and truthful conduct;
2. discernment between appearance and reliable understanding;
3. reflective understanding rather than unexamined repetition;
4. realised understanding expressed through conduct;
5. resistance to falsehood, deception and self-deception;
6. humility concerning one's own knowledge and status;
7. correction and reorientation when present understanding or conduct is deficient;
8. finite-opportunity awareness as a deliberative constraint;
9. independence from praise, blame and socially generated distortion;
10. remembrance/reflection as a mechanism for maintaining orientation.

These remain separate at N0 even where later compression may reveal parent-child relations.

## Boundary controls
Devotional truth claims are not automatically converted into general epistemology. Reflection/remembrance is retained only where a separable formative or deliberative function was admitted. Humility is not equated with passivity or epistemic surrender.

## N0 result
The epistemic domain is internally dense and contains both truth-directed and correction-directed functions. No root count is inferred.
