# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-60 — Sorath Extraction C: Ang 634–641, Ashtapadi Stratum

## Coverage
Sequential extraction of the Sorath ashtapadi/chau-tuka stratum, approximately ang 634–641, before the Var begins at ang 642.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| SOH020 | Ang 634–636 | D/S | Intellectual or ritual accomplishment remains distinguishable from transformed judgement. |
| SOH021 | Ang 634–637 | C/E | Development is represented as requiring sustained practice rather than one-time insight. |
| SOH022 | Ang 637–639 | V/S | Humility and disciplined attention remain safeguards against self-certainty. |
| SOH023 | Ang 637–639 | D/G | Formal standing remains an unreliable proxy for substantive orientation. |
| SOH024 | Ang 639–641 | C/C | Shared practice can transmit constructive orientation across persons. |
| SOH025 | Ang 639–641 | D/S | The actor's own motives remain legitimate objects of scrutiny even within disciplined practice. |
| SOH026 | Ang 639–641 | C/S | Pride can re-enter after apparent development and therefore requires continuing self-audit. |

## Transition control
Canonical indices place Sorath ki Var beginning at ang 642. The Var is extracted separately because its social and collective register may differ from the preceding hymn/ashtapadi material.
