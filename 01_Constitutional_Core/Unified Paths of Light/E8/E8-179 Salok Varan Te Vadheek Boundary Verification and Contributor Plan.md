## E8-179 — Salok Varan Te Vadheek Boundary Verification and Contributor Plan

## Boundary correction
Cross-source structural indexing resolves the transition after the Bhatt Swayyas more precisely than the earlier coarse shorthand.

The post-Swayya sequence is:
- **Salok Varan Te Vadheek / Saloks of the Gurus begins at ang 1410**;
- Guru Nanak material: approximately 1410–1412;
- Guru Amar Das: 1412–1421;
- Guru Ram Das: 1421–1424;
- Guru Arjan: 1424–1426;
- Guru Tegh Bahadur: 1426–1429;
- terminal ang 1429 includes a separately attributed Salok 54 in some indices, followed by Guru Arjan's Mundavani and closing salok;
- Rag Mala begins on ang 1429 and continues to 1430.

## Correction to E8-178
E8-178's statement that the Swayya corpus closes "through ang 1409" is retained as the coarse section boundary, but detailed indices place the terminal Bhatt material into ang 1410 and the Guru-salok section beginning there. Publication-grade reconstruction must therefore preserve the shared-page transition rather than manufacture a hard page break.
