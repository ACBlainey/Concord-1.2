## Distinctive methodological issue
The Guru Granth Sahib is arranged principally by musical/rāga structure rather than as a modern topical ethics code. The SGPC describes a 1,430-page scripture containing compositions of Sikh Gurus as well as Bhagats and Bhattas, arranged primarily by musical form. E8 must therefore avoid keyword-hunting for expected ethics and instead use a predeclared structural sampling/exhaustion plan.

## Non-equivalence control
Devotional, theological and liberation-oriented statements are not automatically converted into secular ethical propositions. They qualify only where a separable behavioural, relational, epistemic, institutional or agency function is supportable.
