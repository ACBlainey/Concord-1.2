# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-355 — N0 Catalogue Construction B: Self-Regulation, Humility and Attachment Families

## Stage control
E8-only N0 construction; external architectures remain firewalled.

## Overcomplete functional families
Provisional internal families include:
1. restraint of egoic self-centering;
2. humility and reduced status-seeking;
3. contentment as stabilising disposition;
4. reduced domination by desire or craving;
5. reduced attachment as support for steadier judgement;
6. reduced fear as support for steadier action;
7. equanimity under praise/blame, gain/loss or adversity;
8. patience and endurance under difficulty;
9. resilience after apparent exhaustion or diminished capability;
10. disciplined self-correction;
11. alignment between inward orientation and outward conduct;
12. resistance to anger-driven or impulse-driven action.

## Boundary controls
Reduced attachment is not coded as rejection of relationship, responsibility or material life. Contentment is not coded as prohibition on development. Equanimity is not indifference to suffering or injustice. Humility is not subordination to arbitrary authority.

## N0 result
The corpus repeatedly links inward regulation to outward behavioural reliability. N0 preserves those links without yet deciding whether they form one parent family or several.
