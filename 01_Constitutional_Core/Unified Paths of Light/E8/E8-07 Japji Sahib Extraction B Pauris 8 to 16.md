# Unified Paths of Light — E8
## E8-07 — Japji Sahib Extraction B: Pauris 8–16

## Coverage
Guru Granth Sahib ang 2–3; listening (*suṇi-ai*), internal acceptance/trust (*mannai*) and transition to Pauri 16.

| ID | Source | Class / scope | Minimally normalised proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| SJ014 | Pauris 8–11 | D/S | Attentive listening is treated as an active route to understanding and transformation. |
| SJ015 | Pauris 8–11 | C/S | Listening is associated with growth in truth, contentment, knowledge and virtue; supernatural claims are not generalised. |
| SJ016 | Pauris 8–11 | D/S | Learning is not reduced to formal recitation or inherited religious status. |
| SJ017 | Pauris 12–15 | V/S | Internalised commitment has consequences for conduct that mere description cannot reproduce. |

| SJ020 | Pauri 15 | C/F | Transformation of the individual is depicted as capable of supporting close relations as well as the self; theological redemption language is not converted directly into a civil duty. |
| SJ021 | Pauri 16 | V/C | Esteem is linked to cultivated orientation rather than simply inherited title or social position. |
| SJ022 | Pauri 16 | D/S | Even highly regarded persons remain epistemically limited before what exceeds human calculation. |

## Repetition control
The repeated *suṇi-ai* and *mannai* refrains are coded as recurrence/emphasis, not dozens of independent principles.

## Emerging E8-internal families
- receptive learning;
- internalisation before conduct;
- anti-performative ethics;
- epistemic limitation;
- cultivated rather than merely conferred standing.

No root architecture is inferred at this stage.
