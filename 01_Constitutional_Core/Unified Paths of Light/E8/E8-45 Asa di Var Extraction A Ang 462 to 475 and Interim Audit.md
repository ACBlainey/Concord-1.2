## E8-45 — Asa di Var Extraction A: Ang 462–475 and Interim Audit

## Coverage
Sequential first-pass extraction of Asa di Var, ang 462–475, attributed principally to Guru Nanak with Guru Angad saloks interspersed. This stratum is treated separately because it contains comparatively direct social, political and behavioural criticism.

## Important development
Asa di Var supplies a denser **social and power-facing register** than much of the earlier rāga material. It strengthens the internal evidence that:
- title/office does not immunise conduct from evaluation;
- ritual status does not cancel exploitation or falsehood;
- power and elite standing are legitimate objects of criticism;
- social convention can reproduce corrupt standards.

This is still not a frozen institutional-accountability principle. Later compression must determine whether these are deepest functions, derived rules, or context-specific applications.
