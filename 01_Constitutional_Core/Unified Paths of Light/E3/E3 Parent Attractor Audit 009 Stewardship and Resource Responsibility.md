# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Parent Attractor Audit 009 — Stewardship and Responsible Resource Use

This audit tests whether possession, wealth or access to resources is ethically constrained by duties concerning use, fairness, waste, need or the common good.

| Tradition | Parent status | Evidential basis |
|---|---|---|
| Qur'an / Islam | PA-S | legitimate property, honest exchange, anti-exploitation, non-waste, social obligations of wealth and accountable common resources |
| Christianity | PA-S | stewardship, aid to poor, restraint of greed, responsible use and accountability for possessions |
| Judaism | PA-S | property rules, gleaning, debt relief, honest commerce, sabbatical constraints and obligations attached to wealth |
| Buddhism | PA-S | right livelihood, generosity, non-attachment, restraint of acquisitiveness and responsible material conduct |
| Hinduism | PA-S | dana, non-covetousness, duty-governed wealth, restraint and responsible household/resource conduct |
| Sikhism | PA-S | honest earning, sharing, seva, langar and rejection of exploitative/self-centred accumulation |
| Chinese/Confucian | PA-S | material provision as governance duty, moderation, righteous rather than merely profitable conduct and common welfare |
| African Traditional | PA-S | communal obligations of wealth, mutual aid, common-good orientation and socially constrained possession in identified traditions |

## Result

**Stewardship and Responsible Resource Use = 8/8 PA-S — Universal parent attractor.**

Conservative formulation:

> **Resources may be used and possessed, but their acquisition and use are ethically constrained by fairness, responsibility, avoidance of destructive excess and obligations extending beyond private appetite.**

The traditions disagree substantially over property institutions and redistribution mechanisms; those remain child-level differences.

> **End of Parent Attractor Audit 009**
