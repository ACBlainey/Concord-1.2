# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Parent Attractor Audit 008 — Self-Regulation, Resilience and Proportionality

This audit tests whether each tradition securely values regulation of impulse, endurance under difficulty, proportionate conduct and resistance to destabilising excess.

| Tradition | Parent status | Evidential basis |
|---|---|---|
| Qur'an / Islam | PA-S | patience, anger control, capacity-sensitive obligation, avoidance of excess, resilience and consequence awareness |
| Christianity | PA-S | patience, temperance, self-control, endurance, restraint of anger and perseverance under adversity |
| Judaism | PA-S | restraint, patience, disciplined conduct, proportional responsibility and endurance |
| Buddhism | PA-S | disciplined mind, restraint of craving/anger, equanimity, Middle Way and patient endurance |
| Hinduism | PA-S | self-control, forbearance, equanimity, moderation and steadiness through gain and loss |
| Sikhism | PA-S | patience, restraint of anger/ego, disciplined living, contentment and resilience |
| Chinese/Confucian | PA-S | self-cultivation, restraint, moderation, reflective correction and endurance in ethical practice |
| African Traditional | PA-S | character discipline, endurance, restraint and socially proportionate conduct in identified ethical traditions |

## Result

**Self-Regulation, Resilience and Proportionality = 8/8 PA-S — Universal parent attractor.**

Conservative parent formulation:

> **Regulate destructive impulses and excess; maintain ethical agency under adversity or changing fortune; and keep responses proportionate to circumstance.**

This does not imply identical ascetic, emotional or disciplinary norms.

> **End of Parent Attractor Audit 008**
