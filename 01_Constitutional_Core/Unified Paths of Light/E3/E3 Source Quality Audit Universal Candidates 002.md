## Finding

This tranche reinforces a recurring pattern.

The most credible universal candidates are not dependent on obscure interpretive equivalence. They concern very old and socially legible behavioural problems:

- dishonest exchange;
- severe need;
- dependant protection;
- malicious speech;
- patience;
- anger;
- social influence.

However, the audit must preserve **resolution**. A tradition teaching “do not lie” does not automatically prove “do not backbite”; general generosity does not automatically prove a structured positive duty under every form of severe need.

The final table should therefore record both the shared parent ethic and the degree to which the specific E3 formulation is independently supported.
