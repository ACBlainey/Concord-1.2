## Corpus-Resolution Closure

The detailed Psalm corpus check resolves the remaining first-pass uncertainty sufficiently to move to quantitative synthesis.

The result is neither total convergence nor two unrelated ethical systems.

Instead, the anchor reversal reveals three layers:

1. **Strongly reproduced ethical architecture** — truth, non-harm, promise integrity, humility, protection of vulnerable persons, restraint of power, patience, generosity, social responsibility, moral accountability and association effects.
2. **Shared higher-order ethics with different granularity** — E3 often converts broad Psalm heuristics into operational rules governing evidence, institutions, commerce, conflict and governance.
3. **Material Qur'an-anchor additions at this extraction resolution** — especially privacy, non-compulsion, consent, relationship procedure, capacity-aware obligation, anti-overconstraint, safe passage, impairment, refusal and developmental agency.

These differences must be preserved in the final statistics rather than absorbed into overly broad matches.
