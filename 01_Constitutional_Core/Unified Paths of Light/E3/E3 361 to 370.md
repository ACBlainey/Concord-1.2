# E3 Unit 361 — Qur'an 25:63
## Do Not Let Provocation Dictate Your Conduct

### Qur'anic Source
Qur'an 25:63 praises people who respond peacefully when addressed by the ignorant or provocative.

### Extracted Ethical Core
- Provocation does not require reciprocal provocation.
- Self-command preserves agency under social pressure.
- De-escalatory speech can interrupt conflict loops.
- Another person's loss of restraint need not determine one's own behaviour.

### Comparative Analysis

| Tradition / Text | Equivalent | Grade | Reduced Core |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Christianity — Matthew 5:39–44 | Retaliatory escalation is strongly constrained. | A/B | Do not mirror hostility. |
| Judaism — Proverbs 15:1 | Gentle response can turn away anger. | A | De-escalate provocative speech. |
| Buddhism — Right Speech | Harsh and divisive speech is constrained. | A | Respond without reproducing harm. |
| Hinduism | Forbearance and disciplined speech strongly converge. | A/B | Maintain self-command under insult. |
| Sikhism | Control of anger and ego strongly converges. | A/B | Do not surrender conduct to provocation. |
| Confucianism | Cultivated conduct remains governed by propriety. | B | Preserve dignity under insult. |
| African Traditional | Conflict norms vary. | B/C | Restraint can prevent escalation. |

### Conclusion
**Audit disposition: merge with Unit 140 and de-escalation cluster.**

# E3 Unit 362 — Qur'an 25:67
## Sustainable Spending Lies Between Waste and Miserliness

### Qur'anic Source
Qur'an 25:67 praises spending that is neither extravagant nor miserly but balanced between the two.

### Extracted Ethical Core
- Opposite extremes can both be ethically defective.
- Resource policy should preserve a sustainable middle.
- Generosity is not identical to uncontrolled expenditure.
- Restraint is not identical to hoarding.
- Balance can be an operational rather than merely rhetorical principle.

- Good character without adequate capability can also be insufficient.

- Role fitness is multidimensional.

# E3 Unit 364 — Qur'an 28:77

### Qur'anic Source

- Material flourishing and ethical restraint need not be opposites.
- Personal benefit should remain bounded by effects on shared systems.
- Beneficence can accompany legitimate self-interest.
- Prosperity does not license corruption.
- Economic success should remain compatible with social continuity.

# E3 Unit 368 — Qur'an 33:5
## Honest Mistakes Should Be Distinguished From Deliberate Wrongdoing

### Qur'anic Source
Qur'an 33:5 distinguishes unintentional error from what the heart deliberately intends.

### Extracted Ethical Core
- Culpability should track intention as well as outcome.
- Error correction and punishment should not be identical processes.
- Systems should distinguish mistake, negligence, recklessness, and deliberate abuse where evidence permits.
- Fear of punishment for innocent error can suppress learning and reporting.
- Intent-sensitive accountability can improve both justice and safety.

### Comparative Analysis

| Confucianism | Moral cultivation distinguishes ignorance/error from wilful misconduct. | B | Correct error proportionately. |
