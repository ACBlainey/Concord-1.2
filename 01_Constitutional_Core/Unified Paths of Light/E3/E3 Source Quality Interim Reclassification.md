## 5. Interpretation

This is not evidence that the latter principles are weaker ethics.

It means they are **less recurrent at the same level of resolution** across the comparison traditions.

Several are precisely the kinds of rules expected to emerge when broad ethics are converted into institutional or machine-checkable constraints.

## 6. Emerging Structural Model

The E3 evidence now supports a three-layer interpretation:

### Layer 1 — Deep cross-traditional behavioural attractors

Truth, life protection, justice, care, generosity/social obligation, restraint, trustworthy relations, non-complicity.

### Layer 2 — Widely recurring elaborations

Patience, anger control, moderation, restorative repair, constrained authority, reciprocal obligation, resilience, preserved agency.

### Layer 3 — Anchor-dependent operational safeguards

Verification, procedure, privacy, consent mechanics, dissolution rules, capacity scaling, anti-overconstraint, threat classification, safe passage, public-resource governance, impaired-agency protection, refusal, developmental agency and institutional-trust synthesis.

This structure is more informative than a single headline percentage.
