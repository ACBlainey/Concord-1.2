# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Source Provenance Repair 004 — Buddhist and Confucian Evidence Anchors

**Status:** Citation-normalisation pass.

---

## 1. Buddhist Evidence

The Buddhist row should distinguish direct canonical ethical rules from later generic summaries of “Buddhist values.”

### B1 — Right Speech

Early Buddhist formulations of right speech prohibit false speech, divisive speech, harsh speech and idle/frivolous harmful speech.

### E3 uses

Strong support for:
- Q001 Truthful Communication;
- Q036 No Ridicule, Defamation, or Degrading Labels at the harmful-speech level;
- Q059 Use Speech to Reduce Rather Than Manufacture Conflict;
- Q060 No Gossip, Backbiting, or Malicious Information Transfer.

**Boundary:** right speech does not automatically establish formal evidentiary procedure.

### B2 — Five Precepts

The precepts provide strong canonical anchors against:
- killing;
- taking what is not given;
- sexual misconduct;
- false speech;
- intoxicants leading to heedlessness.

### E3 uses

Strong support for:
- Q011 Protection of Life;
- Q021 Property by Legitimate Consent at the non-taking parent level;
- Q046 Sexual Non-Exploitation at the broad misconduct level;
- Q001 Truthfulness;
- Q081 impaired-judgement/addiction cluster at the sobriety parent level.

**Boundary:** the intoxication precept does not itself prove E3-Q081's second-order prohibition on exploiting another person's impairment.

### B3 — Anger and Non-Hatred

Dhammapada material repeatedly rejects hatred as the cure for hatred and praises restraint of anger.

### E3 uses

Strong support for:
- Q015 Forgiveness/Reconciliation at non-retaliatory level;
- Q064 Control Anger Before It Controls Action;
- Q014 Peace/De-Escalation.

## 2. Confucian Evidence

The Confucian row should use identified classical passages rather than generic references to harmony.

### C1 — Analects 1.4

Zengzi's self-examination asks whether he has been faithful in dealings, trustworthy with friends and practised what was transmitted.

### E3 uses

Strong support for:
- Q009 Self-Critical Humility / corrigibility;
- Q006 commitment/trust integrity at interpersonal level;
- Q008 alignment between principle and conduct.

### C2 — Analects 2.17

The passage identifies knowledge with recognising what one knows and what one does not know.

### E3 uses

Strong support for:
- Q002 Epistemic Responsibility.

It does not independently prove Q003's report-verification procedure.

### C3 — Analects 12.2 / 15.24

The reciprocal rule commonly rendered as not imposing on others what one would not wish for oneself provides strong evidence for reciprocal regard.

### E3 uses

Strong support for:
- Q062 Reciprocal Courtesy;
- Q048 reciprocal relational obligation at the parent level.

### C4 — Analects 12.7

Government requires material provision, defence and the people's trust; when forced to relinquish elements, trust is treated as indispensable to political survival.

### E3 uses

Strong direct support for:
- Q089 Institutional Trust Is a Shared Resource at least within political governance.

This materially strengthens the Confucian Q089 row while leaving the question of 8/8 universality open.

### C5 — Analects 13.23

The exemplary person can seek harmony without requiring sameness.

### E3 uses

Strong evidence for:
- Q041 Coexistence Without Forced Sameness;
- Q057 Social Cohesion Without Suppression of Difference.

This is especially valuable because it supports the exact distinction between harmony and uniformity rather than generic social cohesion.

---

## 3. Audit Result

Buddhist evidence is particularly strong for primary behavioural restraints.

Confucian evidence is particularly strong for epistemic self-knowledge, reciprocal conduct, political trust and **harmony without sameness**.

Neither tradition should be made to support modern procedural children solely from broad parent ethics.
