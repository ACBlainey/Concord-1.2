# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Structural Synthesis of the Audited Prevalence Results

---

## 1. The Result Is Not a Flat List

The 89 principles do not behave as 89 independent ethical atoms.

They form a layered architecture.

### Layer I — Deep Ethical Attractors

Examples:
- truth;
- protection of life;
- justice;
- care for severe need;
- generosity/social obligation;
- reciprocity;
- moderation;
- non-complicity;
- trustworthy authority;
- repair and reconciliation.

These show the strongest cross-traditional recurrence.

### Layer II — Ethical Elaborations

Examples:
- patience;
- anger control;
- resilience;
- forgiveness;
- relational reciprocity;
- social-bond preservation;
- consequence awareness;
- gentle leadership.

These remain widely recurrent but are more culturally differentiated.

### Layer III — Operational Safeguards

Examples:
- verification before consequential action;
- procedural fairness;
- fiduciary protection;
- privacy;
- non-compulsion;
- consent mechanics;
- structured consultation;
- confidentiality constraints;
- capacity calibration;
- safe passage;
- impaired-agency protection;
- refusal;
- developmental agency.

These are the least uniformly explicit.

## 2. A Possible Comparative Explanation

The pattern is consistent with enduring traditions converging most strongly on **what human social life must protect**, while differing more on **how those protections are operationalised**.

This does not prove that one tradition is more ethically developed than another.

It does show that comparative work loses information if it compresses every procedural child back into a universal parent value.

## 3. Qur'an-First Contribution

The Qur'an-first extraction appears especially productive in generating rules at the boundary between:
- ethics and procedure;
- morality and governance;
- personal virtue and institutional constraint;
- harm prevention and agency preservation.

This helps explain why the E3 catalogue contains both strong cross-traditional attractors and a sizeable lower-prevalence operational layer.

## 4. Relationship to Anchor Reversal

The earlier anchor-reversal result found **71/89 (79.8%)** of the frozen Qur'an-derived catalogue had at least cluster-level functional convergence with the independently Psalm-derived corpus.

The present prevalence work asks a harder question: not whether a Qur'an-derived principle finds a Psalm-side analogue, but how widely it can be securely evidenced across the entire eight-family comparison.

The two results therefore measure different things:
- **anchor robustness**, and
- **cross-traditional prevalence**.

They should not be numerically conflated.

## 5. Implication for Unified Paths of Light

The emerging evidence supports a model in which a relatively compact set of deep ethical attractors can recur across traditions while each tradition supplies additional elaborations, procedures and safeguards.

That is methodologically more informative than either:
- claiming that all religions teach the same ethics; or
- treating every textual difference as evidence of incompatible moral systems.
