## Non-Harm Attractor Analysis

The exact-proposition prevalence table can make Non-Harm appear weaker than expected because no single frozen Q item represents the whole attractor.

## 2. Why Q011 alone is not the ranking of Non-Harm

Q011 measures the exact proposition **Sanctity and Protection of Life**.

Non-Harm is broader. A tradition can express the harm attractor through non-aggression, compassion, bodily protection, restraint, proportionality, protection of vulnerable persons or repair without using an exact sanctity-of-life formulation.

Therefore Q011's Common-tier result must not be interpreted as “Non-Harm is only common in five or six traditions.”

## 3. What can currently be concluded

The E3 evidence strongly indicates that harm limitation is a **deep recurring attractor**, because multiple independently extracted principles across the catalogue repeatedly instantiate prevention, protection, restraint and repair.

However, a numerical 8-tradition prevalence score for the parent attractor has **not yet been independently calculated**. Doing so requires returning to the audited evidence register tradition by tradition and testing the parent function directly.

Accordingly:

> **Non-Harm is structurally one of the strongest higher-order attractors visible in E3, but it should not yet be assigned an 8/8, 7/8 or other numerical rank from child scores alone.**

## 4. Relationship to the Concord

This explains an apparent discrepancy with the Concord Ethical Kernel.

The Concord's **Non-Harm** law operates at a higher abstraction level than most frozen E3-Q principles. E3 frequently records the mechanisms by which non-harm is implemented rather than repeating the parent principle.

The comparison therefore suggests a hierarchy:

**Non-Harm attractor**  
→ prevent unjustified injury  
→ protect vulnerable agents  
→ constrain coercion/exploitation  
→ calibrate responsibility and response  
→ repair harm where possible.

This is precisely the sort of compression relationship the attractor analysis is intended to test across all ten families.
