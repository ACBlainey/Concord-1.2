# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Source-Quality Audit — Universal Candidates 001

**Scope:** First tranche of principles labelled **Universal** in the frozen final catalogue.  
**Principles:** E3-Q001, Q006, Q008, Q009, Q011, Q015, Q016.

---

## Audit Rule

This pass does not ask whether the principle is plausible across traditions. It asks whether an **8/8 claim is presently supported strongly enough to publish as secure**.

A Universal label is retained as **secure** only when every counted tradition has adequate provenance and functional fit. A weakly sourced eighth tradition makes the result **provisionally universal** until repaired.

| Principle | Preliminary label | Source-audit finding | Status after audit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Q001 Truthful Communication** | Universal | Truthfulness is among the strongest cross-traditional ethical candidates. Canonical/foundational support is readily identifiable in the biblical, Jewish, Buddhist, Hindu, Sikh and Confucian traditions. African Traditional support is plausible but must be tied to a named community/corpus rather than “African tradition” generically. | **Provisionally Universal — African provenance repair required.** |
| **Q006 Promise, Covenant, and Commitment Integrity** | Universal | Strong textual support exists across several canonical traditions. The main vulnerability is again the breadth of the African Traditional category and, in some rows, insufficiently precise non-anchor citation. | **Provisionally Universal.** |
| **Q008 Integrity Between Declared Principle and Conduct** | Universal | Strong functional equivalents occur in major textual traditions: hypocrisy, empty profession and conduct inconsistent with declared ethics are widely criticised. The present E3 comparison still requires passage-level source normalisation before 8/8 can be called secure. | **Provisionally Universal.** |
| **Q009 Self-Critical Humility** | Universal | Humility/anti-arrogance has exceptionally broad textual support. The functional claim is strong; the weak point is provenance precision, not apparent ethical absence. | **Provisionally Universal; high-confidence candidate for secure 8/8 after citation repair.** |
| **Q011 Sanctity and Protection of Life** | Universal | Strong non-killing/non-murder/non-harm traditions are identifiable across the major textual traditions. African evidence must be society-specific. “Sanctity” also carries theological connotations not identical across traditions, so the functional comparison should be stated as protection against unjust killing/harm. | **Provisionally Universal at functional level.** |
| **Q015 Forgiveness and Reconciliation** | Universal | Forgiveness, reconciliation or restorative settlement is broadly represented, but these are not identical concepts. Some traditions may support restraint/compassion more directly than interpersonal forgiveness. | **Universal claim requires functional-boundary checking; presently provisional.** |
| **Q016 Justice Without Partiality** | Universal | Impartial/fair judgement is strongly represented in multiple canonical traditions. The present comparison should not infer an 8/8 result from a generic customary-fairness statement for African Traditional Religions. | **Provisionally Universal; strong candidate after provenance repair.** |

## Finding

None of these seven principles should yet be demoted for substantive ethical absence.

Equally, none should be marked **publication-secure 8/8** merely because the preliminary comparison table contains eight positive-looking rows.

The correct status is:

> **high-confidence universal candidates whose weakest evidentiary link must be repaired before final prevalence counting.**

This is especially important because an 8/8 label is binary: one unsupported tradition changes the classification.
