# E3-Q031 — Non-Transactional Aid

**Provisional ancestry:** N032.

## Final Principle

**Where assistance is given to relieve genuine need, do not automatically manufacture a debt of gratitude, obedience, praise, or repayment.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 76:8–9.

### Supporting E3 Units

**188, 317, 396**, with related anti-performative material **025, 054, 199, 406**.

### Boundary

Reciprocal exchange remains legitimate elsewhere. This principle governs aid whose purpose is relief rather than trade.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q032 — Positive Duty Toward Severe Need

**Provisional ancestry:** N033.

## Final Principle

**Do not treat extreme vulnerability as somebody else's problem; provide practical assistance where basic welfare, freedom, or survival is at serious risk.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 74:42–44; 89:17–20; 90:12–16; 107:1–3.

### Supporting E3 Units

**183, 190, 191, 194, 198, 312, 316, 320, 321, 325, 398, 399, 405**.

### Boundary

The principle is strongest under severe vulnerability and does not make every unmet preference an unlimited claim.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal.**

# E3-Q039 — Inclusion Against Elite Capture

**Provisional ancestry:** N040; N086 nested.

## Final Principle

**Do not exclude legitimate low-status participants to gain approval from higher-status actors, and preserve reasonable access to shared participation.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 6:52; 18:28; 58:11; 80:1–10.

### Supporting E3 Units

**125, 172, 219, 259, 302, 318, 329, 383, 414**.

### Nested Applications

- do not court elites by excluding vulnerable participants;
- make room in shared assemblies;
- status alone does not determine access.

### Boundary

Legitimate competence, role, safety, and capacity requirements remain possible.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common / Near-universal.**
