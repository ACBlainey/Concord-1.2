# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Parent Attractor Audit 002 — Truth, Knowledge and Epistemic Integrity

This audit tests whether each tradition securely instantiates the higher-order function of truthfulness, responsible belief, reliable communication and restraint against knowing falsehood.

| Tradition | Parent status | Evidential basis |
|---|---|---|
| Qur'an / Islam | PA-S | truthful speech, testimony, verification, accurate attribution, epistemic restraint and inquiry |
| Christianity | PA-S | truthfulness, false-witness prohibition, honest speech and examination of claims |
| Judaism | PA-S | false-witness prohibition, witness standards, inquiry and truthful judgement |
| Buddhism | PA-S | Right Speech, abstention from false speech and disciplined perception/judgement |
| Hinduism | PA-S | satya/truthfulness, sincerity, knowledge and restraint of delusion |
| Sikhism | PA-S | truth and truthful living, rejection of falsehood and integrity between belief and conduct |
| Chinese/Confucian | PA-S | sincerity/trustworthiness, rectification of knowledge, acknowledgement of knowledge limits |
| African Traditional | PA-S | truthfulness, honesty, trustworthy character and socially responsible communication in identified ethical traditions |

## Result

**Truth, Knowledge and Epistemic Integrity = 8/8 PA-S — Universal parent attractor.**

The traditions differ greatly in formal epistemic machinery. Verification rules, witness procedure and attribution safeguards are not equally explicit. The universal parent is therefore narrower than the full Qur'anic operational package:

> **Seek and communicate truth in good faith; do not knowingly corrupt shared understanding through falsehood; and exercise appropriate humility about what is known.**

This parent result must not be used to mark every verification or evidence child universal.

> **End of Parent Attractor Audit 002**
