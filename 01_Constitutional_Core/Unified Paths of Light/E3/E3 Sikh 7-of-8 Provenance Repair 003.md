## Q082 — Repair Harm Where Possible

Sikh ethics provides strong evidence for compassion, forgiveness, service, reform and restoration of social relations.

But Q082 requires more than forgiveness after harm. It imposes a positive obligation to **repair the harm where repair is possible**.

The present source record does not contain a sufficiently direct Sikh restitution/repair principle to establish the frozen proposition securely.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P**.

**Reason:** restorative orientation is strong; explicit repair obligation remains unproven.
