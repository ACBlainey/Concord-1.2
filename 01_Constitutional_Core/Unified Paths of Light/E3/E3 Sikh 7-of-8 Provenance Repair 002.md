# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Sikh 7-of-8 Provenance Repair 002 — Q059, Q062

## Q059 — Use Speech to Reduce Rather Than Manufacture Conflict

Sikh primary evidence securely condemns slander and malicious speech. That evidence was sufficient to upgrade Q060 because Q060 directly concerns malicious information transfer.

Q059 is different. It requires a positive systems function: speech should **reduce rather than manufacture conflict**.

A prohibition on slander is relevant but does not necessarily establish de-escalatory speech as the frozen principle requires.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P**.

**Reason:** direct evidence for harmful-speech restraint exists; conflict-reduction function remains inferential.

## Q062 — Reciprocal Courtesy

Sikh ethics strongly supports humility, respect, service and equal regard. These are substantial components of courteous social conduct.

The frozen Q062 proposition, however, is explicitly **reciprocal**. General respect does not establish a reciprocity rule unless the source links treatment of others to reciprocal treatment or equivalent mutuality.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P**.

**Reason:** courtesy/respect parent is secure; reciprocity component remains insufficiently evidenced.
