# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Priority Provenance Repair 005 — Q073, Q075, Q077

## Q073 — Resilience Under Gain and Loss

Sikh sources provide direct material for steadiness across changing conditions. Sikh teaching on Hukam and contentment explicitly treats comfort/suffering, success/failure and honour/dishonour with equanimity; scholarly Sikh studies likewise describe contentment as calm acceptance of victory/defeat and pain/pleasure.

**Source-quality:** SQ-A/B where tied to exact SGGS passages; scholarly synthesis corroborates.

**Functional grade:** A/B.

**Decision:** Sikh row is eligible for **S — Secure**.

**Result:** Q073 becomes candidate **8/8 Universal**.

## Q075 — Preserve Social Bonds Against Needless Severance

Sikh community, service and fellowship ethics strongly value social bonds, but this is not identical to the frozen rule against **needless severance**. A direct prohibition or sufficiently close primary formulation has not yet been established.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P**.

**Result:** Q075 remains **7/8 secure**.

## Q077 — Good Association Supports Ethical Conduct

This row has particularly strong Sikh evidence. The Guru Granth Sahib repeatedly treats *sadh sangat*—the company of the virtuous—as formative of conduct. Primary-scripture commentary explicitly describes virtuous company as nurturing changes in deeds and behaviour, while Sikh scholarship identifies the company of the good as a major ethical emphasis.

**Source-quality:** SQ-A/B.

**Functional grade:** A/B.

**Decision:** Sikh row is eligible for **S — Secure**.

**Result:** Q077 becomes candidate **8/8 Universal**.
