# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Candidate Upgrade Citation Control 002 — Q001, Q015, Q031

## Q001 — Truthful Communication

**Primary source:** Sri Guru Granth Sahib, **Ang 62**.  
**Key line:** *Sachahu orai sabh ko, upar sach achar* — truth is high, but truthful conduct/living is higher.

**Functional note:** This directly establishes truth expressed in conduct rather than truth as abstract doctrine. Together with the SGGS rejection of falsehood, it securely supports the behavioural core of Q001.

**SQ:** A.  
**Functional grade:** A/B.  
**Decision:** **Sikh P → S approved.**

## Q015 — Forgiveness and Reconciliation

**Primary source:** Sri Guru Granth Sahib, Sheikh Farid salok, **Ang 1381–1382**.  
**Key behavioural content:** do good to the person regarded as bad and do not retain anger in the mind.

**Functional note:** The passage directly rejects retaliatory hostility and instructs positive conduct toward an offender. This securely supports forgiveness and the behavioural orientation required for reconciliation, even though it does not prescribe a formal reconciliation procedure.

**SQ:** A.  
**Functional grade:** A/B.  
**Decision:** **Sikh P → S approved.**

## Q031 — Non-Transactional Aid

**Primary source:** Sri Guru Granth Sahib, **Ang 286**.  
**Key line:** *Seva karat hoi nihkami* — service is performed without desire for its fruit/reward.

**Functional note:** This is unusually direct evidence for non-transactional service: aid/service is normatively detached from expected return.

**SQ:** A.  
**Functional grade:** A.  
**Decision:** **Sikh P → S approved.**
