# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Sikh 7-of-8 Provenance Repair 001 — Q032, Q055

## Q032 — Positive Duty Toward Severe Need

Sikh primary teaching strongly establishes sharing, service and assistance. SGGS Ang 1245 supplies direct evidence for earning honestly and sharing from what one has, while the broader Sikh institution of langar operationalises provision without status distinction.

The frozen Q032 formulation is narrower: it requires a **positive duty specifically toward severe need**. Neither ordinary sharing nor universal communal provision by itself proves that severity-triggered duty.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P**.

**Reason:** strong parent duty, insufficient evidence for the severity qualifier.

## Q055 — Do Not Cooperate in Wrongdoing

Sikh ethics strongly condemns falsehood and wrongdoing and values moral courage. Sikh history also contains resistance to unjust authority.

However, Q055 is a complicity rule: it prohibits **cooperation in another's wrongdoing**. Personal refusal to do wrong and resistance to injustice are close but not identical.

No exact primary Sikh passage has yet been normalised that establishes the non-assistance/complicity formulation itself.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P**.

**Reason:** wrongdoing-resistance parent is strong; complicity-specific child remains unproven.
