# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Parent Attractor Audit 007 — Peace, Reconciliation and Social Cohesion

This audit tests whether traditions positively value reduction of destructive conflict and restoration or preservation of workable social relations.

| Tradition | Parent status | Evidential basis |
|---|---|---|
| Qur'an / Islam | PA-S | de-escalation, reconciliation, just peace, respectful disagreement, conflict-reducing speech and social cohesion |
| Christianity | PA-S | peacemaking, forgiveness, enemy-love, reconciliation and restraint of retaliation |
| Judaism | PA-S | pursuit of peace, reconciliation, neighbour obligation, dispute resolution and restrained retaliation |
| Buddhism | PA-S | non-hatred, compassion, restraint of anger, harmonious speech and non-violence |
| Hinduism | PA-S | peacefulness, non-injury, forgiveness, self-restraint and duties sustaining social order |
| Sikhism | PA-S | forgiveness, restraint of anger, truthful/non-slanderous speech, sangat and solidarity alongside justified resistance |
| Chinese/Confucian | PA-S | harmony without sameness, reciprocity, relational repair and conduct sustaining social harmony |
| African Traditional | PA-S | reconciliation, restorative practice, communal cohesion and restoration of damaged relationships in identified traditions |

## Result

**Peace, Reconciliation and Social Cohesion = 8/8 PA-S — Universal parent attractor.**

The common parent is not pacifism:

> **Avoid needless destructive conflict; prefer peaceful or restorative resolution where possible; and preserve or rebuild social relations compatible with justice.**

The qualifier “compatible with justice” matters because several traditions reject peace purchased through submission to serious wrongdoing.

> **End of Parent Attractor Audit 007**
