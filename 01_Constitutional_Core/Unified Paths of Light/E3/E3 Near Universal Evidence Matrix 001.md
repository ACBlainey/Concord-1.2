# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Near-Universal Evidence Matrix 001

**Principles:** Q002, Q003, Q010, Q012, Q017.

---

## Q002 — Epistemic Responsibility

Truth, wisdom, disciplined inquiry and caution about falsehood recur widely. The exact E3 principle requires responsibility for what one claims to know, not merely admiration of wisdom.

Biblical/Jewish traditions contain strong warnings against false witness and foolish assertion; Buddhist ethics links right view and right speech; Hindu and Sikh traditions value truthful knowledge; Confucian ethics strongly values learning, reflection and correction. African philosophical traditions document wisdom, truthfulness and socially responsible judgement, but provenance should remain specific.

**Decision:** **Near-universal.** Universal parent cluster of truth/wisdom is stronger than the exact epistemic-duty formulation.

## Q003 — Verify Before Consequential Action

This principle is substantially more operational. Several traditions require careful judgement, truthful evidence or investigation, but broad truthfulness is not enough to prove a verify-before-action rule.

The Psalm anchor did not independently recover this at comparable resolution.

**Decision:** **Common / Near-universal pending direct procedural evidence. Do not retain a high tier by inference from generic wisdom.**

## Q010 — Open and Discriminating Inquiry

Learning, reflection and discernment are widespread. “Listen widely and follow what is best” combines openness with selective judgement and therefore differs from obedience to a fixed authoritative teaching.

Confucian learning/reflection gives strong support; Buddhist investigation and discernment provide another route. Other traditions may support study while placing different boundaries around doctrinal openness.

**Decision:** **Near-universal / Common.**

## Q012 — Non-Aggression and Proportionality

Restraint of violence is widespread, but traditions differ materially over legitimate force, retaliation, punishment and war. Proportionality is more specific than non-harm.

**Decision:** **Near-universal at broad restraint level; Common/Near-universal at exact non-aggression-plus-proportionality formulation.**

## Q017 — Procedural Fairness

Fair judgement is widely valued. Procedure—notice, evidence, hearing, consistent rules and decision process—is much more institutionally specific.

The Psalm anchor-reversal already showed this asymmetry.

**Decision:** **Common / Near-universal. Universal justice must not be used as a proxy for universal due process.**
