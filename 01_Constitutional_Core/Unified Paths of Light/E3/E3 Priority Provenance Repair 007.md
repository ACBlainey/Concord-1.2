# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Priority Provenance Repair 007 — Q001, Q009, Q011

**Scope:** first tranche of the mechanically verified 6/8 boundary set.

## Q001 — Truthful Communication

The two non-secure cells are Sikh P and African P.

### Sikh
The Guru Granth Sahib repeatedly places truth and truthful living at the centre of ethical conduct. This is not merely a corollary of another virtue: it directly supports truthful communication/living.

**Decision:** Sikh P is eligible for **S**, subject to exact-Ang citation normalisation in the final source register.

### African
The existing evidence establishes truth/honesty as a recognised virtue in scoped African ethical traditions, but the current register still relies substantially on scholarly synthesis rather than a single canonical source.

**Decision:** retain African **P** until named-tradition/source normalisation is completed.

**Candidate result:** Q001 may move **6/8 → 7/8**, not 8/8.

## Q009 — Self-Critical Humility

The unresolved rows are Sikh P and African P.

Sikh evidence for humility and ego-restraint is strong and primary-text grounded. However Q009 combines humility with **self-critical** correction. Humility alone does not necessarily establish reflective self-critique.

African evidence likewise supports humility/community-correctability in scoped traditions, but exact equivalence remains insufficient.

**Decision:** retain both **P**.

**Result:** Q009 remains **6/8**.

## Q011 — Sanctity and Protection of Life

The unresolved rows are Sikh P and African P.

Sikh non-harm/compassion evidence strongly supports protection of life, but the frozen formulation combines sanctity and active protection. The existing exact-source record needs normalisation before secure promotion.

African traditions strongly value life and human welfare, but the present evidence remains scoped and heterogeneous.

**Decision:** retain both **P** pending exact-source normalisation.

**Result:** Q011 remains **6/8**.
