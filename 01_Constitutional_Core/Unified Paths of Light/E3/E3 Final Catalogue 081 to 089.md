# E3-Q081 — Do Not Exploit Addiction or Impaired Judgement

**Provisional ancestry:** N090.

## Final Principle

**Avoid practices that predictably impair judgement, generate dependency, or convert impaired agency into conflict and exploitation.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 5:90–91.

### Supporting E3 Units

**079**.

### Boundary

The principle is functional rather than a technical classification of every substance or activity; evidence of impairment, dependency, and harm matters.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q082 — Repair Harm Where Possible

**Provisional ancestry:** N091.

## Final Principle

**Where wrongdoing has caused identifiable harm, accountability should include proportionate repair, restitution, compensation, or corrective action where practicable.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:92; 5:89; related property and obligation passages.

### Supporting E3 Units

**060, 078, 342**, with reform/accountability links **074, 142**.

### Boundary

Some harms cannot be fully reversed; repair does not necessarily erase safeguarding needs.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

---

# E3-Q083 — Distinguish Accident From Deliberate Harm

**Provisional ancestry:** N092.

## Final Principle

**Calibrate responsibility to intention, knowledge, negligence, and preventability rather than treating accidental and deliberate harm as morally identical.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:92; 33:5.

### Supporting E3 Units

**060, 150, 213, 368, 412**.

### Boundary

Accidental harm can still create duties of repair even where culpability is reduced.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal.**

---

# E3-Q084 — Do Not Passively Legitimate Degrading Wrongdoing

**Provisional ancestry:** N093.

## Final Principle

**Where presence, participation, or institutional endorsement would materially legitimise degrading wrongdoing, withdraw support or challenge the conduct rather than normalising it through passive participation.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:140; 6:68.

### Supporting E3 Units

**067, 220**, with non-cooperation support **070, 171, 301**.

### Boundary

Withdrawal is not always the only response; intervention may be preferable where safe and effective.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Near-universal.**

---

# E3-Q085 — Hospitality Has Boundaries

**Provisional ancestry:** N094.

## Final Principle

**Respect hospitality, permission, and the host's legitimate boundaries; welcome does not create unlimited access or entitlement to remain.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 24:27–29; 33:53.

### Supporting E3 Units

**136, 152, 272, 286, 359**.

### Boundary

Emergency safeguarding can create exceptional duties, but ordinary hospitality does not extinguish consent.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

---

# E3-Q086 — Right of Refusal Must Be Respected

**Provisional ancestry:** N095.

## Final Principle

**Where another person has legitimate authority to grant or withhold access, relationship, or participation, a refusal should be accepted without coercive retaliation.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 24:28; related 2:232 and 4:19.

### Supporting E3 Units

**020, 049, 136, 272, 359**.

### Boundary

The principle applies where the person legitimately controls the relevant choice; it does not authorise denial of another person's independent rights.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common / Near-universal.**

---

# E3-Q087 — Developmental Agency Should Expand With Capability

**Provisional ancestry:** N096.

## Final Principle

**Adjust protective controls as relevant capability matures, increasing autonomous control rather than preserving dependency indefinitely.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:5–6; 24:58–59.

### Supporting E3 Units

**046, 139, 275, 360**, with capacity links **071**.

### Boundary

Capability can be domain-specific; autonomy should track relevant competence rather than a simplistic all-or-nothing threshold.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common.**

---

# E3-Q088 — Do Not Covet Another's Advantages Into Injustice

**Provisional ancestry:** N097.

## Final Principle

**Do not allow comparison with another person's advantages to become entitlement, resentment, appropriation, or unjust interference.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:32.

### Supporting E3 Units

**051**.

### Boundary

The principle does not prohibit criticism of unjust inequality; structural justice claims differ from personal covetousness.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal.**

# E3-Q089 — Institutional Trust Is a Shared Resource

**Provisional ancestry:** N100.

## Final Principle

**Protect the reliability of systems of promise, evidence, measurement, office, and procedure because individual abuses can degrade trust for everyone who depends on them.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 16:91–94; 2:282–283; 55:7–9; related trust passages.

### Supporting E3 Units

**029, 030, 055, 082, 089, 109, 120, 169, 189, 245, 352, 380, 394, 419**.

### Boundary

This systems-level synthesis does not erase the more specific operational principles governing promises, evidence, measurement, and authority.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal.**
