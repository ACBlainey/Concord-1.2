# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Priority Provenance Repair 012 — Q050, Q054, Q060

## Q050 — Competence and Trustworthiness in Entrusted Roles

Sikh service and honest conduct strongly support trustworthiness, but competence is a separate requirement. A trustworthy-role ethic cannot be upgraded to the frozen competence-plus-trustworthiness principle without evidence for both components.

African evidence for responsible office likewise needs explicit role-selection or competence provenance.

**Decision:** retain unresolved cells.

**Result:** Q050 remains **6/8**.

## Q054 — Collective Responsibility to Resist Corruption

Sikh traditions of collective responsibility and resistance to injustice are relevant, but resisting injustice broadly is not automatically the same as collective responsibility to resist corruption.

African common-good and anti-corruption norms likewise need named-source evidence at the frozen level.

**Decision:** retain Sikh P and unresolved African status.

**Result:** Q054 remains **6/8**.

## Q060 — No Gossip, Backbiting, or Malicious Information Transfer

Sikh evidence is strong. The Guru Granth Sahib explicitly condemns slander and malicious speech, making this much closer to Q060 than to broader speech principles.

**Decision:** Sikh P is **eligible for S** after exact-Ang citation normalisation.

African P remains unresolved pending named-tradition/source evidence for gossip/backbiting rather than generic honesty.

**Candidate result:** Q060 may move **6/8 → 7/8**.
