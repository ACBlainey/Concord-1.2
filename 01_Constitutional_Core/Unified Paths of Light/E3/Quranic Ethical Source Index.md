## 1. Purpose

This index records the source layer from which the independent Qur'an-derived E3 ethical catalogue was produced.

It replaces the initial ten-unit working index. E3 ultimately proceeded through **420 numbered extraction/audit records** before the source-level audit was closed and principle normalisation began.

The numbered records are **not 420 distinct ethical principles**.

They preserve the evidence path through which ethical propositions were extracted, compared, audited, merged, refined, excluded, and later compressed into the frozen **89-principle E3-Q001–E3-Q089 catalogue**.

---

## 2. Structural Rule

E3 distinguishes three analytical layers:

1. **Source occurrence** — a Qur'anic passage expressing or supporting an ethical proposition.
2. **Extracted proposition** — the behavioural content derived from that occurrence.
3. **Normalised principle** — the stable ethical identity remaining after overlapping propositions are merged.

Accordingly:

> **Source frequency is evidence of emphasis; it is not evidence of principle multiplicity.**

The ten-unit detailed files are administrative containers. They are not equivalent to ten Psalms and should not be counted as ten final principles.

---

## 3. Anchor and Inclusion Method

The complete Qur'an was treated as the anchor corpus. Ethical material was admitted where a verse or short inseparable sequence contained a reasonably separable:

- prescription;
- prohibition;
- virtue or vice;
- duty or responsibility;
- behavioural judgement;
- institutional safeguard;
- or generalisable ethical constraint.

Material was excluded where it was purely theological, cosmological, ritual-only, eschatological without separable behavioural content, or narrative that could not safely be generalised.

Inclusion was determined by Qur'anic ethical content rather than resemblance to the earlier Psalm-derived result.

> **The Psalm synthesis was withheld during Qur'anic extraction and normalisation so that E3 could function as an independent anchor-reversal test.**

## 6. Binding Exclusion — Unit 126

**Unit 126 is excluded from the final behavioural source corpus.**

The earlier treatment of Qur'an 18:71–82 attempted to derive behavioural content from a narrative whose actions depend on exceptional hidden knowledge unavailable to ordinary agents.

Unit 420 formally corrected this.

The passage may remain in the methodological record as a warning about extraction discipline, but it must not serve as ordinary behavioural authority.

This exclusion is binding on normalisation and final synthesis.

---

## 7. Audit Closure at Unit 420

The source audit closed at Unit 420.

This closure does **not** mean:

- every ethically relevant Qur'anic passage was extracted;
- every verse was individually coded;
- no further ethical material exists;
- or literal textual saturation was reached.

Further eligible material remains.

The stopping criterion was **downstream compression redundancy**.

Later extraction was increasingly producing propositions that mapped onto ethical identities already represented in the corpus and would therefore be merged during normalisation.

Continuing source-by-source eight-tradition comparison would have increased evidence-layer volume much faster than principle-layer information.

The closure rule is therefore:

> **Stop detailed extraction when additional source occurrences predominantly reinforce or refine principles already represented, then reopen only if normalisation exposes a material coverage gap.**

See **E3 Canonical Audit Closure.md** for the full rationale.

## 11. Current Evidentiary Status

The source-occurrence and Qur'an-derived principle layers are frozen.

The **cross-traditional prevalence classifications remain auditable**.

Subsequent source-quality work may revise whether a principle is classified Universal, Near-universal, Common, or Limited because documentary quality in the seven comparison traditions is a separate question from whether the Qur'anic principle itself was validly derived.

This distinction prevents weak comparative citation from contaminating the independently derived anchor catalogue.

---

## 12. Reopening Criterion

Source extraction should be reopened only if later analysis identifies a materially distinct ethical proposition that:

1. is supported by the Qur'anic anchor;
2. is not representable without material loss by E3-Q001–E3-Q089;
3. is not merely another source occurrence, contextual application, or refinement of an existing principle;
4. and survives the same generalisability test applied during the canonical audit.

Any such change is a **post-freeze correction** and must be explicitly documented.
