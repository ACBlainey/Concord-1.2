# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3

## Qur'anic Ethical Source Index

**Status:** Working extraction index  
**Purpose:** Establish the Qur'an-first source population before final cross-comparative synthesis.

---

## Method

The complete Qur'an is the anchor corpus. Entries are admitted because they contain a reasonably separable ethical prescription, prohibition, virtue, vice, duty, responsibility, or behavioural judgement. The index is not constructed from the Psalm-derived result.

A source unit may contain one verse or a short contiguous passage. Repeated Qur'anic formulations remain source evidence but will later be normalised so repetition does not inflate cross-traditional prevalence.

This index will remain a working document during extraction and will be frozen before Paper IV synthesis.

## Index Development Rule

Subsequent units will continue through the Qur'an in canonical order, applying the same inclusion test. The index may be split or merged where context shows that an initially identified verse is inseparable from adjacent verses.

> **Inclusion is determined by Qur'anic ethical content; convergence with another tradition is not an inclusion criterion.**
