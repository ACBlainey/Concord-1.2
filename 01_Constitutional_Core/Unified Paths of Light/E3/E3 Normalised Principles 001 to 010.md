# E3-N001 — Truthful Communication

## Normalised Principle

**Communicate truthfully and do not knowingly mix truth with falsehood, conceal material truth, or use deception to manipulate another person's judgement.**

### Operational Meaning

Truth is treated as infrastructure for agency. Deliberate falsehood, strategic concealment, and truth/falsehood mixture impair another person's ability to choose or judge on accurate information.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:42; 33:70; related covenant/deception passages including 16:91–94.

### Supporting E3 Units

003? No — Unit 003 concerns bloodshed/expulsion. Relevant support includes **203, 154, 245, 352, 419**, together with truth-related compound units such as **031, 099, 196, 242, 403**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- truthful speech;
- non-concealment of known truth;
- non-deceptive communication;
- alignment of speech with reality;
- truth reinforcement within community.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Truthfulness is distinct from **verification**. Truthfulness constrains the communicator; verification constrains the receiver or decision-maker.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong functional equivalents occur across the eight-tradition comparison set.

**Preliminary classification: Universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain as distinct.** Truth is foundational to consent, evidence, contracts, governance, and accountability.

# E3-N002 — Epistemic Responsibility

## Normalised Principle

**Do not assert, authorise, or act consequentially on claims beyond what the available knowledge justifies.**

### Operational Meaning

Confidence should track evidence. Ignorance is not automatically blameworthy, but converting unsupported belief into accusation, coercion, or binding authority creates preventable harm.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 17:36; 7:33; 16:116.

### Supporting E3 Units

**121, 223, 246, 255, 330, 415**, with related epistemic-humility material in **126 (excluded as behavioural source), 260, 369**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- do not claim what you do not know;
- do not fabricate normative authority;
- epistemic humility;
- distinguish evidence from certainty;
- do not convert ignorance into coercive judgement.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

This principle does not prohibit provisional reasoning under uncertainty. It requires uncertainty to remain visible and proportionate to action.

The excluded former Unit 126 is not supporting behavioural authority.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong equivalents exist in most traditions, though the exact relation between knowledge and binding religious authority varies.

**Preliminary classification: Near-universal / Common boundary; verify during final prevalence audit.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain as distinct.** It performs a different function from ordinary truthfulness and from report verification.

# E3-N003 — Verify Before Consequential Action

## Normalised Principle

**Verify consequential reports and accusations before acting on them, with stronger evidentiary safeguards where the potential harm is greater.**

### Operational Meaning

Information should pass through an evidentiary threshold before it becomes punishment, exclusion, accusation, or coercive action.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 49:6; 4:94; 24:4; 24:11–16.

### Supporting E3 Units

**061, 133, 134, 163, 267, 269, 293, 374**, with related witness/evidence units **082, 186, 394**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- verify reports;
- serious accusations require evidence;
- do not amplify unverified scandal;
- investigate before harming;
- source reliability matters.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Verification differs from truthful testimony. A truthful speaker has one duty; an institution receiving a claim has another.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Evidence, inquiry, or disciplined judgement has strong functional equivalents across most traditions.

**Preliminary classification: Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain as distinct.** This is an institutional epistemic safeguard.

# E3-N004 — Reliable Testimony and Evidence

## Normalised Principle

**Give testimony honestly and preserve reliable evidentiary procedures, especially where another person's rights or reputation are at stake.**

### Operational Meaning

Justice depends not only on truthful private speech but on reliable formal evidence. Witness systems should deter false testimony and protect against unsupported condemnation.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:282–283; 5:106–108; 24:4; 70:33.

### Supporting E3 Units

**029, 030, 082, 133, 186, 267, 394**, plus procedural witness material in **180**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- truthful testimony;
- witness reliability;
- documentary evidence;
- evidentiary procedure;
- deterrence of false testimony.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

This remains distinct from N003 because a verification system may contain many evidence sources; testimony is one particular responsibility within it.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong equivalents occur across the comparison set.

**Preliminary classification: Universal or Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N005 — Accurate Attribution of Responsibility

## Normalised Principle

**Attribute wrongdoing to the responsible actor and do not transfer guilt, blame, or punishment onto an innocent person.**

### Operational Meaning

Accountability requires accurate causal and moral attribution. Scapegoating creates a second injustice while allowing the responsible actor to escape correction.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:112; 33:5; related individual-responsibility passages.

### Supporting E3 Units

**150, 213, 287, 368, 412**, with related accusation/evidence units **153, 267**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- no scapegoating;
- distinguish error from deliberate wrongdoing;
- do not falsely attribute harm;
- individualised responsibility.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Intent-sensitive culpability is retained as a sub-rule: responsibility should distinguish honest mistake from deliberate wrongdoing where evidence permits.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong functional parallels occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.** Accurate responsibility attribution is central to just accountability.

# E3-N006 — Promise, Covenant, and Commitment Integrity

## Normalised Principle

**Honour legitimate commitments and do not use promises, covenants, or solemn assurances as instruments of deception.**

### Operational Meaning

Commitments enable cooperation across time. Strategic breach can harm both the immediate counterpart and the wider trust network.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 5:1; 8:58; 9:4; 16:91–94; 17:34; 23:8.

### Supporting E3 Units

**069, 091, 093, 095, 109, 119, 132, 202, 227, 229, 231, 243, 245, 266, 352, 394, 419**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- fulfil agreements;
- honour trusts and promises;
- do not betray agreements covertly;
- terminate agreements transparently;
- deceptive commitments damage systemic trust.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

A commitment need not be honoured where the agreement itself is illegitimate or where the agreed procedure permits lawful termination. The principle is integrity, not blind permanence.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Commitment integrity is strongly represented across all or nearly all traditions.

**Preliminary classification: Universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain as a major principle.**

# E3-N007 — Entrusted Responsibility

## Normalised Principle

**Return, preserve, and discharge entrusted responsibilities faithfully; authority over another person's property, information, role, or welfare creates duties rather than unrestricted discretion.**

### Operational Meaning

Trust converts possession or authority into stewardship. The holder's power is constrained by the purpose for which it was entrusted.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:58; 8:27; 23:8; 70:32.

### Supporting E3 Units

**055, 091, 132, 185, 227, 266, 394**, with stewardship links to **046, 170, 299, 381**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- return trusts;
- do not betray entrusted responsibilities;
- stewardship;
- fiduciary reliability;
- entrusted power is purpose-bound.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

This differs from N006 because trust can exist without an explicit promise and can concern property, office, information, or dependency.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong functional equivalents occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N008 — Integrity Between Declared Principle and Conduct

## Normalised Principle

**Do not claim ethical commitments, virtues, or standards that you knowingly refuse to practise.**

### Operational Meaning

Ethical language must not become reputational camouflage. Institutions should compare public commitments with observable conduct.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:44; 61:2–3; 107:4–7.

### Supporting E3 Units

**001, 177, 199, 388, 406**, with performative-giving material in **054**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- hypocrisy between instruction and conduct;
- do not say what you do not do;
- reject performative morality;
- do not use virtue for reputation while withholding substance.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

This principle concerns alignment, not perfection. A person can fail while sincerely trying; the target is knowing performative contradiction or institutionalised hypocrisy.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong equivalents appear across the comparison traditions.

**Preliminary classification: Universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N009 — Self-Critical Humility

## Normalised Principle

**Do not treat yourself, your status, or your moral identity as beyond correction.**

### Operational Meaning

Arrogance impairs learning and enables capture. Ethical systems should preserve the ability to receive correction irrespective of wealth, status, knowledge, or claimed virtue.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 17:37; 31:18–19; 53:32.

### Supporting E3 Units

**122, 149, 168, 256, 285, 298, 367, 379**, with related status material in **080, 125**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- reject arrogance;
- moderate bearing and voice;
- do not self-certify moral purity;
- status does not prove correctness;
- preserve corrigibility.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Humility does not require denial of genuine competence or achievement. It constrains superiority claims and resistance to correction.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Very strong functional equivalents occur throughout the comparison set.

**Preliminary classification: Universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N010 — Open and Discriminating Inquiry

## Normalised Principle

**Listen to relevant alternatives, evaluate them, and follow the best-supported course rather than closing inquiry prematurely.**

### Operational Meaning

Openness and judgement are complementary. Ethical reasoning should neither reject alternatives unheard nor accept every claim indiscriminately.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 39:18; related learning material at 18:66–70.

### Supporting E3 Units

**155, 260, 288, 369**, with responsive-listening material in **226, 417**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- listen broadly;
- evaluate alternatives;
- epistemic humility in learning;
- responsive listening;
- follow the best-supported option.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

This differs from N002. N002 constrains unsupported assertion; N010 establishes a positive process for learning and deliberation.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong functional equivalents occur in most traditions.

**Preliminary classification: Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**
