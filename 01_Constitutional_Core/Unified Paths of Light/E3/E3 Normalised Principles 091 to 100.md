# E3-N091 — Repair Harm Where Possible

## Normalised Principle

**Where wrongdoing has caused identifiable harm, accountability should include proportionate repair, restitution, compensation, or corrective action where practicable.**

### Operational Meaning

Acknowledgement alone may not restore what was damaged. Ethical accountability should seek to repair effects as well as judge conduct.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:92; 5:89; related property and obligation passages.

### Supporting E3 Units

**060, 078, 342**, with reform/accountability links **074, 142**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- compensation after accidental harm;
- expiation/repair after broken commitment;
- restitution;
- corrective action.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Repair must be proportionate and possible. Some harms cannot be fully reversed, and repair does not necessarily erase safeguarding needs.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Restitution and repair mechanisms occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N092 — Distinguish Accident From Deliberate Harm

## Normalised Principle

**Calibrate responsibility to intention, knowledge, negligence, and preventability rather than treating accidental and deliberate harm as morally identical.**

### Operational Meaning

A fair accountability system distinguishes causal involvement from culpability.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:92; 33:5.

### Supporting E3 Units

**060, 150, 368**, with responsibility-attribution support **213, 412**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- accidental harm;
- honest mistake;
- deliberate wrongdoing;
- intention-sensitive accountability;
- culpability calibration.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Accidental harm can still create duties of repair even where blame is reduced.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong equivalents occur throughout the comparison traditions.

**Preliminary classification: Universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N093 — Do Not Passively Legitimate Degrading Wrongdoing

## Normalised Principle

**Where presence, participation, or institutional endorsement would materially legitimise degrading wrongdoing, withdraw support or challenge the conduct rather than normalising it through passive participation.**

### Operational Meaning

Social legitimacy can be conferred by audiences and institutions as well as perpetrators. Non-participation can therefore be an ethical safeguard.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:140; 6:68.

### Supporting E3 Units

**067, 220**, with non-cooperation support **070, 057?** and **301**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- withdraw from degrading discourse;
- no passive legitimisation;
- non-cooperation;
- audience responsibility.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Withdrawal is not always the only response; intervention may be preferable where safe and effective.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong functional analogues occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N094 — Hospitality Has Boundaries

## Normalised Principle

**Respect hospitality, permission, and the host's legitimate boundaries; welcome does not create unlimited access or entitlement to remain.**

### Operational Meaning

Hospitality is reciprocal. Guests retain dignity, but hosts retain agency over private space and reasonable limits.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 24:27–29; 33:53.

### Supporting E3 Units

**136, 152, 272, 286, 359**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- permission before entry;
- accept refusal;
- do not overstay;
- household agency;
- guest/host reciprocity.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Emergency safeguarding can create exceptional duties, but ordinary hospitality does not extinguish consent.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Hospitality and host/guest duties occur across all comparison traditions.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N095 — Right of Refusal Must Be Respected

## Normalised Principle

**Where another person has legitimate authority to grant or withhold access, relationship, or participation, a refusal should be accepted without coercive retaliation.**

### Operational Meaning

Consent is meaningful only if “no” remains an available outcome.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 24:28; related 2:232 and 4:19.

### Supporting E3 Units

**020, 049, 136, 272, 359**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- accept refusal of entry;
- adult relational choice;
- non-coercion;
- permission.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

The right of refusal applies where the person legitimately controls the relevant choice. It does not authorise denial of another person's independent rights.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Functional consent analogues are widespread but historically variable.

**Preliminary classification: Common / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N096 — Developmental Agency Should Expand With Capability

## Normalised Principle

**Adjust protective controls as relevant capability matures, increasing autonomous control rather than preserving dependency indefinitely.**

### Operational Meaning

Protection is justified by vulnerability, not by a permanent entitlement of the protector to control the protected person.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:5–6; 24:58–59.

### Supporting E3 Units

**046, 139, 275, 360**, with capacity links **071**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- test readiness;
- return property control;
- maturity-sensitive privacy;
- graduated agency;
- protection-to-autonomy transition.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Capability can be domain-specific. Increased autonomy should track relevant competence rather than a simplistic all-or-nothing threshold.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Developmental responsibility is widespread; explicit agency-graduation formulations vary.

**Preliminary classification: Common.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N097 — Do Not Covet Another's Advantages Into Injustice

## Normalised Principle

**Do not allow comparison with another person's advantages to become entitlement, resentment, appropriation, or unjust interference.**

### Operational Meaning

Unequal outcomes can create destabilising comparison. Ethical response should focus on legitimate opportunity and one's own responsibilities rather than converting envy into harm.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:32.

### Supporting E3 Units

**051**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- anti-envy;
- no entitlement through comparison;
- focus on legitimate acquisition;
- non-appropriation.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

The principle does not prohibit criticism of unjust inequality. It distinguishes structural justice claims from personal covetousness.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Anti-covetousness and anti-envy are strongly represented across traditions.

**Preliminary classification: Universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N098 — Fair Credit and Recognition

## Normalised Principle

**Do not claim credit, praise, status, or reputation for achievements or virtues that are not genuinely yours.**

### Operational Meaning

Reputational resources can be misappropriated just as material resources can. False credit distorts trust, incentives, and allocation of responsibility.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 3:188.

### Supporting E3 Units

**042**, with performative-virtue links **054, 177, 199**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- false reputation;
- undeserved praise;
- credit integrity;
- status misappropriation.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Receiving genuine recognition is not prohibited. The wrong lies in deliberate misrepresentation or appropriation.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong honesty/humility analogues occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain provisionally; candidate for nesting under N001/N008 during final consolidation.**

# E3-N099 — Correction Should Not Become Humiliation

## Normalised Principle

**When correcting wrongdoing or error, preserve the person's dignity where doing so is compatible with truth, safety, and accountability.**

### Operational Meaning

Correction works best when it targets conduct rather than converting accountability into status destruction.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 3:159; 49:11; related forgiveness and reform passages.

### Supporting E3 Units

**040, 165, 295, 376**, with reform support **074, 142, 156**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- gentle correction;
- no degrading labels;
- reform-compatible accountability;
- dignity during correction.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Dignity does not require secrecy where public accountability is legitimately necessary.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong functional equivalents occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain provisionally.** Final review should test whether it is adequately represented by N037, N060, and N068.

# E3-N100 — Institutional Trust Is a Shared Resource

## Normalised Principle

**Protect the reliability of systems of promise, evidence, measurement, office, and procedure because individual abuses can degrade trust for everyone who depends on them.**

### Operational Meaning

Trust is not merely interpersonal. It is infrastructure. Repeated deception, corrupted measurement, bribery, false testimony, or arbitrary authority imposes external costs on future cooperation.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 16:91–94; 2:282–283; 55:7–9; related trust passages.

### Supporting E3 Units

**029, 030, 055, 082, 109, 169, 245, 352, 380, 394, 419**, with commercial integrity support **089, 120, 189**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- trust-network stability;
- reliable commitments;
- evidence integrity;
- measurement integrity;
- institutional reliability;
- anti-corruption.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

N100 is a systems-level synthesis of mechanisms retained separately elsewhere. It is not intended to erase those operational principles.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Trust as a condition of social order is strongly represented across the comparison traditions.

**Preliminary classification: Universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain as a systems-level principle.**

# Interim Normalisation Findings: E3-N091–N100

The provisional catalogue has now reached **100 normalised principles**.

The rate of genuinely new principle discovery has declined substantially. Several entries in this tranche are already marked as possible subordinate applications or second-order syntheses.

The next methodological task should therefore begin shifting from expansion toward a **coverage and consolidation audit**: mapping every retained source proposition to the provisional catalogue, identifying any genuinely unmapped principle families, and then performing the final merger/nesting pass.

The number 100 is not a target and does not imply completion. It is simply the present provisional catalogue size.
