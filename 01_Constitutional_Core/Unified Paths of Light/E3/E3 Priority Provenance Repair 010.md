# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Priority Provenance Repair 010 — Q030, Q031, Q034

## Q030 — Generosity Without Humiliation

The non-secure cells are Sikh P and Confucian C.

Sikh sharing and seva strongly establish generosity, but the frozen principle additionally prohibits degrading the recipient. The available parent evidence does not yet prove that anti-humiliation condition.

Confucian benevolence and ritual respect provide a plausible corollary but not an exact match.

**Decision:** retain Sikh P and Confucian C.

**Result:** Q030 remains **6/8**.

## Q031 — Non-Transactional Aid

Sikh seva provides particularly relevant evidence because service is normatively framed as selfless rather than as exchange for personal return. This is closer to the frozen principle than generic generosity.

**Decision:** Sikh P is **eligible for S** once the exact primary-text citation is normalised.

The African P remains unresolved because communal mutuality can include reciprocity and therefore does not automatically establish non-transactionality.

**Candidate result:** Q031 may move **6/8 → 7/8**.

## Q034 — Mutual Aid Under Scarcity

Sikh sharing and communal provision strongly support mutual aid. The narrower scarcity condition, however, must be demonstrated rather than inferred from ordinary sharing.

The Confucian row remains C because general reciprocity does not prove resource-sharing under scarcity.

**Decision:** retain Sikh P and Confucian C.

**Result:** Q034 remains **6/8**.
