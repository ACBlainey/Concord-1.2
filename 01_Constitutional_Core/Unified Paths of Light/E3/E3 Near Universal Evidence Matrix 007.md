# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Near-Universal Evidence Matrix 007

**Principles:** Q076, Q078, Q080, Q081, Q084.

---

## Q076 — Correct Corruption Rather Than Rename It Reform

Traditions widely condemn corruption, hypocrisy and false moral claims. E3-Q076 adds an epistemic/governance safeguard: actors must not legitimise harmful conduct merely by describing it as reform.

The parent anti-corruption ethic is extremely broad. The anti-rebranding rule is more specific.

**Decision:** **Common / Near-universal.** Do not count generic opposition to corruption as direct evidence for the full formulation.

## Q078 — Public Infrastructure Should Serve Public Purpose

Most traditions contain duties concerning rulers, common goods, roads, water, land, temples, welfare or communal resources. Yet the modern systems category “public infrastructure” is not a shared ancient textual category.

Functional evidence should concern whether common assets and institutions are expected to serve communal rather than private predatory purposes.

**Decision:** **Common.** Strongly defensible as an E3 systems synthesis, not as a direct near-universal textual formulation.

## Q080 — Do Not Obstruct Beneficial Action for Status or Gain

Opposition to envy, selfish obstruction, corrupt elites and status-seeking is widespread. The exact rule against blocking another person's beneficial action for private status/gain is more specific.

**Decision:** **Common / Near-universal.**

## Q081 — Do Not Exploit Addiction or Impaired Judgement

Traditions widely condemn intoxication, greed, exploitation and taking advantage of vulnerable persons. E3 joins these into a capacity-sensitive rule against exploiting impaired judgement.

The Psalm anchor did not recover an equivalent at comparable resolution.

**Decision:** **Common.** Broader sobriety and non-exploitation principles are much more recurrent.

## Q084 — Do Not Passively Legitimate Degrading Wrongdoing

Non-cooperation with wrongdoing is broad. Q084 concerns passive presence, silence or participation that lends legitimacy to degrading conduct.

This is more demanding than simply not committing the wrong oneself.

**Decision:** **Near-universal / Common.** Strongly related to Q055 but should not inherit Q055's prevalence automatically.

---

## Matrix 007 Finding

These principles are disproportionately **second-order safeguards**.

They govern not only primary wrongdoing, but:

- renaming wrongdoing;
- institutionalising private gain;
- obstructing good;
- exploiting reduced agency;
- and legitimating wrong through passive participation.

Such second-order rules may be less cross-traditionally frequent precisely because they operate one abstraction level above primary prohibitions.
