## Method Note

This tranche normalises correction, social bonds, constructive competition, moral association, public infrastructure, and anti-corruption themes not yet adequately represented as distinct principle identities.

# E3-N081 — Constructive Competition in Good

## Normalised Principle

**Where plurality exists, channel competition toward beneficial conduct rather than identity superiority or destructive domination.**

### Operational Meaning

Competition need not be eliminated. It can be redirected from status conflict toward measurable contribution and good works.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:148; 5:48.

### Supporting E3 Units

**204, 216, 328**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- compete in good;
- plural pathways with constructive rivalry;
- contribution rather than identity superiority;
- prosocial competition.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Competition remains constrained by non-harm, fairness, and anti-capture principles.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Functional equivalents occur widely, though explicit competitive framing varies.

**Preliminary classification: Common / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N082 — Preserve Social Bonds Against Needless Severance

## Normalised Principle

**Do not break legitimate relationships, covenants, or connective social bonds without sufficient reason, especially where severance contributes to wider corruption or fragmentation.**

### Operational Meaning

Civilisation depends on connective tissue. Trust, kinship, institutions, and cooperation can be damaged by irresponsible severance as well as by direct aggression.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:27; 13:25.

### Supporting E3 Units

**107, 202, 243, 332**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- severing what should remain connected;
- covenant rupture;
- fragmentation;
- relationship maintenance;
- social continuity.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

The principle does not require preservation of abusive or illegitimate relationships. Legitimate exit remains protected.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong relational and covenant analogues occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N083 — Correct Corruption Rather Than Rename It Reform

## Normalised Principle

**Evaluate claimed reform by its actual effects and do not legitimise destructive conduct merely by describing it as improvement.**

### Operational Meaning

Language can conceal system degradation. Institutions need outcome-sensitive checks against actors who appropriate reform language while producing corruption.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:11–12.

### Supporting E3 Units

**201, 331**, with corruption material **088, 102, 224, 239**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- corruption presented as reform;
- label/effect distinction;
- false reform claims;
- outcome-sensitive governance.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Bad outcomes alone do not prove malicious intent. The principle requires evaluation rather than automatic acceptance of self-description.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong functional analogues occur widely, though wording differs.

**Preliminary classification: Near-universal / Common.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N084 — Good Association Supports Ethical Conduct

## Normalised Principle

**Cultivate relationships and communities that reinforce truth, patience, compassion, and constructive conduct.**

### Operational Meaning

Ethical behaviour is socially conditioned. Association can strengthen or weaken the ability to sustain good conduct over time.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 9:119; 90:17; 103:3.

### Supporting E3 Units

**099, 192, 196, 236, 321, 323, 348, 400, 403**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- keep company with truthful people;
- mutual encouragement;
- community reinforcement;
- ethical culture;
- social scaffolding for virtue.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Association should not become ideological isolation. N010 protects open inquiry and N042 protects plural coexistence.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Very strong equivalents occur across the comparison set.

**Preliminary classification: Universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N085 — Public Infrastructure Should Serve Public Purpose

## Normalised Principle

**Where power and resources are mobilised for shared infrastructure or public protection, use them for the legitimate common purpose rather than converting them into private enrichment.**

### Operational Meaning

Large cooperative projects concentrate power and resources. Their legitimacy depends on purpose-bound administration and resistance to capture.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 18:94–95.

### Supporting E3 Units

**262**, with common-resource and stewardship links **055, 225, 303, 416**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- cooperative infrastructure;
- public purpose;
- anti-private capture;
- contribution toward common protection;
- purpose-bound administration.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

The source is narrative, but unlike excluded Unit 126 the extracted ethic does not depend on inaccessible hidden knowledge; it concerns observable cooperation and refusal to turn entrusted capacity into private payment.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Public-purpose and stewardship analogues occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Common / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N086 — Make Space for Legitimate Participation

## Normalised Principle

**In shared assemblies and institutions, make reasonable space for others to participate rather than using position or crowding to monopolise access.**

### Operational Meaning

Participation can be denied through mundane physical or procedural mechanisms even without explicit exclusion.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 58:11.

### Supporting E3 Units

**172, 302, 383**, with anti-status inclusion support **219, 318, 414**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- make room;
- access to assembly;
- participation;
- non-monopolisation of shared space;
- recognition of knowledge.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Access may still be governed by legitimate capacity, safety, or role constraints.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Hospitality and assembly-access analogues occur widely, though exact institutional framing varies.

**Preliminary classification: Common.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain provisionally; candidate for nesting under N040 during final consolidation.**

# E3-N087 — Recognise Knowledge Without Converting It Into Domination

## Normalised Principle

**Value genuine knowledge and competence while preserving humility, access, and the corrigibility of those who possess it.**

### Operational Meaning

Expertise deserves recognition because it improves decisions, but expertise must not become a claim to inherent superiority or immunity from correction.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 58:11; 39:18.

### Supporting E3 Units

**155, 172, 288, 302, 369, 383**, with competence material **104, 145**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- recognise knowledge;
- learn from expertise;
- epistemic humility;
- non-dominating expertise;
- competence without caste.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Equal dignity does not require equal epistemic weight on every technical question.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong equivalents occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N088 — Do Not Obstruct Beneficial Action for Status or Gain

## Normalised Principle

**Do not deliberately block legitimate beneficial action merely to preserve status, control, factional advantage, or private gain.**

### Operational Meaning

Harm can occur by obstruction as well as direct aggression. Gatekeeping becomes corrupt when it protects the gatekeeper rather than the legitimate purpose of the system.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 68:10–13; related 107:1–7.

### Supporting E3 Units

**182, 311, 391**, with practical-help material **198, 199, 325, 326**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- obstruction of good;
- gatekeeping;
- status-protective blockage;
- denial of ordinary assistance.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Not every rejected proposal is obstruction. Legitimate quality, safety, evidence, and resource constraints remain valid.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong functional equivalents occur in most traditions.

**Preliminary classification: Near-universal / Common.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N089 — Wealth Must Not Become Impunity

## Normalised Principle

**Do not allow material wealth or accumulated status to shield a person from standards, correction, or accountability that apply to others.**

### Operational Meaning

Economic power can become political, informational, or reputational immunity unless institutions actively preserve equal accountability.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 104:1–3; related 5:8 and 4:135.

### Supporting E3 Units

**066, 071, 197, 215, 324, 404**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- wealth is not immunity;
- status does not cancel accountability;
- equal standards;
- anti-oligarchic capture.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

The principle does not deny legitimate differences in responsibility or procedural protection. It rejects privilege unrelated to the merits of the case.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong equivalents occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain provisionally.** It overlaps N016 but captures a specific capture mechanism important enough for final review.

# E3-N090 — Do Not Exploit Addiction or Impaired Judgement

## Normalised Principle

**Avoid practices that predictably impair judgement, generate dependency, or convert impaired agency into conflict and exploitation.**

### Operational Meaning

Agency can be degraded chemically, behaviourally, or socially. Ethical systems should recognise impairment as a vulnerability rather than an opportunity for extraction.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 5:90–91.

### Supporting E3 Units

**079**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- intoxicant-related impairment;
- addiction;
- gambling-like dependency;
- conflict generated through impaired judgement;
- protection of agency.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

The normalised principle is functional rather than a technical classification of every substance or activity. Evidence of impairment, dependency, and harm matters.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Temperance and anti-addiction analogues occur widely, though specific prohibitions differ.

**Preliminary classification: Common / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**
