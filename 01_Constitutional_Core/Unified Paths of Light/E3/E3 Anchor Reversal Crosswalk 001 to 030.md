## Interim Finding

The first 30 E3 principles already show substantial structural convergence with the Psalm synthesis, but they also reveal a major **granularity asymmetry**.

The Psalm Paper IV summary tends to state broad ethical heuristics such as truthfulness, non-harm, vulnerable protection, humility, promise keeping, and restraint toward wealth. E3 often decomposes these into institutional sub-rules: verification, testimony, attribution, procedure, fiduciary protection, debtor relief, and commercial measurement.

This is not yet evidence that those sub-rules are absent from the Psalm extraction.

All AR-U rows remain unresolved until checked against the detailed Psalm analyses.
