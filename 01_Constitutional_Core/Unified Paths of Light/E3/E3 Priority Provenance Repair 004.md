## Priority Provenance Repair 004 — Q062, Q067, Q072

## Q062 — Reciprocal Courtesy

The remaining Sikh P cannot be upgraded merely from general kindness, service or humility. Those establish regard for others, but the frozen principle is explicitly reciprocal.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P** pending a primary Sikh source that clearly expresses reciprocal treatment/regard.

**Result:** Q062 remains **7/8 secure**.

## Q067 — Avoid Self-Destructive Extremes

Sikh primary evidence is substantially stronger for the parent moderation/self-control ethic. SGGS Ang 1057 explicitly links truth with self-control and the removal of ego and anger; SGGS Ang 939 likewise places contentment and self-control together. These support disciplined moderation rather than self-mortifying excess.

However, Q067 is phrased as avoidance of **self-destructive extremes**. The verified passages establish self-control and moderation but do not by themselves prove the full frozen formulation.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P**, with the documentary gap narrowed.

**Result:** Q067 remains **7/8 secure**.

## Q072 — Future-Consequence Awareness

SGGS Ang 36 explicitly links wrongful deeds with the consequences generated by those deeds. Sikh teaching also repeatedly frames conduct in relation to its consequences.

This is materially closer to Q072 than a generic wisdom citation. The frozen principle requires awareness of future consequence, and the source establishes consequential moral causation.

**Source-quality:** SQ-A primary scripture.  
**Functional grade:** A/B.

**Decision:** Sikh row is eligible for **S — Secure**.

**Result:** Q072 becomes a candidate **8/8 Universal** row after controlled matrix update.
