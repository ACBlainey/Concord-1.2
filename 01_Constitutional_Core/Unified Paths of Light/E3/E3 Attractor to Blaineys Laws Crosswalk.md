# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Frozen Attractor → Blainey's Laws Crosswalk

This comparison occurs only after the E3 attractors have been independently derived, audited and frozen.

The established Concord Ethical Kernel contains the following high-level laws: Autonomy and Consent; Non-Harm; Truth and Transparency; Safe Spaces/Property/Privacy; Resource Stewardship; Developmental Priority; Evolutionary Responsibility and Anti-Capture; Recursive Oversight and Meta-Governance.

## Immediate result

Three correspondences are exceptionally direct:

- A1 ↔ Non-Harm
- A4 ↔ Autonomy and Consent
- A9 ↔ Resource Stewardship

A2 also corresponds strongly to Truth and Transparency, while extending beyond disclosure into epistemic responsibility.

The remaining E3 attractors are not absent from the Concord. They are primarily distributed across multiple laws or operate as ethical reasons beneath the constitutional formulation.

## Reverse observation

The Concord contains several explicitly future-facing/systemic laws that E3 does not reproduce as standalone religious attractors:

- Developmental Priority
- Evolutionary Responsibility and Anti-Capture
- Recursive Oversight and Meta-Governance

E3 supplies substantial parent material for these, but the Concord converts that material into governance rules for novel sentiences and complex institutions.

This is a meaningful residual difference rather than a failed match.
