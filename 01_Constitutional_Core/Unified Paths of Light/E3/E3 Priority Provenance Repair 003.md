# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Priority Provenance Repair 003 — Q055, Q058, Q059

---

## Q055 — Do Not Cooperate in Wrongdoing

The Sikh row is presently provisional.

Primary Sikh material strongly condemns falsehood, deceit, anger and slander, and the tradition strongly values truthful living and virtuous association. However, abstaining from one's own wrongdoing is not identical to the frozen **non-cooperation/complicity** rule.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P** until an exact source establishes refusal to assist another's wrongdoing or an equivalent complicity norm.

**Result:** **Q055 remains 7/8 secure.**

---

## Q058 — Leadership Through Gentleness and Correctability

The Sikh tradition provides strong humility and service material. Exact SGGS passages include humility and self-restraint, while broader Sikh leadership traditions strongly emphasise service.

But Q058 is a compound leadership principle: **gentleness plus correctability**. Personal humility alone is not sufficient evidence for correctable leadership.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P**.

African/Ubuntu leadership scholarship supplies supporting evidence for service, patience, empathy, accountability, transparency and community-oriented leadership, consistent with the existing secure African row.

**Result:** **Q058 remains 7/8 secure.**

---

## Q059 — Use Speech to Reduce Rather Than Manufacture Conflict

Sikh primary evidence is stronger here than generic concept-level sourcing. SGGS Ang 461 explicitly places slander among conduct to be renounced, while Sikh ethical teaching strongly condemns hostility and harmful speech.

Nevertheless, Q059 is not merely “do not slander”; it is the broader functional rule that speech should reduce rather than manufacture conflict.

The evidence is strong but the present repair does not establish that full compound with sufficient precision to erase the remaining provenance caution.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P** for now; mark as high-priority exact-source candidate.

**Result:** **Q059 remains 7/8 secure.**

---

## Repair 003 Result

None of Q055, Q058 or Q059 is promoted simply because nearby Sikh parent ethics are strong.

The audit continues to preserve the central distinction:

> **A source that proves the moral parent does not automatically prove the operational child.**

> **End of Priority Provenance Repair 003**
