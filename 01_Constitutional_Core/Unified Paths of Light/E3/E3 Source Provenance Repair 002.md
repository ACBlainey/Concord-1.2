# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Source Provenance Repair 002 — African Ethical Evidence

**Scope:** Replace generic “African Traditional” claims with scoped, identifiable evidence.

---

## 1. Why This Repair Is Different

African Traditional Religions / African ethical traditions cannot be audited as though they possess one scripture equivalent to the Bible, Qur'an, Guru Granth Sahib or Bhagavad Gita.

The correct unit is therefore **identified tradition + identified evidence**, not a fictitious pan-African canon.

## 2. Approved Scholarly Orientation

The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy's **African Ethics** synthesis documents, with explicit scope and examples, recurring African ethical emphases including:

- character and moral personhood;
- social harmony and cooperative living;
- justice and fairness;
- duty and common good;
- human welfare;
- generosity;
- honesty and truthfulness;
- faithfulness;
- compassion;
- hospitality;
- peace;
- obligations to kin and neighbours.

It also gives specific Akan material rather than relying only on generic continental claims.

This is suitable as SQ-B/C orientation evidence depending on the exact proposition. Where a named maxim or tradition is identified, the evidence can be stronger.

## 3. Specific Akan Dignity Anchor

A documented Akan maxim states, in translation:

> **The human being is more beautiful than gold.**

The associated ethical interpretation places human value above material wealth and connects that value with compassion, generosity and hospitality.

### E3 Use

Strong scoped evidence for the parent content of:

- Q035 Human Dignity Beyond Status;
- Q029 Wealth Carries Social Obligations, as supporting rather than standalone proof;
- Q032 Positive Duty Toward Severe Need, where joined with documented duty/common-good material.

This should be labelled **Akan**, not simply “African.”

## 4. Duty and Common-Good Anchor

The scholarly synthesis characterises documented African ethics as strongly social and duty-oriented, with obligations to others and the common good central to moral life.

### E3 Use

Strong functional support for:

- Q029 Wealth Carries Social Obligations;
- Q032 Positive Duty Toward Severe Need;
- Q034 Mutual Aid Under Scarcity at parent mutual-aid level;
- Q048 Reciprocal Relational Rights and Responsibilities, preferably described as reciprocal relational **obligations** where rights-language would overstate the source;
- Q051 Authority Is a Trust, Not Ownership only where additional political/leadership evidence is supplied.

## 5. Hospitality Anchor

Documented African ethical discussion treats hospitality and generosity as expressions of common humanity and social obligation.

### E3 Use

This can securely support hospitality as a parent ethic.

It **cannot by itself** establish:

- Q085 Hospitality Has Boundaries;
- Q086 Right of Refusal Must Be Respected.

Those require separate boundary evidence.

## 6. Truth and Trust Anchor

Truthfulness, honesty and faithfulness are documented among African moral goods.

### E3 Use

This supports:

- Q001 Truthful Communication;
- Q006 Promise/Commitment Integrity at the broad faithfulness/trust level.

It does not automatically establish formal evidentiary procedure or every covenant mechanism.

## 7. Required Labelling Change

Future matrices should avoid:

**African Traditional — supports X**

and prefer formulations such as:

- **Akan ethical thought — [identified maxim/concept]**
- **Ubuntu/Hunhu scholarship — [identified proposition]**
- **Yoruba ethical/political tradition — [identified source]**
- **African ethics scholarly synthesis — scoped recurrent claim**

## 8. Audit Consequence

Generic African rows previously carrying SQ-C should not automatically be discarded. They should be treated as **repair candidates**.

Where the underlying proposition can be tied to named evidence, upgrade it. Where it cannot, leave it provisional or unproven.

This prevents both overgeneralisation and the opposite error of treating non-scriptural ethical traditions as evidentially empty.

> **End of Source Provenance Repair 002**
