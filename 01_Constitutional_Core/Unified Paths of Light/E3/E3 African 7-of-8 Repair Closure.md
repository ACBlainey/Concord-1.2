Q001, Q015, Q031, Q060, Q063, Q064.

## Outcome

**Promoted:** 0

The reason is not absence of related African ethics. In every case, relevant functional material exists. The limiting issue is whether the evidence presently available and normalised demonstrates the **exact frozen E3-Q proposition** with sufficient named provenance.

## Methodological consequence

This produces a useful asymmetry:

- Sikh repair yielded several upgrades once exact SGGS locations were supplied.
- African repair is more resistant because the evidence architecture is plural, oral/customary as well as textual, and highly sensitive to named-tradition scope.

The appropriate response is not to lower the standard. It is to preserve P until stronger localised evidence is documented.
