# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Universal / Near-Universal Boundary Evidence Matrix 005

**Principles:** Q057, Q058, Q059, Q062, Q072.

---

## Q057 — Social Cohesion Without Suppression of Difference

Community, harmony and unity are widespread ethical goods. The qualification **without suppression of difference** is much more specific and protects plurality against forced conformity.

Some traditions strongly support plural coexistence; others historically frame cohesion through shared doctrine, role or communal identity.

**Decision:** **Near-universal / Common at exact formulation. Universal claims should be restricted to the parent ethic of social cohesion.**

## Q058 — Leadership Through Gentleness and Correctability

Restraint, humility, benevolence and responsiveness in leadership recur widely. Confucian benevolent rulership and remonstrance provide especially strong functional parallels; biblical/Jewish leadership ethics constrain rulers; Sikh and African traditions contain service/relational models of authority.

The combined requirement that leaders be both gentle and correctable is narrower.

**Decision:** **Near-universal at functional level.**

## Q059 — Use Speech to Reduce Rather Than Manufacture Conflict

Truthful, restrained and non-harmful speech has broad support. Buddhist right speech, biblical/Jewish speech ethics, Confucian propriety, Sikh truthful speech and South Asian restraint traditions all supply functional routes. African ethics should be grounded in a named reconciliation or speech tradition rather than a generic harmony claim.

**Decision:** **Strong Near-universal; possible Universal parent principle of conflict-reducing speech after provenance completion.**

## Q062 — Reciprocal Courtesy

Versions of reciprocity are exceptionally widespread. The specific form of courtesy varies by culture, and hierarchical traditions may encode asymmetrical etiquette while still requiring reciprocal moral regard.

**Decision:** **Near-universal at exact courtesy level; reciprocal regard is a stronger Universal candidate.**

## Q072 — Future-Consequence Awareness

Traditions widely teach that conduct has consequences, whether through divine judgement, karma, character formation, social effects or prudential wisdom.

The metaphysics differ substantially, but E3-Q072 is intentionally behavioural: decisions should consider future effects.

**Decision:** **Strong Universal candidate at functional level. The comparison must not pretend that the causal/metaphysical accounts are identical.**

## Matrix 005 Finding

This block demonstrates one of the advantages of functional normalisation.

A principle can converge strongly even where traditions disagree over **why** it is true.

Future-consequence awareness can be supported by divine accountability, karma, cultivation, social causation or prudence without treating those explanatory systems as equivalent.
