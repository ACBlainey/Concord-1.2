# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Source Provenance Repair 001 — Hindu Evidence Anchors

**Scope:** High-confidence Bhagavad Gita anchors that can repair multiple comparative rows without overgeneralisation.

---

## Verified Anchor H1 — Bhagavad Gita 16.2

The verse explicitly groups:

- non-injury;
- truthfulness;
- absence/control of anger;
- peacefulness;
- compassion/kindness toward beings;
- non-covetousness;
- gentleness;
- modesty.

### Secure E3 Uses

This is strong direct Hindu evidence for the parent content of:

- Q001 Truthful Communication;
- Q011 Protection of Life / non-injury;
- Q014 Peace and De-Escalation at the broad peacefulness level;
- Q064 Control Anger Before It Controls Action;
- Q088 Do Not Covet Another's Advantages Into Injustice.

It also supplies supporting evidence for compassion/gentleness clusters, but should not be stretched into procedural rules that the verse does not state.

**Source quality:** SQ-A for the textual claims above.

## Verified Anchor H2 — Bhagavad Gita 13.8

The verse explicitly includes:

- humility;
- unpretentiousness;
- non-injury;
- forbearance;
- sincerity/uprightness;
- steadiness;
- self-control.

### Secure E3 Uses

Strong direct Hindu evidence for:

- Q009 Self-Critical Humility at the humility/unpretentiousness parent level;
- Q011 Protection of Life / non-injury;
- Q063 Patience Under Difficulty through forbearance;
- Q067 Avoid Self-Destructive Extremes only as supporting self-control evidence, not as a complete direct equivalent.

**Source quality:** SQ-A for humility, non-injury, forbearance and self-control.

## Verified Anchor H3 — Bhagavad Gita 10.4

The verse includes intelligence, knowledge, non-delusion, forbearance, truth, self-restraint and calmness among the listed qualities.

### Secure E3 Uses

Supporting Hindu evidence for:

- Q001 Truthful Communication;
- Q002 Epistemic Responsibility at the broad knowledge/non-delusion level;
- Q063 Patience Under Difficulty;
- Q067 moderation/self-control parent cluster.

It does **not** independently prove Q003 verification procedure or Q017 procedural fairness.

## Repair Principle

These anchors demonstrate why one precise source can legitimately support several normalised principles while still respecting boundaries.

The correct method is:

> **reuse the verified passage where its actual behavioural content recurs; do not invent a new source merely to make every row look unique.**
