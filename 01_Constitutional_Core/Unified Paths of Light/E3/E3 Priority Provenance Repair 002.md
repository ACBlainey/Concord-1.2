# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Priority Provenance Repair 002 — Q044, Q048, Q051

---

## Q044 — Child and Dependant Welfare

The current 7/8 row is held below Universal by unresolved Sikh provenance.

Sikh ethics clearly supports compassion, service, sharing and human equality. However, those parent duties do not automatically prove the narrower frozen principle concerning **children and dependants as a protected class**.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P** until an exact primary or authoritative Sikh source explicitly establishes care/protection duties toward children or dependants.

**Result:** **Q044 remains 7/8 secure.**

---

## Q048 — Reciprocal Relational Rights and Responsibilities

### Sikh evidence

Sikh sources strongly establish service, sharing, truthful conduct and social equality. These demonstrate relational duties, but Q048 is specifically reciprocal.

The present evidence is sufficient to support a relational-obligation parent, but this repair does not yet establish an exact reciprocal-rights-and-responsibilities formulation from a primary Sikh passage.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P**.

### African evidence

The African row is materially stronger. The scoped Akan evidence explicitly describes **reciprocity and interdependence** and states that social morality demands mutuality or reciprocity. It also describes a dual moral responsibility for oneself and for others as co-members of the community.

**Decision:** African **S** remains secure with named Akan/scholarly scope.

**Result:** **Q048 remains 7/8 secure.**

---

## Q051 — Authority Is a Trust, Not Ownership

The present Sikh P cannot be upgraded merely from humility, service or equality. Those values constrain authority but do not necessarily establish the frozen trusteeship distinction.

African evidence can support service-oriented and common-good authority, but the matrix already counts that row secure. Contemporary Ubuntu leadership scholarship further describes leadership as service to community, prioritisation of community interests, accountability and responsible resource use; this is supporting rather than canonical evidence.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P** pending exact Sikh authority/trusteeship evidence.

**Result:** **Q051 remains 7/8 secure.**
