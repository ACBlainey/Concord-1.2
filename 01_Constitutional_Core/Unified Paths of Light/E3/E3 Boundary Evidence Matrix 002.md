# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Universal / Near-Universal Boundary Evidence Matrix 002

**Principles:** Q021, Q023, Q028, Q029, Q030.

---

## Q021 — Property by Legitimate Consent

Respect for possession, theft prohibitions and fair exchange are widespread. The narrower requirement that transfer be grounded in mutual consent is explicit in some traditions and implicit in others. African communitarian ethics can strongly protect welfare and social obligations while conceptualising property differently from individual-rights systems.

**Decision:** **Near-universal functional principle; do not claim secure 8/8 on identical property theory.**

## Q023 — No Bribery or Corrupt Purchase of Decision

Biblical/Jewish evidence against bribery is direct. Corrupt gain and compromised judgement are strongly condemned in several other traditions, but an exact bribery prohibition requires source-by-source verification. Generic anti-corruption is not enough.

**Decision:** **Near-universal / Common boundary pending exact bribery evidence.**

## Q028 — Resource Stewardship, Non-Waste, and Balanced Use

Non-waste, moderation, restraint of greed and responsible use recur widely, but this final E3 principle combines several dimensions. A tradition may strongly support moderation without an explicit stewardship doctrine, or stewardship without the same balance rule.

**Decision:** **Near-universal as a compound principle; several components may be universal separately.**

## Q029 — Wealth Carries Social Obligations

This principle survives source scrutiny particularly well. Biblical and Jewish poor-relief duties are direct; Buddhist generosity, Hindu *dana*, Sikh sharing/service and Confucian benevolence provide strong functional equivalents. African ethics provides unusually strong support: documented communitarian ethics explicitly places duties to others, compassion, solidarity, reciprocity and welfare at the centre of moral life.

**Decision:** **Strong Universal candidate at functional level, subject to precise citation normalisation.**

## Q030 — Generosity Without Humiliation

Generosity is exceptionally widespread. The anti-humiliation constraint is more specific. Traditions may require generous aid while not separately articulate preservation of the recipient's dignity in the same passage.

**Decision:** **Near-universal at exact formulation; universal parent ethic of generosity is much stronger.**

## Matrix 002 Finding

Economic ethics demonstrates why the final project should report **nested universality**.

For example:

- generosity may be Universal;
- generosity without humiliation may be Near-universal;
- structured social provision may be Common/Near-universal.

These are not contradictions. They are different levels of ethical resolution.
