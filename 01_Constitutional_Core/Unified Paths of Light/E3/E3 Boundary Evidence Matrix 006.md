# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Universal / Near-Universal Boundary Evidence Matrix 006

**Principles:** Q073, Q075, Q079, Q082, Q085.

---

## Q073 — Resilience Under Gain and Loss

Patience, non-attachment, constancy, contentment and endurance provide strong parallels across the comparison traditions. Traditions differ over whether resilience is grounded in providence, karma, disciplined mind, duty, communal identity or another framework.

**Decision:** **Near-universal / strong Universal candidate at functional level.**

## Q075 — Preserve Social Bonds Against Needless Severance

Family/community solidarity, reconciliation, harmony and maintenance of proper relations are widespread. The word **needless** is important: E3 does not require preservation of abusive or unjust relationships.

Not every source makes that boundary equally explicit.

**Decision:** **Near-universal at the exact bounded formulation; social-bond preservation itself is broader.**

## Q079 — Recognise Knowledge Without Converting It Into Domination

Respect for learning and wisdom is extremely widespread; anti-arrogance is also widespread. E3 combines them into a systems principle: epistemic advantage must not become domination.

The components converge more strongly than the composite formulation.

**Decision:** **Near-universal / Common as a composite; do not count separate respect-for-knowledge and humility passages as automatic direct equivalence.**

## Q082 — Repair Harm Where Possible

Restitution, repentance, restoration, reconciliation and corrective action provide broad functional support. Jewish law and restorative African justice provide particularly direct routes; other traditions may frame repair through restitution, compassion, confession or restored relationship.

**Decision:** **Near-universal and potentially Universal at broad restorative level. Exact repair duties vary.**

## Q085 — Hospitality Has Boundaries

Hospitality and care for guests/strangers are widespread, but Q085 is specifically a **boundary** principle: hospitality does not erase the host's consent, privacy or right to end access.

Many traditions strongly teach hospitality without equally explicit host-boundary doctrine.

**Decision:** **Common / Near-universal. The preliminary Universal/Near-universal label should be treated conservatively.**

---

## Matrix 006 Finding

The final two matrices in this pass reinforce the audit's most persistent lesson:

> **Component universality does not prove composite universality.**

Knowledge + humility does not automatically equal an explicit anti-domination rule. Hospitality + generosity does not automatically establish a right to terminate access.

The final prevalence table must therefore count the **actual normalised principle**, not whichever broader parent ethic makes it easiest to reach eight traditions.

> **End of Boundary Evidence Matrix 006**
