# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Reverse Crosswalk — Published Psalm Synthesis → Frozen Qur'an-Derived Catalogue

**Purpose:** Prevent one-directional measurement bias.

---

## 1. Method

The forward crosswalk asks:

> How much of the Qur'an-derived behavioural catalogue is independently represented in the Psalm extraction?

The reverse crosswalk asks:

> How much of the published Psalm synthesis is independently represented in the frozen Qur'an-derived behavioural catalogue?

Paper IV contains **30 published Tier 1–3 principles**: 10 universal, 10 near-universal and 10 common. Several are theological rather than substrate-neutral behavioural rules. They are retained here rather than silently discarded.

Seven of the ten published Psalm universals have clear behavioural counterparts. Three contain substantial theological/devotional content whose behavioural consequences may converge but whose propositions are intentionally not reproduced by E3's behavioural normalisation.

## 5. Reverse-Direction Finding

The reverse comparison reveals why raw catalogue overlap would be misleading.

The principal Psalm-side “missing” items are disproportionately:

- praise and worship;
- divine/moral-order protection;
- divine judgement;
- divine establishment of leaders;
- vindication;
- restoration;
- devotional seeking.

These were precisely the kinds of propositions E3's extraction method was designed to exclude when they could not be separated into a general behavioural ethic.

Conversely, the forward comparison's Qur'an-side differences are disproportionately procedural: consent, privacy, evidence, capacity, institutional process and conflict safeguards.

The asymmetry is therefore intelligible:

> **Psalm-specific residuals are predominantly devotional/theological; Qur'an-specific residuals are predominantly procedural/operational.**

That is a much more informative finding than a simple overlap percentage.
