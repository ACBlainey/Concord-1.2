## Resolution Finding

This block identifies a meaningful anchor asymmetry rather than merely a difference in wording.

The Qur'an-derived catalogue independently develops a much denser architecture around **privacy, conscience, consent, relationship dissolution, consultation, procedural disagreement and confidentiality**. The Psalm extraction supplies strong higher-order constraints—non-harm, truth, humility, just authority, care and unity—but often does not decompose them into equivalent operational rules.

These AR-D classifications are therefore retained rather than forced into broad umbrella matches.
