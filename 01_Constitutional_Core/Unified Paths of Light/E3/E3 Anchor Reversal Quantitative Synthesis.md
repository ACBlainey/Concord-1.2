# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Anchor-Reversal Quantitative Synthesis

**Dataset:** 89 frozen Qur'an-derived top-level principles  
**Comparison:** Detailed Psalm 1–150 extraction  
**Status:** Post-resolution synthesis

---

## 1. Counting Rule

Four corpus-resolution rows retained boundary grades: three **AR-A/B** and one **AR-B/C**. To avoid inflating convergence, this synthesis assigns each boundary case to the **weaker** category:

- AR-A/B → AR-B
- AR-B/C → AR-C

This produces a conservative single-grade distribution.

## 2. Final Distribution

| Grade | Meaning | Count | Share of 89 |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **AR-A** | Direct functional match | 9 | 10.1% |
| **AR-B** | Strong functional match | 38 | 42.7% |
| **AR-C** | Cluster-level convergence | 24 | 27.0% |
| **AR-D** | Anchor-specific or weakly represented | 18 | 20.2% |
| **Total** |  | **89** | **100%** |

### Conservative aggregate measures

- **Direct + strong functional convergence (A+B): 47/89 = 52.8%.**
- **Any substantive functional convergence (A+B+C): 71/89 = 79.8%.**
- **Weakly represented / anchor-specific at this resolution (D): 18/89 = 20.2%.**

These are not claims that 79.8% of the two scriptures are “the same.” They describe the proportion of the **frozen Qur'an-derived behavioural catalogue** for which the independent Psalm extraction supplies at least cluster-level functional convergence.

## 3. Interpretation

The most important result is structural rather than numerical.

More than half of the Qur'an-derived catalogue has a direct or strong functional counterpart in the independently produced Psalm extraction, despite the reversal of anchor and the deliberate methodological firewall.

A further group converges at cluster level but differs in granularity. Typical examples are:

- broad Psalmic truth ethics versus Qur'anic evidence/verification rules;
- broad justice versus explicit procedural mechanisms;
- care for vulnerable people versus detailed fiduciary/resource rules;
- moral restraint versus explicit conflict procedure;
- humility and constrained rulership versus institutional anti-capture rules.

The AR-D group is therefore especially informative. It identifies where the Qur'an-first extraction adds operational distinctions not independently recovered from the Psalter at comparable resolution.

## 4. Principal AR-D Concentrations

The 18 conservative AR-D principles cluster disproportionately around:

- verification before consequential action;
- procedural fairness;
- privacy and intrusive surveillance;
- freedom of conscience and non-compulsion;
- coexistence without forced sameness;
- intimate consent;
- relationship dissolution;
- rule-governed dispute resolution;
- constrained confidential deliberation;
- capacity-calibrated obligation;
- avoidance of manufactured burdens;
- conduct-based distinction between peaceful difference and active threat;
- safe passage for opponents;
- public-purpose infrastructure;
- impaired-judgement exploitation;
- accident versus deliberate harm;
- refusal rights;
- developmental agency.

This pattern matters more than the raw number. The main asymmetry is not that one anchor possesses morality and the other does not. It is that the Qur'an-derived catalogue frequently **operationalises broad ethical constraints into procedural and institutional rules**.

## 5. What the Numbers Do Not Establish

The distribution does not establish:

- textual identity;
- equal source frequency;
- equal theological justification;
- equal historical application;
- equal emphasis;
- or causal proof that these ethics produced civilizational longevity.

It establishes a narrower and more defensible result: substantial **independent functional recurrence** under anchor reversal.

## 6. Provisional Anchor-Robustness Finding

The anchor-reversal experiment survives its first quantitative test.

The Qur'an-first method did not reproduce an unrelated ethical catalogue. Nor did it simply recreate the Psalm catalogue one-for-one. Instead it independently recovered a large shared behavioural architecture while adding a significant layer of procedural specificity.

That is the result expected from genuine structural convergence with anchor-dependent resolution.

> **71 of 89 Qur'an-derived principles show at least cluster-level convergence with the independently extracted Psalm corpus; 47 show direct or strong functional convergence under a conservative grading rule.**
