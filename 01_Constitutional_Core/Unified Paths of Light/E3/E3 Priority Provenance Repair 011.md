# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Priority Provenance Repair 011 — Q036, Q043, Q049

## Q036 — No Ridicule, Defamation, or Degrading Labels

Sikh primary ethics strongly condemns slander and degrading speech. SGGS material previously identified in the audit provides direct support for renouncing slander.

Because Q036 is a compound containing ridicule, defamation and degrading labels, a slander passage proves a major component but not necessarily the entire formulation.

**Decision:** retain Sikh P unless the final source register supplies direct coverage for the broader compound. African P likewise remains unresolved.

**Result:** Q036 remains **6/8**.

## Q043 — Family Respect With Preserved Moral Agency

Sikh family ethics and human equality support family respect, while Sikh moral agency resists status-based domination. Yet the frozen principle specifically combines familial respect with preservation of independent moral agency.

**Decision:** retain Sikh P and African P; do not construct the compound from unrelated parent passages without explicit justification.

**Result:** Q043 remains **6/8**.

## Q049 — Consultation in Shared Affairs

Sikh collective deliberative institutions provide a potentially strong functional analogue, especially traditions of collective decision and *Sarbat Khalsa/Gurmata*. However, institutional-history evidence must be distinguished from Guru Granth Sahib scripture and cited accordingly.

African consensus and deliberative traditions also provide strong analogues but require named institutional/traditional provenance.

**Decision:** current Sikh P and African evidence require source-register normalisation before any promotion.

**Result:** Q049 remains **6/8** at this stage.
