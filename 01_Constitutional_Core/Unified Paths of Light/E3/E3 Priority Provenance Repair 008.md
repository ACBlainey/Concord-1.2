# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Priority Provenance Repair 008 — Q014, Q015, Q016

## Q014 — Peace and De-Escalation

Sikh P and African P are the unresolved cells.

Sikh primary ethics strongly supports peace, restraint, compassion and overcoming hostility. This is functionally close, but a final exact-source record should demonstrate de-escalation rather than peace as a general aspiration.

African reconciliation traditions provide strong peace/restoration evidence but require named-tradition provenance.

**Decision:** retain both P.

**Result:** Q014 remains **6/8**.

## Q015 — Forgiveness and Reconciliation

Sikh ethics directly values forgiveness and the overcoming of hostility. The functional match is strong enough that the Sikh P is a plausible secure upgrade once exact scripture location is fixed.

African restorative/reconciliation evidence is likewise strong but currently scoped through synthesis.

**Decision:** mark Sikh P **eligible for S** after exact-source normalisation; retain African P.

**Candidate result:** Q015 may move **6/8 → 7/8**.

## Q016 — Justice Without Partiality

Sikh equality and anti-caste evidence is strong, but equality is not identical to impartial adjudication. African common-good/fairness material also does not automatically prove the frozen non-partiality rule.

**Decision:** retain Sikh P and African P.

**Result:** Q016 remains **6/8**.
