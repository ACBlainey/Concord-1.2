# E3-Q001 — Truthful Communication

**Provisional ancestry:** N001; N098 nested.

## Final Principle

**Communicate truthfully and do not knowingly mix truth with falsehood, conceal material truth, misrepresent credit, or use deception to manipulate another person's judgement.**

### Operational Meaning

Truth is infrastructure for agency. Falsehood, strategic concealment, and false reputational claims distort another person's ability to judge persons, events, and responsibility accurately.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:42; 3:188; 33:70; related 16:91–94.

### Supporting E3 Units

**042, 154, 203, 245, 352, 419**, with truth-related compound support **031, 099, 196, 242, 403**.

### Nested Applications

- truthful speech;
- non-concealment of material truth;
- no false credit or undeserved reputation;
- no strategic mixture of truth and falsehood.

### Boundary

Truthfulness constrains the communicator. Verification and evidentiary procedure remain separate duties of receivers and institutions.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal.**

# E3-Q002 — Epistemic Responsibility

**Provisional ancestry:** N002.

## Final Principle

**Do not assert, authorise, or act consequentially on claims beyond what the available knowledge justifies.**

### Operational Meaning

Confidence should track evidence. Uncertainty may be unavoidable, but it should remain visible and proportionate to consequential action.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 17:36; 7:33; 16:116.

### Supporting E3 Units

**121, 223, 246, 255, 330, 415**, with learning-related support **260, 369**.

### Boundary

Former Unit 126 is excluded and supplies no behavioural authority.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Near-universal / Common boundary — final source audit required.**

# E3-Q003 — Verify Before Consequential Action

**Provisional ancestry:** N003; N063 nested.

## Final Principle

**Verify consequential reports and accusations before acting on or amplifying them, with stronger safeguards where potential harm is greater.**

### Operational Meaning

The duty covers both decision and transmission. Unsupported information should not become punishment, exclusion, reputational harm, or viral accusation merely through repetition.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:83, 94; 24:4, 11–16; 49:6.

### Supporting E3 Units

**057, 061, 133, 134, 163, 267, 269, 293, 358, 374**.

### Nested Applications

- verify reports before action;
- serious accusations require evidence;
- do not amplify unverified scandal;
- source reliability matters.

### Boundary

Truthful testimony is a speaker duty; verification is a receiver/institution duty.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Near-universal.**

# E3-Q004 — Reliable Testimony and Evidence

**Provisional ancestry:** N004.

## Final Principle

**Give testimony honestly and preserve reliable evidentiary procedures where another person's rights, property, liberty, or reputation are at stake.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:282–283; 5:106–108; 24:4; 70:33.

### Supporting E3 Units

**029, 030, 082, 133, 186, 267, 314, 394**, with procedural support **180**.

### Boundary

Evidence systems contain more than testimony; this principle governs the reliability of formal evidentiary contribution.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q005 — Accurate Attribution of Responsibility

**Provisional ancestry:** N005.

## Final Principle

**Attribute wrongdoing to the responsible actor and do not transfer guilt, blame, or punishment onto an innocent person.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:112; 33:5.

### Supporting E3 Units

**150, 153, 213, 287, 368, 412**.

### Nested Applications

- no scapegoating;
- no false attribution;
- individualised responsibility.

### Boundary

Culpability calibration between accident, negligence, and intent remains E3-Q083.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q006 — Promise, Covenant, and Commitment Integrity

**Provisional ancestry:** N006; N078 nested.

## Final Principle

**Honour legitimate commitments and reliable counterparties; do not use promises, covenants, or assurances as instruments of deception or opportunistic betrayal.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 5:1; 8:58; 9:4; 16:91–94; 17:34; 23:8.

### Supporting E3 Units

**069, 091, 093, 095, 109, 119, 132, 202, 227, 229, 231, 243, 245, 266, 332, 342, 346, 352, 394, 419**.

### Nested Applications

- fulfil agreements;
- terminate agreements transparently;
- honour reliable out-group counterparties;
- strategic promise-breaking damages wider trust.

### Boundary

Illegitimate commitments need not be preserved blindly; integrity includes legitimate termination procedures.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal.**
