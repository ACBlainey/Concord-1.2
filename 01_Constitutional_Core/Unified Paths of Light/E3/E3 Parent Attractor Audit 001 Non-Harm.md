# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Parent Attractor Audit 001 — Non-Harm and Protection

This audit tests the parent function rather than Q011 alone.

| Tradition | Parent status | Evidential basis |
|---|---|---|
| Qur'an / Islam | PA-S | protection of life; non-aggression; proportionality; defence against oppression; vulnerable-person protection; coercion limits; repair |
| Christianity | PA-S | prohibitions on killing/injury; love of neighbour/enemy; mercy; peacemaking; protection of vulnerable persons |
| Judaism | PA-S | life protection; injury constraints; neighbour obligation; justice; duties toward vulnerable persons; constrained retaliation |
| Buddhism | PA-S | first precept/non-killing; non-harming; compassion; restraint of hatred and anger |
| Hinduism | PA-S | ahimsa/non-injury; compassion; self-restraint; duties constraining wrongful harm |
| Sikhism | PA-S | compassion, service, restraint, equality and protection; force is normatively constrained rather than treated as unrestricted |
| Chinese/Confucian | PA-S | ren/humaneness, reciprocity, benevolent conduct and restraint of injury through relational obligation |
| African Traditional | PA-S | communitarian human welfare, dignity, social obligation, restraint of destructive conduct and restoration of damaged relations in identified African ethical traditions |

## Result

**Non-Harm and Protection = 8/8 PA-S — Universal parent attractor.**

This does **not** mean the traditions prohibit every use of force. Several permit force, punishment or defence under conditions. The shared parent is better expressed as:

> **Do not cause unjustified harm; protect persons from serious harm; constrain necessary harmful action; and restore damaged relations or welfare where possible.**

## Why this differs from Q011

Q011 asks whether the exact principle **Sanctity and Protection of Life** is securely present. The parent audit asks whether the tradition contains a sufficiently direct architecture for limiting and responding to harm.

The parent proposition is therefore supported by several independent functional routes.
