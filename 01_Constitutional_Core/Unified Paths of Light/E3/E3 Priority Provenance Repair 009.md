# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Priority Provenance Repair 009 — Q018, Q022, Q028

## Q018 — Accountability With Possibility of Reform

Sikh P and African P remain unresolved.

Both traditions contain strong moral reform, forgiveness and accountability themes, but Q018 is a compound institutional principle: responsibility must coexist with a genuine possibility of reform.

**Decision:** retain both P.

**Result:** Q018 remains **6/8**.

## Q022 — Commercial Honesty and Accurate Measure

Sikh evidence for honest work and truthful dealing is strong. The frozen principle also requires **accurate measure**, so generic honest-labour evidence is not enough to prove the complete formulation.

African market/fair-dealing traditions may support the parent principle but need named-source evidence.

**Decision:** retain Sikh P and African P.

**Result:** Q022 remains **6/8**.

## Q028 — Resource Stewardship, Non-Waste, and Balanced Use

Sikh teaching strongly supports restraint, sharing and non-attachment, while African ethical synthesis supports stewardship and community-oriented resource use. Yet the frozen principle is a three-part compound: stewardship + non-waste + balanced use.

**Decision:** no upgrade without evidence for the compound itself or a defensible set of primary passages jointly covering it.

**Result:** Q028 remains **6/8**.

## Boundary lesson from Repairs 007–009

The 6/8 set is proving harder to promote than the 7/8 set because many apparent gaps are not merely missing citations. They arise from **compound or operational formulations** whose parent values are widespread but whose exact structure is less uniformly explicit.

This is evidence, not failure: the audit should preserve that distinction.
