# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Parent Attractor Audit 003 — Justice, Fairness and Accountability

This audit tests the parent function of fair treatment, responsibility for wrongdoing, restraint of partiality and restoration of normative order.

| Tradition | Parent status | Evidential basis |
|---|---|---|
| Qur'an / Islam | PA-S | impartial justice, testimony, procedure, accountability, anti-corruption and reform |
| Christianity | PA-S | impartiality, responsibility, justice toward neighbour and vulnerable persons, correction and reconciliation |
| Judaism | PA-S | judicial fairness, witness rules, anti-bribery, proportional responsibility and restitution |
| Buddhism | PA-S | karmic/moral responsibility, right conduct, impartial compassion and correction of harmful conduct |
| Hinduism | PA-S | dharma, responsibility, impartial ethical duty and consequences of conduct |
| Sikhism | PA-S | equality, truthful conduct, responsibility, resistance to injustice and service-oriented authority |
| Chinese/Confucian | PA-S | yi/righteousness, appropriate conduct, responsibility of office, correction and relational fairness |
| African Traditional | PA-S | fairness, responsibility, restoration, communal adjudication and repair within identified customary ethical traditions |

## Result

**Justice, Fairness and Accountability = 8/8 PA-S — Universal parent attractor.**

The common parent can be stated conservatively as:

> **Treat persons and disputes according to morally relevant considerations rather than arbitrary favour; hold agents answerable for wrongdoing; and seek restoration of right order.**

Formal due process, evidentiary rules and institutional remedies remain child-level elaborations and should not inherit 8/8 automatically.

> **End of Parent Attractor Audit 003**
