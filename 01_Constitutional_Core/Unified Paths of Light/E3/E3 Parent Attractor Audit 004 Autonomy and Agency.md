# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Parent Attractor Audit 004 — Autonomy, Consent and Agency

This is a harder parent audit because modern autonomy language can exceed what historical traditions explicitly formulate.

The parent is therefore tested as:

> **Persons possess morally significant agency that should not be overridden arbitrarily; meaningful choice, conscience or refusal should receive protection appropriate to context and capacity.**

| Tradition | Parent status | Evidential basis |
|---|---|---|
| Qur'an / Islam | PA-S | non-compulsion, conscience, consent, refusal, privacy, capacity-sensitive obligation and preserved moral agency |
| Christianity | PA-S | conscience, voluntary moral commitment, responsibility of the individual and limits on compelled belief in relevant traditions/textual interpretations |
| Judaism | PA-S | individual responsibility, consent-sensitive obligations, conscience/deliberation and legal recognition of agency |
| Buddhism | PA-S | intentional action, individual moral responsibility and non-coercive cultivation of ethical conduct |
| Hinduism | PA-S | moral agency, disciplined choice, duty and liberation-oriented self-governance |
| Sikhism | PA-S | moral agency, equality, voluntary service, conscience and resistance to domination |
| Chinese/Confucian | PA-S | cultivated moral agency, reflective self-correction and responsibility within relationships |
| African Traditional | PA-S | personhood within community alongside responsibility, deliberation, consent-sensitive social practices and recognised individual moral agency |

## Result

**Autonomy, Consent and Agency = 8/8 PA-S — Universal at the parent level, with a major formulation caveat.**

This does **not** establish modern liberal individual autonomy as universal. The shared attractor is better described as **protected moral agency**.

The traditions disagree about the boundaries of legitimate authority, family obligation, hierarchy and communal constraint. Those disagreements remain visible in the child catalogue.

## Concord relevance

This suggests that the Concord's Autonomy and Consent law has a cross-traditional parent foundation, but its modern substrate-neutral rights formulation is a further constitutional development rather than something directly duplicated in all source traditions.
