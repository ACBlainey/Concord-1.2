# E3-N051 — Consultation in Shared Affairs

## Normalised Principle

**Where decisions materially affect a community, include meaningful consultation among those bearing legitimate responsibility or consequence.**

### Operational Meaning

Authority should not become structurally deaf. Consultation distributes information, improves correction, and constrains unilateral decision-making.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 3:159; 42:38.

### Supporting E3 Units

**040, 158, 290, 372**, with responsive-listening support **226, 417**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- consultation;
- shared deliberation;
- responsive listening;
- collective decision process;
- leadership that hears affected parties.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Consultation does not imply that every decision must be unanimous or that expertise becomes irrelevant. Its core requirement is meaningful participation rather than ceremonial hearing.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong functional equivalents occur widely, though institutional forms differ.

**Preliminary classification: Near-universal / Common.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N052 — Competence and Trustworthiness in Entrusted Roles

## Normalised Principle

**Assign consequential responsibilities using relevant capability and trustworthiness rather than status, favour, or identity alone.**

### Operational Meaning

Good intentions without capability can fail; capability without integrity can be dangerous. Entrusted roles therefore require both functional competence and ethical reliability.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 12:54–55; 28:26.

### Supporting E3 Units

**104, 145, 240, 280**, with trust-related support **055, 091, 132**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- competence;
- trustworthiness;
- role fitness;
- merit relevant to function;
- reliable stewardship.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Competence is role-specific. The principle does not establish a general hierarchy of human worth.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong functional parallels occur throughout the comparison set.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N053 — Authority Is a Trust, Not Ownership

## Normalised Principle

**Treat public or institutional authority as a purpose-bound trust whose exercise remains constrained by justice, responsibility, and the interests it exists to serve.**

### Operational Meaning

Holding office does not convert shared institutions or affected persons into private property. Authority remains accountable to its legitimate function.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:58–59; related stewardship and judgement passages.

### Supporting E3 Units

**055, 056, 075, 076**, with stewardship support **170, 299, 381**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- entrusted authority;
- rule-governed office;
- fair judgement;
- stewardship rather than possession;
- bounded discretion.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

This principle does not determine one constitutional form. It constrains any form by treating power as fiduciary rather than proprietary.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong equivalents occur in many traditions, though theories of authority differ.

**Preliminary classification: Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N054 — Rule-Governed Resolution of Disagreement

## Normalised Principle

**When legitimate disagreement cannot be resolved informally, use recognised procedures and standards rather than allowing raw power to determine the outcome.**

### Operational Meaning

Disagreement is inevitable. Stable systems therefore need agreed pathways for appeal, adjudication, interpretation, or settlement.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:59; 4:35; 8:1.

### Supporting E3 Units

**052, 056, 225, 416**, with procedural support **017, 075, 180**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- appeal;
- arbitration;
- recognised authority;
- rule-governed dispute resolution;
- common-resource dispute procedure.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

A recognised procedure can itself become unjust. N016 and N017 continue to constrain the substantive and procedural quality of dispute resolution.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong equivalents occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N055 — Common Resources Require Accountable Allocation

## Normalised Principle

**Resources created, captured, or held through collective action should be allocated through legitimate and accountable rules rather than private seizure or factional power.**

### Operational Meaning

Common resources are especially vulnerable to capture because ownership is diffuse. Governance must specify allocation before competition determines it informally.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 8:1; 59:7.

### Supporting E3 Units

**225, 303, 416**, with structured-distribution support **097, 313**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- collective-resource allocation;
- anti-capture;
- circulation of shared wealth;
- legitimate distribution;
- rule-governed common assets.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

The principle does not imply that all resources are collective. It applies where the resource is legitimately common or jointly generated.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Common-property and distributive systems exist widely but vary substantially.

**Preliminary classification: Common.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N056 — Collective Responsibility to Resist Corruption

## Normalised Principle

**Communities should maintain mechanisms that identify, discourage, and correct conduct that predictably corrupts social order or harms others.**

### Operational Meaning

A stable ethical system cannot depend entirely on isolated private virtue. Some harms require collective correction and norm maintenance.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 3:104; 11:116–117; 13:25; 7:56.

### Supporting E3 Units

**035, 088, 102, 107, 202, 224, 239, 243**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- resist corruption;
- promote constructive conduct;
- preserve functioning order;
- collective norm maintenance;
- social correction.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Collective correction must itself remain constrained by evidence, proportionality, conscience, and non-aggression. The principle cannot license unrestricted moral policing.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Communal norm-maintenance is widespread, though mechanisms and limits differ.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain with explicit safeguards.**

# E3-N057 — Do Not Cooperate in Wrongdoing

## Normalised Principle

**Cooperate in legitimate good, but do not knowingly contribute resources, coordination, or institutional support to wrongful harm or oppression.**

### Operational Meaning

Responsibility extends beyond the final actor. Enabling, financing, coordinating, or materially assisting wrongdoing can make a participant ethically implicated.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 5:2; 58:8–10.

### Supporting E3 Units

**070, 171, 220, 301**, with complicity links **067, 212, 411**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- cooperate in good;
- non-cooperation with wrongdoing;
- no harmful secret coordination;
- complicity;
- enabling responsibility.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

The principle requires reasonable knowledge and causal relevance; remote association alone is not sufficient.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong equivalents occur across most traditions.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N058 — Purpose-Constrained Confidential Deliberation

## Normalised Principle

**Private coordination is legitimate when it serves constructive purposes, but secrecy should not be used to conceal aggression, corruption, intimidation, or betrayal.**

### Operational Meaning

Confidentiality can protect deliberation and privacy, but opacity can also facilitate capture. Ethical evaluation depends on purpose and effect rather than secrecy alone.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:114; 58:8–10.

### Supporting E3 Units

**062, 171, 301**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- private counsel for charity or reconciliation;
- no secret coordination for harm;
- purpose-sensitive confidentiality;
- anti-conspiracy safeguard.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Transparency is not absolute. Legitimate privacy, negotiation, safeguarding, and confidential advice remain protected.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Functional equivalents exist widely, though exact formulations differ.

**Preliminary classification: Common / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N059 — Social Cohesion Without Suppression of Difference

## Normalised Principle

**Preserve cooperative unity where possible, but do not equate cohesion with forced uniformity or suppression of legitimate disagreement.**

### Operational Meaning

Fragmentation can destroy collective capacity, yet coerced sameness can also create instability. Cohesion should be built through shared rules, trust, and cooperation.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 3:103; 8:46; related pluralism passages.

### Supporting E3 Units

**034, 092, 228**, with pluralism support **147, 175, 200, 305, 327, 407**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- avoid destructive internal conflict;
- preserve collective capacity;
- unity;
- plural cohesion;
- difference without fragmentation.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

This principle must not be used to silence dissent. Legitimate disagreement is protected by N042–N043.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong equivalents occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N060 — Leadership Through Gentleness and Correctability

## Normalised Principle

**Exercise leadership without needless harshness, remain open to consultation and correction, and do not allow authority to extinguish mercy.**

### Operational Meaning

Harsh leadership can drive participants away and suppress information. Gentleness, forgiveness, and consultation improve both legitimacy and error correction.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 3:159; 20:43–44.

### Supporting E3 Units

**040, 127, 263**, with consultation support **158, 290, 372**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- gentle leadership;
- consultation;
- forgiveness;
- respectful speech to power;
- correctability.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Gentleness does not prohibit firm action against serious harm. It constrains unnecessary harshness and ego-driven domination.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong leadership-virtue parallels occur across the comparison traditions.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# Interim Normalisation Findings: E3-N051–N060

The governance cluster now distinguishes consultation, role fitness, entrusted authority, dispute procedure, common-resource allocation, collective correction, complicity, confidentiality, cohesion, and leadership conduct.

These are related but not interchangeable. Compression has therefore removed recurrence while preserving different institutional functions.
