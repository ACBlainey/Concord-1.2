# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Source Provenance Repair 005 — Sikh Evidence Framework

**Status:** Conservative repair pass pending full passage-level citation register.

---

## 1. Purpose

Sikh rows in the early E3 comparisons often relied on well-known ethical concepts—truthful living, seva, humility, equality, honest labour and sharing—without always preserving exact Guru Granth Sahib provenance.

The repair must therefore be conservative.

## 2. High-Confidence Ethical Clusters

### Truthful Living

Sikh ethical teaching places exceptional emphasis on truth and truthful living.

**E3 relevance:**
- Q001 Truthful Communication;
- Q008 Integrity Between Declared Principle and Conduct.

The second mapping is important: truthful living is behaviourally richer than merely uttering factually correct statements.

### Seva and Sharing

Selfless service and sharing resources with others are central Sikh ethical practices.

**E3 relevance:**
- Q029 Wealth Carries Social Obligations;
- Q031 Non-Transactional Aid;
- Q032 Positive Duty Toward Severe Need;
- Q034 Mutual Aid Under Scarcity.

**Boundary:** seva does not automatically prove a specific state welfare mechanism such as Q033.

### Equality and Anti-Status Ethics

Sikh teaching strongly challenges caste/status hierarchy and affirms human equality.

**E3 relevance:**
- Q035 Human Dignity Beyond Status;
- Q039 Inclusion Against Elite Capture at parent anti-status level.

**Boundary:** anti-caste/equality teaching does not by itself prove a modern institutional anti-capture mechanism.

### Humility and Ego Restraint

Humility and resistance to ego-centred conduct are central.

**E3 relevance:**
- Q009 Self-Critical Humility;
- Q064 anger/self-control clusters as supporting evidence where an exact passage is available.

### Honest Work and Non-Exploitation

The ethical triad commonly expressed through honest labour, sharing and remembrance supplies a strong behavioural-economic orientation.

**E3 relevance:**
- Q022 Commercial Honesty at parent honest-labour level;
- Q029 social obligation of resources.

It should not be stretched into specific measurement, debt or interest rules without direct evidence.

## 3. Citation Discipline

Before a Sikh row becomes publication-grade SQ-A/B, the final evidence register should record:

- exact Guru Granth Sahib passage/Ang where applicable;
- translation/source used;
- whether the claim comes from the Guru Granth Sahib itself, later Sikh tradition, or a modern explanatory source;
- the functional connection to the E3 principle.

Terms such as *seva*, *vand chhakna* and truthful living are useful navigation concepts, not substitutes for source location.

## 4. Provisional Effect on E3

Sikh evidence is unlikely to disappear from the major parent clusters.

The likely revisions concern **granularity**:

Strong parent-level support:
- truth/integrity;
- generosity/service;
- dignity/equality;
- humility;
- honest work;
- social responsibility.

More cautious operational-child support:
- formal verification;
- due process;
- privacy;
- exact consent procedure;
- institutional allocation mechanisms;
- capacity-sensitive duty;
- threat classification;
- safe passage;
- developmental agency.

## 5. Audit Result

The Sikh row should remain substantively important but should not be upgraded on concept-name familiarity alone.

> **A recognised ethical concept identifies where to look; an identifiable source establishes the evidence.**
