# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Candidate Upgrade Citation Control 003 — Q060, Q063, Q064

## Q060 — No Gossip, Backbiting, or Malicious Information Transfer

**Primary source:** Sri Guru Granth Sahib, **Ang 1026**.  
**Key behavioural content:** abandon slander and envy of others.

Additional SGGS passages, including Ang 810 and Ang 875, explicitly condemn slander and ill-will.

**Functional note:** Slander is a direct form of malicious reputational information transfer. The evidence securely establishes the central behavioural prohibition in Q060.

**SQ:** A.  
**Functional grade:** A/B.  
**Decision:** **Sikh P → S approved.**

## Q063 — Patience Under Difficulty

**Primary source:** Sri Guru Granth Sahib, Sheikh Farid saloks, **Ang 1384**.  
**Key behavioural content:** repeated injunctions to establish *sabar* (patience/endurance), including imagery of patience as bow, string and arrow and the difficult discipline of patient spiritual life.

**Functional note:** This is direct primary evidence for patience/endurance under demanding conditions rather than generic calmness.

**SQ:** A.  
**Functional grade:** A.  
**Decision:** **Sikh P → S approved.**

## Q064 — Control Anger Before It Controls Action

**Primary source:** Sri Guru Granth Sahib, Sheikh Farid salok, **Ang 1381–1382**.  
**Key behavioural content:** do good to the bad and do not harbour anger in the mind.

Additional SGGS evidence repeatedly identifies anger as a destructive vice and self-control as a virtue.

**Functional note:** The passage directly instructs restraint of anger in a context where retaliatory conduct would be possible. This is a close behavioural equivalent of Q064.

**SQ:** A.  
**Functional grade:** A.  
**Decision:** **Sikh P → S approved.**
