# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Methodological Findings and Limitations

## Findings

### 1. Anchor reversal is essential
A comparative ethical framework is more credible when the extraction is repeated from an independent anchor rather than merely expanding the first anchor's categories.

### 2. Preservation before compression matters
Source occurrences, normalised principles and higher-order attractors are different analytical objects. Collapsing them too early hides both emphasis and disagreement.

### 3. Parent ethics converge more strongly than operational rules
Traditions often share the ethical function while differing over implementation, scope, procedure or institutional mechanism.

### 4. Negative results are informative
Seventeen 7/8 exact principles remained 7/8 after targeted provenance repair. Refusing unsupported promotion helped distinguish genuine granularity differences from citation gaps.

### 5. Source architecture matters
Textual traditions are easier to audit with verse-level precision than plural oral/customary traditions. African evidence therefore requires named-tradition scope rather than construction of a fictitious pan-African canon.

### 6. Derived convergence is stronger than retrospective matching
The strongest E3 result comes from freezing outputs before comparing them with the pre-existing Concord kernel.

## Limitations

### Corpus completeness
The Qur'anic extraction was closed at 420 audited records because additional extraction was increasingly expected to merge into already identified principles. Closure was based on declining downstream information value, not proof of exhaustive saturation.

### Translation and interpretation
Scriptural meaning is translation- and interpretation-sensitive. E3 analyses behavioural functions rather than claiming definitive theological exegesis.

### Unequal source forms
The eight comparison families do not have equivalent canonical structures. Scripture, commentary, philosophy, customary practice and oral tradition therefore enter the evidence architecture differently.

### Family-level representation
A secure cell for a broad tradition family does not imply every denomination, school, community or historical period within that family teaches the proposition identically.

### Parent-attractor compression
Higher-order compression necessarily introduces interpretation. The parent-attractor audits reduce circularity through freeze rules and evidence controls but cannot eliminate judgement entirely.

### Historical practice versus normative teaching
E3 compares normative ethical material, not whether adherents or institutions historically complied with it.

### Concord comparison
Overlap with Blainey's Laws is evidence of structural convergence, not proof that the Concord's constitutional formulation is uniquely entailed by the source traditions.

## Reproducibility recommendation

Future replications should:
- select a different anchor tradition;
- extract before viewing E3's final attractors;
- preserve source-to-principle mappings;
- freeze the independent catalogue;
- perform bidirectional crosswalks;
- publish failures and residuals alongside matches.

The strongest next test would therefore be **another independently anchored extraction**, not further tuning of E3.
