# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Anchor-Reversal Comparison Method

**Comparison:** Psalm-anchored study ↔ Qur'an-anchored E3 study  
**Author:** Alexander C. Blainey  
**Date:** 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

The E3 Qur'an-derived catalogue was constructed and frozen before exposure to the final Psalm-derived synthesis. The methodological firewall can therefore now be lifted.

The question is not whether identical wording appears in both catalogues. It is:

> **Does reversing the anchor tradition independently recover the same functional ethical architecture?**

The original study used the Psalter as anchor and searched the other seven traditions for functional equivalents. E3 reversed that direction: the Qur'an was used as anchor and the other seven traditions were compared against its extracted behavioural ethics.

This phase compares the independently produced outputs.

---

## 2. Important Asymmetry

The two catalogues are not structurally identical datasets.

The Psalm synthesis reports **over 80 distinct ethical principles** extracted from 150 Psalms and publishes its principal results in convergence tiers. The E3 study records **89 final top-level principles** after 420 audited Qur'anic source occurrences were compressed through a 100-principle provisional catalogue.

Therefore the comparison must not pretend that the published Psalm Paper IV is an 89-row catalogue equivalent to E3.

The comparison will use two levels:

1. **Published Psalm synthesis level** — explicit principles listed in Paper IV.
2. **Underlying Psalm extraction level** — where necessary, the detailed Psalm 1–150 analyses can be consulted to test whether an E3 principle exists in the original extraction even if it was not promoted into Paper IV's summary tables.

This prevents “absence from the summary” from being mistaken for “absence from the Psalm study.”

---

## 3. Matching Grades

Each cross-anchor relationship receives one of five statuses.

### AR-A — Direct Functional Match

The two independently derived principles prescribe substantially the same behaviour and protect substantially the same ethical interest.

### AR-B — Strong Functional Match

The behavioural core is substantially shared, but one catalogue is broader, narrower, or framed through a different operational mechanism.

### AR-C — Cluster-Level Convergence

No one-to-one principle is adequate, but the principle falls within an independently recovered ethical cluster in the other anchor.

### AR-D — Anchor-Specific or Weakly Represented

The principle has no sufficiently strong independent counterpart at the compared resolution.

### AR-U — Unresolved

The published synthesis is insufficient to decide and the underlying Psalm extraction must be checked before classification.

---

## 4. Comparison Rules

1. **No retroactive renaming.** E3-Q001–E3-Q089 remain frozen.
2. **No forced one-to-one mapping.** One Psalm principle may map to several E3 principles and vice versa.
3. **Granularity differences are evidence.** They are not errors to be hidden.
4. **Theological propositions are not automatically behavioural ethics.** A theological Psalm synthesis item may lack an E3 behavioural counterpart without implying ethical contradiction.
5. **Summary absence is not corpus absence.** AR-D should not be assigned solely because an E3 principle is missing from Paper IV's short tables.
6. **Source-frequency and principle identity remain separate.**
7. **Convergence must be functional.** Exact verbal similarity is neither required nor sufficient.
8. **Disagreement is preserved.** The comparison is a robustness test, not a harmonisation exercise.

---

## 5. Primary Psalm-Synthesis Reference Set

Paper IV explicitly identifies the following behavioural or partly behavioural principles:

### Universal Tier
- non-harm;
- truthfulness;
- care for poor and vulnerable;
- humility;
- trust in moral order rather than human power;
- gratitude/praise;
- protection of orphans, widows, and strangers;
- moral accountability;
- seeking the moral order.

### Near-Universal Tier
- courage under danger;
- confession and forgiveness;
- promise keeping;
- non-covetousness/contentment;
- legitimate lament;
- patient waiting;
- distrust of wealth;
- moral vindication/consequence themes.

### Common Tier
- avoid association with the wicked;
- unity;
- transmission of ethical tradition to the next generation;
- several explicitly theological principles concerning worship, divine judgement, divine leadership, refuge, and restoration.

The underlying Psalm extraction is richer than this summary and must be consulted before declaring an E3 principle genuinely anchor-specific.

---

## 6. E3 Comparison Domains

For auditability, E3-Q001–E3-Q089 will be compared in functional domains:

1. truth, evidence, knowledge, and commitment;
2. life, justice, accountability, and conflict;
3. property, exchange, resources, and material responsibility;
4. dignity, privacy, conscience, and plural coexistence;
5. family, intimacy, consent, and developmental responsibility;
6. governance, authority, cooperation, and institutional integrity;
7. self-regulation, resilience, association, and long-horizon conduct.

The domains are organisational only. They do not alter principle identity.

---

## 7. Outputs

The comparison should produce:

- a row-level crosswalk for all 89 E3 principles;
- a reverse crosswalk from the published Psalm principles into E3;
- counts by AR grade;
- identification of one-to-many and many-to-one relationships;
- anchor-specific emphases;
- theological-vs-behavioural asymmetries;
- a final assessment of anchor robustness.

No conclusion about convergence should be written until the unresolved rows have been checked against the detailed Psalm extraction.

---

## 8. Interpretation Rule

The strongest evidence for anchor robustness is not equal catalogue size.

It is independent recovery of the same **functional ethical clusters and behavioural constraints** despite:

- different anchor texts;
- different source structures;
- different source frequencies;
- different extraction granularity;
- and deliberate withholding of the first synthesis during construction of the second.

> **The test is structural convergence, not numerical identity.**
