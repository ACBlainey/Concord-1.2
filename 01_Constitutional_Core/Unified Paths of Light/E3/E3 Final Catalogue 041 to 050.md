# E3-Q041 — Coexistence Without Forced Sameness

**Provisional ancestry:** N042.

## Final Principle

**Permit deep differences of belief or identity to remain unresolved without requiring forced merger, provided parties remain within legitimate non-harm constraints.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 3:64; 29:46; 60:8–9; 109:1–6.

### Supporting E3 Units

**147, 175, 176, 200, 207, 283, 305, 327, 365, 386, 387, 407, 409**.

### Boundary

Pluralism does not require moral equivalence among all behaviours.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common / Near-universal.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:233; 3:159; 42:38.

### Supporting E3 Units

**021, 040, 158, 226, 290, 372, 417**, with relationship-settlement support **064**.

**Near-universal / Common.**
