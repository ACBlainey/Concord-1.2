# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Near-Universal Evidence Matrix 008

**Principles:** Q086, Q087, plus residual tier controls.

---

## Q086 — Right of Refusal Must Be Respected

Many traditions protect persons against particular forms of coercion and recognise limits on demands. The generalised right of refusal is broader and more modern in form.

The E3 evidence derives from specific refusal contexts rather than a universal doctrine that every request may be refused.

**Decision:** **Common.** Preserve the principle, but do not elevate specific anti-coercion precedents into a universal general right without evidence.

## Q087 — Developmental Agency Should Expand With Capability

Traditions commonly recognise stages of childhood, maturity, learning, initiation, responsibility or competence. E3 converts this into a developmental-agency rule: control should recede as capability grows.

That direction-of-travel requirement is much more specific than merely recognising age or maturity.

**Decision:** **Common.** This is an important Qur'an-derived operational principle but not presently demonstrated as near-universal.

## Residual Control — Q040 Freedom of Conscience and Non-Compulsion

Although catalogued as Common rather than Near-universal, Q040 is included here because it is a major agency principle.

Traditions differ sharply over conversion, apostasy, religious authority and coercion. Historical practice also differs from textual ideals.

**Decision:** **Common.** No attempt should be made to force a universal result.

## Residual Control — Q044 Child and Dependant Welfare

Earlier Universal-candidate review found very broad support. The exact structure of dependency differs, but protection/care of children and dependants is strongly recurrent.

**Decision:** **Retain as strong Universal candidate at functional level.**

## Residual Control — Q060 Malicious Information Transfer

The earlier audit found broad speech-ethics support but noted that falsehood, slander, gossip and backbiting are not identical.

**Decision:** **Universal parent cluster of harmful/malicious speech is strong; exact compound remains subject to source-level normalisation.**

## Near-Universal Audit Closure

The Near-universal review is now sufficiently complete to identify a stable pattern:

### Principles tending upward

Broad parent ethics such as:

- constrained authority;
- moderation;
- mutual aid;
- truth;
- non-cooperation with wrongdoing;
- consequence awareness;
- care of dependants.

### Principles tending downward

Operational or second-order rules such as:

- procedural verification;
- formal due process;
- inheritance mechanism;
- structured provision;
- privacy as a general right;
- anti-elite-capture institutional design;
- relationship dissolution procedure;
- confidential-deliberation constraints;
- anti-overconstraint;
- peaceful/threat classification;
- safe passage;
- public-purpose infrastructure;
- impaired-judgement exploitation;
- general refusal rights;
- developmental agency.

This pattern closely matches the earlier anchor-reversal result without being derived from it: **the Qur'an-first catalogue is often more procedurally decomposed than the comparison traditions' broad ethical formulations.**
