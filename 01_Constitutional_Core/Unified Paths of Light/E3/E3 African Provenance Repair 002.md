# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## African Provenance Repair 002 — Q060, Q063, Q064

## Q060 — No Gossip, Backbiting, or Malicious Information Transfer

African ethical scholarship strongly recognises the social importance of reputation, truthful speech, restraint of harmful speech and preservation of communal harmony. Named proverb traditions also contain warnings against destructive speech.

However, the present E3 record has not yet fixed a sufficiently precise named proverb/source whose behavioural content covers gossip/backbiting or malicious information transfer at the frozen Q060 level.

**Decision:** retain African **P**.

## Q063 — Patience Under Difficulty

Endurance, fortitude and resilience are widely documented within African ethical and proverbial traditions. Yet “resilience” as a broad value is not automatically the same as the frozen principle of patience under difficulty.

The current evidence remains a family-level synthesis rather than a sufficiently localised traditional source.

**Decision:** retain African **P**.

## Q064 — Control Anger Before It Controls Action

African moral traditions frequently value self-mastery, social restraint and avoidance of conduct that damages communal relations. Reconciliation institutions also presuppose restraint of retaliatory anger.

Those are strong functional neighbours, but institutional reconciliation cannot be reverse-inferred into an explicit anger-control rule without identified evidence.

**Decision:** retain African **P**.

## Result

No African P→S promotion is made for Q060, Q063 or Q064.

Together with Repair 001, all six African P cells in the current 7/8 set have now been reviewed without forcing an 8/8 result.
