## Pre-Introduction to the Project

The original *Unified Paths of Light*, subsequently developed as *Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity*, used the biblical Psalms as its anchor corpus. Ethical teachings were extracted from the Psalms, reduced to their functional behavioural cores, and then compared with equivalent teachings in seven other enduring religious and philosophical traditions.

That work produced a cross-comparative ethical catalogue and contributed to the ethical foundations from which Blainey's Laws and the Concord Ethical Kernel were later developed.

**E3 repeats the experiment from a different starting point.**

Instead of beginning with the Psalms and asking whether equivalent ethical principles can be found in Islam and the other traditions, E3 begins independently with the **Qur'an**. Its ethical teachings will first be identified from the Qur'anic corpus itself. Only after extraction will those teachings be compared against the other seven traditions used in the original study.

The methodological question is therefore:

> **If the comparative process begins with the Qur'an rather than the Psalms, what ethical core emerges, and how closely does that independently derived core converge with the original result?**

---

## Methodological Mirror

E3 is intended as an **exact methodological mirror in direction**, not as a retrospective search for Islamic support for principles already identified in the Psalms.

The original direction was:

> **Psalms → ethical extraction → comparison with seven other traditions → synthesis**

E3 reverses the anchor:

> **Qur'an → ethical extraction → comparison with seven other traditions → synthesis**

This distinction is essential. The Qur'anic ethical source list must be established from the Qur'an itself before the cross-comparative stage is allowed to determine which principles appear important.

The project therefore avoids selecting Qur'anic passages merely because they resemble ethical principles already discovered in the Psalms.

---

## Scope

The project will examine Qur'anic passages containing identifiable ethical, moral, social, interpersonal, civic, or character-forming instruction.

As in the original study, the analysis is concerned primarily with **functional ethical content** rather than theological truth claims.

| Content Type | Treatment |
|--------------|-----------|
| **Ethical / behavioural instruction** | Included |
| **Moral character instruction** | Included |
| **Social and interpersonal duties** | Included |
| **Justice, responsibility and governance ethics** | Included where behaviourally applicable |
| **Pure ritual prescription** | Normally excluded unless it carries a separable ethical principle |
| **Cosmology** | Excluded as such |
| **Historical narrative** | Excluded unless it contains a separable ethical instruction |
| **Theological assertion** | Not evaluated as a truth claim; behavioural content may be extracted where present |

---

## Anticipated Outcome

No final ethical result is assumed in advance.

The original project provides a comparison target, but it must not become an extraction template for E3.

If the Qur'an-anchored analysis independently produces substantially the same core principles, this would strengthen the hypothesis that the convergence is not simply an artefact of choosing the Psalms as the original anchor text.

If significant differences emerge, those differences are equally important and will be preserved rather than normalised away.

> **Convergence is a result to be tested, not a result to be imposed.**

---

## Relationship to the Original Study

E3 does not replace or revise the original Psalms-anchored work.

The two analyses are intended to remain independently inspectable:

> **Psalm-anchored derivation ≠ Qur'an-anchored derivation**

Their later comparison will therefore constitute a further test of the broader *Unified Paths of Light* hypothesis.
