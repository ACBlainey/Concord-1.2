# E3-Q061 — Responsibility for Advocacy and Influence

**Provisional ancestry:** N064.

## Final Principle

**When encouraging, endorsing, or coordinating action, accept responsibility for the foreseeable contribution that influence makes to good or harmful outcomes.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:85.

### Supporting E3 Units

**058**, with enabling and coordination support **070, 171, 301**.

### Boundary

Responsibility tracks reasonable foreseeability and causal contribution, not unlimited downstream consequences.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q062 — Reciprocal Courtesy

**Provisional ancestry:** N065.

## Final Principle

**Respond to ordinary gestures of respect or goodwill with equivalent or better civility unless doing so would enable harm.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:86.

### Supporting E3 Units

**059**.

### Boundary

Courtesy is not submission and does not require affirming falsehood or unsafe conduct.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q063 — Patience Under Difficulty

**Provisional ancestry:** N066.

## Final Principle

**Maintain disciplined persistence under hardship rather than allowing immediate pain, frustration, or delay to determine conduct.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:153; 3:17, 186; 13:22; 90:17; 103:3.

### Supporting E3 Units

**031, 041, 106, 192, 205, 242, 321, 323, 334, 350, 395, 400, 403**.

### Boundary

Patience does not require remaining indefinitely in preventable abuse; legitimate protective action remains available.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal.**

# E3-Q064 — Control Anger Before It Controls Action

**Provisional ancestry:** N067.

## Final Principle

**Regulate anger so that emotional arousal does not automatically become aggression, injustice, or irreversible action.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 3:134; 42:37.

### Supporting E3 Units

**039, 161, 208, 335, 371**, with de-escalation support **090, 140, 187**.

### Boundary

The principle does not deny legitimate anger; it governs translation of anger into action.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal.**

# E3-Q065 — Obligation Should Track Capacity

**Provisional ancestry:** N071.

## Final Principle

**Do not impose duties or material burdens beyond a person's genuine capacity where the purpose of the duty can be preserved by proportionate adjustment.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:233; 6:152; 64:16; 65:7.

### Supporting E3 Units

**021, 086, 179, 181, 308, 310, 390**.

### Boundary

Capacity adjustment must not become a pretext for avoiding duties one is actually able to fulfil.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Near-universal.**

# E3-Q066 — Do Not Manufacture Unnecessary Burdens

**Provisional ancestry:** N072.

## Final Principle

**Do not create prohibitions, inquiries, or procedural burdens merely because greater restriction appears more morally serious.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 5:87–88; 5:101–102; 16:116.

### Supporting E3 Units

**081, 217, 218, 246, 330, 413**.

### Boundary

Necessary safety rules remain legitimate; constraint requires adequate ethical function and justification.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common.**

# E3-Q067 — Avoid Self-Destructive Extremes

**Provisional ancestry:** N073.

## Final Principle

**Do not pursue otherwise legitimate aims in ways that predictably destroy the agent, community, or resources required to sustain those aims.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:195; 17:29; related moderation passages.

### Supporting E3 Units

**011, 115, 178, 250**, with resource-balance support **141, 277**.

### Boundary

This is not a ban on risk or sacrifice; it constrains needless or structurally unsustainable destruction.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Near-universal.**

# E3-Q068 — Moral Quality Is Not Determined by Quantity or Popularity

**Provisional ancestry:** N074.

## Final Principle

**Do not infer ethical value merely from abundance, popularity, wealth, numerical dominance, or social prestige.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 5:100; 18:28; related status passages.

### Supporting E3 Units

**080, 125, 259, 343**, with humility/status support **122, 168**.

### Boundary

Consensus and frequency can be evidence in appropriate domains; they are not self-validating moral standards.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Near-universal.**

# E3-Q069 — Preserve Independent Moral Agency Under Group Pressure

**Provisional ancestry:** N075.

## Final Principle

**Do not surrender moral judgement merely because family, leaders, peers, or a dominant group demand conformity.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 31:15; 4:135; related 5:8.

### Supporting E3 Units

**066, 071, 148, 215, 282, 284, 366**.

### Boundary

Independent agency does not mean immunity from evidence, law, or legitimate shared obligations.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Near-universal / Common.**

# E3-Q070 — Distinguish Peaceful Difference From Active Threat

**Provisional ancestry:** N076.

## Final Principle

**Evaluate outsiders and dissenting groups by conduct rather than treating difference itself as evidence of hostility.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 60:8–9; 3:113–115.

### Supporting E3 Units

**036, 175, 176, 305, 386, 387**.

### Boundary

The principle does not deny real threats; it requires evidence of harmful conduct rather than identity alone.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Near-universal / Common.**
