## Implication for the Concord

The Concord's Non-Harm law appears to sit at approximately the correct abstraction level for a cross-traditional constitutional kernel.

The E3 evidence does not establish the Concord's complete technical harm function merely by religious convergence. It does, however, independently support the deeper proposition that **unjustified harm limitation and protection are universal ethical attractors across the eight comparison traditions**.

## Broader methodological implication

This demonstrates why the next stage should audit all ten higher-order attractors.

A narrow prevalence table can understate deep convergence when different traditions instantiate the same ethical attractor through different operational descendants.

Conversely, parent compression can overstate convergence if applied loosely. The PA-S controls must therefore remain stricter than thematic similarity.
