# E3-N061 — Use Speech to Reduce Rather Than Manufacture Conflict

## Normalised Principle

**Choose forms of speech that reduce unnecessary hostility and avoid language whose predictable function is to provoke reciprocal degradation or conflict.**

### Operational Meaning

Speech has system effects. A statement can be factually expressive yet still be irresponsibly designed to trigger cycles of retaliation.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 6:108; 17:53; 25:63.

### Supporting E3 Units

**083, 123, 140, 221, 257, 276, 361**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- do not provoke reciprocal desecration;
- use the best available speech;
- answer provocation calmly;
- conflict-sensitive communication.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Conflict-sensitive speech does not require concealment of truth or prohibition of legitimate criticism.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong parallels occur across the comparison set.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N062 — No Gossip, Backbiting, or Malicious Information Transfer

## Normalised Principle

**Do not circulate personal information primarily to degrade, divide, or injure another person when there is no legitimate protective or accountability purpose.**

### Operational Meaning

Information can cause harm through transmission even when the speaker is not the original source. Social systems therefore need norms governing secondary dissemination.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 49:12; 68:10–13.

### Supporting E3 Units

**166, 182, 296, 311, 377, 391**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- backbiting;
- gossip;
- slander;
- malicious transmission;
- divisive speech.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Truthful whistleblowing, evidence-sharing, and protective reporting are not gossip merely because they damage reputation. Purpose, evidence, and legitimate need matter.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong equivalents occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N063 — Do Not Amplify Unverified Harmful Claims

## Normalised Principle

**Do not repeat consequential allegations merely because they are circulating; transmission creates responsibility as well as original authorship.**

### Operational Meaning

Rumour systems scale harm. Each participant in a transmission chain can either dampen or amplify unsupported claims.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 24:11–16; 4:83; 49:6.

### Supporting E3 Units

**057, 134, 163, 269, 293**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- rumour control;
- verify before forwarding;
- information amplification;
- sensitive information handling;
- transmission responsibility.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

This principle overlaps N003 but governs dissemination rather than consequential institutional action. The distinction is operationally useful in information systems.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong functional equivalents occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N064 — Responsibility for Advocacy and Influence

## Normalised Principle

**When encouraging, endorsing, or coordinating action, accept responsibility for the foreseeable contribution that influence makes to good or harmful outcomes.**

### Operational Meaning

Agency can operate through persuasion and coordination as well as direct action. Influence therefore carries ethical responsibility.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:85.

### Supporting E3 Units

**058**, with enabling/coordination support **070, 171, 301**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- responsibility for advocacy;
- contribution to collective action;
- influence accountability;
- indirect participation.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Responsibility should track reasonable foreseeability and causal contribution, not unlimited downstream consequences.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Functional equivalents occur widely but are not always formulated explicitly.

**Preliminary classification: Common / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N065 — Reciprocal Courtesy

## Normalised Principle

**Respond to ordinary gestures of respect or goodwill with equivalent or better civility, unless doing so would enable harm.**

### Operational Meaning

Small reciprocal behaviours maintain low-cost social trust and reduce needless friction.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:86.

### Supporting E3 Units

**059**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- return greetings;
- reciprocal courtesy;
- civility;
- positive reciprocity.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Courtesy is not submission and does not require affirming falsehood or unsafe conduct.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong equivalents occur across the comparison traditions.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain provisionally.** It may later sit beneath a broader reciprocity principle, but its social-friction function is independently clear.

# E3-N066 — Patience Under Difficulty

## Normalised Principle

**Maintain disciplined persistence under hardship rather than allowing immediate pain, frustration, or delay to determine conduct.**

### Operational Meaning

Long-horizon cooperation requires the ability to tolerate temporary difficulty without abandoning legitimate commitments or escalating destructively.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:153; 3:17; 3:186; 13:22; 90:17; 103:3.

### Supporting E3 Units

**031, 041, 106, 192, 205, 242, 321, 323, 400, 403**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- patience;
- endurance;
- restraint under insult;
- perseverance;
- mutual reinforcement of patience.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Patience is not a requirement to remain indefinitely in preventable abuse. Protective action remains available.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Patience/endurance is strongly represented across all eight traditions.

**Preliminary classification: Universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N067 — Control Anger Before It Controls Action

## Normalised Principle

**Regulate anger so that emotional arousal does not automatically become aggression, injustice, or irreversible action.**

### Operational Meaning

Anger can carry information about injury while still being a poor sole decision rule. Ethical systems should preserve a gap between emotion and consequential response.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 3:134; 42:37.

### Supporting E3 Units

**039, 161, 208**, with de-escalation support **090, 140, 187**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- anger restraint;
- emotional self-regulation;
- non-reactivity;
- forgiveness after anger.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

The principle does not deny legitimate anger or require emotional suppression. It governs translation of anger into action.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong equivalents occur throughout the comparison set.

**Preliminary classification: Universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain.**

# E3-N068 — Correct Wrongdoing Without Permanent Moral Fixation

## Normalised Principle

**Permit correction after wrongdoing and do not treat a person's worst act as permanently exhausting their capacity for ethical change.**

### Operational Meaning

Accountability and developmental possibility coexist. Systems should preserve routes back to trustworthy participation where evidence of reform justifies them.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 5:39; 25:70–71; 39:53.

### Supporting E3 Units

**074, 142, 156**, with forgiveness/reform links **012, 068, 241**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- repentance;
- reform;
- correction;
- moral recovery;
- non-permanent exclusion.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Restored trust can be gradual and evidence-sensitive. Forgiveness does not erase safeguarding.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong equivalents occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain, while noting overlap with N018.** Final consolidation should test whether N018 and N068 are distinct enough to remain separate; N018 focuses accountability architecture, N068 developmental identity.

# E3-N069 — Kindness When Refusing a Request

## Normalised Principle

**Where a legitimate request cannot be met, refuse without unnecessary humiliation or contempt.**

### Operational Meaning

Resource limits do not remove the duty to preserve dignity. Ethical systems should distinguish inability to provide from permission to degrade.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 17:28.

### Supporting E3 Units

**114**, with dignity support **194, 322, 402**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- humane refusal;
- respectful communication under scarcity;
- requester dignity.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Kindness does not require granting unsafe, illegitimate, or impossible requests.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong functional equivalents occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain provisionally.** It may ultimately become an application under dignity and generosity rather than a top-level principle.

# E3-N070 — Do Not Reward Performative Virtue Over Substance

## Normalised Principle

**Evaluate ethical conduct by substantive effects and sincere practice rather than by display, reputation, or claims to moral status.**

### Operational Meaning

Systems that reward visible signalling without checking behaviour become vulnerable to moral theatre and capture.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:264; 4:38; 61:2–3; 107:4–7.

### Supporting E3 Units

**025, 054, 177, 199, 306, 326, 388, 406**.

### Merged Proposition Families

- performative giving;
- saying without doing;
- showing off;
- reputation without substance;
- behavioural verification of virtue.

### Boundary / Non-Equivalence Notes

Public communication about good work can be legitimate where it supports transparency, coordination, or learning. The target is status-seeking that substitutes for substance.

### Cross-Traditional Prevalence

Strong anti-hypocrisy and anti-display strands occur widely.

**Preliminary classification: Universal / Near-universal.**

### Normalisation Decision

**Retain provisionally.** It overlaps N008 but adds an institutional incentive dimension that may justify preservation.
