## Q002 — Epistemic Responsibility

Strong direct anchors exist in Qur'an and Confucian Analects 2.17. Other traditions strongly value wisdom, truth and epistemic humility, but those parent values do not automatically instantiate the E3 rule against consequential assertion beyond knowledge.

**Current result:** **Near-universal/Common boundary pending exact rows.**

---

## Q003 — Verify Before Consequential Action

Christian/Jewish evidence is strengthened by Deuteronomy 19:15–19 and Proverbs 18:17. Broad Buddhist/Hindu/Confucian inquiry traditions are insufficient by themselves to prove the operational verification rule.

**Current result:** **Common / Near-universal.** Do not count generic wisdom as verification.

---

## Q004 — Reliable Testimony and Evidence

Exodus 20:16 and Deuteronomy 19 provide strong biblical/Jewish evidence. False-speech rules elsewhere support truthful communication but only count for Q004 where testimony/evidentiary responsibility is actually represented.

**Current result:** **Near-universal exact; Universal parent truthfulness.**

## Q005 — Accurate Attribution of Responsibility

Individual responsibility and anti-scapegoating analogues are widespread, but the final register must distinguish general karmic/moral responsibility from explicit prohibition on transferring guilt to an innocent party.

**Current result:** **Near-universal / strong Common.**

## Q007 — Entrusted Responsibility

Trusteeship and faithful discharge of entrusted roles recur widely. Evidence must establish entrusted responsibility, not merely generic honesty.

**Current result:** **Near-universal functional.**

---

## Q008 — Integrity Between Declared Principle and Conduct

Christian/Jewish hypocrisy critiques, Sikh truthful-living ethics and Confucian self-examination strongly support alignment between professed norm and conduct. Comparable anti-hypocrisy themes exist elsewhere but need exact source normalisation.

**Current result:** **Strong high-tier candidate; exact Universal status open.**

---

## Q009 — Self-Critical Humility

Bhagavad Gita 13.8 supplies direct humility/unpretentiousness evidence; Analects 1.4 supplies explicit self-examination; Christian/Jewish and Buddhist humility/self-correction material is strong. African evidence for humility is stronger than evidence for the exact self-critical mechanism.

**Current result:** **Near-universal secure; possible Universal parent humility.**

---

## Q010 — Open and Discriminating Inquiry

Many traditions value learning, listening and discernment. Fewer explicitly combine openness to alternatives with discriminating selection of the best-supported course.

**Current result:** **Near-universal / Common.**

## Register 001 Finding

The first ten principles confirm the central audit rule:

> **Truth is broadly universal; the procedural decomposition of truth into epistemic restraint, verification and evidentiary machinery is less uniformly explicit.**

This difference should remain visible rather than being compressed away during cross-traditional counting.
