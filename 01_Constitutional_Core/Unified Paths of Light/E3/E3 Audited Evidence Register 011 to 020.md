# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Audited Comparative Evidence Register — Q011–Q020

**Status:** Working row-level register.

---

## Q011 — Protection of Life

Non-killing/non-injury evidence is exceptionally broad: biblical homicide prohibitions, Buddhist first precept, Bhagavad Gita non-injury language, Sikh dignity/equality traditions, Confucian humane ethics and identified African life/human-value ethics.

**Current result:** **Strong Universal candidate.** Prefer “Protection of Life / prohibition of unjust killing” when comparing traditions rather than requiring identical theological language of sanctity.

## Q012 — Non-Aggression and Proportionality

Restraint in violence is widespread, but exact proportionality doctrines vary and some traditions articulate stronger pacifist norms.

**Current result:** **Near-universal broad restraint; Common/Near-universal exact compound.**

## Q013 — Legitimate Self-Defence and Protection Against Oppression

Traditions differ substantially over permissible force, pacifism, just-war conditions and duties to defend others.

**Current result:** **Common.** No Universal inference from the general value of life.

## Q014 — Peace and De-Escalation

Peacemaking and non-hatred are strongly recurrent. Matthew 5:9, Romans 12:18–21 and Buddhist non-hatred material provide direct anchors; other traditions have strong harmony/peace evidence.

**Current result:** **Near-universal secure; Universal parent peacefulness remains plausible.**

## Q015 — Forgiveness and Reconciliation

Forgiveness, non-retaliation, restoration or reintegration recur across the comparison families, though their theological and social rationales differ.

**Current result:** **Strong Universal candidate at functional level.**

## Q016 — Justice Without Partiality

Leviticus 19:15 and Deuteronomy 16:19 directly prohibit partial judgement. Strong justice/fairness analogues recur throughout the comparison set; African justice/common-good scholarship is well established.

**Current result:** **Strong Universal candidate**, with exact “without partiality” wording requiring appropriately direct evidence in each row.

## Q017 — Procedural Fairness

Justice is universal-looking; procedure is not. A tradition's commitment to justice cannot automatically count as evidence for formal hearing, evidentiary or adjudicative safeguards.

**Current result:** **Common / Near-universal.**

## Q018 — Accountability With Possibility of Reform

Moral correction, repentance, rehabilitation, restorative justice and reform are widely recurrent, but institutional form varies.

**Current result:** **Strong Near-universal functional candidate.**

## Q019 — Do Not Shield Wrongdoing Through Loyalty or Office

Anti-corruption and impartial justice support the parent idea. The exact rule against advocacy/office/loyalty shielding a wrongdoer is more specific.

**Current result:** **Common / Near-universal.**

## Q020 — Conflict Intervention Should Restore Just Peace

Peacemaking is broad. The specific duty to intervene between conflicting parties and restore a just settlement is less uniformly articulated.

**Current result:** **Common / Near-universal.**

## Register 002 Finding

The life/justice cluster shows the same hierarchical pattern as the truth cluster:

**deep attractor:** life protection / justice / peace  
↓  
**elaboration:** proportionality / forgiveness / reform  
↓  
**operational safeguard:** due process / anti-shielding / conflict-intervention mechanism

Cross-traditional recurrence generally decreases as the principle becomes more procedurally specific.
