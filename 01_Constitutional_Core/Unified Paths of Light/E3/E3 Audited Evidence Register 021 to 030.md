# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Audited Comparative Evidence Register — Q021–Q030

**Status:** Working row-level register.

---

## Q021 — Property by Legitimate Consent

Prohibitions on theft or taking what is not given are widespread. The stronger formulation that legitimate transfer rests on consent is less uniformly explicit.

**Current result:** **Near-universal functional parent; exact consent formulation remains Near-universal/Common.**

## Q022 — Commercial Honesty and Accurate Measure

Biblical law directly condemns dishonest weights and measures. Comparable commercial honesty norms recur widely, although exact measurement language is not equally explicit in every tradition.

**Current result:** **Strong Universal candidate at commercial-honesty level; exact accurate-measure compound requires row-level confirmation.**

## Q023 — No Bribery or Corrupt Purchase of Decision

Deuteronomy 16:19 provides direct biblical/Jewish evidence. Anti-corruption is very broad, but bribery as a specific mechanism should be separately evidenced.

**Current result:** **Near-universal / Common pending exact bribery rows.**

## Q024 — Protect Property of Vulnerable or Incapacitated Persons

Protection of orphans, dependants and vulnerable persons is widespread. Fiduciary protection of their property is a narrower institutional rule.

**Current result:** **Common / Near-universal exact; strong parent vulnerability ethic.**

## Q025 — Fair Inheritance and Transfer

Inheritance systems are widespread but differ substantially in allocation, kinship structure, gender rules and normative rationale.

**Current result:** **Common.** Mere existence of inheritance custom is not evidence of the same fairness principle.

## Q026 — No Exploitative Finance

Biblical debt/interest restrictions provide strong Jewish and Christian-historical evidence. Other traditions contain anti-exploitation and restraint-of-greed norms, but exact financial mechanisms vary.

**Current result:** **Common / Near-universal at anti-exploitation level; Common for narrower mechanism.**

## Q027 — Relief for Debtors in Genuine Hardship

Debt release, mercy, generosity and protection of the poor recur, but a specific hardship-triggered debtor-relief rule is less uniform.

**Current result:** **Common / Near-universal.**

## Q028 — Resource Stewardship, Non-Waste, and Balanced Use

Non-waste, moderation and stewardship are widely represented. The exact compound joins several recurrent parent norms.

**Current result:** **Near-universal compound; individual moderation/non-waste components may approach Universal.**

## Q029 — Wealth Carries Social Obligations

Biblical poor-relief rules, Sikh sharing/service, African common-good ethics and other traditions strongly reject purely self-regarding possession of wealth.

**Current result:** **Strong Universal candidate at functional level.**

## Q030 — Generosity Without Humiliation

Generosity is exceptionally broad. The requirement that assistance preserve recipient dignity and avoid humiliating performance is more specific.

**Current result:** **Near-universal exact; Universal parent generosity.**

---

## Register 003 Finding

Economic ethics show why prevalence must distinguish **moral objective** from **institutional mechanism**.

Care for the poor, honest exchange, non-theft, moderation and social obligation are deeply recurrent. Inheritance formulae, debt mechanisms, fiduciary rules and welfare structures vary much more strongly.

> **End of Audited Comparative Evidence Register Q021–Q030**
