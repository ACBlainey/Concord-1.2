# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Near-Universal Evidence Matrix 006

**Principles:** Q068, Q069, Q070, Q071, Q074.

---

## Q068 — Moral Quality Is Not Determined by Quantity or Popularity

Traditions frequently warn against following crowds, wealth, status or worldly success as measures of moral truth. The exact epistemic rule separating popularity from moral quality is nevertheless more explicit in some traditions than others.

**Decision:** **Near-universal at functional level.**

## Q069 — Preserve Independent Moral Agency Under Group Pressure

Resistance to corrupt companions, rulers, crowds or kin pressure has strong cross-traditional parallels. However, some traditions place heavy positive weight on conformity to legitimate community and role.

E3's principle is bounded: it protects moral agency against wrongful group pressure, not individualism against all social obligation.

**Decision:** **Near-universal.**

## Q070 — Distinguish Peaceful Difference From Active Threat

This is a sophisticated conflict-classification rule. Many traditions distinguish innocent outsiders from enemies or distinguish conduct from identity, but not all formulate a general obligation to classify threat behaviourally.

**Decision:** **Common.** The broader non-harm principle is much more widespread.

## Q071 — Safe Passage and Protection for a Requesting Opponent

Sanctuary, asylum, hospitality to strangers and mercy toward enemies occur in multiple traditions. Safe passage for an active or former opponent requesting protection is more specific.

**Decision:** **Common.** Do not inflate by counting generic hospitality as equivalent.

## Q074 — Constructive Competition in Good

Many traditions encourage emulation of virtue, striving toward excellence or mutual encouragement in good conduct. The pluralistic formulation—different groups competing constructively in good—is narrower.

**Decision:** **Common / Near-universal.**

---

## Matrix 006 Finding

Conflict ethics again shows E3's tendency toward **classification before coercion**.

Q070 and Q071 are not simply “be peaceful.” They require decision-makers to distinguish peaceful difference, active threat and protected/requesting opponents before applying coercive responses.

That operational resolution is not independently present at the same level across all comparison traditions.
