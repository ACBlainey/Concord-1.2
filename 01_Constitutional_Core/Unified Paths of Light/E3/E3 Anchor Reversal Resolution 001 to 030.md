## Resolution Finding

The first thirty principles show that most apparent summary-level gaps were **granularity gaps**, not absence of the underlying ethical architecture.

The strongest genuine asymmetry in this block concerns **epistemic verification and procedural justice**. The Psalm extraction strongly supplies truth, wisdom, accountability and just rule, but does not independently decompose these into the same evidence-and-procedure machinery as E3.
