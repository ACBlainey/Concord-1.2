# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Comparative Source-Quality Audit Method

**Scope:** Comparative evidence supporting E3's seven non-anchor traditions  
**Date:** 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

The Qur'anic anchor evidence and the identity of E3-Q001–E3-Q089 are now frozen. This audit addresses a different question:

> **How strong is the documentary basis for each claimed cross-traditional equivalent?**

A principle can be correctly extracted from the Qur'an while a comparison row supporting another tradition is weakly cited. Source quality and principle identity must therefore remain separate.

The audit must not alter the frozen Qur'an-derived catalogue merely to increase or decrease convergence.

## 2. Traditions in Scope

The non-anchor comparison traditions are:

1. Christianity / biblical tradition;
2. Judaism;
3. Buddhism;
4. Hinduism;
5. Sikhism;
6. Chinese / Confucian tradition;
7. African Traditional Religions.

## 3. Source-Quality Classes

### SQ-A — Direct canonical/foundational citation

An identifiable primary or foundational text is cited closely enough that the claimed functional ethic can be checked.

Examples include a named biblical passage, Dhammapada verse, Bhagavad Gita passage, Qur'anic verse when used comparatively, Guru Granth Sahib passage, or Analects passage.

### SQ-B — Identifiable authoritative tradition source

The comparison cites an identifiable authoritative source, legal tradition, named doctrine, or well-established textual corpus, but the row is paraphrased or not pinned to the most precise passage.

### SQ-C — Generalised tradition claim

The row states that a tradition generally teaches or practices something without sufficiently precise documentary provenance.

This may be plausible but should not carry the same evidentiary weight as A or B.

### SQ-D — Unsupported, overgeneralised, or materially mismatched

No adequate source is supplied, the claim generalises beyond the evidence, or the cited material does not support the asserted functional equivalence.

## 4. Special Rule for African Traditional Religions

“African Traditional Religion” is not a single canon or uniform ethical system.

Accordingly:

- generic claims about “African tradition” cannot receive SQ-A;
- a strong row should identify a people, community, documented oral corpus, customary institution, proverb collection, ethnographic source, or named philosophical tradition;
- where no such provenance is available, the grade must remain C or D;
- the audit must prefer an honest “no single pan-African equivalent can be asserted” over manufactured universality.

This is not a penalty against oral traditions. It is a provenance rule applied to a highly plural family of traditions.

## 5. Match Grade Versus Source Grade

The existing A/B/C/D **functional-match grade** and the new SQ-A/B/C/D **source-quality grade** measure different things.

A comparison may therefore be:

- strong functional match but weakly sourced;
- weak functional match but precisely sourced;
- both strong;
- or both weak.

The final E3 prevalence tables should not count a tradition as securely present merely because a plausible functional statement was written. The supporting source must meet the chosen evidentiary threshold.

## 6. Conservative Counting Rule

For final publication-quality prevalence:

- **Secure presence:** functional A/B plus source quality A/B.
- **Provisional presence:** functional A/B but source quality C.
- **Corollary/partial:** functional C with adequate provenance.
- **Absent/unproven:** functional D, or source quality D where the claim cannot be repaired.

Where a prevalence tier changes after source audit, the reason must be documented.

## 7. Audit Sequence

1. inventory the comparative rows;
2. identify generic, summarised, or provenance-light claims;
3. prioritise rows that affect Universal/Near-universal/Common classification;
4. upgrade citations where a precise source is available;
5. downgrade unsupported claims where it is not;
6. preserve unresolved cases explicitly;
7. recalculate prevalence only after the audit;
8. keep the frozen Qur'an-derived principle identities unchanged.

## 8. Publication Rule

The final synthesis should distinguish:

> **ethical convergence detected by the comparison**

from

> **ethical convergence demonstrated with publication-quality sourcing.**

The first can guide research. The second is the stronger claim.

> **End of Comparative Source-Quality Audit Method**
