## Catalogue-Label Consistency Control

The audit also exposed a practical problem in the preliminary catalogue labels.

Some records use:

- **Universal**
- **Universal / Near-universal**
- **Near-universal**
- **Near-universal / Common**
- **Common**

These are appropriate as hypotheses during extraction, but they are not final counts.

Therefore the final prevalence synthesis must be regenerated from the **audited tradition-by-tradition evidence matrix**, not by counting the prose labels in the final catalogue files.

## Universal-Candidate Audit Closure

The Universal-candidate pass does **not** certify a final set of universal principles yet.

Its result is more useful:

1. several candidates appear genuinely strong and likely to survive;
2. many are limited mainly by citation/provenance precision;
3. Q083 needs direct verification of culpability distinctions rather than broad accountability analogies;
4. Q089 illustrates a general danger of comparing a **derived systems principle** with traditions that state only its components;
5. African Traditional evidence remains the most frequent source-provenance bottleneck.

The next source-quality pass should therefore move from principle-level prose review to an explicit **tradition × principle evidence matrix**, beginning with these Universal candidates.

That matrix will make it possible to distinguish:

> **8 plausible matches** from **8 adequately evidenced matches**.

Only the latter should become a final Universal result.
