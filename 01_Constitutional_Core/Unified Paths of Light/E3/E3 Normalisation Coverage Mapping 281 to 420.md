## Purpose

This file maps the retained E3 source-occurrence audit into the frozen provisional normalised catalogue **E3-N001–E3-N100**.

The test is deliberately asymmetric: a new principle is not created merely because a source has distinctive wording. A new N101+ principle would be justified only if a materially distinct behavioural ethic could not be represented without loss by the existing catalogue.

Multiple N-numbers indicate a compound source occurrence supporting more than one normalised principle. They do not indicate uncertainty by themselves.

## Pass Finding

For Units 281–420, the mapping identifies **no source occurrence that presently requires creation of a new N101+ principle**.

This does not mean every source is identical to another. Context, recurrence, emphasis, and implementation detail remain preserved at the evidence layer. The finding is narrower: the materially distinct behavioural content of this tranche can be represented within the existing provisional catalogue.

### Binding Exclusion

**Unit 126 remains excluded.** Unit 420 records the reason: Qur'an 18:71–82 depends upon exceptional hidden knowledge and must not be generalised into ordinary permission for harmful action.

### Consequence for Normalisation

With the full 001–420 source-occurrence corpus now mapped across the three coverage files, **no unmapped candidate has emerged that requires N101+**.

The next task is therefore not further principle generation. It is the second-order consolidation of N001–N100: merging or nesting provisional principles where their behavioural distinction does not survive independent scrutiny, while preserving all source mappings.
