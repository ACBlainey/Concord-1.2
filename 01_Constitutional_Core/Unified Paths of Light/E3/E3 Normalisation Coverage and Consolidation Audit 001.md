**Anchor Text:** Qur'an  
**Author:** Alexander C. Blainey

---

## 1. Purpose

The provisional Qur'an-derived catalogue has reached **E3-N100**.

The purpose of this audit is to prevent the normalisation stage from repeating the same error that the extraction stage deliberately avoided: continuing to generate numbered outputs after the informational purpose of the phase has changed.

The next question is no longer:

> What additional wording can be converted into another provisional principle?

It is:

> Does every materially distinct ethical proposition in the retained E3 corpus map into the provisional catalogue, and which provisional principles should now be merged, nested, or retained?

This is the beginning of the **coverage and consolidation phase**.

---

## 2. Why Expansion Is Pausing at N100

The number 100 has no methodological privilege.

Expansion is pausing because recent tranches show three clear patterns:

1. most raw source occurrences already map to established principle families;
2. newly created principles are increasingly contextual applications of earlier principles;
3. several provisional principles have already been marked as candidates for second-order merger.

Continuing directly to N101, N102, and beyond without first testing coverage would risk turning contextual nuance into artificial principle multiplicity.

Therefore the provisional catalogue is temporarily frozen at **N100** while coverage is checked.

## 5. Principles That Should Not Be Over-Merged

The following distinctions presently appear operationally important.

### Truthfulness vs Verification
One governs information production; the other governs information reception and action.

### Privacy vs Reputation
Privacy can be violated by acquiring true information; reputation can be harmed by public dissemination. The protections are related but not identical.

### Justice vs Procedure
A fair rule can be administered unfairly, and a formally fair procedure can enforce an unjust rule.

### Non-Aggression vs Self-Defence
Collapsing these could erase the distinction between initiating harm and responding to it.

### Forgiveness vs Accountability
Forgiveness must remain voluntary and compatible with safety.

### Property Consent vs Commercial Measurement
A voluntary transaction can still be fraudulent if the metric is manipulated.

### Stewardship vs Balanced Use
Stewardship is the general relationship to resources; balance is an equilibrium rule concerning extremes.

### Conscience vs Pluralism
Conscience protects the individual; pluralism governs coexistence among differing persons or groups.

### Competence vs Dignity
Role-relevant competence can justify differentiated responsibility without creating differentiated inherent worth.

### Capacity-Aware Duty vs Developmental Agency
One calibrates burden; the other governs transfer of control as capability changes.

These distinctions should survive unless later mapping shows them to be redundant in actual operation.

## 6. Exclusion Control

Former **Unit 126 — Qur'an 18:71–82** remains excluded as a general behavioural source.

No normalised principle may cite it as permission for ordinary harmful action based on asserted hidden future benefit.

The passage may support only the methodological observation that agents can possess incomplete information, an observation already independently represented by safer sources.

---

## 7. Coverage Test Required Before Catalogue Closure

The next consolidation work should create a source-to-principle mapping across the retained E3 corpus.

Each raw unit should receive one of the following dispositions:

- mapped to Nxxx;
- mapped to multiple Nxxx principles;
- duplicate source occurrence;
- contextual support only;
- excluded;
- or **unmapped candidate**.

Only the final category can justify creation of additional N101+ principles.

This reverses the burden of proof.

From this point onward:

> **A new normalised principle should be created only because a retained ethical proposition cannot be represented without material loss by N001–N100.**

This is the correct stopping discipline for the normalisation stage.

---

## 8. Expected Outcome

The final Qur'an-derived catalogue will almost certainly contain **fewer than 100 top-level principles** after second-order consolidation.

That reduction is not loss of ethical content.

It is the intended result of distinguishing:

- source occurrence;
- contextual application;
- sub-principle;
- principle;
- and systems-level synthesis.

Every removed provisional number should remain traceable to the principle under which its content survives.

# Pass 1 Conclusion

The E3 normalisation stage has now produced a sufficiently broad provisional catalogue to stop open-ended expansion and begin formal coverage testing.

**N001–N100 are therefore frozen as the provisional working set.**

No N101+ principle should be added unless the source-to-principle audit demonstrates a materially distinct ethical proposition that cannot be represented within the existing catalogue.

The next work should map the retained raw corpus against this provisional set and perform the first formal merger/nesting decisions.

> **Preserve the source. Preserve the distinction. Remove only the duplicate principle identity.**
