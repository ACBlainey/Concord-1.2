## Interim Finding

Q031–Q060 strengthens the evidence for shared high-level architecture while exposing the different **textual affordances** of the anchors.

The Psalms strongly foreground broad moral posture—truth, humility, protection, solidarity, restraint, moral accountability, and right association. The Qur'anic extraction produces many explicit procedural, relational, commercial, privacy, consent, and governance rules.

Whether those are genuinely absent from the Psalm-derived corpus cannot be decided from Paper IV's compressed synthesis alone.

The unresolved rows therefore remain a research queue rather than “differences.”
