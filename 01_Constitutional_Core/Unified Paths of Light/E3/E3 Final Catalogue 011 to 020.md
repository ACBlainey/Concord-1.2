# E3-Q011 — Sanctity and Protection of Life

**Provisional ancestry:** N011.

## Final Principle

**Do not intentionally take innocent life, and structure social responsibility around protection and preservation of life.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 5:32; 6:151; 17:33.

### Supporting E3 Units

**060, 073, 085, 118, 251, 252, 340, 356, 357**.

### Boundary

Defensive force and conflict are governed separately by proportionality and protection principles.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal.**

# E3-Q012 — Non-Aggression and Proportionality

**Provisional ancestry:** N012.

## Final Principle

**Do not initiate wrongful aggression, and where force or redress is legitimate, do not exceed the harm or necessity that justifies it.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:190–194; 16:126; 17:33; 42:40–43.

### Supporting E3 Units

**007, 010, 111, 118, 130, 159, 248, 252, 291, 357, 373**.

### Boundary

Proportionality permits restraint or forgiveness; it does not require retaliation.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Near-universal.**

# E3-Q013 — Legitimate Self-Defence and Protection Against Oppression

**Provisional ancestry:** N013.

## Final Principle

**Persons and communities may defend against serious aggression and may bear positive duties to protect people who cannot escape oppression themselves.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:75; 22:39–40; 42:41; related 49:9.

### Supporting E3 Units

**129, 159, 211, 265, 291, 375, 410**.

### Boundary

Protective purpose remains bounded by E3-Q011 and E3-Q012.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common / Near-universal boundary.**

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

# E3-Q016 — Justice Without Partiality

**Provisional ancestry:** N016; N089 nested.

## Final Principle

**Apply justice impartially despite wealth, kinship, status, hostility, group identity, or self-interest; material power must not purchase immunity from ordinary standards.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:58, 135; 5:8, 42; related 49:9 and 104:1–3.

### Supporting E3 Units

**055, 066, 071, 075, 076, 197, 215, 294, 324, 339, 341, 351, 375, 404**.

### Nested Applications

- justice against self-interest;
- justice toward disliked persons;
- no status partiality;
- wealth does not confer impunity.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal.**

# E3-Q017 — Procedural Fairness

**Provisional ancestry:** N017.

## Final Principle

**Use transparent, evidence-sensitive, and reviewable procedures where decisions materially affect another person's rights, property, family, liberty, or reputation.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:282–283; 4:35; 5:106–108; 24:6–9; 65:1–2.

### Supporting E3 Units

**029, 030, 052, 075, 076, 082, 180, 268, 309, 341**.

### Boundary

Procedure governs how decisions are reached; E3-Q016 governs impartial substantive standards.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Near-universal / Common.**

# E3-Q018 — Accountability With Possibility of Reform

**Provisional ancestry:** N018; N068 nested; N099 correction-dignity application partly nested.

## Final Principle

**Wrongdoing should have consequences, but accountability should preserve credible paths for acknowledgement, repair, reform, and restored moral standing where future safety permits.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 5:38–39; 25:68–71; 39:53; related 3:159.

### Supporting E3 Units

**012, 040, 074, 142, 156, 278**, with correction-dignity support **165, 295, 376**.

### Nested Applications

- reform changes future moral status;
- grave wrongdoing need not create permanent exclusion;
- correction should target conduct rather than permanent degradation.

### Boundary

Reform does not automatically cancel restitution, consequences, or safeguarding.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q019 — Do Not Shield Wrongdoing Through Loyalty or Office

**Provisional ancestry:** N019.

## Final Principle

**Do not use loyalty, advocacy, office, or institutional procedure to knowingly protect deliberate wrongdoing from legitimate accountability.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:105–107.

### Supporting E3 Units

**212, 411**, with functional links **066, 076, 160, 292**.

### Boundary

Representation and due process remain legitimate; corrupt concealment does not.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q020 — Conflict Intervention Should Restore Just Peace

**Provisional ancestry:** N020.

## Final Principle

**When intervention in conflict is justified, distinguish aggressor from victim, restrain ongoing aggression, and return to fair reconciliation when aggression ends.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 49:9–10.

### Supporting E3 Units

**164, 294, 375**, with related support **094, 129, 211, 265**.

### Boundary

This governs third-party or communal intervention and therefore remains distinct from individual self-defence.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common.**
