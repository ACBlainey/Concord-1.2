# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Audited Comparative Evidence Register — Q071–Q080

**Status:** Working row-level register.

---

## Q071 — Safe Passage and Protection for a Requesting Opponent

Hospitality, asylum, mercy and enemy-protection analogues exist, but the specific safe-passage rule is not uniformly represented.

**Current result:** **Common.**

## Q072 — Future-Consequence Awareness

Traditions widely require attention to consequences, moral causation, prudence, legacy or future effects, although metaphysical explanations differ.

**Current result:** **Strong Universal candidate at functional level.**

## Q073 — Resilience Under Gain and Loss

Equanimity, endurance, non-attachment and steadiness under changing fortune recur very widely.

**Current result:** **Strong Near-universal; possible Universal functional candidate.**

## Q074 — Constructive Competition in Good

Emulation of virtue and striving in good works recur, but explicit constructive competition is less uniformly framed.

**Current result:** **Common / Near-universal.**

## Q075 — Preserve Social Bonds Against Needless Severance

Kinship, community, reconciliation and relational continuity are broadly valued. The qualifier “against needless severance” prevents the principle from demanding preservation of abusive relationships.

**Current result:** **Near-universal exact; parent social-bond preservation stronger.**

## Q076 — Correct Corruption Rather Than Rename It Reform

Anti-corruption and anti-hypocrisy are broad. The second-order prohibition on legitimising corruption by relabelling it reform is more specific.

**Current result:** **Common / Near-universal.**

## Q077 — Good Association Supports Ethical Conduct

Warnings about harmful association and encouragement of virtuous companions are strongly recurrent across traditions.

**Current result:** **Strong Universal candidate.**

## Q078 — Public Infrastructure Should Serve Public Purpose

Traditions regulate rulers and common goods, but “public infrastructure” is a modern systems abstraction.

**Current result:** **Common.** Preserve as E3 systems synthesis rather than claiming direct textual universality.

## Q079 — Recognise Knowledge Without Converting It Into Domination

Humility, responsible teaching and non-domination have broad components. Their combination into a knowledge/power safeguard is less directly recurrent.

**Current result:** **Common / Near-universal composite.**

## Q080 — Do Not Obstruct Beneficial Action for Status or Gain

Envy, selfish obstruction, status-seeking and corrupt interference are widely criticised. The exact operational rule is narrower.

**Current result:** **Common / Near-universal.**

---

## Register 008 Finding

This tranche contains a mixture of deep recurrent ethics and clearly **systems-level derivations**.

The source-quality audit should not penalise E3-Q078 or Q079 for being useful systems principles, but neither should usefulness be confused with ancient textual universality.

> **End of Audited Comparative Evidence Register Q071–Q080**
