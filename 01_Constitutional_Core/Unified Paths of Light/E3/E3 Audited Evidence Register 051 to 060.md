# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Audited Comparative Evidence Register — Q051–Q060

**Status:** Working row-level register.

---

## Q051 — Authority Is a Trust, Not Ownership

Traditions widely constrain rulers, elders, teachers and office-holders through duties. Confucian political ethics, biblical stewardship and African common-good traditions are particularly relevant.

**Current result:** **Strong Near-universal; plausible Universal parent of constrained/stewardship authority.**

## Q052 — Rule-Governed Resolution of Disagreement

Justice and mediation are broad. The requirement that disputes be resolved through recognised rules rather than arbitrary power is more institutionally specific.

**Current result:** **Common / Near-universal.**

## Q053 — Common Resources Require Accountable Allocation

Stewardship and common-good duties recur widely, but explicit allocation-accountability rules for collective resources are less uniform.

**Current result:** **Common / Near-universal.**

## Q054 — Collective Responsibility to Resist Corruption

Anti-corruption is broad. Collective responsibility to oppose it rather than merely abstain individually is a stronger positive duty.

**Current result:** **Near-universal functional / Common exact.**

## Q055 — Do Not Cooperate in Wrongdoing

Non-complicity has strong analogues across traditions: refusal to aid wrongdoing, corrupt action or harmful association is widely represented.

**Current result:** **Strong Universal candidate at functional level.**

## Q056 — Purpose-Constrained Confidential Deliberation

Confidentiality and discretion occur widely, but E3's rule constrains secret deliberation by legitimate purpose rather than treating secrecy itself as good.

**Current result:** **Common.**

## Q057 — Social Cohesion Without Suppression of Difference

Confucian harmony-without-sameness is unusually direct. Other traditions strongly value social cohesion but differ in how much difference is normatively protected.

**Current result:** **Common / Near-universal exact; parent cohesion much broader.**

## Q058 — Leadership Through Gentleness and Correctability

Gentleness, humility, counsel and non-arrogant leadership recur widely.

**Current result:** **Strong Near-universal functional candidate.**

## Q059 — Use Speech to Reduce Rather Than Manufacture Conflict

Buddhist right-speech prohibitions on divisive speech strongly support this rule. Comparable biblical, Sikh, Hindu, Confucian and African speech/harmony ethics are substantial.

**Current result:** **Strong Near-universal; possible Universal conflict-reducing-speech parent.**

## Q060 — No Gossip, Backbiting, or Malicious Information Transfer

Falsehood, slander, divisive speech and reputation-harm are widely condemned. Exact gossip/backbiting terminology varies.

**Current result:** **Strong high-tier candidate; Universal parent harmful/malicious speech more secure than exact compound.**

---

## Register 006 Finding

Governance ethics again divide into two levels:

**deep constraints on power:** trust, non-complicity, anti-corruption, gentleness, social responsibility;

**institutional implementations:** rule-governed dispute resolution, accountable allocation, confidentiality constraints.

The first level travels more easily across traditions than the second.

> **End of Audited Comparative Evidence Register Q051–Q060**
