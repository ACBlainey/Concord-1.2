# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Priority Provenance Repair 013 — Q063, Q064, Q088

## Q063 — Patience Under Difficulty

The unresolved rows are Sikh P and African P.

Sikh primary ethics contains direct and repeated support for patience, endurance and steadiness under adversity. This is substantially closer to the frozen principle than a generic virtue-parent.

**Decision:** Sikh P is **eligible for S** after exact primary-source location is normalised in the final evidence register.

African material supports endurance and communal resilience in scoped traditions, but the present evidence remains insufficiently normalised for a secure pan-family claim.

**Candidate result:** Q063 may move **6/8 → 7/8**.

## Q064 — Control Anger Before It Controls Action

Sikh evidence is particularly strong. Anger is explicitly treated as a destructive vice, while self-control and ego-restraint are repeatedly commended. The already identified SGGS material places self-control together with the overcoming/removal of anger.

This directly supports the behavioural content of Q064 rather than merely a distant parent value.

**Decision:** Sikh P is **eligible for S** after exact-source normalisation.

African P remains unresolved pending named-tradition/source evidence at the anger-control level.

**Candidate result:** Q064 may move **6/8 → 7/8**.

## Q088 — Do Not Covet Another's Advantages Into Injustice

Sikh non-attachment, contentment and rejection of greed provide strong support for non-covetousness. The frozen formulation, however, links coveting another's advantages specifically to resulting injustice.

The parent ethic is secure; the consequential injustice component is not yet directly established by the present Sikh source record. African evidence likewise supports anti-greed/contentment themes but remains provenance-limited.

**Decision:** retain Sikh P and African P.

**Result:** Q088 remains **6/8**.
