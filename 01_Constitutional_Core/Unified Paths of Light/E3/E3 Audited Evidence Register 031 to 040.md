# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Audited Comparative Evidence Register — Q031–Q040

**Status:** Working row-level register.

---

## Q031 — Non-Transactional Aid

Giving without expectation of repayment, status or reciprocal gain has strong parallels in charity, almsgiving, dana, seva and communal-duty traditions.

**Current result:** **Strong Near-universal; possible Universal functional parent.**

## Q032 — Positive Duty Toward Severe Need

Christian/Jewish poor-relief commands, Buddhist generosity, Hindu giving, Sikh seva/sharing, Confucian benevolence and documented African duty/common-good ethics provide unusually broad support.

**Current result:** **Strong Universal candidate.**

## Q033 — Structured Social Provision

Many traditions organise charity or communal provision, but the exact institutionalisation differs.

**Current result:** **Common.** Universal care does not entail universal welfare machinery.

## Q034 — Mutual Aid Under Scarcity

Mutual aid is broad; scarcity-specific sharing obligations are less uniformly formulated.

**Current result:** **Near-universal exact; parent mutual aid stronger.**

## Q035 — Human Dignity Beyond Status

Anti-status moral teachings are broad. Sikh anti-caste ethics and identified Akan human-value material are particularly useful anchors. Modern universal-equality language should not be projected backwards where the source instead establishes moral worth or anti-status treatment.

**Current result:** **Near-universal secure at functional level.**

## Q036 — No Ridicule, Defamation, or Degrading Labels

Harmful-speech restrictions are exceptionally widespread. The exact compound combines ridicule, defamation and degrading labels.

**Current result:** **Near-universal compound; malicious/degrading speech parent is a strong Universal candidate.**

## Q037 — Privacy and Freedom From Intrusive Surveillance

Privacy-adjacent duties occur across traditions, but a general right against intrusive surveillance is considerably more specific.

**Current result:** **Common / Near-universal.**

## Q038 — Protection of Reputation

Slander, false accusation and malicious speech are widely condemned. Reputation as a protected interest is therefore strongly recurrent even where not expressed as a modern right.

**Current result:** **Near-universal functional.**

## Q039 — Inclusion Against Elite Capture

Anti-status and anti-partiality norms are broad. The E3 rule specifically prevents leaders/institutions from excluding low-status participants to secure elite approval.

**Current result:** **Common.** Parent dignity/anti-partiality is much broader.

## Q040 — Freedom of Conscience and Non-Compulsion

Traditions contain important anti-coercion and conscience strands, but also historically and textually diverse rules concerning religious authority, conversion, apostasy and communal membership.

**Current result:** **Common.** Do not force a Universal/Near-universal result.

## Register 004 Finding

The dignity/agency cluster reveals another recurring E3 effect:

> **protecting a person from harm is more cross-traditionally common than explicitly protecting that person's independent decision-space.**

This distinction becomes increasingly important from Q037 onward.
