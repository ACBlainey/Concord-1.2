# E3-Q071 — Safe Passage and Protection for a Requesting Opponent

**Provisional ancestry:** N077.

## Final Principle

**Where an opponent seeks temporary protection, hearing, or safe passage in good faith, preserve a protected route rather than exploiting vulnerability created by the request itself.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 9:6.

### Supporting E3 Units

**096, 232**.

### Boundary

Protection can remain security-sensitive where credible evidence indicates immediate abuse of safe passage.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common.**

# E3-Q072 — Future-Consequence Awareness

**Provisional ancestry:** N079.

## Final Principle

**Evaluate present conduct partly by its reasonably foreseeable future consequences rather than by immediate gain alone.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 59:18; related 16:94.

### Supporting E3 Units

**174, 385, 419**, with sustainability links **084, 115, 141, 170**.

### Boundary

Foreseeability is bounded; agents are not responsible for every remote or unknowable consequence.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q073 — Resilience Under Gain and Loss

**Provisional ancestry:** N080.

## Final Principle

**Do not allow either loss or success to destabilise judgement into despair, arrogance, or reckless conduct.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 57:23; related patience and anti-arrogance passages.

### Supporting E3 Units

**300**, with support **122, 168, 205, 256**.

### Boundary

The principle does not prohibit grief, joy, ambition, or celebration; it constrains loss of ethical self-command.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q075 — Preserve Social Bonds Against Needless Severance

**Provisional ancestry:** N082.

## Final Principle

**Do not break legitimate relationships, covenants, or connective social bonds without sufficient reason, especially where severance contributes to wider corruption or fragmentation.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:27; 13:25.

### Supporting E3 Units

**107, 202, 243, 332**.

### Boundary

The principle does not require preservation of abusive or illegitimate relationships; legitimate exit remains protected.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q076 — Correct Corruption Rather Than Rename It Reform
