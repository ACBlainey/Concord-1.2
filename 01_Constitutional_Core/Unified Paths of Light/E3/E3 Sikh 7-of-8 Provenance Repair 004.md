# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Sikh 7-of-8 Provenance Repair 004 — Q044, Q048

## Q044 — Child and Dependant Welfare

Sikh ethics strongly supports compassion, family responsibility, service and protection of human dignity. Sikh communal institutions also provide material support without status distinction.

The frozen principle, however, treats **children and dependants as a specifically protected welfare class**. General compassion, family regard or universal provision cannot automatically establish this narrower duty.

No sufficiently direct primary Sikh passage has yet been normalised that identifies children/dependants in the required protective formulation.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P**.

**Reason:** welfare parent is strong; protected-class specificity remains unproven.

## Q048 — Reciprocal Relational Rights and Responsibilities

Sikh ethics strongly supports mutual service, equality, responsibility and regard within relationships. These are substantial components of the frozen principle.

But Q048 requires a reciprocal architecture of **rights and responsibilities**. Evidence for duties alone does not prove reciprocal rights; evidence for equality alone does not prove the full relational structure.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P**.

**Reason:** multiple components are present, but the complete reciprocal rights-and-responsibilities formulation is not yet directly evidenced.
