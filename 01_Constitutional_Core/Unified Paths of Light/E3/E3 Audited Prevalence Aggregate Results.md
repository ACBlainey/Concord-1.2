## 2. Interpretation of the Zero Universal Result

The zero is **not evidence that no Qur'an-derived ethic is functionally universal**.

It results substantially from the decision not to count unresolved provenance as secure. Several principles remain strong Universal candidates but have one or more Sikh/African or exact-formulation rows still graded P rather than S.

The secure matrix therefore answers:

> “How many principles can presently be demonstrated at publication-grade secure level across all eight comparison families?”

It does not yet answer:

> “How many would probably prove universal after all exact-source remediation?”

The aggregate supports the qualitative finding:

**deep ethical attractor → elaboration → operational safeguard**

is associated with declining secure prevalence.

## 5. Provisional Conclusion

The Qur'an-first anchor does not yield a catalogue dominated by isolated Islamic particulars. A substantial majority of the 89 frozen behavioural principles already achieve secure recurrence in at least five of eight families.

At the same time, the audit preserves genuine asymmetry: the Qur'an-first catalogue contains an unusually rich layer of operational and procedural safeguards whose exact formulations recur less uniformly.
