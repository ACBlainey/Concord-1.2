## 1. Purpose

All retained E3 source-occurrence records have now been mapped to the provisional catalogue E3-N001–E3-N100, with Unit 126 excluded and no unmapped source requiring N101+.

This pass tests the provisional principles themselves.

The rule is conservative:

> **Merge or nest only where the behavioural distinction can be preserved without maintaining a separate top-level principle.**

A contextual application is not deleted. Its source evidence and operational qualification are transferred to the surviving parent principle.

## 3. Result of Pass 1

The first consolidation pass removes **11 provisional top-level identities** by nesting them into stronger parent principles:

**N029, N049, N063, N068, N069, N070, N078, N086, N089, N098, N099**.

The surviving catalogue is therefore no longer correctly described by its provisional N-number count. The final catalogue will receive a new continuous identifier series only after all consolidation decisions are complete.

## 4. Important Non-Mergers

Several tempting compressions were rejected.

- **N001 Truthfulness** remains distinct from **N003 Verification**.
- **N003 Verification** remains distinct from **N004 Testimony and Evidence**.
- **N011 Life Protection** remains distinct from **N012 Proportionality** and **N013 Self-Defence**.
- **N015 Forgiveness** remains distinct from **N018 Accountability/Reform**.
- **N016 Justice** remains distinct from **N017 Procedure**.
- **N021 Property Consent** remains distinct from **N022 Commercial Measurement**.
- **N026 Exploitative Finance** remains distinct from **N027 Debtor Hardship**.
- **N038 Privacy** remains distinct from **N039 Reputation**.
- **N041 Conscience** remains distinct from **N042 Plural Coexistence**.
- **N052 Competence** remains distinct from **N036 Equal Dignity**.
- **N071 Capacity-Aware Duty** remains distinct from **N096 Developmental Agency**.

These distinctions materially change either the protected interest, duty-bearer, trigger, or institutional response.
