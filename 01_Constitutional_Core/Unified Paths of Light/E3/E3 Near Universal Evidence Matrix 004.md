## Near-Universal Evidence Matrix 004

**Principles:** Q045, Q047, Q049, Q051, Q053.

---

## Q045 — Consent and Non-Coercion in Intimate Relationships

Many traditions constrain sexual or marital coercion, but their historical marriage systems and theories of consent differ substantially. A general modern consent doctrine cannot be retroactively attributed merely because a tradition prohibits some forms of sexual wrongdoing.

**Decision:** **Common / Near-universal at functional anti-coercion level; not secure Near-universal as a fully modern consent principle.**

## Q047 — Fairness in Relationship Dissolution

Divorce, separation, widowhood and dissolution are regulated across traditions, but rules differ markedly and some traditions historically restrict dissolution itself.

The existence of divorce law is not evidence of the same fairness principle.

**Decision:** **Common.** Preserve as an anchor-specific operational ethic unless stronger cross-traditional evidence is demonstrated.

## Q049 — Consultation in Shared Affairs

Consultation, counsel and deliberation occur widely. Confucian governance, biblical/Jewish counsel, Sikh collective traditions and many African consensus-oriented institutions provide strong functional routes. Yet consultation is not uniformly required as the decision rule.

**Decision:** **Near-universal / Common boundary.**

## Q051 — Authority Is a Trust, Not Ownership

The idea that rulers or office-holders are constrained by duties larger than personal preference is extremely widespread. The metaphysical source of the constraint varies: divine law, dharma, moral cultivation, communal obligation, role ethics or social welfare.

**Decision:** **Strong Near-universal; plausible Universal parent principle of constrained authority.**

## Q053 — Common Resources Require Accountable Allocation

Many traditions regulate shared goods, charity, common land, public office or communal resources. E3's formulation is a systems rule joining common ownership/use with accountable allocation.

**Decision:** **Common / Near-universal.** Broad stewardship does not itself prove an accountable common-resource allocation mechanism.

## Matrix 004 Finding

This tranche again separates **moral constraint** from **institutional machinery**.

Constrained authority appears very broad. The particular procedures by which shared resources or relationships are governed vary much more strongly by tradition.
