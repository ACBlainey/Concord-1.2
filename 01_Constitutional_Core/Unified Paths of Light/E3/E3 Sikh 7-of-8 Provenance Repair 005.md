# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Sikh 7-of-8 Provenance Repair 005 — Q058, Q067 and Band B Closure

## Q058 — Leadership Through Gentleness and Correctability

Sikh teaching provides strong evidence for humility, service, restraint of ego and leadership as responsibility rather than self-exaltation.

Those sources strongly support the **gentleness/humility** component. They do not yet securely establish the second component: leaders must remain **correctable**.

A humble leader may be more correctable, but that inference cannot substitute for evidence of accountability to correction.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P**.

## Q067 — Avoid Self-Destructive Extremes

Sikh primary evidence strongly supports self-control, contentment and resistance to destructive passions. Sikh teaching also rejects forms of empty ascetic display and emphasises disciplined life within the world.

This makes Q067 a close functional neighbour. Yet the frozen principle is framed specifically around avoiding **self-destructive extremes**, not merely moderation or self-control.

**Decision:** retain Sikh **P**.
