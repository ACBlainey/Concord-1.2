# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Parent Attractor Audit 005 — Care, Aid and Social Obligation

This audit tests whether each tradition securely establishes a positive ethical obligation toward the welfare of others, rather than merely prohibiting injury.

| Tradition | Parent status | Evidential basis |
|---|---|---|
| Qur'an / Islam | PA-S | charity, severe-need duty, structured provision, mutual aid, dependant welfare and social obligations of wealth |
| Christianity | PA-S | neighbour-love, material aid, care for poor/sick/stranger and positive service |
| Judaism | PA-S | tzedakah, gleaning, aid to poor, debt relief and communal welfare duties |
| Buddhism | PA-S | dana/generosity, compassion, care and material support |
| Hinduism | PA-S | dana, compassion, hospitality and duties of support |
| Sikhism | PA-S | seva, vand chhakna, langar, sharing and service without status distinction |
| Chinese/Confucian | PA-S | ren/benevolence, care extending through relationships and responsibility for material welfare |
| African Traditional | PA-S | communal obligation, mutual aid, hospitality, care for vulnerable persons and common-good ethics in identified traditions |

## Result

**Care, Aid and Social Obligation = 8/8 PA-S — Universal parent attractor.**

Conservative formulation:

> **Human welfare creates positive obligations: agents should provide appropriate aid, care or support to others, particularly where need or dependency makes assistance morally salient.**

The exact institutional form is not universal. Mandatory redistribution, almsgiving systems, communal provision and voluntary generosity remain distinct child mechanisms.
