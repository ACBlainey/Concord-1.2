# E3-Q021 — Property by Legitimate Consent

**Provisional ancestry:** N021.

## Final Principle

**Do not take, transfer, or consume another person's property without legitimate consent or a valid rule-governed basis.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:188; 4:29.

### Supporting E3 Units

**009, 050, 209, 336**.

### Boundary

Fraud, severe coercion, incapacity, or exploitation can invalidate apparent consent.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q022 — Commercial Honesty and Accurate Measure

**Provisional ancestry:** N022.

## Final Principle

**Use accurate weights, measures, prices, and reciprocal standards; do not obtain advantage by manipulating the metrics of exchange.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 6:152; 7:85; 11:85; 17:35; 26:181–183; 55:7–9; 83:1–3.

### Supporting E3 Units

**086, 089, 101, 120, 144, 169, 189, 238, 254, 279, 319, 344, 345, 349, 380, 397**.

### Boundary

This is an institutional exchange rule, not merely a restatement of generic truthfulness.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal.**

# E3-Q023 — No Bribery or Corrupt Purchase of Decision

**Provisional ancestry:** N023.

## Final Principle

**Do not use wealth, gifts, influence, or concealed transfers to corrupt adjudication or obtain what is not legitimately yours.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:188.

### Supporting E3 Units

**009**, with functional links **055, 066, 071, 076**.

### Boundary

A transfer becomes corrupt where it is intended or reasonably likely to distort entrusted judgement.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q024 — Protect Property of Vulnerable or Incapacitated Persons

**Provisional ancestry:** N024.

## Final Principle

**Where another person cannot yet safely manage property, hold it as a protected trust and return control when capability is established.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:2, 5–10; 6:152; 17:34.

### Supporting E3 Units

**043, 046, 048, 086, 119, 253**.

### Boundary

Protective stewardship is temporary and purpose-bound, not permanent paternalism.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Near-universal / Common.**

# E3-Q025 — Fair Inheritance and Transfer

**Provisional ancestry:** N025.

## Final Principle

**Distribute inherited or transferred resources through recognised rules that protect legitimate claims and prevent powerful parties from consuming the shares of weaker parties.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:7–10; related 2:180–182.

### Supporting E3 Units

**008, 047, 048, 190, 320, 398**.

### Boundary

The functional ethic does not automatically constitutionalise every historical allocation formula.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q026 — No Exploitative Finance

**Provisional ancestry:** N026.

## Final Principle

**Do not structure financial relationships so that another person's vulnerability becomes a mechanism for compounding extraction or unbounded gain.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:275–281; 3:130.

### Supporting E3 Units

### Boundary

The final principle preserves the anti-exploitation ethic without pre-deciding every technical question concerning modern interest, inflation, risk pricing, or investment.

# E3-Q027 — Relief for Debtors in Genuine Hardship

**Provisional ancestry:** N027.

## Final Principle

**Where a debtor genuinely cannot meet an obligation, provide time, restructuring, or remission rather than using hardship as an opportunity for further coercion.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:280.

### Supporting E3 Units

**028**.

### Boundary

The trigger is genuine incapacity, not deliberate fraud or strategic refusal.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common.**

# E3-Q028 — Resource Stewardship, Non-Waste, and Balanced Use

**Provisional ancestry:** N028; N029 nested.

## Final Principle

**Use resources responsibly and avoid both destructive waste and unsustainable extremes of extravagance or miserliness.**

### Operational Meaning

Resources are finite and socially consequential. Stewardship therefore constrains waste while also rejecting a one-sided austerity ethic that treats non-use as automatically virtuous.

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 6:141; 7:31; 17:26–29; 20:81; 25:67; 28:77; 57:7.

### Supporting E3 Units

**084, 087, 113, 115, 128, 141, 146, 170, 222, 250, 264, 277, 281, 299, 355, 362, 364, 381**.

### Nested Applications

- no waste;
- moderation in consumption;
- avoid extravagance;
- avoid destructive miserliness;
- preserve future resource capacity.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q030 — Generosity Without Humiliation

**Provisional ancestry:** N031; N069 nested.

## Final Principle

**Give material help in ways that preserve the recipient's dignity; where a legitimate request cannot be met, refuse without unnecessary humiliation or contempt.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 2:261–264, 267, 268–274; 17:28; 93:9–10.

### Supporting E3 Units

**025, 026, 027, 114, 188, 194, 317, 322, 396, 402**.

### Nested Applications

- give without injury;
- discreet aid;
- humane refusal;
- do not convert assistance into reputational leverage.

### Boundary

This constrains the manner of aid; it does not require granting unsafe, illegitimate, or impossible requests.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**
