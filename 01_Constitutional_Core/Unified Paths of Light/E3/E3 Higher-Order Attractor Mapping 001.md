# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Higher-Order Attractor Mapping 001

This file maps the frozen Q catalogue to provisional higher-order ethical attractors. Mapping is functional and many-to-many.

## A1 — Non-Harm and Protection

Primary members: **Q011, Q012, Q013, Q014, Q020, Q024, Q032, Q044, Q045, Q046, Q055, Q067, Q070, Q071, Q081, Q082, Q083, Q084, Q085, Q086.**

The family spans prevention of injury, protection from oppression/exploitation, safeguarding vulnerable persons, proportional response, safe boundaries and repair.

## A2 — Truth, Knowledge and Epistemic Integrity

Primary members: **Q001–Q010, Q060, Q061, Q079.**

Truth is not only truthful speech. E3 decomposes it into verification, testimony, attribution, intellectual humility, inquiry and responsibility for informational influence.

## A3 — Justice, Fairness and Accountability

Primary members: **Q004, Q005, Q016–Q020, Q023–Q027, Q047, Q052–Q055, Q082, Q083.**

This includes impartiality, procedure, responsibility, anti-corruption, reform, dispute resolution and repair.

## A4 — Autonomy, Consent and Agency

Primary members: **Q021, Q037, Q040–Q43, Q045–Q48, Q65–Q70, Q86–Q88.**

This family includes consent, conscience, privacy, refusal, capacity-sensitive obligation, resistance to group pressure and developmental agency.

## A5 — Care, Aid and Social Obligation

Primary members: **Q024, Q027, Q029–Q35, Q044, Q048, Q062, Q075, Q077.**

This includes material duty, severe need, structured provision, mutual aid, dependant welfare and social bonds.

## A6 — Trust, Responsibility and Institutional Integrity

Primary members: **Q006–Q008, Q019, Q023, Q049–Q58, Q061, Q078, Q080, Q089.**

This includes promises, entrusted responsibility, competent authority, consultation, accountable allocation, anti-corruption and institutional trust.

## A7 — Peace, Reconciliation and Social Cohesion

Primary members: **Q012–Q15, Q020, Q036, Q038–Q42, Q57–Q60, Q62–Q64, Q70–Q71, Q75, Q77, Q82.**

The family spans de-escalation, forgiveness, respectful disagreement, conflict-reducing speech and restoration.

## A8 — Self-Regulation, Resilience and Proportionality

Primary members: **Q009, Q012, Q063–Q69, Q72–Q74, Q81, Q88.**

This includes humility, patience, anger control, capacity, avoidance of extremes, independence from group pressure and resilience.

## A9 — Stewardship and Responsible Resource Use

Primary members: **Q021–Q34, Q053, Q078, Q080.**

This includes legitimate property, honest exchange, anti-exploitation, balanced use, social obligations of wealth and accountable common resources.

## A10 — Dignity, Equality and Anti-Domination

Primary members: **Q016, Q024, Q035–Q48, Q51, Q65, Q69–Q71, Q79–Q80, Q84–Q87.**

This includes dignity beyond status, inclusion, conscience, bodily boundaries, refusal, protection from domination and expanding agency.

## Structural observation

The frozen catalogue is not organised as ten isolated moral commandments. It behaves more like an overlapping network: operational rules sit at intersections between several higher-order attractors.

That is especially clear for harm. Q046, for example, is simultaneously a Non-Harm rule, an Autonomy rule and a Dignity/Anti-Domination rule.
