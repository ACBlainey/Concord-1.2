# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Source-Quality Remediation Plan

---

## 1. Objective

Upgrade the comparative evidence without changing the frozen identity of E3-Q001–E3-Q089.

The target is not to make every tradition match every principle. The target is to make every retained presence/absence claim **auditable**.

## 2. Required Evidence Record

For each tradition/principle relationship retained in the final synthesis, record:

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| E3 principle | Q identifier |
| Tradition | One of seven non-anchor traditions |
| Functional grade | A/B/C/D |
| Source-quality grade | SQ-A/B/C/D |
| Source | Identifiable text, corpus, community, institution, or scholarly documentation |
| Passage/location | As precise as reasonably possible |
| Functional note | Why the source does or does not instantiate the principle |
| Decision | Secure / provisional / corollary / absent-unproven |

This separates evidence from interpretation.

## 3. Remediation Order

### Pass 1 — Universal candidates

Audit every tradition supporting principles provisionally labelled Universal.

Any weak eighth-tradition citation can change an 8/8 result, so these rows have highest information value.

### Pass 2 — Near-universal candidates

Audit the potentially missing tradition and the weakest positive traditions. Determine whether the result is genuinely 7/8 or merely an artefact of source quality.

### Pass 3 — Common candidates

Prioritise boundary cases near the 5/8 threshold.

### Pass 4 — Limited principles

These matter for completeness but are less likely to alter the headline synthesis.

## 4. Tradition-Specific Remediation

### African Traditional Religions

Replace generic pan-African claims with specific provenance wherever possible:

- named people/community;
- named oral or proverb corpus;
- documented customary institution;
- named philosophical tradition such as Ubuntu only where the source genuinely represents the claimed proposition;
- scholarly ethnographic documentation where primary textual form is unavailable.

If this cannot be done, retain C/D rather than inventing precision.

### Confucian / Chinese

Prefer precise Analects, Mencius, Xunzi, Doctrine of the Mean, or other identifiable classical passages appropriate to the claim.

### Sikh

Prefer precise Guru Granth Sahib references or another clearly identified authoritative Sikh source.

### Hindu

Specify the actual source—e.g. Bhagavad Gita, Mahabharata, Upanishadic or dharma-text material—rather than citing “Hinduism” generically.

### Judaism

Prefer Tanakh passages or clearly identified rabbinic/legal sources. Avoid unsupported claims about what Judaism “generally” teaches.

### Buddhism

Retain identifiable canonical references and distinguish early Buddhist canonical material from later schools where relevant.

### Christianity

Use identifiable biblical passages and avoid treating later Christian doctrine as scriptural evidence unless explicitly labelled.

## 5. No-Fabrication Rule

A weak source is not repaired by attaching a plausible citation from memory.

Every upgrade must be checked against the actual source or a reliable scholarly representation of it.

Where verification is unavailable:

> **downgrade the certainty, not the standard.**

## 6. Effect on Existing E3 Work

The following remain frozen:

- canonical audit closure;
- source-occurrence record;
- normalisation logic;
- E3-Q001–E3-Q089 identities;
- anchor-reversal crosswalk as a historical analysis of the two derived catalogues.

The following remain open to correction:

- individual non-Qur'anic comparative citations;
- source-quality grades;
- functional grades where evidence does not support them;
- cross-traditional prevalence tiers;
- final claims about universality.

## 7. Completion Criterion

The source audit is complete when every tradition counted toward a final prevalence tier has:

1. identifiable provenance;
2. an explicit functional rationale;
3. a source-quality grade;
4. and no unresolved citation whose failure would change the tier.

Only then should E3 produce its final equivalent of Paper IV.

## 8. Research Integrity Principle

The source-quality audit may reduce apparent convergence.

That is acceptable.

A lower convergence figure supported by auditable evidence is methodologically stronger than a higher figure built partly from generic tradition summaries.

> **Preserve the derivation. Strengthen the evidence. Let the prevalence fall where the sources place it.**
