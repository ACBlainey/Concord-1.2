# E3 Unit 401 — Qur'an 92:5–10
## Generosity and Miserliness Produce Different Social Trajectories

### Qur'anic Source
Qur'an 92:5–10 contrasts giving and moral restraint with miserliness and self-sufficiency.

### Extracted Ethical Core
- Repeated allocation choices shape character and system trajectory.
- Perceived self-sufficiency can weaken recognition of interdependence.
- Generosity and withholding are not isolated acts when they become patterns.
- Ethical systems should consider feedback loops created by repeated conduct.

### Comparative Analysis

| Tradition / Text | Equivalent | Grade | Reduced Core |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Christianity | Generosity and warnings against self-sufficient wealth strongly converge. | A/B | Repeated giving or withholding shapes moral trajectory. |
| Judaism | Tzedakah and warnings against hard-heartedness strongly converge. | A | Generosity sustains social obligation. |
| Buddhism | Dana and non-attachment directly oppose possessive self-sufficiency. | A | Giving weakens attachment. |
| Hinduism | Dana and rejection of greed provide strong parallels. | A/B | Generosity supports ethical development. |
| Sikhism | Sharing and rejection of greed strongly converge. | A | Wealth should circulate through service. |
| Confucianism | Benevolence is preferred to profit-centred conduct. | B | Habitual allocation shapes social character. |
| African Traditional | Sharing systems vary. | B | Reciprocity can sustain community over time. |

### Conclusion
**Audit disposition: merge with Unit 193 and generosity/miserliness cluster.**

# E3 Unit 402 — Qur'an 93:9–10
## Vulnerable Petitioners Should Not Be Humiliated

### Qur'anic Source
Qur'an 93:9–10 prohibits oppressing the orphan and rebuking the person who asks.

### Extracted Ethical Core
- Low power should not invite harsh treatment.
- The dignity of an applicant matters independently of whether assistance is granted.
- Social-support interfaces can themselves inflict harm.
- Institutions should avoid making vulnerability a condition for humiliation.
- Refusal, where necessary, should remain humane.

- Religious difference does not itself justify forced conformity.

# E3 Unit 410 — Qur'an 4:75
## Protection of the Oppressed Can Create Positive Duties

### Qur'anic Source
Qur'an 4:75 appeals to action on behalf of men, women, and children described as oppressed and seeking release from an unjust environment.

### Extracted Ethical Core
- Non-harm may require intervention rather than neutrality.
- Vulnerability is intensified when people cannot escape oppression themselves.
- Protective duties can extend beyond one's immediate self-interest.
- Women, children, and other low-power persons must not disappear from security analysis.
- Intervention should remain connected to liberation from oppression rather than domination for its own sake.
