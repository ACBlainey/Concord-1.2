# E3-Q051 — Authority Is a Trust, Not Ownership

**Provisional ancestry:** N053.

## Final Principle

**Treat public or institutional authority as a purpose-bound trust constrained by justice, responsibility, and the interests it exists to serve.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:58–59; related stewardship and judgement passages.

### Supporting E3 Units

**055, 056, 075, 076, 160, 170, 292, 299, 351, 381**.

### Boundary

The principle constrains authority without prescribing one constitutional form.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Near-universal.**

# E3-Q052 — Rule-Governed Resolution of Disagreement

**Provisional ancestry:** N054.

## Final Principle

**When legitimate disagreement cannot be resolved informally, use recognised procedures and standards rather than allowing raw power to determine the outcome.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:35, 59; 8:1.

### Supporting E3 Units

**052, 056, 225, 416**, with procedural support **017, 075, 180**.

### Boundary

Recognised procedure remains constrained by substantive justice and procedural fairness.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

---

# E3-Q053 — Common Resources Require Accountable Allocation

**Provisional ancestry:** N055.

## Final Principle

**Resources created, captured, or held through collective action should be allocated through legitimate and accountable rules rather than private seizure or factional power.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 8:1; 59:7.

### Supporting E3 Units

**097, 225, 303, 313, 416**.

### Boundary

The principle applies to legitimately common or jointly generated resources, not automatically to all property.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common.**

# E3-Q054 — Collective Responsibility to Resist Corruption

**Provisional ancestry:** N056.

## Final Principle

**Communities should maintain constrained mechanisms that identify, discourage, and correct conduct that predictably corrupts social order or harms others.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 3:104; 7:56; 11:116–117; 13:25.

### Supporting E3 Units

**035, 088, 102, 107, 202, 224, 239, 243, 270**.

### Boundary

Collective correction must itself remain constrained by evidence, proportionality, conscience, and non-aggression.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q055 — Do Not Cooperate in Wrongdoing

**Provisional ancestry:** N057.

## Final Principle

**Cooperate in legitimate good, but do not knowingly contribute resources, coordination, or institutional support to wrongful harm or oppression.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 5:2; 58:8–10.

### Supporting E3 Units

**067, 070, 171, 212, 220, 301, 411**.

### Boundary

Responsibility should track reasonable knowledge and causal relevance, not remote association alone.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q056 — Purpose-Constrained Confidential Deliberation

**Provisional ancestry:** N058.

## Final Principle

**Private coordination is legitimate for constructive purposes, but secrecy should not be used to conceal aggression, corruption, intimidation, or betrayal.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 4:114; 58:8–10.

### Supporting E3 Units

**062, 171, 301, 382**.

### Boundary

Transparency is not absolute; legitimate privacy, negotiation, safeguarding, and confidential advice remain protected.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Common / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q057 — Social Cohesion Without Suppression of Difference

**Provisional ancestry:** N059.

## Final Principle

**Preserve cooperative unity where possible, but do not equate cohesion with forced uniformity or suppression of legitimate disagreement.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 3:103; 8:46; related pluralism passages.

### Supporting E3 Units

**034, 092, 147, 175, 200, 228, 305, 327, 407**.

### Boundary

Cohesion cannot be invoked to silence legitimate dissent.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q058 — Leadership Through Gentleness and Correctability

**Provisional ancestry:** N060; correction-dignity application from N099.

## Final Principle

**Exercise leadership without needless harshness, remain open to consultation and correction, and preserve dignity when correcting others.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 3:159; 20:43–44.

### Supporting E3 Units

**040, 127, 165, 263, 295, 376**, with consultation support **158, 290, 372**.

### Boundary

Gentleness does not prohibit firm action against serious harm.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

# E3-Q059 — Use Speech to Reduce Rather Than Manufacture Conflict

**Provisional ancestry:** N061.

## Final Principle

**Choose forms of speech that reduce unnecessary hostility and avoid language whose predictable function is to provoke reciprocal degradation or conflict.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 6:108; 17:53; 25:63.

### Supporting E3 Units

**083, 123, 140, 221, 257, 276, 361**.

### Boundary

Conflict-sensitive speech does not require concealment of truth or prohibition of legitimate criticism.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal / Near-universal.**

---

# E3-Q060 — No Gossip, Backbiting, or Malicious Information Transfer

**Provisional ancestry:** N062.

## Final Principle

**Do not circulate personal information primarily to degrade, divide, or injure another person where there is no legitimate protective or accountability purpose.**

### Primary Qur'anic Sources

Qur'an 49:12; 68:10–13.

### Supporting E3 Units

**166, 182, 296, 311, 377, 391**.

### Boundary

Truthful whistleblowing, evidence-sharing, and protective reporting are not gossip merely because they damage reputation.

### Preliminary Cross-Traditional Classification

**Universal.**
