# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Source Provenance Repair 003 — Christian and Jewish Evidence Anchors

**Status:** Citation-normalisation pass.

---

## 1. Purpose

Christian and Jewish rows are generally among the strongest documentary rows in E3 because identifiable textual sources are readily available. The principal risk is therefore not absence of evidence but **overextension from a broad biblical norm to a narrower E3 operational rule**.

## 2. Truth, Testimony and Evidence

### Biblical anchors

- **Exodus 20:16** — prohibition on false witness.
- **Deuteronomy 19:15–19** — serious accusation requires adequate witnesses and false testimony is itself subject to judgement.
- **Proverbs 18:17** — the first presentation can appear correct until examined by another.
- **Ephesians 4:25** — put away falsehood and speak truth.

### E3 uses

Strong evidence for:
- Q001 Truthful Communication;
- Q004 Reliable Testimony and Evidence.

Deuteronomy 19 and Proverbs 18 provide stronger support than generic truthfulness for:
- Q003 Verify Before Consequential Action.

**Boundary:** these sources still should not be used to claim that every modern evidentiary procedure is biblically specified.

## 3. Justice and Partiality

- **Leviticus 19:15** — judgement must not favour either poor or great.
- **Deuteronomy 16:19–20** — do not distort justice, show partiality or take a bribe.
- **James 2:1–9** — condemns status-based partiality.

### E3 uses

Strong direct evidence for:
- Q016 Justice Without Partiality;
- Q023 No Bribery or Corrupt Purchase of Decision.

Supporting evidence for:
- Q035 Human Dignity Beyond Status.

## 4. Care, Poverty and Social Obligation

- **Deuteronomy 15:7–11** — do not harden the heart or close the hand to a poor member of the community.
- **Leviticus 19:9–10** — harvesting rules leave material provision for poor and stranger.
- **Matthew 25:35–40** — feeding, clothing, welcoming and visiting those in need.
- **James 2:15–16** — verbal goodwill without supplying bodily needs is inadequate.

### E3 uses

Strong evidence for:
- Q029 Wealth Carries Social Obligations;
- Q032 Positive Duty Toward Severe Need.

Levitical gleaning can support Q033 Structured Social Provision as a concrete mechanism, but it does not prove that every tradition institutionalises welfare in the same way.

## 5. Debt and Exploitative Lending

- **Exodus 22:25** — lending to the poor is not to be treated as an opportunity for interest extraction.
- **Deuteronomy 23:19–20** — regulates interest-taking.
- **Deuteronomy 15:1–11** — debt release and poverty obligations.

### E3 uses

Strong Jewish/biblical evidence for:
- Q026 No Exploitative Finance at the relevant historical level;
- Q027 Relief for Debtors in Genuine Hardship.

**Boundary:** later Christian and Jewish doctrines differ in interpretation and application. The source should not be converted into a universal modern banking rule.

## 6. Conflict, Enemy and Reconciliation

- **Proverbs 25:21–22** — feed an enemy who is hungry.
- **Matthew 5:9** — blessing of peacemakers.
- **Matthew 5:44** — love enemies and pray for persecutors.
- **Romans 12:18–21** — live peaceably where possible and overcome evil with good.

### E3 uses

Strong functional support for:
- Q014 Peace and De-Escalation;
- Q015 Forgiveness and Reconciliation.

These do not by themselves establish Q071's exact safe-passage mechanism.

## 7. Promise and Trust

- **Leviticus 19:12** — prohibits false swearing.
- **Numbers 30:2** — a vow/pledge should be performed.
- **Matthew 5:33–37** — integrity of one's word.

### E3 uses

Strong direct support for:
- Q006 Promise, Covenant, and Commitment Integrity.

## 8. Audit Result

Christian/Jewish provenance is strong enough that final repair should focus on **correct granularity**, not simply collecting more citations.

> **Broad textual abundance must not be allowed to make an operational child appear universal merely because its parent ethic is heavily attested.**

> **End of Source Provenance Repair 003**
