# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Near-Universal Evidence Matrix 005

**Principles:** Q056, Q061, Q065, Q066, Q067.

---

## Q056 — Purpose-Constrained Confidential Deliberation

Traditions often distinguish legitimate counsel or private deliberation from conspiracy, gossip or secret wrongdoing. But E3's formulation is unusually procedural: confidentiality is legitimate only when bounded by ethical purpose.

**Decision:** **Common.** Do not infer this rule simply from prohibitions on malicious secrecy.

## Q061 — Responsibility for Advocacy and Influence

Moral traditions widely recognise that advice, teaching, example and encouragement can contribute to others' conduct. The explicit attribution of responsibility for downstream effects of advocacy is more specific.

**Decision:** **Common / Near-universal.**

## Q065 — Obligation Should Track Capacity

Many traditions temper duty by ability, circumstance, role or hardship. Yet the generalised principle that obligation must scale with capacity is not equally explicit everywhere.

**Decision:** **Common / Near-universal.** Strong candidate for broader functional convergence, but direct evidence is required before a high tier.

## Q066 — Do Not Manufacture Unnecessary Burdens

This is one of E3's clearer anti-overconstraint principles. Traditions may warn against excessive asceticism, needless legal burdens, oppressive rules or unreasonable demands, but the general systems formulation is not independently widespread at the same resolution.

**Decision:** **Common.**

## Q067 — Avoid Self-Destructive Extremes

Moderation, temperance, the middle way, disciplined consumption and avoidance of excess recur strongly across traditions. The principle is broader than any one dietary or ascetic rule.

**Decision:** **Strong Near-universal; plausible Universal functional parent of moderation/non-excess.**

## Matrix 005 Finding

Q066 is methodologically important even if it does not achieve a high prevalence tier.

A principle need not be universal to be ethically significant. The comparison is intended to measure recurrence, not to make recurrence the sole source of legitimacy.
