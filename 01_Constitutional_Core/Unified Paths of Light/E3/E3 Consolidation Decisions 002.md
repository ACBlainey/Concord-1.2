## 1. Purpose

Pass 2 tests whether further compression would remove genuine operational distinctions.

The standard is not thematic similarity. Two principles may occupy the same ethical domain and still need separate identities if they govern different actors, triggers, protected interests, or remedies.

## 3. Final Compression Judgment

Further top-level merger at this stage would increasingly compress **related but non-equivalent** rules.

The correct endpoint is therefore not the smallest imaginable list. It is the smallest list that still preserves material behavioural distinctions.

Pass 2 finds that the remaining catalogue has reached that threshold.

---

## 4. Final Catalogue Size

After the Pass 1 nestings and Pass 2 non-merger review, the independently derived Qur'an-anchored catalogue resolves to **89 top-level principles**.

The reduction is:

- **420** raw/audited source-occurrence records;
- **100** provisional normalised principles;
- **89** final top-level principle identities.

The final number was not predetermined.

It emerged from the evidence and the loss-minimising compression rule.

---

## 5. What the Number 89 Does Not Mean

It does not mean:

- the Qur'an contains only 89 ethical teachings;
- exactly 89 ethical passages exist;
- every source occurrence was exhaustively extracted;
- subordinate applications are unimportant;
- the Psalm-derived catalogue should contain the same number;
- numerical agreement or disagreement by itself proves anchor robustness.

The number describes the **current E3 normalisation resolution** under the stated method.
