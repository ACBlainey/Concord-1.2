## 4. Major Correction Produced by the Audit

The audit prevents a recurrent false inference:

> **If a parent ethic is universal, every operational child is not therefore universal.**

Examples:
- truth ≠ formal verification procedure;
- justice ≠ due process;
- care ≠ structured welfare;
- dignity ≠ privacy;
- hospitality ≠ right of refusal;
- sobriety ≠ protection from exploitation while impaired;
- trustworthiness ≠ institutional-trust theory.

## 5. Structural Finding

The Qur'an-first extraction frequently decomposes broad ethical values into **behavioural safeguards and decision rules**.

Cross-traditional agreement therefore often declines as abstraction moves from:

**deep value → ethical elaboration → operational safeguard.**

This is a substantive comparative result, not merely a citation problem.
