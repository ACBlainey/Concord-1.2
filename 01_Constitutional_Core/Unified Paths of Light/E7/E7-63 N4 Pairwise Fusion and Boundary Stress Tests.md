# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-63 — N4 Pairwise Fusion and Boundary Stress Tests

## Purpose
A seven-root set may still be overcomplete if two roots can be fused without semantic loss.

## Highest-pressure fusion tests

### CR1 + CR3 — Harm + Agency
**Rejected.** Serious harm can occur without domination; domination can occur without immediately measurable serious injury. Fusion would require a broader invented superterm whose internal distinction remains necessary for derivation.

### CR1 + CR4 — Harm + Care
**Rejected.** Non-harm does not generate positive assistance to severe need where the observer did not cause the need.

### CR1 + CR6 — Harm + Correctability
**Rejected.** Preventing harm and preserving recoverability after error are temporally and functionally distinct.

### CR2 + CR6 — Epistemic Corrigibility + Ethical/Systemic Correctability
**Rejected.** CR2 governs relation of belief/communication/judgement to reality. CR6 governs recoverability of conduct, relationships and institutions. They interact strongly but neither exhausts the other.

### CR2 + CR7 — Knowledge + Development
**Rejected.** Accurate/corrigible judgement does not itself require cultivation of capability; development can occur in domains where the principal issue is skill/responsibility rather than factual correction.

### CR3 + CR4 — Agency + Care
**Rejected.** Protection from domination and positive support under vulnerability can conflict and therefore require separate representation.

### CR3 + CR7 — Agency + Development
**Rejected.** Present protected agency and future capability formation are distinct. Developmental intervention requires boundaries precisely because the two can conflict.

### CR4 + CR5 — Care + Resources
**Rejected.** Stewardship includes entrusted use, waste, continuity and sufficiency outside acute aid relationships.

### CR4 + CR7 — Care + Development
**Rejected.** Need response does not necessarily imply capability formation, and development does not always arise from severe need.

### CR5 + CR6 — Resources + Correctability
**Rejected.** Material stewardship and systemic recoverability remain independent functional dimensions.

### CR6 + CR7 — Correctability + Development
**Rejected.** Correction restores/revises after error; development can expand capability without prior error.

## Lower-pressure pairs
Remaining pairings fail more directly because they concern clearly different objects: reality/harm, reality/resources, agency/resources, harm/development, resources/development, and so forth.

## Result
No pair can be fused without either:
1. losing a distinctive E7 function; or
2. creating an abstract superterm that merely conceals the original distinction.
