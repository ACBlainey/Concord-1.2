### Resolution and source caution
The Daodejing is especially translation-sensitive and textually layered. The present records are suitable for **internal E7 functional normalisation**, not for claims that the English formulations are literal or uniquely correct readings.

Later publication work should check material propositions against at least one critical Chinese edition and multiple translations, especially Chapters 1, 10, 27, 38, 49, 57, 65, 66, 67, 72, 77 and 79.

### E7-internal recurrent families
Without comparison to earlier kernels, the Daodejing repeatedly develops:
- non-domination and non-possession;
- restraint of coercive intervention;
- conflict de-escalation;
- compassion;
- limits on elite extraction;
- sufficiency and restraint of acquisitive desire;
- epistemic humility;
- flexibility under changing conditions;
- prevention before escalation;
- downstream/reversal awareness;
- suspicion of status display and ornamental speech;
- self-regulation before outward control.

### Internal contrast
The Daodejing often reaches social-order conclusions through a very different route from the Confucian texts: reduced intervention, non-contention, non-possession and suspicion of formal moral display rather than cultivation through explicit ritual and role structures.

That difference is preserved rather than normalised away at extraction.
