# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-57 — N3 Application Operators

## Definition
An operator modifies how one or more substantive constraints are applied. It is not itself necessarily a deepest ethical end.

## Provisional E7 operators

### CO1 — Verification and Evidence Test
Check consequential claims against relevant evidence, observation, challenge and demonstrated function.

Derived from N2-01–04.

### CO2 — Perspective and Reciprocity Check
Re-evaluate a judgement from relevant alternative standpoints, including affected-agent and self/other reversal.

Derived from N2-03, N2-16, N2-23.

### CO3 — Consequence Horizon
Evaluate indirect, delayed, distributed and displaced consequences rather than only immediate intended effects.

Derived from N2-08, N2-23, N2-28.

### CO4 — Proportionality
Match response, force, burden or intervention to the seriousness and structure of the problem.

Derived from N2-11–12, N2-20.

### CO5 — Contextual Discrimination
Distinguish relevant differences in circumstance rather than applying one form mechanically across unlike cases.

Derived from N2-05, N2-10.

### CO6 — Function-Over-Form Test
Evaluate names, rituals, offices, inherited practices and appearances by the function they actually perform.

Derived from N2-02, N2-07, N2-19, N2-29.

### CO7 — Self-Audit
Inspect the evaluator's motives, reactivity, assumptions, status incentives and possible partiality before consequential action.

Derived from N2-09, N2-47–49, N2-52.

### CO8 — Recursive Correction
Preserve feedback through which a decision, agent or institution can be challenged and revised after error becomes visible.

Derived from N2-04, N2-33, N2-37, N2-47, N2-54.

### CO9 — Prevention / Early Intervention
Act while a foreseeable failure remains tractable where this reduces later harm and repair burden.

Derived from N2-37, N2-54.

### CO10 — Flexibility and Non-Forcing
Prefer adaptive, lower-force responses where rigid or excessive intervention would damage the relevant function.

Derived from N2-12, N2-35, N2-38, N2-53, N2-55.
