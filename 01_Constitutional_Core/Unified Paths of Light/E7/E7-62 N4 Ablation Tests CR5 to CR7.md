# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-62 — N4 Ablation Tests: CR5–CR7

## CR5 — Responsible Resource Relation

### Ablation attempt
Candidate substitutes:
- CR1 harm restraint;
- CR4 care;
- CR3 agency;
- CO3 consequence horizon under scarcity.

### Failure of regeneration
Those constraints address injury, deprivation and exploitation but do not fully capture entrusted-resource purpose, waste, sufficiency, productive continuity and the ethical relation to resources before a specific victim or acute need is identifiable.

Absorbing these functions into CR1 would turn all stewardship failure into “harm”; absorbing them into CR4 would make all resource responsibility a welfare duty.

**Result: CR5 SURVIVES.**

## CR6 — Correctability and Repair

### Ablation attempt
Candidate substitutes:
- CR1 harm restraint;
- CR2 reality-responsive judgement;
- CO8 recursive correction;
- CO9 prevention.

### Failure of regeneration
CR1 and CR2 can identify harmful/error states. They do not independently require preservation of channels through which decisions, institutions and relationships can be challenged, revised, repaired and reintegrated after failure.

CO8 appears close, but without CR6 it becomes a procedure lacking a substantive requirement to preserve recoverability. Moving the entire function into CO8 would hide a root inside an operator.

**Result: CR6 SURVIVES.**

## CR7 — Capability-Sensitive Development

### Ablation attempt
Candidate substitutes:
- CR3 protected agency;
- CR4 care;
- CR6 correction;
- CC6 developmental difference.

### Failure of regeneration
Respecting present agency and assisting present need does not by itself require development of future capability, learner-sensitive instruction, or responsibility calibrated to realised competence. CC6 only states that capability differences exist.

To regenerate CR7 from CR3 would require redefining agency to include a positive developmental trajectory; from CR4 would require redefining care as education/capability formation.

**Result: CR7 SURVIVES.**
