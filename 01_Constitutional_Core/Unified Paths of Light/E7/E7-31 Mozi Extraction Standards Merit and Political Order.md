# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-31 — Mozi Extraction: Standards, Merit and Political Order

**Primary working source:** *Mozi*, received text, using the Chinese Text Project chapter structure. The frozen E7 boundary admits the declared ethical, political and social-order strata rather than the entire received corpus. Chapter-family locators are retained because many doctrines occur in parallel triplets. Functional propositions below are E7 normalisations, not quotations.

## Admitted strata
This block covers the early ethical/political chapters and the **Exaltation of the Virtuous** and **Identification with the Superior** families. The latter are retained critically: their coordination function is extracted without silently endorsing authoritarian conformity.

## Coverage note
This block preserves a major internal tension: Mohism combines anti-status merit selection and public-benefit standards with a strongly centralised model of normative coordination. Normalisation must not erase that tension.
