# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-05 — Extraction Coverage and Closure Plan

## Extraction order
1. Analects
2. Mencius
3. Xunzi
4. Daodejing
5. Zhuangzi Inner Chapters
6. Mozi declared ethical/political strata

The order is logistical only and carries no authority ranking.

## Resolution
- **Analects:** book/passage or stable saying/exchange.
- **Mencius:** book/section or stable argumentative unit.
- **Xunzi:** chapter/passage or stable argumentative unit.
- **Daodejing:** chapter.
- **Zhuangzi Inner Chapters:** passage/narrative/argumentative unit.
- **Mozi:** chapter/passage/thematic unit with chapter identity retained.

No claim of character-by-character or manuscript-variant exhaustion is made.

## Running audit for each corpus block
Record:
- coverage completed;
- provenance resolution;
- repeated proposition families;
- translation-sensitive concepts;
- narrative/argument ambiguity;
- scope-limited material;
- removals or revisions;
- marginal new functional information.

## Closure modes
Permitted closure:
1. declared corpus exhaustion at chosen resolution;
2. declared subcorpus exhaustion;
3. marginal-information closure only where the plan explicitly permits it.

Not permitted:
- closure because predicted functions have appeared;
- expansion because predicted functions have not appeared.

## Normalisation gate
After primary extraction:
1. mechanical ledger audit;
2. provenance/source-resolution audit;
3. whole-corpus gate;
4. overcomplete E7-only proposition catalogue;
5. duplicate compression;
6. parent-child compression;
7. architectural separation;
8. minimum-stability ablation/fusion;
9. **E7 internal freeze**;
10. only then reopen X1–X7.

## Publication-quality distinction
Internal normalisation may proceed at declared stable passage/chapter resolution. Any coarse or synthesis-level records must remain labelled as such and may require later source-critical verification before publication.

## Experimental firewall
The Research_Offshoots prospective register is known to exist, but its predicted outcomes must not guide extraction decisions.
