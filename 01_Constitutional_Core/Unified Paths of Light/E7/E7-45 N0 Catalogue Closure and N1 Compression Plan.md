## What N0 is
N0 is a semantic working catalogue. It is intentionally redundant. It preserves:
- parent/child overlap;
- domain-specific and general formulations side by side;
- operators that may later separate from roots;
- conditions that may later cease to appear as principles;
- genuine tensions between schools.

N0 is **not** a claim that Chinese ethics contains 120 independent principles.

## N1 rule
N1 may merge propositions only when:
1. their behavioural function is substantially equivalent;
2. no meaningful boundary is lost;
3. disagreement is not being hidden by abstraction;
4. a domain-specific rule remains recoverable from the merged proposition plus context;
5. the merger does not use knowledge of X1–X7 or earlier kernels.

## N1 target
There is **no numerical target**.

N1 should reduce semantic duplication while remaining deliberately above root level.
