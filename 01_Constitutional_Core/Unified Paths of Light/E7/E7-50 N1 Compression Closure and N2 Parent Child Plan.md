## What was merged
N1 merged only propositions whose practical function substantially overlapped. Examples include:
- ignorance/partial-model humility;
- aggression/protective-force distinctions;
- non-possession and relational non-ownership;
- livelihood/extraction constraints;
- loyalty and non-complicity;
- learning plus continuing corrigibility;
- anger/desire regulation.

## What was deliberately not merged
Several high-pressure families remain separated:
- truth/communication versus evidence-responsive judgement;
- harm restraint versus non-domination;
- care versus agency;
- resource stewardship versus care;
- authority legitimacy versus corrective institutional design;
- self-regulation versus developmental capability;
- prevention versus repair/correction;
- coordination versus conformity.

Their separability must be tested rather than assumed.

## Cross-school tensions preserved
N1 still preserves:
- Mencian versus Xunzian anthropology;
- Confucian role/ritual coordination versus Daoist suspicion of imposed form/control;
- Mohist inclusive concern versus hierarchical coordination;
- graded relationship duties versus wider moral consideration;
- strong institutional coordination versus protection of corrective dissent;
- cultivated standards versus perspectival warnings against absolutising partial frames.

Compression has therefore not been achieved by flattening the Chinese corpus into a single school.

## N2 task
N2 now tests **parent–child structure**.

A proposition may be absorbed into a parent only where:
1. the child can be regenerated from the parent plus a clearly stated condition/operator;
2. the child's distinctive boundary remains recoverable;
3. removing it does not erase a genuine cross-school disagreement;
4. no earlier E-series or X1–X7 structure is used to decide the merger.

Expected pressure areas include:
- evidence, truth, classification and corrigibility;
- aggression, proportionality and de-escalation;
- agency, intervention and non-domination;
- care, vulnerability and aid;
- resource use, sufficiency and continuity;
- authority, role and corrective channels;
- development, environment and instruction;
- self-regulation, resilience and flexibility.

There is **no target number** for N2.
