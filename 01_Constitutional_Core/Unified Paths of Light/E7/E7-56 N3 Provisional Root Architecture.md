# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-56 — N3 Provisional Root Architecture

## Status
Blind E7 derivation. X1–X7 and all earlier kernels remain quarantined.

N3 separates propositions by logical role. A **root candidate** must express a substantive constraint that cannot presently be regenerated merely by combining another root with an application operator and recurrent condition.

## Provisional roots

### CR1 — Harm Restraint and Protective Limitation
Avoid unnecessary serious harm and constrain harmful action by relevant protective purpose, necessity and consequence.

Primary N2 support: N2-11–12, with conflict applications throughout the corpus.

### CR2 — Reality-Responsive and Corrigible Judgement
Maintain consequential belief, communication and judgement in responsive relation to relevant evidence, distinctions, uncertainty and correction.

Primary support: N2-01–10.

### CR3 — Protected Effective Agency and Non-Domination
Do not exploit, arbitrarily subordinate, possessively control or unnecessarily override another agent's viable capacity to function and participate.

Primary support: N2-13, N2-17–19.

### CR4 — Care Under Need and Vulnerability
Where genuine need, dependency or reduced effective capacity exists, provide proportionate effective assistance when capable without unnecessary degradation.

Primary support: N2-14, N2-20 and relational applications.

### CR5 — Responsible Resource Relation
Acquire, use, allocate and preserve material resources without destructive extraction, prestige waste, exploitation or disregard for viable sufficiency and future continuity.

Primary support: N2-24–28.

### CR6 — Correctability and Repair
Preserve the capacity to identify error, wrongdoing, institutional failure or relational breakdown; challenge it; correct it; and where possible repair its consequences.

Primary support: N2-04, N2-21, N2-32–37, N2-47, N2-54.

### CR7 — Capability-Sensitive Development
Treat ethical and practical capability as developable and shape instruction, responsibility and developmental environments in relation to actual capacity and conditions.

Primary support: N2-40–46, N2-50–51.

### CR8 — Legitimate and Bounded Power
Entrusted authority should serve legitimate function, remain bounded by role and responsibility, and resist conversion into domination, private benefit or insulated control.

Primary support: N2-29–39.

## Immediate pressure
CR8 is the highest-pressure root candidate. Much of it appears regenerable from:
- CR2 under information asymmetry;
- CR3 under concentrated power;
- CR5 under entrusted-resource conditions;
- CR6 under institutional failure/correction conditions;
- CR7 under role/capability conditions.

It therefore remains **provisional**, not frozen.

CR6 also overlaps CR1 and CR2 but presently carries a distinct temporal/recursive function: preserving the system's capacity to recover after error rather than merely avoiding initial error.
