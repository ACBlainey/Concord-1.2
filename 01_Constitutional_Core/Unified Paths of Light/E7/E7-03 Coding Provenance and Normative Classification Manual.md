# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-03 — Coding, Provenance and Normative Classification Manual

## Ethical Source Unit
An E7 Ethical Source Unit (ESU) is the smallest contextually sufficient passage, exchange, argument, aphorism or narrative unit capable of supporting a distinct behavioural or institutional proposition.

## Normative classes
- **P — Prescriptive:** recommends/prohibits conduct.
- **V — Virtue/character:** identifies cultivated disposition with behavioural consequence.
- **C — Consequential/causal:** links conduct or institutional form to ethical/social result.
- **D — Deliberative:** supplies a procedure for judgement or conflict.
- **N — Narrative/example:** illustrates a norm through action, praise, criticism or consequence.
- **X — Context-bound/non-general:** ethically relevant but not safely generalisable.

## Scope tags
- **G** general interpersonal
- **S** self-cultivation
- **F** family/kinship
- **R** ruler/official/entrusted role
- **C** collective/social coordination
- **W** war/coercive conflict
- **E** education/development
- **M** material/resource relation

## Required provenance fields
Each occurrence record should contain:
1. occurrence ID;
2. work;
3. book/chapter/passage locator at the resolution supported;
4. translation/source edition where relevant;
5. source form;
6. normative class;
7. scope;
8. minimally normalised behavioural proposition;
9. contextual limitation;
10. translation/concept note where material;
11. confidence/provenance note;
12. provisional **E7-internal** cluster only.

## Translation and concept controls
Terms including **rén, yì, lǐ, zhì, xìn, shù, xiào, dé, dào, wúwéi, jiān'ài** and related concepts must not be forced into a single modern English ethical category merely for convenience.

Where translation changes ethical interpretation:
- preserve the transliterated term;
- record the local behavioural function;
- distinguish lexical meaning from the functional proposition inferred.

## Self-cultivation control
Self-cultivation is not automatically coded as social ethics. It qualifies where the passage supplies a behavioural, agency, epistemic, relational or consequential function.

## Ritual control
Ritual is not automatically treated as an ethical root. Code the function actually supported: coordination, respect, role differentiation, discipline, signalling, continuity, etc.

## Hierarchy control
Historical hierarchy, filial structure, gender assumptions and ruler-subject relations must be recorded rather than silently universalised.

## Narrative control
An action in narrative or dialogue is not automatically normative. Look for praise, criticism, consequence, argumentative framing or explicit recommendation.

## Anti-contamination
No proposition may be coded because it resembles X1–X7 or any earlier kernel.
