### Translation/concept sensitivity
Particular caution remains around:
- rén / humane concern;
- yì / rightness;
- lǐ / ritual/propriety;
- xīn / heart-mind;
- xìng / nature;
- qì;
- ruler/minister relational vocabulary;
- “sprouts/beginnings” terminology.

### Recurring E7-internal families
Without external comparison, Mencius repeatedly develops:
- humane concern and its extension;
- material responsibilities of government;
- constraints on aggressive war and oppressive rule;
- reflective correction;
- cultivation of capacities;
- context-sensitive application of rules;
- role/function distinctions;
- legitimate challenge, resignation and withdrawal under defective power;
- aid under need;
- distinction between ethical substance and formal/ritual appearance.

### Tensions preserved
- graded family concern versus wider extension;
- loyalty versus correction of authority;
- office participation versus withdrawal;
- survival/material interest versus higher-order commitment;
- fixed rule versus context-sensitive exception;
- cultivated capacity versus environmental degradation;
- historical hierarchy versus functionally generalisable propositions.
