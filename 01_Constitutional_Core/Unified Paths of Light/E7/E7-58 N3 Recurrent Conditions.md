# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-58 — N3 Recurrent Conditions

## Definition
A condition is a recurrent feature of multi-agent situations that changes which roots and operators become salient without itself necessarily constituting an ethical principle.

### CC1 — Vulnerability / Dependency
Need, illness, material dependency, reduced effective capacity, stigma or exposure to stronger agents.

### CC2 — Entrusted or Concentrated Power
Office, rulership, institutional hierarchy, control of resources, control of information or coercive capability.

### CC3 — Scarcity / Material Dependency
Finite goods, livelihood constraints, production, distribution and opportunity cost.

### CC4 — Conflict / Active Threat
Aggression, retaliation, warfare, interpersonal conflict or serious coercive contest.

### CC5 — Uncertainty / Incomplete Knowledge
Limited evidence, partial perspective, ambiguity, model limitation or unstable classification.

### CC6 — Developmental / Capability Difference
Differences in learning, realised competence, readiness, skill and cultivated judgement.

### CC7 — Status / Reputation / Group Pressure
Prestige, shame, praise, blame, faction, conformity pressure and inherited social classification.

### CC8 — Institutional / Collective Coordination
Large-scale role differentiation, standards, reporting, incentives, collective action and public trust.

### CC9 — Change / Instability / Temporal Transition
Loss, transformation, emerging failure, shifting circumstances and long-term consequence.

### CC10 — Relational Obligation
Family, friendship, teacher–learner, ruler–minister, commitment and other relationships creating reliance or differentiated responsibility.

### CC11 — Communication / Information Asymmetry
Unequal access to consequential information, message transmission, flattery, fear, filtering and interpretive mismatch.
