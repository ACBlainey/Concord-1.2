# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-38 — Provenance Audit B: Daodejing, Zhuangzi and Mozi

## Daodejing
Declared resolution: received 81 chapters.

The extraction deliberately omitted chapters where no separable behavioural proposition was supportable and repeatedly flagged metaphorical/metaphysical normalisation.

**Resolution:** PASS WITH TRANSLATION LIMITATION for internal normalisation.

High-priority later verification remains for Chapters 1, 10, 27, 38, 49, 57, 65–67, 72, 77 and 79.

## Zhuangzi Inner Chapters
Declared boundary: Inner Chapters 1–7 only; narrative/argumentative-unit resolution.

Outer and Miscellaneous chapters were not imported.

**Resolution:** PASS WITH INTERPRETIVE LIMITATION for internal normalisation.

Controls:
- perspectival reasoning must not be converted into unrestricted relativism;
- narrative action is not automatically normative;
- disability/status examples must remain historically situated;
- synthesis records CZ-015, CZ-042 and CZ-055 must be marked during frequency/duplicate analysis.

## Mozi
Declared boundary: selected ethical, political and social-order strata, with doctrinal triplets treated as families.

**Resolution:** PASS WITH FAMILY-LEVEL LIMITATION for internal normalisation.

Controls:
- parallel triplets are evidence of emphasis, not multiplicity;
- “inclusive care” terminology remains translation-sensitive;
- centralised coordination is not silently converted into endorsement of unlimited authority;
- theological premises are not independently secularised;
- MO-020, MO-052 and cross-family E7-34 records require synthesis flags.

## Result
All three blocks are adequate for **internal functional normalisation at their declared resolution**.

They are not yet source-critical publication editions.
