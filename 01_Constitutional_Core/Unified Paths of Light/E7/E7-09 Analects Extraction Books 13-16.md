**Primary working source:** *Confucian Analects*, James Legge translation, *The Chinese Classics*, Vol. I; checked against the public-domain Chinese/English Project Gutenberg edition (eBook 4094). Locators use received Analects book.chapter numbering. Functional propositions are E7 normalisations, not quotations.

## Coverage note
Books 13–16 show unusually dense governance, role, language/function and self-cultivation material. Historical hierarchy is retained as context rather than converted automatically into universal political rules.
