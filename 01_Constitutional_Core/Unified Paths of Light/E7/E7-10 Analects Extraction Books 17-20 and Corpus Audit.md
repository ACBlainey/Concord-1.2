## Analects primary extraction audit

- Books 1–4: CA-001–024

- Books 9–12: CA-049–072

The number is an **ethical occurrence ledger**, not the number of chapters in the Analects and not a claim that every sentence contains a distinct ethical principle.

### Provenance resolution
Working locators are received book.chapter references. The working translation is James Legge, with the Chinese/English public-domain edition used as the stable reference base. Where modern functional language appears, it is explicitly an E7 normalisation rather than a claimed literal translation.

### Translation-sensitive areas flagged
Particular caution remains around:
- rén;
- yì;
- lǐ;
- dé;
- zhì;
- xìn;
- shù;
- filial/kinship vocabulary;
- “rectification of names”;
- ruler/minister role terminology.

### Repetition
High recurrence does not imply multiple roots. Repeated families include:
- self-examination and correction;
- learning and developmental capability;
- truthful/reliable speech;
- judgement of persons by conduct rather than appearance/status;
- role responsibility under authority;
- trust as a condition of collective action;
- ritual/form as a mechanism whose ethical function must be examined;
- reciprocity/perspective;
- regulation of reactive or status-driven conduct.

### Internal tensions preserved
The extraction does not force resolution of:
- family loyalty versus public truth/evidence;
- ritual conformity versus underlying humane function;
- withdrawal from corrupt institutions versus continued social responsibility;
- harmony versus conformity;
- retaliation/justice versus indiscriminate beneficence;
- hierarchy as historical form versus generalisable ethical function.
