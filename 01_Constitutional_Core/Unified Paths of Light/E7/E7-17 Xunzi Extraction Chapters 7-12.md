Because translation and textual segmentation vary, passage-level publication verification remains a later source-critical task.

## Coverage note
Chapters 7–12 are governance-heavy. Ancient monarchical and rank structures are recorded as context; their functional propositions are not treated as endorsements of fixed hierarchy.
