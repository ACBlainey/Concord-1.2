# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-34 — Mozi Extraction: Ethical Epistemology and Social-Order Synthesis

**Primary working source:** *Mozi*, received text, using the Chinese Text Project chapter structure. The frozen E7 boundary admits the declared ethical, political and social-order strata rather than the entire received corpus. Chapter-family locators are retained because many doctrines occur in parallel triplets. Functional propositions below are E7 normalisations, not quotations.

## Purpose
This file completes the declared primary Mozi ethical/political strata by extracting cross-chapter functional propositions that depend on **arguments spanning the doctrinal families**, without opening the later Mohist Canons as a new primary corpus.

## Internal tension register
The Mozi block contains tensions that must survive normalisation:
1. inclusive moral consideration versus strongly hierarchical political coordination;
2. merit against hereditary status versus extensive top-down behavioural shaping;
3. public standards versus the risk that a unified standard suppresses legitimate disagreement;
4. frugality/public benefit versus possible undervaluation of aesthetic, ritual or plural goods;
5. empirically oriented “tests” versus theological premises in parts of the corpus.

These tensions are data, not defects to be edited away.
