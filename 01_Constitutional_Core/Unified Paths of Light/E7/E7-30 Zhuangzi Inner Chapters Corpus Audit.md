## Provenance resolution
Records use chapter plus stable narrative/argument identifiers rather than invented paragraph numbers. This is adequate for E7 internal normalisation but requires later source-critical verification against Chinese text and multiple translations before publication.

## Translation-sensitive areas
Particular caution remains around:
- dào;
- dé;
- qí / equalising or making even;
- shì/fēi;
- xīn / heart-mind;
- wúyòng / uselessness;
- zìrán;
- transformation/change terminology;
- “fasting of the mind” and “sitting in forgetfulness.”

## E7-internal recurrent families
Without comparison to any previous kernel, the Inner Chapters repeatedly develop:
- perspectival and epistemic humility;
- resistance to treating partial classifications as exhaustive;
- non-coercive/skilled action responsive to structure;
- caution in intervention under power asymmetry;
- protection against status, bodily appearance and imposed utility becoming total measures of worth;
- correction of the evaluator as well as the evaluated;
- adaptability under change;
- preservation of agency/function under vulnerability;
- distinction between benevolent intention and actual consequence.

## Important internal contribution
The Zhuangzi creates a particularly strong methodological pressure on the E7 normalisation stage: some apparent ethical disagreements may arise because texts operate at different **levels of description, standpoint or imposed function**.

That observation is retained internally. It must not be used to dissolve genuine disagreements merely by declaring them perspectival.

## Corpus integrity
No Outer or Miscellaneous Chapter material was imported to strengthen weak themes. No comparison with X1–X7, E3–E6 or Blainey's Laws has occurred.
