# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-64 — N4 Operator–Root Leakage and Minimum-Stability Result

## Operator leakage test
The final attack asks whether an apparent operator secretly carries a substantive root omitted from CR1–CR7.

### CO1 Verification
Procedure for CR2. No independent end.

### CO2 Perspective and Reciprocity
Changes standpoint for application of CR1–CR4 and judgement under uncertainty. No independent end.

### CO3 Consequence Horizon
Extends temporal/distributed evaluation of CR1, CR4 and CR5. No independent end.

### CO4 Proportionality
Controls degree of response. Requires a substantive constraint to say what is being protected.

### CO5 Contextual Discrimination
Selects relevant application conditions. No independent end.

### CO6 Function-Over-Form
Tests whether inherited forms serve underlying constraints. No independent end.

### CO7 Self-Audit
Applies CR2/CR6 reflexively to the evaluator. No new root required.

### CO8 Recursive Correction
Operationalises CR6 across repeated decision cycles. It does not replace CR6.

### CO9 Prevention / Early Intervention
Temporal operator applying especially CR1, CR5 and CR6.

### CO10 Flexibility and Non-Forcing
Application strategy serving CR1/CR3/CR6 under changing conditions; not a standalone ethical end.

## Condition leakage test
CC1–CC11 describe circumstances that alter salience. None supplies a normative direction without one or more roots.

Power is the strongest test: CC2 can magnify harm, domination, information distortion, resource misuse and blocked correction, but “power exists” alone does not state what ought to happen.

## Minimum-stability result
The seven-root architecture survives:
- individual ablation;
- highest-pressure cross-root substitution;
- pairwise fusion;
- operator-root leakage;
- condition-root leakage;
- the earlier CR8 power-root elimination.

Further compression is possible only by introducing broader vocabulary that semantically contains multiple surviving functions. That would reduce the **number of labels**, not the functional dimensionality.
