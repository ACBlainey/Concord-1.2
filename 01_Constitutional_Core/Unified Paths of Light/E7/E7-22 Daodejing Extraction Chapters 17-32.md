# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-22 — Daodejing Extraction: Chapters 17–32

**Primary working source:** *Daodejing* (Laozi), received 81-chapter text. Chapter locators use conventional numbering. Because wording differs substantially among received-text traditions and translations, functional propositions below are E7 normalisations rather than quotations; translation-sensitive claims remain flagged for later source-critical audit.
