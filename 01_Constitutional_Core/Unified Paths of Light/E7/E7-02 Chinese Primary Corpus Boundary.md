# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-02 — Chinese Primary Corpus Boundary

## Boundary principle
E7 requires a corpus broad enough to expose early Chinese ethical disagreement and variation, but sufficiently bounded to avoid retrospective source hunting.

The experiment therefore uses a **pre-Qin / early classical Chinese primary corpus** with distinct philosophical voices rather than treating “Chinese ethics” as synonymous with one school.

## Frozen primary derivation corpus

### Confucian / Ru core
1. **Analects (Lúnyǔ)** — complete received text.
2. **Mencius (Mèngzǐ)** — complete received text.
3. **Xunzi (Xúnzǐ)** — complete received text.

### Daoist contrast
4. **Daodejing (Dàodéjīng)** — complete received text.
5. **Zhuangzi (Zhuāngzǐ)** — Inner Chapters as primary high-confidence stratum; Outer/Miscellaneous material withheld from primary derivation.

### Mohist contrast
6. **Mozi (Mòzǐ)** — ethical, political and social-order chapters, including material on inclusive concern, merit, economy, anti-aggression and standards of judgement.

## Why this is not labelled simply “Confucian”
A Confucian-only corpus would test one major Chinese lineage. The present question is stronger: whether a broader early Chinese ethical field, containing internal disagreement about ritual, hierarchy, human nature, government, desire, war and social order, compresses toward a stable functional architecture.

## Withheld validation / expansion material
Not admitted during primary derivation:
- Zhuangzi Outer and Miscellaneous Chapters;
- later Confucian commentaries;
- Han and later state Confucian synthesis;
- Neo-Confucian traditions;
- later Daoist religious corpora;
- Chinese Buddhist texts;
- Legalist texts as a validation/contrast corpus rather than primary ethical anchor;
- later popular/religious syncretic traditions.

These may be used only after E7 internal freeze under a separately declared validation purpose.

## Historical-textual caution
Received texts can contain composite strata and later editorial transmission. E7 therefore tests a declared textual corpus, not a claim that every passage derives from a single historical author or date.

## Boundary lock
**PRIMARY CORPUS FROZEN BEFORE ETHICAL EXTRACTION.**

The boundary may not be expanded because a predicted function appears weak or absent.
