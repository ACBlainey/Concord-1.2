# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-29 — Zhuangzi Inner Chapters Extraction: Chapters 6–7

**Primary working source:** *Zhuangzi*, Inner Chapters (1–7), received text. Locators use conventional chapter numbering/titles and thematic passage descriptions because paragraph numbering varies by edition. Functional propositions below are E7 normalisations rather than quotations. Translation-sensitive passages remain flagged for later source-critical audit.
