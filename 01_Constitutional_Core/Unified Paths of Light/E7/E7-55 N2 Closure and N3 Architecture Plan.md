## What N2 established
Many domain rules can already be regenerated from broader parents plus context. Examples:
- truthful speech from reality-responsive judgement under communication conditions;
- opportunity-cost reasoning from consequence evaluation under scarcity;
- de-escalation from harm restraint under conflict where equivalent protection is available;
- corrective counsel from information integrity under concentrated power;
- anti-luxury rules from resource continuity under entrusted-resource conditions;
- learner-sensitive instruction from developable capability under developmental asymmetry.

- public trust;
- anti-status rules;
- intervention restraint.
