# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-37 — Provenance Audit A: Analects, Mencius and Xunzi

## Audit question
Are the three Ru/Confucian-family blocks sufficiently sourced at their **declared resolution** to enter internal blind normalisation?

### Analects
Declared resolution: received book/passage numbering.

The extraction records use conventional book.chapter locators and explicitly distinguish functional E7 normalisation from literal translation. Book 10 was treated cautiously as synthesis-level embodied-practice evidence rather than artificially expanded.

**Resolution:** PASS for internal normalisation.

Flags retained:
- rén, yì, lǐ, dé, zhì, xìn and shù are translation-sensitive;
- family and ruler/minister formulations must not be silently universalised;
- CA-069 is synthesis-level;
- CA-094 is intentionally retained near CA-093 pending duplicate compression because numbering/translation presentation varies.

### Mencius
Declared resolution: seven-book A/B structure and stable argumentative units.

The extraction preserves the “sprouts/beginnings,” xīn, xìng, qì and role vocabulary as translation-sensitive rather than replacing them with earlier ethical terminology.

**Resolution:** PASS for internal normalisation.

Flags retained:
- historical exempla require Mencius' evaluative framing before normative use;
- anthropological claims about human capacities remain source claims, not experimental assumptions;
- function-over-form examples must not become unrestricted exception rules.

### Xunzi
Declared resolution: chapter/thematic passage.

This is coarser than Analects/Mencius and was explicitly labelled so during extraction.

**Resolution:** PASS WITH RESOLUTION LIMITATION for internal normalisation.

Flags retained:
- several modern functional formulations are synthesis-level paraphrases;
- Chapters 25–32 contain composite/poetic/collected material requiring extra caution;
- Chapter-title/textual-strata uncertainty remains;
- publication-grade use requires later passage-level Chinese/translation verification.

## Result
All three blocks may enter E7 internal normalisation **provided source-resolution labels remain attached**. This is not publication-grade source certification.
