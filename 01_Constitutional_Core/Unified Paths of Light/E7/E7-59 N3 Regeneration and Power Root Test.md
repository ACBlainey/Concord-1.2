## Regeneration tests

### Corrective counsel to rulers
CR2 + CR6 + CO1 + CO8 under CC2 + CC11
→ consequential authority should remain reachable by candid corrective information.

### Anti-luxury public expenditure
CR5 + CO3 under CC2 + CC3
→ entrusted resources should not be consumed for prestige where livelihood and continuity bear the cost.

### De-escalation
CR1 + CO4 + CO10 under CC4
→ prefer lower-harm means where they provide equivalent protection.

### Learner-sensitive instruction
CR7 + CO5 under CC6
→ match instruction and responsibility to actual developmental capability.

### Anti-stigma capability judgement
CR2 + CR3 + CO6 under CC1 + CC7
→ embodiment, punishment, rank or reputation should not exhaustively determine worth or capability.

### Non-complicity / withdrawal
CR1 + CR3 + CR6 + CO5 under CC2 + CC10
→ loyalty does not require enabling serious wrongdoing; exit may become justified when correction is impossible.

### Resource rebalancing
CR4 + CR5 + CO3 + CO4 under CC1 + CC3
→ severe material imbalance can warrant proportionate redistribution where viable sufficiency is impaired.

### Intervention restraint
CR1 + CR3 + CO2 + CO10 under CC1/CC5
→ do not impose a supposedly beneficial transformation that predictably destroys another's viable functioning.

## CR8 power-root test
Question: does **Legitimate and Bounded Power** add a substantive constraint that cannot be regenerated?

Its principal functions regenerate as follows:
- legitimate public purpose → CR5/CR3 + CC2;
- non-domination → CR3 + CC2;
- accurate information to power → CR2 + CR6 + CC2/CC11;
- bounded jurisdiction → CR3 + CO4/CO5 + CC2/CC8;
- competent office → CR7 + CO6 + CC2;
- anti-private enrichment → CR5 + CC2/CC3;
- anti-capture/corrective channels → CR6 + CR2 + CC2/CC11;
- heightened responsibility → CO3 + CC2.
