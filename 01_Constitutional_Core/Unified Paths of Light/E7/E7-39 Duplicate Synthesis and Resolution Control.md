# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-39 — Duplicate, Synthesis and Resolution Control

## Purpose
Prevent the 542-record ledger from being mistaken for 542 independent propositions.

## Four record types
### O — Direct occurrence
A passage or stable argumentative unit directly supports the normalised function.

### F — Repeated-family occurrence
Parallel textual families restate substantially the same doctrine. Especially important in Mozi.

### S — Synthesis record
A proposition is explicitly summarised across several passages/chapters. It may aid architecture discovery but must not count as an independent textual occurrence when measuring recurrence.

### L — Lower-resolution thematic record
The proposition is supported at chapter/thematic resolution but requires later passage-level verification for publication.

## Explicit synthesis controls
Already identifiable examples include:
- CA-069;
- CX-082;
- CZ-015, CZ-042, CZ-055;
- MO-020, MO-052;
- MO-053–068 where explicitly cross-family synthesis rather than single-passage occurrence.

These records remain usable for conceptual normalisation but cannot inflate source frequency.

## Near-duplicate controls
Examples such as Analects reciprocity records CA-093/CA-094 are retained until blind duplicate compression. Retention now prevents editorial judgement from pre-empting N1.

Mohist doctrinal triplets must be compressed by proposition rather than counted three times merely because parallel chapters exist.

## Resolution rule
A lower-resolution record may support a candidate principle if:
1. the source family genuinely supports the function;
2. the wording does not exceed the source's declared resolution;
3. no precise passage claim is invented;
4. later source-critical audit can still challenge it.

## Frequency rule
# **SOURCE RECURRENCE IS EVIDENCE OF EMPHASIS, NOT PRINCIPLE MULTIPLICITY.**

Normalisation must therefore operate on semantic function, not raw record count.

## Result
Duplicate/synthesis structure is controlled sufficiently to proceed to the whole-corpus gate.
