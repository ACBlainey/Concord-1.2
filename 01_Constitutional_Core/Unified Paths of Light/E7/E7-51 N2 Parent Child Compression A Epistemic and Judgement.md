# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-51 — N2 Parent–Child Compression A: Epistemic and Judgement

## Status
Blind E7 normalisation. X1–X7 and earlier kernels remain quarantined.

N2 asks whether N1 propositions can be regenerated from broader parents plus identifiable operators/conditions.

| N2 ID | Parent proposition | N1 absorbed / retained |
|---|---|---|
| N2-01 | Maintain a reality-responsive and corrigible relation between belief, communication and consequential judgement. | 001,002,006,008 |
| N2-02 | Judge persons, claims and classifications by relevant evidence and demonstrated function rather than appearance, status or presentation. | 003,004 |
| N2-03 | Preserve perspective sensitivity without treating partial standpoint as exhaustive or all judgements as equivalent. | 005 |
| N2-04 | Protect consequential information against distortion or suppression by power, fear, favour and status. | 007 |
| N2-05 | Attribute causes and responsibility according to actual control, intention, circumstance and consequence. | 009,010 |
| N2-06 | Use standards capable of constraining private preference and applying consistently across comparable cases. | 011 |
| N2-07 | Evaluate inherited forms, traditions and labels by substantive function rather than authority or display alone. | 012 |
| N2-08 | Evaluate action by actual and foreseeable consequences, including indirect effects and displaced alternatives. | 013,014 |
| N2-09 | Examine reactive motives and emotional distortion before consequential judgement. | 015 |
| N2-10 | Apply standards context-sensitively while preserving principled boundaries and the capacity to compare conflicting obligations. | 016,017 |

### Regeneration examples
- truthful communication = N2-01 under communication conditions;
- epistemic humility = N2-01 + N2-03 under uncertainty;
- classification integrity = N2-02 under naming/coordination conditions;
- opportunity-cost reasoning = N2-08 under scarcity/allocation conditions.

N1-007 survives separately as N2-04 because ordinary truthfulness does not by itself regenerate protection of information under concentrated power.
