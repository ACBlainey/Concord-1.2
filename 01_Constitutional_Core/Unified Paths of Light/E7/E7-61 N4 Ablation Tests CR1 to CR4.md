# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-61 — N4 Ablation Tests: CR1–CR4

## Status
Blind minimum-stability testing. X1–X7 and all earlier kernels remain quarantined.

A root survives only if removing it causes a distinctive ethical function to disappear or requires another root to be semantically expanded beyond its prior meaning.

## CR1 — Harm Restraint and Protective Limitation

### Ablation attempt
Candidate substitutes:
- CR3 agency/non-domination;
- CR4 care;
- CR6 correctability;
- CO4 proportionality under conflict.

### Failure of regeneration
These can explain coercive harm, failure to aid, or repair after harm, but they do not independently prohibit serious destructive injury to an agent who is neither being dominated nor presently dependent and where later repair is impossible.

Expanding CR3 to cover all serious injury would redefine agency into a general harm principle. Expanding CR4 would redefine care beyond need/vulnerability.

**Result: CR1 SURVIVES.**

## CR2 — Reality-Responsive and Corrigible Judgement

### Ablation attempt
Candidate substitutes:
- CR6 correction;
- CO1 verification;
- CO7 self-audit;
- CO8 recursive correction.

### Failure of regeneration
Operators describe procedures but do not supply the substantive requirement that belief, representation and consequential judgement remain answerable to reality. CR6 can repair discovered error but does not by itself prohibit knowingly distorted representation before failure.

**Result: CR2 SURVIVES.**

## CR3 — Protected Effective Agency and Non-Domination

### Ablation attempt
Candidate substitutes:
- CR1 harm restraint;
- CR4 care;
- CR6 correction;
- CO10 non-forcing.

### Failure of regeneration
A domination structure can constrain meaningful choice without producing immediately measurable serious harm and without placing the subject in a conventional need-state. Non-forcing is an operator and cannot supply the underlying reason that another agent's viable functioning matters.

Making CR1 cover arbitrary subordination would expand “harm” into agency by definition.

**Result: CR3 SURVIVES.**

## CR4 — Care Under Need and Vulnerability

### Ablation attempt
Candidate substitutes:
- CR1 avoid harm;
- CR3 respect agency;
- CR5 resource responsibility.

### Failure of regeneration
All three can prohibit causing injury or exploitation while still permitting passive abandonment of severe need where the capable agent did not create the condition. E7 contains positive-assistance material that cannot be preserved merely as non-interference.

**Result: CR4 SURVIVES.**
