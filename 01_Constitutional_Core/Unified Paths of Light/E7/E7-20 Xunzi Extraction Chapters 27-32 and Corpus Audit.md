### Provenance resolution
The Xunzi block is coded primarily at chapter/thematic-passage resolution. This is sufficient for internal E7 extraction and later normalisation, but **not yet a publication-grade passage-by-passage source audit**.

Chapters with composite, poetic or later-collected character are explicitly treated more cautiously.

### Translation-sensitive concepts
Particular care is required around:
- xìng (“nature”);
- wěi / conscious activity or deliberate effort;
- lǐ / ritual;
- yì / appropriate/right conduct;
- fēn / distinctions or divisions;
- míng / names;
- xīn / heart-mind;
- tiān / Heaven/natural order.

### E7-internal recurrent families
Without external comparison, Xunzi strongly develops:
- capability through cultivation;
- self-regulation;
- correction and remonstrance;
- evidence/function over appearance/status;
- sustainable material administration;
- role competence;
- constraints on concentrated authority;
- language/classification integrity;
- causal attribution;
- proportionality;
- social coordination through learned norms;
- the danger of partial perspectives.

### Important internal contrast
Mencius and Xunzi disagree sharply in their theories of human nature, yet both generate substantial ethical architecture around cultivation, learning, environmental conditions and realised capability. This is an **internal E7 observation**, not an external kernel comparison.
