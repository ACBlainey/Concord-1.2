# Unified Paths of Light — E7
## E7-11 — Mencius Extraction: Book 1 (Liang Hui Wang I–II)

**Primary working source:** *The Works/Sayings of Mencius*, James Legge translation, checked against the Chinese Text Project received-text structure. Locators use the conventional seven-book A/B divisions. Functional propositions below are E7 normalisations, not quotations.
