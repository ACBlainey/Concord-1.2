## Immediate methodological observations
Without comparison to any earlier ethical kernel, the combined Chinese corpus exhibits:
- substantial disagreement about human nature, ritual, hierarchy, intervention and the proper degree of political coordination;
- repeated separation between outward form/status and underlying function;
- unusually rich self-cultivation and evaluator-correction material;
- recurring attention to power-distorted information;
- recurrent consistency tests across scale, group and standpoint;
- strong concern with material conditions and institutional shaping of conduct;
- both pro-structuring and anti-overcontrol traditions within the same experimental corpus.

These observations must not be compressed into roots yet.
