## Coverage note
Books 1–4 were read at chapter/passage resolution. Records above retain distinct ethical functions rather than one record per chapter. Genealogical, ceremonial and context-only passages with no separable behavioural proposition were not forced into the ledger.

## Running audit
Strong early recurrence appears around self-cultivation, truthful/accurate judgement, correction, role responsibility, non-coercive governance and the distinction between outward form and underlying function. These are **E7-internal observations only**.
