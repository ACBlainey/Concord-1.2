## Chinese-specific material above root level
The absence of a new deepest root does **not** mean E7 added nothing distinctive. Chinese source families generated unusually visible elaborations around:
- ritual/form versus underlying function;
- self-cultivation and reflexive regulation;
- perspective and standpoint;
- low-force/non-forcing intervention;
- relational and role-sensitive coordination;
- merit and competence in office;
- information distortion under hierarchy;
- consistency across interpersonal and state scale;
- public-benefit and opportunity-cost reasoning.

These largely occupy operator, condition or derived-rule layers rather than requiring an eighth frozen root.

## Important negative result
**Legitimate and Bounded Power** appeared as an E7-internal eighth root candidate but failed minimum-stability before X was reopened. Its functions were regenerated from the seven surviving roots under power/institutional conditions.

This strengthens rather than weakens the reverse test: a plausible Chinese residual was explicitly allowed to emerge and was eliminated internally rather than suppressed during comparison.
