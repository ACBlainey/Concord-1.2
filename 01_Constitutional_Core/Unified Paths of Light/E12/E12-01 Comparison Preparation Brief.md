# E12-01 — Scientology Comparison Preparation Brief

**Status:** **READY FOR SOURCE RESOLUTION AND CORPUS FREEZE**

## Rationale

Scientology is much newer than the traditions used in the founding Unified Paths comparisons. Its distinctive terminology, source structure and ethical claims may provide an unusually informative stress test.

The purpose is not to assume that its ethics are either novel or defective. The experiment must allow convergence, divergence, unusual structure and methodological difficulty to emerge from the source material.

## Required Preparation

Before extraction:
- define the primary source corpus;
- distinguish foundational texts, ethics-related texts, institutional policy and later commentary;
- establish source authority and edition/provenance controls;
- determine what material is legitimately accessible and citable;
- freeze inclusion/exclusion rules;
- preserve distinctive terminology rather than prematurely translating it into U1–U8;
- register predictions before extraction.

## Method

**Source resolution → Corpus freeze → Independent extraction → Provenance audit → Internal normalisation/compression → Minimum-stability test → E12 kernel freeze → U1–U8 comparison**

Special attention should be paid to whether unusual terminology masks familiar ethical functions or represents genuinely different structure.

## Current State

Ready to begin source/corpus preparation when comparative work resumes.
