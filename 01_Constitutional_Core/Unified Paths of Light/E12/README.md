## Purpose

E12 examines Scientology as a very recent religious/ideological system with a distinctive body of teachings.

Its recency and potentially unusual ethical architecture make it a useful stress case for the Unified Paths method.

The experiment asks whether independent extraction produces:
- familiar root ethical functions;
- materially different weighting or partitioning;
- unusual compound rules;
- additional candidate roots;
- tensions or absences relative to U1–U8;
- or ethical structures that resist the established compression model.

No outcome is presumed.

## Experimental Position
