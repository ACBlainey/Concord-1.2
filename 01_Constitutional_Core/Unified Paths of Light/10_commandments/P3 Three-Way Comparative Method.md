# Ten Commandments Comparative Project
## P3 — Three-Way Comparative Method

The project now compares three structures rather than treating the Decalogue only as a comparator for the Concord:

1. **The Decalogue** — command units from Exodus 20 and Deuteronomy 5.
2. **The Concord Ethical Kernel / Blainey's Laws** — the pre-existing minimal constitutional ethics.
3. **The independently derived E3 Qur'an-first universal attractor layer** — ten frozen parent attractors obtained before comparison with Blainey's Laws.

This creates a useful triangular test.

## Why the triangle matters

A direct Concord–Decalogue match may reflect genuine shared structure, but it does not by itself distinguish deep convergence from historical inheritance.

E3 provides a second independently derived compression route.

The most informative cases are therefore:

- **all three converge directly** — candidate deep shared root;
- **Decalogue matches E3 nuance more closely than the cleaner Blainey's Law** — possible evidence that E3 has retained a downstream branch that the Concord kernel compressed away and later regenerates operationally;
- **Decalogue matches Blainey's Law more directly than E3** — possible evidence that the E3 parent remains at a different abstraction level;
- **E3 and Concord converge but Decalogue does not** — candidate deeper principle not explicitly encoded in the Decalogue;
- **Decalogue retains material absent from both** — genuine theological, covenantal or historical residual.

## Anti-forcing rule

No three-way convergence is assumed.

The numerical coincidence that E3 produced ten parent attractors and the Decalogue is traditionally called the Ten Commandments has no evidential weight by itself.

The comparison concerns function, derivability and residual information.

## Key diagnostic

For every Decalogue command the project asks:

> Does the command map most naturally to a minimal Concord law, to one of E3's more nuanced parent attractors, to a combination of principles, or to neither?

Where E3's additional nuance improves the match, that fact will be recorded rather than collapsed into the cleaner Concord law.
