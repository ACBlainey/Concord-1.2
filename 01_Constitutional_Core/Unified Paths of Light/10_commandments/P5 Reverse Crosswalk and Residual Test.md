# Ten Commandments Comparative Project
## P5 — Reverse Crosswalk and Residual Test

A forward comparison can exaggerate agreement because broad principles can absorb narrow commandments. This paper reverses the direction.

## Blainey's Laws → Decalogue

The Decalogue strongly represents **Non-Harm**, **Truth**, property/boundary protection and elements of stewardship.

It represents **Autonomy** indirectly rather than as an explicit general principle.

It does not directly formulate **Developmental Priority**, **Evolutionary Responsibility/Anti-Capture**, or **Recursive Oversight/Meta-Governance**. These remain later civilisational/systemic deductions.

## Residuals belonging to the Decalogue

Three categories remain irreducible without interpretive inflation:

1. **exclusive divine allegiance**;
2. **idolatry prohibition as a theological command**;
3. **Sabbath worship/covenant content**.

These are not failures of the Concord or E3 comparison. They show that the Decalogue is not merely a secular ethical kernel.

## Residuals belonging to E3/Concord

Conversely, the Decalogue does not explicitly state broad:
- positive social aid;
- general autonomy/consent;
- procedural justice/accountability;
- anti-domination/equality;
- general resource stewardship;
- recursive institutional oversight;
- developmental agency;
- anti-capture.

Some are inferable from or compatible with Decalogue commands, but compatibility is not identity.

## Significance of the E3 nuances

The reverse test supports the intermediate-layer hypothesis.

The E3 attractors frequently preserve the **reasoning bridge** between a minimal root and a concrete command.

The clearest example is false witness:

**Truth**  
→ **Truth + Epistemic Integrity + Justice**  
→ **do not corrupt testimony about another person.**

Likewise:

**Non-Harm / Autonomy / Stewardship**  
→ **Self-Regulation + Responsible Resource Relation**  
→ **do not covet what belongs within another's protected domain.**

Thus the additional E3 wording may be neither a new fundamental law nor redundant decoration. It may be the expected **fractal sub-branching of a deeper root principle as that principle approaches a concrete domain**.

## Provisional conclusion

The three systems occupy different resolutions:

**Blainey's Laws:** unusually compressed substrate-neutral roots.

**E3 attractors:** higher-order universal functional branches.

**Decalogue:** compact theological/covenantal and behavioural rules, several of which instantiate those branches very clearly.

The correspondence is therefore strongest when analysed hierarchically rather than by attempting one-to-one matching of ten labels against ten labels.
