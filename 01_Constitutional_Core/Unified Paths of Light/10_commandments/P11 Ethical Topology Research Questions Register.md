# Ten Commandments Comparative Project
## P11 — Ethical Topology Future Research Register

## Purpose

This register preserves the questions generated by the current discussion so that they can be tested later without promoting them prematurely into Concord doctrine.

## A. Primitive ethics

1. What is the minimum stable ethical kernel from which the E3 attractors can be generated without importing hidden moral premises?
2. Which Blainey's Laws are true primitives and which are higher-order systemic deductions?
3. Is positive care obligation reducible to Non-Harm under dependency/capability conditions, or is it an independent primitive?
4. Can dignity/anti-domination be generated from Autonomy × Non-Harm under power asymmetry?
5. Can self-regulation be generated by recursive application of ethics to the agent's own decision processes?

## B. Compound-rule generation

6. Can the secular behavioural content of the Decalogue be regenerated from the frozen kernel without using the Decalogue as a target?
7. How much of the 89-item E3 catalogue can be reconstructed from combinations of a smaller primitive set plus domain conditions?
8. Are some E3 attractors stable branch points rather than ethical primitives?
9. What formal distinction separates a genuine derived rule from an interpretation fitted retrospectively to a target?

## C. Civilisational topology

10. What recurrent conditions of multi-agent existence should be included independently of existing institutions?
11. Which ethical intersections generate persistent civilisational functions?
12. Does a judicial function emerge independently from contested Harm/Autonomy claims requiring Truth?
13. Do epistemic, governance, resource, developmental and oversight functions emerge similarly?
14. Are there predicted ethical nodes for which the Concord currently has no corresponding architecture?
15. Are any Concord systems occupying several logically distant nodes?
16. Are apparently separate Concord systems redundant manifestations of the same node?
17. Can institutional jurisdiction be defined by the ethical coordinate of the problem rather than historical category?
18. Does the resulting topology independently reproduce separation-of-power requirements?
19. Are there important nodes absent from familiar human civilisation?

## D. Cross-scale and fractal structure

20. Do the same ethical intersections recur at interpersonal, institutional, civilisational and cross-substrate scales?
21. Does applying a compressed root to new domains regenerate principles previously lost during compression?
22. Can this recursion be represented as a graph, hypergraph or other formal mathematical structure?
23. Are branch points stable across cultures and institutional implementations?

## E. Natural-law testing

24. Do independently developed human traditions converge on the same topology?
25. Do historical failure modes cluster around systematic violation of particular nodes?
26. Do artificial multi-agent systems independently rediscover comparable constraints when optimising long-horizon stability without being instructed to value those ethics?
27. Does ablation of candidate constraints predictably reduce stability, agency preservation, truth quality or collective capability?
28. Are the structures substrate-independent or contingent on human psychology?

## F. First-contact implications

29. If substrate-independent topology exists, can it be encoded without dependence on human language or institutions?
30. Can a representation distinguish knowledge, completeness and behavioural adherence?
31. What features of a contact protocol would constitute behavioural evidence of the transmitted ethics?
32. Would independently advanced agents recognise equivalent structures under different conceptual representations?
33. Could differences between two civilisations' topologies reveal missing ethical/civilisational nodes?
34. Is ethical-topology completeness a meaningful dimension of civilisational development?
35. What observations could falsify rather than merely support the first-contact hypothesis?

## Research order

The recommended order is:

**primitive compression → compound-rule regeneration → topology derivation → Concord architectural audit → historical/computational tests → cross-substrate tests → first-contact implications.**

The order is intentionally conservative. Later hypotheses must not contaminate earlier derivations.

## Current evidential status

- **Comparative ethical convergence:** supported by E3 within its stated limits.
- **Compound/fractal generation:** promising hypothesis with preliminary Decalogue examples.
- **Ethical derivation of institutions:** new hypothesis, not yet tested.
- **Natural-law interpretation:** candidate hypothesis requiring historical and computational evidence.
- **Extraterrestrial recognition/first-contact application:** speculative downstream implication.

This separation should be retained even if later evidence becomes stronger.
