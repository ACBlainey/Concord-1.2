# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-53 — N2 Parent–Child Compression C: Power, Self-Regulation and Development

## Entrusted power
P52–P62 → five parents:

**N2-27 Functional Legitimacy of Authority** — Authority is ethically assessed by the function it serves, not possession or status alone.

**N2-28 Heightened Responsibility Under Power** — Greater/entrusted power carries greater responsibility because its errors and abuses propagate outward.

**N2-29 Competent and Correctable Office** — Consequential roles require competence/integrity and access to truthful corrective counsel.

**N2-30 Constraint of Concentrated Power** — Delegated or informal power should remain reviewable where predation, corruption or suppression of correction is possible.

**N2-31 Purpose-Bounded Institutional Means** — Secrecy, exceptional authority and institutional reputation require functional justification and cannot become self-justifying ends.

## Self-regulation
P63–P70 → four parents:

**N2-32 Regulation of Reactive Drives** — Regulate anger, greed, compulsive desire and pride before they govern consequential conduct.

**N2-33 Reflective Self-Scrutiny** — Examine motives and conduct rather than locating ethical failure exclusively outside oneself.

**N2-34 Steadiness and Non-Extremity** — Cultivate patience/equanimity and avoid destructive extremes.

**N2-35 Practice Over Display** — Ethical discipline is tested by conduct rather than austerity, status or reputation alone.

## Development
P71–P79 → five parents:

**N2-36 Developable Ethical Capability** — Ethical judgement/capability can be cultivated through practice.

**N2-37 Capability-Sensitive Instruction** — Match instruction and developmental demand to readiness and capacity.

**N2-38 Capability-Sensitive Responsibility** — Match responsibility to actual capability and relevant conditions.

**N2-39 Inquiry and Applied Learning** — Permit staged inquiry, recognition of ignorance and testing of learning through conduct.

**N2-40 Developmental Corrigibility** — Reopen conclusions when learning or new information changes the feasible action space; capability alone is not wisdom.

## Result
28 N1 propositions → **14 N2 parents**.
