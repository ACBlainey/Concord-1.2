# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-57 — N3 Architectural Separation B: Operators

The corpus repeatedly supplies procedures that alter how ethical constraints are applied without themselves behaving like independent ends.

### HO1 — Proportionality
Scale intervention, force, burden or response to the seriousness and structure of the problem.

### HO2 — Consequence Horizon
Consider foreseeable downstream effects, escalation, dependency and future action-space.

### HO3 — Verification
Test consequential factual claims before committing action.

### HO4 — Contextual Discrimination
Distinguish materially different cases rather than applying surface labels mechanically.

### HO5 — Conflict Comparison
When duties collide, compare seriousness, reversibility, alternatives and affected agents.

### HO6 — Perspective and Standing Check
Include the standpoint and reasons of agents directly subjected to consequential decisions where feasible.

### HO7 — Recursive Review
Reopen belief, judgement, policy or exceptional action when correction-relevant evidence appears.

### HO8 — Purpose-Boundary Test
Evaluate whether an instrument, office, secrecy rule, emergency exception or role practice still serves the function that justified it.

### HO9 — Self-Regulation Check
Before consequential action, test whether anger, greed, pride, compulsion or status pressure is distorting judgement.

### HO10 — Practice Validation
Test claimed virtue, learning, authority or discipline against actual conduct and consequences.

## Architectural result
These operators explain why many N2 propositions disappeared from root candidacy without disappearing from the ethical system.

A rule can be ethically indispensable while remaining **procedural rather than root-level**.
