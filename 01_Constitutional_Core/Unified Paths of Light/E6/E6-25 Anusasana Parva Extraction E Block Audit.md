## Strong internal families
Anuśāsana adds or reinforces:
- non-injury and compassion;
- perspective-taking/reciprocity;
- positive aid under need;
- capacity-sensitive giving;
- respectful rather than humiliating aid;
- protection of dependants;
- non-exploitative acquisition and use of resources;
- truthfulness with hard-case contextual reasoning;
- commitment/reliance;
- conduct over status or outward austerity;
- power as responsibility;
- protection under vulnerability;
- capability-sensitive duty and instruction;
- acknowledgement, reform and changed conduct.

## Tensions preserved
- ahiṃsā versus legitimate protective force;
- truthfulness versus disclosure that facilitates grave harm;
- generosity versus duties to existing dependants;
- universal moral language versus historically asymmetric role structures;
- respect for authority versus authority fallibility;
- role-duty versus higher evaluative standards.
