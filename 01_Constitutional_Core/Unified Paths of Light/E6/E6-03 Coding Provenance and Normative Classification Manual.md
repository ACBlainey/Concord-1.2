# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-03 — Coding, Provenance and Normative Classification Manual

## Ethical Source Unit
An ESU is the smallest contextually sufficient passage, teaching, rule or narrative unit capable of supporting a distinct behavioural proposition.

## Normative classes
**P — Prescriptive:** explicit behavioural instruction/prohibition.  
**V — Virtue/character:** quality explicitly commended or condemned with behavioural relevance.  
**C — Consequential/causal:** conduct linked to ethically relevant consequences.  
**D — Deliberative:** reasoning procedure for resolving conduct or duty.  
**N — Narrative normative evidence:** narrative whose evaluative framing supports a behavioural proposition.  
**X — Context-bound/non-general:** recorded but not promoted without additional support.

## Scope tags
**G** general human/agent scope  
**R** role-specific  
**S** life-stage/social-status specific  
**A** ascetic/renunciant  
**H** householder  
**K** king/ruler/governance  
**C** conflict/war context

## Provenance fields
Each extraction record should contain:
- E6 occurrence ID;
- text/work;
- book/chapter/section/verse or stable locator;
- translation/source edition;
- source form;
- normative class;
- scope;
- minimally normalised behavioural proposition;
- contextual limitation;
- confidence/provenance note;
- provisional internal cluster only if it arises from E6 itself.

## Narrative rule
Narrative action is not automatically normative. Record whether:
1. narrator praises/condemns it;
2. consequences supply evaluative framing;
3. dialogue explicitly interprets it;
4. competing duties remain unresolved.

## Translation rule
Where a term materially controls interpretation—especially dharma, ahiṃsā, satya, dāna, dama, tyāga, karma, svadharma—record the source term and avoid forcing a single English equivalent.
