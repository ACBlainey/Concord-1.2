# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-31 — Rāmāyaṇa Structured Narrative Sample Charter

## Status
**FINAL PRIMARY-CORPUS SAMPLE FROZEN BEFORE EXTRACTION.**

The Rāmāyaṇa is sampled across narrative functions rather than mined for isolated agreeable verses.

## Frozen episode families
1. Ayodhyā succession, Kaikeyī's boons, Daśaratha's promise and Rāma's exile.
2. Forest encounters: protection, hospitality, ascetic communities and conflict.
3. Sītā's abduction, alliance formation and search.
4. Vāli–Sugrīva conflict and the ethics of intervention.
5. Hanumān in Laṅkā: intelligence, restraint, communication and proportional action.
6. Rāvaṇa's counsel and refusal of corrective advice.
7. War, surrender/defection, treatment of enemies and aftermath.
8. Sītā's ordeal and later social-judgement material as an explicit ethical-tension control.

## Critical rule
Narrative centrality is not ethical endorsement. The treatment of Sītā, social hierarchy, gender roles, warfare and royal authority must be allowed to generate tension or counterevidence.

## Version/provenance rule
The later provenance audit must identify the textual/translation basis and flag passages whose placement or textual history materially affects interpretation.
