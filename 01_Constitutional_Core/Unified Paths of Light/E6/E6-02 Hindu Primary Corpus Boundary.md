# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-02 — Hindu Primary Corpus Boundary

## Objective
Define the E6 search space before ethical extraction.

## Primary derivation corpus

### Bhagavad Gītā
Complete 18 chapters.

### Principal Upaniṣadic ethical sample
A predefined core set:
- Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad
- Chāndogya Upaniṣad
- Taittirīya Upaniṣad
- Īśā Upaniṣad
- Kena Upaniṣad
- Kaṭha Upaniṣad
- Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad
- Praśna Upaniṣad
- Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad

The Upaniṣads are not assumed to be primarily ethical texts. Only contextually supportable behavioural propositions qualify.

### Mahābhārata ethical/didactic strata
Predefined focus:
- Bhagavad Gītā is handled separately above;
- Śānti Parva;
- Anuśāsana Parva;
- ethically explicit narrative cases elsewhere only under a documented sampling rule, not opportunistic search.

### Rāmāyaṇa
Use a structured narrative sample across major kāṇḍas to test duty, rule conflict, loyalty, power, care, truth and role conduct without assuming every narrative action is endorsed.

## Withheld validation corpora
Held out until E6's internal architecture is frozen:
- Dharmaśāstra/Manusmṛti;
- Yoga Sūtras;
- later Vedānta commentarial traditions;
- Purāṇic materials;
- Bhakti corpora;
- later regional/sectarian Hindu literature.

These can later test stability, historical development and domain elaboration.

## Boundary rationale
The corpus combines philosophical, dialogical, didactic and narrative forms while avoiding an unbounded claim to represent all Hindu traditions.

## Closure
Primary corpus expansion is prohibited merely because a predicted X root appears weak or absent.
