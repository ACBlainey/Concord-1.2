## N4 attack procedure
For every root:
1. **Deletion:** remove it and attempt to regenerate its unique function.
2. **Fusion:** merge it with the most plausible neighbour and test semantic loss.
3. **Operator substitution:** ask whether it is really a procedure rather than a constraint.
4. **Condition substitution:** ask whether apparent roothood is only a recurrent situation.
5. **Adversarial case:** identify an E6 case where the reduced architecture gives the wrong or incomplete result.

A root survives only if removal causes meaningful loss.
