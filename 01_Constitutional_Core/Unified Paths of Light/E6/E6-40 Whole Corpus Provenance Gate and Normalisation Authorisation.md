## Normalisation safeguards
1. Source frequency is emphasis evidence, not automatic principle multiplicity.
2. Narrative action is not prescription.
3. Role-specific rules do not automatically universalise.
4. Theological/metaphysical propositions do not automatically become behavioural roots.
5. Synthesis language must not masquerade as source wording.
6. Tensions survive compression unless the corpus itself resolves them.
7. No X1–X7, E3/E4/E5 or Blainey-law comparison during E6 normalisation.
8. No corpus expansion to fill a perceived ethical gap.
