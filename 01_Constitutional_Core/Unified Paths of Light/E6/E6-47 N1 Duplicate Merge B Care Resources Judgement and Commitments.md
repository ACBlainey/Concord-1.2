# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-47 — N1 Duplicate Merge B: Care, Resources, Judgement and Commitments

### Care/dependency
P25 Provide proportionate assistance under serious need when capable.
P26 Protect dependants and agents with reduced effective capacity.
P27 Treat vulnerability as increasing rather than cancelling ethical salience.
P28 Give with regard to need, circumstance and recipient dignity.
P29 Match positive aid obligations to actual capacity.
P30 Preserve legitimate responsibilities to existing dependants when aiding others.
P31 Extend compassion beyond immediate reciprocal exchange.

### Resources
P32 Avoid exploitative or greed-driven acquisition.
P33 Respect legitimate possession/resource claims.
P34 Use controlled or entrusted resources with regard to legitimate obligations.
P35 Preserve resources required for dependants and continuing productive function.
P36 Evaluate resource use by meaningful benefit, not quantity/accumulation alone.
P37 Consider downstream/future consequences of resource use.

### Judgement/responsibility
P38 Judge conduct and evidence rather than status alone.
P39 Distinguish intention, knowledge, means, constraint and foreseeable consequence.
P40 Give affected persons meaningful standing to challenge consequential judgement.
P41 Do not use procedural ambiguity to legitimate substantive wrong.
P42 Apply ethical constraints even where self-interest favours exception.
P43 Preserve correction after mistaken judgement and permit changed conduct to matter.
P44 Consider seriousness, reversibility and available alternatives where duties conflict.

### Commitments/relationships
P45 Treat commitments as consequential because others rely upon them.
P46 Do not treat fidelity as sufficient justification for a reckless or wrongful commitment.
P47 Reciprocal commitments create reciprocal responsibilities.
P48 Permit challenge to loyalty where loyalty facilitates wrongdoing.
P49 Relationship does not erase another person's independent standing.
P50 Care, instruction and dependency create role-sensitive responsibilities.
P51 Preserve social bonds without making them absolute.

## Result
Distinct positive-care, resource, adjudicative and commitment functions remain visible after duplicate removal.
