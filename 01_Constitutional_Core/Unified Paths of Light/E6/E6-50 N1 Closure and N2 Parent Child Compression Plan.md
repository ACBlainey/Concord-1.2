## N2 question
For each proposition:

> If a broader proposition is retained together with the relevant context/operator, can this narrower proposition be regenerated without losing a meaningful ethical boundary?

If yes, the narrower proposition becomes a child/application rather than an independent principle.

## High-pressure families
Likely parent-child pressure exists within:
- protection → force/proportion/peace/adversary constraints;
- epistemic integrity → verification/uncertainty/correction;
- effective agency → refusal/compulsion/affected-person standing;
- care → vulnerability/dependency/capacity;
- resource responsibility → acquisition/use/future consequences;
- judgement → evidence/context/reversibility;
- entrusted power → counsel/review/correction;
- self-regulation → anger/greed/pride;
- development → instruction/capability/application;
- contextual application → emergency/necessity/exception;
- correctability → acknowledgement/change/repair/reform.

## Important non-merger warning
N2 must not assume that because one principle often motivates another, it is its parent.

Examples:
- truth does not automatically contain care;
- protection does not automatically contain agency;
- agency does not automatically contain resource stewardship;
- self-regulation does not automatically contain institutional correction.
