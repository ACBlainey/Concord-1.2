# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-54 — N2 Parent–Child Compression D: Emergency, Repair and Cross-Family Pressure

## Emergency/context
P80–P88 → four parents:

**N2-41 Context-Sensitive Application** — Ethical application may change with materially changed conditions without making every constraint arbitrary.

**N2-42 Necessity-Bounded Exception** — Genuine emergency may modify ordinary implementation, but necessity must be distinguished from convenience.

**N2-43 Exception Containment** — Exceptional permission remains constrained by purpose, duration and residual restraint.

**N2-44 Conflict and Survival Discrimination** — Under unavoidable conflict, compare harms, alternatives, reversibility and preservation of future responsible agency.

## Repair/correction/continuity
P89–P96 → four parents:

**N2-45 Acknowledgement and Behavioural Correction** — Recognised error or wrongdoing should produce relevant change in conduct.

**N2-46 Repair and Reform** — Repair harm where feasible and permit genuine reform to affect later judgement.

**N2-47 Cooperative Reconstruction** — Restore conditions required for ordinary cooperative life after severe disruption.

**N2-48 Anti-Erosion Correctability** — Preserve channels for corrective information and prevent repeated exceptions from silently dissolving shared restraints.

## Cross-family pressure
Several N2 parents now appear potentially non-root:
- N2-02 may be an operator applying N2-01.
- N2-03 may combine N2-01 with conflict conditions.
- N2-04 may combine consequence horizon with repair.
- N2-15 may be N2-13 plus agency/status constraints.
- N2-19 may be resource responsibility plus temporal consequence.
- N2-23 may be a conflict-resolution operator.
- N2-31 may be power plus purpose-bounding operator.
- N2-32–35 may be enabling operators/agent conditions rather than social ethical roots.
- N2-41–44 are especially operator/context-like.
- N2-45–48 may share a deeper correctability function.

No merger is executed merely because of this pressure. N3 will test architectural type explicitly.
