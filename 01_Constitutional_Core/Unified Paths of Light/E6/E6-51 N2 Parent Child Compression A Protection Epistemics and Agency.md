# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-51 — N2 Parent–Child Compression A: Protection, Epistemics and Agency

## Protection
P01–P08 compress to four parent functions:

**N2-01 Harm Restraint and Protection** — Avoid unnecessary serious injury and protect life/vulnerability where capable.

**N2-02 Protective Proportionality** — Where force or coercion is necessary for protection, constrain it by purpose, circumstance and scale.

**N2-03 De-Escalation and Adversary Reclassification** — Prefer non-destructive settlement where protection remains achievable, and permit surrender/refuge or changed conditions to alter adversarial treatment.

**N2-04 Consequence and Recovery Horizon** — Consider escalation and downstream harm, and preserve repair/reconstruction after severe injury.

P03/P04/P05/P06/P07/P08 become children of these four.

## Epistemics
P09–P18 compress to five:

**N2-05 Truthful Representation** — Do not knowingly misrepresent material reality or manufacture false belief.

**N2-06 Consequential Verification** — Test relevant factual claims before consequential action and distinguish evidence from accusation/reputation.

**N2-07 Epistemic Humility** — Represent uncertainty and knowledge limits honestly; proportion confidence accordingly.

**N2-08 Corrigible Inquiry** — Permit relevant questioning and revise belief/action when materially better information appears.

**N2-09 Corrective Knowledge Transmission** — Preserve channels through which reliable and corrective information can survive status, flattery and suppression.

## Agency
P19–P24 compress to three:

**N2-10 Protected Effective Agency** — Do not convert relationship, status, capability or dependency into ownership of another's responsible agency.

**N2-11 Meaningful Refusal and Participation** — Respect meaningful refusal and distinguish effective voluntary participation from nominal compliance produced by compulsion or impaired judgement.

**N2-12 Affected-Agent Standing** — Where feasible, include directly affected agents in consequential decisions concerning them and permit changed conduct to alter prior categorisation.
