## Īśā Upaniṣad

|---|---|---|---|---|

## Internal note
Taittirīya supplies unusually explicit conduct instructions and a procedure for uncertainty. Īśā supplies compact material on non-covetous use, action, non-hatred and complementary modes of understanding.
