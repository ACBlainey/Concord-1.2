## N1 merge rules
N1 will merge propositions only where:
1. behavioural function is substantially identical;
2. differences are source wording or domain instantiation rather than independent constraint;
3. regeneration after merge does not lose a meaningful boundary.

N1 will not merge merely because:
- two propositions often co-occur;
- one can motivate the other;
- both sound virtuous;
- one tradition-specific concept can be translated using the same English word;
- a merger would make the catalogue resemble a known external architecture.
