## Non-root residuals
E6 nevertheless contains substantial distinctive elaboration:
- self-regulation of anger, greed, pride and attachment;
- duty/role reasoning;
- emergency ethics;
- ruler/counsel architecture;
- promise and relational reliance;
- renunciant/householder tensions;

Therefore “no root residual” does **not** mean “no Hindu ethical distinctiveness.”
