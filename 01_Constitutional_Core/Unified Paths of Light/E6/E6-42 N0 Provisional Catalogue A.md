# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-42 — N0 Provisional Catalogue A

## Status
**OVERCOMPLETE. DO NOT TREAT AS FROZEN PRINCIPLES.**

### Harm, protection and conflict
N0-001 Avoid unnecessary injury.
N0-002 Preserve life where serious competing reason does not override.
N0-003 Protect vulnerable persons from predation.
N0-004 Restrain stronger actors from exploiting weaker actors.
N0-005 Distinguish aggression from protective force.
N0-006 Use force for protective function rather than private anger.
N0-007 Limit coercion by circumstance and proportion.
N0-008 Prefer peaceful settlement where it can secure legitimate protection.
N0-009 Consider foreseeable retaliation and escalation.
N0-010 Do not treat victory as proof that the means were justified.
N0-011 Preserve restraint toward enemies where feasible.
N0-012 Permit refuge/surrender to alter adversarial status.
N0-013 Avoid multiplying harm through anger or revenge.
N0-014 Repair social conditions after collective violence.

### Truth, knowledge and inquiry
N0-015 Speak truthfully.
N0-016 Do not use literal truth deliberately to manufacture false belief.
N0-017 Consider whether disclosure directly enables grave wrongful harm.
N0-018 Distinguish established fact from reputation or accusation.
N0-019 Verify identity and relevant facts before consequential action.
N0-020 Hear competing accounts where facts are disputed.
N0-021 Recognise uncertainty and limits of knowledge.
N0-022 Revise belief when better information appears.
N0-023 Do not pretend knowledge one does not possess.
N0-024 Seek competent guidance where uncertainty exceeds one's capacity.
N0-025 Test inherited or authoritative claims through relevant questioning.
N0-026 Preserve corrective information against flattery and suppression.
N0-027 Match confidence to evidence.
N0-028 Distinguish outward information accumulation from understanding.
N0-029 Preserve reliable transmission of consequential knowledge.

### Agency, consent and refusal
N0-030 Do not assume ownership of another person's agency from relationship/status.
N0-031 Respect meaningful refusal.
N0-032 Include directly affected persons in decisions about their protection where feasible.
N0-033 Do not exploit impaired judgement or compulsion.
N0-034 Distinguish nominal participation from meaningfully voluntary participation.
N0-035 Do not exploit dependence to obtain compliance.
N0-036 Preserve agency under vulnerability where possible.
N0-037 Treat bodily/intimate boundaries as ethically significant.
N0-038 Distinguish protective assistance from overriding the protected person's judgement without sufficient reason.
N0-039 Allow changed allegiance/conduct to alter prior categorisation.
N0-040 Do not infer entitlement from capability alone.

### Care, need and dependency
N0-041 Respond positively to serious need when capable.
N0-042 Protect dependants.
N0-043 Give with regard to recipient need and circumstance.
N0-044 Do not humiliate recipients through aid.
N0-045 Match aid obligation to capacity.
N0-046 Do not sacrifice dependants merely for performative generosity.
N0-047 Treat vulnerability as increasing ethical salience.
N0-048 Extend concern beyond immediate kin/status where the source supports it.
N0-049 Hospitality can create positive protective duties.
N0-050 Compassion is more than abstention from aggression.
