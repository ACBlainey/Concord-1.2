## Status

Before extracting narrative ethics outside the already sampled didactic strata, the following episode families are frozen:

1. Dice game, Draupadī's challenge and assembly response.
2. Yakṣa–Yudhiṣṭhira dialogue.

5. Selected battlefield hard cases: Bhīṣma, Droṇa, Karṇa and post-action moral evaluation.

## Purpose
The sample tests ethics under collision:
- authority versus legitimacy;
- role versus conscience;
- truth versus protection;
- promise versus coercion;
- loyalty versus wrongdoing;
- peace versus protective force;
- procedural form versus substantive justice.

## Narrative rule
An action is not coded as ethically endorsed merely because a central or admired character performs it.

Evidence requires at least one of:
- explicit praise/condemnation;
- consequence-based evaluative framing;
- reasoned dialogue about the action;
- later acknowledgement/repair;
- repeated narrative contrast.

## Anti-selection rule
Episodes are not added because they appear likely to satisfy the frozen X prediction.
