## E6-37 — Provenance Audit A: Bhagavad Gītā and Upaniṣads

## Bhagavad Gītā
The HG ledger uses chapter/verse locators throughout. This is adequate for the declared first-pass resolution.

### Audit controls
- Treat Arjuna's chapter-1 arguments as deliberative/narrative evidence, not settled doctrine.
- Preserve warrior-specific scope for svadharma/war passages.
- Preserve the distinction between detachment from possessive outcome-control and indifference to consequences.
- Do not modernise guṇa psychology into contemporary psychological science.
- Keep 18.63 as teacher-context deliberative agency, not a freestanding modern autonomy declaration.

**Resolution: PASS at verse-family level.**

## Upaniṣads
