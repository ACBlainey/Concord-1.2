# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-52 — N2 Parent–Child Compression B: Care, Resources, Judgement and Commitment

## Care
P25–P31 → four parents:

**N2-13 Positive Care Under Need** — Provide proportionate assistance where serious need exists and capacity to help is available.

**N2-14 Dependency Protection** — Protect dependants and agents whose effective capacity is reduced.

**N2-15 Dignified Aid** — Need does not erase recipient standing; assistance should avoid needless humiliation and attend to circumstance.

**N2-16 Capacity-Bounded Obligation** — Positive duties are shaped by actual capacity and legitimate competing responsibilities.

## Resources
P32–P37 → three parents:

**N2-17 Non-Exploitative Acquisition and Possession** — Do not obtain or control resources through predation, exploitation or greed-driven disregard of legitimate claims.

**N2-18 Responsible Resource Use** — Controlled or entrusted resources should be used with regard to legitimate obligations and meaningful benefit.

**N2-19 Continuity-Aware Stewardship** — Preserve resources and productive conditions required by dependants and foreseeable future obligations.

## Judgement
P38–P44 → four parents:

**N2-20 Evidence-Responsive Judgement** — Judge conduct from relevant evidence rather than status, accusation or reputation.

**N2-21 Responsibility in Context** — Consider intention, knowledge, means, constraint and foreseeable consequence when attributing responsibility.

**N2-22 Challenge and Correctability of Judgement** — Consequential judgement should remain challengeable by affected agents and corrigible after error.

**N2-23 Conflict Discrimination** — Where duties collide, compare seriousness, reversibility and feasible alternatives rather than relying on a single label.

## Commitments
P45–P51 → three parents:

**N2-24 Reliance-Sensitive Commitment** — Commitments matter because other agents organise action around them.

**N2-25 Ethical Limits of Loyalty and Promise** — Fidelity does not transform a reckless/wrongful commitment or loyalty-facilitated wrongdoing into ethical conduct.

**N2-26 Relational Responsibility Without Ownership** — Relationships create responsibilities but do not erase independent standing or make social bonds absolute.

## Result
27 N1 propositions → **14 N2 parents**.
