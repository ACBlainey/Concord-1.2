## Frozen roots

### H1 — Harm Restraint and Protection
Avoid, prevent and reduce serious unjustified harm, and protect life or vulnerability where capacity and circumstance create a relevant responsibility.

### H2 — Truthful and Reality-Responsive Judgement
Maintain truthful representation and make consequential belief and judgement responsive to relevant evidence, uncertainty and correction.

### H3 — Protected Effective Agency
Do not convert power, relationship, status, dependency or capability into arbitrary ownership or suppression of another agent's meaningful choice and standing.

### H4 — Positive Care Under Need
Where serious need, dependency or reduced effective capacity exists, provide proportionate assistance when meaningful capacity to help is available.

### H5 — Responsible Resource Relation
Acquire, control and use resources without exploitation and with regard to legitimate claims, entrusted obligations, dependency, meaningful use and continuity.

### H6 — Correctability and Repair
Preserve the capacity for error, wrongdoing and harmful outcomes to be identified, challenged and corrected, with repair and genuine reform recognised where feasible.

### H7 — Capability-Sensitive Development
Treat judgement and ethical capability as developable, and match instruction, responsibility and expectations to actual capability, conditions and developmental state.
