# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-38 — Provenance Audit B: Mahābhārata Didactic Strata

## Finding
Śānti and Anuśāsana were deliberately extracted at section/thematic-family resolution, but many HS/HA rows do not yet carry stable section numbers.

That resolution can support **exploratory normalisation**, but it does not support publication claims that each row is a passage-level quotation-equivalent extraction.

## Classification
Records are therefore treated as one of:
- **D — direct/recurrent doctrinal family:** proposition closely tracks a recurring explicit teaching;
- **S — synthesis:** functional proposition summarises multiple related passages/cases;
- **C — cautious/contextual:** plausible ethical content but requires translation/section verification before strong citation.

## High-confidence families
The following are sufficiently recurrent to remain in normalisation:
- ahiṃsā/non-injury;
- truthfulness;
- compassion;
- dāna/generosity;
- restraint of anger/greed;
- ruler protection duties;
- counsel and self-control under power;
- proportion/context in daṇḍa;
- emergency modification of ordinary duty;
- care for dependants;
- conduct over outward status/display.

## Claims retained only as synthesis
Detailed formulations concerning modern-sounding oversight, institutional correction, evidentiary procedure, emergency sunset, or proportional policy must remain labelled synthesis unless later tied to stable passages.

## Rule
Normalisation may compress the ethical function of these records, but later publication must not cite the internal HS/HA wording as if it were the wording of the Mahābhārata.

## Status
**PASS FOR INTERNAL NORMALISATION AT DECLARED THEMATIC RESOLUTION.**
**PASS NOT EQUIVALENT TO PASSAGE-LEVEL PUBLICATION VERIFICATION.**
