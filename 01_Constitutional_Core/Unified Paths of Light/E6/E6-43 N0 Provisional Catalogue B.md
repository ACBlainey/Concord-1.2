# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-43 — N0 Provisional Catalogue B

## Resources and possession
N0-051 Avoid covetous or greed-driven acquisition.
N0-052 Do not acquire resources through exploitation.
N0-053 Respect legitimate possession and resource claims.
N0-054 Use controlled resources with regard to legitimate obligations.
N0-055 Convert surplus/control into meaningful use where serious need exists.
N0-056 Preserve resources needed by dependants.
N0-057 Entrusted resources carry obligations distinct from unrestricted consumption.
N0-058 Resource extraction should not destroy the productive base on which future welfare depends.
N0-059 Quantity of giving alone does not determine ethical quality.
N0-060 Consider future consequences of resource use.

## Judgement, justice and responsibility
N0-061 Judge conduct rather than status alone.
N0-062 Distinguish accusation from established responsibility.
N0-063 Distinguish intention, knowledge, means and foreseeable consequence.
N0-064 Distinguish deliberate harm from accident/constrained action where relevant.
N0-065 Give affected parties meaningful standing to challenge consequential decisions.
N0-066 Do not exploit procedural ambiguity to impose substantive wrong.
N0-067 Apply principles even where self-interest favours exception.
N0-068 Treat constrained alternatives as relevant to responsibility.
N0-069 Preserve possibility of correction after mistaken judgement.
N0-070 Changed conduct may matter after wrongdoing.
N0-071 Do not equate public approval with ethical legitimacy.
N0-072 Consider seriousness and reversibility where duties conflict.

## Authority, office and institutional function
N0-073 Authority is justified by function rather than possession alone.
N0-074 Entrusted power carries heightened responsibility.
N0-075 Select consequential agents for competence and integrity.
N0-076 Hear truthful unwelcome counsel.
N0-077 Do not punish correction merely because it challenges power.
N0-078 Monitor delegated power for predation/corruption.
N0-079 Informal power can disable formal safeguards.
N0-080 Public resources should serve legitimate public function.
N0-081 Confidentiality requires a protective/function-based reason.
N0-082 Institutions require effective correction mechanisms, not recognition of wrong alone.
N0-083 Temporary/emergency authority should remain purpose-limited.
N0-084 Preserve continuity beyond momentary preference of the office-holder.
N0-085 Greater power magnifies consequences of error.
N0-086 Institutional reputation does not automatically outweigh direct individual harm.

## Commitments and relationships
N0-087 Treat promises/commitments as consequential because others rely on them.
N0-088 Do not create reckless commitments whose future performance foreseeably harms others.
N0-089 Reciprocal commitments generate reciprocal obligations.
N0-090 Permit challenge to loyalty where loyalty would facilitate wrongdoing.
N0-091 Relationship does not erase another person's standing.
N0-092 Respect responsibilities created by care, instruction and dependency.
N0-093 Preserve social bonds without making them absolute.
N0-094 Evaluate commitment by both fidelity and the ethical quality of what was promised.
