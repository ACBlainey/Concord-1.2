# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-61 — N4 Ablation A: Harm, Truth, Agency and Care

## HR1 Harm Restraint and Protection
**Deletion test:** FAILS. Care, agency and resource constraints do not independently prohibit serious injury to a competent non-dependent agent.

**Fusion with HR4:** FAILS. Positive assistance under need and restraint against unjustified injury have different activation conditions.

**Result:** **SURVIVES.**

## HR2 Truthful and Reality-Responsive Judgement
**Deletion test:** FAILS. Harm/agency constraints do not themselves require accurate representation, verification or uncertainty-sensitive judgement.

**Instrumental-reduction test:** FAILS. The E6 corpus criticises falsehood, pretended knowledge, flattery and epistemic arrogance even before a particular downstream injury is demonstrated.

**Result:** **SURVIVES.**

## HR3 Protected Effective Agency
**Deletion into HR1:** FAILS. A paternalistic action can reduce expected harm while still overriding meaningful refusal or treating another as owned.

**Deletion into HR2:** FAILS. Accurate knowledge of another's wishes does not itself require respecting them.

**Result:** **SURVIVES.**

## HR4 Positive Care Under Need
**Deletion into HR1:** FAILS. Mere non-injury does not generate the positive obligation to feed, protect, support or assist a vulnerable/dependent agent where capacity exists.

**Fusion with HR3:** FAILS. Respecting agency does not by itself create material assistance.

**Result:** **SURVIVES.**

## Interim
HR1–HR4 all survive independent ablation.
