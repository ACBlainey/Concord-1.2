## Registered prediction
Before Hindu extraction, E6-04 predicted that independent Hindu derivation would substantively represent frozen X1–X7, while allowing different count, hierarchy, emphasis and domain elaboration.

## Evidential update
This is stronger evidence than the earlier E3/E4/E5 convergence because Hindu material was not used to construct X1–X7 and the prediction was registered before E6 extraction.

It supports the narrower hypothesis that the seven functional regions are not merely an artefact of fitting the three construction derivations.

## What the result does not establish
It does not prove:
- metaphysical natural law;
- that exactly seven roots are uniquely correct;
- independence from all historical cultural transmission;
- exhaustive representation of Hindu ethics;
- equivalence among Hindu, Buddhist, Jewish and Qur'anic moral thought;
- universal agreement on conflict resolution;
- that every future holdout must recover all seven.

## Alternative explanations still live
Possible contributors include:
- genuine recurrent cooperation constraints;
- broad human social/cognitive commonalities;
- historical cultural interaction;
- methodological convergence caused by functional abstraction;
- corpus-boundary effects;
- shared problems of complex social life.

The result discriminates against simple overfitting to E3/E4/E5, but not among all these explanations.

## Scientific consequence
The correct response to a successful holdout is not to declare universality. It is to increase the difficulty of the next test.
