# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-62 — N4 Ablation B: Resources, Correctability, Development and Power

## HR5 Responsible Resource Relation
**Reduction attempt:** HR3 can constrain dispossession/exploitation; HR4 can generate sharing under need; HO2 can carry future consequences.

**Failure of reduction:** none alone preserves the E6 distinction that acquisition, possession, entrustment, use, accumulation and continuity of material resources create a persistent ethical relation even where immediate coercion or severe need is absent.

**Result:** **SURVIVES.**

## HR6 Correctability and Repair
**Operator-substitution test:** recursive review can identify error, but an operator does not itself require that repair, reform or institutional correction remain ethically available.

**Reduction into HR1:** harm restraint does not entail recognition of changed conduct or corrigible institutions.

**Result:** **SURVIVES.**

## HR7 Capability-Sensitive Development
**Reduction attempt:** HR4 + HR3 under HC6 can protect and respect learners.

**Failure:** this does not independently generate the positive developmental function repeatedly present in E6: cultivate judgement, stage instruction, match responsibility to capacity and test learning in practice.

**Result:** **SURVIVES.**

## HR8 Legitimate and Bounded Power
**Deletion test:** PASSES.

Its functions regenerate as:
- legitimacy/counsel → HR2 + HR6 under HC2/HC11;
- non-domination → HR3 under HC2;
- protective responsibility → HR1/HR4 under HC2;
- competence → HR7 + HO10 under HC2;
- bounded secrecy/exception → HO8 under HC2/HC7/HC11;
- review of delegated power → HR6 + HO7 under HC2/HC11.

No unique residual remains that requires HR8 as a deepest root.

**Result:** **ELIMINATED AS ROOT.**

This does not make power constraints dispensable. They survive as high-priority derived rules.
