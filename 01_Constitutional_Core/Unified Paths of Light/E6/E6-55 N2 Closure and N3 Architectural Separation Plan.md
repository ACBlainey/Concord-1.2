## N3 question
N3 no longer asks only “can these propositions merge?”

It asks:

> **What architectural role does each proposition play?**

Candidate types:

### Root
A constraint that cannot be regenerated from other retained constraints plus operators/conditions without material loss.

### Operator
A transformation or decision procedure that determines how roots apply: proportionality, verification, contextual discrimination, conflict comparison, recursive review, etc.

### Condition
A recurrent feature of the situation that changes application: vulnerability, power asymmetry, uncertainty, scarcity, conflict, dependency, emergency, developmental difference, temporal consequence.

### Derived/domain rule
A stable application generated by roots + operators + conditions.
