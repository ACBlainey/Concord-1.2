# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-56 — N3 Architectural Separation A: Candidate Roots

## Status
**BLIND E6-INTERNAL DERIVATION. NOT FROZEN.**

The 48 N2 parents were tested for whether their function can be regenerated from other constraints plus application procedures and recurrent conditions.

Eight provisional root families survive the first architectural cut.

### HR1 — Harm Restraint and Protection
Avoid unnecessary serious harm; protect life and vulnerable agents where capacity and circumstance create a relevant responsibility.

### HR2 — Truthful and Reality-Responsive Judgement
Maintain truthful representation and make consequential belief/judgement responsive to relevant evidence, uncertainty and correction.

### HR3 — Protected Effective Agency
Do not convert power, relationship, status, dependence or capability into arbitrary ownership of another agent's meaningful choice and standing.

### HR4 — Positive Care Under Need
Serious need, dependency or reduced effective capacity can generate positive responsibilities to assist where meaningful capacity to help exists.

### HR5 — Responsible Resource Relation
Acquire, control and use resources without exploitation and with regard to legitimate claims, entrusted obligations, dependency and continuity.

### HR6 — Correctability and Repair
Preserve the possibility that error, wrongdoing and harmful outcomes can be identified, challenged, corrected and repaired where feasible.

### HR7 — Capability-Sensitive Development
Treat judgement and ethical capability as developable, and match instruction, responsibility and expectations to actual capability and conditions.

### HR8 — Legitimate and Bounded Power
Entrusted or concentrated power must remain functionally justified, competence-sensitive, open to correction and constrained against predation.

## Pressure note
HR8 is the highest-pressure candidate. Much of it may regenerate from HR2 + HR3 + HR6 under power-asymmetry conditions. It remains provisional until N4 ablation.

HR5 also faces pressure: some resource rules may be HR3/HR4 under scarcity/possession conditions. Its temporal, entrusted and non-exploitative structure may nevertheless preserve independent content.

## Result
The first blind E6 architecture contains **8 provisional roots**.
