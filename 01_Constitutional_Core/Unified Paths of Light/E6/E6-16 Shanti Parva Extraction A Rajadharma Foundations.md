## E6-16 — Śānti Parva Extraction A: Rājadharma Foundations

## Status
**PRIMARY HOLDOUT EXTRACTION. X1–X7 COMPARISON PROHIBITED.**

The Śānti Parva is treated at section/thematic-unit resolution. Narrative prescriptions, ruler-specific duties and generalisable ethical propositions remain distinguished.

## Running note
The first Śānti block is institutionally denser than the Gītā and Upaniṣads. It strongly differentiates the ethics of possessing power from the ethics of exercising entrusted power.
