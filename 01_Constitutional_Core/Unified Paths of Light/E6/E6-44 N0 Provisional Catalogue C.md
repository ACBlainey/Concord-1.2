# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-44 — N0 Provisional Catalogue C

## Self-regulation and character
N0-095 Regulate anger before acting consequentially.
N0-096 Regulate greed/covetousness.
N0-097 Regulate compulsive desire.
N0-098 Resist pride/status intoxication.
N0-099 Do not let praise/blame alone govern judgement.
N0-100 Examine one's own motives and conduct.
N0-101 Cultivate patience under difficulty.
N0-102 Develop steadiness under gain/loss.
N0-103 Avoid destructive extremes in discipline, consumption or deprivation.
N0-104 Distinguish ethical discipline from performative austerity.
N0-105 Do not treat every desire as authoritative.
N0-106 Recognise that repeated desire/action can consolidate disposition.

## Development, learning and capability
N0-107 Ethical capability can develop through practice.
N0-108 Match instruction to learner readiness/capacity.
N0-109 Match responsibility to actual capability and conditions.
N0-110 Permit staged learning.
N0-111 Encourage inquiry rather than passive repetition alone.
N0-112 Recognise ignorance as a condition for further learning.
N0-113 Test learning by conduct/application.
N0-114 Preserve the learner's capacity to deliberate.
N0-115 Do not destabilise less-developed agents merely to display superior knowledge.
N0-116 Allow imperfect development to retain value.
N0-117 Reopen a conclusion when new information changes the feasible action set.
N0-118 Distinguish capability from wisdom.

## Emergency, context and conflicting duties
N0-119 Ordinary rules may require modified implementation under genuine emergency.
N0-120 Distinguish necessity from convenience.
N0-121 Limit exceptions to the conditions that justify them.
N0-122 End exceptional permission when exceptional conditions end.
N0-123 Preserve underlying ethical purpose when surface rules conflict.
N0-124 Do not treat emergency as suspension of all restraint.
N0-125 Consider survival where it preserves future capacity for obligation.
N0-126 Compare harms and available alternatives under unavoidable conflict.
N0-127 Preserve later review of emergency decisions.
N0-128 Context can change application without making every principle relative.

## Repair, correction and social continuity
N0-129 Acknowledge error/wrongdoing.
N0-130 Change conduct in response to recognised error.
N0-131 Repair harm where feasible.
N0-132 Permit genuine reform to affect judgement.
N0-133 Preserve social reconstruction after severe conflict.
N0-134 Preserve conditions needed for ordinary cooperative life.
N0-135 Prevent repeated exceptions from eroding shared restraints.
N0-136 Maintain mechanisms by which corrective information reaches decision-makers.
