# Unified Paths of Light — E6
## E6-01 — Hindu Holdout Experiment Charter

## Status
**PROSPECTIVE HOLDOUT TEST OPEN.**

E6 is not another fitting corpus. The shared X1–X7 kernel was frozen from E3, E4 and E5 before E6 begins.

## Primary question
Does an independently derived Hindu ethical architecture substantively recover the seven frozen functional regions without using them to guide extraction or compression?

## Rules
1. X1–X7 are immutable during E6.
2. E6 extraction must not search for X roots.
3. E6 may produce any number, hierarchy or wording of principles.
4. A predicted root may be Recovered, Ambiguous or Absent.
5. Absence must not trigger citation hunting or corpus expansion.
6. E6 findings cannot retrospectively redefine success.
7. Hindu theological/metaphysical material is retained only where needed to understand a behavioural proposition; theology is not translated automatically into generic ethics.
8. Dharma must not be flattened into a modern moral synonym: role, stage, duty, cosmic order, law and ethical conduct must be distinguished where relevant.
9. Historical hierarchy, caste/varna, gender and role-specific rules are recorded rather than silently universalised.
10. Provenance quality is built into extraction from the first record.
