## Recurrent internal families
Without external comparison, the Upaniṣadic block repeatedly supports:
- truthfulness;
- recognition of ignorance and limits of knowledge;
- inquiry and correction;
- disciplined learning;
- restraint of desire/impulse;
- generosity;
- compassion/non-hatred;
- conduct over outward status or ritual form;
- responsible action rather than empty withdrawal;
- non-covetous relation to possessions;
- staged development and learner-sensitive instruction;
- humility regarding personal power/authorship.

## Important negative result
Māṇḍūkya produced essentially no independent social-behavioural ethic at the chosen resolution. It has not been forced to yield one.

This is methodologically useful: corpus membership does not guarantee ethical extraction yield.
