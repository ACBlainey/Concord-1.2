# Bhagavad Gītā block audit

## Coverage
All 18 chapters have now been reviewed under the E6 coding rules.

**87 occurrence records** are retained in this first-pass ledger.

This is an occurrence count, not a count of unique principles.

## Strong recurrent internal families
Without external comparison, the Gītā repeatedly develops:
- action without possessive fixation on reward;
- consequence-sensitive judgement;
- role/duty and conflicts of obligation;
- self-regulation of desire, anger, greed and reactive attachment;
- disciplined development of judgement;
- compassion/non-injury/friendliness;
- truthful and beneficial communication;
- generosity and reciprocal contribution;
- conduct over status/display;
- guidance appropriate to capability;
- learning, inquiry and transmission;
- non-covetous/resource restraint;
- recognition of wider social consequences.

## Tensions preserved
The extraction does not harmonise:
- non-injury with battlefield duty;
- broad equal regard with historically differentiated social roles;
- personal deliberation with scriptural/teacher authority;
- detachment from outcomes with consequence-sensitive action.

These tensions are evidence and must survive into normalisation.
