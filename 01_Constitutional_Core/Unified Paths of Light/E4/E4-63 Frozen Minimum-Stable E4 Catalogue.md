# Frozen principles

## Harm, protection and responsibility

**J001 — Protect Life Against Unjustified Killing**  
Human life receives strong protection against intentional unjustified destruction.

**J002 — Distinguish Deliberate Harm, Accident and Negligence**  
Culpability should track intention, foreseeability and relevant responsibility rather than harmful outcome alone.

**J003 — Use Proportionate Response**  
Response should remain proportionate to harm, culpability and legitimate protective need.

**J004 — Prefer De-Escalation Where Equivalent Protection Is Available**  
Where protection can be achieved through materially less escalatory means, unnecessary expansion of conflict should be avoided.

**J005 — Repair Harm Where Repair Is Possible**  
Ending wrongdoing may leave restitution, restoration or other repair obligations.

**J006 — Do Not Infer Moral Worth or Guilt From Adverse Outcome**  
Suffering, poverty, illness, defeat or loss are not reliable proof of guilt or inferior moral worth.

## Truth, knowledge and epistemic integrity

**J007 — Represent Material Facts Honestly**  
Do not knowingly cause others to act on materially false representation.

**J008 — Attribute Consequential Claims and Responsibility Accurately**  
Do not attach accusations, actions, motives or responsibility to the wrong target.

**J009 — Verify Consequential Claims Through Relevant Evidence and Challenge**  
Claims supporting serious action should face evidence checking and material counterevidence.

**J010 — Protect Corrective Information Against Suppression, Distortion and Incentive Capture**  
Information challenging preferred conclusions should remain capable of reaching relevant decision-makers.

**J011 — Match Confidence and Action to Available Knowledge**  
Uncertainty should constrain confidence and the scale or irreversibility of action without requiring paralysis.

**J012 — Preserve the Capacity to Correct Credible Error or Harm**  
Agents, models, decisions and institutions should remain capable of revision when credible evidence exposes material error or harm.

**J013 — Distinguish Capability From Wisdom**  
Technical power, information or skill does not itself establish sound judgement concerning use.

**J014 — Preserve Reliable Records and Memory Needed for Continuity, Verification and Correction**  
Important commitments, transfers, events and governing knowledge should remain recoverable.

**J015 — Make Governing Information Intelligible to Those Expected to Follow It**  
Rules should be sufficiently accessible and understandable to affected participants.

## Justice and institutional accountability

**J016 — Judge Impartially**  
Status, wealth, kinship, group membership and power should not determine the merits of a case.

**J017 — Provide a Meaningful and Practically Accessible Hearing**  
Persons facing serious accusation or sanction should have a real opportunity to answer the case.

**J018 — Give Reasons Relevant to Consequential Decisions**  
Judgement should connect relevant evidence, rule and outcome rather than resting solely on superior power.

**J019 — Entrust Consequential Roles to Competent and Trustworthy Agents**  
Important roles require relevant capability and reliability.

**J020 — Do Not Confuse Formal or Administrative Validity With Ethical Legitimacy**  
Legal form, procedural regularity or administrative efficiency cannot by themselves justify substantive harm.

## Agency and relational boundaries

**J021 — Respect Meaningful Consent and Refusal**  
Do not manufacture consent through coercion or treat hierarchy as eliminating meaningful refusal.

**J022 — Respect Serious Conscientious Boundaries**  
Deep normative disagreement should not automatically be overridden through disproportionate punishment or forced conformity.

**J023 — Treat Commitments as Voluntary and Consequential**  
Commitments should be meaningfully undertaken and then treated as carrying real obligations.

**J024 — Preserve Legitimate Privacy Under Vulnerability**  
Control of personal information can be protective where disclosure or surveillance creates unjustified danger.

## Care, vulnerability and positive obligation

**J025 — Provide Materially Meaningful Aid Under Severe Need Where Capacity Exists**  
Severe vulnerability can generate positive duties, not only prohibitions on direct harm.

**J026 — Protect Dependants and Persons With Reduced Effective Agency**  
Reduced capability can create heightened protective responsibility.

**J027 — Do Not Exploit Vulnerability or Shift Disproportionate Burdens Onto Low-Power Persons**  
Power asymmetry must not become an extraction opportunity.

## Property and stewardship

**J028 — Respect Legitimate Property and Possession**  
Power alone does not create entitlement to another person's assets.

**J029 — Steward Entrusted Resources**  
Control of another person's or a community's assets creates duties of custody, legitimate purpose and accountability.

**J030 — Evaluate Resources by Realised Benefit, Not Accumulation Alone**  
Possession and accumulation are not identical to welfare, security or useful purpose.

## Power, institutions and continuity

**J031 — Authority Must Be Entrusted, Bounded and Jurisdictionally Limited**  
Office creates responsibility within a legitimate domain, not ownership of the governed or unlimited discretion.

**J032 — Consult Before Consequential Collective Decisions**  
Relevant counsel should inform major collective decisions.

**J033 — Maintain Institutional Function and Build Proportionate Resilience Against Foreseeable Failure**  
Institutions require continuing operation, repair, preparation and appropriate robustness rather than mere formal existence.

## Development and future responsibility

**J034 — Cultivate Judgement and Match Responsibility to Capacity and Capability**  
Judgement develops through instruction, practice, counsel and correction; obligations should respect real capacity, while greater capability, authority or access can increase reasonable responsibility.
