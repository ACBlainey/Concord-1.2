## E4-70 — Psalm V4 Candidate and Tension Audit

## Status

**HOLDOUT EXCEPTION AUDIT.**

# 1. V4 candidate: Psalm 133

Psalm 133 positively celebrates people dwelling together in unity.

The question is whether this supplies a **new behaviourally constraining ethical principle** absent from J001–J034.

## Candidate formulation

> Preserve or promote social cohesion.

## Test A — Does the source prescribe a distinct behaviour?

Only weakly.

The Psalm praises a state of communal unity. It does not specify:
- what may be sacrificed to obtain unity;
- whether dissent may be suppressed;
- whether unjust unity is preferable to principled disagreement;
- what behavioural duty applies when cohesion conflicts with truth, justice or conscience.

## Test B — Can the beneficial state be generated by existing principles?

Substantial routes already exist:
- J004 de-escalation;
- J007–J012 epistemic integrity/correction;
- J016 impartiality;
- J021 meaningful consent/refusal;
- J022 conscience;
- J025–J027 care/non-exploitation;
- J031 bounded authority;
- J032 consultation.

These principles can produce cooperative cohesion without making cohesion itself supreme.

## Test C — Would adding a cohesion principle create new risk?

Yes.

An unconstrained “preserve cohesion” principle could justify:
- suppression of dissent;
- forced conformity;
- concealment of conflict;
- exclusion of inconvenient minorities.

Such outcomes would conflict with several frozen principles.

## Resolution

**Psalm 133 is reclassified from V4-CANDIDATE to V2.**

It provides a **positive-value refinement**: ethically structured coexistence can produce beneficial social cohesion.

It does **not** establish an independent behavioural root.

# 2. Tension families

## T-A — Imprecatory retaliation

Examples include Psalms 35, 58, 69, 79, 83, 109, 137, 139 and others.

Some requests envision:
- severe destruction;
- humiliation;
- intergenerational/family-directed punishment;
- retaliatory suffering.

### Assessment
These are genuine textual tensions with J003 proportionality, J004 de-escalation and especially accurate individual responsibility under J008/J016.

They are not catalogue failures because the holdout contains material advocating or expressing a competing response, rather than an ethical distinction missing from the catalogue.

**Classification:** retained canonical tension.

## T-B — Prosperity/retribution generalisation

Psalms 1, 37, 92, 112, 128 and related passages can sound like strong correlations between righteousness and flourishing or wrongdoing and collapse.

Yet Psalms 44, 73 and 88 themselves complicate such a model.

### Assessment
The Psalter contains **internal tension**, not a single uniform outcome doctrine.

J006 is therefore not invalidated by the holdout; several Psalms directly support it.

**Classification:** internal canonical tension / genre-conditioned claim.

## T-C — Collective punishment

Some historical and imprecatory passages extend consequences beyond the immediately responsible agent.

Psalm 109 and Psalm 137 provide especially strong pressure cases.

### Assessment
This conflicts with individualised attribution where taken as human behavioural prescription.

The material is preserved rather than harmonised.

**Classification:** genuine tension, not V4.

## T-D — Enemy classification

Laments often presuppose that opponents are wicked, deceitful or violent from the speaker's perspective.

### Assessment
Because the genre is first-person/communal lament, enemy descriptions cannot automatically satisfy J009 verification.

**Classification:** epistemic limitation of genre.

## T-E — Guaranteed protection

Some trust Psalms use categorical protection language.

Other Psalms document unresolved suffering, defeat and abandonment.

### Assessment
The theological claim is not converted into an empirical human guarantee.

**Classification:** V5 theological claim with internal experiential tension.

# 3. Does any tension expose a missing root?

The audit tested whether the tension families require a principle absent from J001–J034.

No such requirement survives.

The relevant evaluative machinery is already present:
- proportionality;
- de-escalation;
- accurate attribution;
- verification;
- uncertainty calibration;
- impartiality;
- correction;
- bounded authority.

The tensions challenge **application and source consistency**, not catalogue completeness.
