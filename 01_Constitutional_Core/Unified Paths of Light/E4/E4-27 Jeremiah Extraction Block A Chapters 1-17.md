# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-27 — Jeremiah Extraction Block A: Chapters 1–17

## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — no cross-anchor comparison.**

## Source-form note

Jeremiah combines prophetic poetry, public accusation, symbolic action, autobiographical material and political crisis. Ethical propositions are extracted only where behaviour or institutional function is sufficiently explicit. Theological explanations of national catastrophe are retained as theology rather than treated as demonstrated empirical causation.

## Block A observation

Jeremiah 1–17 repeatedly treats institutional failure as an information problem as well as a conduct problem: warnings are dismissed, specialist interpreters can distort governing texts, reassurance can substitute for repair, and legitimacy claims can survive after ethical function has deteriorated.
