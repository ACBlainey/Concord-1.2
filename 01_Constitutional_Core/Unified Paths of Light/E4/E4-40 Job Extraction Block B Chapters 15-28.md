## Block B observation

Job 15–28 contains one of E4's strongest source-level demonstrations of **model correction by counterexample**.

The friends repeatedly apply a simple moral-causal model. Job points to observable cases that do not fit it. The book therefore requires later normalisation to preserve a distinction between:

**a valid ethical norm** and **an invalid inference that every adverse outcome proves violation of that norm.**
