# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-39 — Job Extraction Block A: Chapters 1–14

## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — no cross-anchor comparison. Psalms remain withheld.**

## Source-form note

Job is a dialogical wisdom work. Statements by Job, the friends, the narrator and the divine speeches are not assumed to have identical normative authority. The book deliberately stages competing explanations of suffering; therefore character speech is recorded as argument unless subsequently supported by the narrative frame or broader internal resolution. In particular, suffering is not treated as evidence of guilt merely because a speaker claims that it is.

## Block A observation

Job immediately functions as a powerful **anti-simplification corpus**. Its dialogue repeatedly presents plausible moral rules and then destabilises their mechanical application to a particular sufferer.

The primary source therefore forces E4 to distinguish:

**ethical principle → evidential claim about a person → causal explanation of their suffering.**

Those are not interchangeable.
