## Block B observation

Ezekiel 18 is one of E4's clearest internal statements of individual moral responsibility and correctability: descent does not mechanically transfer guilt, and changed behaviour changes moral assessment.

Ezekiel 22 then moves in the opposite analytical direction—not inherited guilt, but **distributed present responsibility** across interacting institutional roles.
