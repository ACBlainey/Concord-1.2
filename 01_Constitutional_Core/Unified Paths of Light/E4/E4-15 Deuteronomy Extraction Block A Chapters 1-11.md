# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-15 — Deuteronomy Extraction Block A: Chapters 1–11

## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — no cross-anchor comparison.**

## Source-form note

Deuteronomy is framed largely as Moses' retrospective speeches: earlier events are interpreted, prior laws are restated, and new or reformulated rules are introduced. Repetition is retained as evidence of emphasis but does not automatically create a new principle.

## Extracted source occurrences

### E4-DEU-001 — Deuteronomy 1:9–18
**Source form:** retrospective institutional instruction  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Adjudicative burden should be distributed among capable officials rather than concentrated unsustainably in one leader.  
**Provisional cluster:** distributed adjudication.

### E4-DEU-002 — Deuteronomy 1:13
**Source form:** appointment rule  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Public responsibility should be entrusted to persons recognised as wise, discerning and experienced.  
**Provisional cluster:** competence in office.

### E4-DEU-003 — Deuteronomy 1:16–17
**Source form:** judicial instruction  
**Normative status:** explicit

**Normative status:** explicit

### E4-DEU-005 — Deuteronomy 1:19–33

**Source form:** covenantal exhortation

## Block A observation

Deuteronomy 1–11 is highly reflective: it does not merely repeat earlier material but repeatedly interprets memory, prosperity, failure and institutional experience.

Distinctive source-level emphases include accurate transmission of rules, intergenerational preservation of governing knowledge, resistance to equating success with moral superiority, and deliberate memory of former vulnerability.
