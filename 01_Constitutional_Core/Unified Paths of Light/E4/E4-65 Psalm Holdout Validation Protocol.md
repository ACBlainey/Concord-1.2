# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-65 — Psalm Holdout Validation Protocol

## Status

**POST-FREEZE CANONICAL HOLDOUT VALIDATION OPEN.**

The E4 catalogue was frozen at J001–J034 before Psalms was admitted.

# 1. Experimental question

Given a minimum-stable ethical catalogue derived from the rest of the Tanakh:

> **How much of the ethical material independently observable in Psalms is recovered by the frozen E4 catalogue, and what genuinely new or tension-producing material remains?**

This is not a search for confirmation.

# 2. Nature of the holdout

Psalms is a **canonical holdout**, not a perfectly independent cultural corpus.

Limitations include:
- shared Israelite/Jewish cultural and textual environment;
- lexical and theological overlap;
- Psalmic material or close parallels appearing elsewhere;
- Chronicles containing Psalm parallels, which E4 attempted to quarantine where recognised;
- possible common inherited traditions.

Therefore Psalm recovery tests **within-canon robustness and compression**, not independent civilisational convergence.

# 3. Validation classes

Every ethically relevant Psalm source unit encountered after freeze receives one primary class:

### V1 — Exact/Direct Recovery
The behavioural proposition is directly covered by one frozen J principle.

### V2 — Nuance or Refinement
The frozen principle covers the ethical core, while the Psalm contributes a meaningful nuance, boundary condition or implementation detail.

### V3 — Compound Expression
The Psalmic proposition is recovered through the interaction of two or more frozen J principles.

### V4 — Genuine Candidate Addition
The Psalm contains behaviourally meaningful ethical content that cannot be regenerated from J001–J034 without importing an additional premise.

### V5 — Non-Comparable / Context-Bound
The material is primarily theological, liturgical, imprecatory, cosmological, symbolic or otherwise unsuitable for direct behavioural comparison.

### VT — Tension
The Psalm materially conflicts with, limits or complicates a frozen principle or its expected application.

A unit may receive a secondary tension flag where appropriate, but one primary validation class should be assigned.

# 4. Anti-confirmation controls

The following are prohibited:

1. changing J001–J034 after seeing Psalm evidence;
2. defining vague similarity as recovery;
3. forcing theological claims into behavioural categories;
4. treating a V3 compound as a V1 direct match;
5. hiding V4 additions;
6. hiding imprecatory or retaliatory tensions;
7. treating source frequency as multiple principles;
8. claiming cultural independence from within-canon recovery.

# 5. Source-unit rule

The Psalm validation unit is the smallest contextually sufficient passage that yields a distinct behavioural proposition.

Repeated refrains are counted as source recurrence, not new principles.

Genre must be recorded where it affects interpretation:
- hymn;
- lament;
- wisdom;
- royal;
- thanksgiving;
- penitential;
- imprecatory;
- historical;
- pilgrimage/communal;
- mixed.

# 6. Special Psalm controls

## Imprecation
Calls for severe divine or human retaliation are recorded as tension/context rather than silently excluded.

## Divine justice
Theological claims that God rewards, punishes, knows or judges are not automatically converted into human behavioural rules.

## Enemy language
Enemy descriptions are not presumed to establish objective guilt without narrative/evidential context.

## Prosperity
Claims linking righteousness and prosperity are tested against E4's Job/Ecclesiastes-derived outcome cautions rather than harmonised automatically.

## Kingship
Royal language does not automatically establish general authority ethics.

## Ritual
Worship practice is excluded unless a separable behavioural ethic is present.

# 7. Validation workflow

Psalms will be traversed in ordered blocks.

For each ethically relevant unit record:

- Psalm reference;
- source form/genre;
- behavioural proposition;
- primary validation class;
- matching J principle(s), if any;
- nuance/tension notes.

After full traversal:

1. count V1–V5 and VT;
2. identify which J principles receive Psalm support;
3. identify frozen J principles not independently represented in Psalms;
4. inspect all V4 candidate additions;
5. inspect all tensions;
6. distinguish principle recovery from source frequency;
7. report holdout coverage without modifying the frozen catalogue.

# 8. Success and failure conditions

The holdout is informative under every outcome.

High recovery would support the claim that the frozen non-Psalm catalogue captures a substantial portion of ethical structure recurring in Psalms.

Material V4 additions would show that the primary derivation corpus missed ethical distinctions preserved by Psalms.

Material VT tensions would show internal canonical disagreement or contextual limits requiring explicit treatment.

Low recovery would challenge the adequacy or abstraction level of the frozen catalogue.
