# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-02 — Extraction Protocol and Contamination Controls

## 1. Objective

E4 must derive its ethical catalogue from its own anchor corpus rather than rediscovering structures already known from E3, Psalms, the Ten Commandments project or the Concord.

The primary methodological risk is not deliberate manipulation. It is unconscious normalisation toward familiar categories.

This document freezes controls intended to reduce that risk.

## 2. Quarantined comparison material

During primary extraction and normalisation, do not use as target structures:

- the original Psalm-derived catalogue;
- E3 Qur'an-derived Q001–Q089 catalogue;
- E3 A1–A10 higher-order attractors;
- E3↔Psalms crosswalk;
- Blainey's Laws / Concord Ethical Kernel;
- Ten Commandments P4–P8 crosswalk and derivation results;
- Ethical Topology of Civilisation mappings.

These materials may be consulted only after the E4 catalogue has reached its declared freeze point, except where required to administer procedural methodology rather than ethical content.

## 3. Extraction record

Each E4 source occurrence should preserve, where applicable:

**E4 source occurrence ID**  
**Tanakh locator**  
**canonical division**  
**genre/source form**  
**context span**  
**extracted behavioural proposition**  
**brief extraction rationale**  
**translation/semantic note**  
**uncertainty flag**  
**later normalised-principle mapping**

The extraction record should remain recoverable after consolidation.

## 4. Extraction test

A candidate proposition should satisfy:

1. **Behavioural relevance** — it concerns action, restraint, treatment, responsibility, relation or governance of conduct.
2. **Contextual sufficiency** — enough source context is retained to avoid changing the meaning through isolation.
3. **Separability** — where theological or ritual framing exists, a behavioural proposition can be separated without misrepresenting the source.
4. **Non-duplication at occurrence level** — repeated source occurrences may be retained as evidence, but should not be invented as distinct principles merely because they recur.
5. **Interpretive restraint** — the proposition should not require importing later Concord concepts to become ethically meaningful.

## 5. Narrative control

Narratives require particular caution.

An action described in scripture is not automatically endorsed by scripture.

For narrative material classify, where possible:

- explicit prescription;
- explicit approval;
- explicit condemnation;
- consequence-linked evaluation;
- character speech;
- narrator description;
- ethically ambiguous event.

Do not infer a normative principle merely because a character performs an action.

## 6. Legal-material control

Legal rules may contain:

- a general behavioural principle;
- a historically specific implementation;
- ritual/covenantal content;
- several compound principles simultaneously.

Extraction should preserve the rule first. Substrate-neutral normalisation occurs later.

Do not prematurely replace a concrete rule with a broad root principle.

## 7. Prophetic-material control

Prophetic criticism may provide unusually clear evidence concerning the ethical interpretation of earlier institutions and practices.

However, theological claims and behavioural claims must remain distinguishable.

Where a passage criticises conduct, extract the behavioural proposition only where the criticised or required conduct is sufficiently clear.

## 8. Wisdom-material control

Compact sayings may contain:

- direct ethical rules;
- prudential advice;
- empirical generalisations;
- theological claims;
- rhetorical exaggeration.

Prudential success advice is not automatically an ethical principle.

## 9. Normalisation sequence

After source extraction:

**source occurrence → behavioural proposition → provisional normalised principle → consolidation candidate → frozen E4 principle.**

Normalisation should remove unnecessary culture-specific implementation while preserving meaningful behavioural distinctions.

It must not remove a distinction merely because another anchor lacks it.

## 10. Compound-rule protection

The recent Ten Commandments work suggests that some rules may later prove compound.

E4 must not assume this during extraction.

A proposition that appears to combine several ethical dimensions should initially be preserved at the level supported by the source.

Compound decomposition is a downstream analytical test.

## 11. Source hierarchy protection

The M3 compression–decompression hypothesis must not influence extraction.

Do not assume:

- Torah = ethical roots;
- later texts = branches;
- older = more fundamental;
- newer = more domain-specific.

Record hierarchy and chronology independently. Test their relationship to ethical abstraction only after catalogue freeze.

## 12. Lineage-control protection

Because E4 belongs to the Abrahamic cluster, similarities with Christianity and Islam are expected possibilities but not extraction targets.

Do not resolve ambiguity toward a principle merely because a similar principle exists in Psalms or the Qur'an.

Cross-family and within-family convergence are later analytical stages.

## 13. Freeze criteria

The E4 catalogue may be frozen when:

- the predefined derivation corpus has been traversed;
- source occurrences have been recorded;
- extracted propositions have been normalised;
- consolidation has reached a stable catalogue;
- unresolved ambiguities are documented;
- no comparison-derived edits have been introduced.

The freeze should record catalogue size, identifiers and version.

## 14. Post-freeze validation

After freeze:

### Validation A — Psalms holdout
Compare the E4 catalogue with the independently studied Psalm material.

### Validation B — post-biblical Jewish sample
Use a deliberately diverse external sample and classify observations:

**V1 Recovery**  
**V2 Nuance/refinement**  
**V3 Compound expression**  
**V4 Genuine candidate addition**  
**V5 Context-bound/non-comparable**

Neither validation stage silently rewrites the frozen catalogue.

## 15. Later comparisons

Only after validation may E4 be systematically compared with:

- E3 Qur'an catalogue;
- E3 higher-order attractors;
- original Psalm synthesis;
- Ten Commandments findings;
- Concord Ethical Kernel.

## 16. Failure is informative

The experiment is not successful only if it converges.

Potentially important results include:

- ethical roots absent from E4;
- Jewish-specific principles;
- major abstraction differences;
- contradictions;
- catalogue instability;
- holdout failure;
- external V4 additions;
- evidence that the extraction method behaves differently across genres.

All such results must be preserved.

## 17. Frozen pre-extraction rule

> **E4 must discover what its anchor contains before asking whether it resembles anything already known.**

> **Protocol frozen before primary ethical extraction begins.**
