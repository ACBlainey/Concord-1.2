# 1. Frozen attractors

## JA1 — Protection, Proportionality and Repair
**Core function:** protect agents against unjustified injury; distinguish responsibility; constrain response; prefer protective de-escalation where viable; repair harm where possible.  
**Primary children:** J001–J005.

## JA2 — Epistemic Integrity and Correctability
**Core function:** maintain a reliable, revisable relationship between belief, information, decision and reality.  
**Primary children:** J006–J015.

## JA3 — Fairness, Accountability and Legitimate Judgement
**Core function:** constrain consequential judgement through impartiality, meaningful hearing, relevant reasons, trustworthy adjudication and substantive legitimacy.  
**Primary children:** J016–J020.

## JA4 — Voluntary Agency, Conscience and Personal Boundaries
**Core function:** protect meaningful choice, refusal, conscience, voluntary commitment and legitimate privacy.  
**Primary children:** J021–J024.

## JA5 — Care Under Need and Power Asymmetry
**Core function:** create positive duties toward severe need/reduced agency while preventing exploitation and disproportionate burden shifting.  
**Primary children:** J025–J027.

## JA6 — Responsible Possession and Resource Use
**Core function:** legitimate possession and stewardship should direct resources toward responsible realised benefit rather than accumulation alone.  
**Primary children:** J028–J030.

## JA7 — Bounded Collective Power and Institutional Continuity
**Core function:** collective authority must be entrusted and limited, consult affected agents, and preserve legitimate institutional function against foreseeable failure.  
**Primary children:** J031–J033.

## JA8 — Development of Judgement and Capability-Proportionate Responsibility
**Core function:** cultivate judgement and calibrate burden, role and responsibility to actual capability and developmental condition.  
**Primary child:** J034, with cross-support from J011–J013, J026 and J033.

# 3. Irreducibility audit

### JA1 ≠ JA3
Protection does not guarantee fair judgement.

### JA2 ≠ JA3
Accurate knowledge does not guarantee impartial justice.

### JA3 ≠ JA7
Fair adjudication is not identical to legitimate collective power.

### JA4 ≠ JA5
Agency cannot be reduced to welfare; welfare cannot be reduced to consent.

### JA5 ≠ JA1
Positive aid exceeds a prohibition on causing harm.

### JA6 ≠ JA7
Resource stewardship is not equivalent to governance.

### JA8 ≠ JA2
Development includes responsibility calibration beyond epistemic formation.

### JA8 ≠ JA4
Developmental capability and mature voluntary agency are related but not identical.
