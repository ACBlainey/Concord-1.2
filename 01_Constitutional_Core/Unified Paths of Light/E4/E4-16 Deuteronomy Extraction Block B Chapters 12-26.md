## Block B observation

Deuteronomy 12–26 is one of the densest institutional-ethical sections encountered so far. It combines justice administration, limits on rulers, evidential thresholds, debt release, poverty relief, labour protection, property boundaries, refuge, collateral limits and welfare allocation.

It simultaneously contains some of the Torah's strongest ethical tensions: religious coercion, destruction commands, captive-woman law, severe family sanctions, hereditary exclusions and explicit in-group/out-group asymmetries.

Both sides remain in the E4 evidence base.
