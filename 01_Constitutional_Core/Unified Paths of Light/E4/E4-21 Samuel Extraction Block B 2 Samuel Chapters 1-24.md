## Extracted source occurrences

### E4-SAM-044 — 2 Samuel 1:1–16
**Source form:** report of Saul's death / adjudication  
**Normative status:** conflict narrative with contested self-report  
**Proposition:** **AMBIGUITY FLAG.** A consequential self-report can expose the reporter to judgement, but the episode is too context-bound to establish a general evidential standard from confession alone.

### E4-SAM-045 — 2 Samuel 1:17–27
**Source form:** lament  
**Normative status:** poetic/relational  
**Proposition:** A political or military opponent's death need not erase recognition of relationship, loss or human worth.  
**Provisional cluster:** grief / humanisation beyond conflict.

### E4-SAM-046 — 2 Samuel 2–4
**Source form:** succession/civil conflict narratives  
**Normative status:** military/political  
**Proposition:** **MOSTLY QUARANTINED.** Rival claims, assassinations and retaliatory violence are preserved without generalising political violence.

### E4-SAM-047 — 2 Samuel 3:26–39
**Source form:** Joab kills Abner  
**Normative status:** unauthorised retaliatory killing  
**Proposition:** A subordinate's private vengeance can undermine a leader's public settlement and create liability beyond the formal policy of the institution.  
**Provisional cluster:** private retaliation / institutional responsibility.

### E4-SAM-048 — 2 Samuel 4:5–12
**Source form:** assassination and attempted reward  
**Normative status:** explicit rejection  
**Proposition:** A ruler need not reward violence committed ostensibly for the ruler's benefit when the act itself violates the ruler's stated standard.  
**Provisional cluster:** refusal to benefit from wrongful violence.

### E4-SAM-049 — 2 Samuel 5–6
**Source form:** consolidation of rule / ark narrative

**Proposition:** **NO DISTINCT GENERAL EXTRACTION.**

### E4-SAM-051 — 2 Samuel 8:15

### E4-SAM-053 — 2 Samuel 10

### E4-SAM-057 — 2 Samuel 12:5–13
