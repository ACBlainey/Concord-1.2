# 3. What closure means

Closure means:

1. every admitted primary corpus division has been traversed;
2. source-level ethical material has been recorded before cross-anchor comparison;
3. negative, ambiguous and tension material has been preserved rather than normalised away;
4. Psalms has remained outside the derivation corpus;
5. later frameworks have remained quarantined from extraction.

Closure does **not** mean:
- every verse has become an ethical proposition;
- every extracted occurrence will survive normalisation;
- the Tanakh is internally uniform;
- the catalogue is already minimal;
- E4 has demonstrated universality;
- the resulting principles have been compared with the Concord.

# 7. Important methodological findings from extraction

## 7.1 Source frequency is not principle multiplicity

A principle may recur hundreds of times without becoming hundreds of principles. Conversely, a rare source occurrence may preserve a meaningful distinction that should not be lost.

## 7.2 Speaker and genre matter

Job, prophetic literature, lament, court narrative and apocalyptic texts make it impossible to treat every sentence as equivalent normative speech.

## 7.3 Procedure and substance are separable

E4 contains sophisticated procedure attached both to ethically attractive and ethically troubling objectives. Good procedure does not prove a good objective.

## 7.4 Lawfulness and ethical legitimacy are separable

Esther, Daniel, Kings and other texts contain formally valid rules capable of producing grave harm.

## 7.5 Ethical norm and causal inference are separable

Job and Ecclesiastes particularly challenge the inference:

**adverse outcome = proof of guilt.**

## 7.6 Institutional form and ethical function are separable

Prophetic material repeatedly depicts institutions retaining recognisable form while their ethical function is degraded through exploitation, bribery, falsehood or suppression of correction.

## 7.7 Protection and agency can coexist

Ruth, legal materials and several narrative cases show vulnerable persons receiving protection without necessarily losing all decision-making agency.

## 7.8 Institutional memory has ethical significance

Records, public readings, archives, precedent, historical confession and rediscovered governing texts repeatedly function as mechanisms for continuity and correction.

## 7.9 Corrigibility emerges without being pre-imposed

Several narratives expose the danger of decisions or rules that cannot be revised after error or manipulation becomes visible.

This observation remains source-derived and is not yet mapped to Concord recursive oversight.

But the E4 catalogue must be derived from E4's own records and wording rather than copied into the architecture of E3.

## Normalisation controls

For every proposed merge ask:

1. Are these occurrences behaviourally equivalent?
2. Would merging them erase a meaningful difference in protected interest, duty, agency, procedure or failure mode?
3. Is one merely a domain-specific implementation of the other?
4. Are they compound expressions of more basic principles?
5. Is recurrence evidence of emphasis rather than multiplicity?
6. Does the proposed principle survive across source forms?
7. Is a tension record being accidentally converted into a positive rule?

No target principle count is set in advance.
