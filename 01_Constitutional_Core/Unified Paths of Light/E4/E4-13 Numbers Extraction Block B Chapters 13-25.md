# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-13 — Numbers Extraction Block B: Chapters 13–25

## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — no cross-anchor comparison.**

## Extracted source occurrences

### E4-NUM-019 — Numbers 13:25–33
**Source form:** reconnaissance report  
**Normative status:** contested report  
**Proposition:** Decision-making under threat can be distorted when factual observation, inference and fear-amplifying interpretation are not clearly distinguished.  
**Notes:** The scouts agree on some observations but differ sharply in interpretation and recommendation.  
**Provisional cluster:** evidence / interpretation / fear.

### E4-NUM-020 — Numbers 14:1–10
**Source form:** collective crisis narrative  
**Normative status:** contested decision  
**Proposition:** Collective fear and majority agreement do not by themselves establish that a judgement is sound.  
**Provisional cluster:** group pressure / decision quality.

### E4-NUM-021 — Numbers 14:11–25
**Source form:** intercession after collective failure  
**Normative status:** theological dialogue  
**Proposition:** Severe collective judgement can be challenged by appeal to prior commitments, reputation, mercy and proportional response.  
**Provisional cluster:** intercession / restraint.

### E4-NUM-022 — Numbers 14:26–45
**Source form:** consequence and failed reversal narrative  
**Normative status:** theological/conflict-specific  
**Proposition:** **MOSTLY QUARANTINED.** The episode distinguishes acknowledgement from reckless attempted reversal, but its military/theological form resists clean generalisation.

### E4-NUM-023 — Numbers 15:22–31
**Source form:** wrongdoing law  
**Normative status:** explicit distinction  
**Proposition:** Unintentional wrongdoing and deliberate defiance are treated as different categories of culpability.  
**Provisional cluster:** intent / culpability.

### E4-NUM-024 — Numbers 15:29
**Source form:** equal-law provision  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Native and resident outsider are subject to the same stated rule for unintentional wrongdoing within the covered jurisdiction.  
**Provisional cluster:** equal application.

### E4-NUM-025 — Numbers 15:32–36

**Proposition:** **QUARANTINED.** The capital sanction is not converted into a timeless proportionality rule.

### E4-NUM-026 — Numbers 16

### E4-NUM-030 — Numbers 19

## Block B observation
Numbers 13–25 adds important source-level material concerning evidence under fear, majority pressure, differentiated culpability, leadership accountability, negotiated territorial access and independence from corrupting inducement.

The repeated quarantine of rebellion, conquest and punitive narratives is deliberate.
