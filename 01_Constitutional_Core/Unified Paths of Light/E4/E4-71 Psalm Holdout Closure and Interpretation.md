# 3. Meaning of the result

The strongest defensible statement is:

> **Within the defined E4 method, a 34-principle ethical catalogue derived from the non-Psalm Tanakh was sufficient to represent every behaviourally comparable ethical proposition identified during a subsequent full-Psalter holdout traversal, either directly, as a refinement, or as a compound expression.**

This is evidence of **within-canon structural robustness**.

# 4. Why the result matters

Psalms were not merely sampled after the fact. They were intentionally excluded from derivation because:
- they were central to the earlier Unified Paths work;
- their inclusion before freeze could contaminate E4;
- withholding them created a genuine test against unseen canonical material.

The result therefore gives stronger evidence than simply deriving principles from the complete Tanakh and then observing that the Tanakh contains them.

# 5. Why the result must not be overstated

Psalms and the derivation corpus are not culturally independent.

They share:
- language;
- traditions;
- historical environment;
- theological concepts;
- intertextual relationships.

Therefore the result does not independently establish natural law or cross-civilisational universality.

It tests **generalisation inside a related corpus**.

# 6. Tensions are part of the result

The holdout did not become “100% agreement.”

Important tensions remain around:
- imprecatory retaliation;
- family/collective punishment;
- enemy attribution;
- prosperity/retribution;
- categorical protection claims.

The recovery statistic concerns whether comparable **ethical distinctions** require new principles.

A text can be representable by a catalogue while disagreeing with how one or more principles should be weighted or applied.

That distinction is central.

# 7. A deeper methodological finding

The holdout supports a distinction between:

### Catalogue completeness
Does the frozen set contain the ethical dimensions needed to describe the new material?

and

### Source agreement
Does the new material apply those dimensions in the same way?

Psalms perform strongly on the first while containing real disagreement/tension on the second.

This distinction should be retained in later E3/E4 and natural-law work.

This is compatible with a **fractal or combinatorial ethical architecture** in which concrete rules are generated from intersections among a smaller set of constraints.

It does not yet prove that model, but it makes it experimentally useful.
