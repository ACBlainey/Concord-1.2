## Status

**BLIND NORMALISATION — MINIMUM-STABILITY TEST.**

Input: E4-C01–E4-C65.

# 1. Test

For each candidate, ask:

> If this principle is removed or merged upward, can its ethically material behavioural constraint be regenerated from the remaining catalogue without importing an additional premise?

- **I — irreducible at this resolution**
- **D — derivable/compound**
- **S — subordinate implementation worth retaining in notes but not as an independent principle**

# 3. Surviving minimum-stability set

Removing D and S candidates leaves **37 principles** that currently survive the irreducibility test.

This is not yet the frozen catalogue. A final adversarial merge test is required because some remaining principles may still share a deeper parent.

# 4. Why compression now slows sharply

At 65, many candidates were domain implementations. At 37, attempted merges increasingly require importing a new premise.

Examples:

- **Truth ≠ Verification:** sincerity does not establish correctness.
- **Non-Harm ≠ Positive Aid:** refraining from injury does not feed a starving person.
- **Consent ≠ Dignity:** permitted treatment can still be degrading.
- **Justice ≠ Corrigibility:** a fair process can still reach an erroneous result.
- **Stewardship ≠ Property:** legitimate possession does not imply entrustment, and entrustment need not imply ownership.
- **Capacity-sensitive burden ≠ Development:** one constrains obligation; the other concerns cultivation of judgement.
- **Records ≠ Transparency:** preserved information may remain inaccessible; accessible information may not be durably preserved.

These are genuine information boundaries, not wording preferences.

# 5. Status of subordinate rules

Subordinate rules are not discarded from E4 evidence. They will be retained as **derived applications** beneath the frozen principles.

This permits later fractal analysis:

**principle → domain condition → derived rule**

without falsely counting every branch as a separate root.
