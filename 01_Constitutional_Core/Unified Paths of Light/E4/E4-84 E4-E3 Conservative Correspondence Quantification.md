# 1. Why two denominators are required

The catalogues were frozen at different resolutions:

- E4: 34 minimum-stable principles;

A single “percent same” number would therefore be misleading.

Two questions must be kept separate:

1. How many E4 roots have strong E3 representation?
2. How many E3 principles can be reconstructed from E4 roots/compounds?

# 2. Conservative J→Q scoring rule

For quantification only:

- **Strong correspondence:** X1, X1/X2, X2, or clearly bounded X2/X3.
- **Compound correspondence:** X3 where all required content is visibly present.
- **Partial/residual pressure:** any classification containing X4 unless a later audit resolves it.
- **Residual:** X5.
- **Tension:** XT.

This deliberately penalises ambiguous matches.
