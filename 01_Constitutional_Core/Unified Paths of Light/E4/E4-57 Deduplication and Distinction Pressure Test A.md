## E4-57 — Deduplication and Distinction Pressure Test A

## Status

**BLIND NORMALISATION — PRESSURE TEST. NOT FROZEN.**

Input: P-E4-001 through P-E4-054 from E4-55/E4-56.  
Psalms and all external comparison frameworks remain withheld.

# Method

Each candidate is tested for:
- exact or near duplication;
- parent/implementation relation;
- compound structure;
- source-genre artefact;
- irreducible behavioural distinction.

A merge is accepted only where the retained formulation can regenerate the ethically material content of both candidates.
