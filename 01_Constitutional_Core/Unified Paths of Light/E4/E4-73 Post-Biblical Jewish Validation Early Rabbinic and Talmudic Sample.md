## Emerging developmental observation

Compared with the Tanakh/Psalms, rabbinic material often shifts ethical resolution from:

**principle or narrative → case**

toward:

**case conflict → distinction → priority → procedure.**

This may represent ethical **decompression into jurisprudential machinery**, rather than creation of wholly new ethical roots.

That hypothesis remains provisional until later strata are sampled.
