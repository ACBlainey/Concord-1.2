# 2. Experimental question

> When two anchor-first ethical derivations are independently frozen at different resolutions, what functional structure is shared, what remains anchor-specific, and what differences are explained by compression level rather than ethical content?

# 3. Critical asymmetry

E3 and E4 are not numerically equivalent objects.

E3 froze at 89 principles after normalisation.

E4 underwent additional minimum-stability pressure to 34 principles.

Therefore a one-to-one count would be methodologically invalid.

Expected valid relations include:

- one J ↔ one Q;
- one J ↔ several Q;
- several J ↔ one Q;
- compound correspondence;
- partial correspondence;
- genuine residual.

# 4. Comparison classes

### X1 — Direct functional correspondence
Substantially the same protected interest or behavioural constraint.

### X2 — Parent/child correspondence
One catalogue expresses a broader root while the other preserves a more specific branch.

### X3 — Compound correspondence
A principle in one catalogue is reconstructed through multiple principles in the other.

### X4 — Partial overlap
Meaningful shared content exists, but each retains non-trivial residual.

### X5 — Residual
No adequate functional counterpart without importing an unstated bridge premise.

### XT — Tension
The catalogues contain materially conflicting normative direction.

# 5. Blindness after unblinding

The catalogues are now visible to each other, but comparison must remain non-optimising.

Forbidden:
- renaming J or Q principles;
- merging principles after seeing correspondence;
- promoting weak resemblance to direct match;
- using E3 attractors to rescue a failed catalogue-level match during first pass;
- using Blainey's Laws as an intermediary;
- treating shared Abrahamic lineage as independent cultural convergence.

# 6. Lineage dependence

Judaism and Islam are distinct traditions but historically/genealogically related within the Abrahamic family.

Therefore E4↔E3 convergence is stronger evidence than within-canon holdout recovery, but weaker evidence of independence than convergence across unrelated tradition families.

The correct label is:

**cross-anchor, cross-tradition, within-family convergence.**

# 10. Falsification conditions

The convergence hypothesis is weakened if:
- many J principles have no plausible Q counterpart;
- many Q principles require concepts absent from E4;
- matches depend mainly on vague moral vocabulary;
- correspondence appears only after using E3 attractors or Concord as bridges;
- tensions are systematically hidden.
