# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## Judaism / Tanakh Anchor-Reversal Experiment

## Status

**ACTIVE — experimental design frozen before ethical extraction.**

## Purpose

E4 is the next anchor-reversal experiment in the Unified Paths comparative ethics programme.

Its primary purpose is to derive an ethical catalogue independently from a Jewish scriptural anchor and then test its relationship to previously derived catalogues only after the E4 catalogue has been frozen.

E4 also provides the first practical test of the expanded methodology developed after E3.

## Primary derivation corpus

The primary E4 derivation corpus is:

> **The Tanakh excluding Psalms.**

Psalms are deliberately withheld because they formed the original Psalm-first ethical extraction and therefore cannot function as uncontaminated derivation material for E4.

The resulting experiment is not claimed to exhaust “the ethics of Judaism.” It derives the ethical structure recoverable from the declared biblical anchor corpus under the Unified Paths method.

## Experimental sequence

**Tanakh minus Psalms → ESU extraction → proposition normalisation → consolidation → frozen E4 catalogue → Psalms holdout validation → post-biblical external validation → cross-anchor comparison.**

No E3, Psalm, Decalogue, higher-order attractor or Concord Ethical Kernel comparison is permitted during the independent derivation phase.

## Secondary research opportunities

After the E4 catalogue is frozen, the dataset may support tests concerning:

- ethical compression and decompression across textual strata;
- source hierarchy and ethical abstraction;
- within-Abrahamic convergence;
- lineage dependence;
- fractal ethical structure;
- compound ethical rules.

These are downstream analyses and must not influence extraction.

## Methodology dependencies

E4 is governed by the methodology folder, especially:

- M1 — Anchor-Reversal Comparative Ethics Protocol;
- M2 — Large and Distributed Corpus Sampling Plan;
- M3 — Source Hierarchy and Ethical Compression–Decompression Hypothesis;
- M4 — Lineage Dependence and Cross-Family Convergence.

## Evidential limitation

Judaism, Christianity and Islam form a historically related Abrahamic cluster. Agreement among their anchor experiments is therefore evidence of within-family convergence, not three fully independent discoveries.

The stronger tests of lineage-independent convergence will occur in subsequent non-Abrahamic anchor experiments.

> **E4 design begins from independence of derivation, not expectation of convergence.**
