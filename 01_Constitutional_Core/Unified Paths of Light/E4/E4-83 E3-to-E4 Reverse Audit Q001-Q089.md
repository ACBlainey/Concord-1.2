- a direct root;
- parent/child relation;
- compound construction;
- or, in a small number of cases, partial correspondence.

This is not the same as saying the catalogues are identical.

E3 frequently preserves concrete branches that E4 compressed into broader roots.

Examples:
- Q026/Q027 finance and debt → J025/J027/J028;
- Q043–Q048 family/intimate relations → J021/J023/J026/J027;
- Q059/Q060 speech domains → J004/J007/J008/J027;
- Q071 safe passage → J001/J003/J004;
- Q081 impaired judgement → J021/J026/J027.

These are not yet declared genuine residuals. Each may be a branch/compound whose explicitness was lost during E4 minimum-stability compression.
