## Methodological observation

The Twelve provide a useful internal replication because similar ethical structures recur across many short prophetic corpora rather than depending on one large author's vocabulary.

Particularly recurrent is the sequence:

**advantage or authority → opportunity to exploit vulnerability → extraction or distortion → institutional/social harm.**

The inverse also recurs:

**warning → reception → behavioural change → altered response.**

Neither sequence has yet been compared with E3, Psalms, the Concord Ethical Kernel or later Concord architecture.
