# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-33 — The Twelve Extraction Block A: Hosea, Joel, Amos and Obadiah

## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — no cross-anchor comparison.**

## Source-form note

The Twelve are treated as distinct prophetic books within a single Jewish canonical collection. Their poetry, symbolic actions and judgement oracles require the same quarantine controls used for Isaiah, Jeremiah and Ezekiel. Ethical extraction privileges explicit behavioural and institutional content over theological prediction.

# Hosea

### E4-TWL-001 — Hosea 1–3
**Source form:** marriage/family symbolism  
**Normative status:** theological, gendered symbolic action  
**Proposition:** **MAJOR QUARANTINE.** The symbolic marriage narrative is not used to infer general sexual, marital or gender ethics.

### E4-TWL-002 — Hosea 4:1–2
**Source form:** social indictment  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Social breakdown is associated with absence of truthfulness and fidelity together with killing, stealing, deception and escalating violence.  
**Provisional cluster:** truth / trust / violence / property.

### E4-TWL-003 — Hosea 4:4–9
**Source form:** priestly/knowledge critique  
**Normative status:** theological with role responsibility  
**Proposition:** Persons entrusted with preserving and teaching normative knowledge can contribute to wider failure when they neglect or exploit that role.  
**Provisional cluster:** knowledge stewardship / office responsibility.

### E4-TWL-004 — Hosea 4:11
**Source form:** impaired judgement statement  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Intoxicating excess can impair judgement.  
**Provisional cluster:** self-regulation / cognition.

### E4-TWL-005 — Hosea 5–7

# Amos

### E4-TWL-019 — Amos 1–2
**Source form:** oracles against nations and Israel  
**Normative status:** theological with explicit wrongdoing catalogues  
**Proposition:** **PARTLY EXTRACTABLE.** Specific wrongs are separated below; divine punishment is not universalised.

### E4-TWL-020 — Amos 1:3–2:3
**Source form:** international wrongdoing catalogue  
**Normative status:** explicit accusations  
**Proposition:** Extreme wartime cruelty, population trafficking, treaty betrayal, pursuit of displaced persons and desecration of the dead are condemned across group boundaries.  
**Provisional cluster:** conflict restraint / trafficking / agreement integrity.

### E4-TWL-021 — Amos 2:6–8
**Source form:** social injustice accusation  
**Normative status:** explicit

# Obadiah

**Proposition:** **MOSTLY QUARANTINED.**

## Block A observation

Across four very different books, a recurring distinction appears between possessing advantage and exploiting vulnerability. Commercial, military, institutional and disaster contexts all generate versions of the same source-level problem without yet requiring them to be normalised into one principle.
