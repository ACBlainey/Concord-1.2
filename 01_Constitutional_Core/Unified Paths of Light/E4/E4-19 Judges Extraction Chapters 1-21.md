## E4-19 — Judges Extraction: Chapters 1–21

## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — no cross-anchor comparison.**

## Source-form note

Judges is unusually important methodologically because much of its ethical information is conveyed through deterioration, violence, failed leadership and destructive social consequences rather than through exemplary rules.

The repeated description that Israel lacked a king is not treated automatically as endorsement of monarchy. Narrative diagnosis and institutional prescription are kept separate.

## Methodological observation

Judges provides strong evidence that an ethical corpus can communicate through **anti-models**.

The recurrent structure is often:

**weak coordination / captured authority / private grievance → coercion → retaliation → factional escalation → attempted repair → additional harm.**

This should not be compressed prematurely into a preferred theory of governance. The important source-level fact is that failed institutions and failed remediation themselves carry ethical information.
