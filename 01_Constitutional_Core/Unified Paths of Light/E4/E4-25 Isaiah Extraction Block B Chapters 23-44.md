**Source form:** Tyre oracle  
**Normative status:** geopolitical/economic/theological

### E4-ISA-038 — Isaiah 24–27
**Source form:** universal judgement/restoration poetry  
**Normative status:** theological/apocalyptic  
**Proposition:** **MOSTLY QUARANTINED.** Broad justice and peace ideals are retained as recurrence, not multiplied into new rules.

### E4-ISA-039 — Isaiah 26:7–10
**Source form:** justice reflection  
**Normative status:** poetic/theological

**Provisional cluster:** impaired judgement / entrusted role.

## Block B observation

Isaiah 23–44 continues a strong epistemic thread alongside justice: impairment, concealment, pressure for reassuring messages, propaganda, claimed predictive authority and the need to discriminate between assertion and evidence.

These observations arise from Isaiah's own recurring material and remain unconnected to the frozen E3 catalogue or Concord.
