# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-24 — Isaiah Extraction Block A: Chapters 1–22

## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — no cross-anchor comparison.**

## Source-form note

Isaiah shifts E4 into dense prophetic poetry, accusation, warning and political critique. Ethical Source Units therefore often span poetic parallelism rather than isolated verses. Theological judgement is not automatically converted into empirical causation.

## Extracted source occurrences

### E4-ISA-001 — Isaiah 1:10–17
**Source form:** prophetic critique of worship  
**Normative status:** explicit ethical priority  
**Proposition:** Ritual performance does not compensate for persistent wrongdoing; reform requires ceasing harm, learning good, seeking justice and defending vulnerable persons.  
**Provisional cluster:** conduct over ritual / justice / vulnerable protection.

### E4-ISA-002 — Isaiah 1:17
**Source form:** positive ethical exhortation  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Justice includes active intervention for oppressed and poorly protected persons rather than merely abstaining from direct harm.  
**Provisional cluster:** positive justice / advocacy.

### E4-ISA-003 — Isaiah 1:21–23
**Source form:** institutional corruption accusation  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Public institutions become corrupt when officials pursue illicit gain, accept inducements and neglect claims of vulnerable people.  
**Provisional cluster:** bribery / institutional capture / neglected justice.

### E4-ISA-004 — Isaiah 1:24–31
**Source form:** judgement/purification oracle  
**Normative status:** theological  
**Proposition:** **PARTLY QUARANTINED.** Removal of corrupt actors is linked to restoration, but divine judgement is not converted into a general purge doctrine.

### E4-ISA-005 — Isaiah 2:2–4
**Source form:** future peace oracle  
**Normative status:** normative vision  
**Proposition:** A desirable order is represented as one in which disputes are adjudicated and resources devoted to warfare can be redirected toward productive life.  
**Provisional cluster:** adjudication / peace / productive redirection.

### E4-ISA-006 — Isaiah 2:6–22

## Block A observation

Isaiah 1–22 strongly distinguishes institutional form from ethical function. Worship, law, office, wealth and military preparation can all remain formally present while being ethically hollowed out by violence, bribery, exploitation, semantic inversion or status-seeking.

This is recorded as an Isaiah-internal observation only.
