# Torah-wide primary extraction audit

## 1. Status

The five-book Torah traversal is now complete under the E4 frozen protocol.

This is **not** a Torah ethical catalogue and **not** a final normalisation.

The 367 records are source-occurrence evidence from which later E4 propositions and principles may be consolidated only after the complete Tanakh-minus-Psalms derivation corpus has been traversed.

## 2. Source-form variation observed

### Genesis
Ethical meaning frequently emerges through extended narrative arcs:

**action → consequence → recognition/rebuke → repair.**

### Exodus
The corpus increasingly shifts toward:

**rule → circumstance → liability/remedy.**

### Leviticus
Dense ritual material coexists with concentrated ethical legislation and explicit social asymmetries.

### Numbers
Administrative and narrative material repeatedly exposes institutional edge cases, including legal petition, precedent and recursive correction.

### Deuteronomy
Earlier experience and law are repeatedly reinterpreted through memory, public instruction, institutional constraint and explicit social provision.

The ESU method has therefore proved materially preferable to fixed verse-size extraction across the Torah.

## 5. Important methodological finding

The Torah does not behave like a flat ethical rulebook.

At source level it contains at least four simultaneously observable processes:

1. **repetition** — earlier rules recur;
2. **elaboration** — general obligations receive domain-specific procedures;
3. **revision/edge-case handling** — cases expose gaps requiring correction;
4. **tension/asymmetry** — protections coexist with rules that limit their scope or conflict with them.

This means later compression must distinguish:

**source frequency** from **principle multiplicity**, and **ethical convergence** from **internal textual consistency**.
