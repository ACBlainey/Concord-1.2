## E4-08 — Exodus Extraction Block C: Chapters 25–40 and Exodus Audit

## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — no cross-anchor comparison.**

## Source-form note
Exodus 25–31 and 35–40 contain extensive sanctuary, priestly, craft and ritual instructions. Under the frozen E4 protocol, technical cultic specification is not converted into substrate-neutral ethics merely because it is prescriptive.

The block therefore contains fewer ethical extractions than the legal material in chapters 20–23.

## Methodological observation

Exodus reveals a different extraction profile from Genesis.

Genesis often establishes ethical meaning through extended narrative arcs.

Exodus increasingly shifts toward:

**explicit rule → defined circumstance → differentiated liability/remedy.**

This is precisely the source-form variation that the ESU protocol was designed to preserve.

The shift also provides future material for M3's compression–decompression hypothesis, but no such analysis is performed during extraction.
