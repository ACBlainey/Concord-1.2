# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-72 — Post-Biblical Jewish Validation Sampling Protocol

## Status

**POST-FREEZE EXTERNAL-WITHIN-TRADITION VALIDATION PROTOCOL.**

The frozen E4 catalogue remains J001–J034. The Psalm holdout is closed.

# 1. Experimental question

> Does the frozen 34-principle catalogue remain capable of representing ethically relevant material across deliberately diverse post-biblical Jewish sources without modification?

This is a test of **developmental robustness within a tradition**, not independent civilisational convergence.

# 2. Why sampling is necessary

Post-biblical Jewish literature is too large, distributed and internally diverse for an exhaustive traversal comparable to the Tanakh extraction.

A validation sample must therefore be:
- predefined before detailed extraction;
- chronologically distributed;
- genre-diverse;
- not selected because it resembles E4;
- capable of producing disagreement as well as confirmation.

# 3. Predefined strata

## S1 — Early rabbinic normative ethics
Representative source families:
- Mishnah;
- Tosefta where useful for contrast;
- tractate Avot as a concentrated ethical source.

Purpose:
- interpersonal duties;
- adjudication;
- responsibility;
- learning;
- authority;
- social conduct.

## S2 — Talmudic legal-ethical reasoning
Representative passages from Babylonian Talmud, with Palestinian Talmud comparison where methodologically useful.

Purpose:
- case reasoning;
- dispute;
- minority views;
- evidence;
- harm;
- property;
- public responsibility;
- institutional reasoning.

## S3 — Medieval legal codification
Representative material from major halakhic codification, especially Maimonides' Mishneh Torah.

Purpose:
- systematisation;
- role duties;
- charity;
- harm;
- repentance;
- property;
- adjudication.

## S4 — Medieval philosophical ethics
Representative material from Maimonides' ethical/philosophical writing and another materially different philosophical voice where useful.

Purpose:
- virtue;
- intellectual responsibility;
- human capability;
- moderation;
- development.

## S5 — Musar / character-development literature
Representative works may include:
- Duties of the Heart;
- Orchot Tzaddikim;
- Mesillat Yesharim.

Purpose:
- inward discipline;
- motivation;
- habit;
- character formation;
- self-correction.

## S6 — Modern Jewish ethical/social thought
A deliberately heterogeneous sample of modern sources, avoiding a single denominational or ideological stream.

Purpose:
- modern institutions;
- dignity;
- social obligation;
- power;
- responsibility;
- new technological/social conditions where present.

# 4. Sampling rule

Each stratum should contribute enough ethically relevant units to expose:
- direct recovery;
- refinement;
- compound expression;
- genuine additions;
- tensions.

No quota is set for matching J001–J034.

The sampling target is **diversity of ethical mechanism**, not a desired coverage percentage.

# 5. Validation classes

Use the existing post-freeze classes:

- **V1** direct recovery;
- **V2** nuance/refinement;
- **V3** compound expression;
- **V4** genuine candidate addition;
- **V5** non-comparable/context-bound;
- **VT** tension.

# 6. Independence discipline

This phase has more distance from the primary Tanakh corpus than the Psalm holdout, but it remains genealogically dependent.

Therefore evidence must be labelled:

**within-tradition developmental recurrence**

rather than:

**independent cross-cultural convergence.**

Later E3/E4 comparison will address a different question.

# 7. Source hierarchy

Where possible use:
1. primary text in a documented edition/translation;
2. established scholarly translation;
3. specialist secondary scholarship for context;
4. tertiary summaries only for navigation, never as sole evidence for a proposition.

# 8. Interpretive controls

### Legal material
Preserve the concrete case before abstracting.

### Aggadah
Do not automatically convert story or rhetoric into binding rule.

### Minority opinion
Record disagreement rather than treating later dominance as proof that alternatives were absent.

### Historical context
Do not project modern concepts backward merely because functional resemblance exists.

### Theology
Exclude claims that cannot be separated into behavioural ethical content.

### Modern material
Do not treat recent vocabulary as proof that an underlying ethical distinction is recent.

# 9. Anti-selection control

The sample is frozen at the **stratum level** before detailed validation.

Sources that produce awkward, exclusionary, hierarchical or otherwise tension-producing material are not removed for poor fit.

# 10. Closure condition

This is a validation sample, not a new exhaustive extraction.

Close when:
- all six strata have been sampled;
- new units predominantly reproduce existing validation classes;
- plausible V4 candidates have been specifically challenged;
- major internal tensions have been documented;
- additional sampling shows declining information gain.

# 11. Sequence

1. Early rabbinic + Talmudic validation.
2. Medieval legal + philosophical validation.
3. Musar + modern validation.
4. V4/tension audit.
5. Close post-biblical phase.
6. Only then open cross-anchor E4↔E3 comparison.

> **J001–J034 remain frozen.**
