# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-51 — Chronicles Extraction Block B: 2 Chronicles 1–18

## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — no cross-anchor comparison. Psalms remain withheld.**

## Source-form note

Second Chronicles continues the Chronicler's theological retelling of monarchy. Parallel material already encountered in Kings remains a valid source occurrence but does not create a new principle merely by repetition. Divine reward/punishment sequences are not converted into empirical sociology. Temple and ritual material is retained only where a separable behavioural or institutional proposition exists.

## Block B observation

This block repeatedly separates **institutional capability from institutional legitimacy**. Skilled labour, wealth, military strength, public works and administrative sophistication can coexist with forced labour, excessive burdens, coercion or suppression of corrective information.

The Rehoboam sequence is especially clear:

**burden complaint → consultation → selection of domination-oriented advice → increased burden → withdrawal of cooperation → institutional fracture.**

This remains an E4-internal observation.
