# 1. Candidate addition

The recurring V4 pressure identified in S2 and S3 is:

> **When two otherwise valid obligations conflict, use an ethically constrained method to determine priority or accommodation.**

This appears in:
- scarcity allocation;
- preservation of life versus ordinary rules;
- captive-redemption limits;
- communal safety versus individual action;
- competing legal duties.

# 2. Is this a first-order ethical principle?

A first-order principle normally identifies:
- a protected interest;
- a prohibited relation;
- a positive duty;
- an epistemic requirement;
- or a constraint on power/action.

Conflict resolution differs.

It operates **over relationships among already valid principles**.

Its inputs are ethical claims; its output is a decision about precedence, accommodation or residual obligation.

This makes it structurally closer to a **meta-operation** than another content-bearing root.

# 3. Can it be regenerated from J001–J034?

The frozen catalogue already supplies major inputs:

- J001 — severity of life threat;
- J002 — culpability;
- J003 — proportionality;
- J008/J009 — accurate responsibility and evidence;
- J011 — uncertainty calibration;
- J016/J017 — impartial process/hearing;
- J020 — substantive legitimacy beyond formalism;
- J021/J022 — agency and conscience;
- J025/J026 — severe need/dependency;
- J029 — entrusted resources;
- J031 — bounded authority;
- J033 — systemic resilience;
- J034 — capacity/capability.

However, none of these alone says:

> compare multiple simultaneously valid obligations and choose or construct a resolution.

Therefore the operation is not identical to any J principle.

# 4. Does that require J035?

**No.**

Adding “resolve conflicts among principles” to the first-order catalogue would mix two architectural levels:

### Level A — ethical content
What interests, duties, constraints and relations matter?

### Level B — ethical composition
What happens when multiple valid Level-A constraints apply simultaneously?

The post-biblical evidence therefore identifies a missing **operator**, not a missing ethical value.

# 5. Proposed post-freeze operator

Without modifying J001–J034, record:

## O-E4-01 — Constrained Ethical Conflict Resolution

When valid ethical principles materially conflict:

1. identify all materially implicated principles;
2. verify relevant facts and uncertainty;
3. identify affected agents and asymmetries;
4. test whether accommodation can satisfy multiple principles;
5. where full satisfaction is impossible, minimise unnecessary violation using proportionality;
6. give special attention to irreversible/severe harm and reduced-agency vulnerability;
7. preserve reasons for the decision;
8. retain residual obligations for repair, review or later correction;
9. keep the decision corrigible where possible.

This operator is **derived from the frozen catalogue plus the structural fact of principle collision**.

It is not J035.
