## Status

## Source-form note

Ruth is narrative. Actions are not assumed normative merely because sympathetic characters perform them. Particular attention is paid to outsider status, widowhood, subsistence access, labour vulnerability, kinship obligations, public transaction procedure and the agency of Ruth and Naomi.

## Methodological observation

Ruth supplies a compact but unusually integrated case in which **protection and agency coexist**. Vulnerability is not answered by erasing the vulnerable person's decision-making capacity. Aid, workplace safety, subsistence access, public procedure and voluntary commitment repeatedly operate alongside Ruth's own choices.
