## Status

## Source-form note

Ezekiel combines visions, symbolic performances, legal-ethical catalogues, historical allegory and severe judgement imagery. Symbolic actions are not treated as ordinary behavioural prescriptions. Gendered and sexualised national allegories are quarantined where their rhetoric would otherwise distort substrate-neutral extraction.
