# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-56 — Provisional Normalised Catalogue B: Care, Resources, Institutions and Development

## Status

**PROVISIONAL NORMALISATION — NOT FROZEN.**

Continuation of E4-55. Derived only from E4's closed non-Psalm corpus.

# E. Care, vulnerability and positive obligation

### P-E4-047 — Provide Aid Under Severe Need Where Capacity Exists
Extreme vulnerability can create a positive obligation to assist rather than merely refrain from direct harm.

### P-E4-048 — Protect Dependants and Persons With Reduced Effective Agency
Children, incapacitated persons and others unable to secure basic interests independently require heightened protection.

### P-E4-049 — Protect Widows, Orphans, Poor Persons, Migrants and Socially Exposed Outsiders From Exploitation
Reduced bargaining power or social standing must not be converted into an opportunity for extraction.

### P-E4-050 — Design Aid to Preserve Dignity and Effective Agency
Assistance should avoid needless humiliation and, where possible, enable recipients to continue acting productively for themselves.

### P-E4-051 — Do Not Abandon the Sufferer Merely Because Their Condition Is Difficult to Explain
Solidarity and presence can remain duties even where causal explanation is uncertain.

### P-E4-052 — Use Social Power to Represent Persons Unable to Secure a Hearing
Those with access or influence can have obligations to advocate for people who cannot effectively represent themselves.

### P-E4-053 — Include Vulnerable Persons in Collective Benefit
Communal celebration, provision and public systems should not be structured so that lack of resources automatically excludes the poor.

### P-E4-054 — Recognise Compounding Vulnerability
Displacement, bereavement, scarcity, disability, outsider status, debt and institutional collapse can interact rather than operating as isolated harms.

# F. Property, labour, exchange and resources

### P-E4-055 — Respect Legitimate Property and Possession
Power does not by itself create a right to seize another person's land, goods or productive assets.

### P-E4-056 — Use Honest Measurement and Exchange
Weights, measures, prices and transactional representations should not be manipulated for hidden advantage.

### P-E4-057 — Do Not Exploit Debt or Scarcity
Financial or material vulnerability must not be used to create disproportionate dependency, dispossession or extraction.

### P-E4-058 — Provide Relief Where Debt Becomes Destructive
Restoration may require return of assets, remission of extractive charges or release from unsustainable dependency.

### P-E4-059 — Pay and Protect Workers Fairly
Productive labour should not be extracted while denying workers due compensation, safety or basic treatment.

### P-E4-060 — Do Not Shift Public Burdens Disproportionately Onto Low-Power Groups
Administrative convenience or public purpose does not automatically justify forced labour or asymmetric extraction.

### P-E4-061 — Steward Entrusted Resources
Control of another person's or a community's assets creates duties of custody, proper purpose and accountable use.

### P-E4-062 — Create Verifiable Resource Transfers
Important assets should be measured, recorded or witnessed sufficiently to permit later accounting.

### P-E4-063 — Allocate Shared Resources According to Declared Purpose
Institutional assets should not be privately redirected through status, patronage or conflict of interest.

### P-E4-064 — Distinguish Possession From Realised Welfare
Resource accumulation is not identical to benefit, security or flourishing.

### P-E4-065 — Avoid Wasteful or Self-Defeating Accumulation
Accumulation without a stopping condition or beneficial purpose can undermine rather than increase welfare.

# G. Leadership, power and institutional integrity

### P-E4-066 — Treat Authority as Entrusted and Bounded
Office grants responsibility within a role, not ownership of the governed or unlimited personal discretion.

### P-E4-067 — Select for Competence and Trustworthiness
Entrusted roles should be matched to relevant capability and reliability.

### P-E4-068 — Consult Before Consequential Collective Decisions
Leaders should seek relevant counsel rather than treating concentrated authority as sufficient knowledge.

### P-E4-069 — Preserve Independent Counsel
Advice should not be selected, rewarded or punished merely according to whether it confirms the leader's preference.

### P-E4-070 — Prevent Private Grievance From Becoming General Policy
Personal resentment or status injury should not be amplified through institutional power into rules harming uninvolved persons.

### P-E4-071 — Prevent Collective Punishment and Group Generalisation
Wrongdoing or nonconformity by one member does not justify treating an entire population as culpable.

### P-E4-072 — Prevent Capture Through Patronage, Kinship and Private Networks
Institutional resources and decisions should not be redirected by relationships that override public purpose.

### P-E4-073 — Preserve Corrective Channels Against Leadership Drift
Institutions require ways to detect and challenge divergence after success, succession or concentration of power.

### P-E4-074 — Make Institutions Corrigible
Rules, decrees and procedures should permit repair when their harmful effects or manipulation become known.

### P-E4-075 — Distribute Roles and Responsibilities
Complex institutions become more resilient when functions are allocated rather than concentrated in one person.

### P-E4-076 — Make Responsibility Traceable
Named roles, records and explicit custody help identify who is responsible for a decision, asset or function.

### P-E4-077 — Maintain Institutions After Founding
Recurring functions require ongoing staffing, provisioning, repair and review rather than one-time creation.

### P-E4-078 — Adapt Institutional Form When Conditions Change
Preserving purpose may require changing roles, procedures or operating structure.

### P-E4-079 — Prepare Succession Before Transfer
Continuity improves when successors receive knowledge, resources, role clarity and practical support before assuming full responsibility.

### P-E4-080 — Use Public Memory to Detect Institutional Drift
Archives, public readings, historical reflection and governing records can expose divergence between present practice and prior commitments.

### P-E4-081 — Do Not Confuse Administrative Scale With Ethical Legitimacy
Efficient bureaucracy can amplify both beneficial and harmful decisions.

### P-E4-082 — Build Repair Capacity Before Crisis Where Possible
Periods of stability can be used for maintenance, redundancy and preparation against foreseeable disruption.

# H. Social relations, inclusion and cohesion

### P-E4-083 — Preserve Dignity Across Status Differences
Poverty, labour status, outsider identity, defeat or social rank do not erase morally relevant standing.

### P-E4-084 — Do Not Humiliate or Degrade Needlessly
Ridicule, degrading labels and public humiliation can constitute harms independent of material injury.

### P-E4-085 — Resist Exclusion Based Solely on Group Identity
Group difference alone is insufficient evidence of individual threat, disloyalty or moral inferiority.  
**Notes:** Retained provisionally despite substantial internal tension evidence.

### P-E4-086 — Permit Outsider Incorporation Through Conduct and Participation
Narratives repeatedly show outsiders functioning as agents, workers, claimants or community members rather than only as threats.

### P-E4-087 — Preserve Social Bonds Without Making Loyalty a Shield for Wrongdoing
Kinship and group solidarity have value but do not cancel accountability.

### P-E4-088 — Use Speech to Reduce Rather Than Manufacture Conflict
Rumour, public accusation, ridicule and propaganda can escalate conflict; communication can instead clarify and de-escalate.

### P-E4-089 — Preserve Collective Memory
Communities can use recurring practices, records and teaching to maintain knowledge across generations.

# I. Self-regulation, development and resilience

### P-E4-090 — Regulate Anger Before Acting
Anger can distort proportionality, speech and the use of power.

### P-E4-091 — Exercise Patience Under Difficulty
Delay can improve judgement where immediate reaction would escalate harm or error.

### P-E4-092 — Accept Correction
Individuals and leaders should remain capable of revising conduct when credible error is identified.

### P-E4-093 — Cultivate Judgement Through Instruction, Practice and Counsel
Reliable judgement develops rather than appearing fully formed without learning.

### P-E4-094 — Do Not Treat Age, Status or Popularity as Proof of Wisdom
Experience may matter, but insight and error do not map perfectly onto hierarchy.

### P-E4-095 — Resist Destructive Peer and Group Pressure
Collective participation does not remove individual responsibility for joining harmful conduct.

### P-E4-096 — Consider Future Consequences
Immediate gain should be evaluated against foreseeable downstream effects.

### P-E4-097 — Act Under Uncertainty Without Pretending Certainty
Incomplete prediction need not cause paralysis, but action should remain proportionate to uncertainty.

### P-E4-098 — Diversify Where Dependence on One Uncertain Outcome Creates Avoidable Fragility
Distribution of effort or resources can improve resilience under uncertainty.

### P-E4-099 — Preserve Capability Through Preparation and Maintenance
Resilience depends partly on work performed before failure occurs.

### P-E4-100 — Distinguish Failure From Moral Worth
Loss, defeat, illness or poverty should not automatically become a judgement of a person's ethical value.

# J. Stewardship, continuity and future consequence

### P-E4-101 — Preserve Resources for Future Users
Current control does not exhaust responsibility where assets, institutions or environments will serve successors.

### P-E4-102 — Transfer Knowledge Alongside Authority
Succession without relevant knowledge can leave formal authority operationally weak.

### P-E4-103 — Protect Institutional Memory Against Loss or Suppression
Records and teaching should survive leadership turnover sufficiently to permit later verification and correction.

### P-E4-104 — Distinguish Founding From Sustained Operation
Creation of a system does not satisfy the continuing duties required to keep it functional.

### P-E4-105 — Repair Degraded Systems Rather Than Merely Rename Them Functional
Institutional form without working ethical function should not be treated as adequate performance.

### P-E4-106 — Match Obligation and Burden to Real Capacity
Expectations, roles and public burdens should account for actual capability rather than assuming identical capacity across persons or conditions.

### P-E4-107 — Expand Responsibility With Capability
Greater knowledge, authority, resources or access can increase what an agent can reasonably be expected to protect or repair.

### P-E4-108 — Preserve Future Correctability
Present choices should avoid unnecessarily eliminating the ability of future agents to revise harmful or mistaken arrangements.
