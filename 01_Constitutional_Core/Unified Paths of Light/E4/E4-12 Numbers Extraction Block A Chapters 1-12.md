# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-12 — Numbers Extraction Block A: Chapters 1–12

## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — no cross-anchor comparison.**

## Source-form note
Numbers combines census and camp administration, ritual law, purity regulation, institutional allocation and conflict narratives. Administrative description is not automatically ethical prescription. Extraction is limited to separable behavioural structures.

## Extracted source occurrences

### E4-NUM-001 — Numbers 1–2
**Source form:** census / camp organisation  
**Normative status:** administrative  
**Proposition:** **NO GENERAL ETHICAL EXTRACTION.** Population enumeration and camp arrangement are retained as institutional context.

### E4-NUM-002 — Numbers 3–4
**Source form:** role allocation / service census  
**Normative status:** explicit administrative allocation  
**Proposition:** Specialised collective work may be divided among defined groups with differentiated responsibilities rather than assigned indiscriminately.  
**Provisional cluster:** role allocation / responsibility.

### E4-NUM-003 — Numbers 4:17–20
**Source form:** hazardous/sacred task boundary  
**Normative status:** explicit protective restriction  
**Proposition:** Access to high-risk specialised tasks may require role boundaries intended to prevent serious harm.  
**Notes:** Sacred context retained; generalisation is limited to the protective structure.  
**Provisional cluster:** role boundary / harm prevention.

### E4-NUM-004 — Numbers 5:1–4
**Source form:** camp purity regulation  
**Normative status:** ritual/purity  
**Proposition:** **QUARANTINED AGAINST PUBLIC-HEALTH ANACHRONISM.** Separation rules are not automatically redescribed as medical ethics.

### E4-NUM-005 — Numbers 5:5–8
**Source form:** wrongdoing/restitution law  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Acknowledged wrongdoing against another person requires restitution, with additional compensation, and provision exists for cases where the injured party lacks an available kin recipient.  
**Provisional cluster:** restitution / repair.

### E4-NUM-006 — Numbers 5:11–31
**Source form:** suspected-adultery ordeal  
**Normative status:** ritual/judicial procedure with major asymmetry  
**Proposition:** **ETHICAL TENSION / QUARANTINE.** The procedure addresses unresolved suspicion but is gender-asymmetric and ritualised; it is not normalised into a general evidential standard.

### E4-NUM-007 — Numbers 6:1–21
**Source form:** Nazirite vow  
**Normative status:** voluntary religious commitment

**Provisional cluster:** voluntary commitment / self-binding.

### E4-NUM-008 — Numbers 6:22–27

### E4-NUM-009 — Numbers 7

### E4-NUM-010 — Numbers 8:23–26
**Source form:** service-age regulation

### E4-NUM-011 — Numbers 9:6–14
**Source form:** ritual exception / inclusion rule  
**Normative status:** explicit accommodation

**Provisional cluster:** equal rule within participation.

### E4-NUM-013 — Numbers 10
**Source form:** movement/signalling administration  
**Normative status:** administrative  
**Proposition:** **NO GENERAL ETHICAL EXTRACTION.**

### E4-NUM-014 — Numbers 11:1–15
**Source form:** complaint / leadership overload narrative  
**Normative status:** explicit distress  
**Proposition:** Concentrating the burdens of a population upon one leader can become unsustainable and should not be assumed to be a viable governance model.  
**Provisional cluster:** leadership burden / institutional sustainability.

### E4-NUM-015 — Numbers 11:16–17,24–30
**Source form:** delegated leadership narrative  
**Normative status:** explicit solution  
**Proposition:** Shared leadership can distribute responsibility and reduce destructive concentration of administrative burden.  
**Provisional cluster:** distributed responsibility.

### E4-NUM-016 — Numbers 11:26–29
**Source form:** challenge to unauthorised participation  
**Normative status:** explicit response  
**Proposition:** A leader need not suppress beneficial capability merely because it appears outside the expected authorised group.  
**Notes:** The prophetic context remains specific; extraction is limited to the anti-monopoly structure.  
**Provisional cluster:** non-monopoly of beneficial capability.

### E4-NUM-017 — Numbers 12:1–10
**Source form:** leadership/family challenge narrative  
**Normative status:** mixed theological judgement  
**Proposition:** **PARTLY QUARANTINED.** The episode involves status challenge, speech against leadership and divine sanction; no broad rule against criticism of authority is inferred.

### E4-NUM-018 — Numbers 12:11–15
**Source form:** acknowledgement/intercession narrative  
**Normative status:** explicit acknowledgement and restoration sequence  
**Proposition:** Recognition of wrongdoing can be followed by intercession, bounded consequence and eventual reintegration rather than permanent exclusion.  
**Provisional cluster:** accountability / reintegration.
