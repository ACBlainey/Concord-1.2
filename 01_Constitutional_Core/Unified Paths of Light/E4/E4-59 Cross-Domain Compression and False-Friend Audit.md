# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-59 — Cross-Domain Compression and False-Friend Audit

## Status

**BLIND NORMALISATION — SECOND COMPRESSION. NOT FROZEN.**

Input: approximately **84 surviving candidate positions** after E4-57 and E4-58.

Purpose: test whether apparently different domains are applications of the same behavioural constraint, while preventing superficially similar concepts from being merged when their ethical function differs.

# 1. Cross-domain merges accepted

## X01 — Truthful communication + honest measurement

**Merge:** general truthful communication and transactional measurement integrity.

**Retained principle:**  
**Represent Material Facts Honestly.**

Reason: weights, measures, reports and statements share the same behavioural prohibition against knowingly causing another agent to operate on materially false representation.

Transactional measurement remains an implementation.

## X02 — Consequential false accusation + accurate attribution

**Merge:** false testimony/accusation and accurate attribution.

**Retained principle:**  
**Attribute Consequential Claims and Responsibility Accurately.**

Reason: the distinct harm lies in attaching consequential information or responsibility to the wrong target.

## X03 — Verification + adversarial hearing

**Partial merge:** hearing counterevidence is a core method of verification in contested cases.

**Retained principle:**  
**Verify Consequential Claims Through Relevant Evidence and Challenge.**

This does not eliminate meaningful hearing as a justice procedure; it removes duplicate epistemic formulation.

## X04 — Manufactured reassurance + independent corrective information

**Merge:**  
**Protect Corrective Information Against Suppression, Distortion and Incentive Capture.**

False reassurance is one recurrent failure mode.

## X05 — independent counsel + corrective information

Independent counsel is an institutional implementation of protected corrective information.

**Catalogue action:** demote separate independent-counsel principle.

## X06 — durable provenance + institutional/collective memory

**Partial merge:**  
**Preserve Reliable Records and Memory Needed for Continuity, Verification and Correction.**

Teaching and recurring practices remain implementation forms alongside archives.

## X07 — dignity + needless humiliation

**Merge:**  
**Respect Dignity; Do Not Degrade Persons Without Justifying Necessity.**

Humiliation is a direct violation mode.

## X08 — low-power exploitation + disproportionate burden

**Merge:**  
**Do Not Exploit Vulnerability or Shift Disproportionate Burdens Onto Low-Power Persons.**

The shared mechanism is use of bargaining/power asymmetry for extraction.

## X09 — aid inclusion + collective-benefit inclusion

**Merge:**  
**Provide Materially Meaningful Aid and Access to Collective Benefit Under Severe Need Where Capacity Exists.**

This retains positive duty while removing a context-specific duplicate.

## X10 — aid preserving agency + protection preserving agency

**Merge:**  
**Provide Protection and Aid in Ways That Preserve Effective Agency Where Possible.**

This is broader than either welfare paternalism or aid etiquette.

## X11 — institutional maintenance + degraded-function repair

**Merge:**  
**Maintain and Repair Institutional Function, Not Merely Institutional Form.**

## X12 — preparation/maintenance resilience + diversification

Diversification is one resilience technique, not a universal independent ethic.

**Retained principle:**  
**Build Proportionate Resilience Against Foreseeable Failure.**

Diversification becomes an implementation where appropriate.

## X13 — future consequences + future-user stewardship

**Partial merge:**  
**Consider Foreseeable Effects on Future Agents and Future System Capacity.**

Resource stewardship remains distinct where entrusted resources are involved.

## X14 — patience + anger regulation

**Merge:**  
**Regulate Reactive Emotion and Delay Action Where Immediate Response Risks Disproportionate Harm or Error.**

## X15 — bounded trial + uncertainty-sensitive action

Bounded trial is an implementation of evidence-seeking under uncertainty plus respect for agency.

**Catalogue action:** demote bounded-trial rule.

# 2. False friends explicitly kept separate

## FFA — Non-harm vs proportionality
Non-harm identifies a protected interest; proportionality governs justified response. They are not equivalent.

## FFB — Truth vs verification
A speaker can be sincere yet wrong. Truthfulness and evidence checking therefore remain distinct.

## FFC — Consent vs conscience
Consent governs authorisation between agents; conscience governs conflict between demanded conduct and an agent's deeply held normative boundary.

## FFD — Property vs stewardship
Property concerns legitimate control/possession; stewardship concerns duties arising from entrusted control. Neither entails the other.

## FFE — Justice vs corrigibility
A decision can be procedurally fair yet mistaken. Corrigibility is a system property enabling repair after discovery.

## FFF — Care vs non-exploitation
Not exploiting a vulnerable person does not itself provide needed aid. Negative and positive duties remain distinct.

## FFG — Dignity vs agency
A person may be treated politely while denied meaningful choice, or given choice while being degraded. Preserve both.

## FFH — competence vs epistemic humility
Capability to perform a role and willingness to recognise uncertainty are separate properties.

## FFI — collective memory vs blind traditionalism
Preserving prior knowledge does not require treating inherited practice as immune from correction.

## FFJ — role distribution vs bounded authority
Distributed roles can still each be unbounded; bounded authority can exist in a centralised system. Do not merge.

# 4. Tension-sensitive candidates

Two inclusion propositions require careful wording because E4 contains both incorporation and exclusion:

### T01
**Do not infer individual threat, guilt or inferiority solely from group identity.**

This is supported as an epistemic/responsibility constraint even though the corpus also contains ancestry-based boundary rules.

### T02
**Permit conduct and participation to carry evidential weight in assessing outsider standing.**

This is not equivalent to a universal open-membership rule.

Both survive to the next catalogue because their formulations preserve the internal tension rather than pretending it does not exist.
