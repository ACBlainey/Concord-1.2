# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-10 — Leviticus Extraction Block B: Chapters 11–20

## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — no cross-anchor comparison.**

## Extracted source occurrences

### E4-LEV-013 — Leviticus 11–15
**Source form:** dietary/purity/health-status regulation  
**Normative status:** predominantly ritual purity and bodily-status law  
**Proposition:** **MOSTLY CONTEXT-BOUND.** No modern public-health principle is inferred automatically from purity legislation.

### E4-LEV-014 — Leviticus 13–14
**Source form:** diagnosis/isolation/re-entry procedures  
**Normative status:** priestly purity administration  
**Proposition:** **QUARANTINED AGAINST ANACHRONISM.** The text contains inspection, separation and re-entry procedures, but these are not redescribed as modern disease-control ethics without independent textual warrant.

### E4-LEV-015 — Leviticus 16
**Source form:** Day of Atonement ritual  
**Normative status:** cultic/theological  
**Proposition:** **NO GENERAL ETHICAL EXTRACTION.**

### E4-LEV-016 — Leviticus 17
**Source form:** sacrifice/blood regulation  
**Normative status:** covenantal/ritual  
**Proposition:** **NO ADDITIONAL GENERAL EXTRACTION.**

### E4-LEV-017 — Leviticus 18
**Source form:** sexual/kinship prohibitions  
**Normative status:** explicit legal boundaries  
**Proposition:** Intimate conduct is subject to defined relational and kinship boundaries rather than treated as ethically unrestricted.  
**Notes:** Individual prohibitions vary in their capacity for substrate-neutral generalisation; no modern sexual ethic is imposed retrospectively.  
**Provisional cluster:** intimate/kinship boundaries.

### E4-LEV-018 — Leviticus 18:6–18
**Source form:** close-kin sexual prohibitions  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Close kinship can create protected relational boundaries against sexual access.  
**Provisional cluster:** kinship boundary.

### E4-LEV-019 — Leviticus 18:19–23
**Source form:** sexual/cultic prohibitions  
**Normative status:** explicit but heterogeneous  
**Proposition:** **PARTLY QUARANTINED.** The rules should not be collapsed into a single modern category; each remains source-specific pending later normalisation.

### E4-LEV-020 — Leviticus 19:3–4
**Source form:** family/religious commands  
**Normative status:** mixed  
**Proposition:** Respect toward parents is explicitly required; idolatry prohibition remains theological and is not secularised.  
**Provisional cluster:** intergenerational/family obligation.

### E4-LEV-021 — Leviticus 19:9–10
**Source form:** harvest law  
**Normative status:** explicit positive provision  
**Proposition:** Productive property rights may be deliberately limited so that poor persons and resident outsiders retain access to part of the harvest.  
**Provisional cluster:** provision / property limitation.

### E4-LEV-022 — Leviticus 19:11
**Source form:** behavioural prohibition  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Theft, deception and false dealing with others are prohibited.  
**Provisional cluster:** property / truth / transactional integrity.

### E4-LEV-023 — Leviticus 19:12
**Source form:** oath rule  
**Normative status:** theological framing with behavioural content  
**Proposition:** Invoking a trusted or sacred authority falsely to strengthen one's claim is prohibited.  
**Provisional cluster:** truthful representation / oath integrity.

### E4-LEV-024 — Leviticus 19:13
**Source form:** labour/property law  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Exploitation, robbery and wrongful withholding of earned wages are prohibited.  
**Provisional cluster:** labour fairness / property integrity.

### E4-LEV-025 — Leviticus 19:14
**Source form:** vulnerability rule  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Another person's sensory or physical vulnerability must not be exploited to harm, humiliate or endanger them.  
**Provisional cluster:** disability/vulnerability protection.

### E4-LEV-026 — Leviticus 19:15
**Source form:** adjudicative law  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Judgement should not be distorted by either sympathy for poverty or deference to power; cases should be judged fairly.  
**Provisional cluster:** impartial justice.

### E4-LEV-027 — Leviticus 19:16
**Source form:** speech/harm law  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Malicious circulation of information about others is prohibited, and passivity where another's life is endangered is constrained.  
**Provisional cluster:** harmful speech / protective duty.

### E4-LEV-028 — Leviticus 19:17
**Source form:** relational command  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Hidden hatred should not replace direct correction; wrongdoing may need to be confronted without assuming another person's guilt as one's own burden.  
**Provisional cluster:** correction / relational conflict.

### E4-LEV-029 — Leviticus 19:18
**Source form:** relational command  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Revenge and enduring grievance against one's community are prohibited, while positive regard for the other is required.  
**Provisional cluster:** non-retaliation / positive regard.

### E4-LEV-030 — Leviticus 19:23–25
**Source form:** agricultural maturation rule  
**Normative status:** explicit but partly ritual  
**Proposition:** Productive use can be delayed rather than maximised immediately; however the specific fruit-tree timetable remains covenantal/agricultural and is not universalised.  
**Provisional cluster:** delayed use / restraint.

### E4-LEV-031 — Leviticus 19:26–31
**Source form:** religious/customary prohibitions  
**Normative status:** predominantly theological/cultural  
**Proposition:** **QUARANTINED.** No general ethical extraction is forced from divination, bodily-marking or medium prohibitions.

### E4-LEV-032 — Leviticus 19:32
**Source form:** social respect command  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Advanced age is treated as grounds for social respect rather than disregard.  
**Provisional cluster:** respect across age.

### E4-LEV-033 — Leviticus 19:33–34
**Source form:** outsider-protection law  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Resident outsiders must not be wronged and should receive the same neighbourly regard demanded within the native community.  
**Provisional cluster:** outsider inclusion / equal regard.

### E4-LEV-034 — Leviticus 19:35–36
**Source form:** commercial law  
**Normative status:** explicit  
**Proposition:** Measurement in judgement and commerce must be accurate rather than manipulated for advantage.  
**Provisional cluster:** accurate measure / commercial honesty.

### E4-LEV-035 — Leviticus 20
**Source form:** offence/penalty legislation  
**Normative status:** explicit severe sanctions with theological framing  
**Proposition:** **PARTLY QUARANTINED.** The chapter demonstrates that prohibited conduct and sanction are distinguished categories, but its specific capital and exclusion penalties are not treated as timeless proportionality benchmarks.

## Block B observation

Leviticus 19 produces an unusually dense ethical concentration inside a book otherwise dominated by ritual and purity material. Its source-level propositions include positive provision for poor persons and outsiders, wage protection, vulnerability safeguards, impartial judgement, intervention where life is threatened, limits on revenge, outsider inclusion and accurate commercial measure.

This concentration must not be allowed to erase the low-yield and context-bound character of surrounding chapters.
