## Methodological observation

Two distinctions are especially clear inside Ezekiel:

**1. Warning responsibility ≠ outcome ownership.**  
The watchman must warn; the warned person retains agency over response.

**2. Individual liability ≠ distributed institutional responsibility.**  
Ezekiel 18 rejects automatic inherited guilt, while Ezekiel 22 identifies simultaneous present responsibility across multiple institutional roles.

These are compatible distinctions within the source itself and should not be collapsed during later normalisation.
