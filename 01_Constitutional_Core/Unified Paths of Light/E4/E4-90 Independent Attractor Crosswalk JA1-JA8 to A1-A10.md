# 4. Why 8 ≠ 10

The difference is primarily **partition geometry**.

E3 separates:
- peace/social cohesion from non-harm;
- dignity/anti-domination from autonomy/care/institutional integrity;
- self-regulation/resilience/proportionality into a distinct parent.

E4 instead:
- embeds de-escalation/repair/proportionality partly within JA1;
- distributes dignity/anti-domination functions across JA4, JA5 and JA7;
- gives developmental/capability responsibility its own JA8;
- gives bounded institutional power its own JA7.

Thus the two derivations cut a similar ethical space along different axes.
