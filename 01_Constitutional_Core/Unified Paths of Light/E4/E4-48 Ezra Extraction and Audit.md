## Status

## Source-form note

Ezra combines imperial decrees, lists, institutional restoration, correspondence, legal-religious reform and difficult intermarriage material. Administrative actions are not assumed ethically normative merely because they support restoration. The expulsion of foreign wives and children in Ezra 9–10 is retained as major tension evidence and is not universalised.

## Methodological observation

Ezra is especially useful for distinguishing **procedure from substantive legitimacy**. Archival verification, hearing, role competence, audit trails and case-by-case processing can be procedurally sophisticated while the underlying policy remains ethically contestable.

That distinction must survive later compression:

**good procedure does not automatically make the objective good; a disputed objective does not make procedural evidence irrelevant.**
