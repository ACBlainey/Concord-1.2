# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-18 — Joshua Extraction: Chapters 1–24

## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — no cross-anchor comparison.**

## Source-form note

Joshua is dominated by conquest, territorial allocation, covenantal loyalty and transition from Mosaic to post-Mosaic leadership. Because military success is not automatically ethical endorsement, conquest narratives are not converted into general behavioural permission. Particular attention is paid to agreements, responsibility, evidence, allocation, refuge and conflict prevention.

## Extracted source occurrences

**Source form:** succession commission  
**Normative status:** explicit leadership transition  
**Proposition:** Institutional continuity can be maintained by clearly commissioning a successor after the death of a foundational leader.  
**Provisional cluster:** succession / continuity.
