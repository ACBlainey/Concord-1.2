## E4-36 — Proverbs Extraction Block A: Chapters 1–10

## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — no cross-anchor comparison. Psalms remain withheld.**

## Source-form note

Proverbs differs sharply from the legal, narrative and prophetic corpora already traversed. Wisdom sayings often express probabilistic social regularities rather than exceptionless laws. E4 therefore does not convert prudential maxims into guaranteed causal claims. Repetition is retained as source frequency and will not later be mistaken for principle multiplicity.

## Block A observation

Proverbs introduces a high-density **developmental epistemology** into E4: judgement is taught, practised, corrected, transmitted and strengthened over time. The corpus repeatedly treats correctability as a capacity that can itself be cultivated or lost.
