# Unified Paths of Light — E4
## E4-50 — Chronicles Extraction Block A: 1 Chronicles 1–29

## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — no cross-anchor comparison. Psalms remain withheld.**

## Source-form note

Chronicles retells and restructures material known from Samuel–Kings while adding genealogical, liturgical and administrative emphases. Repeated events remain valid source occurrences but are not counted later as independent principles merely because they recur. The Chronicler's theological causal interpretations are not converted into empirical sociology. Psalms quoted or embedded within Chronicles are **not used as Psalm-holdout evidence**; where a passage substantially reproduces Psalmic material, it is quarantined from E4 ethical derivation to preserve the holdout.

## Block A observation

First Chronicles is unusually rich in **institutional continuity mechanics**: records, specialised roles, rotations, custody, succession preparation, public transfer and adaptation of roles after environmental change.

It also creates a crucial holdout-control issue. The Chronicler embeds material parallel to Psalms. Such passages are explicitly excluded from E4 ethical derivation so that later Psalm validation remains genuinely external to the frozen E4 catalogue.
