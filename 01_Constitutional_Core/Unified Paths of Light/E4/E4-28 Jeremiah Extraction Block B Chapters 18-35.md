## Block B observation

Jeremiah 18–35 adds unusually explicit material about information provenance: whether a speaker genuinely possesses the authority claimed, whether predictions survive empirical testing, whether messages are falsely attributed, and whether institutions punish dissent instead of evaluating it.

It also adds a clear reform-integrity pattern: emancipation declared under pressure and then reversed when conditions change.
