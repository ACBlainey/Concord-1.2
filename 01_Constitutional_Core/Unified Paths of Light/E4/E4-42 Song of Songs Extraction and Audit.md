## Status
**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — no cross-anchor comparison. Psalms remain withheld.**

## Source-form note

Song of Songs is lyric love poetry. Speaker attribution is not always certain, metaphor is dense, and later allegorical readings are not imported into the primary extraction. Descriptive erotic imagery is not automatically converted into prescriptive sexual ethics. Extraction is limited to relational structures sufficiently explicit in the poem itself.

## Methodological observation

Song of Songs is a useful genre-control corpus. It contains comparatively little institutional ethics but substantial evidence about **mutual desire, relational voice and non-forcing of intimacy**. These should not be inflated into a complete theory of consent, yet neither should lyric genre cause their source-level recurrence to disappear.
