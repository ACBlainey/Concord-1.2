# 3. Adversarial merge tests

## A + C?
Protection and justice frequently interact.

**Reject merge.** A system could minimise bodily harm while retaining biased hearings and illegitimate adjudication.

## B + C?
Good judgement requires good information.

**Reject merge.** Epistemic truth can exist without fair treatment; fair intent cannot substitute for accurate evidence.

## C + G?
Judgement is institutional.

**Reject merge.** C applies to consequential judgement even outside formal institutions; G concerns collective power and system continuity.

## D + E?
Care often protects agency.

**Reject merge.** Welfare can be provided coercively; agency can be respected without fulfilling positive aid duties.

## D + A?
Consent reduces harm.

**Reject merge.** Consensual conduct can still create harm, and non-harm alone does not protect refusal/conscience.

## E + A?
Aid prevents harm.

**Reject merge.** Positive duty cannot be fully reduced to prohibition on causing injury.

## F + G?
Resources are institutionally governed.

**Reject merge.** Stewardship/possession applies to individuals and institutions; bounded authority is not reducible to resource management.

## H + D?
Development concerns agency.

**Reject merge.** Mature autonomous agents and developing/reduced-capability agents require different responsibility calibration; developmental formation is not identical to consent.

## H + B?
Judgement development is epistemic.

**Reject merge.** Capability-sensitive responsibility includes burden and role assignment beyond truth-seeking.

# 4. Adversarial split tests

## C-A split non-harm from repair?
Repair is a temporally downstream response to harm, but its positive duty is distinct.

**Pressure noted.** However J005 remains functionally tied to restoring the protected state after violation. Keep together at attractor level.

## C-B split truth from corrigibility?
A system can report truth yet resist correction.

**Pressure substantial.** But verification, confidence calibration, records and correction form a feedback architecture around reliable relation to reality. Keep parent broad enough to include both.

## C-G split bounded power from resilience?
A bounded institution can be fragile; a resilient institution can be authoritarian.

**Pressure substantial.** These are distinct subfunctions. Yet both concern legitimate persistence/exercise of collective institutional capability. Keep provisionally together and flag for final stability test.

## C-H split development from responsibility calibration?
They are causally linked but conceptually separable.

**Pressure substantial.** Development changes capability; capability changes legitimate responsibility. The frozen J034 itself joins them, so splitting the attractor would exceed the resolution of its sole primary child.
