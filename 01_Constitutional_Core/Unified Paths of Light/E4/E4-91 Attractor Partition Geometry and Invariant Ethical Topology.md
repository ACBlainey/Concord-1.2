# 3. Candidate invariant dimensions

The E3/E4 comparison suggests at least the following recurrent dimensions beneath both partitions:

1. **Protection from unjustified harm**
2. **Reliable relation to truth/reality**
3. **Fair consequential judgement**
4. **Protected agency and meaningful choice**
5. **Positive care under need/vulnerability**
6. **Responsible control/use of resources**
7. **Constraint of concentrated or entrusted power**
8. **Correction, adaptation and resilience**
9. **Capability/development-sensitive responsibility**
10. **Relational/social conditions reducing domination and destructive conflict**

These are not declared a new frozen attractor list. They are a descriptive coordinate system inferred from overlap.

# 4. Why some dimensions fuse

### Harm + peace
De-escalation can be represented as a branch of harm minimisation or as an independent social-cohesion parent.

### Agency + dignity
Anti-domination can be represented as protection of agency, protection of vulnerable standing, or a distinct dignity/equality parent.

### Truth + correction
Correctability can be represented inside epistemic integrity or institutional resilience.

### Power + institutions
Bounded authority can be treated as justice, trust, anti-domination or institutional integrity.

### Development + self-regulation
Capability-sensitive responsibility can be represented as development, self-regulation, care or agency depending on compression boundary.

# 5. Invariance test

A dimension is a stronger candidate for invariant topology when:
- both independently derived systems preserve its function;
- removing it creates detectable ethical failure;
- the systems may group it differently but cannot eliminate it;
- its concrete applications regenerate across multiple domains.

By this criterion, numerical cluster identity is unnecessary.

# 8. Falsifiable prediction

If the topology hypothesis is correct, a genuinely independent future corpus should:

1. derive its own principles without access to E3/E4;
2. compress to an independently chosen number of attractors;
3. possibly use different labels and cluster boundaries;
4. nevertheless preserve most of the same functional dimensions;
5. expose genuine residual dimensions where the topology model is incomplete.

Failure to recover major dimensions would weaken the invariant-topology hypothesis.

# 9. Methodological consequence

Future experiments should not ask only:

> Did the new tradition reproduce the ten attractors?

They should ask:

> What attractors arise independently, and what transformation maps their functional topology onto the previously observed topology?

This is a stricter and less confirmation-prone test.

# 10. Natural-law implication

If unrelated cultural lineages, and eventually non-human multi-agent systems, independently reconstruct the same functional topology under survival/cooperation pressure, the natural-law hypothesis becomes progressively stronger.

At present E3/E4 alone cannot establish that independence.

The result is best stated as:

> **Within two independently processed but genealogically related scriptural traditions, higher-order ethical compression yields different partitions over a strongly overlapping functional topology.**

That is the current evidence boundary.
