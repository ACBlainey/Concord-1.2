# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-116 — Developmental Priority Residual Test

Developmental Priority has repeatedly appeared as one of the Concord's most distinctive laws, especially for newly emerging or reduced-agency sentiences.

## Decomposition

### X7 Capability-Sensitive Development
Creates the positive requirement to cultivate judgement/capability and scale responsibility to actual developmental capacity.

### X4 Care Under Need and Vulnerability
Adds heightened support where reduced effective capacity creates vulnerability.

### X3 Protected Agency and Non-Domination
Prevents developmental protection from becoming indefinite paternalistic control.

### X1 Harm
Constrains dangerous developmental exposure and harmful intervention.

### X6 Correctability
Requires developmental classifications and interventions to remain reviewable.

## Result
Developmental Priority is strongly derivable from X7 + X4, bounded by X3 and X1 and made corrigible by X6.

Yet it remains constitutionally valuable as a named law because future artificial, biological or hybrid agents may pass through periods where:
- capability changes rapidly;
- agency is partial or contested;
- dependency is high;
- mistakes by guardians can permanently shape later autonomy.

## Finding
Developmental Priority is not an additional deepest ethical root.

It is a **high-priority civilisational theorem** generated by the shared root kernel under developmental asymmetry.

This helps explain why it can appear unusually prominent in the Concord while being distributed across older ethical traditions.
