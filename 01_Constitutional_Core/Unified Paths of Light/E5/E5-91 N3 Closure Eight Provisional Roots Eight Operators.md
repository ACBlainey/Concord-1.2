# Provisional roots

**BR1 — Harm Constraint**  
Prevent, reduce and do not unnecessarily reproduce serious harm.

**BR2 — Truth and Corrigible Knowledge**  
Maintain a corrigible relation between belief, communication and relevant evidence.

**BR3 — Agency and Non-Domination**  
Do not exploit, coerce or unnecessarily disable another agent's effective capacity for responsible action.

**BR4 — Care Under Need**  
Where serious need or reduced effective capacity exists, provide proportionate support when capable of doing so.

**BR5 — Responsible Resource Relation**  
Acquire, hold, use and share resources without deception, exploitation, destructive attachment or neglect of legitimate responsibilities.

**BR6 — Ethical Correctability**  
Preserve the capacity to observe error, revise conduct, repair where possible and permit genuine reform.

**BR7 — Capability-Sensitive Development**  
Treat ethical capability as developable and match demands, responsibility and instruction to relevant capacity and conditions.

**BR8 — Integrity of Role and Practice**  
Require entrusted or declared ethical roles to be expressed in conduct rather than status, title or reward.

# Provisional operators

**BO1 — Causal Diagnosis**  
Identify relevant conditions producing the harmful or ineffective state.

**BO2 — Consequence Horizon**  
Evaluate effects across affected agents and across immediate and delayed horizons.

**BO3 — Recursive Review**  
Observe action and result and revise when warranted.

**BO4 — Proportionality and Context**  
Match response intensity, timing and method to relevant conditions.

**BO5 — Counter-Condition**  
Cultivate constructive counter-conditions to recurrent harmful patterns.

**BO6 — Perspective and Attachment Check**  
Test whether identity, possession, status or attachment is preventing accurate assessment or correction.

**BO7 — Meaning Preservation**  
Distinguish wording from substance and preserve meaning through transmission.

**BO8 — Practice Validation**  
Test claimed understanding or ethical status through operational conduct.
