# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-42 — Khuddaka Extraction D: Theragāthā

## Status

**PRIMARY EXTRACTION.** E3, E4, candidate topology and Concord remain quarantined.

## Source-level propositions

### E5-THAG-001
Ethical development requires recognising one's own harmful dispositions rather than locating the problem exclusively in other agents.

### E5-THAG-002
Anger, craving and conceit are represented as trainable conditions rather than immutable identity.

### E5-THAG-003
Constructive companionship can materially support persistence, learning and correction.

### E5-THAG-004
Solitude can be instrumentally valuable where association repeatedly stimulates harmful conduct, but isolation is not itself the ethical end.

### E5-THAG-005
Material simplicity can reduce dependency and distraction when voluntarily adopted for a developmental purpose.

### E5-THAG-006
Renunciation loses ethical value if converted into pride, status competition or contempt.

### E5-THAG-007
Repeated attention and disciplined practice can transform entrenched behavioural tendencies.

### E5-THAG-008
Adversity does not remove all agency over one's response; endurance can coexist with active cognitive regulation.

### E5-THAG-009
A teacher's instruction becomes ethically effective through the learner's practice rather than through authority alone.

### E5-THAG-010
Recognition of impermanence can reduce possessive responses to status, body and material conditions.

### E5-THAG-011
Past harmful conduct need not permanently determine future character where genuine transformation occurs.

### E5-THAG-012
Learning from prior error can itself become evidence of development.

### E5-THAG-013
Sensory attraction and aversion should be observed before they become automatic action.

### E5-THAG-014
Discipline includes maintenance: constructive states require continued cultivation after initial attainment.

### E5-THAG-015
A person's public identity as a practitioner is weaker evidence than actual reduction of greed, hostility and confusion.

### E5-THAG-016
Gratitude toward teachers and supporters is compatible with preserved personal responsibility for one's development.

## Control note

Autobiographical and poetic claims are not automatically universalised. The behavioural proposition is retained only where the verse supplies a separable ethical/developmental function.
