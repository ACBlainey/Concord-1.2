## E5-27 Samyutta Nikaya Extraction E SN46-SN50

## Status

**PRIMARY EXTRACTION.** E3, E4, candidate topology and Concord remain quarantined. Records are provisional source-level propositions, not normalised principles.

## Extraction records

### E5-SN-329
**Reviewed range:** SN 46–50.  
**Proposition:** Beneficial cognitive qualities can be deliberately cultivated; reliable judgement is condition-dependent rather than automatic.

### E5-SN-330
**Reviewed range:** SN 46–50.  
**Proposition:** Sensual fixation, ill will, lethargy, agitation and doubt can obstruct knowledge and should be treated as causal impediments to judgement.

### E5-SN-331
**Reviewed range:** SN 46–50.  
**Proposition:** Mindfulness, investigation, energy, tranquillity, concentration and equanimity can function together as conditions supporting clearer knowledge.

### E5-SN-332
**Reviewed range:** SN 46–50.  
**Proposition:** Compassion and goodwill can be deliberately developed as alternatives to hostility rather than treated as spontaneous traits only.

### E5-SN-333
**Reviewed range:** SN 46–50.  
**Proposition:** The same mental practice may need different emphasis according to whether the mind is sluggish, agitated or balanced; intervention should fit condition.

### E5-SN-334
**Reviewed range:** SN 46–50.  
**Proposition:** Ethical development requires maintaining useful qualities after they arise, not merely generating them once.

### E5-SN-335
**Reviewed range:** SN 46–50.  
**Proposition:** Claims that knowledge or ignorance arise without causes should be tested against observable conditions affecting cognition.

### E5-SN-336
**Reviewed range:** SN 46–50.  
**Proposition:** Practice should be evaluated by whether it reduces craving, hostility and confusion rather than by label alone.

### E5-SN-337
**Reviewed range:** SN 46–50.  
**Proposition:** Advice to another agent should consider that agent's present cognitive condition and capacity to benefit.

### E5-SN-338
**Reviewed range:** SN 46–50.  
**Proposition:** Cultivated equanimity is compatible with investigation and concern; it is not simple indifference.
