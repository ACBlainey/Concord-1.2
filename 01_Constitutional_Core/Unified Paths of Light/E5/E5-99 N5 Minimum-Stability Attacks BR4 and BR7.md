# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-99 — N5 Minimum-Stability Attacks: BR4 Care and BR7 Development

## BR4 elimination attempt
Hypothesis:

[
BR4 stackrel{?}{=} BR1
]

If omission under severe need is defined as harm, positive care can be absorbed into non-harm.

### Failure
That move is definitional expansion rather than compression. A person can refrain from causing another's crisis yet still face a source-supported positive expectation to aid where serious need and capacity to help coincide.

BR1 prohibits/reduces harmful conduct. BR4 adds an affirmative duty.

**Decision: BR4 SURVIVES.**

## BR7 elimination attempt
Hypothesis:

[
BR7 stackrel{?}{=} BR3 + BR6
]

Agency protects present capacity; correctability preserves revision.

### Failure
Neither requires:
- cultivating capability;
- matching instruction to developmental readiness;
- scaling responsibility to actual capacity;
- maintaining conditions for progressive ethical competence.

Respect for present agency is not identical to development of future agency.

**Decision: BR7 SURVIVES.**
