# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-84 — N2 Parent-Child Compression C: Motivation, Resilience, Status and Care

## Status
**N2 PARENT-CHILD PRESSURE.**

## Motivation
**N2-M1 — Evaluate and Regulate Upstream Motivation Where It Predictably Shapes Harmful Conduct**

Absorbs:
- intention as ethically consequential;
- greed/craving, hostility and confusion as harmful roots;
- constructive counter-conditions;
- internal motivation alongside visible outcome.

Retained:
- corruption of ethical discipline into pride/status;
- possessive identity blocking correction where not already epistemically absorbed.

## Resilience
**N2-RS1 — Maintain Ethical Orientation Through Uncontrollable Change, Loss and Social Evaluation**

Absorbs:
- preparation for ageing/illness/loss/death;
- gain/loss, praise/blame, status/disgrace not determining ethical worth;
- stability under adversity.

The separate claim that prior grief/harm does not permanently remove developmental possibility is absorbed by N2-C2, not this resilience parent.

## Status and dignity
**N2-ST1 — Evaluate Ethical Standing by Relevant Conduct and Understanding Rather Than Inherited or Superficial Status**

Absorbs:
- birth/rank/age/appearance/eloquence;
- gender/social-position incapacity inference;
- ethical/spiritual identity as basis for contempt.

Retained:
- shared vulnerability as a constraint on degrading treatment, because this supplies an independent reason for treatment rather than merely an evaluation rule.

## Care
**N2-CA1 — Provide Proportionate Support Under Severe Need, Dependency or Reduced Effective Capacity Where Capacity to Help Exists**

Absorbs:
- severe-need support;
- dependant support;
- vulnerable-companion support.

Retained:
- gratitude;
- reciprocal duties;
- material conditions affecting agency, pending cross-domain compression.
