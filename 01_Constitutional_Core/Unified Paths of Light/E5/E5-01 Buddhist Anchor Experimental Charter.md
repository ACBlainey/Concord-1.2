# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-01 — Buddhist Anchor Experimental Charter

## Status

**NEW INDEPENDENT ANCHOR EXPERIMENT.**

E5 tests whether a substantially non-Abrahamic Buddhist derivation independently reconstructs ethical structure previously observed in E3 and E4.

# 1. Primary question

> If an early Buddhist corpus is extracted and compressed without access to the frozen Qur'an-first, Jewish-derived, Concord, or previously inferred topology structures, what minimum-stable ethical principles and higher-order attractors emerge?

# 2. Quarantined comparison material

During primary derivation and attractor compression, the following are comparison-prohibited:

- E3 Q001–Q089;
- E3 A1–A10;
- E4 J001–J034;
- E4 JA1–JA8;
- Blainey's Laws;
- Decalogue results;
- candidate invariant topology from E4-91.

They may be reopened only after E5 principles and attractors are frozen.

# 3. Independence objective

E5 is intentionally harder than E4.

Judaism and Islam are genealogically related. Early Buddhist literature arises from a substantially different South Asian religious-philosophical environment and may organise ethical material around different concepts.

The experiment must therefore permit genuine divergence.

No expected number of principles or attractors is specified.

# 4. Extraction rule

An Ethical Source Unit (ESU) is the smallest contextually sufficient passage, teaching, rule, narrative judgement or documented formulation that yields a distinct behavioural ethical proposition.

Do not count:
- metaphysics alone;
- cosmology alone;
- ritual identity alone;
- liberation doctrine alone;
- descriptive psychology without behavioural implication.

Do count a proposition where such material yields a separable behavioural constraint, duty, protected interest, decision rule, developmental practice or social/institutional implication.

# 5. Buddhist-specific controls

## Intention
Record intention-sensitive ethics without assuming that intention exhausts consequences or social effects.

## Monastic versus general scope
Do not universalise a monastic discipline rule into a general ethical root unless its function survives outside the institutional monastic context.

## Liberation versus civil ethics
Distinguish practices aimed specifically at liberation from constraints relevant to cooperative multi-agent life.

## Mental-state ethics
Do not discard inward-facing ethical material merely because it regulates craving, anger, attention or judgement. Test whether it changes behaviour, agency or harm.

## Narrative authority
As in E4, action alone is not normative. Record whether conduct is prescribed, praised, criticised, causally warned against, spoken by a character, or merely narrated.

# 7. Failure is admissible

E5 may:
- recover similar topology;
- recover only part of it;
- reveal genuinely new dimensions;
- contradict previous structures;
- resist stable compression.

All outcomes are evidentially useful.

# 8. Core methodological rule

> **Derive internally. Freeze. Challenge externally. Compare only afterward.**

E5 begins under that constraint.
