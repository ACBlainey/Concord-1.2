# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-62 — Aṅguttara Nikāya Fine-Provenance Audit A: AN 1–2

## Status
**FINE-PROVENANCE AUDIT. NORMALISATION BLOCKED.**

## Resolution problem
AN is organised by numerical sets and contains very high formulaic repetition. The first pass therefore recorded ethical functions at book level. This audit tests those functions without pretending that each thematic record equals one individual sutta.

## AN 1
Repeated short discourses strongly support:
- trainability and malleability of mind;
- consequences of developed versus undeveloped mental qualities;
- heedfulness versus negligence;
- importance of constructive association;
- repeated cultivation rather than one-off assent.

**PV2:** repeated-family provenance.  
**PV3:** general “ethical capability is trainable” formulation is a faithful compression, not one isolated quotation.

## AN 2
Paired formulations support:
- distinguishing constructive from harmful qualities;
- gratitude and recognition of benefit;
- learning plus reflection;
- reciprocal dimensions of relationship;
- complementary qualities rather than single-factor development.

**PV2/PV3.**

## Modernisation control
The exploratory language of “reciprocal responsibilities” and “developmental enablement” is retained only as functional synthesis where the canonical formulation is narrower.

## Audit result
No PV5 removal required.  
No PV6 addition.

> **AN 1–2 PASS AT DECLARED REPEATED-FAMILY RESOLUTION.**
