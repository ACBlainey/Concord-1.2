### E5-AN-362
**Reviewed range:** AN 3.  
**Proposition:** Greed, hostility and delusion function as roots from which harmful actions can develop.

### E5-AN-363
**Reviewed range:** AN 3.

### E5-AN-364
**Reviewed range:** AN 3.

### E5-AN-365

### E5-AN-371
