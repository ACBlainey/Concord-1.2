# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-98 — N5 Minimum-Stability Attacks: BR5 Resources and BR6 Correctability

## BR5 elimination attempt
Hypothesis:

[
BR5 stackrel{?}{=} BR1 + BR3 + BR4
]

Non-taking is largely captured by agency/non-domination and harm. Aid under scarcity is captured by care. Exploitative acquisition is captured by harm/agency.

### Residual test
Consider prudent use or preservation of legitimately controlled resources where:
- no immediate victim exists;
- no severe-need claimant is present;
- no coercion occurs;
- waste or negligent depletion nonetheless destroys future capacity.

BR1/BR3/BR4 do not cleanly require stewardship without broadening “harm” to absorb every future resource inefficiency.

**Decision: BR5 SURVIVES.**

## BR6 elimination attempt
Hypothesis:

[
BR6 stackrel{?}{=} BR2 + BO3
]

Truth plus recursive review can identify error and update behaviour.

### Residual test
They do not necessarily require:
- preserving mechanisms through which correction remains possible;
- permitting genuine reform after wrongdoing;
- repairing prior harm where possible;
- resisting irreversible closure when correction remains feasible.

To derive these, BR2 would have to be expanded from epistemic truth into institutional/moral corrigibility.

**Decision: BR6 SURVIVES.**
