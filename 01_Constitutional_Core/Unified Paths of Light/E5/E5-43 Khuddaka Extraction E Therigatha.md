# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-43 — Khuddaka Extraction E: Therīgāthā

## Status

**PRIMARY EXTRACTION.** E3, E4, candidate topology and Concord remain quarantined.

## Source-level propositions

### E5-THIG-001
Socially assigned status does not exhaust an agent's capacity for knowledge, discipline or ethical development.

### E5-THIG-002
Gendered expectations should not be treated as proof of cognitive or developmental incapacity.

### E5-THIG-003
Freedom from coercive or exploitative relationships can materially expand effective agency.

### E5-THIG-004
Severe loss, grief and vulnerability need not permanently eliminate the capacity for development.

### E5-THIG-005
Direct understanding of grief's causal conditions can change the agent's relationship to suffering without denying the reality of loss.

### E5-THIG-006
Past exploitation, poverty or socially degraded status need not permanently determine ethical worth.

### E5-THIG-007
A person's own testimony about lived suffering is relevant evidence and should not be erased by external status judgements.

### E5-THIG-008
Material dependency can constrain practical freedom; reducing exploitative dependency may therefore have ethical significance.

### E5-THIG-009
Sexual objectification and externally imposed valuation can conflict with development of self-directed agency.

### E5-THIG-010
Ageing and bodily change undermine attempts to ground worth solely in appearance.

### E5-THIG-011
Constructive teaching can restore practical agency where grief or prior conditions have narrowed perceived options.

### E5-THIG-012
Mental discipline can enable an agent to resist internalised social judgements rather than automatically reproduce them.

### E5-THIG-013
Renunciation of a harmful condition is ethically meaningful when chosen through understanding rather than imposed by another.

### E5-THIG-014
Ethical development can occur after major life disruption; developmental opportunity is not confined to an ideal starting condition.

### E5-THIG-015
Recognition of shared vulnerability can weaken status-based contempt.

### E5-THIG-016
Achievement should not be interpreted as proof that structural vulnerability was unreal; transformation and prior harm can both be true.

## Control note

The Therīgāthā contains unusually valuable first-person evidence about grief, gender, dependency, vulnerability and transformation. The extraction preserves these functions without silently translating them into modern political categories.
