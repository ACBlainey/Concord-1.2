# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-73 — Provisional Buddhist Ethical Catalogue A: P-B01–P-B30

## Status
**N0 OVERCOMPLETE CATALOGUE — NOT FROZEN.**

### Harm and response
P-B01 Refrain From Unjustified Killing and Deliberate Physical Harm  
P-B02 Extend Concern Beyond Immediate In-Group Boundaries  
P-B03 Do Not Answer Hostility by Reproducing Hostility  
P-B04 Interrupt Anger Before It Becomes Harmful Action  
P-B05 Reduce Conditions That Recurrently Generate Violence  
P-B06 Distinguish Unavoidable Pain From Avoidable Reactive Harm  

### Possession and material conduct
P-B07 Do Not Take What Has Not Been Given  
P-B08 Acquire Resources Without Deception or Exploitation  
P-B09 Protect Legitimately Acquired Resources Prudently  
P-B10 Use Resources in Balanced Relation to Real Needs and Responsibilities  
P-B11 Use Material Capacity to Support Dependants and Legitimate Need  
P-B12 Cultivate Generosity Without Converting Giving Into Status Competition  
P-B13 Reduce Possessive Attachment Where It Generates Fear, Conflict or Harm  

### Sexual and relational conduct
P-B14 Respect Sexual and Relational Boundaries  
P-B15 Do Not Exploit Vulnerability in Intimate or Dependent Relationships  
P-B16 Evaluate Relationship Conduct by Fidelity, Responsibility and Consequence Rather Than Desire Alone  

### Truth and communication
P-B17 Do Not Deliberately Communicate Falsehood  
P-B18 Do Not Use Speech to Manufacture Division  
P-B19 Do Not Use Speech to Humiliate or Needlessly Injure  
P-B20 Avoid Communication That Is Empty, Reckless or Irresponsible  
P-B21 Prefer Speech That Is True, Beneficial and Appropriately Timed  
P-B22 Use Communication to Reduce Rather Than Escalate Conflict  
P-B23 Preserve Meaning Over Verbal or Factional Victory  

### Epistemic discipline
P-B24 Do Not Treat Tradition, Hearsay, Text, Reasoning or Prestige Alone as Conclusive  
P-B25 Investigate Teachers and Claims Before Entrusting Confidence  
P-B26 Preserve Truth Without Claiming Certainty Beyond the Evidence  
P-B27 Recognise the Limits and Conditions of One's Own Perspective  
P-B28 Do Not Treat Debate Victory as Proof of Truth  
P-B29 Revise Judgement When Relevant Evidence or Experience Defeats the Prior View  
P-B30 Reject Malformed Questions or False Alternatives Rather Than Accept Their Premises

## Note
This catalogue is deliberately redundant. No attempt has yet been made to minimise principle count.
