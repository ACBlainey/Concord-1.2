# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-56 — Saṃyutta Nikāya Fine-Provenance Repair A: SN 1–11

## Status
**FINE-PROVENANCE REPAIR. NORMALISATION BLOCKED.**

## Why SN requires different treatment
The exploratory SN files recorded propositions against broad saṃyutta ranges. The Saṃyutta contains many short discourses and repeated formulas, so a thematic statement can be textually faithful while still lacking publication-grade occurrence provenance.

The repair therefore asks whether each thematic proposition can be tied to:
1. an identified discourse/passage;
2. a documented repeated family; or
3. an explicitly labelled synthesis.

## SN 1–11 repair findings

### Non-retaliation and anger
Early Sagāthāvagga material contains multiple verse exchanges concerning anger, hostility, restraint and response to abuse.

**Classification:** PV2/PV3.  
The exploratory propositions are retained as repeated-family/synthesis evidence rather than falsely assigned to one unnamed sutta.

### Generosity
Generosity and the consequences/qualities of giving recur in early connected-discourse material.

**Classification:** PV2.  
Do not collapse all gift passages into one proposition about modern distributive ethics.

### Companionship
Constructive association and the ethical effect of companions are present, but some of the strongest canonical formulations occur elsewhere in SN as well.

**Classification:** PV2/PV3.  
Retain the function while later provenance map records representative occurrences.

### Status and authority
Verse material repeatedly challenges superficial status, appearance or prestige as sufficient evidence of ethical quality.

**Classification:** PV3/PV4.  
Modern “authority” language is narrowed to prestige/status inference.

### Attention and mental-state regulation
Early SN supports restraint and recognition of mental states, but the first-pass wording about “early intervention” is analytic synthesis.

**Classification:** PV3.

### Speech and conflict
Truthful/non-hostile speech and avoidance of escalation are represented across repeated material.

**Classification:** PV2/PV3.

### Instability of power/pleasure
Impermanence and unreliability of worldly status/pleasure are strongly recurrent.

**Classification:** PV2.

## Audit judgement
No exploratory SN 1–11 proposition is yet granted PV1 solely from thematic fit.

No proposition is deleted merely because it was initially recorded at coarse resolution.

The provenance repair will maintain a distinction between:
- **verified theme**, and
- **verified individual occurrence**.

That distinction is essential before numerical claims are made.
