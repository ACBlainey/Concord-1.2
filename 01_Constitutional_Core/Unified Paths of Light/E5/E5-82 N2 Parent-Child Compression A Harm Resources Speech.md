# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-82 — N2 Parent-Child Compression A: Harm, Resources and Speech

## Status
**N2 PARENT-CHILD PRESSURE. COMPARISON QUARANTINE ACTIVE.**

## Harm family
A parent formulation can absorb several narrower applications:

**N2-H1 — Prevent, Reduce and Do Not Unnecessarily Reproduce Harm**

Children absorbed:
- refrain from unjustified killing/deliberate physical harm;
- non-retaliation where retaliation reproduces harm;
- anger interruption insofar as it prevents harmful action;
- de-escalation where equivalent protection is available;
- reduction of recurrent harm-producing conditions.

Retained separately:
- distinguishing unavoidable pain from secondary reactive harm;
- causal diagnosis and practicable intervention;
- consequence review.

These are operators for identifying or managing harm rather than merely instances of non-harm.

## Material family
**N2-R1 — Acquire, Hold and Use Material Resources Without Deception, Exploitation or Irresponsible Possessiveness**

Absorbs:
- non-taking;
- non-deceptive acquisition;
- prudent stewardship/balanced use;
- possessive attachment where it generates conflict.

Retained separately:
- generosity;
- positive support under dependency/severe need;
- material conditions constraining ethical agency.

These impose positive or developmental functions not entailed by mere responsible possession.

## Speech family
**N2-S1 — Communicate Truthfully and Without Needless Harm or Manufactured Division**

Absorbs:
- deliberate falsehood;
- divisive speech;
- humiliating/injurious speech;
- irresponsible/reckless speech;
- conflict-escalating speech.

Retained separately:
- truth/benefit/timing test;
- preserving meaning over verbal/factional victory.

The three parent formulations replace multiple domain children while preserving distinct operators and positive duties.
