## SN 1–45 running audit

The first Saṃyutta pass now reaches **SN 45**. Unlike DN/MN, SN contains a very large number of short discourses organised by thematic collections, so raw discourse count is not being mistaken for ethical principle count. Repetition remains evidence of emphasis.

Early SN material strongly reinforces conditional causation, trainability of reaction, non-retaliation, restraint of anger, social influence on development, epistemic proportionality, non-possessive treatment of changing experience, and the coordinated nature of ethical training.
