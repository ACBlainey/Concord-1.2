# Important observation

Several of these 49 are clearly not the same *kind* of thing.

For example:
- “prevent harm” is a root constraint;
- “review conduct recursively” is an operator;
- “fair use of common resources” is a domain application;
- “ethical capability is trainable” is a developmental proposition;
- “preserve continuity across leadership loss” is institutional.

Therefore the next compression must not simply ask which sentences resemble one another.

It must ask:

> **Can multiple domains be regenerated from a deeper principle plus an operator or condition?**

That is the N3 test.
