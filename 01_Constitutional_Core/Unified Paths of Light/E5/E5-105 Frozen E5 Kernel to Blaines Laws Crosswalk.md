## Key observation
All seven independently frozen E5 roots have clear functional representation in the eight-law Concord kernel.

The mismatch in counts is informative rather than problematic. Blainey's Laws separate some future-facing civilisational safeguards that the Buddhist corpus generates only compositionally or does not foreground as independent ancient-source roots:
- explicit anti-capture/evolutionary responsibility;
- recursive meta-governance;
- safe-space/property/privacy institutionalisation.

Conversely, E5 expresses **Care Under Need** and **Ethical Correctability** as unusually deep independent roots rather than leaving them solely distributed across other constraints.

## Interpretation
The Concord kernel appears to operate at a civilisational design layer, while E5's seven roots are the result of minimum compression of one ethical tradition's source-supported behavioural structure.

Their strong overlap despite those different purposes is therefore more informative than a simple one-to-one count comparison.
