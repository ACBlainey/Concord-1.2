## Additional regions
E3 foregrounds peace/social cohesion and dignity/equality as higher-order attractors.
E4 foregrounds bounded collective power/institutional continuity.
E5 foregrounds operators such as causal diagnosis, attachment checking and practice validation.

These additional regions are not absent from the other systems; they are often represented compositionally.

## CT0 result
Seven functional regions are substantively represented in all three derivations.

This does not yet prove seven shared roots. CT1 must formulate them conservatively and CT2 must test whether representation survives without semantic stretching.
