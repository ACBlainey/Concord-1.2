# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-72 — Blind Normalisation Protocol

## Status
**NORMALISATION OPEN. COMPARISON QUARANTINE REMAINS ACTIVE.**

## Objective
Compress the provenance-audited E5 Buddhist source propositions into the lowest stable set of ethically meaningful principles without consulting E3, E4, Blainey's Laws, the Decalogue project or the candidate invariant topology.

## Rules

1. Normalise by behavioural function, not vocabulary.
2. Source recurrence is evidence of emphasis, not principle multiplicity.
3. Preserve a distinction whenever merging would change what an agent is permitted, prohibited, expected or trained to do.
4. Separate:
   - ethical constraints;
   - positive ethical duties;
   - epistemic disciplines;
   - developmental practices;
   - composition/decision operators;
   - context-bound monastic or soteriological practices.
5. Do not manufacture modern rights language where the corpus supports only a narrower function.
6. Do not universalise monastic practice without a surviving general function.
7. Do not treat mental-state ethics as irrelevant merely because it acts upstream of observable behaviour.
8. Do not assume the final number of principles or attractors.
9. Do not consult quarantined comparison catalogues to decide what is missing.
10. Every later merge must state what distinction is lost or preserved.

## Compression sequence

**N0** — deliberately overcomplete functional catalogue.  
**N1** — exact/near-duplicate merge.  
**N2** — parent-child pressure.  
**N3** — cross-domain merge pressure.  
**N4** — root/operator/context separation.  
**N5** — minimum-stability adversarial merge.  
**Freeze** — only when further compression causes meaningful behavioural loss.

- E3 Q catalogue;
- E3 A attractors;
- E4 J catalogue;
- E4 JA attractors;
- Blainey's Laws;

- candidate deeper ethical topology.
