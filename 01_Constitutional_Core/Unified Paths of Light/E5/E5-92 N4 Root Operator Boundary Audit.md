# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-92 — N4 Root–Operator Boundary Audit

## Status
**N4 ARCHITECTURAL CLASSIFICATION. COMPARISON QUARANTINE ACTIVE.**

A root specifies a substantive ethical constraint or duty. An operator specifies how roots are interpreted, applied, tested or corrected.

## Root review
BR1 Harm — root. Removing it permits otherwise competent systems to inflict avoidable serious harm.

BR2 Truth and Corrigible Knowledge — root. It constrains both belief and communication, not merely the method of review.

BR3 Agency and Non-Domination — root. It prohibits coercive/exploitative disabling of effective agency independently of whether measurable harm is immediately visible.

BR4 Care Under Need — root. It creates a positive obligation not supplied by restraint alone.

BR5 Responsible Resource Relation — root provisionally. Stewardship and legitimate resource relation retain behavioural content not fully generated by harm, agency or care.

BR6 Ethical Correctability — **boundary case**. Its imperative to preserve repair/reform capacity is substantive; recursive review is procedural. Keep the root, but strip procedural wording into BO3.

BR7 Capability-Sensitive Development — root. It supplies a positive developmental duty and capacity-sensitive responsibility.

BR8 Integrity of Role and Practice — **boundary case**. Some content is generated by BR2 + BO8, but entrusted-role misuse remains substantively constrained.

## Operator review
BO1–BO8 remain operators. None independently specifies a complete ethical direction. For example, causal diagnosis can optimise harmful goals unless constrained by roots; proportionality can proportionately pursue a bad objective; recursive review can efficiently improve exploitation.

## Finding
No operator is promoted to root. BR6 and BR8 survive but are narrowed.

> **N4 ROOT/OPERATOR BOUNDARY: 8 ROOTS + 8 OPERATORS SURVIVE.**
