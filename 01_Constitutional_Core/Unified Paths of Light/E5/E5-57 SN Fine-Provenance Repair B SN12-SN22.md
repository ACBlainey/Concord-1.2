# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-57 — Saṃyutta Nikāya Fine-Provenance Repair B: SN 12–22

## Status
**FINE-PROVENANCE REPAIR. NORMALISATION BLOCKED.**

## Findings

### SN 12 — Nidānasaṃyutta
Conditional arising provides strong provenance for analysing harmful states through conditions rather than treating them as uncaused traits.

**PV1/PV2:** causal-condition structure.  
**PV3:** translating this into a general intervention methodology remains synthesis.

### SN 13 — Abhisamayasaṃyutta
Comparative images concerning the magnitude of what is understood versus not understood support epistemic humility only indirectly.

**PV3/PV4:** retain cautiously; do not turn soteriological comparison into a generic quantitative epistemology.

### SN 14 — Dhātusaṃyutta
Association according to dispositions/elements supports the observation that agents and environments can reinforce tendencies.

**PV2/PV3:** behavioural-developmental synthesis.

### SN 15 — Anamataggasaṃyutta
Long-horizon recurrence of suffering supports seriousness about repeated harmful cycles.

**PV3:** cosmological frame remains explicit; no universal civil rule is inferred directly.

### SN 16 — Kassapasaṃyutta
Simplicity, teaching integrity and concern about decline in authentic practice are supported.

**PV1/PV3:** preserve role-specific/monastic scope where applicable.

### SN 17 — Lābhasakkārasaṃyutta
Gain, honour and fame are repeatedly described as dangers capable of corrupting development.

**PV2:** strong repeated-family provenance.

### SN 18–19
Material is predominantly doctrinal/supernatural or context-specific for E5 ethical purposes.

**PV5-context:** do not manufacture general ethics merely to fill the range.

### SN 20 — Opammasaṃyutta
Attention to authentic teaching and vulnerability to attractive but less useful discourse supports transmission-integrity concerns.

**PV1/PV3:** avoid modern media analogies in the source ledger.

### SN 21 — Bhikkhusaṃyutta
Conduct, restraint and interpersonal behaviour among practitioners provide contextual ethical evidence.

**PV3:** monastic scope retained.

### SN 22 — Khandhasaṃyutta
Non-possessive relation to changing experience is strongly supported.

**PV1/PV2:** source-grounded developmental function.  
**PV4:** do not silently translate non-self into modern autonomy doctrine.

## Result
Thematic provenance survives. Several ranges appropriately yield little or no generalisable ethical material.
