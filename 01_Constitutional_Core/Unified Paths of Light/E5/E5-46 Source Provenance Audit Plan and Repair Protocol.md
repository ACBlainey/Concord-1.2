# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-46 — Source Provenance Audit Plan and Repair Protocol

## Status

**OPERATIVE REPAIR PROTOCOL.**

# 1. Objective

Convert the exploratory first-pass ledger into a traceable derivation record without changing its ethical conclusions to fit later comparison targets.

# 2. Audit order

### Stage A — DN
Verify E5-04–06 against DN 1–34.

### Stage B — MN
Verify E5-07–22 against MN 1–152.

### Stage C — SN
Revisit SN at finer canonical resolution. This is the highest-priority repair because the first pass was thematic.

### Stage D — AN
Revisit AN Books 1–11 at finer canonical resolution, prioritising propositions not already securely tied to identified passages.

### Stage E — Khuddaka
Verify Dhammapada, Udāna, Itivuttaka, Sutta Nipāta, Theragāthā and Therīgāthā occurrence provenance.

# 3. Record outcomes

Each existing proposition receives one of:

**PV1 — verified direct occurrence**  
The proposition is directly supported by an identified passage.

**PV2 — verified repeated family**  
The proposition accurately represents a repeated canonical formula; representative and recurrence locations are recorded.

**PV3 — verified synthesis**  
The proposition is a faithful synthesis of multiple identified passages but should not be represented as a single-source occurrence.

**PV4 — revise wording**  
The ethical function is supported but the current wording overstates or modernises the source.

**PV5 — remove**  
The proposition cannot be adequately grounded within the frozen primary corpus.

**PV6 — additional candidate**  
Audit reveals an ethically distinct source proposition omitted by the first pass.

# 4. Anti-confirmation rule

PV6 additions are permitted only because the source audit discovers them.

They must not be added to fill an apparent gap relative to E3, E4, Blainey's Laws or any hypothesised topology.

# 5. Translation rule

Where ethical interpretation depends materially on translation:
- record the disputed term;
- inspect at least one alternate translation or Pāli lexical evidence;
- preserve uncertainty if the functional proposition remains translation-sensitive.

# 6. Repetition rule

A repeated formula may establish emphasis without generating multiple principles.

The audit should therefore preserve both:
- **occurrence evidence**, and
- **semantic uniqueness**.

These must not be conflated.

# 7. Audit outputs

The audit should eventually produce:
1. verified occurrence ledger;
2. provenance map;
3. additions/removals/revisions register;
4. resolution-equivalence statement across collections;
5. final audit count under a declared counting rule;
6. explicit authorisation—or refusal—to begin blind normalisation.

# 8. Integrity principle

> **A slower traceable derivation is more valuable than a faster catalogue whose provenance cannot be reconstructed.**

The E5 experiment remains in quarantine until this audit is complete.
