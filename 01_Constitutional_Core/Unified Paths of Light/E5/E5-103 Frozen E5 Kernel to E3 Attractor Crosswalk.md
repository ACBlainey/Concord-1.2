## Reverse coverage
All seven E5 roots have substantive representation in E3.

E3 A6 Trust/Responsibility/Institutional Integrity and A7 Peace/Reconciliation are largely generated in E5 as domain compositions rather than deepest roots.

E3 A8 Self-Regulation/Resilience/Proportionality is divided in E5 between B7 development and reusable operators.

E3 A10 Dignity/Equality/Anti-Domination is compressed principally into B3 plus B1/B7.

## Interpretation
The striking result is not numerical equality. E3 has ten higher-order attractors while E5 freezes seven roots at a deeper compression level.

The stronger finding is **topological coverage despite different partition resolution**: every frozen E5 root lands in the independently derived E3 architecture, while several E3 attractors appear in E5 as compositions rather than roots.

This is consistent with the hypothesis that different corpora can partition a common ethical space at different resolutions.
