### E5-MN-091 — MN 1
**Normative status:** D/V. **Scope:** E/G.  
**Proposition:** Do not convert perception and conceptual possession into unexamined certainty; disciplined understanding includes loosening the tendency to treat experienced things as 'mine' or as extensions of self.

**Normative status:** D/V. **Scope:** E/G.  
**Proposition:** Use different responses for different classes of harmful influence: understand some, restrain some, use necessities reflectively, endure unavoidable difficulty, avoid preventable danger, remove harmful states, and cultivate beneficial states.

### E5-MN-093 — MN 2
**Normative status:** D/V. **Scope:** G/E.  
**Proposition:** Practical restraint should be proportionate to the causal route by which harm arises rather than applying one undifferentiated rule to every problem.

### E5-MN-094 — MN 3

### E5-MN-095 — MN 4

### E5-MN-097 — MN 5
**Normative status:** D/V. **Scope:** G/E.

### E5-MN-101 — MN 8
