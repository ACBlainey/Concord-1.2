# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-88 — N3 Cross-Domain Compression B: Operator Separation

## Status
**N3 ARCHITECTURAL COMPRESSION.**

Several N2 survivors are better represented as reusable ethical operators rather than independent roots.

## O1 — Causal Diagnosis
Identify conditions producing the harmful or ineffective state before selecting intervention.

## O2 — Consequence Horizon
Evaluate consequences across affected agents and across immediate and delayed horizons.

## O3 — Recursive Review
Observe action and result, compare with intended ethical function, and revise when warranted.

## O4 — Proportionality and Context
Match response intensity, timing and method to relevant conditions rather than assuming maximal intervention is best.

## O5 — Counter-Condition
Where a harmful pattern is conditionally sustained, cultivate a constructive counter-condition rather than relying only on suppression.

## O6 — Perspective/Attachment Check
Test whether identity, possession, status or attachment to a view is preventing accurate assessment or correction.

## O7 — Meaning Preservation
Distinguish substantive disagreement from verbal disagreement and preserve source meaning through transmission.

## O8 — Practice Validation
Test claimed understanding through observable practice rather than title, eloquence or assertion alone.

## Consequence
These operators can combine with multiple roots.

Examples internal to E5:
- R1 + O1 + O4 → proportionate harm intervention;
- R2 + O3 + O6 → corrigible inquiry;
- R3 + O4 → capacity-sensitive non-coercion;
- R4 + O2 + O4 → proportionate care;
- R5 + O2 → prudent resource stewardship;
- R7 + O3 + O5 → adaptive ethical development;
- R8 + O8 → role integrity.

This removes pressure to retain every procedural formulation as an independent ethical root.
