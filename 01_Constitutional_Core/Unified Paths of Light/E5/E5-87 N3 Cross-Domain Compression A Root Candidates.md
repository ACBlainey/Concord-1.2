# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-87 — N3 Cross-Domain Compression A: Root Candidates

## Status
**N3 ARCHITECTURAL COMPRESSION. COMPARISON QUARANTINE ACTIVE.**

The 49 N2 items are tested for whether domain-specific rules can be regenerated from deeper constraints plus context.

## Candidate root R1 — Harm Constraint
**Prevent, reduce and do not unnecessarily reproduce serious harm.**

Regenerates killing restraint, non-retaliation, anger-to-harm interruption, non-exploitative conduct, degrading-treatment constraints and parts of communication ethics.

It does not alone generate truthfulness, generosity, developmental cultivation or epistemic humility.

## Candidate root R2 — Reality/Truth Relation
**Maintain a corrigible relation between belief, communication and relevant evidence.**

Regenerates evidence-calibrated confidence, truthfulness, correction after evidence, resistance to prestige as proof, and parts of source/transmission integrity.

It does not alone generate non-harm or positive care.

## Candidate root R3 — Agency/Non-Domination
**Do not exploit, coerce or unnecessarily disable another agent's effective capacity for responsible action.**

Regenerates vulnerability non-exploitation, relational boundaries, reduction of coercive dependency, status-based incapacity controls and learner-practice protections.

## Candidate root R4 — Care Under Need
**Where serious need or reduced effective capacity exists, provide proportionate support when capable of doing so.**

This is not derivable from mere non-harm without converting omission into harm by definition. It therefore remains an independent positive-duty candidate.

## Candidate root R5 — Responsible Resource Relation
**Acquire, hold, use and share resources without deception, exploitation, destructive attachment or neglect of legitimate responsibilities.**

Regenerates non-taking, balanced stewardship, common-resource rules and parts of generosity.

Generosity beyond responsibility remains under pressure between R4 and R5.

## Candidate root R6 — Ethical Correctability
**Preserve the capacity to observe error, revise conduct and permit genuine reform.**

Regenerates recursive review, feedback, reform after wrongdoing and community correction.

## Candidate root R7 — Capability-Sensitive Development
**Treat ethical capability as developable and match demands, responsibility and instruction to relevant capacity and conditions.**

Regenerates trainability, learner-sensitive instruction, capability-sensitive responsibility and developmental opportunity.

## Candidate root R8 — Integrity of Role and Practice
**Require entrusted or declared ethical roles to be expressed in conduct rather than status, title or reward.**

Regenerates anti-prestige corruption, role integrity and conduct-over-status applications.

## Result
Eight candidate roots survive the first architectural pass. They are **not frozen** and no claim is made that eight is the final number.
