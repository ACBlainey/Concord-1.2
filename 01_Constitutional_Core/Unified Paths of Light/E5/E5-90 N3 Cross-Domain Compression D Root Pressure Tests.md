# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-90 — N3 Cross-Domain Compression D: Root Pressure Tests

## Status
**N3 ADVERSARIAL ROOT TEST.**

## Can R4 Care collapse into R1 Harm?
No.

A system may refrain from directly harming a vulnerable person while refusing proportionate aid under severe need. The behavioural outputs differ. R4 survives.

## Can R5 Resources collapse into R1/R3?
Not completely.

Non-taking and exploitation are partly generated by harm/agency, but stewardship, balanced use and responsibility for entrusted resources retain independent behavioural content. R5 survives provisionally.

## Can R6 Correctability collapse into R2 Truth?
No.

Accurate belief does not by itself require institutions or agents to preserve mechanisms for repair, reform or recursive correction. R6 survives.

## Can R7 Development collapse into R3 Agency?
No.

Respecting present agency does not imply cultivating future capability or matching responsibility to capacity. R7 survives.

## Can R8 Integrity collapse into R2 Truth?
Partly, but not completely.

Truth constrains false representation, while entrusted-role integrity additionally constrains misuse of position for gain/status and demands operational alignment between role and conduct.

R8 survives N3, but remains a candidate for later root/operator reclassification.

## Can resilience be a root?
No at this stage.

Resilience under gain/loss is regenerated by R6/R7 plus attachment/perspective operators. It does not require a ninth root.

## Can companionship be a root?
No.

Constructive association is a developmental condition primarily generated by R7 plus O5/O6.

## Can gratitude be a root?
No.

It is retained as a domain virtue/relational practice but is not necessary to regenerate the wider ethical architecture.

## Result
Eight roots survive N3 adversarial pressure.

They remain provisional until N4 root/operator/context separation and N5 minimum-stability testing.
