## Extraction records

### E5-AN-397
**Reviewed range:** AN 6.  
**Proposition:** Before teaching or advising, examine whether the communication is timely, true, gentle, useful, grounded in goodwill and appropriate to the listener.

### E5-AN-398
**Reviewed range:** AN 6.  
**Proposition:** A community deteriorates when members become quarrelsome, contemptuous, resistant to correction or inattentive to shared training.

### E5-AN-399
**Reviewed range:** AN 6.  
**Proposition:** Communal stability is supported by bodily, verbal and mental goodwill, fair sharing, compatible ethical conduct and shared understanding.

### E5-AN-400
**Reviewed range:** AN 6.  
**Proposition:** Generosity should be assessed not only by object transferred but by intention before, during and after giving.

### E5-AN-401
**Reviewed range:** AN 6.  
**Proposition:** A gift can support both recipient welfare and development of reduced possessiveness in the giver.

### E5-AN-402
**Reviewed range:** AN 6.  
**Proposition:** Remembering one's own beneficial conduct can support confidence, but should not become superiority over others.

### E5-AN-403
**Reviewed range:** AN 6.  
**Proposition:** Ethical conduct is strengthened when agents recognise consequences extending beyond immediate gratification.

### E5-AN-404
**Reviewed range:** AN 6.  
**Proposition:** A person should inspect whether greed, hostility and confusion are present and whether practices actually reduce them.

### E5-AN-405
**Reviewed range:** AN 6.

### E5-AN-406
**Reviewed range:** AN 6.

### E5-AN-407
**Reviewed range:** AN 6.

### E5-AN-408
**Reviewed range:** AN 6.
