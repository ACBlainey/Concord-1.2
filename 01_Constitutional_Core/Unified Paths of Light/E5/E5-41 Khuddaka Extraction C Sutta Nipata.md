## Extraction records

### E5-K-499
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** Refrain from harming living beings, whether strong or weak, visible or unseen, near or far; goodwill is deliberately extended beyond immediate in-group boundaries.

### E5-K-500
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** Cultivate concern for others with the intensity of protecting something deeply valued, while avoiding possessive domination.

### E5-K-501
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** Anger, contempt and humiliating speech undermine peaceful coexistence.

### E5-K-502
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** Do not deceive another or despise them because of status, difference or weakness.

### E5-K-503
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** Birth does not make a person ethically superior; conduct is a more relevant basis of evaluation.

### E5-K-504
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** Possessiveness over views can generate dispute; intellectual non-attachment can reduce factional conflict.

### E5-K-505
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** A disputant should not treat victory in argument as proof of truth or worth.

### E5-K-506
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** Peaceful conduct includes restraint from killing, theft, deception and exploitative sexual behaviour.

### E5-K-507
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** Craving for possessions and status can generate guarding, conflict and fear.

### E5-K-508
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** Contentment can reduce the pressure to acquire at others' expense.

### E5-K-509
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** One should remain open to learning rather than treating prior opinion as invulnerable identity.

### E5-K-510
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** A competent teacher or adviser should communicate without manipulating the learner for gain or status.

### E5-K-511
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** Solitude may support development where social association is corrupting, but companionship is preferable where a genuinely constructive companion is available.

### E5-K-512
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** Ethical worth is not established by ritual purity, austerity or inherited category alone.

### E5-K-513
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** Speech should be truthful, non-abusive and directed toward reducing rather than manufacturing conflict.

### E5-K-514
**Reviewed source:** Sutta Nipāta.  
**Proposition:** A person's treatment of vulnerable beings provides evidence about ethical development that declarations alone do not supply.
