# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-68 — Khuddaka Provenance Audit B: Udāna and Itivuttaka

## Status
**SOURCE-PROVENANCE AUDIT.**

## Udāna
The first-pass themes concerning attachment, change, perspective and release from reactive fixation are supportable, but several are interpretive compressions of narrative-plus-utterance units.

**PV1/PV3.**

The statement that conflict arises when agents treat partial viewpoints as complete is retained as **PV3 synthesis**, not as a direct modern epistemic maxim.

## Itivuttaka
Strong recurring provenance exists for:
- greed, hostility and delusion as harmful roots;
- non-greed, non-hostility and clearer understanding as constructive alternatives;
- intentional action and consequence;
- goodwill/non-hostility;
- generosity;
- cultivation/abandonment;
- careful attention and development.

**PV1/PV2.**

## Scope control
Liberation-oriented claims remain explicitly soteriological where their behavioural function cannot be cleanly separated.

## Result
No frozen PV5 deletion.  
No PV6 addition.
