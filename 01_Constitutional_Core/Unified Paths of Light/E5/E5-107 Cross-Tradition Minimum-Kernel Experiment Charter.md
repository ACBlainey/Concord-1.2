# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-107 — Cross-Tradition Minimum-Kernel Experiment Charter

## Objective
Test whether the independently derived E3, E4 and E5 ethical architectures can be compressed into a shared minimum functional kernel without using Blainey's Laws as a target.

## Inputs
- E3 Qur'an-first frozen attractors A1–A10;
- E4 Judaism-first frozen attractors JA1–JA8;
- E5 Buddhist frozen roots B1–B7.

Blainey's Laws and prior candidate invariant topology are excluded from kernel construction. They may be compared only after the cross-tradition candidate is frozen.

## Rule
A candidate shared root must:
1. have substantive functional representation in all three derivations;
2. survive translation across different partition resolutions;
3. retain a distinct behavioural obligation under ablation;
4. not be created by merely broadening a label until everything fits;
5. distinguish root content from operator, condition and domain application.

## Procedure
CT0 map common functional regions.
CT1 formulate deliberately conservative shared-root candidates.
CT2 perform three-way representation test.
CT3 adversarial ablation/fusion.
CT4 freeze only minimum-stable survivors.
CT5 compare frozen result to Blainey's Laws.

> **THE TARGET IS NOT NUMERICAL AGREEMENT. THE TARGET IS THE LOWEST STABLE FUNCTIONAL INTERSECTION.**
