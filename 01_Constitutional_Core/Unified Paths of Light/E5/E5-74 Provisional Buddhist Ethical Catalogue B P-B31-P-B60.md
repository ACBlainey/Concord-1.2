# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-74 — Provisional Buddhist Ethical Catalogue B: P-B31–P-B60

## Status
**N0 OVERCOMPLETE CATALOGUE — NOT FROZEN.**

### Self-observation and correction
P-B31 Examine Conduct Before, During and After Action  
P-B32 Examine One's Own Faults Before Fixating on Those of Others  
P-B33 Identify Harmful Mental States Before They Become Automatic Behaviour  
P-B34 Match Intervention to the Condition Producing the Harmful State  
P-B35 Abandon Practices Shown Not to Produce Their Intended Beneficial Result  
P-B36 Replace Harmful Patterns With Constructive Alternatives  
P-B37 Preserve Capacity for Correction After Error  
P-B38 Treat Past Wrongdoing as Correctable Where Genuine Transformation Occurs  
P-B39 Judge Reform by Changed Conduct Rather Than Declaration Alone  

### Development and capability
P-B40 Treat Ethical Capability as Trainable Rather Than Fixed  
P-B41 Develop Attention as a Condition of Responsible Action  
P-B42 Develop Understanding Alongside Rule-Following  
P-B43 Match Instruction to Learner Readiness and Capacity  
P-B44 Preserve Learner Participation in Development Rather Than Substitute Teacher Authority for Practice  
P-B45 Maintain Constructive Qualities After They Arise  
P-B46 Restore Constructive Capacities When They Weaken  
P-B47 Distinguish Specialised Cognitive Attainment From Ethical Completion  
P-B48 Do Not Infer Ethical Superiority From Unusual Ability or Experience  
P-B49 Distinguish Capability From Wisdom  
P-B50 Know the Limits of One's Own Capability  

### Motivation and upstream causes
P-B51 Treat Intention as Ethically Consequential  
P-B52 Identify Greed, Hostility and Confusion as Recurrent Roots of Harm  
P-B53 Cultivate Non-Greed, Non-Hostility and Clearer Understanding as Counter-Conditions  
P-B54 Evaluate Internal Motivation as Well as Visible Outcome  
P-B55 Do Not Convert Ethical Discipline Into Pride, Prestige or Domination  
P-B56 Reduce Craving Where It Drives Exploitation, Conflict or Loss of Judgement  

### Resilience
P-B57 Prepare Cognitively for Ageing, Illness, Loss and Death  
P-B58 Do Not Treat Gain, Loss, Praise, Blame, Status or Disgrace as Reliable Measures of Ethical Worth  
P-B59 Maintain Ethical Orientation Under Adversity and Changing Fortune  
P-B60 Do Not Let Grief or Prior Harm Be Treated as Permanent Incapacity for Development
