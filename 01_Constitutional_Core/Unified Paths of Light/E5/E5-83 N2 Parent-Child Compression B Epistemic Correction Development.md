# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-83 — N2 Parent-Child Compression B: Epistemic Discipline, Correction and Development

## Status
**N2 PARENT-CHILD PRESSURE.**

## Epistemic family
**N2-E1 — Calibrate Belief and Confidence to Evidence While Remaining Open to Correction**

Absorbs:
- source/claim testing before consequential confidence;
- certainty no stronger than evidence;
- non-possessive relation to views;
- debate victory not proving truth;
- revision when evidence defeats a view.

Retained:
- malformed-premise rejection;
- meaning-versus-wording distinction;
- source-meaning integrity.

These concern reasoning structure or transmission rather than only confidence calibration.

## Correction family
**N2-C1 — Observe Conduct and Outcomes Recursively and Revise Ineffective or Harmful Practice**

Absorbs:
- before/during/after review;
- iterative re-observation;
- abandoning ineffective practice;
- preserving corrective feedback.

**N2-C2 — Preserve the Possibility and Capacity for Genuine Ethical Reform**

Absorbs:
- correction after error;
- restoration of weakened constructive capacities;
- reform after wrongdoing judged by changed conduct;
- developmental opportunity after failure/loss/degradation.

## Development family
**N2-D1 — Treat Ethical Capability as Trainable and Match Developmental Demands to Relevant Capacity and Conditions**

Absorbs:
- continued cultivation;
- capacity-sensitive instruction;
- condition/context/timing-sensitive intervention;
- capability limits.

Retained:
- attention as enabling capacity;
- understanding beyond rule-following;
- specialised attainment not equalling ethical completion;
- learner practice not replaceable by authority.
