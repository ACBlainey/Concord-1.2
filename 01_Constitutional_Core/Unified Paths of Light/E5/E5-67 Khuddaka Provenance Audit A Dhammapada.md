## Status

## Verified families
The first-pass Dhammapada propositions are strongly supported at verse/family level for:
- mind preceding/shaping conduct;
- hostility not being ended by reciprocal hostility;
- grievance rehearsal sustaining hostility;
- heedfulness versus negligence;
- self-examination before fault-finding;
- conduct versus empty speech;
- anger restraint/non-retaliation;
- conduct and understanding over age/appearance/status;
- restraint from killing;
- analogy between one's own vulnerability to harm and that of others;
- dangers of possessive attachment;
- value of corrective companionship;
- association effects;
- appearance/eloquence versus wisdom;
- victory/defeat and hostility;
- non-anger, generosity and truth as constructive responses;
- integrated bodily/verbal/mental restraint;
- personal responsibility for ethical development.
