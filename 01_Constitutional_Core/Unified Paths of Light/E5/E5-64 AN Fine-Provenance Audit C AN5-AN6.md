## E5-64 — Aṅguttara Nikāya Fine-Provenance Audit C: AN 5–6

## Status
**FINE-PROVENANCE AUDIT.**

## AN 5
Repeated and identifiable numerical teachings support:
- conditions for responsible speech/advice;
- receptivity to correction;
- reflection on ageing, illness, separation and death;
- legitimate acquisition and prudent use of resources;
- vigilance concerning ethical decline;
- recurring five-precept restraint families.

**PV2/PV3.**

**PV4:** broad claims about negligent governance exposing others to preventable harm require narrower attribution unless tied to a specific ruler/governance discourse.

## AN 6
Strong recurring material supports:
- communal concord versus quarrelsomeness;
- goodwill in body, speech and mind;
- fair/shared use of communal resources in relevant monastic contexts;
- generosity and intention;
- self-examination of greed, hostility and confusion;
- learning, reflection and practice;
- communication responsibility.

**PV2/PV3.**

**Scope control:** fair sharing in a monastic community cannot automatically be universalised into a complete civil distributive theory.

## Audit result
No frozen PV5 deletion.  
No PV6 addition.
