# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-58 — Saṃyutta Nikāya Fine-Provenance Repair C: SN 23–35

## Status
**FINE-PROVENANCE REPAIR.**

### SN 23 — Rādhasaṃyutta
Questioning is repeatedly used to clarify attachment, identity and release.

**PV2/PV3:** supports disciplined inquiry but remains doctrinally situated.

### SN 24 — Diṭṭhisaṃyutta
Fixation on speculative views is repeatedly connected with attachment and conflict/error.

**PV2:** strong repeated-family evidence for non-dogmatic relation to views.

### SN 25
Developmental transition and confidence are represented.

**PV3:** preserve soteriological scope.

### SN 26–27
Material concerning arising, fading and danger of attachment supports impermanence/non-appropriation themes.

**PV2/PV3.**

### SN 28
Accounts of advanced meditative attainment are not automatically ethical propositions.

**PV5-context:** no need to derive a general principle from specialised attainment.

### SN 29–30
Non-human/cosmological narrative material is retained as corpus context where relevant but not inflated into general ethics.

**PV5-context.**

### SN 31–32
Specialised narrative/disciplinary material supplies limited independent ethical yield.

**PV5-context/PV3.**

### SN 33–34
Concentration and causal conditions support trainability and condition-sensitive development.

**PV2/PV3:** avoid equating meditative competence with general moral competence.

### SN 35 — Saḷāyatanasaṃyutta
Sensory contact, feeling, attraction/aversion and reaction provide strong provenance for upstream regulation of conduct.

**PV1/PV2:** repeated causal family.

## Result
This block reinforces a methodological point: corpus coverage does not require every canonical division to contribute a new general ethic.

> **SPECIALISED MATERIAL MAY BE VERIFIED AND STILL HAVE ZERO ROOT-ETHIC YIELD.**
