## Pairwise fusion tests

### Harm + Care
Possible parent: welfare.

Fails because negative restraint and positive assistance generate different obligations. “Welfare” merely hides both inside a broader word.

### Truth + Correctability
Possible parent: epistemic integrity.

Fails because BR6 includes repair/reform and institutional openness to correction beyond belief accuracy.

### Agency + Development
Possible parent: flourishing agency.

Fails because present non-domination and future capability cultivation can conflict and require balancing. Fusion erases the distinction needed to detect paternalism.

### Resources + Care
Possible parent: stewardship.

Fails because responsible resource use applies without severe need, while care can apply without resource ownership.

### Harm + Agency
Possible parent: non-domination/non-injury.

Fails because coercion may be wrong without demonstrable injury, and harm may occur without coercion.

### Correctability + Development
Possible parent: adaptive development.

Fails because systems can be corrigible without a positive duty to cultivate capability, and developmental systems can be poorly corrigible.

### Truth + Agency
Possible parent: informed autonomy.

Fails because truth duties extend beyond consent contexts and agency constraints extend beyond information.

## Polarity test
