# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-69 — Khuddaka Provenance Audit C: Sutta Nipāta

## Status
**SOURCE-PROVENANCE AUDIT.**

## Strongly verified families
The first-pass extraction is well grounded for:
- broad goodwill toward living beings;
- restraint from deception, contempt and humiliating treatment;
- conduct rather than birth as a basis of ethical evaluation;
- conflict generated by attachment to views;
- restraint from treating debate victory as proof of truth;
- non-killing, non-taking and sexual restraint;
- craving/possessiveness as drivers of conflict and fear;
- contentment;
- learning without rigid identity attachment;
- conduct over ritual purity or inherited category;
- truthful/non-abusive speech;
- constructive versus corrupting companionship.

**PV1/PV2.**

## Wording controls
“Universalised goodwill,” “anti-humiliation,” and “learner manipulation” are modern functional labels. They may be useful during later normalisation but must not be presented as literal source terminology.

**PV3/PV4.**

## Important control
The Sutta Nipāta provides unusually compact ethical formulations, but compactness must not cause multiple verses expressing one function to become multiple principles.

No PV5 deletion.  
No PV6 addition.
