## Structural implication
E4's eight-attractor partition and E5's seven-root partition appear to cut substantially the same functional space differently.

The strongest divergence is therefore not presence/absence but **where correctability is located in the hierarchy**:
- E5 elevates it to a deepest root;
- E4 distributes it through several higher-order attractors.

This is a valuable target for later minimum-kernel testing.
