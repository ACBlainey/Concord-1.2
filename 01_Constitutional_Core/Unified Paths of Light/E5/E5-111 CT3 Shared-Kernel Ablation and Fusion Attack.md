# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-111 — CT3 Shared-Kernel Ablation and Fusion Attack

## Ablation
Remove C1: no general constraint against unjustified serious injury. **Loss.**
Remove C2: no independent truth/evidence constraint. **Loss.**
Remove C3: coercion/domination can survive where immediate injury is unclear. **Loss.**
Remove C4: positive aid under severe need disappears unless omission is redefined as harm. **Loss.**
Remove C5: stewardship/non-waste/future resource responsibility becomes ad hoc. **Loss.**
Remove C6: truth can identify error without requiring repair, challenge or correction mechanisms. **Loss.**
Remove C7: present agency/correction does not require capability cultivation or capacity-scaled responsibility. **Loss.**

## Fusion attacks

C1 + C4 → “welfare”: fails; restraint and positive assistance remain behaviourally distinct.

C2 + C6 → “correct truth”: fails; institutional/moral repair exceeds epistemic correction.

C3 + C7 → “agency”: fails; present non-domination and future capability development can pull in different directions.

C4 + C7 → “developmental care”: fails; adults in acute need may require care without developmental asymmetry, while development can be required without severe need.

C5 + C4 → “stewardship”: fails; resource responsibility exists outside acute need.

C1 + C3 → “non-harmful autonomy”: fails; harm without coercion and coercion without immediate injury remain distinguishable.

## Result
No candidate can be removed or pairwise fused without either:
- losing a three-way-supported behavioural distinction; or
- hiding the distinction inside an expanded parent term.

> **CT3 RESULT: SEVEN CANDIDATE ROOTS SURVIVE MINIMUM-STABILITY ATTACK.**
