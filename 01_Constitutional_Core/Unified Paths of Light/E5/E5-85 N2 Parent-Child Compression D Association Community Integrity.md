# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-85 — N2 Parent-Child Compression D: Association, Community and Integrity

## Status
**N2 PARENT-CHILD PRESSURE.**

## Association
**N2-A1 — Shape Association to Support Ethical Development and Correction**

Absorbs:
- constructive companionship;
- recognition that association reinforces tendencies;
- corrective friendship;
- strategic solitude where association is persistently corrupting.

Gratitude and support under vulnerability are not merely association-selection rules and remain elsewhere.

## Community
**N2-CO1 — Maintain Cooperative Community Function Through Deliberation, Correctability and Continuity**

Absorbs:
- regular cooperative deliberation;
- concord with necessary correction;
- useful procedure without procedural infallibility;
- preservation of shared knowledge/function across leadership change;
- conditions enabling correction of decline.

Retained:
- fair sharing of common resources;
- wording-versus-meaning distinction;
- entrusted-role corruption;
- respect for experience without infallibility where it operates as an epistemic rather than institutional rule.

## Integrity
**N2-I1 — Require Operational Conduct to Match Claimed Ethical Understanding and Entrusted Role**

Absorbs:
- declared teaching tested by conduct;
- behavioural rather than titular role integrity;
- ethical-role corruption for gain/status insofar as it violates entrusted function.

Retained:
- preservation of source meaning against distortion;
- authority not substituting for the agent's own practice.
