# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-95 — N4 Boundary Pressure: BR5, BR6 and BR8

## Status
**ADVERSARIAL CLASSIFICATION TEST.**

The three roots most vulnerable to reclassification are tested more aggressively.

# BR5 Responsible Resource Relation

### Attempted derivation
BR1 Harm + BR3 Agency + BR4 Care can explain many resource rules.

### Residual
They do not fully explain the corpus's positive concern with prudent acquisition, preservation, balanced use and non-wasteful relation to material capacity where no immediate victim or severe need is present.

**Decision:** BR5 survives.

# BR6 Ethical Correctability

### Attempted derivation
BR2 Truth + BO3 Recursive Review could produce revision.

### Residual
Knowing that one is wrong and possessing a review procedure do not themselves require preserving another agent's opportunity for reform, repairing prior harm where possible, or maintaining correction mechanisms against closure.

**Decision:** BR6 survives, narrowly defined around preserved repair/reform/correction capacity.

# BR8 Integrity of Role and Practice

### Attempted derivation
BR2 Truth + BO8 Practice Validation can expose hypocrisy.

### Residual
A role-holder may make no false claim yet still exploit entrusted position for gain, prestige or domination. The source corpus repeatedly treats corruption by gain/honour/status as a substantive ethical failure.

### Remaining concern
BR8 may ultimately be a domain-specific expression of non-domination plus resource/truth constraints rather than a deepest root.

**Decision:** BR8 survives N4 but enters N5 as the highest-pressure merge candidate.

## N4 conclusion
BR5 and BR6 are strongly retained. BR8 remains provisional.

> **N5 MUST ATTEMPT TO ELIMINATE BR8 BEFORE FREEZE.**
