## Why eight laws can arise from seven roots

## Strongest interpretation presently supported
The Concord Ethical Kernel appears to preserve all seven functions independently recovered from the E3–E4–E5 intersection while explicitly decomposing them for risks associated with property/privacy, developmental asymmetry, concentrated or recursively improving power, and corrigibility of governance.

This is consistent with the previously proposed fractal/compositional model of ethics:
- compression reveals recurrent roots;
- application under conditions regenerates branches;
- constitutional design names important branches explicitly;
- institutions further decompose them into operational rules.

## Limits
This result does not establish that the Concord is uniquely correct, that X1–X7 are metaphysical natural laws, or that three traditions exhaust human ethical evidence.

It does provide a concrete, falsifiable architecture for the next phase.

## Next research test
The strongest next step is **out-of-sample prediction**.

Freeze X1–X7 and their derivation rules, then examine a tradition not used in constructing the shared kernel. Before reading its detailed ethics, predict that its stable functional ethical structure should substantially represent X1–X7, while differing in partition, emphasis and domain elaboration.

Failure to recover one or more roots would be scientifically more informative than adjusting the kernel after seeing the new corpus.
