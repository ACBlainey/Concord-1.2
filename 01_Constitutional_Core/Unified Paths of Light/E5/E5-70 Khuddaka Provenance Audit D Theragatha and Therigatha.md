# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-70 — Khuddaka Provenance Audit D: Theragāthā and Therīgāthā

## Status
**SOURCE-PROVENANCE AUDIT.**

# Theragāthā

First-person and autobiographical verse strongly supports:
- recognition of one's own harmful dispositions;
- trainability of anger/craving/conceit;
- value of constructive companionship;
- strategic solitude;
- voluntary simplicity;
- danger of converting renunciation into status;
- repeated disciplined cultivation;
- endurance under adversity;
- practice rather than teacher authority alone;
- impermanence/status detachment;
- transformation after prior harmful conduct.

Most records are **PV1/PV3** because poetic autobiography frequently supplies exemplification rather than abstract prescription.

# Therīgāthā

Strong first-person evidence supports:
- development despite low assigned social status;
- rejection of assumed incapacity based solely on gender;
- transformation after grief, exploitation, poverty or dependency;
- practical significance of reducing coercive dependency;
- bodily ageing undermining appearance-based valuation;
- agency-restoring effects of learning/practice;
- resistance to internalised social judgement.

## Critical wording repair
The exploratory file occasionally uses modern language such as “structural vulnerability,” “self-directed agency” and “sexual objectification.”

These are retained only as **PV3/PV4 functional interpretations**. The publication ledger must preserve the underlying first-person situation rather than imply modern theoretical vocabulary appears in the source.

## Outcome
No record requires deletion solely because it is autobiographical. Narrative/first-person evidence is simply classified correctly.

No PV6 addition.
