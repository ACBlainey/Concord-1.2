# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-05 — Dīgha Nikāya Extraction B: DN 11–20

## Status

**PRIMARY EXTRACTION — SOURCE-GROUNDED.**

No comparison with E3/E4/Concord is permitted.

## Extraction records

### E5-DN-034 — DN 11
**Status:** V/E. **Scope:** E/G.  
**Proposition:** Intellectual problems should not be treated as solved merely by appealing to increasingly prestigious authorities; inquiry should reach the relevant basis of the claim.

### E5-DN-035 — DN 11
**Status:** V/N. **Scope:** E/G.  
**Proposition:** A person should be willing to recognise the limits of claimed knowledge rather than preserve authority through pretended certainty.

### E5-DN-036 — DN 12
**Status:** V/E. **Scope:** E/G.  
**Proposition:** Criticism should be answered by examining the actual basis of the charge and relevant evidence, not merely by attacking the critic.

### E5-DN-037 — DN 12
**Status:** D/V. **Scope:** E/G.  
**Proposition:** Ethical and cognitive development should be assessed longitudinally rather than inferred from a single display or claim.

### E5-DN-038 — DN 13
**Status:** V/E. **Scope:** E/G.  
**Proposition:** Repeated transmission or tradition does not by itself demonstrate that teachers possess direct knowledge of what they claim.

### E5-DN-039 — DN 13
**Status:** D/V. **Scope:** G/E.  
**Proposition:** A professed ideal should be reflected in the dispositions and conduct cultivated by its path.

### E5-DN-040 — DN 14
**Status:** N/V. **Scope:** G.  
**Proposition:** Ethical exemplars are represented through recurrent qualities rather than lineage alone; narrative authority must still be distinguished from universal prescription.

### E5-DN-041 — DN 15
**Status:** D/V. **Scope:** G/E.  
**Proposition:** Harmful social and personal outcomes arise through interacting conditions; intervention should attend to causal chains rather than isolated symptoms.

### E5-DN-042 — DN 15
**Status:** D/V. **Scope:** G/E.  
**Proposition:** Attachment and possessiveness can contribute causally to conflict, guarding, quarrelling and violence; regulating upstream motives may prevent downstream harm.

### E5-DN-043 — DN 16
**Status:** V/N. **Scope:** R/G.  
**Proposition:** Collective stability is supported by regular consultation and meeting rather than arbitrary unilateral action.

### E5-DN-044 — DN 16
**Status:** V/N. **Scope:** R/G.  
**Proposition:** Stable institutions should not casually abolish established legitimate procedures merely for immediate convenience.

### E5-DN-045 — DN 16
**Status:** V/N. **Scope:** R/G.  
**Proposition:** Communities should respect experienced contributors and preserve conditions that allow competent counsel to function.

### E5-DN-046 — DN 16
**Status:** V/N. **Scope:** G/R.  
**Proposition:** Social stability is damaged by coercive treatment of women and girls; abduction/detention against their will is presented as a condition of decline.

### E5-DN-047 — DN 16
**Status:** V/N. **Scope:** R/G.  
**Proposition:** Legitimate communities should protect worthy places, practices and persons from arbitrary neglect or deprivation where these sustain shared social goods.

### E5-DN-048 — DN 16
**Status:** V/N. **Scope:** M/R.  
**Proposition:** Institutional communities should meet regularly, act in concord, respect established procedures, and value experienced members.

### E5-DN-049 — DN 16
**Status:** D/V. **Scope:** G/E.  
**Proposition:** Teachings and claims should be checked against preserved doctrine/practice rather than accepted solely because a respected individual attributes them to an authority.

### E5-DN-050 — DN 16
**Status:** V/N. **Scope:** G.  
**Proposition:** Personal affection or grief should not erase the need to recognise impermanence and changing conditions; resilience includes preparing for unavoidable loss.

### E5-DN-051 — DN 17
**Status:** N/V. **Scope:** R/G.  
**Proposition:** Material prosperity and political grandeur are temporary; possession and status should not be treated as permanent grounds of worth or security.

### E5-DN-052 — DN 18
**Status:** V/E. **Scope:** G/E.  
**Proposition:** Claims about the moral status or destiny of others should not be made carelessly; epistemic authority carries responsibility.

### E5-DN-053 — DN 19
**Status:** N/V. **Scope:** R/G.  
**Proposition:** A ruler or powerful agent may have positive duties to provide conditions supporting welfare, security and constructive livelihood.

### E5-DN-054 — DN 19
**Status:** N/V. **Scope:** G/R.  
**Proposition:** Power is ethically evaluated partly by how it affects the welfare and security of those subject to it.

### E5-DN-055 — DN 20
**Status:** D/V. **Scope:** G/E.  
**Proposition:** Cultivation of benevolent intention is treated as ethically significant because inward orientation influences conduct toward others.

### E5-DN-056 — DN 20
**Status:** D/V. **Scope:** G/E.  
**Proposition:** Ethical development includes deliberate reduction of hostility and cultivation of goodwill rather than behavioural restraint alone.

## Local observations

DN 11–20 adds unusually strong material on:
- epistemic humility;
- authority versus knowledge;
- causal analysis of conflict;
- upstream motive regulation;
- consultation;
- procedural continuity;
- anti-coercion;
- institutional memory/verification;
- power and welfare;
- cultivated goodwill.

DN 16 is especially important for later institutional analysis, but no E3/E4 comparison is permitted at this stage.

> **DN 11–20 extraction block closed provisionally.**
