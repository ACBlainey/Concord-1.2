# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-54 — Majjhima Nikāya Provenance Audit D: MN 121–152

## Status
**SOURCE-PROVENANCE AUDIT.**

## Range findings

### MN 121–130
Material on absence/presence discrimination, developmental staging, means-end adequacy, conflict conditions and finite opportunities for correction is broadly supported.

**PV4:** several first-pass formulations were deliberately abstract. Retain them as synthesis rather than claiming isolated prescriptive wording.

### MN 131–140
Present-focused practice, cautious causal inference, conditional emotion and contextual giving are supported at functional level.

**PV4 pressure:** “giving should not become domination/humiliation” is broader than a clean single-passage formulation in this range. Retain only as a synthesis candidate, not direct occurrence.

### MN 141–150
MN 141 strongly supports diagnosis by problem/origin/cessation/path. Care under illness/vulnerability and condition-sensitive teaching are represented in the range. Greed/hostility/delusion remain recurrent evaluative criteria.

### MN 151–152
Continuing self-audit and developed sensory regulation are supported. MN 152 clearly distinguishes experiencing sensory valence from being governed by the response.

## PV5 review
No record is deleted solely because it is interpretive. However, the contextual-giving/humiliation formulation from MN 131–140 is explicitly downgraded to PV3/PV4 and cannot later be counted as an independently verified direct rule.

## Result
No frozen PV5 deletion. No PV6 addition.

> **MN 121–152 AUDIT PASSES SUBJECT TO RECONCILIATION.**
