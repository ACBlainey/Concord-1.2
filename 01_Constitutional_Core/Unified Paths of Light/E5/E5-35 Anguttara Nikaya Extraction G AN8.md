## Status

## Extraction records

### E5-AN-421
**Reviewed range:** AN 8.  
**Proposition:** Gain and loss, status and disgrace, praise and blame, pleasure and pain are unstable conditions; judgement should not be wholly governed by them.

### E5-AN-422
**Reviewed range:** AN 8.  
**Proposition:** Resilience involves remaining ethically oriented through changing fortune rather than treating favourable outcomes as proof of worth.

### E5-AN-423
**Reviewed range:** AN 8.  
**Proposition:** Household welfare depends on energetic competence, protection of legitimately acquired resources, balanced living and constructive relationships.

### E5-AN-424
**Reviewed range:** AN 8.  
**Proposition:** Longer-term household flourishing also depends on confidence, ethical conduct, generosity and understanding rather than material assets alone.

### E5-AN-425
**Reviewed range:** AN 8.  
**Proposition:** A lay agent can combine productive economic life with substantial ethical development.

### E5-AN-426
**Reviewed range:** AN 8.  
**Proposition:** Generosity should be proportionate to means; giving that destroys the giver's ability to meet legitimate responsibilities is not automatically superior.

### E5-AN-427
**Reviewed range:** AN 8.  
**Proposition:** A competent leader should understand both immediate welfare and longer-term development of those affected by decisions.

### E5-AN-428
**Reviewed range:** AN 8.  
**Proposition:** Mutual respect in intimate relationships includes fidelity, responsible resource management and reciprocal regard; historical role assumptions require later contextual audit.

### E5-AN-429
**Reviewed range:** AN 8.  
**Proposition:** Speech and action under conflict should be restrained from retaliation even when the agent believes themselves wronged.

### E5-AN-430
**Reviewed range:** AN 8.  
**Proposition:** A community should distinguish substantive ethical disagreement from competition for prestige or factional loyalty.

### E5-AN-431
**Reviewed range:** AN 8.  
**Proposition:** Ethical practice should remain robust when it produces neither praise nor visible reward.

### E5-AN-432
**Reviewed range:** AN 8.  
**Proposition:** Responsibility for dependants includes both material provision and conditions supporting their development.
