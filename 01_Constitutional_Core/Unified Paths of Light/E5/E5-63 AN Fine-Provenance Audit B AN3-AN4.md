# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-63 — Aṅguttara Nikāya Fine-Provenance Audit B: AN 3–4

## Status
**FINE-PROVENANCE AUDIT.**

## AN 3
Strong recurring families support:
- greed, hostility and delusion as harmful roots;
- their opposites as constructive roots;
- intention as ethically consequential;
- examination of action and consequence;
- confidence integrated with learning/understanding;
- cultivation and abandonment as active developmental processes.

**PV2/PV3.**

The first-pass statement that an action should be examined for harm to self/others/both is functionally consonant with early Buddhist analysis but should not be represented as one unique AN 3 formula without passage-level mapping.

**PV3/PV4.**

## AN 4
Strong families support:
- four distortions/biases involving desire, hostility, delusion and fear;
- temporal evaluation of pleasant/unpleasant actions and consequences;
- qualities of reliable friendship;
- household resource competence and balanced living;
- ethical/material dimensions of lay welfare;
- communication and practical judgement;
- differentiated instruction/capability.

**PV2/PV3.**

## Historical-context control
