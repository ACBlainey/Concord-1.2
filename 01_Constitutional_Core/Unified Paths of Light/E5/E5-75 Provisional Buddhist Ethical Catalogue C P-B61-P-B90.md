# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-75 — Provisional Buddhist Ethical Catalogue C: P-B61–P-B90

## Status
**N0 OVERCOMPLETE CATALOGUE — NOT FROZEN.**

### Status, dignity and social evaluation
P-B61 Evaluate Ethical Quality by Conduct Rather Than Birth or Inherited Status  
P-B62 Do Not Infer Wisdom From Rank, Age, Appearance or Eloquence Alone  
P-B63 Do Not Treat Socially Assigned Inferiority as Proof of Developmental Incapacity  
P-B64 Do Not Use Ethical or Spiritual Identity as Grounds for Contempt  
P-B65 Recognise Shared Vulnerability as a Constraint on Degrading Treatment  

### Friendship and association
P-B66 Prefer Constructive Companionship Where Available  
P-B67 Recognise That Association Can Reinforce Harmful or Constructive Tendencies  
P-B68 A Reliable Friend Gives Relevant Correction Rather Than Mere Approval  
P-B69 Support Companions Under Vulnerability and Changing Fortune  
P-B70 Use Solitude Strategically Where Available Association Is Persistently Corrupting  

### Community and institutional conduct
P-B71 Meet and Deliberate Regularly Where Shared Function Requires Coordination  
P-B72 Preserve Concord Without Suppressing Necessary Correction  
P-B73 Respect Useful Procedures Without Treating Procedure as Infallible  
P-B74 Preserve Shared Knowledge Needed for Continuity  
P-B75 Distinguish Disputes About Wording From Disputes About Meaning  
P-B76 Do Not Let Gain, Honour or Status Corrupt an Entrusted Role  
P-B77 Maintain Conditions That Allow Communities to Correct Decline  
P-B78 Share Common Resources Fairly Within the Relevant Community  
P-B79 Respect Experience Without Treating Seniority as Infallibility  
P-B80 Preserve Function Across Leadership Loss Rather Than Depend Entirely on One Person  

### Care and reciprocal responsibility
P-B81 Respond Constructively to Severe Need Where Capacity Exists  
P-B82 Protect and Support Dependants  
P-B83 Recognise Benefit Received and Practice Gratitude  
P-B84 Treat Reciprocal Relationships as Carrying Duties, Not Merely Claims  
P-B85 Do Not Abandon a Vulnerable Companion Merely Because Conditions Become Difficult  

### Agency and dependency
P-B86 Reduce Coercive or Exploitative Dependency Where It Constrains Effective Agency  
P-B87 Do Not Infer Incapacity Solely From Gender or Social Position  
P-B88 Preserve the Possibility of Voluntary Transformation  
P-B89 Do Not Treat Teacher, institution or ritual as a substitute for the agent's own ethical practice  
P-B90 Allow Developmental Opportunity After Failure, Loss or Social Degradation
