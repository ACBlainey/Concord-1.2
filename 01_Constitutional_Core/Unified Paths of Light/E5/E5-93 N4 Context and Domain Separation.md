# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-93 — N4 Context and Domain Separation

## Status
**N4 CONTEXT CLASSIFICATION.**

The remaining N2/N3 material is tested for whether it belongs in the root set, operator set, or the conditions under which those components generate rules.

## Recurrent conditions

**BC1 Vulnerability / reduced effective capacity**  
Activates stronger care, non-exploitation and capacity-sensitive duties.

**BC2 Entrusted power / role asymmetry**  
Activates integrity, truth and non-domination constraints.

**BC3 Scarcity / material dependency**  
Activates stewardship, care, consequence and proportionality.

**BC4 Conflict / provocation**  
Activates harm, agency, causal diagnosis, attachment checking and proportionality.

**BC5 Learning / developmental asymmetry**  
Activates development, agency, truth and practice-validation requirements.

**BC6 Collective coordination**  
Activates continuity, deliberation, shared-resource and correction rules.

**BC7 Uncertainty / incomplete knowledge**  
Activates calibrated confidence, recursive review and proportional response.

**BC8 Loss / instability / uncontrollable change**  
Activates correctability, development and attachment/perspective disciplines.

**BC9 Status / prestige / identity pressure**  
Activates truth, agency, integrity and attachment checks.

**BC10 Transmission across persons or time**  
Activates truth, meaning preservation, continuity and practice validation.

## Reclassified domain expressions
The following do not require independent roots:
- companionship;
- gratitude;
- fair common-resource sharing;
- leadership continuity;
- regular assembly;
- wording-versus-meaning disputes;
- resilience under praise/blame or gain/loss;
- non-monastic availability of development;
- specialised-attainment controls.

They remain meaningful derived rules or evidential applications.
