# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-59 — Saṃyutta Nikāya Fine-Provenance Repair D: SN 36–45

## Status
**FINE-PROVENANCE REPAIR.**

### SN 36 — Vedanāsaṃyutta
The distinction between unavoidable painful feeling and secondary reactive suffering is strongly source-grounded.

**PV1/PV2:** especially important for response regulation.

### SN 37
Gendered and household material requires historical-scope control.

**PV4/V5-context:** no modern gender doctrine inferred automatically.

### SN 38–40
Questioning, training and specialised meditative material support development but provide limited independent general ethics.

**PV3/V5-context.**

### SN 41
Lay-householder material supports generosity, ethical conduct and development outside monastic life.

**PV1/PV3:** useful scope evidence.

### SN 42
Speech, teaching and community interaction appear in mixed contexts.

**PV3:** retain only clearly separable behavioural functions.

### SN 43
Unconditioned/liberation terminology is primarily soteriological.

**V5-context:** not converted into a civil ethical root.

### SN 44
Undetermined/speculative questions support restraint about malformed or non-useful questions.

**PV1/PV3:** epistemic function is present but should not become blanket anti-inquiry.

### SN 45 — Maggasaṃyutta
The path's repeated structure provides strong provenance for coordinated right view, intention, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness and concentration.

**PV2:** repeated-family evidence.  
The structure demonstrates that ethical behaviour, cognition and training are treated as interdependent.

## Result
The strongest generalisable ethical yield in this range comes from response to suffering, lay ethical scope, disciplined inquiry and coordinated practice.

> **SN 36–45 REPAIR COMPLETE.**
