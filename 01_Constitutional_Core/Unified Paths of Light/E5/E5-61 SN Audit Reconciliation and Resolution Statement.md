# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-61 — Saṃyutta Nikāya Audit Reconciliation and Resolution Statement

## Status
**SN AUDIT CLOSED AT REPEATED-FAMILY / THEMATIC RESOLUTION.**

## 1. What was repaired
E5-56–60 revisited SN 1–56 after the exploratory pass had used broad thematic ranges.

The repair establishes that the main extracted functions are grounded in identifiable canonical families, while also identifying many divisions whose material is specialised, doctrinal, cosmological or context-bound rather than independently productive of general ethical propositions.

## 2. Resolution statement
The Saṃyutta cannot honestly be represented as having received line-by-line extraction of every short sutta.

Instead, its publication-grade claim is narrower:

> **All 56 saṃyuttas were covered, and candidate ethical propositions were verified at discourse-family/thematic level, with direct, repeated, synthetic and context-bound evidence explicitly distinguished.**

This is an appropriate resolution for a highly repetitive connected-discourse corpus provided that later normalisation does not treat recurrence-family records as independent principles.

## 3. Numerical discipline
The exploratory SN record count is **not** converted into an exhaustive canonical-occurrence count.

No claim is made that every repeated formula has been individually enumerated.

## 4. Substantive survivors
Strongly supported SN families include:
- causal/conditional analysis;
- restraint of anger and non-retaliatory response;
- gain/status as corrupting pressures;
- non-dogmatic relation to views;
- upstream regulation of sensory/reaction chains;
- distinction between pain and secondary reactive suffering;
- lay as well as monastic ethical development;
- coordinated speech/action/livelihood/attention;
- deliberate cultivation and balancing of mental capacities;
- systematic self-observation;
- diagnostic problem/cause/cessation/path reasoning.

## 5. Context-only material
Cosmological, supernatural and specialised meditative passages remain represented as source context but are not forced into the ethical catalogue.

## 6. Integrity judgement
SN: **PASS AT DECLARED FAMILY/THEMATIC RESOLUTION.**

This is deliberately different from claiming exhaustive individual-sutta extraction.
