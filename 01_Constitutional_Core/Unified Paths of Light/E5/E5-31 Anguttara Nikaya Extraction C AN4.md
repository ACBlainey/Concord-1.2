# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-31 Anguttara Nikaya Extraction C AN4

## Status

**PRIMARY EXTRACTION.** E3, E4, candidate topology and Concord remain quarantined. Records are provisional source-level propositions, not normalised principles.

## Extraction records

### E5-AN-373
**Reviewed range:** AN 4.  
**Proposition:** Consequential decisions can be distorted by desire, hostility, delusion or fear; impartial judgement requires guarding against all four.

### E5-AN-374
**Reviewed range:** AN 4.  
**Proposition:** An action may initially be unpleasant yet produce benefit, or initially pleasant yet produce harm; evaluate temporal consequences rather than immediate feeling alone.

### E5-AN-375
**Reviewed range:** AN 4.  
**Proposition:** Friendship is tested by behaviour under adversity, confidence, vulnerability and changing fortune rather than pleasant words alone.

### E5-AN-376
**Reviewed range:** AN 4.  
**Proposition:** A reliable friend restrains another from harmful action, supports beneficial action, shares relevant knowledge and does not abandon them in difficulty.

### E5-AN-377
**Reviewed range:** AN 4.  
**Proposition:** Household partnership is strengthened by shared ethical conduct, generosity and practical understanding rather than attachment alone.

### E5-AN-378
**Reviewed range:** AN 4.  
**Proposition:** A person responsible for resources should acquire them legitimately, protect them prudently, maintain balanced expenditure and sustain constructive relationships.

### E5-AN-379
**Reviewed range:** AN 4.  
**Proposition:** Material prosperity is incomplete without confidence, ethical conduct, generosity and understanding.

### E5-AN-380
**Reviewed range:** AN 4.  
**Proposition:** A community or decision-maker should know when to speak, what is factual, what is beneficial and how communication will affect others.

### E5-AN-381
**Reviewed range:** AN 4.  
**Proposition:** A competent agent should understand both task and limits of capability; responsibility should be matched to actual competence.

### E5-AN-382
**Reviewed range:** AN 4.  
**Proposition:** Different kinds of persons require different instructional responses; equal concern does not require identical treatment.

### E5-AN-383
**Reviewed range:** AN 4.  
**Proposition:** One should examine whether a practice leads to welfare for oneself and others over time rather than merely conforming to convention.

### E5-AN-384
**Reviewed range:** AN 4.  
**Proposition:** Ethical stability requires preserving beneficial qualities against foreseeable decline, not merely achieving them once.

## Aṅguttara running audit

The Aṅguttara first pass now reaches the Book of Fours. Its numerical organisation creates substantial recurrence but also unusually explicit compact formulations about judgement, friendship, household responsibility, resource use, competence and developmental calibration. Repetition remains evidence of emphasis, not principle multiplicity.
