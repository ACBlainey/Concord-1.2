# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-51 — Majjhima Nikāya Provenance Audit A: MN 1–40

## Status
**SOURCE-PROVENANCE AUDIT. NORMALISATION BLOCKED.**

## Audit summary by range

### MN 1–10
The first-pass propositions concerning non-appropriative cognition, differentiated responses to harmful influences, role integrity, self-examination, correctability, mental corruptions, replacement of harmful conduct, causal analysis and mindfulness are substantially source-grounded.

**PV4 pressure:** MN 1's wording about conceptual possession is interpretive and should remain close to the source's treatment of conceiving/appropriation rather than modern ownership language.

### MN 11–20
Material on examining attachment, entrenched motivation, correctability, environmental conditions, conceptual proliferation, repeated thought and graduated intervention is supported.

**PV1/PV3 distinction:** several records are faithful syntheses of discourse arguments rather than isolated prescriptions and must be labelled accordingly.

### MN 21–30
Non-retaliation under provocation, functional use of teachings, interpretation responsibility, multiple interventions, means-end testing, staged confidence and critique of gain/honour are strongly represented.

**Important provenance repair:** the extreme-provocation/non-retaliation material belongs securely to **MN 21 Kakacūpama**. This confirms why similar language in the original DN 21 record required narrowing.

### MN 31–40
Communal goodwill/fair sharing, plural excellence, entrusted care, warning recognition, debate accountability, rejection of ineffective self-mortification, source attribution and role-through-conduct are substantially supported.

## Audit classifications

- **PV1/PV2:** majority of records in MN 1–40.
- **PV3:** recurrent for broad developmental summaries.
- **PV4:** required where modern systems language exceeds source wording.
- **PV5:** no deletion frozen in this first MN audit block.
- **PV6:** none added.

## Cross-file provenance correction

This audit confirms a useful mechanical finding:

> A proposition may be valid somewhere in the corpus yet still be invalidly attributed to a particular passage.

The DN audit therefore correctly removed/narrowed records rather than rescuing them using MN evidence.
