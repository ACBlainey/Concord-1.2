## Purpose

E11 examines a comparatively recent religious tradition with a substantial and well-developed textual corpus.

The experiment is intended to test whether a later religious tradition produces:
- a genuinely additional root ethical principle;
- a reinterpretation or repartitioning of older ethical roots;
- new compound ethical structures;
- different weighting or emphasis;
- or substantial convergence with the existing Unified Paths kernel.

## Terminology

The experiment should distinguish the historical/religious movement, its denominations and its source texts rather than treating “Mormonism” as a single undifferentiated textual object. Corpus boundaries must be explicitly frozen before extraction.

## Experimental Position

E11 is an **out-of-sample later-religion comparison**. It does not alter the frozen E2–E9 results retrospectively.
