# Foundation Papers

This directory contains the principal source papers and foundational documents from which the current Concord framework was developed.

These documents are retained here because they provide important intellectual foundations, source material, or early formulations for principles and mechanisms incorporated into the Concord.

## Unified Paths of Light — Origin of the Ethical Foundation

The **Unified Paths of Light** is an important origin point in the development of the Concord's ethical framework.

It represents an extensive earlier work in which ethical teachings were collected, compared, harmonised and examined. The principal Concordian output of this work was the development of **Blainey's Laws**, which subsequently became the ethical kernel from which the wider constitutional framework was developed.

The complete Unified Paths of Light work is **not duplicated in this repository**.

The full work consists of more than 800 pages and is preserved in the original Concord V1 repository as part of the project's development history and provenance. Copying the entire work into V1.1 would substantially increase the size of the canonical repository without adding corresponding operational value.

Accordingly:

> **Unified Paths of Light is treated as a referenced historical source, rather than a current V1.1 foundation document.**

The development chain is:

```text
Unified Paths of Light
        ↓
Harmonisation and examination of ethical teachings
        ↓
Blainey's Laws
        ↓
Ethical Kernel
        ↓
Constitutional Constraints
        ↓
Concord Architecture
```

The complete historical source can therefore be found in the **Concord V1 archive**.

V1.1 preserves the resulting foundational documents and principles while maintaining a clear reference to their origin.

## Status of Foundation Material

Foundation papers should not automatically be interpreted as current operational policy. They may represent:

- historical source material;
- original theoretical foundations;
- early formulations of Concordian principles;
- methodological foundations;
- ethical source material; or
- concepts subsequently developed into current Concord architecture.

Where a foundation paper has been superseded or substantially developed, the current Concord document takes precedence.

The purpose of this directory is therefore **provenance and intellectual continuity**, not the preservation of every historical document inside the current operational architecture.

## Relationship to Concord V1

The original **Concord V1 repository** remains the complete development and provenance archive.

V1.1 is the current canonical architecture and intentionally does not duplicate every historical source.

Where an important foundation is retained only in V1, this repository should reference its origin rather than reproduce it unnecessarily.

This approach follows a broader Concord principle:

> **Preserve the knowledge required to understand the present without requiring the present to carry everything that came before it.**
