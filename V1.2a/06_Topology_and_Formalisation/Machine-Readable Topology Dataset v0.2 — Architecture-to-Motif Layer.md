# 1. Purpose

v0.2 adds recurrent architectural motifs as typed analytical nodes.

These motifs are not new ethical roots and are not constitutional rules. They are recurrent relational structures extracted from frozen derivations after quantitative similarity analysis identified candidate architecture families.

The layer is intended to test whether motif presence/absence can support structural diagnosis.

# 4. Extension Pass — A4, A5, A6 and A8

The same motif definitions were then applied to the remaining frozen derivations without requiring them to fit either original family.

## A4 — Provisional, Plural and Correctable Knowledge

Supported motif edges:

- M02 — institutional/expert/majority commitment does not self-justify truth;
- M03 — evidence/provenance governs warranted commitment;
- M04 — challenge and correction are explicit;
- M05 — prior commitment does not inherit truth when evidence changes;
- M06 — conclusions remain reversible;
- M07 — useful epistemic function persists while explanations/methods can be replaced;
- M08 — classifications/explanations remain provisional;
- M13 — consequential conclusions remain revisable.

## A5 — Adaptive Sufficiency, Agency and Stewardship

Supported motif edges:

- M01 — allocation mechanisms are constrained by legitimate functions and actual scarcity;
- M02 — resource control does not self-justify allocation power;
- M03 — resource reality precedes allocation;
- M04 — allocation power is accountable and correction is explicit;
- M05 — historical allocation legitimacy does not automatically survive changed conditions;
- M06 — mechanisms must change when scarcity/productive conditions change;
- M07 — legitimate allocation/stewardship functions persist while mechanisms can be replaced;
- M10 — contribution/capability may matter functionally without becoming moral rank;
- M11 — resource asymmetry/coercive dependency requires anti-domination;
- M13 — allocation mechanisms remain revisable.

## A6 — Agency-Preserving Developmental Support

Supported motif edges:

- M01 — support/substitution is bounded to demonstrated need and function;
- M02 — need does not create general authority over the supported participant;
- M04 — support decisions are contestable;
- M05 — existing support authority does not retain legitimacy after its justification changes;
- M06 — support/restrictions adjust as capability changes;
- M07 — legitimate support can continue while mechanisms and authority relations change;
- M09 — need/dependency does not erase standing;
- M10 — capability is domain-specific and consequence-sensitive;
- M11 — support asymmetry creates capture risk and requires safeguards;
- M12 — enabling environments/support can be necessary for effective agency;
- M13 — capability/support classifications are reassessed and corrected.

## A8 — Adaptive Functional Continuity and Regenerative Recovery

Supported motif edges:

- M01 — continuity protection begins with legitimate-function identification;
- M02 — continuity custodianship does not self-justify permanent authority;
- M03 — provenance/rationale and epistemic recoverability are explicit;
- M04 — inherited claims and constraints must remain challengeable;
- M05 — historical existence does not create automatic preservation entitlement;
- M06 — obsolete implementations may die and be replaced;
- M07 — this motif is explicit: functions survive while implementations can change;
- M13 — recursive renewal makes continuity mechanisms themselves replaceable.

# 7. Immediate Pattern

M01–M07 are not confined to the original F1 family.

They recur strongly in A4, A5, A6 and A8 as well.

Likewise, several F2 motifs extend outside A3/A7:

- M10 appears in A5 and A6;
- M11 appears in A5 and A6;
- M09 and M12 appear in A6;
- M13 appears in A4, A5, A6 and A8.

Therefore the first two quantitative families were not isolated motif islands.

They exposed two dense entry points into a wider architectural grammar.

---

# 8. Important Missingness Rule

A zero in this matrix means:

> **No source-grounded edge was established by this extraction pass.**

It does not mean:

> **The architecture lacks the property in reality.**

Before any zero is interpreted diagnostically, the relevant frozen source must be checked specifically for the candidate motif and then compared with independently recorded failure modes.

This rule is essential to prevent the topology from manufacturing gaps from incomplete encoding.
