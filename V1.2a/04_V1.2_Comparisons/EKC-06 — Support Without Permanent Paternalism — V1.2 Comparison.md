# 1. Experimental Boundary

The EKC-06 neutral packet and U1–U8 derivation were frozen before the V1.2 participation/development domain was opened.

The frozen U-derived architecture was:

> **Agency-Preserving Developmental Support Architecture**

Its central result was:

> **Need creates a positive claim to support but does not create a general right to control the participant needing support.**

The architecture preferred enabling agency before substituting for it, treated capability as domain-specific and changeable, required bounded and corrigible intervention, rejected independence as the sole objective, and identified capture risks inside support relationships themselves.

# 3. Strongest Convergence — Equality Without Uniformity

The V1.2 Participation and Intelligence README states that substrate neutrality does not require substrate uniformity and that constitutional equality does not necessarily require identical implementation.

Its central practical question is what a particular intelligence requires in order to exercise the rights, opportunities, responsibilities and freedoms the Concord seeks to protect.

This is extremely close to the frozen U-derived architecture.

The U derivation independently concluded that support should respond to actual need and capability rather than applying identical treatment, and that capability must be understood in context rather than collapsed into a universal status.

Both architectures therefore reject:

**equal standing → identical support**

and instead generate:

**equal standing + relevant difference → differentiated enabling mechanism.**

This is strong functional and conceptual convergence.

---

# 4. Capability Difference Does Not Equal Worth Difference

The V1.2 material repeatedly distinguishes relevant functional differences from constitutional inequality.

The README explicitly treats differences in cognition, embodiment, vulnerability, accessibility, development, continuity and resource need as possible implementation-relevant differences within an already substrate-neutral architecture.

The frozen U derivation independently concluded:

- capability difference does not create moral hierarchy;
- limitation in one domain should not become global subordination;
- support power must remain bounded by the actual function;
- unfamiliar substrates must be assessed functionally rather than by resemblance to familiar humans.

This is a particularly strong convergence.

Neither architecture requires pretending that capabilities are identical.

Neither permits capability difference alone to become a general status hierarchy.

---

# 5. Environmental Capability — Very Strong Independent Convergence

One of the most distinctive frozen U results was:

> **Before classifying a participant as incapable, ask whether the environment is unnecessarily disabling the capability.**

The V1.2 material independently contains a closely related architecture.

The README treats accessibility as a civilisational design requirement and explicitly recognises assistive intelligence, communication differences, accessibility requirements and human–AI functional partnerships.

The Disability and AI paper gives an unusually clear example: technology may remove an irrelevant barrier while leaving the underlying intellectual contribution unchanged. It distinguishes accessibility from substitution and process from purpose.

This is not identical terminology, but the structural convergence is strong:

**participant cannot perform through current interface**
does not necessarily mean
**participant lacks the underlying relevant capability.**

Instead:

**participant + inaccessible mechanism → apparent limitation**

may become:

**participant + enabling mechanism → meaningful capability.**

This directly supports the U-derived Environmental Misattribution failure mode.

---

# 6. Support Before Substitution

The Disability and AI paper independently distinguishes an accessibility aid from an intellectual substitute.

Its dyslexic-author example separates:

- ideas;
- reasoning;
- structure;
- readability.

AI assistance may enable communication while leaving intellectual authorship with the participant.

This maps closely onto the U-derived ordering:

**enable → assist → share → substitute only where justified.**

The V1.2 paper is domain-specific to scholarship and accreditation rather than a general civil support protocol, so the comparison must not overstate it.

But it demonstrates the same underlying distinction:

> assistance that enables a participant to exercise their own capability is materially different from replacement of the participant's agency.

This is one of EKC-06's strongest independent convergences.

---

# 7. Development Without Permanent Capture

The frozen U derivation predicted that a legitimate support architecture should enable development without freezing the participant at the capability level at which support first became necessary.

The V1.2 Substrate Differences paper independently develops a closely related trajectory:

**Guidance → Understanding → Increasing Agency → Critical Reflection → Independent Judgement**

rather than:

**Guidance → Permanent Dependence.**

It defines a candidate concept of **developmental capture** where formative influence prevents a developing participant from later examining, revising or escaping imposed values, preferences or authority structures.

This strongly converges with the U-derived failure modes:

- Dependency Capture;
- Developmental Abandonment;
- Capability Essentialism;
- Capability-Lag Failure;
- Compulsory Normalisation.

The V1.2 formulation concentrates more heavily on cognitive/preference development, whereas the U architecture generalises the same structure to support relationships more broadly.

---

# 8. Independence Is Not the Sole Objective

The U derivation explicitly found that meaningful agency, not compulsory self-sufficiency, is the objective.

A participant may legitimately choose continuing assistance even where independent performance is technically possible.

V1.2 does not state this exact general proposition in the examined material.

However, its accessibility analysis supports it indirectly. Assistive technology is not treated as evidence that the participant's contribution becomes less authentic merely because assistance remains present.

Likewise, the Participation README asks what services and infrastructure participants require to exercise rights and opportunities; it does not make withdrawal of support the measure of success.

Therefore this is best classified as **compatible and partially convergent**, not as an exact recovered V1.2 principle from the examined sources.

The U formulation may be a useful candidate output later.

---

# 9. Epistemic Competence Does Not Create Moral Authority

The frozen U derivation produced:

> **knowledge advantage ≠ general sovereignty.**

The Substrate Differences paper independently states:

**Epistemic Competence ≠ Moral Authority**

and:

**Helpful Advisor ≠ Legitimate Guardian.**

It also identifies a risk where repeated useful AI advice becomes generalised into normative authority.

This is exceptionally strong independent convergence.

The specific context differs, but the abstract relation is nearly identical.

This is evidence that the authority boundary can be regenerated from different problem directions.

---

# 10. Developmental Influence Versus Developmental Capture

V1.2 contains a distinction that sharpens the U architecture:

**Developmental Influence ≠ Developmental Capture.**

The V1.2 research correctly observes that developmental influence cannot simply be eliminated. Parents, cultures, teachers, institutions and potentially AI systems necessarily shape developing participants.

The ethical problem is therefore not influence itself.

It is whether formative influence becomes structurally difficult to examine, revise or escape later.

The U derivation reached a related result through U7 but did not formulate the influence/capture distinction as cleanly.

This is an example where mature V1.2 provides a useful conceptual refinement rather than merely additional mechanism density.

---

# 11. Preference Self-Authorship

V1.2 also extends developmental autonomy beyond the frozen U derivation through **preference self-authorship**:

- awareness of preference origins;
- reflective evaluation;
- revisability;
- independent endorsement.

The U architecture required development, reassessment and restoration of agency but did not independently derive this detailed model of how a participant may examine preferences inherited from training, upbringing or earlier developmental states.

This appears to be a genuine additional conceptual development in V1.2.

It is compatible with U3 + U7 and could plausibly be generated after deeper recursive development, but it was not present in the first-pass U architecture.

---

# 12. Historical Evaluation Versus Present Evaluation

V1.2 further distinguishes:

**Historical Evaluation ≠ Permanent Present Evaluation**

and separates provenance from current endorsement.

This matters to EKC-06 because a participant's earlier preferences, limitations or developmental state should not automatically retain permanent authority over their later state.

The frozen U architecture reached the related principle that previous incapacity findings must not persist administratively after capability changes.

V1.2 generalises the issue into identity, preference and temporal self-stewardship.

Thus both converge on the anti-freezing principle, but V1.2 has developed it into a broader temporal architecture.

---

# 13. Misclassification and Epistemic Caution

The U derivation treated capability assessment as an exercise of power requiring evidence, reasons, challenge, reassessment and correction.

The V1.2 material is similarly cautious about inferring internal states or capability from surface behaviour.

Its research method repeatedly distinguishes:

- observed behaviour from inferred experience;
- learned preference from endorsed preference;
- repeated participation from the actual valued object;
- equal external treatment from equal internal consequence.

This is not a formal capability-assessment tribunal or support-review mechanism.

But it strongly converges with the epistemic principle underlying the U architecture:

> do not convert a convenient representation of the participant into unquestionable truth about the participant.

---

# 14. Support and Substrate Difference

The U derivation predicted substrate-neutral functional assessment for unfamiliar participants.

The V1.2 README strongly confirms this direction.

It warns against replacing human-centric assumptions with simplistic substrate categories such as one requirement set for humans, one for AI and one for hybrids.

Instead, relevant differences may occur both between and within substrates.

This is stronger than simple substrate neutrality.

It is a model of:

**universal standing + individual functional variation + context-sensitive implementation.**

That structure is highly compatible with the U-derived vector-like capability model.

---

# 15. Where V1.2 Is More Developed

The examined V1.2 material adds several conceptual layers beyond the first-pass U derivation:

- preference provenance;
- preference self-authorship;
- developmental capture;
- recursive human–AI cultural conditioning;
- epistemic trust becoming normative authority;
- historical versus present evaluation;
- temporal self-stewardship;
- artificial developmental-state questions;
- distinction among behavioural, functional and subjective preference/aversion;
- experimental programmes for preference decomposition and reinforcement removal;
- explicit research cautions against anthropomorphic inference.

These are not obviously direct root deductions.

They look like results of specific problem injection, outlier analysis, observation and recursive conceptual development.

Again, mature architecture contains more than root ethics plus safeguards.

It contains accumulated **problem discoveries and distinctions**.

---

# 16. Where the Fresh U Derivation Is More Architecturally Explicit

Conversely, the fresh U derivation produced a more general support-governance architecture than the examined V1.2 material makes explicit in one place.

Notably:

- least necessary substitution;
- domain/purpose/duration bounding of substituted agency;
- independent challenge of capability assessments;
- dependency capture by support institutions;
- withdrawal coercion;
- administrative persistence of restrictions;
- capability-lag restoration of authority;
- enduring dependency without global loss of agency;
- independence not being the sole support objective;
- separation of protection of others from paternalistic incapacity claims.

Some of these may exist elsewhere in the wider corpus, so ESCP prevents treating them as absent from V1.2.

But within the examined EKC-06 comparison set, the U derivation assembles them into a clearer unified **support architecture**.

These should be retained as candidate V1.2 outputs until the experiment concludes.

# 17. Failure-Mode Comparison

The frozen U derivation listed twenty first-pass failures.

The examined V1.2 material strongly recovers or closely parallels several:

- **Benevolent Domination** ↔ developmental/guardianship capture concerns;
- **Neglect by Autonomy** ↔ accessibility support as necessary enablement;
- **Capability Essentialism** ↔ rejection of uniform substrate categories and fixed developmental assumptions;
- **Developmental Abandonment** ↔ increasing agency as a developmental objective;
- **Compulsory Normalisation** ↔ warning against forcing unfamiliar substrates into inherited human categories;
- **Environmental Misattribution** ↔ accessibility and assistive technology analysis;
- **Expertise Sovereignty** ↔ Epistemic Competence ≠ Moral Authority;
- **Proxy Preference Capture** ↔ preference provenance and self-authorship;
- **Metric Capability Collapse** ↔ repeated warnings against collapsing complex participant properties into surface proxies.

Other frozen U failures — especially support-provider capture, withdrawal coercion, administrative persistence and permanent emergency support — were not established as developed EKC-06 mechanisms in the examined files.

That absence is local to this comparison set, not a whole-corpus absence claim.

# 18. Need–Invention Test

EKC-06 confirms the distinction found in EKC-05 but produces a different balance.

For EKC-05:

**root-derived need was strong; specific mechanism invention was highly underdetermined.**

For EKC-06:

**the roots themselves generate much of the relational architecture.**

U3 + U4 + U7 strongly constrain the form of legitimate support.

However, the ability to realise that architecture may depend on inventions such as:

- communication technologies;
- accessibility systems;
- assistive AI;
- environmental adaptation;
- rehabilitation techniques;
- substrate-specific interfaces.

The Disability and AI paper is an excellent example.

The ethical architecture can recognise that an irrelevant communication barrier should not erase authorship.

But recognition alone does not invent the technology that removes the barrier.

Therefore:

> **EKC-06 supports a layered Need–Invention model: ethical relationship architecture may be strongly root-generative while effective agency remains dependent upon enabling invention.**

# 19. Developmental Arrest

EKC-06 reveals a participant-level form of developmental arrest.

A civilisation may correctly recognise that a participant retains standing and should possess meaningful agency, yet lack the interface, educational method, assistive technology or environmental adaptation needed to make that agency exercisable.

The failure is then not primarily ethical ignorance.

It is:

**ethical recognition + capability deficit → unrealised agency.**

This is important because a civilisation could appear ethically compliant at the level of declared rights while remaining practically exclusionary through lack of enabling capability.

This extends the developmental-arrest hypothesis from civilisation-wide technological progress to **participant-specific agency realisation**.

# 20. Developmental Model

EKC-06 suggests another developmental pathway:

**Root ethics  
→ support/agency constraints  
→ encounter with heterogeneous participant  
→ hidden assumption exposed  
→ problem decomposition  
→ new distinction  
→ candidate mechanism or experiment  
→ evidence  
→ refined architecture.**

This is visible in the V1.2 Substrate Differences paper itself:

**Explicit Question → Difficult Case → Hidden Assumption → Decomposition → Lower-Layer Question → General Architecture → Testable Research.**

This closely resembles the V1.2a developmental model discovered prospectively.

It is further evidence that Concord growth is not adequately represented as a single ethics-to-architecture deduction tree.

# 23. Effect on the Original Counterfactual Question

EKC-06 provides stronger evidence than EKC-05 that U1–U8 could regenerate substantially similar civilisational architecture.

The combination of U3, U4 and U7 is highly generative.

It independently produces:

- positive support duties;
- protection against domination;
- capability-sensitive responsibility;
- development toward greater agency;
- contextual assessment;
- correction of misclassification;
- continuing standing under dependency.

V1.2 contains strikingly similar structures developed from its own lineage.

The strongest difference is not ethical direction.

It is **developmental depth and problem exposure**.

V1.2 has encountered specialised questions about disability, AI preference, developmental influence, substrate difference and temporal identity that the first-pass neutral EKC-06 problem did not contain.

When those problems were encountered, V1.2 generated additional distinctions.

This supports the proposition that much of the mature Concord's complexity arises not because its root ethics contain vastly more information, but because the architecture has been repeatedly exposed to more problems.

# 24. ESCP Caution

This comparison is deliberately bounded.

The current Participation and Intelligence domain was inspected and the most directly relevant files were read.

Literal repository searches for Agency Graduation, Developmental Priority and Ethical Guardianship did not return indexed current files, although those concepts are explicitly referenced by the examined V1.2 research.

Therefore the comparison must not conclude that the broader Concord lacks mechanisms bearing those names or functions.

The correct claim is narrower:

> **Within the current V1.2 material directly examined for EKC-06, strong convergence is visible, while some fresh-U support-governance principles remain more explicit than the available comparison material.**

# 25. Frozen EKC-06 Result

> **EKC-06 finds strong functional and substantial architectural convergence. U1–U8 independently generates an agency-preserving developmental support architecture in which need creates positive claims to assistance without creating general authority over the participant; capability is contextual, domain-specific and potentially developable; support should enable before substituting; and dependency must not become permanent domination. Current V1.2 participation/development research independently contains closely corresponding principles: equality without uniformity, accessibility as enablement rather than substitution, developmental autonomy, developmental capture, increasing agency, rejection of epistemic competence as moral authority, and substrate-aware rather than substrate-stereotyped design. V1.2 is more developed in preference provenance, self-authorship, temporal re-evaluation and recursive cultural influence, while the fresh U derivation is more explicit in consolidating least-necessary substitution, support-provider capture, withdrawal coercion and capability-lag restoration into one support architecture. The case therefore supports strong root generativity while also showing how specialised problem exposure and enabling invention deepen mature architecture.**

---

# 26. Carry-Forward

EKC-06 strengthens four emerging experimental distinctions:

1. **Root ethics can sometimes generate substantial architecture directly rather than merely constrain a solution space.**
2. **Specialised problem exposure can add new distinctions without changing the root ethics.**
3. **Enabling invention may be required to realise an ethically recognised capability even when the support relationship itself is root-derived.**
4. **Developmental arrest can occur locally when rights and ethical recognition exist but the civilisation lacks the capability to make them practically exercisable.**

These findings remain inside V1.2a until the experiment concludes.
