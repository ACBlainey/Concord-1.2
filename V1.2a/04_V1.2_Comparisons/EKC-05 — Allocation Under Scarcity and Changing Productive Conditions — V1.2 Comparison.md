# 1. Experimental Boundary

The U1–U8 derivation for EKC-05 was frozen before the V1.2 comparison anchors were opened.

The frozen U-derived architecture was **Adaptive Sufficiency, Agency and Stewardship Architecture**.

Its core proposition was that resources should be coordinated so serious avoidable deprivation is constrained, meaningful agency is preserved, productive contribution can matter without becoming moral rank, common and future resources are stewarded, resource power cannot become arbitrary domination, and allocation mechanisms adapt when scarcity or productive conditions change.

The frozen derivation explicitly did not uniquely generate capitalism, socialism, markets, planning, currency, prices, wages, tax, profit, ownership, inheritance, universal income, rationing, contribution scores, need scores, resource credits, energy accounting, central banking or a specific monetary system.

Its pre-comparison prediction was that if mature V1.2 contained highly specific economic mechanisms, a substantial portion should require explanation through empirical modelling, engineering choice, external problem injection or failure-driven development rather than direct root deduction.

This comparison tests that prediction.

# 2. Post-Freeze Historical Provenance

After the U derivation had been frozen, but before the V1.2 anchors were opened, the researcher supplied historical provenance:

- the original Ratchet economic system was introduced through an existing paper written before its Concord integration;
- the practical problem was interoperability between existing economic systems / media of exchange and the Concord.

This information was not used to generate the frozen U architecture.

The V1.2 Ratchet paper independently states that Ratchet began as an economic and monetary proposal. It later describes its development approximately as:

**Initial monetary idea → questions about backing → useful work → contribution → evidence → provenance → trust → reciprocity → participation → general coordination architecture.**

It also states that the V1 archive and original local files preserve deeper documentary provenance.

The current V1.2 corpus therefore supports the broad claim that Ratchet entered as a monetary/economic idea and was subsequently transformed through recursive development. The precise contents and chronology of the pre-Concord local paper are not established by the two EKC-05 comparison anchors alone.

# 4. First Major Result — The U-Kernel Did Not Need to Invent Ratchet

The strongest EKC-05 result is not disagreement between U1–U8 and Ratchet.

It is the distinction between **ethical-functional necessity** and **mechanism invention**.

The U derivation generated requirements concerning sufficiency, vulnerability and need, agency, non-domination, stewardship, accountability, contribution without moral ranking, adaptation to changing productive conditions and future consequences.

Ratchet satisfies or operationalises many of these requirements.

But the U derivation did not generate Ratchet's particular monetary machinery.

V1.2 itself does not claim that those mechanisms are ethical necessities. Ratchet V4.2 repeatedly labels them candidate or experimental mechanisms.

Most importantly, V1.2 states that the economic layer is optional and that the coordination architecture can be tested without a new currency.

This strongly supports the frozen U-derived conclusion:

> **The roots constrain what a legitimate allocation system must protect and prevent more strongly than they determine how the economic system must operate.**

---

# 5. Need Versus Invention

EKC-05 therefore exposes a distinction that was not explicit in the initial experimental model.

A civilisation can derive a need without deriving the invention that satisfies it.

For resource coordination:

**Ethical roots → legitimate requirements**

but not necessarily:

**Ethical roots → specific currency / market / accounting mechanism.**

A mechanism such as Progressive Monetary Backing is better represented as a candidate invention introduced into the permissible solution space and then subjected to ethical, empirical and failure-based constraints.

The historical Ratchet path is provisionally better represented as:

**Coordination / interoperability problem  
→ monetary candidate idea  
→ Concord ethical and constitutional filtering  
→ recursive questioning  
→ expansion into contribution/evidence/trust/reciprocity  
→ failure analysis  
→ safeguards  
→ general coordination architecture  
→ candidate monetary layer**

rather than:

**Ethical kernel → direct deduction of Ratchet.**

This is consistent both with the post-freeze historical report and with Ratchet V4.2's own account of its development.

# 6. Functional Convergence

Despite mechanism-level underdetermination, there is substantial functional convergence.

## 6.1 Sufficiency and Need

The U architecture gives priority to severe avoidable deprivation and recognises need independently of equivalent prior contribution.

Ratchet V4.2 similarly refuses to equate current conventional productivity with entitlement to dignity or protection and explicitly retains the problem of provision for retirement, disability, illness, care and reduced earning capability.

Ratchet does not prescribe one final baseline-provision mechanism. Pensions, universal income, insurance, community support, public provision and hybrids remain possibilities.

The requirement is stronger than the mechanism.

## 6.2 Contribution Without Moral Rank

The U derivation allows contribution to matter while rejecting its conversion into moral rank.

Ratchet explicitly distinguishes effort, difficulty, contribution, usefulness, payment, capability, worth and authority. Economic contribution does not create constitutional authority.

This is strong convergence.

## 6.3 Resource Reality

The U derivation requires allocation to remain grounded in actual scarcity and productive conditions.

Ratchet explicitly states that monetary backing does not create physical resources and lists food, energy, housing, land, compute, materials and infrastructure as realities monetary representation cannot substitute for.

This strongly converges with U2 + U5.

## 6.4 Anti-Domination

The U architecture requires that control of resources not become arbitrary domination.

Ratchet addresses this through bounded treasury power, capture analysis, separation of economic power from constitutional authority, prevention of contribution-authority and reputation-authority feedback loops, recognition of local and central capture, and attention to practical rather than merely formal exit.

The mature architecture is much more detailed, but the function converges strongly.

## 6.5 Stewardship and Future Consequences

The U architecture treats commons and future consequences as legitimate constraints.

Ratchet connects monetary health to physical resources, ecological sustainability, resilience and long-term productive capacity.

Roadway rejects short-term expansion where it damages long-term viability and treats resource renewal and future adaptability as components of civilisational stability.

## 6.6 Adaptation to Changing Conditions

The U derivation places strong weight on adaptation through U8.

Ratchet's controlled-persistence principle allows beneficial changes to persist while harmful, obsolete or failed mechanisms remain reversible. Persistence weakens when circumstances materially change.

Dynamic monetary policy may respond to productive capacity and resource constraints. Roadway evaluates both system position and trajectory.

This strongly converges with the U requirement that allocation mechanisms change when scarcity and productive conditions change.

# 8. Failure-Driven Resilience

Ratchet V4.2 contains much more failure knowledge than the first-pass U derivation.

The frozen U derivation independently anticipated eighteen failure modes, including sufficiency creep, contribution capture, need gaming, metric substitution, administrative overreach, dependency lock-in, bottleneck capture, artificial scarcity, commons overconstraint, future-claim inflation, emergency persistence, allocation complexity, localism failure, centralism failure, automation lag, abundance shock, optimisation fragility and Ethical Goodhart Failure.

V1.2 contains close analogues to many of these and additional developed safeguards.

Particularly strong overlaps include:

### Metric substitution / Ethical Goodhart failure

Ratchet states that no metric should become its own objective and explicitly warns that capable AI optimisation can maximise measured contribution without maximising actual usefulness.

### Contribution capture / gaming

Ratchet anticipates fake contributions, contribution laundering, collusion, reputation manipulation and gaming of incentives.

### Administrative overreach / allocation complexity

Ratchet explicitly treats coordination overhead and governance through complexity as failure modes.

### Localism and centralism failure

Ratchet rejects both naive decentralisation and uncontrolled centralisation and identifies capture at both levels.

### Emergency persistence

Ratchet requires triggers, scope, duration, review and expiry for extraordinary measures.

### Optimisation fragility

Ratchet treats measurement as intervention and requires second-order-effect analysis.

The roots generated many of the same **failure categories** spontaneously, but mature V1.2 has accumulated substantially more detailed **failure-handling machinery**.

# 10. Fresh U-Derivation Contributions

The comparison is not simply mature V1.2 containing everything the fresh derivation found.

The U derivation places unusually explicit emphasis on several issues.

## 10.1 Scarcity Classification

The U architecture makes changing scarcity itself a distinct architectural layer.

Ratchet responds dynamically to resource conditions, but the frozen U derivation's explicit distinction among changing scarcity states provides a cleaner high-level lens for asking when allocation controls should tighten or relax.

## 10.2 Abundance Shock

The frozen derivation explicitly identifies the risk that mechanisms appropriate under scarcity remain after abundance changes their justification.

Ratchet contains adaptation and controlled-persistence machinery capable of addressing this, but **abundance shock** is a useful explicit failure formulation.

## 10.3 Future-Claim Inflation

The U derivation identifies a possible failure where claims made on behalf of future participants expand until present agency is excessively constrained.

V1.2's Roadway and proportionality concepts can address this, but the failure is usefully explicit in the U analysis.

## 10.4 Ethical Goodhart Failure

Ratchet strongly anticipates metric gaming, but the U derivation's framing is broader:

> operational proxies for ethical purpose can become the target and thereby displace the ethical purpose itself.

This may generalise beyond Ratchet.

These are candidates for later V1.2 reintegration review, but must remain inside V1.2a until the experiment concludes.

# 11. Roadway and Developmental Arrest

Roadway produces an important result for the Need–Invention hypothesis.

It explicitly says equilibrium does not mean stagnation and identifies a civilisational Roadway containing sufficient capacity for creation, adaptation, cooperation, correction, resource renewal, knowledge generation, technological development and long-term continuity.

It also recognises **safe stagnation** as a potentially rational temporary state under uncertainty.

This supports a distinction between destructive instability, temporary rational pause and long-term capability limitation.

Roadway aims to preserve the capacity for continued creation and adaptation, but it still does not guarantee that a required invention will occur.

Thus the comparison does not dissolve the Need–Invention Gap.

It sharpens it.

A civilisation can preserve the **conditions for invention** without possessing the power to guarantee invention.

# 12. Developmental-Model Interpretation

EKC-05 provides the clearest case so far for the expanded Concord developmental model.

The evidence is best represented as:

**Root ethics  
→ constrain legitimate resource relations**

plus:

**External practical problem  
→ need for economic/interoperability mechanism**

plus:

**Injected monetary idea  
→ candidate architecture**

then:

**Ethical / constitutional filtering  
→ contribution and participation constraints**

then:

**Recursive questioning

then:

**Failure exposure

then:

**Architectural generalisation  
→ Ratchet becomes general coordination infrastructure**

then:

**Empirical humility  
→ monetary mechanisms retained as experimental candidates.**

This is not a pure deduction tree.

It is a recursive evolutionary development process.

# 15. Effect on the Failure-Driven Resilience Hypothesis

EKC-05 supports the hypothesis but also modifies it.

The earlier model emphasised:

**Root ethics → first-order architecture → failure → safeguards → resilience.**

EKC-05 shows an additional path:

**Root ethics → recognised need / constraints**

while:

**Invention / external idea → candidate mechanism**

followed by:

**ethical filtering → failure exposure → safeguards → mature architecture.**

Therefore some mature Concord complexity is not merely stored failure knowledge.

Some is also **stored invention and engineering history**.

A better decomposition is:

**Mature Architecture  
= Root-Ethical Constraints  
+ Selected Inventions  
+ Engineering Choices  
+ Stored Failure Knowledge  
+ Environmental / Historical Adaptation.**

This is a major experimental finding.

---

# 16. Effect on the Need–Invention Hypothesis

EKC-05 provides preliminary support for the Need–Invention Gap.

The strongest evidence is not that Ratchet's monetary mechanisms are correct.

It is that:

1. the U roots generated the need for legitimate resource coordination without generating a unique economy;
2. V1.2 itself treats the monetary layer as optional and its specific mechanisms as experimental;
3. Ratchet V4.2 records an origin in an initial monetary idea followed by recursive expansion;
4. the researcher independently supplied post-freeze provenance that the Ratchet seed existed as an earlier paper addressing economic interoperability.

This is consistent with:

> **Need can be root-derived while the mechanism satisfying that need enters through invention.**

It is not yet proof that invention is fundamentally irreducible to recursion.

That stronger hypothesis remains open.

---

# 17. Effect on the Original Counterfactual Question

The original experiment asks:

> If the Concord had been developed from U1–U8 rather than Blainey's Laws, would substantially the same civilisation have emerged?

EKC-05 gives a more nuanced answer than the earlier cases.

At the level of ethical resource constraints and broad coordination functions, there is substantial convergence.

At the level of specific economic mechanisms, the experiment provides no basis for expecting the same architecture to emerge.

U1–U8 could plausibly support Ratchet.

They could also plausibly support materially different economic inventions satisfying the same constraints.

Therefore the mature V1.2 economic architecture cannot be treated as a necessary consequence of either kernel merely because it is ethically compatible with them.

This is exactly the type of underdetermination EKC-05 was selected to test.

# 18. ESCP Caution

This comparison examined the two sealed EKC-05 V1.2 anchors and the immediately visible Ratchet development statements.

It does not establish that every relevant economic, historical or developmental document in the wider Concord corpus has been examined.

In particular, the original pre-Concord Ratchet paper reported by the researcher has not been independently reconstructed from these anchors.

Therefore:

- the broad developmental pattern is supported;
- exact historical chronology beyond the examined evidence remains provenance to be recovered;
- absence of a mechanism from this comparison must not be treated as absence from the entire Concord corpus.

# 19. Frozen EKC-05 Result

> **EKC-05 finds strong functional convergence but substantial mechanism-level underdetermination. U1–U8 independently generates a constrained resource-coordination architecture centred on sufficiency, agency, contribution without moral ranking, stewardship, non-domination, accountability and adaptation. Mature V1.2 Ratchet strongly embodies these functions but adds a large body of specific economic inventions, coordination mechanisms and recursively accumulated safeguards that are not uniquely derivable from the root kernel. Ratchet's own development account and post-freeze historical provenance are consistent with a path in which an economic invention was injected to solve a practical coordination/interoperability problem, then ethically filtered, recursively expanded and hardened against failure. The result supports the developing distinction among root-derived necessity, enabling invention, engineering choice and stored failure knowledge.**
