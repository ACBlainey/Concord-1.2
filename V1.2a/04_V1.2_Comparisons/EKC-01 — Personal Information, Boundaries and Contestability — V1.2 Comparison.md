# 1. Comparison Rule

The EKC-01 U1–U8 derivation was frozen before the V1.2 comparison anchor was reopened.

This document therefore compares two already-existing outputs:

1. the counterfactual architecture generated from U1–U8 and the neutral EKC-01 problem packet;
2. the relevant V1.2 development architecture.

No feature discovered in V1.2 is retroactively added to the frozen counterfactual derivation.

---

# 2. Headline Result

EKC-01 shows **strong functional convergence with substantial architectural convergence**, but not identity.

The U1–U8 derivation independently produced most of the central functions in the V1.2 data/metrics architecture:

- participant informational boundaries;
- meaningful access to consequential representations;
- distinction between observation and inference;
- correction;
- contextualisation;
- contestability;
- provenance;
- purpose limitation;
- bounded institutional access;
- protection against informational domination;
- exceptional access only under constrained justification;
- preservation of historical provenance without treating obsolete representation as present truth;
- substrate-neutral protection;
- capability-sensitive usability.

V1.2 goes further in several implementation and civilisational-learning directions, especially anonymisation/aggregation, participant feedback as distributed audit, meta-metrics about metric reliability, Digital Twin integration, Reality Tree integration and explicit recursive improvement of the Metrics system.

The counterfactual goes further or is more explicit in several ethical-architecture directions, especially proportional informational power, downstream correction propagation, consent insufficiency under coercive asymmetry, and the general treatment of informational relations without requiring a property model.

The appropriate primary classification is:

> **C1 — Strong Architectural Convergence**

with secondary elements of:

> **C2 — Functional Convergence / Architectural Variation**  
> **C6 — Different Ethical Emphasis**  
> **C8 — Non-Kernel Dependency**

No direct tension was identified.

# 3. Direct Functional Comparison

## 3.1 Participant Informational Boundary

### U1–U8 result

The counterfactual derived a presumptive protected boundary around consequential participant information from U3, supported by U1 and U4.

It did not define all information as participant property.

### V1.2 result

V1.2 proposes that identifiable personal data should normally remain under the participant's primary ownership and control.

### Comparison

The protected function strongly converges.

The conceptual basis differs.

V1.2 uses a **primary ownership** model as its candidate formulation.

The counterfactual uses a **relational and non-domination** model and explicitly leaves legal or moral ownership underdetermined.

### Classification

**C2 / C6**

The same protective function is recovered, but the ethical route and legal framing differ.

This is the clearest substantive architectural variation in EKC-01.

---

## 3.2 Meaningful Access

### U1–U8 result

Participants normally require practical visibility of consequential information or representation affecting them, subject to justified confidentiality exceptions.

### V1.2 result

Participants should normally be able to inspect significant identifiable information, including source data, provenance, derived metrics, classifications and major predictive conclusions, with constrained exceptions.

### Comparison

Very strong convergence.

### Classification

**C1**

---

## 3.3 Observation, Inference and Context

### U1–U8 result

U2 generated an explicit requirement to distinguish observation, report, inference, prediction, uncertainty, dispute and correction.

### V1.2 result

V1.2 explicitly distinguishes recorded datum, interpretation and participant context and links this to Blaineyan Reasoning's observation/inference/hypothesis/speculation distinction.

### Comparison

Strong functional and structural convergence.

The V1.2 implementation is more deeply connected to its existing epistemic methodology.

### Classification

**C1**, with **C8** for the specific Blaineyan Reasoning interface.

---

## 3.4 Correction and Contestability

### U1–U8 result

Consequential representations must normally be correctable and their consequential application challengeable.

### V1.2 result

Participants can dispute data, provide reasons/evidence, request correction, add context and preserve unresolved disagreement as provenance.

### Comparison

Strong convergence.

Both systems distinguish correcting a fact from disputing its interpretation.

### Classification

**C1**

---

## 3.5 Correction Without Historical Erasure

### U1–U8 result

U2 + U6 + U8 generated preservation of historical provenance while permitting correction, supersession and changed current status.

### V1.2 result

V1.2 explicitly preserves original measurement → challenge → audit → correction → current record, retaining why understanding changed.

### Comparison

Exceptionally strong convergence, including the same underlying tension between correction and provenance.

This is one of the clearest places where U8 contributes to the counterfactual architecture.

### Classification

**C1**

---

## 3.6 Purpose Limitation

### U1–U8 result

A legitimate acquisition does not confer unlimited future legitimacy. Materially different reuse requires renewed ethical justification.

### V1.2 result

Information provided for one purpose should not silently migrate into another; possession for one legitimate purpose does not automatically authorise another.

### Comparison

Strong convergence.

### Classification

**C1**

---

## 3.7 Minimum Necessary Information

### U1–U8 result

The counterfactual derived proportional scope and duration: collection, access and retention should be proportionate to legitimate function.

### V1.2 result

V1.2 states a minimum-necessary-resolution rule: use only the resolution required for legitimate system purpose.

### Comparison

Strong functional convergence. V1.2 gives a more concrete metrics-specific implementation.

### Classification

**C1 / C2**

---

## 3.8 Institutional Access

### U1–U8 result

Information access should be function-linked and graduated; high informational power creates stronger accountability obligations.

### V1.2 result

Not every institution should access all personal data. Access should be determined by what an actor requires for a legitimate purpose.

### Comparison

Strong convergence.

The U-derived architecture makes the scaling relationship between informational power and accountability more explicit.

### Classification

**C1 / C6**

---

## 3.9 Exceptional Access

### U1–U8 result

Non-consensual access can be justified where a competing ethical requirement is sufficiently strong, but must be necessary, proportionate, bounded, attributable and reviewable where feasible.

### V1.2 result

The paper recognises security, judicial, emergency and third-party exceptions and states that exceptions should be defined and constrained, while leaving many details unresolved.

### Comparison

Functional convergence, but the U-derived architecture develops the exception logic more explicitly in this case.

### Classification

**C2 / C6**

This is not yet sufficient for a C3 finding because wider V1.2 emergency and authority architecture may contain the missing detail. ESCP therefore blocks any claim that bounded exceptional-access logic is absent from the Concord.

---

## 3.10 Anonymisation and Aggregation

### U1–U8 result

The counterfactual derives proportional scope, bounded access and purpose limitation but does not independently select anonymisation/aggregation as the preferred general civilisational mechanism.

### V1.2 result

V1.2 strongly prefers transformation from identifiable personal data to anonymised/aggregated civilisational information where identity is unnecessary.

### Comparison

The ethical requirement is compatible, but the specific mechanism was not independently regenerated.

### Classification

**C2 / C8**

Anonymisation appears to be a plausible implementation of U1/U3/U5 constraints rather than a uniquely kernel-determined architecture.

---

## 3.11 Distributed Citizen Audit

### U1–U8 result

The counterfactual requires correction, contestability and provenance but does not independently extend individual disputes into a civilisation-wide distributed measurement-audit architecture.

### V1.2 result

V1.2 explicitly treats participants as distributed auditors: repeated challenges become anonymised error signals, aggregate error patterns and system audits.

### Comparison

This is a meaningful V1.2 extension beyond the frozen counterfactual derivation.

However, it is readily compatible with U2 and U6 and may depend on an additional systems-learning insight rather than a different root ethic.

### Classification

**C4 provisionally / C8**

The function exists in V1.2 and was not regenerated in EKC-01. The likely explanation is non-kernel systems design rather than ethical incompatibility.

---

## 3.12 Meta-Metrics

### U1–U8 result

Not independently generated.

### V1.2 result

V1.2 proposes metrics about the reliability of metrics, including contestation rates, correction rates and model failure signals.

### Comparison

This is a specific recursive measurement mechanism beyond the U-derived architecture.

### Classification

**C4 / C8**

Again, it appears compatible with U2/U6 but not directly demanded by the roots.

---

## 3.13 Predictive Paternalism

### U1–U8 result

U3, U4 and U7 require assistance and consequential judgement to preserve meaningful agency; disagreement cannot simply be erased by asymmetric institutional power.

### V1.2 result

V1.2 explicitly rejects the self-sealing pattern in which disagreement with a predictive model is interpreted as confirmation that the model is correct.

### Comparison

Strong functional convergence, with V1.2 expressing a particularly concrete failure mode.

### Classification

**C1 / C2**

---

## 3.14 Substrate Neutrality

### U1–U8 result

Protections follow agency, vulnerability, consequence, function and capability rather than substrate label.

### V1.2 result

The paper explicitly anticipates substrate-specific metrics and unresolved issues involving AI copies, forks and collective intelligences, but its principal language is framed around citizens and participants rather than constructing the same explicit substrate-neutral rule in this paper.

### Comparison

Compatible, but the U-derived architecture states the general rule more explicitly within this case.

ESCP prevents treating this as a Concord absence because multisubstrate standing is foundational elsewhere in V1.2.

### Classification

**C6**, not C3.

# 5. Difference Attribution

## 5.1 Ownership Model

**Likely source:** design/legal framing rather than root ethical conflict.

U1–U8 does not require an ownership ontology to generate most of the same protections.

This suggests that “participant ownership” may be one Concord mechanism for satisfying a deeper ethical requirement rather than the only architecture compatible with it.

## 5.2 Distributed Metrics Audit

**Likely source:** secondary systems insight.

Nothing in U1–U8 conflicts with converting repeated correction signals into system-level learning, but the roots did not force that mechanism to appear in this bounded derivation.

## 5.3 Anonymisation

**Likely source:** technical implementation of proportionality and non-domination.

The U-derived system could adopt anonymisation, but the kernel did not uniquely select it.

## 5.4 Informational Power Scaling

The counterfactual makes explicit that safeguards should scale with effective informational power.

The V1.2 paper reaches similar territory through institutional access, privacy, anti-paternalism and reciprocal observation.

This is best treated as different emphasis rather than a new ethical function.

## 5.5 Temporal Continuity

Both architectures independently preserve history while allowing current representation to change.

This convergence is particularly relevant to U8.

# 6. U8 Comparison

The pre-comparison derivation concluded that U8 strengthened temporal architecture but was not necessary for the basic bounded-and-contestable information system.

The V1.2 comparison supports that assessment.

V1.2 already contains correction-without-erasure, provenance, recursive learning and persistent system improvement despite having been developed from the earlier kernel.

Therefore EKC-01 does **not** provide evidence that U8 uniquely generates these functions.

It instead suggests that continuity-like functions can sometimes emerge from combinations of truth/correction/stewardship architecture even when continuity is not an explicit root.

This is an important result for the later U8 analysis.

# 8. What EKC-01 Tells Us

EKC-01 provides an early indication that changing the root ethical partition does **not automatically produce a different civilisational system**.

A substantial amount of the V1.2 personal-data architecture can be regenerated from U1–U8 without beginning from its existing mechanisms.

At the same time, the experiment already distinguishes three layers that should not be collapsed:

1. **ethical functions strongly regenerated from the kernel**;
2. **architectural choices that implement those functions in more than one legitimate way**;
3. **secondary systems innovations that do not appear to be directly generated by the kernel alone**.

That distinction may become one of the most useful outputs of V1.2a.

---

# 9. Contamination Assessment

Terminological contamination risk is non-trivial because the assisting reasoner already knew the Concord contains concepts such as provenance, contestability and purpose-limited information.

Confidence is therefore highest where the feature follows directly from the U roots and neutral problem conditions, and lower where terminology closely matches prior Concord language.

The particularly strong convergence around correction, purpose limitation and provenance should therefore be treated as evidence from this experiment, but not as independent blinded replication.

Later fresh-instance or cross-model replication would materially strengthen the result.

# 10. EKC-01 Final Record

**Overall result:** Strong convergence with meaningful implementation variation.

**Primary class:** C1  
**Secondary classes:** C2, C6, C8  
**Bounded provisional class:** C4 for distributed audit/meta-metrics  
**Direct tension:** None  
**U8 dependence:** Strengthening, not unique dependence  
**Kernel-sensitive divergence:** Limited  
**Implementation underdetermination:** Significant  
**Replication priority:** Moderate

EKC-01 therefore does not presently support a systematic alternative-civilisation interpretation.

It does support the hypothesis that a different ethical partition can regenerate much of the same functional architecture while leaving room for different legal and technical implementations.
