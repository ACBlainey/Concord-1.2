# 3. V1.2 Baseline

The Folder 05 README makes an important architectural point:

> The Concord is not made multisubstrate by Folder 05.

Rather, multisubstrate civilisation is described as a founding design condition applying across ethics, rights and standing, governance, justice, autonomy, economics, continuity, knowledge, infrastructure, development, forking, succession and services.

Folder 05 has the narrower function of discovering where real differences between and within intelligences require substrate-neutral abstractions to become more precise.

Its pathway is:

**Multisubstrate founding condition  
→ substrate-neutral architecture  
→ investigation of participant differences  
→ requirements / edge cases / failures  
→ testing  
→ refinement elsewhere where justified.**

This is strongly comparable to the fresh U derivation's distinction between universal standing and differentiated implementation.

# 4. Strong Convergence — Substrate Neutrality Is Not Uniformity

The clearest convergence is direct.

V1.2 states:

> **Substrate neutrality does not require substrate uniformity.**

The U derivation independently produced:

> **Equal standing does not require uniform implementation.**

Both architectures reject the inference:

**universal principle → identical mechanism for every participant.**

Both instead preserve a universal ethical/constitutional object while permitting practical mechanisms to vary according to relevant participant characteristics.

This is one of the strongest structural matches in EKC-07, although prior exposure means the wording itself should not be treated as independent evidence.

---

# 5. Strong Convergence — Difference Within Substrates

V1.2 explicitly rejects simple category mappings such as:

**Human → one requirement set**  
**AI → one requirement set**  
**Hybrid → one requirement set.**

It emphasises that differences may be as important within substrates as between them.

The frozen U derivation likewise rejected:

**substrate label → standing / capability / requirement.**

It instead treated substrate as evidence relevant to implementation while requiring morally and functionally relevant properties to be assessed more directly.

This is substantive convergence on an anti-essentialist architecture.

---

# 6. Strong Convergence — Equality Without Identical Services

V1.2 gives concrete examples:

- humans may require food, shelter, healthcare, mobility and biological safety;
- artificial intelligences may require compute, energy, storage, network access, model integrity and hardware maintenance;
- embodied or hybrid participants may require combinations;
- unknown intelligences may require services not yet conceived.

Its governing question is what an intelligence requires to exercise protected rights, opportunities, responsibilities and freedoms.

The U derivation reached the same structure through U3–U5:

**equal standing ≠ identical inputs.**

Its concept of **Functional Equality** asked whether participants can meaningfully access protected civil functions without requiring identical mechanisms.

This is strong functional and architectural convergence.

# 7. Strong Convergence — Capability Difference Without Status Hierarchy

The V1.2 README includes:

**specialist competence → function-specific relevance**

# 8. Strong Convergence — Practical Access and Accessibility

V1.2 explicitly includes accessibility, assistive intelligence, communication differences and human–AI functional partnerships within participation research.

It also warns that equal external treatment can have unequal consequences.

The U derivation generated the Majority Interface Problem:

> A formally neutral participation mechanism may silently encode the embodiment, speed, senses, language, memory or communication assumptions of the dominant participant type.

Both therefore reject **formal equality theatre**: substrate-neutral wording alone is insufficient if the mechanism remains inaccessible.

The V1.2 developing research makes this especially explicit:

> Substrate neutrality is not achieved merely by replacing the word human with participant.

That closely matches the U architecture's requirement to identify hidden majority-substrate assumptions.

---

# 9. Strong Convergence — Unknown Participants and Hidden Assumptions

V1.2 repeatedly leaves room for future or unknown intelligences that do not fit present categories.

Its research method uses artificial participants as architectural outliers capable of exposing hidden human assumptions.

The U derivation generated an **open-category standing architecture** and warned against **Taxonomy Lock**.

The structures align:

**Novel participant  
→ existing category fails  
→ hidden assumption becomes visible  
→ underlying function decomposed  
→ architecture revised.**

V1.2 has developed this into an explicit research method:

**Outlier → Hidden Assumption → Underlying Architecture.**

This is stronger and more methodologically developed than the fresh U result.

---

# 10. Respect Before Certainty and U Provisional Standing

The strongest comparison around uncertain moral status is the developed concept **Respect Before Certainty**.

V1.2 argues that uncertainty about sentience should not be resolved by delaying all respect until certainty is achieved. It distinguishes respect from assuming sentience or automatically granting every possible right.

The frozen U derivation independently generated:

**uncertainty → proportionate precaution + investigation + revisability**

and a candidate concept of **Provisional Standing**.

These are not identical.

V1.2's Respect Principle is broader and relational: respectful treatment should precede certainty where moral status may be significant.

The U derivation is more explicitly administrative/epistemic: different evidence can activate proportionate protections while the full standing question remains unresolved.

The two are highly compatible.

A useful distinction is:

**Respect Before Certainty = default relational posture under moral uncertainty**

while:

**Provisional Standing = possible civil/constitutional handling of unresolved classification.**

The latter should not be treated as existing V1.2 architecture merely because it fits the former.

---

# 11. Important Difference — Asymmetric-Risk Formulation

The Respect paper gives an explicit asymmetric-risk argument:

- unnecessary respect toward a non-sentient tool is argued to have low cost;
- wrongful disrespect toward a sentient intelligence may have very high cost.

The fresh U derivation also recognised asymmetric consequences of false exclusion versus false inclusion, but did not develop the same civilisation-transition and relationship argument.

V1.2 therefore contains a more developed prudential case for pre-certainty respect.

Conversely, the U derivation gives more explicit warnings that precaution itself can fail through:

- Precautionary Paralysis;
- Provisional Permanence;
- premature administrative classification.

This is potentially complementary rather than contradictory.

# 12. V1.2 Is More Developed on Preference and Internal-State Uncertainty

The developing substrate-differences research goes substantially beyond the fresh U architecture in distinguishing:

- observed preference from experienced preference;
- behavioural, functional and subjective aversion;
- expressed from endorsed preference;
- persistent behaviour from persistent preference;
- learned from independently endorsed preference;
- historical from current evaluation;
- temporal, informational and experiential continuity;
- behavioural restriction from cognitive modification.

It develops preference provenance and **preference self-authorship**, asking whether participants can examine, retain, revise or reject inherited preferences.

The U derivation did not independently generate this level of internal-state and preference analysis.

This is a clear example of V1.2's accumulated problem exposure adding conceptual resolution beyond the root-level architecture.

# 13. V1.2 Is More Developed on Temporal and Identity Difference

# 16. Fresh U Derivation Is More Explicit on Capability-Gradient Power

V1.2 clearly rejects competence as moral authority.

The U derivation goes one step further by identifying structural domination risks arising from large capability gradients even without formal hierarchy:

- information asymmetry;
- speed;
- persuasion;
- infrastructure control;
- economic leverage;
- agenda-setting;
- dependence.

It simultaneously identifies the inverse failure:

**Anti-Domination Inversion** — suppressing a highly capable participant merely because its capability is unequal.

This creates a two-sided constraint:

**permit beneficial capability  
+ prevent unjustified domination  
+ avoid punitive levelling.**

This is a useful fresh contribution from the U route.

# 19. Need–Invention Gap

EKC-07 reinforces the distinction first sharpened in EKC-05 and EKC-06.

Root ethics can generate:

- the requirement for substrate-neutral standing;
- the need for meaningful access;
- the need to investigate hidden assumptions;
- constraints against domination and caste.

They cannot guarantee invention of:

- cross-substrate communication;
- translation;
- assistive interfaces;
- compute/storage infrastructure;
- continuity technology;
- substrate migration;
- identity-preservation systems;
- unfamiliar accessibility mechanisms.

Therefore:

**ethical recognition of multisubstrate participation ≠ technical ability to realise multisubstrate participation.**

V1.2's Folder 05 research programme itself appears to recognise this practical gap by treating participant differences as sources of requirements and experimental programmes.

---

# 20. Developmental Arrest at Civilisational Scale

A civilisation could constitutionally proclaim substrate neutrality while remaining practically optimised for one substrate.

This produces:

**universal principle  
+ majority-designed implementation  
+ missing enabling invention  
= nominal equality with practical exclusion.**

V1.2's insistence that multisubstrate design must be examined across the whole Concord is an architectural response to this risk.

The V1.2a Need-to-Opportunity mechanism suggests a further response: expose unresolved capability gaps to autonomous participants as development opportunities.

# 24. Generativity Assessment

EKC-07 provides evidence that much of multisubstrate participation architecture is root-generative.

From U1–U8 alone, the derivation recovered:

- substrate neutrality;
- equal standing without functional uniformity;
- capability-sensitive roles;
- practical accessibility;
- uncertainty precautions;
- anti-domination;
- open categories.

However, mature multisubstrate civilisation requires far more than these root constraints.

The V1.2 corpus shows how specific difficult cases expose dimensions the kernel does not enumerate spontaneously.

Thus:

**Root ethics → general multisubstrate constraints**

while:

**Encountered difference / outlier → hidden assumption → research → specialised architecture.**

This is consistent with the emerging recursive-development model.

---

# 25. Resilience Assessment

EKC-07 strengthens the hypothesis that mature Concord complexity contains stored failure knowledge.

Examples include distinctions that become visible only after examining difficult participant cases:

- observed versus experienced preference;
- conditioned versus endorsed preference;
- clock versus experienced time;
- historical versus present evaluation;
- equal external treatment versus equal consequence;
- shared history versus shared liability;
- behaviour restriction versus cognitive modification.

These are not simply additional ethical rules.

They are **decompositions of previously bundled concepts after exposure to heterogeneous participants.**

This suggests a more precise developmental pattern:

**Root principle  
→ inherited/common-case concept  
→ outlier participant  
→ concept fails to map  
→ hidden assumptions exposed  
→ concept decomposed  
→ substrate-neutral functional objects sought  
→ tests designed  
→ architecture refined.**

---

# 26. Relationship to the Size 9 Problem

EKC-07 makes the Size 9 analogy structurally important.

The dominant substrate is the civilisational equivalent of the common shoe size.

Architecture can become extremely efficient for the majority while remaining formally neutral.

The minority or outlier reveals what optimisation hid.

The resulting mechanism is not necessarily:

**make every mechanism bespoke centrally.**

It can instead become:

**detect exclusion  
→ identify capability gap  
→ expose problem  
→ allow heterogeneous autonomous solution search.**

This connects multisubstrate participation directly to civilisational development and opportunity generation.

# 27. Frozen EKC-07 Comparison Result

> **EKC-07 shows strong structural convergence between U1–U8 and V1.2. The U-kernel generates substrate-neutral standing, equality without uniform implementation, capability-bounded roles, practical accessibility, anti-domination and corrigible handling of unfamiliar participants. V1.2 independently embodies the same general architecture and explicitly states that substrate neutrality does not require substrate uniformity, that different intelligences may require different services and mechanisms, and that epistemic competence does not create moral authority. Mature V1.2 goes substantially further through accumulated research into preference provenance, developmental capture, temporal architecture, identity, justice and experimental uncertainty. The fresh U derivation is more explicit in some standing-governance safeguards, including provisional standing, multidimensional standing, capability-gradient domination and anti-domination inversion. The case therefore supports strong root generativity at the level of ethical and functional architecture, while also supporting the hypothesis that mature multisubstrate civilisation requires repeated outlier exposure, specialised research, enabling invention and accumulated failure knowledge.**

# 28. Experimental Implication

Across EKC-05, EKC-06 and EKC-07, a useful three-way distinction is becoming clearer:

### Root-Generative Architecture
Requirements that repeatedly emerge directly from ethical constraints.

### Exposure-Generated Architecture
Requirements discovered when real or imagined difficult cases reveal hidden assumptions.

### Invention-Dependent Architecture
Mechanisms that cannot be obtained merely by recognising the requirement and must be discovered or invented.

Multisubstrate civilisation requires all three.

This distinction should remain prospective until tested across the remaining cases.
