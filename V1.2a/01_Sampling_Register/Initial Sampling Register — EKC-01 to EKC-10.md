---

# 1. Purpose

This register fixes the initial heterogeneous sample before neutral problem packets or U1–U8 counterfactual derivations are produced.

Selection is based on functional diversity and discriminatory value, not on whether a case is expected to converge with or diverge from V1.2.

The ten functional categories were fixed in the experimental protocol before these individual cases were selected.

---

# 2. Selection Rules

The initial sample uses one primary case for each predefined functional category.

A selected case should:

1. represent a materially distinct civilisational problem;
2. have enough existing V1.2 architecture to permit meaningful later comparison;
3. be reducible to a neutral problem without requiring the existing solution to be copied into the problem statement;
4. expose different combinations of U1–U8;
5. include both likely control cases and potentially kernel-sensitive stress cases;
6. include at least one strong continuity/U8 case;
7. avoid over-sampling a single Concord subsystem.

Selection does **not** imply endorsement of the existing V1.2 mechanism or an expectation that U1–U8 will regenerate it.

# 4. Sample Balance

The initial ten-case sample deliberately contains:

- relatively direct control cases: EKC-03 and EKC-04;
- agency/non-domination cases: EKC-01, EKC-02, EKC-06, EKC-07 and EKC-09;
- positive-care/development stress: EKC-06;
- economic/resource underdetermination: EKC-05;
- explicit continuity/U8 stress: EKC-08;
- cross-civilisational heterogeneity: EKC-10;
- system-scale governance and emergency cases: EKC-02 and EKC-09.

The purpose is not equal numerical representation of every root. It is heterogeneous exposure to different ethical interactions.

---

# 5. Anti-Selection-Bias Note

These cases were selected after the experimental protocol and its ten functional categories were frozen, but before any V1.2a U-kernel derivation was performed.

Existing V1.2 file names and high-level domains were inspected to verify that suitable comparison material exists. Detailed V1.2 mechanisms are **not** to be used when constructing the counterfactual solution.

Because the researcher and assisting AI already possess some prior knowledge of the Concord, perfect cognitive blinding is impossible. The experiment relies on procedural separation, explicit provenance and frozen pre-comparison outputs rather than claiming full blindness.

---

# 6. Comparison-Anchor Rule

The V1.2 paths above are **sealed comparison anchors**, not derivation sources.

They identify where the later comparison is expected to begin.

During neutral packet construction and U1–U8 derivation:

- do not reopen these files to obtain their solutions;
- do not copy their mechanisms into neutral problem statements;
- do not infer that their titles prescribe the counterfactual answer.

After each counterfactual result is frozen, the corresponding anchor may be opened and the wider V1.2 corpus searched where necessary under ESCP discipline.

This Version 1.0 sampling register is frozen before preparation of the first neutral problem packet.
