# 1. Candidate Position

**HIF-003 — Meaning-Preserving Translation**

> Determine and preserve the invariant civil meaning/function of an object while permitting its representation to change across systems, modalities and substrates.

# 2. Frozen Requirements

T1 distinguish source object/meaning from representation.
T2 identify material civil invariants before transformation.
T3 permit representation to change where identical form is impossible/inappropriate.
T4 preserve provenance of transformations.
T5 expose known information loss and uncertainty.
T6 detect materially significant translation divergence.
T7 allow challenge/correction of transformed representation.
T8 avoid assuming lexical/formal similarity proves semantic equivalence.
T9 support contextual differences without manufacturing artificial agreement.
T10 support human, AI and unfamiliar future substrates.
T11 preserve function where exact representation cannot be preserved.
T12 avoid claiming complete formal capture of meaning where unsupported.
