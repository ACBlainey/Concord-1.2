# 1. Invention Position

**IF-003 — Cross-Modality Authoritative Civil Reachability**

Required capability:

> A substrate- and modality-flexible civil reachability architecture that preserves authoritative notice, participant response, privacy, provenance and practical accessibility when a participant cannot use the default digital channel.

# 2. Core Problem

A civil message may be formally available while being practically inaccessible.

Therefore:

**Transmission ≠ Receipt ≠ Comprehension ≠ Opportunity to Respond.**

The architecture must preserve civil meaning while allowing the delivery modality to adapt.

# 3. Frozen Constraints

C1 authoritative content must remain identifiable across modality transformation;
C2 participant must not lose rights because the default modality is inaccessible;
C3 accessibility adaptation must not silently alter legal/civil meaning;
C4 the system must distinguish message content from its presentation;
C5 participant response must be possible through an accessible modality;
C6 provenance must show material transformations;
C7 privacy must not be unnecessarily weakened by accessibility support;
C8 assistive intermediaries must not acquire general authority over the participant;
C9 the architecture must support human, machine and future substrate differences;
C10 inability to use one channel must not be interpreted as refusal;
C11 urgent communications require an escalation path when the preferred modality fails;
C12 equivalent meaning matters more than identical sensory form;
C13 participant preferences should guide modality where practicable;
C14 disputes over transformation/meaning must be reviewable;
C15 the architecture must permit new modalities without redesigning civil identity.

# 4. Failure Tests

F1 participant cannot read text;
F2 participant cannot hear audio;
F3 participant cannot use digital device;
F4 machine participant requires structured representation;
F5 translation changes legally significant meaning;
F6 assistant/intermediary misrepresents message;
F7 private information exposed through adaptation;
F8 urgent notice sent through inaccessible preferred channel;
F9 participant response transformed incorrectly;
F10 multiple modalities produce conflicting interpretations;
F11 future substrate has no human-equivalent sensory modality;
F12 participant temporarily loses normal capability;
F13 authority claims delivery because a technical endpoint received data;
F14 adaptation service unavailable;
F15 later dispute requires reconstruction of what participant actually received.

# 5. Freeze

C1–C15 and F1–F15 are frozen before internal mechanism generation.
