# 1. Candidate Position

**HIF-002 — Privacy-Preserving Verifiability**

> Produce enough trustworthy evidence to support consequential civil claims, review and recovery without requiring comprehensive observation of the participant or system.

# 2. Frozen Requirements

V1 consequential claims can carry sufficient evidence for audit/review.
V2 evidence collection is bounded by legitimate function and consequence.
V3 unnecessary participant-level information is not retained merely because verification is useful.
V4 provenance can establish origin/transformation without becoming universal surveillance.
V5 protected/anonymous sources can remain usable where appropriate.
V6 corrections propagate without requiring unnecessary re-identification.
V7 evidence quality/uncertainty remains visible.
V8 verification infrastructure remains auditable and contestable.
V9 privacy transformation must not silently destroy relevant meaning.
V10 access to exceptional identifiable evidence is bounded and reviewable.
V11 evidence/verification does not itself acquire decision authority.
V12 the mechanism remains applicable across human, AI and other substrates.
