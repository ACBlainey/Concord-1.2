# V1.2 to V1.3 Migration Map — Root Architecture Pass 001

**Status:** MIGRATION PLANNING / NO FILE MOVEMENT AUTHORISED

## 1. Principle
V1.2 is a developmental architecture. V1.3 should present the resulting civilisational architecture without cleaning up V1.2 in place.

> **V1.2 preserves developmental provenance; V1.3 presents the resulting civilisational architecture.**

Repository Folder != System != Function != Operational Domain != Authority.

## 2. Proposed V1.3 root classes
- 00 Front Door — WHY, orientation, Citizens Guides and reading paths.
- 01 Constitutional Core — ethical kernel, constitutional constraints, rights and foundational architecture.
- 02 Civilisational Domains — the provisionally frozen 18-domain topology.
- 03 Cross-Domain Architecture — intersections that connect domains without inheriting their authority.
- 04 Portable Modules and Methods — reusable methods/modules.
- 05 Applications and Implementations — pilots and concrete deployments.
- 06 Research and Development — active research, sketches, candidate architecture and experiments.
- 07 Open Questions — unresolved questions.
- 08 Reference, Evidence and Development History — evidence, evaluations, legacy copies and provenance.
- 99 Repository Administration — housekeeping, migration records and repository maintenance.

Exact numbering/naming remains provisional until detailed mapping is complete.

## 5. Root migration register

### 00_Front_Door
**Type:** orientation / WHY / onboarding.
**Destination:** Front Door.
**Action:** retain root function and normalise.
Preserve provenance of the multiple WHY investigations even if V1.3 presents a cleaner reading path.

### 01_Constitutional_Core
**Type:** mixed.
**Action:** split conceptually.
Retain ethical kernel, foundational Concord material, recursive constitutional architecture and genuine constitutional constraints in Constitutional Core.
Move Governance architecture to Governance domain.
Move Judiciary V2 to Judiciary domain.
Split Constitutional Emergency V2 between constitutional constraints and Emergency Coordination/Governance/Law interfaces where appropriate.
Unified Paths of Light provenance may require separation between constitutional result and Research/Development History.

### 02_Epistemic_and_Research
**Type:** mixed Research domain + portable methods + research programmes.
**Action:** split.
Research programmes and evidence/validation work -> Research domain.
Blaineyan Reasoning and Reality Trees -> Portable Modules and Methods.
Active topology/formalisation investigations -> Research and Development unless graduated.
Participation & Intelligence ultimately becomes a specialised Research subdivision/programme rather than a peer root.

### 03_Continuity_and_Memory
**Type:** mixed Continuity + KCS + provenance/memory + personal data/metrics.
**Action:** split.
Continuity Protocol, Legacy Ladder and capability recovery -> Continuity domain.
KCS/change propagation -> Cross-Domain Architecture.
Temporal provenance material -> Historical where appropriate.
Personal Data and Metrics requires file-level classification among Historical participant records, Metrics, Research and Identity.

### 04_Coordination_and_Economy
**Type:** mixed Economy + Metrics.
**Action:** split.
Ratchet, Roadway Equilibrium and economic/resource coordination -> Economy domain.
Metrics Full -> Metrics and State Observation unless detailed review identifies domain-specific sections.
Measurement != Economic Authority.

### 05_Participation_and_Intelligence
**Type:** specialised participant/substrate research + some portable architecture.
**Action:** demote from root and split.
Substrate differences, intelligence differences and substrate-neutral design research -> Research domain.
Exit Interview portable form -> Portable Modules.
Participant-recognition/standing principles -> source-resolve to Constitutional/Cross-Domain architecture as appropriate.

### 06_Portable_Modules
**Type:** portable modules and module development.
**Destination:** Portable Modules and Methods.
**Action:** retain function and normalise.
Important distinction: a portable representation of a domain system is not the authoritative domain architecture.
> **Portable Copy != Domain Authority**

### 07_Applications
**Type:** applications/pilots.
**Destination:** Applications and Implementations.
Mars/GTP remains deployment/application architecture, not a civil domain.

### 08_Active_Development
**Type:** development lifecycle, sketches, candidate principles/architecture and experiments.
**Destination:** primarily Research and Development; graduated outputs route to architectural homes.
Preserve development provenance.
> **Graduation Changes Architectural Home; It Does Not Erase Development History**

### 09_Open_Questions
**Type:** unresolved question register + closed-question history.
**Destination:** Open Questions plus Development History.
Central indexing can coexist with domain-specific question references.
Closed questions are preserved, not deleted.

### 10_Reference_and_Development
**Type:** mixed reference, comparative research, evidence, history, foundation papers, external evaluation and legacy copies.
**Action:** split.
Development History, Legacy Copies, External Evaluations and provenance-heavy material -> Reference/Evidence/Development History.
Active Comparative Research, literature engagement and empirical investigation -> Research where appropriate.
Companion/foundation-paper collections may remain publication/reference artefacts.
This root should not remain a catch-all in V1.3.

### 11_Intercivilisational_Architecture
**Type:** Intercivilisational Relations domain + reusable wrapper/protocol architecture.
**Action:** promote domain function; split portable elements where useful.
Diplomacy/interoperation, peaceful distance, rights translation, disputes/jurisdiction/externalities -> Intercivilisational Relations.
Reusable wrapper mechanisms may also have portable representations.
Common Protocol != Common Government.

### 12_Civil_Attention
**Type:** Civil Attention domain + companion methodology.
**Action:** promote domain function; preserve portable representation.
Problem reports, suggestions, petitions, central resolution/feedback and domain interfaces -> Civil Attention.
Civil Attention Routes; It Does Not Inherit Destination Authority.

### Domains
**Type:** transitional domain-discovery workspace + graduated domain prototypes.
**Action:** source of V1.3 target architecture, not copied as-is.
Historical and other domain architecture become the V1.3 Civilisational Domains root.
Migration planning/register material later becomes Development History/Repository Administration.

### V1.2a
**Type:** experimental comparative fork, topology/formalisation research and replication tests.
**Action:** preserve as experimental provenance; extract only graduated results.
U1-U8 derivations, comparisons, adversarial tests, topology/formalisation and invention-frontier work -> Research and Development / Development History according to state.
V1.2a should not remain a peer civil architecture in V1.3.

### 99_Housekeeping
**Type:** repository administration.
**Destination:** Repository Administration.
No civil authority follows from repository maintenance.

### Root files
README -> V1.3 navigation/orientation.
STARTING_WORDS -> Front Door or root orientation according to final role.
LICENSE -> root repository/legal material.
Starting wordcount -> development/repository provenance.

## 6. Central transformation
V1.2 currently mixes three maps:

**A. Map of the Civilisation** — domains, constitutional core and interfaces.

**B. Map of the Tools Used to Design the Civilisation** — portable modules, reasoning methods, formalisation and testing.

**C. Map of the Development Journey** — active development, experiments, evidence, legacy copies, open questions and comparative work.

V1.3 should make those maps explicitly separate.
