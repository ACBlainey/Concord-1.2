# Historical Domain

**Project:** The Concord
**Status:** DOMAIN RESOLUTION / STARTING HYPOTHESIS / NOT YET FULLY SOURCE-RESOLVED
**Date:** 25 September 2026

## Starting Functional Hypothesis

> **Historical is the Concord's durable epistemic memory domain: responsible for preserving what the Concord knows, has known, observed, received, decided, recorded, retired or placed into protected long-term custody, while preserving provenance, integrity and appropriate access boundaries.**

This is a starting hypothesis for source resolution, not a completed domain specification.

## Basic Functional Boundary

**Event / Activity → Operational Domain → Operational Record → Archival Threshold → Historical Domain → Protected Historical Record**

Operational domains may retain information legitimately required for active operation. Once information becomes archival, provenance-bearing, long-term evidentiary material or protected black-box material, Historical is the candidate default custodian.

> **Historical Custody ≠ Authority to Access**

> **Restricted Access ≠ Historical Erasure**

## Candidate Contents to Source-Resolve

Current candidates include provenance records; historical metrics and snapshots; archives and institutional records; governance decisions and decision history; voting records; protected ballot storage; judiciary/evidentiary records; audit trails; Threat Think Tank stored information; Civil Attention history; road-camera and other sensor/video black-box storage; Wake Me When records; public-library material; system/version/change history; AI tools or code removed through self-stewardship; and dormant/cold-stored AI or continuity states where legitimate.

These are candidate inclusions. Source resolution must determine existing architecture and boundaries.

## Access Architecture

Historical should compose existing Concord mechanisms rather than invent an independent access-control system.

**Historical Record → Protected Space Classification → Access Request → Contextually Bounded Authority Check → Permitted View / Transformation → Audited Output**

This can support public material, operationally private records, sealed evidence and highly protected black-box storage within the same domain while preserving different access conditions.

Possible authorised outputs include public copies, anonymised views/datasets, statistical extracts, redacted records, bounded evidentiary extracts and authorised originals where legitimate.

## Dormant AI / Computational Custody

Historical may potentially provide protected long-term custody for dormant computational entities or continuity material.

> **Preservation of an Intelligence ≠ Activation of an Intelligence**

> **Authority to Store ≠ Authority to Inspect ≠ Authority to Copy ≠ Authority to Modify ≠ Authority to Reactivate**

Historical custody would not itself confer reactivation authority. Wake conditions, continuity rights, agency and legitimate authority remain with appropriate Concord mechanisms.

## Relationship to Epistemic Functions

Provisional distinction for testing:

**Epistemic functions** concern how the Concord determines what it currently has reason to believe.

**Historical** concerns durable preservation of what was known, believed, observed, decided or recorded at particular times, including provenance needed to understand those records later.

## Relationship to Civil Attention

Historical may preserve Civil Attention's durable records and provenance, but does not thereby acquire authority to classify, investigate or resolve Civil Attention Issues.
