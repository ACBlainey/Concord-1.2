## 2. Civil Attention Creates a Traceable Operational Chain

The integrated architecture explicitly defines:

`Submission → Issue → Domain Action Object → Domain Work → Domain Return → Central Resolution Record → Participant Feedback / Review`.

Historical implication:

> **Historical Issue History Should Preserve Material State Transitions Across the Resolution Chain Rather Than Only the Initial Submission and Final Disposition.**

## 3. Intake Provenance and Resolution Provenance Are Different

Candidate principle:

> **Historical Submission Provenance ≠ Historical Resolution Provenance.**

The first establishes what entered the system; the second records what the civilisation did with it.

> **Historical Central Record Custody ≠ Historical Evidence That the Central Recorder Held Substantive Decision Authority.**
