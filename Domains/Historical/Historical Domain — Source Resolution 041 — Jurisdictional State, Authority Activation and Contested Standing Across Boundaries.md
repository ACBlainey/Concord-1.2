**Project:** The Concord

**Status:** ACTIVE DOMAIN RESOLUTION / INTERCIVILISATIONAL ARCHITECTURE PASS  
**Date:** 25 September 2026

## 1. Purpose

This pass examines the large, comparatively under-read `11_Intercivilisational_Architecture` family, including:

- Burden-Bearing Authority;
- Civilisational Wrapper architecture;
- procedural wrapper architecture;
- Minimum Necessary Capability;
- Functionally Bounded Summits;
- Cross-Boundary Externality Recognition and Responsibility Mechanism;
- Emergent Polities, Sentient Secession and Recognition.

Most of these sources are explicitly review-copy, active-development or non-canonical architecture. Their status is preserved here.

The Historical question is:

> **How should Historical represent events where authority, standing, jurisdiction, recognition and responsibility are bounded, activated, disputed or changed over time?**

The central result is:

> **Historical records must preserve the difference between what happened, who claimed authority or standing, what authority was actually activated, what jurisdiction was accepted or disputed, and what later recognition or responsibility was established.**

## 2. Standing Authority and Activated Authority Are Different

Burden-Bearing Authority distinguishes invested authority from activated authority.

Candidate Historical principle:

> **Historical Possession of an Authority Basis ≠ Historical Exercise of That Authority.**

## 70. Relationship to ESCP

This pass exposes numerous traps:

- role mistaken for activated authority;
- influence mistaken for legitimate authority;
- interaction mistaken for jurisdiction;
- communication mistaken for recognition;
- negotiation mistaken for endorsement;
- harm mistaken for causation;
- causation mistaken for responsibility;
- responsibility mistaken for jurisdiction;
- standing mistaken for proof;
- recognition treated as binary;
- later recognition projected backward;
- control mistaken for legitimacy;
- non-recognition mistaken for non-existence;
- bounded agreement expanded retrospectively;
- refusal treated as erasing the underlying case;
- political decision treated as ontological truth.

Therefore:

> **Historical Evaluation-Space Completeness across boundaries requires independent representation of claim, standing, evidence, causation, responsibility, jurisdiction, authority, control, communication and recognition.**

## 71. Source-Resolution Result

This was one of the last large under-read V1.2 source families.

Despite its size, it did not reveal a new broad Historical content domain.

Instead, it substantially refines the already established intercivilisational and authority history architecture by showing that several apparently simple historical labels are actually multi-dimensional time-varying states.

The most important new operational refinement is:

> **jurisdictional-state and recognition-state provenance.**

## 72. Strongest Formulations

> **Historical Possession of an Authority Basis ≠ Historical Exercise of That Authority.**

> **Historical Cross-Boundary Interaction ≠ Historical Jurisdiction Transfer.**

> **Historical Communication With an Entity ≠ Historical Recognition of That Entity's Claimed Political Status.**

> **Historical Responsibility Finding ≠ Historical Jurisdictional Authority by Default.**

> **Historical Recognition ≠ Binary Historical Variable.**

And the strongest combined formulation:

> **At a civilisational boundary, Historical must remember not merely that two parties interacted, but what each claimed, what each recognised, what authority was actually activated, what jurisdiction remained disputed, what responsibility was established, and which questions were still unresolved — because contact, cooperation, control, responsibility, jurisdiction and recognition are different historical facts.**
