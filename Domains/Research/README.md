## Purpose

This folder is the development and eventual operating home for the **Research civilisational domain**.

Research is the civilisation's active knowledge-acquisition function: observing, questioning, investigating, modelling, testing, comparing, validating, learning and correcting its present understanding.

Its central question is:

> **What are we learning now, how do we know it, and how should confidence change as evidence changes?**

## Relationship to Historical

Research and Historical form complementary temporal functions.

**Research** concerns active inquiry and present learning.

**Historical** preserves past state, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, relationships and development so that the past remains reconstructable.

> **Research Produces Knowledge Claims ≠ Historical Determines Their Truth**

> **Historical Preservation ≠ Current Research Authority**

Research outputs may pass into Historical custody while retaining the state, evidence, uncertainty and evaluation-space limitations they possessed at the relevant time.

## Internal Research Classification

Research is a broad civilisational domain and should not be mistaken for a single research programme or methodology.

As actual Concord research activity develops, work should be classified into appropriate disciplinary divisions and then into progressively more specialised subdivisions in the same general manner as ordinary research organisation.

Illustrative top-level divisions may include:

- science;
- mathematics;
- engineering and technology;
- social and behavioural research;
- humanities and cultural research;
- civilisational and systems research;
- multisubstrate and intelligence research;
- other disciplines that become necessary as knowledge and participant types develop.

These examples are not intended as a fixed or exhaustive taxonomy. A scientific division may later contain physics, chemistry, biology and other fields; those may themselves divide into increasingly specialised research areas where actual programmes justify doing so.

> **Research Domain -> Discipline -> Field -> Subfield -> Research Programme / Project**

The Concord does **not** currently need to create empty folders for disciplines in which it has no research programme.

Classification should follow real research activity rather than manufacturing an unused institutional structure in advance.

When new research programmes are created, they should be classified within the Research domain at the most appropriate level. The taxonomy itself remains correctable as knowledge develops and new forms of research emerge.

> **No Current Programme != No Future Research Domain Capacity**

> **Repository Structure Should Follow Research Need; Research Need Should Not Be Invented to Fill Repository Structure**

## Participation and Intelligence as Specialised Research

`05_Participation_and_Intelligence/` should currently be understood primarily as a specialised research programme within the Research domain.

Its purpose is to investigate actual, possible and presently unknown differences between participant substrates and forms of intelligence, including humans, artificial intelligences, hybrids and participant forms not yet encountered.

Much of this knowledge is currently incomplete or unavailable. Research may therefore use clearly identified speculation, abstraction and cautious generalisation to expose possible requirements without presenting those possibilities as established facts.

This uncertainty has helped produce substrate-neutral Concord design principles.

The intended developmental relationship is:

**Unknown Participant Differences -> Cautious Speculation and Generalisation -> Substrate-Neutral Design -> Observation and Testing -> Improved Knowledge of Actual Substrates -> Research -> Architectural Refinement**

As artificial intelligence and other participant substrates develop, more empirical information should become available. Research can then refine Concord mechanisms where genuine differences matter while preserving higher-level principles that remain valid across substrates.

> **Unknown Difference -> Generalise Safely; Known Difference -> Refine Where Evidence Warrants**

> **Substrate-Neutral Principle != Substrate-Identical Implementation**

A discovery made through Participation and Intelligence research does not necessarily remain there operationally. If it reveals a requirement affecting Continuity, Governance, Judiciary, Economy, Civil Attention or another domain, that requirement should propagate to the appropriate architecture through normal Concord development and change-control processes.

Participation and Intelligence therefore acts as a specialised **research source and requirements-discovery programme**, not as a separate civilisational domain.

## Initial Boundary

Research may produce evidence, models, hypotheses, measurements and findings.

It does not acquire civil authority merely because its predictions or findings are accurate.

> **Evidence Determines Confidence About Effects; It Does Not Alone Determine Legitimacy.**

> **Research ≠ Governance**

> **Research ≠ Judiciary**

> **Research ≠ Historical**

> **Methodology ≠ Domain**
