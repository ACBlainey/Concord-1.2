## Purpose

This folder is the development and eventual operating home for the **Research civilisational domain**.

Research is the civilisation's active knowledge-acquisition function: observing, questioning, investigating, modelling, testing, comparing, validating, learning and correcting its present understanding.

Its central question is:

> **What are we learning now, how do we know it, and how should confidence change as evidence changes?**

## Relationship to Historical

Research and Historical form complementary temporal functions.

**Research** concerns active inquiry and present learning.

**Historical** preserves past state, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, relationships and development so that the past remains reconstructable.

> **Research Produces Knowledge Claims ≠ Historical Determines Their Truth**

> **Historical Preservation ≠ Current Research Authority**

Research outputs may pass into Historical custody while retaining the state, evidence, uncertainty and evaluation-space limitations they possessed at the relevant time.

Blaineyan Reasoning, Reality Trees and other methodologies may be **used by Research** without becoming the Research domain itself.

## Initial Boundary

Research may produce evidence, models, hypotheses, measurements and findings.

It does not acquire civil authority merely because its predictions or findings are accurate.

> **Evidence Determines Confidence About Effects; It Does Not Alone Determine Legitimacy.**

> **Research ≠ Governance**

> **Research ≠ Judiciary**

> **Research ≠ Historical**

> **Methodology ≠ Domain**
