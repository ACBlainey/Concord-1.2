## Purpose

This folder is the development and eventual operating home for the **Intercivilisational Relations civilisational domain**.

The domain concerns how the Concord coexists, communicates, recognises, interoperates and maintains bounded relationships with other civilisations, jurisdictions and substantially independent civil systems.

Its scope includes diplomacy and interoperability but is not reducible to either.

> **Intercivilisational Relationship ≠ Internal Governance**

## Existing Source Architecture

The principal current source family is:

`11_Intercivilisational_Architecture/`

That material was developed primarily around civilisational wrappers and interoperability with existing countries and other civilisations.

Wrappers are therefore treated as **mechanisms and interfaces serving this domain**, not as the domain itself.

## Cross-Domain Character

Intercivilisational Relations necessarily interfaces with multiple internal domains.

Examples include:

- **Governance** — diplomatic decisions, agreements and authorised public action;
- **Judiciary** — jurisdiction, disputes, rights recognition and externalities;
- **Economy and Resource Coordination** — trade, exchange and shared-resource relationships;
- **Constitutional Core** — limits on external agreements and authority;
- **Civil Attention** — participant concerns involving external relationships;
- **Historical** — provenance and reconstruction of relationships, agreements and events;
- **Continuity** — continuity of critical external relationships and dependencies;
- **Research** — investigation of unfamiliar civilisations, systems and interoperability requirements.

Its cross-domain interfaces do not make it merely a subdivision of Governance.

The persistent civil function is the management of the civilisation's boundary relationships with other civilisations.

## Core Boundary Principles

> **Common Protocol ≠ Common Government**

> **Wrapper Adoption ≠ Concord Oversight**

> **Technical Compatibility ≠ Political Subordination**

> **Diplomatic Relationship ≠ Transfer of Sovereignty**

> **Interoperation ≠ Internal Uniformity**
