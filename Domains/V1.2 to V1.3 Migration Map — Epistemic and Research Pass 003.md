## 1. Governing distinction

The V1.2 folder mixes three different things:

1. **Research activity** — investigation, evidence, validation, experiments and open inquiry.
2. **Methods used to conduct inquiry** — Blaineyan Reasoning, Reality Trees and related tools.
3. **Architectures discovered through research that now operate across the civilisation** — most notably Civilisational Developmental Topology.

V1.3 should separate them by current function.

> **Origin in Research != Permanent Ownership by Research**

A system discovered through research may graduate into another architectural role.

## 4. Civilisational Developmental Topology — graduated cross-domain architecture

The CDT folder requires special treatment.

Its README states:

- founding validation complete;
- canonical architecture area;
- operational sufficiency for the founding developmental-control test;
- explicit absence of civil authority;
- interfaces with KCS, State Map, Civilisation Clock and legitimate owner domains;
- development targets remain owned by their legitimate domains.

Its current function is therefore larger than a research programme.

CDT asks:

> **What is required, what exists, how developed is it, what is blocking it, and what should be developed next?**

This operates across the entire civilisation.

### Classification
**CROSS-DOMAIN ARCHITECTURE — developmental state, dependency and priority topology**

### V1.3 destination
Cross-Domain Architecture / Civilisational Developmental Topology

### Research retains
- ongoing validation;
- topology completeness investigation;
- independent replication;
- new model testing;
- empirical evaluation of development-order performance;
- revisions generated by evidence.

### Historical retains
- V1.2a experimental provenance;
- founding validation sequence;
- superseded topology/state versions;
- prior test records.

### Operational boundaries
CDT may:
- represent required functions;
- represent ownership;
- identify interfaces/dependencies;
- represent maturity/state;
- expose consequential gaps;
- generate evidence-informed development queues.

CDT may not:
- acquire the authority of a domain;
- command implementation merely because a gap exists;
- override constitutional allocation of authority;
- convert priority evidence into permission.

> **Development Priority Evidence != Authority to Develop or Deploy**

This confirms the wider principle:

> **Measuring a Civilisation != Governing It**

## 6. Epistemic principles remain Research-domain constraints

The current README contains important domain-level epistemic rules:

- Observation != Inference != Hypothesis != Speculation
- Idea != Evidence
- Formalisation != Validation
- Internal Consistency != External Evidence
- Prediction != Authority
- Convergence Is Evidence; It Is Not Authority
- Reality is the final arbiter of empirical claims

These should survive in the V1.3 Research README/domain architecture.

They govern Research behaviour but do not give Research authority over other domains.

Research may produce evidence that triggers review.

The legitimate owner domain determines action within constitutional/legal constraints.

## 7. Historical relationship

Research produces unusually large quantities of material that should not remain permanently "live."

A lifecycle is needed:

**ACTIVE QUESTION**
-> **RESEARCH PROGRAMME**
-> **EVIDENCE / RESULT**
-> **REPLICATION / CHALLENGE**
-> **CURRENT KNOWLEDGE STATE**
-> where operationally relevant, integration into owner domain
-> **HISTORICAL PRESERVATION OF PRIOR STATES AND PROVENANCE**

Historical should preserve failed hypotheses, superseded models, stopping decisions and development pathways without forcing Research to treat all prior work as current.

> **Research Requires the Freedom to Supersede; Historical Requires the Duty Not to Erase.**

## 9. Finding

The old folder name **Epistemic and Research** was useful during development because epistemic methods and research programmes developed together.

The V1.3 living-civilisation topology can now separate them.

The Research domain becomes the civilisation's organised capacity to learn.

Portable methods become tools available to the whole civilisation.

CDT becomes a cross-domain state/development architecture.

Historical preserves how each was discovered and changed.

The resulting relationship is:

**RESEARCH asks and tests**
-> **METHODS support inquiry**
-> **CROSS-DOMAIN ARCHITECTURE observes relationships/state**
-> **OWNER DOMAINS act within legitimate authority**
-> **HISTORICAL preserves the path through time**

This is a cleaner civil architecture than retaining all epistemic material under one research-project folder.
