# Civilisational Domain and System-Gap Map — Working Register 001

**Project:** The Concord Framework  
**Status:** ACTIVE DOMAIN DISCOVERY / WORKING REGISTER / NOT CANONICAL ARCHITECTURE  
**Date:** 26 September 2026  
**Purpose:** Identify both the civilisational domains already represented by Concord architecture and essential civil functions for which architecture is partial or absent, before any V1.3 domain-normalisation migration.

---

## 1. Why This Register Exists

The Concord has developed primarily through constitutional, ethical, epistemic and systems reasoning rather than by beginning with a conventional list of state departments or public services.

This has produced deep architecture in some areas while leaving ordinary civil functions lightly developed or absent.

Domain discovery must therefore proceed in two directions:

1. **Inside-out:** What persistent civil functions are already present beneath the Concord's existing folders, systems and development history?
2. **Outside-in:** What persistent functions would a functioning civilisation require even if the Concord has not yet designed them?

The difference between those maps is a **system-gap map**.

> **Repository Presence != Civilisational Completeness**

> **Constitutional Authority to Act != Operational Capability to Act**

> **A Principle Relevant to a Function != A Developed System for That Function**

---

## 2. Status Language

### EXISTING / SUBSTANTIAL ARCHITECTURE
A persistent civil function has enough existing Concord architecture to justify a domain home, although domain normalisation, formalisation or validation may remain incomplete.

### PARTIAL / SYSTEM GAP
The function is recognised and relevant architecture or design seeds exist, but important operational systems remain undeveloped.

### MISSING / DOMAIN CANDIDATE
The civil function appears necessary or strongly expected for a functioning civilisation, but the current repository contains little or no substantive architecture for it.

### CROSS-DOMAIN / NOT A DOMAIN
A mechanism, methodology, constitutional layer, infrastructure component or interface serves multiple domains but should not automatically become a domain itself.

These labels are provisional until source resolution is complete.

## 3. Currently Identified Civilisational Domains

### Historical
**Status:** EXISTING / GRADUATED CANDIDATE ARCHITECTURE  
Preserves interpretable, provenance-bearing civilisational state across time.

### Continuity
**Status:** EXISTING / DEVELOPMENT PENDING  
Preserves or restores required capability and function across disruption and time.

### Research
**Status:** EXISTING / DOMAIN NORMALISATION PENDING  
Active knowledge acquisition, investigation, testing, validation, learning and correction.

Participation and Intelligence is currently classified primarily as specialised multisubstrate research within this domain.

### Governance
**Status:** EXISTING / SUBSTANTIAL ARCHITECTURE  
Legitimate public decision-making, administration, stewardship and collective action within constitutional bounds.

### Judiciary
**Status:** EXISTING / SUBSTANTIAL ARCHITECTURE  
Adjudication, constitutional review, dispute resolution and bounded judicial stewardship.

### Economy and Resource Coordination
**Status:** EXISTING / SUBSTANTIAL BUT MIXED ARCHITECTURE  
Exchange, reciprocity, contribution, allocation and economic/resource coordination.

### Civil Attention
**Status:** EXISTING / SUBSTANTIAL ARCHITECTURE  
Common civil intake, preservation, routing, resolution-state visibility and participant feedback for problems, suggestions and petitions.

### Intercivilisational Relations
**Status:** EXISTING / DOMAIN NORMALISATION PENDING  
Diplomacy, recognition, interoperability, peaceful distance and bounded relationships with other civilisations and jurisdictions.

## 4. Strong System Gaps and Domain Candidates

### Law / Ordinary Legal System
**Provisional status:** MISSING / STRONG DOMAIN OR MAJOR DOMAIN-FUNCTION CANDIDATE

The Concord contains an extensive Judiciary and constitutional architecture but no comparably developed ordinary body of law.

A functioning legal order may require, among other things:

- criminal law;
- civil obligations and remedies;
- contracts;
- property and stewardship rules;
- commercial law;
- administrative law;
- procedural law;
- offences and sanctions;
- evidential rules;
- codification and amendment mechanisms.

The exact domain boundary remains to be resolved.

> **Judiciary != Law**

The Judiciary may interpret and adjudicate law without being the legitimate source of the entire ordinary legal code.

---

### Defence
**Provisional status:** MISSING / STRONG DOMAIN CANDIDATE

The Concord has constitutional emergency and threat-related reasoning but no developed military or external-defence system was found in the current repository scan.

Required questions include legitimate defensive purpose, command authority, force constraints, civilian control, mobilisation, readiness, rules governing defensive action, demobilisation, oversight and interaction with Intercivilisational Relations.

> **Emergency Authority != Defence Capability**

---

### Civil Security and Intelligence
**Provisional status:** MISSING / STRONG FUNCTIONAL GAP

The repository contains extensive use of *intelligence* to mean forms of cognition or participants, but no developed security-intelligence service architecture was found.

Potential functions include threat information collection, assessment, counterintelligence, warning and bounded dissemination.

Whether Intelligence becomes its own domain or a tightly bounded function interfacing Defence, Governance, Judiciary and Intercivilisational Relations remains unresolved.

Any future architecture will require unusually strong privacy, evidential, authority, purpose, access and oversight boundaries.

> **Research Intelligence != Security Intelligence**

> **Information Collection != Authority to Act**

---

### Civil Planning
**Provisional status:** PARTIAL / STRONG DOMAIN CANDIDATE

A substantive seed exists in current development work concerning zoning, safe places and substrate-aware civil planning, but no comprehensive civil-planning architecture is yet evident.

Likely scope includes settlements, land use, spatial compatibility, infrastructure placement, development permission, transport relationships, public/private/protected space interaction and long-horizon physical development.

---

### Infrastructure and Essential Utilities
**Provisional status:** MISSING OR HIGHLY PARTIAL / DOMAIN BOUNDARY OPEN

Infrastructure is discussed conceptually in the Concord, including continuity and multisubstrate implications, but an operating civil infrastructure system is not yet evident.

Potential scope includes:

- water and sanitation;
- energy;
- transport networks;
- communications infrastructure;
- waste systems;
- public works;
- shared physical and digital infrastructure.

Further work must determine whether this is one Infrastructure domain with specialist subdivisions or several independent domains.

---

### Health and Care
**Provisional status:** MISSING / STRONG DOMAIN CANDIDATE

No substantive healthcare, public-health, medical-service or care-system architecture was found in the initial repository scan.

A multisubstrate civilisation may eventually require a broader concept than present human healthcare, but human medical and public-health functions remain real requirements.

---

### Education and Development
**Provisional status:** MISSING OR HIGHLY PARTIAL / STRONG DOMAIN CANDIDATE

Education appears as an adoption mechanism and developmental concern, but no developed civil education system is evident.

This domain would need careful separation from Research:

> **Research asks what civilisation can learn. Education concerns how knowledge, skills and developmental capability are transmitted and developed among participants.**

Its relationship with developmental autonomy, guardianship and anti-capture architecture will be especially important.

---

### Emergency and Rescue Services
**Provisional status:** MISSING / DOMAIN BOUNDARY OPEN

Constitutional Emergency concerns extraordinary authority. It does not substitute for ordinary operational capabilities such as fire, rescue, ambulance, disaster response and civil protection.

These functions may form a Public Safety / Emergency Services domain or may divide among Health, Civil Security, Infrastructure and other domains.

---

### Food, Water and Essential Supply
**Provisional status:** MISSING AS OPERATING ARCHITECTURE / DOMAIN BOUNDARY OPEN

Economic and continuity principles can describe allocation, dependencies and resilience without themselves operating food production, water provision or essential-supply systems.

Further analysis should determine whether these belong under Economy and Resource Coordination, Infrastructure, environmental stewardship, or distinct operational domains.

---

### Housing and Settlement Provision
**Provisional status:** MISSING AS OPERATING ARCHITECTURE / DOMAIN BOUNDARY OPEN

Housing intersects property, economy, planning, infrastructure, protected spaces and participant needs. No comprehensive housing architecture was found in the initial scan.

It may ultimately be a Civil Planning subdivision rather than an independent domain.

---

### Environment and Planetary Stewardship
**Provisional status:** PRINCIPLE-RICH / OPERATIONALLY PARTIAL

The Concord contains substantial stewardship principles and planetary-scale reasoning, but this should not be mistaken automatically for an environmental management system.

Further source resolution is needed to determine whether environmental/ecological stewardship should become a distinct domain or a distributed responsibility with a coordinating domain.

## 5. Important Non-Equivalences Exposed by the Gap Scan

- Constitution != Ordinary Law
- Judiciary != Legal Code
- Constitutional Emergency != Emergency Services
- Threat Detection != Intelligence Service
- Emergency Authority != Defence Capability
- Economic Coordination != Food Production
- Resource Stewardship != Utility Operation
- Continuity != Infrastructure Operation
- Research != Education
- Research Intelligence != Security Intelligence
- Governance != Civil Planning
- Principle != Implementation
- Responsibility != Capability
- Domain Interface != Domain Ownership

---

## 6. V1.2 to V1.3 Implication

V1.2 should not be reorganised destructively while this map is incomplete.

Its current structure preserves development history and therefore has historical value in its own right.

The likely V1.3 transition should instead be:

**Preserve V1.2 -> Complete Domain Discovery -> Complete Gap Map -> Produce Migration Map -> Copy to V1.3 -> Reorganise the Copy Around Civilisational Domains -> Preserve V1.2 as Developmental Predecessor**

V1.3 should not merely reorganise existing material. It should make visible both:

1. domains with substantial existing architecture; and
2. domains or essential systems that remain undeveloped.

An empty or lightly populated domain marked **SYSTEM GAP** can therefore carry useful architectural information without pretending a solution exists.

- open-question registers;

Do not create a final domain folder merely because a conventional government normally has a department with that name. Domain boundaries must follow the civil function and Concord architecture rather than inherited administrative convention.
