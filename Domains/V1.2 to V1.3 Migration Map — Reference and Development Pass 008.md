# V1.2 to V1.3 Migration Map — Reference and Development Pass 008

**Status:** MIGRATION PLANNING / SOURCE-RESOLVED / NO FILE MOVEMENT AUTHORISED

## 1. Core finding

10_Reference_and_Development is a V1.2 catch-all combining material with fundamentally different civil functions and temporal states.

It should **not exist as a top-level V1.3 category**.

Its contents resolve principally into:

1. **Research** — live comparison, evidence, validation and external evaluation;
2. **Historical** — intellectual provenance, development history, legacy copies and superseded source states;
3. **Current architecture / owner domains** — where a source paper remains the authoritative current expression of a live Concord system;
4. **Applications / publication artefacts** — where a document is primarily a presentation, book or external-facing synthesis.

> **Reference Is a Relationship, Not a Civil Function.**
>
> **Development Is a State, Not a Permanent Architectural Home.**

## 2. Comparative Research

Current examples compare Concord with David Wood's technoprogressive governance commitments and Thomas Gangale's Martian charter.

### Classification
**RESEARCH / COMPARATIVE RESEARCH**

### Destination
Research / Comparative Research.

When a comparison closes:
- conclusions/current knowledge remain accessible to Research;
- completed study and source-state provenance are preserved by Historical;
- any validated architectural change routes to its legitimate owner.

Comparison with an external system does not give that external system authority within Concord.

> **Comparison Can Inform Architecture; It Does Not Import Authority.**

## 3. Empirical Evidence

The README explicitly distinguishes:
- evidence that Concord methodology has been used;
- evidence that individual Concord systems/principles are effective.

It also distinguishes observation, interpretation, hypothesis and unresolved questions.

### Classification
**RESEARCH / EVIDENCE AND VALIDATION**

### Destination
Research / Evidence and Validation.

Active empirical case studies, experiments, replication and validation belong to Research.

Historical should preserve completed evidence states, original records and superseded interpretations where retention is legitimate.

The evidence archive should not become a static Historical store while it is still being used to update confidence.

> **Evidence May Be Historical in Origin While Remaining Active in Research.**

Historical preserves what evidence existed and how it was understood at a time.
Research evaluates what that evidence currently supports.

## 4. Development History

The present Development_History material includes repository word-count records and maintenance/development notes.

### Classification
**HISTORICAL / DEVELOPMENT PROVENANCE**

### Destination
Historical / Concord Development History.

Repository state, reorganisation history, prior architecture and development-process records are precisely the kind of temporal/provenance information Historical exists to preserve.

They should not remain mixed with live Research.

## 5. External Evaluations

External evaluations are evidence about how outside evaluators interpreted or assessed Concord.

### Classification
Primarily **RESEARCH / EXTERNAL EVALUATION** while actively considered.

### Lifecycle
External evaluation received
-> provenance captured
-> Research assesses method/scope/evaluation space
-> findings may affect current architecture
-> original evaluation remains Historical evidence/provenance.

An external evaluation is not automatically authoritative merely because it is external.

> **External Evaluation != External Authority**

## 6. Foundation Papers

The Foundation Papers README explicitly states that these are principal source/foundational documents retained for:
- intellectual foundations;
- source material;
- early formulations;
- provenance;
- intellectual continuity.

It explicitly warns:

> **Foundation papers should not automatically be interpreted as current operational policy.**

It also states that current Concord documents take precedence where a foundation paper has been superseded or developed.

### Primary classification
**HISTORICAL / INTELLECTUAL AND ARCHITECTURAL PROVENANCE**

### Destination
Historical / Concord Intellectual Foundations.

This is one of the clearest cases where V1.3 should distinguish **origin** from **current authority**.

A foundational paper may be historically indispensable while having no current operational authority.

> **Foundational Importance != Current Authority**

### Paper 4 Blainey's Laws
The historical/foundation paper belongs in Historical provenance.

The current authoritative Ethical Kernel remains in Constitutional Core.

This preserves derivation without creating duplicate canonical authority.

## 7. Companion Papers and Companion Papers Vol II

These large collections are source/synthesis publications spanning multiple Concord systems.

They should not be treated as a single current civil domain merely because they are bound together as a publication.

### Treatment
Perform document-level/source-resolution during migration.

Possible classifications:
- Historical intellectual/development provenance;
- Research source material;
- current architecture duplicated elsewhere;
- public-facing publication/application;
- source of still-unmigrated architecture requiring extraction.

The bound volumes should generally be preserved intact in Historical as publication/source states even where current architecture has been extracted elsewhere.

> **A Book Can Preserve a System's Development Without Being the Operational Location of That System.**

## 8. Literature Engagement Volumes

These works compare Concord with external scholarship and intellectual traditions.

### Classification
**RESEARCH / LITERATURE ENGAGEMENT / COMPARATIVE RESEARCH**

### Destination
Research.

Completed editions should also have stable Historical provenance.

Any resulting architectural changes graduate to owner domains.

The literature itself does not become constitutional or operational merely because Concord engages with it.

## 9. PhD Critical Review

The document is a critical/presentation synthesis describing Concord as a research portfolio, its claimed contribution, methodology, relation to scholarship, strengths and limitations.

### Classification
**RESEARCH / EXTERNAL-ACADEMIC PRESENTATION AND SELF-EVALUATION**, with Historical provenance.

It is not civil architecture.

If retained as a publication-facing artefact, a copy/index may also be surfaced through Applications/Publications, but the underlying research/evaluation role remains clear.

## 10. Legacy Copies

### Classification
**HISTORICAL / LEGACY AND SUPERSEDED COPIES**

### Destination
Historical.

Legacy copies should not remain beside current architecture in a generic reference folder.

Their value is exactly that they preserve prior states, wording, context and provenance.

## 11. V1.3 information-state distinction

The dissolution of this folder reveals an important general model:

**CURRENT QUESTION / TEST / COMPARISON**
-> Research

**CURRENT AUTHORITATIVE CIVIL ARCHITECTURE**
-> legitimate owner domain / Constitutional / Cross-Domain Architecture / Portable Modules

**COMPLETED OR SUPERSEDED STATE**
-> Historical

**BOUND PUBLICATION / COMMUNICATION PRODUCT**
-> Applications and Implementations / Publications where useful

These roles may coexist through references without requiring duplicate ownership.

## 12. Source versus authority

A major migration rule follows:

> **Source != Authority**

A document can be:
- the historical source of an idea;
- evidence used to develop it;
- a publication explaining it;
- a superseded formulation;
- or the current authoritative architecture.

Those states must not be inferred merely from age, title or folder location.

For V1.3, every consequential source should be able to answer:

**What role does this document have now?**

## 13. Historical and Research boundary

This pass further sharpens the relationship:

### Research
asks:
- What do we currently know?
- What should be tested?
- What does current evidence support?
- What remains uncertain?
- What should change?

### Historical
asks:
- What was known/believed/proposed?
- What evidence existed?
- What changed?
- When and why?
- What was superseded?
- What was the evaluation space at the time?

Thus:

> **Research Maintains the Current Epistemic Frontier; Historical Preserves the Path by Which It Was Reached.**

## 15. Consequence for V1.3 root

10_Reference_and_Development should disappear.

No replacement root is required.

Its contents become intelligible because they are classified by actual function and temporal state.

This continues the V1.3 transformation from:

**repository organised around how Concord was written**

to:

**repository organised around what the civilisation is and does**.

## 16. Final finding

The category **Reference and Development** was useful during construction because it provided somewhere for important material that had not yet found a precise architectural home.

Its disappearance in V1.3 is therefore not a criticism of V1.2.

It is evidence that the architecture has matured enough to classify material that previously required a catch-all.

> **A Mature Architecture Should Reduce the Need for "Miscellaneous" by Making Relationships Explicit.**
