# V1.2 to V1.3 Migration Map — Coordination and Economy Pass 005

**Status:** MIGRATION PLANNING / SOURCE-RESOLVED / NO FILE MOVEMENT AUTHORISED

## 1. Finding

The V1.2 root name **Coordination and Economy** combines a genuine civil domain with several civilisation-wide methods/architectures.

The word **coordination** is too broad to remain part of the Economy domain name.

Many domains coordinate. Coordination itself is not automatically economic authority.

> **Coordination Mechanism != Economic Domain**
>
> **Economic Use != Economic Ownership**

## 2. Economy domain

The V1.3 Economy and Resource Coordination domain should own persistent functions such as:

- production;
- exchange;
- allocation;
- resource coordination;
- monetary/economic mechanisms;
- labour/employment economic interfaces;
- public finance interfaces;
- supply/logistics economic functions;
- public asset/resource economic stewardship;
- economic participation mechanisms where their primary function is exchange/allocation.

The domain remains constitutionally bounded:

> **Capability != Worth**
>
> **Contribution != Moral Worth**
>
> **Economic Success != Ethical Legitimacy**
>
> **Wealth != Constitutional Standing**

The present V1.2 folder contains less purely economic architecture than its name implies. This is itself useful evidence of the missing-domain work already identified: public finance, labour, supply logistics and related economic subdomains remain underdeveloped.

## 3. Ratchet V4 — Cross-Domain Coordination Architecture

Ratchet began as an economic/monetary proposal but its own canonical specification explicitly states that it expanded into a general protocol concerned with:

- participation;
- contribution;
- evidence;
- provenance;
- verification;
- contextual trust;
- reputation;
- reciprocity;
- exchange;
- community coordination;
- measurement;
- feedback;
- controlled persistence;
- correction.

It also explicitly states that the monetary architecture is **one application** of the more general coordination protocol.

### Classification

**CROSS-DOMAIN ARCHITECTURE / GENERAL COORDINATION PROTOCOL**

### V1.3 primary home

Cross-Domain Architecture / Ratchet Coordination Architecture

### Economy relationship

Economic and monetary mechanisms derived from Ratchet belong as implementations/interfaces within the Economy domain.

Candidate examples named in the source include:
- Progressive Monetary Backing;
- Personal Backing Balance;
- Personal Backing Ratio;
- Proof of Useful Contribution implementations;
- particular treasury structures.

These require separate testing and should not cause the general Ratchet protocol to be owned by Economy.

### Boundary

Ratchet itself states:
- Ratchet does not govern;
- Ratchet does not judge;
- Ratchet is not the Constitution;
- Ratchet is not KCS;
- Ratchet is not Metrics;
- Ratchet is not Roadway Equilibrium;
- Ratchet is not Recursive Oversight;
- Ratchet is not Continuity.

This supports cross-domain classification.

> **A Protocol That Coordinates Exchange May Serve Economy Without Becoming the Economy.**

## 4. Metrics Full — Cross-Domain Architecture

Metrics Full describes multidimensional observation of civilisation rather than an economic measurement system.

Its source explicitly says each domain is observed independently and relationships between domains are then examined.

### Classification

**CROSS-DOMAIN ARCHITECTURE / METRICS AND STATE OBSERVATION**

### Destination

Cross-Domain Architecture / Metrics and State Observation

### Domain relationships

Every domain may define/produce domain-relevant measurements.

Research validates measurement methods.

Historical preserves prior measurements, definitions and provenance.

CDT/State Map may consume appropriate metrics.

Governance and other domains may use evidence produced by metrics but do not automatically control the measurement system.

Participant-identifiable source data remains constrained by the participant-record safe-space architecture.

### Boundary

> **Measurement != Authority**
>
> **Measurement != Reality**
>
> **Metric Position != Participant Worth**

The use of constitutional language in the current paper does not require Metrics to remain in Constitutional Core or Economy. Constitutional constraints on measurement can remain constitutional; the observation architecture is cross-domain.

## 6. The word "Coordination"

V1.2 used coordination as a useful umbrella because Ratchet, Metrics and Economy developed together.

In V1.3 the umbrella can dissolve.

Different forms of coordination now have explicit homes:

- economic/resource coordination -> Economy;
- civil input routing -> Civil Attention;
- emergency coordination -> Cross-Domain Emergency Coordination;
- knowledge/dependency coordination -> KCS;
- development-state coordination -> CDT;
- general contribution/trust/reciprocity coordination -> Ratchet;
- spatial/development coordination -> Civil Planning;
- external coordination -> Intercivilisational Relations;
- collective legitimate decision coordination -> Governance.

Therefore no generic top-level **Coordination** domain or root is required.

## 9. Architectural consequence

This pass leaves the **Economy domain smaller than the old folder**, but more accurate.

That is not loss of economic architecture. It reveals how much of the old folder was actually general-purpose civil coordination machinery and how much Economy remains to be developed.

The resulting structure is:

**ECONOMY**
-> owns production, exchange, allocation and resource/economic coordination

**RATCHET**
-> cross-domain participation/contribution/trust/reciprocity protocol

**METRICS**
-> cross-domain observation and measurement

**ROADWAY EQUILIBRIUM**
-> portable reasoning/balance module

**HISTORICAL**
-> preserves their provenance and superseded states

This is a stronger civil topology than retaining them together because they happened to develop beside one another.
