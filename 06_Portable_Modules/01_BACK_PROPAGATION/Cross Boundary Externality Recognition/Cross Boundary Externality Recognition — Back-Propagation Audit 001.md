## Material deltas

1. **Context-declared materiality and MATERIALITY-UNCERTAIN** — one consequence may have different materiality states under different legitimate contexts. **G/C/F — incorporate.**
2. **Scoped standing record** — distinguish directly affected, authorised representative, consultation/advocacy standing, evidence-bearing third party and disputed/unknown standing; record scope/source/limits. **G/I/F — incorporate.**
3. **Responsibility Mapping ≠ Quantitative Apportionment.** Domain-specific apportionment remains external. **C/F — incorporate.**
4. **Dimensional classifications** — evidence, responsibility, authority/coordination, participation and case disposition may coexist without contradiction. **C/I — incorporate semantics, not portable IDs.**
5. **Actor-relative minimum response** — each actor chooses only within independently legitimate authority; shared action needs legitimate shared interface. **G/F — incorporate.**
6. **Assertion disposition ≠ Case disposition** — unsupported/refuted subclaim must not automatically close the underlying case. **G/F — incorporate.**
7. **Candidate Opened ≠ Escalation Required** — opening is triage. **C/F — incorporate.**
8. **Consequence-relative primary boundary** — one incident may contain multiple boundaries; map the primary boundary per claimed consequence plus material secondary boundaries. **G/I — incorporate.**
9. **Responsibility threshold** — distinguish possible/plausible/supported causal contribution from normative responsibility. **G/F — incorporate.**
10. **Causal Contributor ≠ Responsible Party** — permit non-agent environmental/system and unidentified contributors. **G/F — incorporate.**
11. **Passive absence ≠ refusal** — non-participation state requires an applicable pathway and affirmative refusal/non-participation, not mere absence or lack of authorisation. **G/F — incorporate.**
12. **Unsupported Assertion ≠ Automatically Separate Externality** — a new candidate requires a separately alleged material cross-boundary consequence. **C/F — incorporate.**
13. **Parallel voluntary coordination** — compatible independently chosen actions may be recorded without manufacturing shared authority. **G/I — incorporate.**
14. **Explicit review-trigger record** — evidential, materiality, standing, authority, consequence, recurrence or outcome changes may reopen review. **G/I — incorporate.**
15. **Dimension-specific unresolved state** — unresolved status should identify the affected dimension where possible. **C/I — incorporate.**
16. **Authorisation scope beyond standing** — authority to raise does not imply authority to receive protected information, negotiate, settle, bind or accept compensation. **G/F/I — incorporate.**
