## Material return candidates

The blind test exposed four narrow source-relevant clarifications:

1. **Capability Floor ≠ Capability Boundary/Ceiling.**  
   The floor is the minimum positive ability required for sufficient function; the ceiling/boundary limits unsupported excess.

2. **Operational Discretion Within Capability ≠ Authority to Expand Capability.**  
   Judgement in exercising an established capability does not create authority to enlarge one's own access, decision rights or persistence.

3. **Logging Capability Exercise ≠ Legitimising Capability Exercise.**  
   Provenance and auditability support accountability but cannot themselves justify a capability grant, assumption or exercise.

4. **Activation-time aggregate review.**  
   Where reasonably feasible, known overlapping roles, credentials, delegated access and persistent permissions should be checked before or at activation of consequential capability, not only during later review.
