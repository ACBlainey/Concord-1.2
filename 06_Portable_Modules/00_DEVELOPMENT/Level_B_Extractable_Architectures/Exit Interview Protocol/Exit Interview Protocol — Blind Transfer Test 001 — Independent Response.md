## K. EI-C classifications

## L. Failure-mode checks

Risks identified included testimony laundering, single-case overreaction, popularity capture, consent bundling, remedy laundering, interviewer conflict, selection-bias blindness, survivorship bias, category forcing, archive immortality, re-identification and metric gaming.

## M. Specification problems

The independent tester identified twelve bounded ambiguities:

1. no explicit NON-RESPONSE invitation/response state;
2. RECORD does not distinguish audio from written notes;
3. RETAIN does not encode duration/purpose;
4. PUBLIC QUOTATION is not an explicit consent field;
5. selective deletion is not fully proceduralised;
6. pattern thresholds are under-specified;
7. historical non-interview cases are under-specified;
8. active-contributor corroboration is under-specified;
9. no fallback where a directly involved interviewer is the only available interviewer;
10. EI-C classifications overlap without priority rules;
11. EI-C8 urgency threshold is not operationalised;
12. anonymisation/re-identification risk lacks a methodology.

## N. Final transfer assessment

> **EI-T3 — FUNCTIONAL TRANSFER**

The tester found v0.1 usable without Concord source context. Core safeguards transferred successfully: voluntary exit, granular consent, testimony-versus-fact discipline, privacy boundaries, pattern caution, severe-single-case referral, bounded routing, retention limits and resistance to popularity optimisation.

The remaining issues were recorded as specification ambiguities rather than fundamental architectural failures.
