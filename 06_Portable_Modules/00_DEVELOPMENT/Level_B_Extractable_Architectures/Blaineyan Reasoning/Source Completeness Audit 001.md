## 1. Purpose

This audit asks whether the first portable specification captures enough of the V1.2 Blaineyan Reasoning source to function as a faithful operational module without requiring the reader to reconstruct the method from the full book.

It is not an empirical validation of the methodology and does not test whether the method improves reasoning.

---

## 2. Audit rule

The portable module is not required to reproduce every philosophical discussion, historical reflection or Concord-specific application in the source book.

Material is required where omission would materially change:

- what the method is;
- how it is used;
- its epistemic boundaries;
- its failure modes;
- its operational constraints;
- its portability;
- its stated evidential status;
- its proposed testing programme.

## 4. Material intentionally not promoted into the operational core

Several substantial discussions in the source book are not necessary to operate the portable method and should not be silently converted into requirements.

### 4.1 Reasoning as natural law

The source considers whether reasoning methodologies are cultural inventions, emergent properties of intelligence, or manifestations of deeper natural principles.

This remains speculative research territory.

It is not required to use Blaineyan Reasoning and is therefore not part of the operational specification.

### 4.2 Methodologies as civilisational compression

The source explores whether preserved methodologies could function as regenerative civilisational memory and permit lost knowledge to be reconstructed.

This is an important Concord-level implication, but it is not necessary for standalone use of the method.

### 4.3 AI alignment through methodology

The source proposes methodological alignment as a possible complement to architectural approaches.

This is a research hypothesis, not an established property of Blaineyan Reasoning. It should not become a claim of the portable module without separate evidence.

### 4.4 Educational and professional implications

The source discusses possible educational and professional applications.

The portable specification permits such testing but does not claim demonstrated educational benefit.

### 4.5 Authorial and autobiographical material

The book contains important provenance concerning the author's reasoning history, isolation, education, dyslexia, human-AI collaboration and development of the Concord.

This material remains relevant to historical interpretation of the source but is not required for an operational portable specification.

## 5. Reality Trees boundary

The audit confirms that the portable specification now handles this correctly.

Reality Trees are historically and methodologically associated with Blaineyan Reasoning and can be used during branch generation and uncertainty representation.

However:

> **Blaineyan Reasoning → may use → Reality Trees**

does not imply:

> **Blaineyan Reasoning = Reality Trees**

or:

> **Reality Trees require Blaineyan Reasoning**

The complete Reality Trees method should remain independently portable.

## 6. Case-study coverage

The V1 Prison Planet case study materially improves the portable specification because it provides a preserved worked reasoning journey rather than only retrospective description.

It supports the operational pattern:

> **Speculative Question → Branching Possibility Space → Extreme Exploration → Failure-Mode Discovery → Pruning / Reconsideration → Grounded Question → Practical Design Insight**

It also supports the Scout/Cartographer distinction and the rule:

> **Separate exploration from validation.**

The case study should remain provenance evidence rather than being rewritten as though its exploratory branches were established claims.

---

## 7. Audit additions

The first specification was already strong on operational procedure but compressed several source-level concepts too aggressively.

This audit therefore added:

1. **Methodology as an abstraction layer** — explicit distinction between cognitive architecture/capability and reasoning methodology.
2. **Method as an independent design variable** — the testable proposition that methodology can be varied without changing underlying architecture.
3. **Portability and substrate independence** — the module must not assume human cognition, a particular AI architecture, Concord adoption or human-AI collaboration.
4. **Methodological fluency** — repeated-use and longitudinal effects are preserved as research questions rather than assumed benefits.

These additions improve fidelity without importing the source book's broader speculative claims into the operational core.

---

## 8. Remaining non-source-completeness work

No blocking source omission was identified in this first audit.

The remaining development questions are different:

- Can another instance apply the specification without access to the source book?
- Does it preserve epistemic labels under pressure to reach a conclusion?
- Does it branch usefully without generating uncontrolled recursion?
- Does it recognise when the apparent problem is misframed?
- Does it hand off to evidence rather than treating reasoning as validation?
- Can it operate without Reality Trees?
- Can it use Reality Trees without conflating the two methods?
- Does the specification transfer to a non-Concord problem?
- Are the stopping conditions operationally adequate?

These require usability and comparative testing rather than further source extraction.

---

## 9. Audit conclusion

**Finding:** The available source corpus is sufficient for the first full portable Blaineyan Reasoning module.

The development specification captures the operational core, epistemic safeguards, recursion controls, limitations, worked-case logic and proposed validation programme. The omissions found during this audit concerned framing and portability rather than missing operational stages and have been corrected.

No further theory-writing is required before testing.

> **Next gate: blind usability / transfer testing.**

A successful test would not empirically validate Blaineyan Reasoning as superior. It would establish the narrower result that the portable specification is sufficiently complete and intelligible for independent application.
