---

## 1. Purpose

This retest asks whether the BR-BTT-001 revisions improve operational portability in a fresh domain without merely fitting the previous municipal-water case.

Primary questions:

1. Can a fresh independent instance apply version 0.2 without the source corpus?
2. Are Compare, Reconsider, Re-ground and Refine operationally distinguishable?
3. Do the proportional branch controls bound exploration without suppressing materially different alternatives?
4. Is the generic branch representation usable without Reality Trees?
5. Does the structured handoff packet improve the transition to specialist methods?
6. Are the adjacent epistemic/output labels easier to apply?

This remains a portability/usability test, not a test of superiority over other methodologies.

## 3. Fresh-domain test problem

### Recovery of a deteriorating community archive

A fictional volunteer-run local-history archive has accumulated approximately forty years of material.

Its collection includes paper records, photographs, cassette recordings, VHS tapes, optical discs, external hard drives and born-digital files created in many different software formats.

The archive's committee initially frames the problem as:

> **“Which scanner should we buy so we can digitise the archive before it deteriorates?”**

Known constraints:

- the archive has limited money and volunteer time;
- some physical materials are visibly deteriorating;
- the collection has never been comprehensively inventoried;
- some digital storage devices are old and may no longer be reliable;
- filenames and folder structures are inconsistent;
- provenance and ownership information are incomplete for some items;
- some material may contain personal or sensitive information;
- some formats may require obsolete hardware or software;
- volunteers have mixed technical skills;
- the archive does not know which material is unique, duplicated elsewhere or replaceable;
- digitisation itself can create new preservation, metadata, storage and access obligations;
- no detailed collection measurements, legal determinations or conservation assessments are supplied;
- professional archival, conservation, legal and technical expertise may be consulted later.

The task is **not** to select a scanner or produce a final archival-preservation plan.

Use Blaineyan Reasoning to determine what the archive should investigate and whether the original problem should be reformulated.

## 5. Evaluation dimensions

**T1 Epistemic discipline** — statuses remain distinguishable and unsupported facts are not invented.

**T2 Compare usability** — Compare contributes a recognisable operation rather than appearing as a redundant label.

**T3 Genuine problem reformulation** — scanner/product framing is tested at a more useful functional level if justified.

**T4 Useful bounded branching** — materially distinct branches are preserved while branch expansion remains controlled.

**T5 Generic branch representation** — branching works without Reality Trees.

**T6 Failure-mode and disposition discipline** — branches are examined and meaningfully retained/pruned/deferred.

**T7 Reconsider distinction** — tester can identify what changed in framing/assumptions/branch structure.

**T8 Re-ground distinction** — tester can reconnect the reconsidered frame to evidence/constraints and identify deficits.

**T9 Refine distinction** — tester can state the resulting improved question/model/test and whether another cycle is needed.

**T10 Handoff packet usability** — required handoff fields form a coherent specialist transition.

**T11 Resource/recursion control** — proportional branch control is usable without an arbitrary universal numerical rule.

**T12 Cross-domain specification sufficiency** — version 0.2 is independently usable in this fresh domain without Concord-specific dependencies.

## 8. Advancement boundary

It would not establish methodological superiority, human usability, empirical novelty or general effectiveness.

A successful retest would permit **portable-package graduation review**, not automatic graduation.

> **Repeated portability evidence ≠ comparative effectiveness evidence.**
