## 6. Unpredicted useful findings

In accordance with the rule added to the BTT-002 frozen key, useful findings not added retrospectively to the score are preserved separately.

The clean evaluator exposed or sharpened:

1. **Scoped state must become explicitly first-class.** One object may be VALIDATED in one scope and PROVISIONAL in another.
2. **Procedure versus capability needs a boundary rule.** Not every procedural rule is an operational capability.
3. **Relation scope needs explicit qualifiers.** SUPERSEDES must be able to mean “supersedes for generations 4+” rather than globally.
4. **Redacted derivative relation needs clarification.** A redacted lesson can derive from a sealed source without exposing it.
5. **Capability vocabulary should avoid AVAILABLE collision.**
6. **Negative-search lifecycle needs guidance.** A later successful search should supersede/correct the earlier bounded search result without pretending the earlier search was false.
7. **Dependency-record minimum may be useful at the interface boundary.**
8. **Permanent aggregation of contribution evidence deserves an explicit anti-collapse warning.**

These are specification/interface refinements, not kernel failures.

## 7. Authorised bounded revision

Before producing the graduation candidate, a bounded v0.3 revision may:

- make state assignments explicitly scope-qualified;
- clarify procedure versus operational capability;
- add relation qualifiers such as scope/context without creating a dependency ontology;
- clarify redacted-derivative representation;
- disambiguate stored-object availability from capability activation vocabulary;
- specify negative-search-result lifecycle;
- define only the minimum dependency-record interface needed to hand off to KCS Change Propagation;
- strengthen the warning against permanent universal aggregation of contextual contribution evidence.

No expansion into governance, truth authority, continuity or dependency propagation is authorised.

## 8. PMEDG finding

The methodological issue first observed in BTT-001 recurred in BTT-002:

> **Blind tests can produce useful unpredicted findings that must be preserved without retroactively altering the frozen prediction score.**

This is now a repeated empirical workflow finding rather than a one-test observation.

PMEDG should therefore be amended in its next revision to require:

> **Unpredicted findings are recorded separately from frozen-prediction scoring. They may justify bounded revision, but must never be inserted retrospectively into the scored prediction set.**
