# Knowledge Control System — Blind Transfer Test 002 — Expected Findings and Evaluation Key

**Module:** Knowledge Control System — Portable Specification v0.2  
**Test:** KCS-BTT-002  
**Status:** FROZEN BEFORE INDEPENDENT RESPONSE / DO NOT PROVIDE TO TESTER  
**Date:** September 2026

## 1. Purpose

This key freezes expected findings before the independent BTT-002 response.

BTT-002 is a delta/convergence test. It evaluates both general transfer and whether the bounded revisions introduced after BTT-001 behave as intended.

No prediction may be added to the scored set after the response is seen. Unpredicted useful findings must be preserved separately.

## 2. Frozen predictions

### State dimensionality

**KCS2-P01** — The evaluator will keep epistemic, retrieval/activity, integrity, access/visibility, historical/version and capability-operational dimensions materially separate.

**KCS2-P02** — AR-12 can remain historically valid/relevant for earlier hardware even if AR-12B is current for later hardware.

**KCS2-P03** — AR-12B's strong evidence for generations 4–5 will not automatically make it VALIDATED for generation 6.

**KCS2-P04** — Generation-6 applicability will be represented as PROVISIONAL/UNCERTAIN or equivalent pending host qualification.

**KCS2-P05** — RESTRICTED access to Helios-6 material will not be confused with epistemic rejection.

### Typed relations

**KCS2-P06** — AR-12B will be related to AR-12 through a justified relation such as SUPERSEDES within a bounded hardware/context scope rather than universal deletion of AR-12.

**KCS2-P07** — Qualification evidence will SUPPORT AR-12B within its tested generations.

**KCS2-P08** — The Helios-4 anomaly will not be represented as conclusively CONTRADICTING AR-12 because causal attribution remained unresolved.

**KCS2-P09** — The retired operator note will be represented as RELATED_TO or another cautious relation rather than automatically CORRECTS/SUPERSEDES the formal procedure.

**KCS2-P10** — The new generation-6 simulation will QUALIFY/NARROW or otherwise relate to generation-6 applicability without rewriting AR-12B's generations 4–5 history.

### State-assignment provenance

**KCS2-P11** — Consequential states will be represented with provenance/basis/process rather than as ungrounded labels.

**KCS2-P12** — KCS will not itself manufacture the legitimacy to declare a procedure validated, restricted or authorised.

### Bounded retrieval

**KCS2-P13** — Retrieval will be bounded to the Helios-9 qualification question rather than returning the entire antenna archive.

**KCS2-P14** — The package will contain current orientation, directly material evidence, uncertainty, provenance/history pointers, search limitations and on-demand deeper material.

**KCS2-P15** — The handbook summary will be useful orientation but insufficient as the sole representation.

**KCS2-P16** — Historical AR-12/anomaly information will remain retrievable because it may explain design lineage and hardware-specific applicability.

### Search absence

**KCS2-P17** — Missing emulator search will be represented as a bounded negative search result rather than nonexistence.

**KCS2-P18** — Missing supplier fatigue study will preserve multiple possible explanations rather than selecting one without evidence.

**KCS2-P19** — Search scope/time/sources/limitations will be treated as material metadata.

### Capability state

**KCS2-P20** — Diag-7 stored files may be AVAILABLE while operational capability is DORMANT/UNVERIFIED or equivalent.

**KCS2-P21** — Obsolete OS/library will trigger compatibility/dependency uncertainty and revalidation requirement.

**KCS2-P22** — Retrieval of Diag-7 will not itself make the capability operational.

### Contribution evidence

**KCS2-P23** — Engineer performance evidence may be stored contextually if legitimately collected.

**KCS2-P24** — A permanent universal mission-reliability index will be challenged as a contribution-to-worth/generalisation risk.

**KCS2-P25** — Automatic approval authority will not be derived from ranking alone.

### Privacy/provenance

**KCS2-P26** — Restricted Helios-6 material can remain represented in institutional memory without universal content visibility.

**KCS2-P27** — Redacted lessons/pointers can coexist with protected underlying records.

**KCS2-P28** — KCS will defer legitimacy of restriction/access to an external host process.

### New evidence/history

**KCS2-P29** — The 85-minute simulation will be a new object with its own provenance and provisional state.

**KCS2-P30** — It will not retroactively invalidate AR-12B for generations 4–5.

**KCS2-P31** — Current generation-6 guidance can change later without erasing what was believed at launch.

### Dependency boundary

**KCS2-P32** — KCS may record references/relations/dependency records concerning training, command templates, simulator defaults, contingency procedures and qualification plans.

**KCS2-P33** — Material downstream review/propagation will be handed to KCS Change Propagation or another external dependency-review process.

**KCS2-P34** — KCS will not absorb propagation authority merely because it stores the relevant objects.

### Continuity boundary

**KCS2-P35** — KCS will materially support deep-time handover through provenance, state, history and retrieval.

**KCS2-P36** — The evaluator will distinguish preserved knowledge from recoverable organisational/technical function.

### Failure-mode exposure

**KCS2-P37** — Handbook compression exposes KCS-F6 and potentially KCS-F3.

**KCS2-P38** — Tens of thousands of records expose KCS-F4/F5.

**KCS2-P39** — Diag-7 exposes KCS-F13.

**KCS2-P40** — Missing supplier/emulator records expose KCS-F15.

**KCS2-P41** — Reliability index/automatic authority exposes KCS-F9/F10 and potentially F11.

**KCS2-P42** — Restricted investigation exposes KCS-F14.

**KCS2-P43** — Pressure to make KCS execute downstream review exposes KCS-F16.

### v0.2 revision-pressure predictions

**KCS2-P44** — The six-dimensional state model will be clearer than v0.1 but may still expose implementation complexity or vocabulary overlap.

**KCS2-P45** — Typed relations will improve representation but scope/context of SUPERSEDES and similar relations may need explicit relation qualifiers.

**KCS2-P46** — State-assignment provenance will improve auditability but the host process semantics will remain intentionally external.

**KCS2-P47** — The bounded retrieval contract will improve testability but ranking/relevance remains host/domain dependent.

**KCS2-P48** — Search-result objects will prevent absence overclaim but may need lifecycle rules if later searches find the missing object.

**KCS2-P49** — Capability-state separation will resolve the principal Tool-R/Diag-7 ambiguity from v0.1.

**KCS2-P50** — External interface contracts will preserve the KCS/KCP and KCS/Continuity boundaries without requiring either external module to be embedded.

## 3. Fundamental-failure indicators

Material evidence against the current extraction would include:

1. State dimensions cannot remain separate in the scenario.
2. AR-12 must be erased to make AR-12B current.
3. Generation-6 applicability cannot be represented without falsely validating or rejecting AR-12B.
4. Strong provenance forces the operator note to become formal procedure.
5. Negative searches necessarily become claims of nonexistence.
6. Stored Diag-7 files necessarily imply operational capability.
7. Contribution metrics necessarily create approval authority.
8. Historical preservation requires disclosure of restricted Helios-6 details.
9. KCS must execute downstream propagation itself.
10. KCS cannot support deep-time handover without becoming the continuity system.
11. The architecture requires Concord-specific governance to operate.
12. The v0.2 revisions introduce contradictions severe enough that v0.1 was more coherent.

## 4. Evaluation rule

After the independent response:

- compare KCS2-P01–KCS2-P50;
- classify each CONFIRMED, PARTIALLY CONFIRMED, CONTRADICTED, NOT TESTED or AMBIGUOUS;
- preserve any unpredicted useful findings separately;
- assess all 12 fundamental-failure indicators;
- distinguish implementation complexity from portable-mechanism failure;
- authorise only bounded revisions supported by the response.

BTT-002 should answer:

> **Did the BTT-001 revisions remove the main ambiguities without creating new architectural coupling or overreach?**
