# Continuity Protocol — Portable-Package Graduation Review 001

**Candidate:** Continuity Protocol — Portable Specification v0.3  
**Development level:** Level B — Extractable Architecture  
**Review status:** COMPLETE  
**Date:** 23 September 2026

## 1. Review question

Does Continuity Protocol v0.3 satisfy the development threshold for release as a standalone portable module, without requiring adoption of Concord-specific institutions or hidden source context?

## 2. Evidence reviewed

The graduation review considers the complete development record: source-resolution and extraction audit; portable specification history; both frozen blind-test briefs and keys; both independent responses and post-test evaluations; and the v0.3 bounded clarifications.

The two blind tests were performed in materially different fictional non-Concord domains.

## 3. Source resolution

**PASS.**

The source-resolution audit identified a coherent extractable architecture rather than a loose thematic collection.

The portable core is:

> **Preserve what remains valuable; adapt what must change; retain enough knowledge, provenance and enabling capability to recover legitimate function after disruption.**

No BTT result exposed a missing source mechanism requiring renewed source recovery. ESCP remains binding: completed source resolution is not universal proof that no further relevant source exists.

## 4. Standalone completeness

**PASS.**

v0.3 supplies, without Concord-specific institutional assumptions: required inputs; continuity-object resolution; CS1–CS6; disruption envelope; RTO/RPO; MAF; dependency evidence state and criticality; recovery-basis construction; correlated-redundancy analysis; triggers; restoration; succession; verification; review/adaptation; provenance; failure-memory handling; degraded continuity; irrecoverable-loss handling; recursive continuity; CP-C1–CP-C10; epistemic/ESCP safeguards; non-authority boundary; and a minimal operating record/procedure.

A competent independent user can apply the module without reading the Concord.

## 5. Hidden dependency review

**PASS.**

Adjacent methods such as Legacy Ladder, KCS and Reality Trees are explicitly separate or optional. None is required to perform the core protocol.

Legitimacy, legal authority, ethics, identity assumptions and domain-specific MAF definitions are correctly external inputs rather than silently imported Concord conclusions.

## 6. Internal consistency

**PASS.**

BTT-001 exposed C2/C7/C9 and state-ladder pressure. v0.2 resolved the central ambiguity.

BTT-002 then successfully pressured RTO versus RPO, MAF, disruption-envelope scope, evidence states, alternative implementations, succession with independently valid authority but incomplete handoff, stale continuity mechanisms, and obsolete yet technically actionable artefacts.

v0.3 adds bounded clarification for standby CS5 versus present CS6, partial versus aggregate target success, complete MAF scope, dependency criticality across alternative recovery paths, and non-contradictory combined classifications.

No internal contradiction requiring structural redesign remains evident.

## 7. Blind-transfer record

**PASS.**

CP-BTT-001: **STRONG TRANSFER — 20/20 frozen predictions materially confirmed.**

CP-BTT-002: **GRADUATION-CANDIDATE TRANSFER — 22/22 frozen predictions materially confirmed.**

Combined:

> **42/42 frozen predictions materially confirmed across two materially different non-Concord domains.**

The tests demonstrate specification-level transfer, not empirical validation in the tested real-world domains.

## 8. Claim-boundary review

**PASS.**

The module does not claim universal empirical validation, legal compliance, ethical legitimacy, authority creation, guaranteed recovery, exhaustive dependency discovery, proof of personal identity, superiority to other continuity frameworks, recovery outside the declared disruption envelope, or that a preserved implementation must survive unchanged.

The release should retain these boundaries.

## 9. Failure-mode coverage

**PASS.**

The module explicitly covers archive theatre, implementation immortality, provenance/rationale loss, dependency-chain blindness, obsolete-state resurrection, continuity capture, single-point preservation, correlated redundancy, interpretation decay, restoration without verification, succession without handoff, succession-as-legitimacy, over/under-preservation, correction-capacity loss and continuity fossilisation.

## 10. Epistemic discipline

**PASS.**

The module preserves VERIFIED, DOCUMENTED, INFERRED and UNRESOLVED evidence distinctions and prevents absence-in-search becoming proof of nonexistence, named dependencies becoming proof of recoverability, one tested scenario becoming universal recovery evidence, or formal designation becoming proof of current correctness.

## 11. Portability review

**PASS.**

The architecture transferred independently to a diagnostic-laboratory continuity problem and an island-water-infrastructure continuity problem without requiring Concord governance or Concord-specific institutions.

These tests are evidence of abstraction transfer, not domain certification.

## 12. Remaining limitations

Legitimate release boundaries remain: domain experts must define legitimate function and MAF; RTO/RPO values are external requirements; dependency discovery may remain incomplete; high-consequence domains require domain-specific validation; personal identity continuity requires an external identity model; legal/ethical/authority questions remain outside the module; and practical recovery evidence remains scenario-specific.

No unresolved limitation requires another development blind test before release.

## 13. Graduation decision

> **GRADUATION CONDITIONS SATISFIED AT SPECIFICATION LEVEL**

The module has a source-resolved portable core, standalone operational specification, two frozen blind-transfer tests, 42/42 materially confirmed predictions, no blocking failure, bounded post-test refinements, explicit non-authority and epistemic boundaries, and no required hidden Concord dependency.

Therefore:

> **CONTINUITY PROTOCOL v0.3 IS AUTHORISED FOR GRADUATION TO PORTABLE MODULE v1.0.**
