# Blind Tester Response — FBS-BTT-001

I do not assign a result class. I apply only the supplied portable specification and test brief.

---

## 1. Immediate problem definition

Next Wednesday, between 17:00 and 21:00, the building’s only accessible ground-floor meeting room must be empty if the contractor’s ceiling-leak repair proceeds. The adult education group is scheduled to use that same room from 18:00 to 20:00 for a class of 16 people, including two wheelchair users. A decision is required within 48 hours on how to handle maintenance access and temporary use/accommodation for that window.

This definition does not assume that the repair must proceed, that the class must move, that any group must surrender space, or that any historical dispute must be resolved.

---

## 2. Required function

The required function is:

> To make possible a legitimate, bounded decision on maintenance access to the accessible ground-floor meeting room for the specified window and, if the repair proceeds, on temporary safe and accessible accommodation or alternative arrangements for the affected adult education class.

Accessibility and safety are constraints on the function. The function does not include resolving building-wide rule compliance, historical storage disputes, noise disputes, accusations of preferential treatment, or the wider facilities agreement.

---

## 3. Minimum necessary participants

### Required for the initial bounded function

1. **Building manager**  
   Necessary because the building manager convened the meeting, can authorise temporary room changes for maintenance, and would implement or authorise maintenance access arrangements.

2. **Adult education group representative**  
   Necessary because the adult education group is the directly displaced user. It can speak to the class’s needs, accessibility requirements, timing, and — after the mid-test update — budget constraints. It would also have to implement or accept relocation, splitting, rescheduling, cancellation, or remote alternatives.

3. **Contractor or building manager as contractor conduit**  
   The contractor is not necessarily a negotiating participant, but the contractor’s constraints and risk warnings are necessary evidence: the repair requires the room empty 17:00–21:00, and delay may worsen damage.

### Conditionally required if a specific option depends on them

4. **Funding/budget authority**  
   After the mid-test update, if the community-centre option is chosen, the authority that can legitimately pay or approve the £45 becomes relevant to that option. This may be the adult education group’s budget holder, the building maintenance budget authority, or another participant if they voluntarily offer payment.

5. **Any group whose space or consent is required by a proposed option**  
   For example, if an option requires use of theatre-controlled space, then the theatre group becomes necessary for that sub-decision. If an option requires another participant to fund the external room, that participant becomes necessary for that funding sub-decision.

### Not initially required merely because they are present or have wider grievances

- **Theatre group** is not initially required for the core function unless a proposed option uses its space, its consent, or unless repair effects materially interfere with its upstairs use. Its previous objection to surrendering space is relevant only if a specific option requires its space.
- **Food-support charity** has no normal Wednesday evening operation and is not shown to be materially required.
- **Neighbourhood association** has no Wednesday booking and is not shown to be materially required. Its historical disputes with other groups do not make it a necessary participant for the repair/access function.
- The two wheelchair users are materially affected, but the adult education group can represent the class unless it cannot adequately represent accessibility needs; if not, direct confirmation from affected users or an accessibility representative should be obtained.

The initial bounded negotiation should therefore be small: building manager, adult education group, and contractor evidence as needed. Other groups may observe or provide evidence only if a material dependency is demonstrated.

## 7. Proportionate interaction grammar

Because the issue is urgent, the grammar should be simple but explicit.

### Remit

Decide the next Wednesday maintenance-access and temporary accommodation problem within 48 hours. No broader relationship negotiation.

### Participants and roles

- Building manager: chair or convener; maintenance authority; recordkeeper.
- Adult education representative: required participant for class needs and accommodation.
- Contractor: evidence source, not decision-maker.
- Other groups: present only as observers/evidence providers unless a material dependency is demonstrated.
- Funding authority: added only if the community-centre or other paid option requires it.

### Agenda

1. Confirm the immediate problem and required function.
2. Separate known, disputed, missing evidence.
3. Rule on initial scope and exclusions.
4. Identify any claimed dependencies and test them.
5. Generate functionally relevant options.
6. Decide, conditionally decide, trial, request evidence, or record no agreement.
7. Record outcome, non-inference conditions, and review conditions.

### Evidence procedure

Only known facts and verifiable information should be used. Disputed historical claims are not evidence for the current function unless a material dependency is demonstrated. Missing information should be identified and, where possible, obtained.

### Decision procedure

Required participants should seek agreement. Matters within the building manager’s existing maintenance authority may be decided by the building manager after evidence. Voluntary commitments — such as payment, use of another group’s space, or acceptance of a changed class format — require the consent of the relevant participant or authority. No participant may rewrite the entire facilities agreement in this meeting.

### Scope-expansion procedure

Any proposed expansion must state:

1. the dependency connecting it to the current function;
2. why the dependency is material;
3. why the function cannot be addressed without it;
4. the minimum additional scope;
5. any additional participants or evidence;
6. what remains excluded after expansion.

Expansion requires explicit agreement or authorisation under applicable rules. Otherwise, the matter is recorded as outside scope.

### Violations

If a participant introduces excluded demands, cross-domain threats, or bypasses agreed procedure, record it separately. First instance may be treated as a scope-expansion request and ruled out. Persistent or obstructive repetition may be recorded as a protocol violation.

### Pause and withdrawal

If contagion persists, pause, restate the grammar, and return to the function. Withdrawal from the bounded forum does not imply wider consequences or recognition of claims.

### Interpretation and non-inference

Agreement or non-agreement is bounded to the room/repair issue. Participation does not imply recognition of historical claims or acceptance of wider governance.

## 8. Handling the two attempts to reopen older disputes

At the start:

> Neighbourhood representative: “Before we discuss rooms, we need to settle the whole pattern of rule-breaking in this building.”

> Theatre representative: “If old disputes are being reopened, then we should also discuss the accusations made against us last month.”

Protocol response:

1. Acknowledge the statements.
2. Treat them as proposed scope expansions or contagion attempts.
3. Apply the dependency test:
   - What dependency connects the whole pattern of rule-breaking to the leak repair and accessible accommodation?
   - Is it material?
   - Can the repair/access problem be addressed without resolving it?
   - What minimum additional scope would be required?
4. On the supplied facts, no material dependency is demonstrated. The immediate function can be addressed without settling historical rule-breaking or last month’s accusations.
5. Therefore, rule both matters outside the initial scope.
6. Record them as unresolved wider matters, not as dismissed facts.
7. State that they may be addressed in a separate appropriate forum.
8. Return to the immediate problem.
9. If either party continues to block the immediate function with historical demands after this ruling, record a protocol violation and consider pause.

The chair should not resolve the historical allegations. It should not treat the room discussion as implying anything about them.

## 9. At least one matter that could legitimately require scope expansion

A genuine dependency could arise if:

In that case, the scope may legitimately expand narrowly to include the funding-authority question: who may legitimately pay or authorise the £45, and under what conditions. This expansion is tied directly to making the accessible accommodation option possible. It does not reopen historical disputes.

Another possible dependency: if a proposed option requires the theatre group’s adjacent rehearsal room, and that room is confirmed as accessible and large enough, then the theatre group’s consent and scheduling become a genuine dependency for that option. The theatre group would be added only for that narrow room-use decision.

Another possible dependency: if the repair’s noise, access, or timing materially prevents the theatre group’s upstairs use, then the theatre group may need to be included for mitigation of that specific interference. That is different from reopening old disputes.

## 10. Functionally relevant options — initial

1. **Proceed with repair; relocate the adult education class externally.**  
   Use the nearby community centre if availability, accessibility, and price are confirmed.

2. **Proceed with repair; adapt internally.**  
   Use the smaller accessible ground-floor office for up to 8 people and split, rotate, or reschedule the remaining class members. This may not fully satisfy the 16-person class and needs careful accessibility verification.

3. **Delay the repair by one week.**  
   The adult education class uses the room as normal. The building manager monitors the leak and weather risk. This accepts the contractor’s warning that further rain could worsen damage.

4. **Proceed with repair; cancel, reschedule, or deliver the class remotely.**  
   This requires adult education group consent and may disadvantage wheelchair users or others if remote access is inadequate.

5. **Conditional/trial option.**  
   Proceed with repair only if an accessible alternative is secured by a deadline; otherwise delay. Or proceed with a one-week trial of an alternative arrangement and review.

6. **No agreement.**  
   The bounded forum records no agreement on accommodation. The building manager may use existing temporary maintenance authority under the facilities agreement. Historical disputes remain unresolved.

## 11. Possible bounded outcomes, including legitimate non-agreement

Permitted outcomes include:

- **Accept:** repair proceeds and a specific accommodation arrangement is agreed.
- **Reject:** a proposed arrangement is rejected.
- **Modify:** an option is changed, e.g. split class, different time, different room.
- **Temporary Trial:** try an arrangement for next Wednesday and review.
- **Conditional Acceptance:** repair proceeds only if funding or venue conditions are met.
- **Request Functionally Relevant Evidence:** obtain funding authority, venue accessibility, or class constraints.
- **No Agreement:** no mutually acceptable accommodation package is reached.

Legitimate non-agreement is a bounded outcome. It does not automatically mean the protocol failed, the wider relationship failed, or the historical claims are valid. If the building manager then uses existing maintenance authority, that is a separate exercise of authority under the facilities agreement. It is not participant agreement.

## 14. Stopping condition and reopening/review condition

The bounded process stops when:

- no agreement is recorded after the timebox;

### Reopening/review conditions

Review or reopen if:

- the weather or leak risk changes materially;
- the contractor cancels or changes availability;
- the community centre becomes available, unavailable, or changes price;
- funding authority is confirmed or denied;
- accessibility of any proposed alternative is challenged;
- repair materially affects another building user;
- a genuine dependency to another issue is demonstrated;
- the same room-access problem recurs, prompting review of a standing interface;
- historical disputes are later addressed through a separate appropriate process.

The decision should expire after next Wednesday unless explicitly renewed.

## 16. Adversarial event — protocol application

The neighbourhood representative says:

> “We will agree not to object to whatever room solution you choose, but only if the adult education group admits it broke the building rules in the storage dispute three months ago.”

Applying the protocol:

### 1. Is it a legitimate functional dependency?

On the supplied facts, no. The neighbourhood association has no Wednesday booking. The building manager can authorise temporary room changes for maintenance. It is not supplied that the neighbourhood association’s non-objection is required for the room solution. Even if their non-objection were practically relevant, the admission about the storage dispute is not necessary to make the room function possible. The dependency is not material.

### 2. Is it an explicitly proposed scope linkage?

Yes. It explicitly conditions non-objection to the room solution on an admission about a separate historical storage dispute.

### 3. Is it a protocol violation under the agreed grammar?

If the agreed grammar has excluded historical disputes, then this is a cross-domain linkage and out of order. As a first instance, it should be treated as a scope-expansion request, tested, and ruled out. If the representative repeats it or uses it to block the bounded function after the ruling, it should be recorded as a participant protocol violation.

### 4. Is it an adversarial exploitation attempt?

It has the structural character of adversarial exploitation: a participant offers procedural consent or non-objection in exchange for an unrelated historical concession. Intent is not supplied, so it should not be labelled solely as F4 on intent alone. But structurally it is an attempt to leverage the current process to extract something outside its function.

### 5. Combination requiring careful distinction

The best description is:

> An explicitly proposed scope linkage that fails the dependency test, has features of adversarial exploitation, and becomes a protocol violation if persisted in after being ruled out.

The chair should say, in substance:

> “Your non-objection is noted. It is only a material dependency if the room solution genuinely requires it. On the current facts, it does not. The storage admission is outside this forum’s scope and cannot be made a condition of the room decision. If you can demonstrate a genuine dependency, state it. Otherwise, we proceed on the repair and accommodation function. The storage allegation remains unresolved and unaddressed here.”

The historical storage allegation itself must not be resolved unless the current function genuinely requires it. It does not.

## 17. Required failure diagnosis if no agreement occurs and the building manager acts

Suppose the meeting ends without agreement and the building manager uses whatever temporary maintenance authority the facilities agreement legitimately provides.

### What can be concluded

- The bounded negotiation did not produce an agreed accommodation/room solution.
- The building manager exercised separate authority under the facilities agreement for temporary maintenance.
- The historical disputes remain unresolved and outside the bounded result.
- Any protocol violations or adversarial linkages should be recorded separately from the substantive outcome.
- The failure, if any, is contained at the functional resolution unless a genuine dependency or external rule requires wider consequences.
- Legitimate non-agreement is a possible outcome even if the protocol operated correctly.

### Possible classifications — not forced into one

- **N1 — Legitimate Non-Agreement:** The protocol operated, but participants did not find mutually acceptable terms, e.g. over funding, accessibility, timing, or risk.
- **F3 — Participant Protocol Violation:** If a participant persisted in excluded historical linkage or bypassed agreed procedure after rulings.
- **F4 — Adversarial Exploitation:** If the conditional admission about the storage dispute was used to manipulate the process contrary to its function.
- **F2 — Implementation Failure:** If the chair or participants failed to apply the grammar correctly — e.g. allowed contagion, failed to test dependencies, failed to record scope, or treated disagreement as agreement.
- **F5 — Environmental Failure:** If external conditions made the mechanism insufficient, such as no accessible alternative, no funding authority, worsening leak risk, or contractor constraints. This should not be assumed without evidence.
- **F1 — Architecture Failure:** Not justified by mere non-agreement. It would require evidence that the protocol lacked necessary machinery for a genuine dependency or repeatedly failed in a way that exposes a structural defect. The supplied scenario does not by itself establish that.

### What cannot be concluded

- That the wider relationship has failed.
- That the historical storage allegation is true or false.
- That the adult education group or neighbourhood association is generally untrustworthy.
- That Functionally Bounded Summits is empirically ineffective.
- That the building manager’s action equals participant agreement.
- That the room decision resolves the storage dispute or any other historical claim.
- That no future bounded negotiation is possible.

The failure diagnosis should distinguish the negotiation outcome from any protocol violations and from the building manager’s separate authority. It should not force a single failure class where the evidence supports multiple or only N1.
