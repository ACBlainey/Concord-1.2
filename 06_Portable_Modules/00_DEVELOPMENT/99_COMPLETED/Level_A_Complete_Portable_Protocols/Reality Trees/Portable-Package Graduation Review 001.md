# Reality Trees — Portable-Package Graduation Review 001

**Module:** Reality Trees  
**Candidate specification:** v0.3 — post-transfer graduation candidate  
**Review status:** COMPLETED  
**Decision:** **READY FOR PORTABLE PACKAGING / GRADUATION CONDITIONS SATISFIED AT SPECIFICATION LEVEL**  
**Date:** September 2026

---

## 1. Review question

Has Reality Trees progressed far enough from a Concord-derived candidate into a sufficiently source-resolved, operationally specified and independently transferable method to justify a standalone portable release?

This review concerns **specification-level portability**, not empirical superiority or validation of claimed cognitive benefits.

---

## 2. Development chain reviewed

The review considers the following chain:

> **Originating Reality Trees concept → later Concord specialised formulation → source resolution → source recovery → v0.2 recovered-scope specification → RT-BTT-002 → RT2-T2 → v0.3 supported revisions**

The earlier RT-BTT-001 brief is retained as provenance for the narrower pre-recovery specification and was superseded before execution.

---

## 3. Source integrity

### Finding: PASS

The source audit initially resolved the later canonical Concord formulation.

Subsequent author-supplied originating material demonstrated that this formulation had specialised the method and partially lost its earlier generality. The audit was reopened rather than pretending the earlier finding remained complete.

The recovered primitive is now represented as:

> **Represent materially relevant possible realities, explanations, choices or pathways as branches so that they can remain simultaneously visible and separately weighted without premature binary collapse.**

The portable specification preserves both:

1. the broad originating possibility/choice primitive;
2. later epistemic, experimental and organisational applications.

New AI-memory and persistent-uncertainty implications remain explicitly separated as hypotheses rather than back-projected into the historical source.

---

## 4. Operational completeness

### Finding: PASS

v0.3 supplies:

- a root/present-state discipline;
- branch-generation rule;
- material-distinction rule;
- recursive decomposition;
- multi-dimensional weighting;
- separation of personal belief, evidential support and contextual decision weight;
- assumptions and causal examination;
- optional consequences/failure-mode examination;
- testing boundary;
- branch status/disposition distinction;
- updating;
- bounded stopping;
- lightweight and extended modes;
- misuse/failure modes;
- provenance guidance;
- minimum input/output;
- scope and evidential boundaries.

No blocking operational omission remains from RT-BTT-002.

---

## 5. Simplicity preservation

### Finding: PASS

The recovered method is intentionally simple.

The post-test revisions do not introduce:

- mandatory numerical scoring;
- Bayesian inference;
- formal decision optimisation;
- mandatory causal modelling;
- prescribed visual notation;
- exhaustive branching;
- mandatory worked examples;
- or a formal conflict-resolution algorithm between weighting dimensions.

The method can therefore remain lightweight while supporting more structured use where consequence warrants it.

> **Additional structure is optional depth, not a requirement to turn every choice into a formal analysis.**

---

## 6. Independent transfer evidence

### Finding: PASS WITH BOUNDED CLAIM

RT-BTT-002 tested the recovered broader scope on a non-Concord practical choice problem.

The independent response successfully demonstrated:

- general choice branching rather than only truth hypotheses;
- preference/evidence/context separation;
- recursive decomposition;
- provisional action/investigation without certainty;
- changed-context reweighting;
- stable personal preference alongside changed contextual suitability;
- branch discipline;
- bounded stopping;
- operation without Blaineyan Reasoning.

Formal result:

> **RT2-T2 — TRANSFER SUCCESS WITH MINOR REVISION**

The five supported minor revisions were incorporated into v0.3.

A repeat AI test of the same transfer question would add little evidence relative to testing genuinely different claims.

---

## 7. Relationship to Blaineyan Reasoning

### Finding: CLEANLY BOUNDED

The relationship remains:

> **Blaineyan Reasoning → may use → Reality Trees**

Reality Trees is neither equivalent to nor dependent upon Blaineyan Reasoning.

RT-BTT-002 provides direct transfer evidence that Reality Trees can operate independently for the tested task.

---

## 8. Weighting architecture

### Finding: DISTINCTIVE FEATURE PRESERVED

The recovered source distinction between personal belief and evidential weighting survives into the portable method.

v0.3 generalises this cautiously into separable dimensions where relevant:

> **Branch existence ≠ personal belief weight ≠ evidential support weight ≠ contextual decision weight.**

These dimensions are not required to be numerical and must not be silently aggregated.

This feature allows a selected action or investigation to differ from the branch a participant personally prefers or currently considers most likely.

---

## 9. Choice without certainty

### Finding: OPERATIONALLY DEMONSTRATED WITHIN TEST BOUNDARY

RT-BTT-002 demonstrates that the method can support a provisional choice while retaining multiple unresolved branches.

Therefore the following limited method-level statement is supported:

> **Choice under uncertainty does not require certainty about one branch.**

This does not establish an autonomous AI decision architecture. It shows only that the representational method can be used this way in the tested prompted task.

---

## 10. Persistent AI reasoning and weighted epistemic memory

### Finding: OUTSIDE GRADUATION CLAIM

The Active Development sketch concerning:

- persistent branch topology;
- weighted epistemic memory;
- corrigible memory;
- analogue-like semantic state on digital hardware;
- and AI action under unresolved epistemic alternatives

is a source-inspired architectural hypothesis.

Reality Trees v1.0 must not claim that RT-BTT-002 validates those hypotheses.

They remain appropriate future research.

---

## 11. Human usability

### Finding: UNTESTED

The originating concept was developed partly as a human cognitive aid, including forced-binary belief conflict and ordinary decision-making.

The present blind transfer evidence is AI-instance evidence.

Accordingly:

> **AI-instance portability ≠ demonstrated human usability or benefit.**

Human testing may be valuable later, but it is not a blocking condition for packaging a method explicitly labelled with its evidential boundary.

---

## 12. Comparative effectiveness

### Finding: UNTESTED

No evidence currently establishes that Reality Trees:

- improve decisions relative to ordinary reasoning;
- reduce cognitive bias;
- reduce cognitive dissonance;
- outperform decision trees, argument maps, Bayesian methods or other approaches;
- improve AI reasoning;
- or improve long-term memory.

These are empirical/comparative questions and must remain open.

## 14. Decision

> **READY FOR PORTABLE PACKAGING / GRADUATION CONDITIONS SATISFIED AT SPECIFICATION LEVEL**

Reality Trees may now be packaged as a standalone v1.0 portable module in the main `06_Portable_Modules` area.

Development records should remain in `00_DEVELOPMENT` for provenance.

The standalone release should contain the operational method and concise provenance/evidence boundaries, but should not require readers to inspect the development record in order to use it.

---

## 15. Evidential boundary for release

A v1.0 release may state that:

> **Reality Trees is a source-resolved portable method for explicitly representing multiple possible realities, explanations, choices or pathways with separable weighting dimensions, and a fresh AI instance successfully transferred the recovered-scope specification to a bounded non-Concord choice problem without Blaineyan Reasoning.**

It must not state that:

- the method is empirically superior;
- it improves human cognition;
- it has been validated across populations or domains;
- it improves autonomous AI reasoning;
- weighted epistemic memory has been validated;
- or all represented branches deserve equal credibility.

> **Specification-level graduation ≠ empirical validation.**
