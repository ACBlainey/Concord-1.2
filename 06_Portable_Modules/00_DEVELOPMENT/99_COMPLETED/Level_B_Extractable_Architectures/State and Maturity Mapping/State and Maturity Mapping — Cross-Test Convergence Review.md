# State and Maturity Mapping — Cross-Test Convergence Review

**Module:** State and Maturity Mapping (SMM)  
**Method:** PMEDG v1.2  
**Evidence base:** Source Resolution and Extraction Audit 001; Portable Specifications v0.1 and v0.2; SMM-BTT-001; SMM-BTT-002  
**Status:** CROSS-TEST CONVERGENCE / BOUNDED REVISION AUTHORISED  
**Date:** September 2026

## 1. Purpose

This review asks whether the two independent blind transfer tests converge on the same portable mechanism, whether their differences reveal a fundamental defect or bounded specification pressure, and whether another blind transfer test is presently justified.

It does not upgrade SMM merely because two tests passed. The decision is based on what uncertainty remains after comparing the tests.

## 2. Test record

### BTT-001

Specification: v0.1.

Independent classification:

> **SMM-T3 — FUNCTIONAL TRANSFER**

Frozen-key result:

> **50 / 50 frozen predictions materially confirmed**

> **0 / 14 fundamental-failure indicators observed**

The first transfer test established that the extracted mechanism could operate in a materially non-Concord laboratory/research setting. It exposed bounded clarification pressure rather than kernel failure and authorised v0.2.

### BTT-002

Specification: v0.2.

Independent classification:

> **SMM-T3 — FUNCTIONAL TRANSFER**

Frozen-key result:

> **52 / 52 frozen predictions materially confirmed**

> **0 / 14 fundamental-failure indicators observed**

The second transfer test used distributed spacecraft mission operations / ground-segment readiness and deliberately pressured the clarifications introduced after BTT-001.

The evaluator applied those clarifications operationally and again reported no fundamental mechanism failure.

## 3. Cross-test invariant convergence

The following invariants survived both independent transfer domains.

### 3.1 Presence ≠ maturity

Both tests preserved the distinction between whether a function exists/is supplied and how developed or assured it is.

**Convergence:** STRONG.

### 3.2 Maturity ≠ context-relative sufficiency

Both tests rejected a context-free inference from general maturity to adequacy for every use context.

BTT-002 further demonstrated explicit context-set use.

**Convergence:** STRONG.

### 3.3 Local absence ≠ systemic absence

Both tests preserved distributed and interface-supplied capability instead of treating lack of local implementation as absence.

BTT-002 additionally pressured a legacy one-owner schema and lossy export.

**Convergence:** STRONG.

### 3.4 Dependency blockage ≠ local developmental deficiency

Both tests retained upstream blockage separately from local capability maturity.

**Convergence:** STRONG.

### 3.5 Bounded non-discovery ≠ proven global absence

Both tests preserved uncertainty when the declared search/source-resolution boundary did not justify a definitive required-function gap.

**Convergence:** STRONG.

### 3.6 Stale ≠ false or automatically insufficient

Both tests preserved prior historical state while creating current review pressure after material change.

BTT-002 confirmed the revised v0.2 rule but exposed a remaining default-current-state interface ambiguity.

**Convergence:** STRONG AT MECHANISM LEVEL; BOUNDED INTERFACE CLARIFICATION REMAINS.

### 3.7 UNKNOWN and DISPUTED are operational states

Neither test required binary forcing of uncertainty or disagreement.

**Convergence:** STRONG.

### 3.8 Consequence ≠ centrality

Both tests preserved low-connectivity/high-consequence issues and kept final prioritisation external.

**Convergence:** STRONG.

### 3.9 Aggregate/projection ≠ underlying state truth

Both tests rejected scalar or lossy representations as substitutes for the multidimensional source state.

BTT-002 directly exercised a headline readiness aggregate and a lossy one-owner export.

**Convergence:** STRONG AT MECHANISM LEVEL; SERIALISATION/DISCLOSURE CLARIFICATION REMAINS.

### 3.10 Need for ownership ≠ authority to invent ownership

Both tests preserved unknown/unassigned ownership and kept ownership resolution external.

**Convergence:** STRONG.

### 3.11 State mapping ≠ substantive authority

Neither test required SMM to become the decision, funding, mission-command, ownership or priority authority.

**Convergence:** STRONG.

### 3.12 Current state ≠ destruction of history

Both tests preserved provenance, dispute and prior state. BTT-002 additionally exercised explicit CORRECTS and scope-limited SUPERSEDES/QUALIFIES relations.

**Convergence:** STRONG AT MECHANISM LEVEL; RELATION-SCOPE CLARIFICATION REMAINS.

### 3.13 Unresolved gap ≠ automatic new development candidacy

Both tests preserved active-work state and legitimate suppression/continuation rather than duplicating work.

**Convergence:** STRONG.

### 3.14 Mapping ≠ perpetual development

Both tests permitted NO_ACTION/HOLD and did not interpret stopping as proof of perfection.

**Convergence:** STRONG.

### 3.15 Portability does not require Concord machinery

Both independent evaluators operated without requiring Concord, CDT, KCS or the Civilisation Clock.

**Convergence:** STRONG.

## 4. What changed between tests

BTT-001 did not merely produce a successful transfer result. It exposed eleven bounded specification/interface pressures that were incorporated into v0.2.

BTT-002 then deliberately re-pressured those areas. The resulting evidence shows three important things:

1. **The v0.2 revisions did not destabilise the kernel.**
2. **The revisions were usable in a second materially different domain.**
3. **The remaining ambiguities moved further toward representation/interface precision rather than revealing a new architectural contradiction.**

This is evidence of convergence rather than repeated identical testing.

## 5. Recurrent versus newly sharpened pressure

Several pressures recur across both tests:

- assessment-unit/partial-function semantics;
- stale consequential state;
- candidate-absence escalation;
- aggregation disclosure;
- active-work suppression;
- ownership gaps;
- distributed-supply projection;
- assessment history/supersession;
- interface-evidence stewardship;
- consequence preservation;
- context-relative representation.

Their recurrence does not indicate failure because BTT-002 successfully used the v0.2 mechanism in each area. It does indicate that the portable specification should close remaining interface ambiguity before graduation.

BTT-002 sharpened these into twelve bounded revision targets:

1. composite assessment-unit decision rule;
2. context-set storage/reference pattern;
3. default stale-current-state treatment;
4. candidate-absence materiality criterion;
5. distributed-supply projection/reference contract;
6. interface-evidence stewardship response;
7. scope-limited assessment relations;
8. ownership/governance-escalation candidacy;
9. canonical candidate-response vocabulary consistency;
10. minimum aggregate disclosure/refusal rule;
11. consequence carriage through downstream projections;
12. HOLD versus NO_ACTION semantics.

No item requires replacement of the core state architecture.

## 6. Fundamental-failure convergence

Across the two blind tests:

- frozen predictions materially confirmed: **102 / 102**;
- fundamental-failure indicators observed: **0 / 28 test-specific checks**;
- independent transfer classifications: **2 × SMM-T3 FUNCTIONAL TRANSFER**;
- wholly unpredicted mechanism failures: **0**.

These totals are evidence summaries, not statistical probabilities and not proof of universal validity.

The significant point is structural: two materially different non-Concord domains failed to force collapse of the same core distinctions.

## 7. Residual uncertainty

Residual uncertainty now falls primarily into four classes.

### A. Portable representation precision

Examples: context-set serialization, distributed-supply projection, aggregate disclosure format.

### B. State-transition precision

Examples: default treatment of stale current state and scope-limited assessment relations.

### C. Candidate-response vocabulary

Examples: ownership/governance escalation, interface-evidence stewardship response, CONTINUE_EXISTING_WORK consistency, HOLD versus NO_ACTION.

### D. Host-boundary declarations

Examples: assessment-unit granularity and adequacy of the declared search/source-resolution boundary.

These are real issues and should be resolved. However, current evidence does not show that another blind domain is needed to discover whether the kernel can transfer.

## 8. Is BTT-003 justified now?

### Decision

> **NO IMMEDIATE BTT-003 REQUIRED**

A third blind test at the current specification state would predominantly re-test distinctions already independently exercised twice.

The more informative next operation is to produce a bounded v0.3 specification that resolves the residual interface ambiguities without expanding SMM's authority or conceptual scope.

BTT-003 should become necessary only if v0.3 introduces a materially new mechanism, materially changes an invariant, or if graduation review identifies an unresolved portability question that cannot be settled from the existing two tests.

A later implementation/conformance test may also be useful, but that is a different question from whether the abstract mechanism transfers.

## 9. v0.3 revision authorisation

A bounded v0.3 is authorised.

The revision should implement the twelve targets in Section 5 while preserving the tested kernel.

It must not:

- create a universal maturity score;
- turn SMM into a priority engine;
- make SMM an ownership resolver;
- make SMM own dependency discovery;
- make SMM own external evidence;
- create a universal quantitative search threshold;
- require one owner;
- subordinate consequence to centrality;
- convert stale into false/automatically insufficient;
- convert bounded candidate absence into certainty;
- remove legitimate HOLD/no-action;
- require Concord, CDT, KCS or the Civilisation Clock.

## 10. Graduation implication

The evidence supports moving SMM from repeated transfer testing toward **graduation preparation**, but not immediate graduation of v0.2.

The appropriate sequence is:

1. produce **Portable Specification v0.3** as a bounded convergence revision;
2. audit v0.3 against the BTT-001 and BTT-002 evidence to ensure no unsupported mechanism has been added;
3. prepare the portable package/README and implementation/conformance guidance required by PMEDG;
4. perform graduation review;
5. run an additional blind test only if the revision or graduation audit exposes a material unresolved transfer question.

## 11. Cross-test conclusion

> **CROSS-TEST CONVERGENCE: ESTABLISHED**

> **CORE MECHANISM: STABLE ACROSS TWO MATERIALLY DIFFERENT NON-CONCORD TRANSFER DOMAINS**

> **RESIDUAL ISSUES: BOUNDED SPECIFICATION / INTERFACE PRECISION**

> **BTT-003: NOT CURRENTLY REQUIRED**

> **NEXT ACTION: PORTABLE SPECIFICATION v0.3**

This conclusion is deliberately bounded. It does not claim universal validity or implementation completeness. It records that the current transfer evidence is sufficient to stop repeating domain-transfer tests until a material reason for another test appears.
