## 1. Purpose

This review asks whether the Blaineyan Reasoning development candidate has completed the work required to leave the extractable-architecture development stage and be packaged as a standalone portable module.

This is not an empirical-validation review and does not ask whether Blaineyan Reasoning is superior to other methodologies.

---

## 2. Graduation criteria

A portable-package candidate should demonstrate:

1. identifiable source provenance;
2. sufficient source extraction;
3. a standalone operational specification;
4. explicit epistemic boundaries;
5. removal of unnecessary Concord-specific dependencies;
6. clear boundary from adjacent portable modules;
7. resource and misuse controls;
8. usable stopping and specialist-handoff conditions;
9. at least one independent non-Concord transfer test;
10. revision from observed defects;
11. no known blocking portability defect;
12. preservation of unresolved claims as unresolved rather than silently promoting them.

## 4. Human–AI asymmetry and sensor-fusion boundary

A potentially important research hypothesis has emerged outside the blind-test sequence.

The methodology originated primarily through human use and includes high-speculation, inventive and broad possibility-space exploration. AI use may exhibit different strengths, potentially including procedural precision, consistency, classification, systematic examination and re-grounding. Human use may or may not provide broader inventive movement.

A further hypothesis is that Blaineyan Reasoning could function as a synthesis protocol in heterogeneous Human–AI Sensor Fusion, allowing different cognitive fields of view to contribute to a shared branch space.

Neither proposition is established.

They should therefore be cross-linked as future research rather than built into the portable module as assumed role allocation.

In particular:

> **Human ≠ necessarily Scout.**  
> **AI ≠ necessarily Cartographer.**

Either substrate may perform either function.

> **Potential complementarity ≠ demonstrated asymmetry.**

This hypothesis does not block portable graduation.

---

## 5. What graduation means

Graduation means:

- the architecture has been extracted into a standalone portable specification;
- source completeness has been checked to the level required for packaging;
- the specification has survived repeated independent AI usability transfer;
- known non-blocking defects from those tests have been addressed;
- boundaries and unresolved claims are explicit.

Graduation does **not** mean:

- scientifically validated;
- superior to alternative methodologies;
- proven effective for humans;
- proven effective across all domains;
- proven to increase creativity or invention;
- proven to improve AI reasoning;
- proven to improve human–AI teams;
- canonical outside its project of origin.

## 6. Decision

> **READY FOR PORTABLE PACKAGING / GRADUATION CONDITIONS SATISFIED AT SPECIFICATION LEVEL**

No further same-question blind AI transfer test is required before packaging.

The next operation should be packaging rather than further theory development:

1. create the clean standalone portable-module file in the main `06_Portable_Modules` area;
2. preserve the development specification, audits, tests and evaluations in `00_DEVELOPMENT`;
3. include concise provenance and evidential-status sections in the standalone package;
4. avoid importing development-history detail that is unnecessary for users of the module;
5. cross-link, but do not merge, the separate Reality Trees module;
6. retain the human–AI asymmetry / Sensor Fusion idea as a separate research hypothesis.

---

## 7. Final boundary

The evidence presently supports:

> **Blaineyan Reasoning has reached a sufficiently specified and repeatedly transferable form to be packaged as a standalone portable reasoning methodology.**

The evidence does not presently support:

> **Blaineyan Reasoning has been shown to be a superior or generally validated reasoning methodology.**

Those are different claims and must remain different.
