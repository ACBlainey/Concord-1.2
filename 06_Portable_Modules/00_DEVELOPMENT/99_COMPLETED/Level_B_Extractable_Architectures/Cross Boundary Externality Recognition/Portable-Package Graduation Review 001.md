## 4. Standalone completeness — PASS

v0.3 defines purpose/scope, inputs, operating chain, affected parties, scoped standing, evidence/causality, contextual materiality, causal contributors/responsibility, authority/coordination, actor-relative response, non-participation, low-trust operation, correction/provenance, multi-boundary mapping, assertion disposition, case states, dimensional outputs, failure modes, safeguards, operating procedure and review triggers.

No Concord-specific institution is required.

## 5. Hidden dependency removal — PASS

The module may interface with MNC, FBS, CWA, rights/standing translation, evidence/provenance systems and domain-specific authorities, but none is silently required for the basic architecture. Legal, scientific, compensation, rights and enforcement decisions are externalised.

## 6. Internal consistency — PASS

Central separations remain coherent: Consequence ≠ Responsibility; Responsibility ≠ Authority/Jurisdiction; Authority/Jurisdiction ≠ Remedy; Remedy ≠ General Authority; Standing to Raise ≠ Authority to Decide; Severity ≠ Proof of Causation; Causal Contributor ≠ Responsible Party; Responsibility Mapping ≠ Quantitative Apportionment; Candidate Opened ≠ Escalation Required; Assertion Disposition ≠ Case Disposition; Unsupported Assertion ≠ Automatically Separate Externality; Refusal ≠ Exoneration; Refusal ≠ Automatic Authority Over Refuser.

## 7. Blind transfer — PASS AT SPECIFICATION LEVEL

BTT-001, interconnected computing/research-data infrastructure: 22/22 frozen predictions materially confirmed; CB-T3; no fundamental failure.

BTT-002, watershed/agriculture/environmental-resource interaction: 28/28 frozen predictions materially confirmed; CB-T3; no fundamental failure.

> **50/50 frozen predictions materially confirmed across two materially different non-Concord domains.**

Both testers independently found the module usable without Concord source context.

## 8. v0.3 delta review — PASS

The changes are clarificatory, not architecturally transformative. They add explicit records for multi-context materiality and standing scope; separate causal contribution from normative responsibility; distinguish plausible/supported and non-agent/unidentified contributors; make CB-C12 dimension-specific; distinguish passive absence from refusal; bound assertion-generated externalities; clarify consequence-relative primary boundaries; represent parallel voluntary coordination; and define review-trigger records.

They do not change the core operating chain, manufacture authority, reverse tested distinctions or invalidate BTT-001/BTT-002. A third blind test is not required for specification-level graduation.

## 9. Failure-mode coverage — PASS

The specification covers authority, standing, recognition, refusal and evidence laundering; boundary denial; accusation-as-proof; single-cause compression; remedy expansion; scope contamination; uncertainty erasure; mitigation/responsibility conflation; non-participation paralysis; and provenance loss.

## 10. Epistemic discipline — PASS

UNKNOWN, DISPUTED, INCOMPLETE and uncertainty-bearing states remain representable. Evidence is separated from causation; causation from responsibility; responsibility from apportionment; materiality from legal breach; assertion disposition from case disposition; case opening from escalation; coordination from authority.

The portability claim remains bounded to specification-level transfer.

## 11. Portability — PASS

> **Effects can cross boundaries even when authority does not.**

The mechanism transferred independently into two domains with different actors, consequences, evidence structures and authority relationships. No source-civilisation dependency was required.

## 12. Remaining limitations

Release boundaries, not blockers: domain-specific evidence standards; legal jurisdiction/enforceability; specialist causal attribution; quantitative apportionment; compensation authority; domain-specific standing rules; no real operational empirical validation; context-dependent materiality thresholds; and possible need for secure evidence/provenance infrastructure in low-trust deployment.
