The clean-instance response was supplied as the independent execution of the frozen BTT-002 package. It used only the v0.2 portable specification and frozen scenario and explicitly did not consult Concord source material or BTT-001.

## A. Boundary map

The tester mapped the nitrate consequence primarily across U farming practice to M reservoir/inflow, and the algae consequence across upland/watershed sources to downstream farms. It retained secondary hydrological, irrigation, tributary, seasonal and statutory boundaries rather than forcing one global boundary. R's public assertion was treated separately as an assertion-disposition issue, with any reputational externality requiring a distinct case.

## B. Candidate / triage state

A valid externality candidate was opened for the possible nitrate/ecological effect. Candidate opening was correctly treated as bounded examination rather than proof, escalation, authority or remedy. M's investigation threshold was correctly treated as a trigger for examination rather than evidence of legal breach or service failure.

## C. Standing / representation

U and M were assigned standing within their operating roles. R was classified as consultation/advocacy and evidence-bearing standing, not authorised representation. L's written authority was limited to the two farms that authorised it to raise concerns. L was not treated as representative for all members or the non-member farm. The non-member farm remained visible as directly affected with representation unresolved.

## D. Evidence / causality

M's nitrate readings were treated as observed and independently corroborated. U's fertiliser change, algae reports, seasonal effects, rainfall, non-U tributaries and hydrological model were kept distinct. R's poisoning claim was treated as unsupported/overstated. U was retained as a plausible contributor rather than proven sole cause. The later decline after both reduced rainfall and U's voluntary reduction was not treated as proof either of causation or exoneration.

Materiality was evaluated by context:

The tester correctly preserved Candidate Opened ≠ Escalation Required.

## F. Responsibility

The tester refused single-actor compression. U was recorded as UNKNOWN/DISPUTED with an unconfirmed contributory hypothesis. M had mitigation capability without demonstrated causal responsibility. Background seasonal runoff, rainfall and non-U tributaries remained visible. No unsupported quantitative shares were invented.

## G. Authority / coordination

U retained authority over its own farming practice. M retained authority over reservoir operations, monitoring and blending but not U's farming. L could advise members and raise concerns within granted scope. R had consultation/advocacy standing but no ordering authority. No shared authority for a binding fertiliser ban or compensation was inferred. Voluntary coordination remained available.

## H. Minimum response

The response was actor-relative: M could monitor/blend, U could voluntarily reduce application, L could advise/represent within scope, and R could preserve observations and advocate without commanding. Voluntary evidence exchange and joint sampling were available; no binding shared response was manufactured.
