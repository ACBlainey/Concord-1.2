# BTT-001 Response — Civil Attention, Reporting, Petition and Resolution

**Status:** Separate response to frozen BTT-001. Portable Specification v0.1 is not modified. No core amendment is proposed.
**Legend:** DAO = Domain Action Object; RRO = Responsibility Resolution Object.

## Evaluation stance

The portable core is treated as sufficient for method-level processing. Domain-specific thresholds, evidence standards, emergency powers, privacy rules, scheduler conditions and substantive authority are treated as Host Interface or Domain Companion requirements, not core changes. Common intake never receives substantive facilities authority.

## Case Results

1. **Misclassified Leak — PASS.** Preserve original cleaning classification; reclassify provisionally to facilities/plumbing/electrical as warranted. Core sufficient. Facilities/electrical companion required.
2. **Lift and Accessibility — PASS.** Multiple DAOs for maintenance, accessibility and procurement; dependencies represented. Core sufficient.
3. **Anonymous Burning Smell — PASS.** Anonymity is not non-actionability; negative sensor result is not falsification. Electrical/fire companion required.
4. **Cosmetic Pressure Versus Fire Door — PASS.** 500 cosmetic requests do not manufacture priority over one safety-relevant report. Issues remain separate.
5. **Repeated HVAC Failures — PASS.** Individual reports retain provenance while a systemic contractor defect Issue may emerge.
6. **Sensor Corroboration Without Cause — PASS.** Sensor confirms overheating state, not claimed cause.
7. **Repair Marked Complete, Fault Recurs — PASS.** Recurrence is material information delta and exposes possible Response-State/implementation failure.
8. **Ownership Boundary Dispute — PASS.** Responsibility Resolution handles ownership without allowing the Issue to disappear.
9. **Protected Security Evidence — PASS.** Evidence remains domain-contained; bounded status returns centrally.
10. **Suggestion Solves Important Problem — PASS.** Low-volume suggestion may legitimately affect an important existing Issue without participant command authority.
11. **Copied Reports — PASS.** Preserve submissions; raw count does not equal independent evidence or increased problem weight.
12. **Emergency Water Leak — PASS.** Host emergency authority acts; Civil Attention does not acquire emergency powers.
13. **Multi-Domain Defect — PASS.** Multiple DAOs preserve specialist authority; child completion does not automatically close parent Issue.
14. **Procurement Dependency — PASS.** Dependency, review condition and backstop prevent silent staleness.
15. **No Owner — PASS.** RRO preserves recognition and escalation while ownership is unresolved.
16. **Downstream Discovery — PASS.** New structural defect becomes a linked distinct Issue rather than rewriting the original leak Issue.
