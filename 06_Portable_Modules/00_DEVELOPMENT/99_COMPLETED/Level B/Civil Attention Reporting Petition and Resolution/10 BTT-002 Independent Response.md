## External / Upstream Dependency Finding

The test does **not** require a new portable-core object for external ownership.

Existing architecture is sufficient:

**Local Issue + DAO/RRO + Represented External Dependency + Local Responsibility + Mandatory Return + Review/Backstop**

External actors cannot be assigned authority by the core. The host may communicate, escalate, fork, replace, mitigate or otherwise respond through legitimate local authority.

> **External Dependency ≠ Transfer of Local Responsibility**
