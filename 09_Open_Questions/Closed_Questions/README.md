# Closed Questions

This folder contains Concord questions that have been closed, retired, answered at a defined conceptual level, rejected as premises, or moved outside required Concordian civil scope.

Closed does not mean permanently immune from reconsideration. A question may be reopened if new evidence, changed architecture, or a newly identified failure mode invalidates the basis for closure.

Historical development and full provenance remain preserved in frozen Concord V1.1. This V1.2 folder should contain only the concise current closure record needed to understand what was closed, why, and under what conditions it may reopen.
