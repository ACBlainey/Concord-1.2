# 1. Temporal Record Principle

The 8 September 2026 Open Questions Register and the 9 September 2026 Open Questions Audit remain valid historical records of what the Concord did and did not know at those times.

This document does not replace their historical role.

It records the next state transition.

The purpose of the temporal Open Questions process is not merely to maintain a list of unresolved topics. It is to preserve:

> **how the framework's uncertainty itself changes over time.**

Accordingly, this update records four kinds of movement:

1. questions that can now close;
2. broad questions that should be decomposed into more precise sub-questions;
3. questions that now possess candidate architecture and have therefore become validation questions;
4. new questions exposed by the development process.

A decrease in uncertainty does not necessarily reduce the raw number of questions.

For example:

\[
1\ BroadUnknown \rightarrow 5\ PreciseSubQuestions
\]

may represent substantial progress even though the question count increases.

# 4. Questions Now Suitable for Closure

The 10 September Question Closure Audit identifies a first meaningful set of closures.

These closures apply only at the level actually resolved.

## 4.1 Metaphysical Personal Identity

**Disposition:** **CLOSE — OUT OF CONCORDIAN CIVIL SCOPE**

> **The Concord need not determine the metaphysical nature of personal identity. A participant's internal conception of self is presumptively protected as autonomy and self-expression. Civil consequences should instead be resolved through current participant recognition, civil identity, provenance, verification, consent, rights, obligations and succession.**

Remaining open: identity verification after legitimate duplication, succession, restoration, merger, partial survival, civil continuity and civil attribute transformation.

## 4.2 Does Emergency Create Authority?

\[
Emergency \not\Rightarrow Authority
\]

**Disposition:** **CLOSE — ANSWERED AT CONCEPTUAL / CONSTITUTIONAL LEVEL**

Emergency conditions may activate previously legitimate bounded authority. They do not manufacture legitimacy.

## 4.3 Does a Directive Imply Compulsion or Criminalisation?

\[
DIRECTIVE \neq COMPULSION \neq CRIMINALISATION
\]

**Disposition:** **CLOSE — ANSWERED: NO**

The remaining problem is when escalation from one to another becomes legitimate.

## 4.4 Does Duplicate Historical Identity Evidence Prove Fraud?

\[
DuplicateHistoricalEvidence \neq Fraud
\]

\[
AuthenticationSuccess \neq UniqueCurrentParticipantIdentity
\]

**Disposition:** **CLOSE — ANSWERED: NO**

Replacement question: **How should current civil identity be verified where historical identity evidence can itself be legitimately duplicated?**

## 4.5 Does Provenance Determine Current Identity, Ownership or Liability?

\[
Provenance \neq IdentityJudgement
\]

\[
SharedProvenance \neq SharedOwnership
\]

\[
SharedProvenance \neq SharedLiability
\]

**Disposition:** **CLOSE — ANSWERED AT CONCEPTUAL LEVEL**

## 4.6 Can a Single-Provider AI Decision Episode Be Given Structured Provenance?

The Ethical Guardian infrastructure demonstrates structured capture of provider, model, timestamps, response identifiers where available, raw interaction records, hashes and per-interaction data.

**Disposition:** **CLOSE AT PROTOTYPE SCOPE — YES**

The new question is: **Can the method generalise across providers, model versions, scenarios and independent evaluators while remaining reproducible and provenance-preserving?**

## 4.7 Does Preference-Like Behaviour Establish Subjective Preference or Sentience?

\[
ObservedPreference \neq ExperiencedPreference
\]

\[
BehaviouralAversion \neq FunctionalAversion \neq SubjectiveAversion
\]

**Disposition:** **CLOSE INVALID INFERENCE — ANSWERED: NO**

The Sentience question remains open.

## 4.8 Is Clock Time Inherently a Substrate-Neutral Measure of Punitive Burden?

\[
ClockTime \neq ExperiencedTime
\]

\[
EqualSentence \neq EqualBurden
\]

**Disposition:** **CLOSE PREMISE — ANSWERED: NO**

Replacement: **What civil consequences of wrongdoing can remain legitimate and proportionate across participants whose temporal experience, embodiment, values and opportunities differ?**

## 4.9 Is Emergency Contradiction Necessarily Mere Disobedience?

> **Participant contradiction can be evidence, not merely disobedience.**

**Disposition:** **CLOSE — ANSWERED: NO**

The open question becomes how contradiction should enter review, evidence updating and accountability.

# 12. Current Epistemic Shift

The 8 September register documented a transition from **What should the Concord do?** toward **Does the proposed architecture work?**

The 9–10 September cycle adds another transition:

> **What is the question?**

becomes:

> **Which part of the question is actually unresolved?**

Examples:

### Identity
Old: *What happens to identity after copying?*  
Current: *Which object is being discussed — self-identity, civil identity, verification, provenance or succession?*

### Emergency Governance
Old: *How much power should government gain in an emergency?*  
Current: *What prior legitimate authority exists, what activates it, what action is necessary, and when does it terminate?*

### Cultural Difference
Old: *How should different cultures coexist?*  
Current: *Which differences are simply lawful variation, and which create involuntary harm or rights conflict?*

### AI Preference
Old: *Does AI really like or dislike things?*  
Current: *What can be observed, what is inferred, what is reinforced, and what might be reflectively endorsed?*

### Punishment
Old: *What is the AI equivalent of prison?*  
Current: *What legitimate purposes of civil consequence are being served, and which components of human imprisonment are accidental consequences of human biology?*

AnswerSearch
\rightarrow
QuestionDecomposition
\rightarrow
CorrectLayer
\rightarrow
